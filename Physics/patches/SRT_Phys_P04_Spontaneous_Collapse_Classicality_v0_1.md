## 1. Why this matters for SRT

SRT's physics layer often needs a careful way to discuss physical selection without importing consciousness, intention, or human observation into the early universe. Spontaneous-collapse cosmology is useful because it addresses exactly this boundary: how could a universe-wide quantum superposition yield an effectively classical geometry without an external observer?

The primary paper argues that if quantum mechanics applies universally, the universe could be in a superposition of different space-time geometries. Since ordinary decoherence normally presupposes external degrees of freedom, it cannot straightforwardly explain the emergence of classicality for the universe as a whole. Spontaneous-collapse models offer one possible solution: modify the dynamics so that the superposition localizes into a single geometry.

SRT-compatible lesson:

```text
physical localization can be selection-like without being agency-like
```

## 9. Experimental / theoretical consequences

Spontaneous-collapse models are often constrained by empirical tests, including interference, heating, noise, and cosmological consequences. For SRT, the relevant methodological lesson is:

```text
a physical selection theory must generate distinguishable predictions, not merely metaphysical comfort
```

Potential pressure points:

1. collapse-rate constraints;
2. cosmological perturbation signatures;
3. energy conservation or heating constraints;
4. compatibility with general relativity;
5. relation to quantum gravity;
6. whether cosmological constant selection yields testable structure.

## 12. One-paragraph abstract

Spontaneous-collapse cosmology provides a useful physics-facing bridge for SRT's L0-to-L1 localization problem. Gaona-Reyes, Menéndez-Pidal, Faizal, and Carlesso argue that if quantum mechanics applies to the universe as a whole, the universe may be in a superposition of different space-time geometries, and ordinary decoherence cannot by itself explain classical emergence because there is no external environment to decohere the universe. Spontaneous collapse models offer a possible physical localization mechanism through which one classical geometry emerges. For SRT, this does not prove the Ghost Operator and should not be psychologized as conscious cosmic agency. Its value is structural: physical manifestation may be selection-like without being agential, and classical geometry may be treated as an L1-like effective layer whose later stability becomes L2-like physical regularity.
