## Suggested paragraph

Spontaneous-collapse cosmology offers a physics-facing analogue for SRT's L0-to-L1 localization problem. If the universe can be described as a quantum superposition of possible geometries, then ordinary decoherence is insufficient as a complete explanation because the universe as a whole lacks an external environment. Collapse models attempt to solve this by modifying the dynamics so that one classical geometry is physically localized. SRT should not identify this with consciousness or agency; its value is structural: a manifest layer may arise through physical localization of latent alternatives without requiring an external observer.
