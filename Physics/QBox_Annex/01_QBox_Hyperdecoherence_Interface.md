## 1. External concept overview

The QBox result is relevant to SRT because it proposes a formal situation in which ordinary quantum theory is not the final basal description, but an access-limited layer emerging from a deeper causally indefinite post-quantum theory through a hyperdecoherence map.

SRT should not overclaim this as proof of its ontology. The result is useful only as a structural interface:

```text
what appears as a stable reality layer may be a constrained-access projection of a deeper possibility structure
```

In SRT-facing language, this becomes a layered analogy:

```text
L0_deeper causal/atemporal possibility structure
  -- access restriction / hyperdecoherence / selection filter -->
L1_quantum-effective reality layer
  -- ordinary decoherence / measurement / environment -->
L1_classical-effective reality layer
```

The key bridge is not that QBox equals SRT. The key bridge is that QBox gives a physics-side example of a deeper structure becoming a lower-level effective theory through a rule that removes, hides, or renders inaccessible part of the underlying structure.

## 3. Boundary: L0 is not a hidden classical substrate

The QBox result is useful because it helps avoid a naive hidden-variable reading. A deeper layer beneath quantum theory need not look classical, deterministic, local, or causally ordered. It may be structurally stranger than quantum mechanics.

SRT-facing boundary:

```text
L0 is not a classical hidden world behind appearances.
L0 is a richer possibility structure whose projection may produce quantum-like or classical-like layers.
```

This boundary is safer than saying “L0 is QBox.” The latter is forbidden without a separate formal and empirical justification.

## 4. Layered analogy

A cautious layered interface:

```text
post-quantum latent structure
  -> access restriction / hyperdecoherence
quantum-effective layer
  -> ordinary decoherence / measurement / environmental stabilization
classical-effective layer
  -> repetition / constraint stabilization / law-like sedimentation
observer-stable L2 regularities
```

This analogy should be used to clarify access limitation and effective-layer formation. It should not be used to assert that QBox is the physical mechanism behind SRT.

## 5. Guardrails

Do not claim:

```text
QBox proves SRT
L0 literally is QBox
SRT requires retrocausal signalling
QBox confirms post-quantum physical reality
hyperdecoherence is experimentally established as the mechanism of quantum theory
```

Safe claims:

```text
QBox is a structural analogue for access-limited effective layers.
QBox pressure-tests whether SRT is overprojecting L1 assumptions into L0.
Hyperdecoherence can be used as an analogy for constrained accessibility.
A manifest theory may arise as the accessible projection of a richer latent domain.
```

## 7. Public-facing paragraph

Recent work on QBox theory and hyperdecoherence suggests that standard quantum theory may be recoverable from a deeper causally indefinite post-quantum structure when access to part of that structure is restricted. SRT should not identify its L0 with QBox, nor treat this as proof of SRT. But the structural lesson is important: what appears as a stable reality layer may be the accessible projection of a richer latent domain. This fits SRT's broader claim that manifest reality is not simply given; it is what becomes available, stable, and operational under a selection/access operator.
