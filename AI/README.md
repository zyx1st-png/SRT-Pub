## Status distinction

- `AI_POSITIONING_NOTE.md`: architecture-state guardrail and stake-bearing spectrum; use as the first safety layer.
- `_SRT_AI_Bridge.md`: bridge foundation for the AI domain; it does not replace Core or canonical definitions.
- `SRT_AI_Claim_Status.md`: claim-level guardrail; it prevents file-level over-reading of AI owner files.
- `CompactCore` files: current short stable summaries.
- Owner longforms: full domain arguments; may contain formal claims, explanatory discourse, and historical/interface material.
- Operational rubrics: evaluation tools; they are not canonical theory definitions.
- Split / Annex subdirectories: reading support, historical interface material, and extraction staging; they do not automatically elevate claims.

## Core guardrails

1. Do not collapse competence into stake.
2. Do not collapse persistence into consciousness.
3. Do not generalize inference-only `d_AI \approx 0` to training loops, persistent-memory systems, or future embodied systems without restating the architecture state.
4. Do not treat LLM capability comparison as SRT endorsement or denial of AI subjecthood.
5. Do not move or redefine `d-value`, `\Psi_f`, `L_0/L_1/L_2`, `\hat{G}_\theta`, or `T_dir` inside AI-domain files.
