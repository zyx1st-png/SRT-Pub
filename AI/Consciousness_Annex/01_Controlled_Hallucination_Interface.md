## Guardrails

- Controlled hallucination is an external perceptual-regime interface, not a SRT consciousness definition.
- Per `R4-C3 / ENG-03` (`P1-05`), this table **removes** legacy proxy `d-value` intervals and `Psi_f` state columns: perceptual constraint regimes and local physiological/computational regulation burdens do not define or serve as direct proxies for canonical `d` or canonical `Psi_f` (`_SRT_D_VALUE_CANONICAL.md / _SRT_PSI_F_CANONICAL.md`).
- Perceptual continuity does not by itself close `HP-B` phenomenality, establish Stage 2 `Bearer (P+E)` in artificial systems, or imply AI consciousness.
- **Subjecthood Threshold `S0–S6`** interfaces remain in the owner / rubric layer (strictly isolated from Primitive `S0`, **AI 赌注谱系 `S0–S4`**, and **AI 三型社会技术中介维度**).
