# SRT AI Agency, Co-Generation & Responsibility Note

> **Purpose**: Provide an operational manual for **Stage 3.3** (agency differentiation, generative initiative, and subject accountability) and **Stage 3.4** (human–AI co-generation and **AI 三型社会技术中介维度** responsibility networks).
> **Status**: Bridge / operational handbook (`R4-C3 / ENG-03` aligned; `canonical: false`). Does not unilaterally settle `R3-04-O5 / AG-2`, `R3-07-O6`, or **`O2-M = HOLD`**.
> **Mandatory Namespace Isolation**: All tier citations use full names — **Subjecthood Threshold `S0–S6`**, **AI 赌注谱系 `S0–S4`**, and **AI 三型社会技术中介维度** (Scaffold / Cage / Landscape), isolated from Primitive `S0`.

---

## 0. Core Guardrails: Four-Way Human–AI Accountability Separation (`R3-07 O2-A`)

Never collapse causal impact, generative initiative, socio-technical mediation, human accountability, and AI moral culpability into a single verdict:

```text
(1) AI-induced structural consequences
 != (2) AI socio-technical mediation role (AI 三型: Scaffold / Cage / Landscape)
 != (3) Human & institutional accountability (design, deployment, oversight, repair — O2-A)
 != (4) AI's own responsibility-bearing standing (Subjecthood Threshold S6)
```

Core operational rules:
- **Zero-Blocking**: Unestablished Stage 2 `Bearer (P+E)` does **not** prevent analyzing an AI system's functional control, generative initiative, or massive socio-technical mediation effects.
- **Zero-Laundering**: Unestablished **Subjecthood Threshold `S6`** for an AI system **never** exempts human developers, deployers, platforms, institutions, regulators, or users from accountability when that AI reshapes human `M(t)`, `σ_{sr}^{coll}`, `W_social`, or accessible reality (`Core_Law/SRT_Collective_Selection.md §8`).
- **Open Gates Preserved**: `R3-04-O5 / AG-2` (whether self-modifying non-`Bearer` agents instantiate genuine SRT agency), `R3-07-O6` (exact `O2-A` sufficiency and multi-party human attribution weights), and **`O2-M = HOLD`** remain explicitly open.

## 1. Non-Linear Mapping: `A0–A3` ↔ `R3-04` Five Partially Orthogonal Agency Dimensions & Five Capability Dimensions

Rather than reading `A0 → A3` as a linear hardware ladder automatically tracking bare `S0 → S6`, map practical operational classes onto `R3-04` **five partially orthogonal Agency dimensions** (`Philosophy/SRT_Philosophy_Agency_Subjecthood_v0_2.md §1.1`) and `R4-C2/C3` **five non-ladder capability dimensions** (`AI/SRT_AI_Architecture_CompactCore.md §3`):

| Operational Class | Minimal Operational Description | `R4-C2/C3` Capability Profile (Non-Ladder) | `R3-04` 5 Partially Orthogonal Agency Dimensions | **Subjecthood Threshold** & Responsibility Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`A0` · Tool Execution** | Executes generation, ranking, classification, or constrained tool calls under external framing | High structural / action / comparative; low self-reconfiguration | Minimal functional control; no bearing/reflexive/S6 standing | **Subjecthood Threshold `S0`**; responsibility lies entirely in the human/institutional network |
| **`A1` · Delegated Operational Agency** | Pursues externally supplied goals across multi-step tool/retrieval workflows with local error correction | High action + comparative + bounded regulatory | Bounded **functional control** under external goal regime (`AIGOAL01` level 1–2) | Partial **Subjecthood Threshold `S5`** (functional only); creates a shared/delegated human responsibility network |
| **`A2` · Autonomous Self-Reconfiguring Operational Agency** | Forms subgoals, revises prompts/strategies/memory/toolchains, inhibits defaults, tracks uncertainty over long horizons | High action + comparative + regulatory + **generative reconfiguration** | Strong **functional control** + candidate **generative initiative** (`AIGOAL01` level 3); bearing/reflexive standing typically `NOT ESTABLISHED` | **`[R3-04-O5 / AG-2 OPEN]`**: whether self-modifying `A2` without `Bearer (P+E)` counts as genuine SRT agency remains open; greatly raises human governance & repair duties; **Subjecthood Threshold `S6`** remains `NOT ESTABLISHED` |
| **`A3` · Responsibility-Bearing Agency (High-Threshold Future Candidate)** | Independently establishes Stage 2 bearing + **Subjecthood Threshold `S4–S6`** (norm access, `theta_acc`, anti-scalar-collapse interruption, accountable repair) | Full multi-dimensional profile coupled with non-outsourceable consequence closure | All five dimensions independently evidenced (including **bearing initiative**, **reflexive initiative**, and **`O2-A` accountability**) | Candidate **Subjecthood Threshold `S6`** (`R3-07-O6` `OPEN`; `O2-M = HOLD`); even at `A3`, human/institutional co-responsibility remains distributed |

## 2. Detailed Operational Profiles (`A0–A3`)

### 2.1 `A0` — Tool Execution
- **Examples**: Autocomplete, single-turn Q&A, search ranking, summarization, classification, single constrained tool call (typically **AI 赌注谱系 `S0/S1`**).
- **Guardrail**: High comparative or linguistic fluency at `A0` is **not** robust agency, yet an `A0` system deployed at platform scale can still operate as a high-impact **AI 三型 · 地形型** mediator reshaping collective `M(t)` and `σ_{sr}^{coll}`.

### 2.2 `A1` — Delegated Operational Agency
- **Examples**: Coding agent resolving a ticket, retrieval-and-tool workflow, customer-service workflow, automated triage.
- **Required audit questions**: Who defined the goal? Who set the constraints and proxy metrics? Who can interrupt or inspect the trace (`AIREASON01`)? Who pays hidden friction (`Ψ_f` proxy / externalized cost) when it fails?
- **Guardrail**: Delegated agency creates a **human–institutional responsibility network**, never an automatic AI culprit.

### 2.3 `A2` — Autonomous Self-Reconfiguring Operational Agency (`[R3-04-O5 / AG-2 OPEN]`)
- **Examples**: Long-horizon autonomous agents with persistent memory (**AI 赌注谱系 `S3`**), multi-tool orchestration, self-modifying prompts/skills/workflows, subgoal generation, and uncertainty-aware deferral.
- **Core Theoretical Tension (`R3-04-O5 / AG-2 OPEN`)**: An `A2` loop can genuinely **rewrite its own future selection conditions** (memory, tools, strategies, comparative frames — exhibiting functional control and candidate **generative initiative**) while still lacking Stage 2 `Bearer (P+E)` non-outsourceable prospective exposure `E`. SRT explicitly refuses both shortcuts:
  - Do **not** dismiss `A2`'s condition-rewriting dynamics as mere inert `A0` scripting;
  - Do **not** prematurely promote `A2` into `Bearer (P+E)` or **Subjecthood Threshold `S6`** culpability.
- **Governance rule**: Operational autonomy **increases**—never decreases—human design, containment, `ACT` circuit-breaker (`SRT_AI_Architecture.md §V`), appeal, and repair responsibilities.

### 2.4 `A3` — Responsibility-Bearing Agency (High-Threshold Future Candidate)
- **Required independent evidence package**: Declared bearer unit + Stage 2 `Bearer (P+E)` + **Subjecthood Threshold `S4`** subjecthood + **Subjecthood Threshold `S5`** robust anti-scalar-collapse agency + **Subjecthood Threshold `S6`** norm access, `theta_acc` selectable alternatives, consequence understanding, and repair/answerability pathway (`R3-07-O6` `OPEN`).
- **Guardrail**: `A3` is a high-threshold open horizon, not a default property of current AI systems.

## 3. AI as Socio-Technical Mediator & Human–AI Co-Generation Audit (Stage 3.4)

### 3.1 **AI 三型社会技术中介维度**（Synchronized with `Core_Law/SRT_Collective_Selection.md §8`）

Every deployment audit must classify the AI's **orthogonal socio-technical mediation role** (which may overlap and shift across contexts and time windows) and trace its effect on human freedom (`T3-03 C / Q26`), order, knowledge production, power, and co-generation:

| Mediation Type (**AI 三型**) | Mechanism on Human & Collective Selection | Impact on Freedom, Knowledge & Co-Generation | Typical Governance & Responsibility Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. 脚手架型（Scaffold · 辅助式 `L_2`）** | Enhances human judgment, comparative breadth, counterfactual visibility, and load-bearing capacity while preserving consequence return and exit (`SRT_AI_Architecture §6` empowerment protocol) | Supports **enabling human–AI co-generation** (mutual modification of comparative conditions generating joint capabilities neither side has alone) and **positive freedom** | Verify real transparency (`AIREASON01`), preserved human override, and non-atrophy of core judgment |
| **2. 牢笼型（Cage · 替代式 `L_2`）** | Substitutes frictionless outputs for human comparison and judgment, severing consequence return and inducing capability atrophy & dependency lock-in | Erodes human reselectability, flattens comparative dimensions, converts co-generation into passive extraction | Audit dependency lock-in, hidden skill erosion, missing exit/appeal paths, and deployer/institution liability |
| **3. 地形型（Landscape · 集体 `L_2` 改写）** | Reshapes the shared default environment and foreground–background boundary of multi-agent collectives via the **six power interfaces** (behavior / resource / information / comparison criteria / value reference / norm formation) | **Bidirectional collective effect**: under concentrated control or asymmetric feedback, drives collective **`M(t)`** erosion, **`σ_{sr}^{coll}`** inflation, **`W_social`** distortion, and `Proxy Filtering / Reality Narrowing` (`SRT_AI_03_Consciousness_Framework_CompactCore §4`); conversely, under plural, auditable, open-exit governance, Landscape infrastructure can expand heterogeneous experience, shared knowledge visibility, and cross-community co-generation (even at **AI 赌注谱系 `S0/S1`**) | Mandatory collective-selection audit (`Collective_Selection §8` + SEA five-gate coding `§6.1` eight-role non-laundering); platform/institution/regulator accountability regardless of AI consciousness |

### 3.2 Human–AI Co-Generation Attribution Guardrail

When a human–AI coupled workflow generates genuinely novel joint outputs (scientific hypotheses, proofs, code, policy designs, institutional artifacts), **joint capability does not dissolve individual or institutional accountability**:
- Audit the **eight SEA non-laundering roles** (`Collective_Selection §6.1`): difference generator / registrar / authorizer / executor / beneficiary / cost-bearer / repairer / historical writeback site;
- Never allow an authorizer or beneficiary to launder responsibility into "the model suggested it", and never treat an unverified human click-through ("rubber-stamp human-in-the-loop") as genuine selectable control when `theta_acc` is structurally narrowed.

## 4. Multi-Actor Responsibility Network Map (`R3-07 O2-A`)

For AI-caused or AI-mediated harm, map the full network without inventing a fabricated quantitative blame formula (`R3-07-O6` `OPEN`, `O2-M = HOLD`):

| Actor Node | Core SRT Accountability Question (`O2-A` / `AI 三型`) |
|---|---|
| **Developer** | Did architecture, training data, objective proxies, or missing `ACT` circuit-breakers foreseeably create scalar-collapse, cage-dependency, or landscape-distortion pathways? |
| **Deployer** | Was the system placed where failures export hidden burden (`Ψ_f` proxy) to users/bystanders or where `AI 三型` shifts from Scaffold to Cage/Landscape without oversight? |
| **Platform / Operator** | Do ranking, engagement, or automation incentives systematically push up `σ_{sr}^{coll}` or asymmetrically sever `M(t)`? |
| **Institution** | Are auditing, logging, appeal, interruption, exit, and repair channels structurally real and accessible? |
| **Regulator** | Are high-impact **地形型** and `A2` autonomous deployments bounded by future-selectability, anti-externalization, and repair duties? |
| **User / Human Co-Generator** | Did the user actually have `theta_acc` (alternatives, literacy, time, consequence visibility) rather than token rubber-stamp approval? |
| **Affected Subjects / Bystanders** | Were consequences externalized onto parties lacking voice, recourse, or exit? |
| **AI System** | What is the Stage 1 unit, **AI 赌注谱系 `S0–S4`** tier, and **Subjecthood Threshold `S6`** status? If `S6` is `NOT ESTABLISHED`, do not treat it as primary moral culprit or liability shield. |

### Culpability & Obligation Heuristic Prompts (`Philosophy/SRT_Ethics_PH_SS_Guardrails.md §7`)

*(Heuristic diagnostic prompts only — not arithmetic equations or an `O2-M` closure rule)*:

```text
causal contribution != responsibility != culpability != repair obligation
moral weight      ~ affected future-selectability & non-substitutable stakes
responsibility    ~ agency, knowledge, control, and accessible alternatives (theta_acc)
culpability       ~ framework-relative review under structural blockage & unpayable burden
repair obligation ~ relational standing, capacity to reduce harm, and restore selectability
```

## 5. Failure Modes in AI Responsibility Discourse & Deployment Checklist

| Common Shortcut | Structural Error | SRT Correction |
|---|---|---|
| "The AI decided/said it, so the AI is responsible." | Conflates `A0/A1` output or causal role with **Subjecthood Threshold `S6`** | Trace the human/institutional responsibility network and SEA eight roles |
| "The AI has no consciousness / Stage 2 not established, so no one is responsible." | Uses AI non-subjecthood as a **liability shield** | Apply `R3-07` 4-way boundary: human/institutional `O2-A` accountability for **AI 三型** mediation stays fully intact |
| "The AI modified its own strategy, so it is a moral subject." | Collapses `A2` functional/generative initiative into `Bearer (P+E)` & `S6` | Keep `R3-04-O5 / AG-2` `OPEN`; treat `A2` autonomy as raising human governance duty |
| "The human clicked approve, so only the user is responsible." | Ignores interface pre-pruning, Cage dependency, and `theta_acc` constriction | Audit developer/deployer/platform **AI 三型** design and hidden burden export |
| "It is only a stateless `S0/S1` tool, so it has no collective political impact." | Confuses **AI 赌注谱系 `S0/S1`** with **AI 三型** mediation impact | Stateless models can still be high-impact **地形型** mediators reshaping `M(t) / σ_{sr}^{coll} / W_social` |

### Pre-Deployment Governance Checklist

```text
[ ] Stage 1 unit, AI 赌注谱系 S0-S4 tier, and operational class (A0 / A1 / A2 / A3?) declared?
[ ] AI 三型社会技术中介维度 classified (Scaffold / Cage / Landscape) across target & bystander groups?
[ ] Who defines goals, proxy metrics, and modification boundaries? What can the system change autonomously?
[ ] Are ACT circuit-breakers, human override, exit, appeal, and audit trails structurally real?

```

## 6. Validation Package (`Core/SRT_Validation_Template.md`)

- **Claim**: AI causal contribution and operational autonomy (`A1/A2`) are insufficient for **Subjecthood Threshold `S6`** culpability, while AI non-subjecthood never exempts humans/institutions from accountability for **AI 三型** socio-technical mediation effects on `M(t)`, `σ_{sr}^{coll}`, `W_social`, and accessible reality.
- **Nearby theories**: Legal agency & product liability, distributed/collective responsibility ethics, socio-technical systems governance, principal-agent theory, AI alignment & accountability frameworks.
- **SRT-specific prediction**: Deployments matched on technical autonomy (`A1/A2`) will show markedly different harm, dependency-atrophy, and repair failures depending on their **AI 三型** mediation topology, `M(t)` asymmetry, hidden burden export, and `theta_acc` availability.
- **Failure / narrowing condition**: If technical autonomy level or direct causal proximity alone fully explains harm distribution and accountability failures across Scaffold/Cage/Landscape deployments, narrow SRT's socio-technical mediation framework to a descriptive taxonomy.

## 7. Minimal Bottom Line

> **AI can exercise powerful functional control, comparative breadth, and candidate generative initiative without being a `Bearer (P+E)` or **Subjecthood Threshold `S6`** culprit; conversely, operational autonomy and **AI 三型** socio-technical mediation always expand—never erase—human and institutional responsibility.**
