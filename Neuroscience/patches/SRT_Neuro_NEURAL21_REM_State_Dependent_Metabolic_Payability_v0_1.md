# SRT Neuroscience Patch NEURAL21: REM State-Dependent Metabolic Payability v0.1

> Status: bounded neuroscience bridge.  
> Canonical caution: this patch does not define Psi_f, d-value, dreaming, consciousness, L2, or the neural implementation of G_hat_theta. It adds a state-dependent metabolic interface and preserves the distinction between source findings, SRT mappings, and open hypotheses.

## 0. Source anchor

## 1. Why this matters for SRT

The source places pressure on a neuron-first and supply-equals-usable-energy picture of brain computation. REM sleep shows a state in which:

~~~
BBV increases
+ astrocytic pyruvate increases
+ neuronal ATP decreases
~~~

and the BBV transition begins before the overt REM state. The SRT-relevant point is not that blood vessels “make the decision,” but that the conditions under which internal processing can be selected and sustained are distributed across neurons, astrocytes, vasculature, neuromodulation, and the body.

This gives SRT a bounded implementation bridge:

> Before an internally generated L1-candidate becomes stable, the embodied selection landscape may already be reconfigured at the level of resource allocation and support topology.

This is a P3 bridge claim, not a P0/P1 statement about the origin of consciousness.

## 3. Main SRT bridge claim

### Claim NEURAL21

For an embodied neural system, the implementation-level selection context should be represented as a composite state:

\[
\theta_{\mathrm{emb}}
=
(\theta_{\mathrm{neural}},
\theta_{\mathrm{astro}},
\theta_{\mathrm{vascular}},
\theta_{\mathrm{neuromod}},
\theta_{\mathrm{body}})
\]

The candidate transition can be written:

\[
L_0^{\mathrm{accessible}}(L_2,\theta_{\mathrm{emb}})
\xrightarrow{\hat G_{\theta_{\mathrm{emb}}}}
L_1^{\mathrm{REM\text{-}associated}}
\xrightarrow{\mathrm{plasticity/replay}}
\Delta L_2
\]

The final arrow is a hypothesis about a possible sleep-maintenance route, not a result established by this paper.

The metabolic readout should remain a projection:

\[
\Psi_f^{\mathrm{met\text{-}proxy}}
=
\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{met}}
(
\Delta BBV,\Delta pyruvate,\Delta ATP,
\mathrm{recovery},\mathrm{state}
)
\]

with:

\[
\Psi_f^{\mathrm{met\text{-}proxy}}
\neq
\Psi_f
\]

The source therefore strengthens the SRT rule that metabolic variables constrain payability but do not define the canonical burden.

## 4. New claim cluster

### NEURAL21a — pre-REM resource preconfiguration

A state transition can be preceded by a distributed change in resource-allocation dynamics. In SRT terms, the selection landscape may be prepared before a candidate L1 is overtly stabilized.

### NEURAL21b — supply, substrate, and payment are distinct

BBV, astrocytic pyruvate, and neuronal ATP are not interchangeable. SRT-facing experiments should separately model:

~~~
available supply
!= intracellular substrate
!= immediately usable energy
!= paid selection burden
!= recovery capacity
~~~

### NEURAL21c — dream-related L1 and L2 must be separated

REM-associated internal activity may be used as a candidate window for temporary L1 formation and later L2 maintenance, but the study does not directly measure dream content, replay, or stable writeback.

### NEURAL21d — non-neuronal support topology is implementation-relevant

Astrocytes and vasculature are not automatically selectors or subjects. They are candidate components of the embodied support topology through which an organism's selection operator is realized.

### NEURAL21e — energetic cost is not concern

A large ATP change may indicate a payment or recovery burden, but it does not establish that the processed content had high d-value, non-substitutability, or consequence return.

## 5. Experimental and operational consequences

### H-NEURAL21a: causal separation of preparation and transition

In mice, manipulate vascular tone, astrocyte-to-neuron substrate transfer, or neuronal mitochondrial production in separate windows while measuring:

- REM transition probability and latency;
- BBV, pyruvate, and ATP separately;
- hippocampal-cortical replay markers;
- post-sleep memory selection and reversal.

A result in which metabolic manipulation changes transition or replay without simply scaling total neural activity would sharpen the state-dependent payability bridge.

### H-NEURAL21b: human dream-report coupling

Where technically feasible, combine high-density EEG with arterial-spin-labeling or related hemodynamic measures and a phosphorus-MRS energy readout, using awakenings for dream reports. Separate:

- dream vividness;
- emotional or stake-related content;
- external-memory incorporation;
- next-day memory persistence;
- subjective fatigue.

The SRT-specific prediction is that post-sleep writeback and future-selection change should not be reducible to dream vividness or ATP/BBV magnitude alone.

### H-NEURAL21c: matched metabolic load, different concern

Compare REM episodes or internally generated sleep states with similar metabolic signatures but different emotional stake and later memory consequences. If d-gated concern predicts writeback or reselectability better than metabolic load, the SRT bridge gains a discriminating window.

## 6. Boundary cautions

1. REM is a physiological state, not a direct measure of dream phenomenology.
2. “Dreaming drains energy” is a public framing; the source study did not test human fatigue.
3. The paper's three ATP mechanisms remain unresolved.
4. Temporal precedence of BBV does not by itself establish vascular causation.
5. BBV, pyruvate, ATP, blood flow, oxygen consumption, and total energy expenditure must not be collapsed.
6. Psi_f remains the canonical payability burden; metabolic measures are conditional projections or proxies.
7. High energy demand, REM activity, or memory replay does not establish canonical d-value, consciousness-as-subjecthood, or L2 replacement.
8. The primary data are from male mice, with limited individual-level replication for several episode analyses.
9. Ordinary neurovascular, astrocytic, mitochondrial, and plasticity models remain live competitors. SRT must provide differential predictions rather than merely rename their variables.

## 8. One-paragraph abstract

NEURAL21 records a state-dependent metabolic bridge from a peer-reviewed mouse study of REM sleep. The study found that cortical blood volume and astrocytic pyruvate rise while neuronal ATP falls, with vascular preparation beginning before overt REM. SRT reads this as evidence that the embodied selection context includes neural, glial, vascular, neuromodulatory, and bodily support topology, while preserving strict proxy boundaries around Psi_f, d-value, dream phenomenology, and L2 writeback. The patch proposes separate measurements for supply, substrate, immediate neuronal energy, payment, and recovery, and identifies causal and human multimodal experiments that could distinguish metabolic load from concern-weighted writeback and future-selection change.
