## 1. Why this matters for SRT

Bowler et al. provide a particularly strong pressure case because they combine:

```text
training-history manipulation
+
RNN internal-structure analysis
+
model-side structural intervention
+
mouse behavioral translation
+
MEC population recording
```

The resulting mechanism family already pays:

```text
structured history
-> persistent recurrent organization
-> characteristic higher-order dynamics
-> changed later strategy accessibility / learning trajectory
-> transfer / generalization differences
```

This is close enough to several weak readings of SRT history / sedimentation language that it must be treated as an **absorber**, not as easy supporting evidence.

The main correction is:

```text
history changes future accessibility
!= SRT-specific residual

history forms a low-dimensional scaffold
!= SRT-specific residual

support + inhibition appears in a learned network
!= SRT-specific residual
```

Any SRT claim that survives only by renaming these established dynamics has failed the owner-side subtraction burden.

## 2. Source-paid structure: from local coupling to higher-order scaffold

The paper forces a useful hierarchy.

### 2.1 Local support / inhibition is too weak

Both shaped and unshaped RNNs exhibit a broad nearby-excitation / more-distant-inhibition pattern.

Therefore:

```text
local mutual support + relative inhibition
```

is not sufficient to identify the successful historical organization.

The discriminating organization is higher-order:

```text
recurrent weight structure
-> dominant eigenspectrum
-> low-dimensional dynamical motif
-> attractor / trajectory organization
-> later strategy
```

This matters directly to the current Cycle-2 pressure because a vague appeal to a center-surround-like pattern can no longer carry the theoretical load.

### 2.2 Present structure can mediate historical efficacy

The RNN eigenvalue intervention supports a model-side causal reading:

```text
history H
-> present organization S
-> future behavior F
```

while also showing that part of `S` can be imposed without reproducing the original `H` and still bias `F` in the shaped direction.

Safe consequence:

> Historical efficacy may be mediated by current structural organization; the source does not support an irreducible provenance claim once the relevant current structure is given.

This independently reinforces the repository's earlier refusal to treat history-localization provenance by itself as a Level-2 discriminator.

## 3. Cycle-2 absorption pressure

### 3.1 What Bowler already pays

A generic recurrent implementation can already realize:

```text
history organization
-> support / inhibition-weighted recurrent topology
-> persistent dynamical scaffold
-> changed future transition geometry
-> flexible or rigid later strategy
```

It can therefore absorb weak formulations such as:

```text
"history creates a center"
"history changes what is later reachable"
"support + suppression creates a stable pattern"
"a latent background constrains current response"
"multiple dynamical dimensions create a vertical structure"
```

None of those formulations by itself distinguishes the strengthened Cycle-2 commitment from ordinary learning dynamics.

### 3.2 What Bowler does not pay

The source begins downstream of the most difficult Cycle-2 question.

The RNN already has:

```text
identifiable units
system boundary
inputs / outputs
task semantics / target
learning rule
```

and the mouse is already an individuated organism.

Thus the source does **not** explain:

```text
pre-completed-unit differentiation
-> first persistent Selection-position / One formation
```

nor does it establish that the SRT event-level foreground/background relation is ontologically prior to the units whose recurrent dynamics are measured.

### 3.3 Consequence for the strengthened claim

The current strengthened Cycle-2 claim is scoped to:

```text
selection-position
sedimentation layer
structural necessity relative to current L0 commitments
```

NEURAL36 does not adjudicate that claim. It adds a pressure condition:

> If the strengthened claim can be fully instantiated as ordinary history-shaped recurrent topology, latent-state gating or attractor geometry over already individuated units, then the strengthened wording has not yet escaped generic-dynamics absorption.

The surviving burden remains to show what the sedimented foreground/background relation contributes **before or in the genesis of a persistent Selection-position**, or to expose a prospective implementation-level difference that a strong recurrent-dynamics rival cannot preserve.

## 4. Foreground/background mapping: use only as a bounded analogy

Bowler makes it useful to preserve two different kinds of "background":

| Source-side structure | Bounded SRT analogy | Guard |
|---|---|---|
| more-distant inhibitory coupling | suppressed competitor | does not equal SRT relative backgrounding |
| latent trial-phase / temporal scaffold | unobtrusive enabling background | not a suppressed alternative; not an SRT ontology claim |
| cue-dependent response dimension | currently action-relevant foreground | not equivalent to manifest actuality |
| partly orthogonal combination of scaffold and cue dimensions | contextual gating of what can become response-potent | multidimensional gating alone is already paid by generic dynamics |

This table is deliberately asymmetric with the source. It is an SRT comparison aid, not a claim that Bowler's PCA / eigenspectrum reveals SRT foreground/background ontology.

The author-source guard already present in Cycle 2 remains important:

```text
suppressed competing alternative
!=
unobtrusive enabling background
```

Bowler gives a concrete implementation reason not to collapse them.

## 5. Healthy / lethal `L2`: what the paper hardens and what it does not

### 5.1 Strong negative controls supplied by the source

The paper supports several non-sufficiency statements:

```text
stable organization
!= generatively healthy organization

locally successful learning history
!= future-flexible history

low-dimensional scaffold
!= generative health

generalization to novel probes
!= consequence-sensitive self-revision
```

Poor shaping is especially useful because a locally adequate subtask can form a strategy that later becomes difficult to refine.

### 5.2 Why Fig. 7 does not complete generative reselectability

The novel probes are intentionally unrewarded so that they reveal the current strategy without training a new one.

Therefore Fig. 7 asks:

```text
existing scaffold + novel input
-> what output / trajectory follows?
```

It does **not** ask:

```text
existing scaffold + new consequence
-> can the system revise its own comparison rules / gates / candidate-generation conditions?
```

The current `Core/SRT_OPEN_TENSIONS.md` owner requires the latter kind of consequence-sensitive revisability for generative health.

Hence:

> Bowler supplies a strong **pre-operational scaffold and false-positive control** for generative reselectability, but not a completed positive criterion.

## 7. Prospective P4 consequence — not a result of Bowler et al.

If the repository later reopens a bounded generative-reselectability test, Bowler suggests a cleaner contrast than transfer/generalization alone.

### 7.1 Minimal design shape

Create systems / cohorts with different histories but match the present endpoint as tightly as feasible on:

```text
current task performance
current reward contingency
current task exposure
current observable strategy/readout
current low-dimensional state summary where feasible
```

Then introduce a condition in which the old scaffold is systematically inadequate:

```text
rule reversal
or
context recombination
or
structured perturbation followed by recovery
```

and allow consequence feedback to return.

Measure prospectively:

```text
latency to escape the old strategy / attractor
change in candidate-entry / switching accessibility
formation of a new context or gating partition
hysteresis after reversal
recovery / relapse after perturbation
whether the system changes only policy weights or reorganizes its comparison structure
```

### 7.2 Required comparison

This is not an SRT-only experiment by default. A future D-track attempt must compare against a strong recurrent-learning / meta-learning / contextual-gating rival with matched information and fitting budgets.

If ordinary recurrent adaptation preserves every declared endpoint, no SRT discriminator has been earned.

This section extends existing NEURAL25 logic; it does not authorize a new experiment file in this PR.

## 11. One-paragraph abstract

Bowler et al. provide a strong external absorption test for SRT history and sedimentation language. Their RNN, mouse-behavior and MEC results show that structured experience can be mediated by persistent recurrent organization, higher-order eigenspectrum / attractor scaffolds and altered future strategy accessibility; importantly, broad local excitation-plus-inhibition appears in both successful and unsuccessful networks, so that motif alone is not the discriminating structure. The source therefore pays a large downstream chain using ordinary recurrent dynamics and pressures any Cycle-2 wording that reduces "vertical" sedimentation to multidimensional gating or history-shaped attractor geometry. At the same time, the study begins with already individuated units / organisms and does not address pre-One genesis. Its unrewarded novel probes test generalization of an acquired scaffold rather than consequence-sensitive revision of the scaffold itself, so the paper hardens but does not satisfy SRT's generative-reselectability criterion. NEURAL36 retains only this reverse constraint and future-test refinement; no new ontology, symbol, level or D-track claim is introduced.
