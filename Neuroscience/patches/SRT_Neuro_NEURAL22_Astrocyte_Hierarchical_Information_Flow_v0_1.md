# SRT Neuroscience Patch NEURAL22: Astrocyte Hierarchical Information Flow v0.1

> **Status**: bounded neuroscience implementation bridge.  
> **Canonical caution**: this patch does not define `L_2`, `G_hat_theta`, `Psi_f`, `d`, `T_dir`, consciousness, or the origin of selectability. It adds a candidate intra-astrocytic support topology to the existing intercellular astrocyte-network bridge.

## 0. Source anchor

- Oswaldo Pérez, James Schummers, and Mónica López-Hidalgo. “Astrocyte heterogeneity and hierarchical information flow: reframing glial computation.” *Frontiers in Cellular Neuroscience* 20 (2026): 1843853. DOI: `10.3389/fncel.2026.1843853`.
- SourceCard: `Materials/2026/SRC_2026_08_04_Neuro_Perez_Astrocyte_Hierarchical_Information_Flow.md`.

## 1. Why this matters for SRT

Current N12 treats gap-junction-coupled astrocyte networks as candidate non-neuronal support topology. The present source adds a missing internal scale: within one astrocyte, distal processes, intermediary nodes, the soma, and later-recruited domains can occupy different temporal-centrality roles. The resulting bridge is:

```text
intercellular glial topology
rests on
intra-astrocytic weighted topology
```

A neural state can be locally detected without necessarily being admitted to whole-cell recruitment, sustained by the relevant support architecture, or redistributed toward the domains needed for the next transition.

## 3. Main bridge claim

### Claim NEURAL22

Astrocyte support topology should be modeled as nested across at least two biological scales:

\[
L_{2,\mathrm{glia}}^{\mathrm{bridge}}(t)
=
\mathcal G\!\left(
H_{\mathrm{intra}}(t),
H_{\mathrm{inter}}(t),
M(t),
C_{\mathrm{circuit}}(t),
\mathcal H(t)
\right),
\]

where `H_intra` is inferred subcellular topology, `H_inter` is intercellular astrocyte coupling, `M` is local organelle/ionic/metabolic support, `C_circuit` is neuronal and vascular context, and `H` is developmental, learning, aging, and disease history. Every term is a bridge coordinate; the expression does not redefine canonical `L_2`.

```text
local detection
!= cross-domain admission
!= whole-cell recruitment
!= intercellular support
!= neural stabilization
!= behavioral reselection
```

## 4. Dynamic-center correction

The source pressures biological readings that assume one fixed center performs selection. The soma is not consistently the earliest initiator and can act as a hub or later receiving domain, while some cells exhibit peripheral local hubs.

```text
multiple local candidate processes
-> topology-dependent competition and cooperation
-> dynamic hub formation
-> thresholded cross-scale recruitment
-> selective redistribution
```

This is compatible with a composite implementation of `G_hat_theta`, but does not locate the full operator in astrocytes or in any single node.

## 5. Relation to N12

N12 provides the intercellular layer; NEURAL22 adds the intracellular layer:

```text
microdomain detection
-> single-astrocyte integration
-> astrocyte-network coupling
-> neuronal and vascular support
-> altered future transition reachability
```

This extends:

```text
can represent != can sustain != can transition
```

into:

```text
can be detected locally
!= can recruit the cell
!= can recruit the glial network
!= can sustain the neural trajectory
!= can support reselection
```

## 6. Relation to `Psi_f`, `d`, and `T_dir`

Candidate physiological variables include mitochondrial placement, calcium handling, ionic buffering, microdomain synchrony, hub centrality, whole-cell activation threshold, and recovery dynamics. They may support a bounded `Psi_f` proxy window, but are not canonical `Psi_f`.

The source does not measure stake-coupled consequence sensitivity, irreversible risk, first-person concern, or self-readable selection direction. Therefore:

```text
calcium amplitude != d
hub centrality != d
whole-cell activation != consciousness
graph direction != T_dir
```

## 7. Claim cluster

- **NEURAL22a — nested topology:** subcellular topology can condition whether local activity enters cell-wide and network-wide coordination.
- **NEURAL22b — dynamic centers:** initiation, mediation, and reception can shift among distal domains, peripheral hubs, and soma.
- **NEURAL22c — count is insufficient:** active-domain count alone may miss spatial placement, timing, hub structure, organelle support, and circuit context.
- **NEURAL22d — recruitment is not monotonic improvement:** broader propagation may amplify irrelevant activity; more calcium is not automatically healthier integration.
- **NEURAL22e — pathology may be routing-first:** disease may alter hub placement, modular boundaries, thresholds, amplification, and recovery before mean activity changes.

## 8. Experimental consequences

### H-NEURAL22a: topology-aware prediction

Compare models predicting somatic calcium surges from active microdomain count alone versus count plus spatial distribution, timing, graph centrality, and organelle state. The bridge predicts added explanatory value from the topology-aware model.

### H-NEURAL22b: matched input, different topology

Under comparable neuronal stimulation, compare astrocytes with different centralized/modular organization or experimentally altered hub structure. Outcomes include somatic activation probability, response latency/duration, recovery time, propagation, synaptic plasticity, and behavioral transition measures where available.

### H-NEURAL22c: routing may change before mean activity

In aging, epilepsy, or hyperexcitability models, test whether initiator distribution, modularity, centrality, path latency, or threshold changes precede robust changes in total calcium-event rate.

### H-NEURAL22d: nested models

Compare:

```text
neuronal connectivity
neuronal connectivity + intercellular astrocyte topology
neuronal connectivity + intercellular topology + intra-astrocytic hierarchy
```

The strongest compatible result is incremental prediction of learning, persistence, recovery, fatigue, or transition flexibility beyond neuronal connectivity alone.

### H-NEURAL22e: causal perturbation

Perturb a graph-identified hub, a matched non-hub domain, or somatic integration while controlling activity magnitude. A causal bridge requires differential effects on propagation, stabilization, or downstream function, not temporal centrality alone.

## 9. Boundary cautions

1. `Efferent`, `hub`, and `afferent` are inferred graph roles, not established anatomical identities.
2. Temporal precedence does not prove causal information transfer.
3. The complete proposed redistribution cycle was not observed in the analyzed window.
4. The study uses anesthetized ferret visual cortex under brief visual stimulation.
5. The cited ~23% threshold is from separate work and is not universal.
6. Centralized and modular architectures are both observed; neither is globally healthy or pathological by definition.
7. Calcium dynamics do not define computation, consciousness, stake, `d`, `T_dir`, `Psi_f`, or `L_2`.
8. Astrocytes are not promoted as the hidden seat of mind.
9. Disease applications require direct causal and behavioral validation.
10. Ordinary physiology, network dynamics, and graph models remain primary competitors; SRT must add differential predictions rather than relabel them.

## 11. Abstract

NEURAL22 adds intra-astrocytic hierarchy to SRT's existing astrocyte-network bridge. The bounded implication is that non-neuronal transition support is nested: local microdomains must be routed and admitted within a cell before they can contribute to intercellular glial coordination and neural stabilization. The patch is a P3 implementation bridge and P4 experimental window, not a definition of `L_2`, `G_hat_theta`, `Psi_f`, `d`, `T_dir`, or consciousness.
