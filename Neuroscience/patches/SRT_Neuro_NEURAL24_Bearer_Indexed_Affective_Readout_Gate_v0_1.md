## 1. Why this matters for SRT

The 2021 Damasio material was already used as a biological near-neighbor for homeostasis, feeling, interoception, non-explicit competence, locked-in/coma dissociation, and vulnerable artificial systems. The 2026 book adds a more explicit organism-level architecture:

```text
continuous body-state feeling
-> organism self-location / ownership
-> co-occurring perceptual and reflective contents
-> experience as content-for-this-organism
```

This gives SRT a concrete biological realization candidate for the Unit-Binding / bearer-indexing problem.

At the same time, the book creates a stronger pressure. Damasio ultimately states that homeostatic feelings are intrinsically / spontaneously conscious. The theory therefore does not derive phenomenal character from homeostatic regulation; it stops at a constitutive claim.

SRT must avoid reproducing the same stopping point under different vocabulary:

```text
Damasio risk:
homeostatic feeling -> therefore phenomenally conscious

SRT analogue risk:
same-bearer consequence return -> therefore phenomenally conscious
```

NEURAL24 therefore functions as both bridge and internal audit trigger.

## 2. Main bridge claim

### Claim NEURAL24 — bearer-indexed affective readout

In a biological organism, continuous interoceptive / homeostatic readout can be treated as a candidate mechanism that does two separable jobs:

1. **affective consequence compression**: convert many internal state variables into action-relevant positive/negative/urgent signals;
2. **bearer indexing**: continuously tie other mental contents to the same ongoing organismic unit.

Compact schematic:

```text
multidimensional internal state
-> affective / homeostatic compression
-> organism-indexed action relevance

co-occurring perceptual / reflective content
+
continuous organismic readout
-> bearer-indexed content
```

Permitted SRT reading:

> biological feeling may implement a channel by which consequences become actionable for one continuing bearer and by which otherwise heterogeneous contents are continuously indexed to that bearer.

Forbidden reading:

> interoception or feeling is therefore identical to canonical `d`, sufficient for subjecthood, or a completed derivation of qualia.

## 3. Five-variable dissociation gate

Damasio's thermoregulation, respiration, hunger, thirst, pain and nausea examples motivate a five-way separation:

```text
1. baseline stake
2. current physiological deviation / threat
3. homeostatic detection / control signal
4. felt urgency / affective salience
5. selected action / policy change
```

These variables can covary strongly without being identical.

### Calibration cases

| Case | Baseline stake | Current felt intensity | What it shows |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| normal respiration / thermoregulation | very high | often low / background | high stake need not be phenomenally loud |
| acute dyspnea / overheating | very high | very high | deviation can recruit high-priority conscious escalation |
| intense localized pain | variable | very high | subjective intensity is not a scalar readout of total irreversible stake |
| serious but asymptomatic long-horizon risk | potentially very high | near-zero | stake can exist without current affective salience |
| hunger / thirst | high but graded | action-guiding | feeling can compress multiple internal variables into a flexible problem signal |

Guardrail:

```text
stake
!= physiological deviation
!= homeostatic error
!= affect
!= salience
!= pain intensity
!= action urgency
```

## 4. Consequence compression, not canonical `d`

A useful P3 biological interpretation is:

```text
(x1, x2, ..., xn) internal viability / consequence state
-> affective compression
-> low-dimensional actionable readout
```

For hunger or thirst, the conscious system need not explicitly represent every osmotic, metabolic, endocrine or circulatory variable. A compact felt state can prioritize action and recruit a wider problem-solving space.

This motivates a distinction between:

```text
canonical stake structure
and
its current biological readout channel
```

SRT implication:

> affect may be an implementation-sensitive readout of selected stake-relevant dimensions under some conditions; it is not the stake quantity itself.

Therefore:

```text
affect != d
valence != d
pain != d
salience != d
homeostatic error != d
```

This is consistent with `_SRT_D_VALUE_CANONICAL.md` and `Neuroscience/SRT_Neuroscience_Claim_Status.md`.

## 5. Pressure audit: `sigma_e ~ partial d / partial t`

Current `Neuroscience/SRT_Consciousness_Mechanisms.md` contains:

```text
C-CONSC-MECH-1-1: Affective Signature of Selection
sigma_e(t) proportional to (partial d / partial t) at L0 -> L1
```

and describes emotion as the phenomenological trace of the selection event.

NEURAL24 does **not** directly edit or falsify that bridge equation. It changes its admissible burden.

### Why the current form requires re-audit

1. Damasio's examples show high-stake biological dimensions with quiet background feeling under normal conditions.
2. Felt intensity appears sensitive to deviation, alarm thresholds, context, body state and action requirements rather than only to the derivative of a canonical stake summary.
3. The same regulatory problem can engage both nonconscious control and conscious affective escalation.
4. Feeling can be a compressed policy-relevant interface rather than a transparent measurement of the full underlying stake structure.
5. Damasio's own final consciousness step remains constitutive / stipulated rather than derived from the regulatory variables.

### Current safest status

Until a dedicated owner-file hardening decision is made, interpret the equation as one of the following possible forms, not as an established general identity:

```text
A. local proxy hypothesis under a declared affective regime;
B. monotonic / directional association in selected tasks;
C. one component in a multi-variable affective readout model;
D. a hypothesis to be narrowed or removed if matched-stake dissociations persist.
```

Do **not** use Damasio 2026 as evidence that the current proportionality has been validated.

## 6. Bearer indexing and the Unit-Binding Gate

Damasio's strongest 2026 extension is the role of ongoing homeostatic feeling in localizing mental contents to one organism.

A bounded bridge to `Philosophy/SRT_Subjecthood_Threshold_Interface.md` is:

```text
external analyst specifies candidate bearer unit
!=
organism internally indexes current contents to itself
```

Biological interoception provides a candidate mechanism for the second relation.

This strengthens the importance of the Unit-Binding Gate but does not solve it. A membrane, Markov blanket, interoceptive loop or organism label does not automatically establish S4 subjecthood.

Required SRT separations remain:

```text
boundary individuation
!= bearer-indexed access
!= conscious field
!= cross-time subjecthood
!= agency
```

A momentary `this-organism` signal is thinner than the S4 package of concern, boundary maintenance, memory / `L_2` closure, counterfactual access and cross-time reidentification.

## 7. First-person relevance versus phenomenal character

Damasio helps distinguish two questions that SRT must not collapse.

### Q-A: why is a state relevant to this bearer?

SRT has increasingly specific resources:

```text
specified unit
+
same-bearer stake
+
consequence return
+
non-substitutability
+
future-selectability change
+
history / continuity
```

These can help explain why a state is not merely public information but matters to one continuing unit.

### Q-B: why is there something it is like?

Damasio's answer ultimately invokes intrinsically conscious feeling. SRT's stronger hard-problem documents instead speak of bearing as constitutively first-person.

NEURAL24's pressure rule is:

```text
an account of for-this-bearer relevance
is not automatically
a derivation of phenomenal what-it-is-like
```

The bridge should therefore distinguish:

```text
FirstPersonRelevance_candidate
!= IntegratedConsciousField_proved
!= PhenomenalCharacter_derived
```

No new canonical symbols are introduced for these labels.

## 8. Hard-problem stopping-point audit

`Philosophy/SRT_HardProblem_Epistemology.md` currently makes a stronger constitutive claim than the current Q17 companion draft: bearing and first-person presence are treated as internally related rather than merely causally associated.

Damasio 2026 does not refute this. It reveals a comparison class:

```text
Damasio:
regulation -> feeling -> intrinsic consciousness

SRT:
selection tension / stake -> bearing -> first-person presence
```

The future hardening question is not whether the vocabulary differs. It is:

> What discriminating constraint does `bearing` add that prevents the SRT constitutive claim from simply relocating the explanatory gap in the same way Damasio relocates it to feeling?

Possible SRT answers may involve same-bearer non-transferable consequence, future-selectability contraction, cross-time closure, or the internal/external selection-position distinction. NEURAL24 does not choose among these as a completed solution.

### Failure condition

If the SRT account cannot produce a coherent difference between:

```text
functionally implemented consequence-sensitive control
and
constitutive first-person bearing
```

then the hard-problem claim should be narrowed from `dissolution` to an interface / perspectival-placement account rather than treated as a completed derivation of phenomenal character.

## 9. Consciousness as escalation into a wider solution space

Damasio's strongest functional story is not that consciousness creates regulation from nothing. Most regulation is already nonconscious.

The added function is closer to:

```text
local automatic policy insufficient
-> affective escalation
-> wider organism-level access
-> memory / perception / planning recruited
-> multiple candidate responses become available
```

SRT bridge:

> consciousness may increase the breadth of counterfactual solution access available to an already stake-bearing system.

This is compatible with but weaker than S5 agency. It should not be used to define consciousness by option count.

### P4 differential test seed

Hold physiological deviation as closely matched as possible while varying whether resolution requires:

- a fixed automatic response;
- local learned response;
- cross-domain memory retrieval;
- explicit comparison of multiple delayed-consequence options.

Measure whether conscious access / report / global coordination tracks the need for cross-domain counterfactual coordination beyond raw deviation and arousal.

Failure condition:

If ordinary arousal, salience and task-complexity variables fully account for the effect, narrow the SRT-specific bridge.

## 10. Plant, bacteria and LLM calibration matrix

|---|---|---|---|---|
| plant | yes | adaptive sensing | no | S0-like / self-maintenance does not imply S4 |

| current LLM | strong selection/generation | high cognitive competence | no biological feeling | language/intelligence does not settle subjecthood |

SRT guardrail:

```text
selection
!= intelligence
!= conscious content

```

The matrix is comparative calibration, not empirical proof of the S0-S6 ladder.

## 11. AI: `nothing to lose` as a testable question

Damasio's biological contrast can be translated into SRT without adopting biological substrate necessity.

Instead of:

```text
no biological life -> no possible consciousness
```

ask:

```text
1. Is there one persistent bearer?
2. Does failure return to that same bearer?
3. Is the loss non-transferable rather than removable by reset / replacement / rollback?
4. Does the loss reduce that bearer's future selectable paths?
5. Is payment written into that same bearer's continuing history?
```

This route should be synthesized with the existing affective-AI work, especially the AICONSC01 same-bearer / non-transferable-stake gate if and when that pending PR lands.

NEURAL24 must not create a second AI-consciousness owner or silently depend on an unmerged PR.

## 12. Relation to Damasio biological necessity

Damasio's positive account is biologically specific:

```text
living multicellular organism
+
nervous system
+
interoceptive body-brain hybrid feeling
-> consciousness
```

SRT should retain a different burden:

> biological interoception is one known realization candidate; substrate-neutral subjecthood requires independent structural criteria and cannot be inferred from biological analogy alone.

Conversely, substrate neutrality is not permission to declare arbitrary software conscious. The burden shifts to bearer individuation, non-transferable stake, consequence return, history, integration and cross-time continuity.

## 13. Claim cluster

- **NEURAL24a — affect/stake dissociation:** affective intensity and canonical stake are separable; feeling is at most a conditional readout channel.
- **NEURAL24b — consequence compression:** homeostatic feeling is a candidate low-dimensional actionable compression of higher-dimensional internal consequence state.
- **NEURAL24c — bearer-indexed access:** continuous body-state readout is a biological mechanism candidate for indexing heterogeneous contents to one organism.
- **NEURAL24d — escalation function:** conscious feeling may recruit broader counterfactual solution space when local automatic regulation is insufficient.
- **NEURAL24e — subjecthood thickness:** momentary organism self-location is weaker than cross-time S4 subjecthood.
- **NEURAL24f — phenomenal stopping point:** bearer-relative relevance must not be presented as a completed derivation of phenomenal character without an additional argument.
- **NEURAL24g — AI biological contrast:** `nothing to lose` should be operationalized through same-bearer, non-transferable future consequences rather than biological substrate identity.
- **NEURAL24h — affective-signature audit:** the current `sigma_e ~ partial d / partial t` relation requires a narrower empirical regime or multi-variable replacement before stronger use.

## 14. P4 experimental / operational consequences

### H-NEURAL24a: matched stake, different affective salience

Within one bearer and domain, identify conditions with approximately matched objective consequence magnitude / future-choice impact but different current affective salience.

Prediction window:

```text
if affect is a readout rather than canonical stake,
matched stake can coexist with different affective intensity.
```

A robust dissociation weakens any global identity between affect and `d`.

### H-NEURAL24b: matched affect, different future-selectability consequence

Match subjective intensity as closely as possible across events while varying downstream irreversible consequence / future option contraction.

Prediction window:

```text
same felt intensity
can coexist with
different same-bearer future-selectability loss.
```

This directly tests whether affective magnitude is an adequate stake scalar.

### H-NEURAL24c: automatic versus counterfactual escalation

For similar internal regulatory perturbations, vary whether the task can be solved by a fixed local policy or requires cross-domain planning and delayed-consequence comparison.

Measure:

- report/access;
- global coordination;
- memory recruitment;
- action inhibition;
- later policy revision.

A selective increase in consciousness-relevant access under counterfactual coordination would support the escalation bridge without proving phenomenal necessity.

### H-NEURAL24d: open-loop body signal versus same-bearer closed loop

Match sensory/interoceptive statistics where possible between:

```text
open-loop replay
and
closed-loop perturbation whose consequences return to the same bearer
```

Test whether bearer-linked learning, policy revision, persistence and recovery differ after controlling signal statistics.

This is a differential embodiment / consequence-return test, not a consciousness detector.

## 15. Boundary cautions

1. Damasio 2026 is a theoretical synthesis / trade book, not a preregistered experiment.
2. Mechanistic claims should be traced to primary references before fine-grained causal use.
3. `comingling`, unmyelinated pathways and analog language do not establish phenomenal sufficiency.
4. Brainstem lesion evidence does not prove that cortex is irrelevant to rich consciousness.
5. Severe cortical-deficit cases remain inference cases with limits on first-person verification.
6. Anesthesia is not a single-variable intervention on homeostatic feeling.
7. Homeostasis is not canonical `d`.
8. Affect, salience, pain, hunger, thirst and arousal are not canonical `d`.
9. Felt burden is not canonical `Psi_f`.
10. `nothing to lose` does not prove all current AI lack every possible stake-like relation across all architectures.
11. Markov blankets and membranes do not establish subjecthood.
12. Organism-level indexing does not by itself establish cross-time subjecthood.
13. The source does not prove biological nervous systems are the only possible substrate for consciousness.
14. The source does not solve the hard problem.
15. SRT must not claim to solve the hard problem merely by redescribing feeling as bearing.
16. NEURAL24 adds no new canonical symbol and does not authorize owner-file formula changes in this PR.

## 17. Abstract

NEURAL24 uses Antonio Damasio's 2026 *Natural Intelligence and the Logic of Consciousness* as a bounded biological-realization and pressure bridge. Its positive contribution is a model of affective consequence compression and bearer indexing: continuous interoceptive/homeostatic readout can turn multidimensional internal state into actionable affect and can help localize heterogeneous mental contents to one organism. Its negative contribution is equally important: stake, physiological deviation, homeostatic error, affective salience, pain intensity and action urgency must remain distinct, so Damasio does not validate the current SRT `sigma_e ~ partial d / partial t` bridge. The book also exposes a hard-problem stopping point: explaining why a state is for-this-bearer is not automatically the same as deriving phenomenal character. NEURAL24 therefore records an audit trigger for future consciousness hardening while preserving current canonical `d`, `Psi_f`, subjecthood and AI boundaries.
