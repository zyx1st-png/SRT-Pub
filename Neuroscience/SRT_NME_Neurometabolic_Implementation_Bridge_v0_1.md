# NME × SRT: Neurometabolic Recursive Implementation Bridge v0.1

> **Role**: A concrete, bounded P3 implementation candidate and P4 discrimination plan, not a replacement neuroscience framework or canonical definition. The purpose is to repair a missing *physical mechanism chain* in SRT's neural bridge: physiological regulation changes which neural candidates remain available, stable and reusable.
>
> **Source status**: Deacon, Garcia-Valdecasas & Jacob (2026), *The medium is the message: How neurometabolic entanglement generates conscious experience*, arXiv:2610.30217v1, **theoretical preprint** (sections 2–7; especially figures 1–5). The article integrates existing physiological findings and proposes NME; it does **not** experimentally demonstrate that NME generates phenomenality.
>
> **Programme boundary**: B-class, noncanonical material bridge. It does not reopen the paused Neuroscience deep-well programme, mark a pending NEURAL hook as landed, update any P0/P1 owner, or conclude HP-B. Follow the active Neuroscience Reconstruction Framework and future Consciousness owner for any stronger promotion.

## 0. Exact explanatory target and source/evidence separation

**Target**: In an already formed, embodied neural system, how can local information processing modify the physiological support of *subsequent* processing, thereby changing candidate accessibility, competitive selection, persistence, report/action availability and downstream history?

This is a downstream neural realization problem. It does not derive primitive Selection, first One formation, a Bearer, or phenomenal subjecthood from metabolism.

Keep four assertions separate:

| Grade | Content | Status / source |
|---|---|---|
| Physiological background | Neural firing, neuromodulation, blood flow, substrate availability and plasticity interact across timescales | Established broad neuroscience families; individual directions and estimates are study-specific |
| NME proposal | Cortex → forebrain value assessment → midbrain/brainstem regulatory nuclei → altered forebrain support forms a recursively entangled, partly compressive loop | Authors' theoretical interpretation, not demonstrated necessity or sufficiency for consciousness |
| SRT translation | Such a loop is one possible mechanism configuring the embodied neural selection context and biasing stabilization / later historical efficacy | New P3 **bridge hypothesis**, not canonical identity |
| Test | Explicit matched comparisons, independent neural/vascular/metabolic measurements, perturbations and non-SRT rivals | P4 prospective design; no results claimed here |

The source uses a **Möbius-like analogy**. It neither demonstrates a mathematically nonorientable neural topology nor makes a single three-synapse route exhaustive of conscious activity. NME's proposed bottleneck integrates physiological regulation; **physiological coordination ≠ content binding ≠ phenomenal unity ≠ one experiencer**.

## 1. What concrete mechanism is missing from the existing SRT neural account?

The inherited mechanisms describe candidate competition, normalization, ignition/global availability, concern-weighted stabilization and plastic writeback. NEURAL21 already extends the support topology to astrocytes, vasculature, neuromodulation and body state. Nevertheless, an explicit **causal passage between these levels** is not yet consolidated:

1. Which anatomical paths let an existing cortical pattern alter its own *future* physiological feasibility?
2. Which resource and neuromodulatory variables can change the neural gain/threshold/stability landscape independently of input identity?
3. Which effects are fast neural modulation versus slower vascular/metabolic responses, and how do mismatched timescales matter?
4. Which history persists as short-lived metabolic context, and which alterations create longer-lived, testable future-choice constraints?
5. Which measured patterns would discriminate this bridge from ordinary global-arousal, recurrent-network, neurovascular and history-dependent baseline models?

This document supplies an **implementation hypothesis**, not a new SRT essence. An information-processing account remains a live rival even when physiology is causally indispensable.

## 2. Causal anatomy and candidate multi-loop chain

NME sections 4–5 / figures 3–4 propose a useful coarse path:

    Distributed cortical ensembles
      → basal/limbic forebrain valuation (examples: nucleus accumbens, amygdala, hypothalamus)
      → midbrain / brainstem regulation (examples: locus coeruleus, raphe nuclei, substantia nigra)
      → broad ascending neuromodulatory influence
      → cortical excitability, synaptic gain/plasticity and regionally varying vascular support
      → new cortical competition, task access and action
      ↻ next evaluation / physiological support cycle

Body-to-brain endocrine, visceral and circulating signals can influence the regulator nuclei; local neurovascular coupling and glial metabolism coexist with global ascending modulation. This is a **network of interacting loops**, not a mandatory one-way three-station pipeline.

| Site / role | NME-grounded mechanism candidate | What to measure | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cortical populations | Pattern propagation, anticipatory local activity, recurrent stabilization | E/MEG, LFP/spikes where available, task decoding, perturbation response | Decoding ≠ access or consciousness |
| Forebrain value assessment | Positive/negative/neutral relevance influences regulator state | Task-specific behavior, physiological/region-level proxies; direct human nucleus measures only where feasible | Valence ≠ canonical d-value or irreversible stake |
| LC–norepinephrine axis | Widespread gain changes; noradrenaline–pericyte vascular modulation is one studied route | Pupil + neural readouts; regional CBF/CBV; relevant pharmacological or animal interventions | Pupil is not LC firing; vasoactive and direct neural effects must be separated |
| Raphe serotonergic / nigral dopaminergic systems | Broad regulation of responsiveness, plasticity and physiological conditions | Suitable neural, receptor/neuromodulation and vascular readouts | Transmitters carry task/state signals too; not only metabolic messages |
| Local vascular / astrocytic support | State-dependent blood delivery, substrate handling, neuronal energetic capacity | CBF, CBV, CMRO2 estimates, astrocytic substrate, neuronal ATP in suitable models | Supply ≠ delivery ≠ usable ATP ≠ expenditure |
| Organism / history | Interoception, bodily regulation, arousal, later learning and action consequences | Autonomic measures, body state, longitudinal performance and retention | Organism-level consequence ≠ component-level Bearer or experiential unity |

**Evidence handles**, not new NME experiments: Korte et al. (2023) on LC noradrenaline and pericytes; Sun et al. (2026) on LC–neuron–pericyte vascular dynamics; Jacob et al. (2023) on cognition/metabolism; sources cited in the NME preprint. The NEURAL21 REM mouse material separately motivates glial/vascular/substrate distinction, not an NME proof.

### 2.1 Three interacting timescale families

- **Fast** (typically milliseconds to subsecond): spikes, circuit-level competition, some neuromodulatory changes, short-lived access and motor decisions.
- **Intermediate** (subsecond to seconds or longer, mechanism dependent): distributed neuromodulation, vascular responses and restoration of physiological support; conventional hemodynamic responses often lag neural activity by seconds.
- **Slow** (minutes to days and beyond): recovery, plasticity, consolidation, learned policy/baseline changes and durable changes in later availability.

No unique clock period is asserted for a conscious percept or a completed “Möbius cycle.” A slow vascular signal cannot by itself explain millisecond content discrimination. Conversely a rapidly decodable neural content may depend on a historically prepared slower physiological background.

## 3. Minimal model: physics-facing states, not primitive SRT quantities

For a declared organism, task, temporal window and measurement boundary, let:

- x(t) = multiregional neural population state (content/competition);
- v(t) = forebrain value-assessment state;
- u(t) = ascending neuromodulatory regulatory state;
- r(t) = vascular / metabolic support state, represented as **a vector**, not a scalar “energy budget”;
- a(t) = astrocytic/substrate state where measured;
- b(t) = interoceptive and systemic bodily state;
- h(t) = retained neural / physiological / behavioral history with explicit horizon;
- s(t) = exogenous sensory and task inputs.

One **candidate state-space factorization** is:

    v(t + Δv) = V[x(t), b(t), h(t)]
    u(t + Δu) = U[v(t), b(t), u(t)]
    r(t + Δr) = R[r(t), x(t), u(t), a(t), b(t)]
    x(t + Δx) = F[x(t), s(t), u(t), r(t), h(t)]
    h(t + Δh) = H[h(t), x(t), u(t), r(t), action(t), consequences(t)]

Here Δv, Δu, Δr, Δx and Δh **need not coincide**. The factorization is a falsifiable model family, not a discovered neural law or claim that all effects are routed through u. Local x → r and u → x paths are permitted alongside u → r → x.

For a declared neural access task, the candidate gate/readout can be operationalized as:

    p(access at t+τ | x, u, r, h, s, task)
      = sigmoid(β0 + βx X + βu U + βr R + βxr X×R + βh H + ...)

This is a *statistical access/readout model*, not a definition of SRT's Gate, not the primitive selection operator, and not a phenomenality detector. The joint-term hypothesis βxr ≠ 0 tests whether current support conditions modify the effect of measured neural evidence.

For an empirical metabolic projection, report a vector-valued readout:

    m_proxy(t) = (CBF, CBV, calibrated/uncertain CMRO2, substrate, ATP, recovery kinetics, state)

Any conditional Ψ_f-related proxy must declare a mapping Π_met(m_proxy, task, time window, error model), **without equating Ψ_f with oxygen consumption, BOLD, ATP, effort or FEP surprise**. Same for d-value: modeled organism-relative stakes are not dopamine, valence, salience or high arousal.

### 3.1 Candidate implementation chain (SRT interface only)

    Established L2/history and formed physiological context
      → accessibility / eligibility / local normalization under embodied conditions
      → candidate stabilization, re-entry, global availability (observable outputs)
      → action, consequences and measured subsequent restructuring
      → later feasibility / reselectability change

This chain can constrain a model-level realization of Gate / θ_emb. It **does not** establish L0 itself, a universal L0→L1 neural reduction or the necessity of neurometabolic entanglement for first-person experience. Treat L0/L1/L2 here as the existing SRT analytic aspects, not three anatomically localized compartments.

## 4. What NME actually adds; where SRT has a distinct candidate

| Why unconscious neural activity remains possible | Neural processing usually continues outside awareness | Local absorption without higher-order load-bearing foregrounding (HP-B candidate) | a positive phenomenal gate theorem |

The strongest **HP-B interface** is the proposed background-to-foreground reorganization *of the same independently established Bearer*. NME-like physiological context can alter which differences are feasible or salient at a formed Gate; **it does not identify the shared modulator with phenomenal compression**. HP-B BP-Q1 remains a noncanonical P3 bridge posit and the experiencer transition remains open.

## 5. Four distinct kinds of unity and four different measurements

1. **Physiological common-mode regulation**: shared changes in regional CBF/CBV/neuromodulatory markers.
2. **Functional integration**: cross-region effective interaction, task routing, perturbation propagation, binding behavior.
3. **Phenomenal co-presentation**: independently investigated content unity, including behavioral and report/no-report limitations.
4. **Subject ownership / individuation**: attributed first-person bearer, not supplied by a common blood supply or a selected ROI set.

These are not monotonically equivalent. A low-dimensional resource bottleneck may organize the *conditions* for differentiated content without encoding that content. Split-brain and dissociated-network cases are pressure tests, not pre-decided refutations of either account.

## 6. Matched-control discrimination cards (prospective; no completed test)

All cards must name a task horizon, level of analysis, participant/organism bearer, measured object, intervention target, adequate controls and a simpler baseline. No single negative proxy is a consciousness absence verdict.

### H-NME-1 — Physiological support beyond a neural-only predictor

- **Contrast**: comparable stimuli and measured neural evidence, differing independently estimated local support state or a feasible targeted support intervention.
- **Readouts**: held-out access/error, neural response thresholds, regional CBF/CBV, uncertain CMRO2, recovery timing.
- **Rivals**: neural recurrent activity alone; global arousal/gain alone; direct neuromodulator→neuron change without metabolic mediation.
- **Support**: r- or x×r-related terms improve held-out prediction and, ideally, intervention effects have a support-mediated component not absorbed by x/u/arousal.
- **Weakening**: only neural/gain/arousal predictors matter or vascular changes are noncausal correlates. Failure of a single vascular proxy does not refute all physiological effects.
- **Scope**: validates a conditional implementation effect, **not** phenomenality.

### H-NME-2 — Shared metabolic regulator versus content binding

- **Contrast**: shared neuromodulatory/vascular regulation matched while cortical interregional communication or content integration differs; reciprocal contrast if feasible.
- **Readouts**: regulator/common-mode physiology, content binding behavior, neural effective connectivity, access/report where justified.
- **Rivals**: cortical/thalamo-cortical integration with regulator supplying arousal but not the binding operation.
- **Support**: specific physiological–binding mediation beyond generic arousal, with a declared spatial/temporal route.
- **Weakening**: shared physiology persists while binding dissociates, with content unity predicted wholly by cortical coupling.
- **Scope**: even a supported effect does not count experiencers.

### H-SRT-NME-3 — Matched metabolic burden, different consequence/stake structure

- **Contrast**: matched task inputs, neural complexity, arousal, reward/salience and m_proxy as closely as feasible; independently vary ethical participant-relevant commitment or delayed consequence structure.
- **Readouts**: later choice revision, memory transformation, retention/recovery and future-option use; prospective d-related *proxy* is estimated independently.
- **Rivals**: NME value/valence regulation, ordinary reinforcement learning, uncertainty, motivation and memory consolidation.
- **Support for an SRT increment**: prospectively specified consequence-return/history terms improve held-out post-task selectability prediction after the above rival terms.
- **Weakening**: all benefit is absorbed by NME valence, standard reinforcement or memory variables; no independently measured residue.
- **Scope**: historical/concern-linked discriminant only, **not** a d-value measure or consciousness admission.

### H-SRT-NME-4 — Present-state matching, distinct effective history

- **Contrast**: matched observable neural/vascular/metabolic state, different verified prior trajectories; define tolerance, omitted-state confounds and same-unit criterion before analysis.
- **Readouts**: next-transition eligibility, response to controlled perturbation, later stable choice / memory change.
- **Rivals**: latent unmeasured neural state, ordinary metaplasticity, predictive control and trial-selection bias.
- **Support**: distinct history improves out-of-sample next-state prediction and survives stronger latent-state baselines.
- **Weakening**: history term disappears with adequate latent-state control or fails genuine held-out replication.
- **Special constraint**: Neuroscience Framework first-well Case B / DATA-ACCESS-0 NO-GO remains in force; **not authorized as a reopened deep well**.

### H-NME-5 — Metabolic work, mental effort and experienced intensity are separable

- **Contrast**: distinguish metabolic work change, perceived effort, arousal, stimulus novelty and phenomenal clarity under suitable matching.
- **Readouts**: effort ratings and, separately, conscious content measures; neural/vascular/metabolic vectors with uncertainty.
- **Rivals**: generic task difficulty, prediction error, arousal, response demand.
- **Support**: specified regulatory work provides an independent, calibrated effect for a stated experiential dimension.
- **Weakening**: strong unconscious metabolic work or high-vividness/low-work cases violate an asserted universal monotonic map; narrow the claim to a condition.
- **Scope**: no cost-to-qualia identity.

### H-NME-6 — Internally driven reorganization versus feeling of agency

- **Contrast**: motor/predictive initiation, external perturbation, feedback congruence and outcome attribution varied without conflating them.
- **Readouts**: actual control performance, subjective authorship, timing, neural modulation and vascular lag.
- **Rivals**: comparator/forward-model accounts and postdictive inference.
- **Support**: neurometabolic directionality explains pre-registered additional variance over prediction/attribution baselines.
- **Weakening**: apparent internal/external direction has no independent effect after standard agency predictors.
- **Scope**: sense of agency ≠ controlled transition ≠ responsibility.

## 7. Measurement and identification contract

- **Declare the measurement object**: neuron, ensemble, ROI, network, vascular territory, participant, or organism. Their boundaries and histories do not coincide automatically.
- **Separate biological quantities**: CBF, CBV, BOLD, inferred CMRO2, glucose delivery, astrocytic pyruvate, neuronal ATP and total energy expenditure must not collapse into one scalar. The NEURAL16 BOLD–CMRO2 uncertainty gate applies: sign claims require uncertainty/indeterminate-class reporting.
- **Direct and mediated paths**: neuromodulators can alter excitability/plasticity directly and blood flow indirectly. If possible estimate both; do not assign all effects to vascular mediation.
- **Lags and feedback**: fit plausible multi-lag / dynamical models; account for hemodynamic delay, autocorrelation, common drivers and temporal aggregation.
- **Do not condition on a post-treatment collider** when “matching neural activity” in an intervention. Declare estimand, pre/post windows, intervention target and assumptions.
- **Control sources of spurious unity**: global arousal, respiration, cardiac effects, pupil, head motion, task difficulty, vascular reactivity, sedation/drug effects and reporting demands.
- **Cross-validation**: train/held-out partitions by participant and session when possible; add ablations (neural-only, NME physiological, full SRT+NME) with identical targets.
- **Source/falsifiability**: single paper figures are not new experiments; preregister decisive contrasts, data-access gate, missing-data and measurement-error assumptions before any P4 causal or phenomenality claim.

## 8. Rival models and possible outcomes

| Model | Predictors allowed | What it could already explain |
|---|---|---|
| R0 neural-only recurrent / global-workspace | stimulus, neural signals, neural connectivity, recurrent access | local content, access, many binding/report effects |
| R1 generic arousal and value | R0 + arousal, neuromodulator, motivation/reward/valence | salience, gain, response readiness without special metabolic mediation |
| R2 NME-physiology | R1 + independent support vectors, vascular/glial dynamics, lagged interaction | support-limited availability, neural-metabolic recursive regulation |
| R3 ordinary historical predictive/control | R2 + memory, latent-state, standard learning/reinforcement history | next-state and long-term behavior without SRT constructs |
| R4 SRT-facing bounded bridge | R3 + independently operationalized same-unit consequence return, concern and future-selectability discriminants | **only if** prospectively adds non-redundant held-out prediction |

A likely valid outcome is **physiological causal support with no incremental SRT prediction**. That strengthens NME-like neuroscience and requires SRT to treat this case as translation-only. Conversely an incremental future-selectability term does not demonstrate original SRT ontology, phenomenality or an exclusive neural mechanism.

## 10. Near-term deliverables (without starting a new deep well)

1. **Model card**: declare one suitable public / collaborative dataset, state/participant unit, sampling rates, neural + physiology availability, task labels, lag horizon and missingness. Data access must be checked first.
2. **Frozen comparison**: R0/R1/R2/R3/R4 on the same outcome and held-out fold; preregister minimal detectable improvement rather than selecting a post hoc significance threshold.
3. **Causal mechanism check**: where ethical and feasible, perturb neuromodulation, vascular regulation or metabolic substrate separately while collecting neural and behavioral outcomes.
4. **Phenomenal-boundary check**: record what task measures access/report, what measures content binding, what is merely common physiology, and what is *not* measurable as first-person ownership.
5. **Revision trigger**: positive, negative or ambiguous outcome → update only the affected mechanism claim / proxy validity, without silently changing SRT canonical definitions.

### Questions intentionally left OPEN

- Does an NME-like loop have a necessary, sufficient or neither relation to consciousness?
- Which physiological modulation is constitutive versus permissive, and at which temporal scale?
- Is the proposed metabolic “bottleneck” a binding mechanism, a gain/arousal coordinator, or both?
- Which formed organism/network is the historical and consequence-bearing unit across distributed loops?
- When, if ever, does a formed Gate/Bearer become an experiencer? HP-B remains a candidate, not an answer established here.
- Can SRT independently outperform NME-plus-standard learning, or is it a conceptual interpretation in this domain?
