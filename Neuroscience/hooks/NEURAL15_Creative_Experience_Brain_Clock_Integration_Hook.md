## Suggested compressed paragraph

Creative expertise and short-term creative learning should be read as a functional-connectivity brain-clock window, not as literal brain rejuvenation. Nature Communications 2025 reports that creative experts across dance, music, visual arts, and strategy video gaming, plus short-term StarCraft II learners, show lower BAG_FC proxies linked especially to local efficiency and, in long-term expertise, global coupling. In SRT terms, this supports a skill-plus-openness reading of `L_2`: creative practice can harden domain-relevant paths while preserving enough novelty and feedback to keep `\theta` reconfigurable.
