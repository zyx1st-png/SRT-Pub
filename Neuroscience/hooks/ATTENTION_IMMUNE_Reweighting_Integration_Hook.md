## 4. Suggested paragraph

> Human histamine skin-prick experiments provide a direct example in which voluntary attentional allocation changes a downstream bodily trajectory: directing attention toward inflammation-related sensations reduced wheal and flare magnitude relative to distraction, and this replicated after visual input and task structure were matched. Sensory attenuation weakened late resolution without abolishing the effect, while similar reported sensation could coexist with different wheal outcomes. This supports an implementation bridge from attentional reweighting to physiological control, but it does not identify attention with stake, paid selection cost or phenomenality, and HRV remains an indirect autonomic proxy rather than proof of a vagal mediator.
