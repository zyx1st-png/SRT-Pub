## 3. Suggested native neuroscience paragraph

> An object representation can remain stable without remaining static. Human medial-temporal-lobe recordings show that an established concept can be recovered across altered presentations, indirect discourse references and task-relevant relations. This suggests a useful implementation-level criterion of stability: re-identifiability. What persists need not be an unchanged copy held continuously in activity; history can preserve routes by which an identity is found again and recruited into current binding or maintenance.

## 4. Suggested native book paragraph

> 我们说对象化把流动“切成一个东西”，很容易让人误以为脑中从此保存着一个静止副本。可真正重要的稳定性也许不是静止，而是**还能回来**：角度变了、名字换了、只剩一句“他”，我们仍能重新找到“同一个人”。对象之所以成为对象，不只因为它曾被切出边界，也因为变化之后仍保留了一条重新进入这个身份的路。稳定不是停止变化，而是在变化中仍可被重新识别。
