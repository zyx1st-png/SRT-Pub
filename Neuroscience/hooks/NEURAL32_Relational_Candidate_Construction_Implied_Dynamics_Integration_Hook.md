## 3. Suggested native neuroscience paragraph

> High-level visual selection should not be pictured as choosing among bare category labels. In natural-scene body perception, EBA and FBA responses are shaped by interpretable features including implied human motion, number of people and body size, and the implied-motion contribution is strongly conditioned by whether the moving entity is a human body rather than an animal or vehicle. These results support a candidate-construction stage in which lower-level visual information is organized into relational, action-relevant representations before later competition and stabilization determine current perceptual commitment. In this sense, “selection before representation” means selection before stabilized `L1` representation, not before every precursor encoding or provisional representation.
