## 3. Suggested native paragraph

> Astrocyte support topology is nested rather than purely brain-wide. Within one astrocyte, distal microdomains, intermediary hubs, the soma, and later-recruited domains can occupy different temporal and centrality roles, and the organization may be relatively centralized or modular. This suggests that local neural activity must pass through a subcellular support-admission architecture before it can recruit whole-cell and intercellular glial coordination. The finding does not locate a unitary selector in the soma; it instead supports a dynamic-center model in which initiation, mediation, and redistribution depend on current topology, threshold, organelle support, circuit context, and history.
