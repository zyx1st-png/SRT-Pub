## 3. Suggested native paragraph

> Historical policy takeover and the amount of scripted behavior should be measured separately. A system may execute strongly without clear evidence that current outcome evaluation has been replaced, or may adopt an outcome-insensitive policy while producing only modest output. The stronger SRT diagnostic is whether consequences can reopen the policy and restore reselection, not repetition count alone.

## 4. Suggested table

| Dimension | Operational question | Candidate measures | SRT caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| strategy conversion | Has control become relatively insensitive to current outcome or motivation? | outcome devaluation, contingency degradation | operational automaticity is not proof of no real choice |
| execution gain | How much behavior is produced after policy activation? | frequency, duration, vigor, persistence | high output is not habit depth by itself |
| reselection capacity | Can the policy be reopened after a rule or consequence change? | reversal latency, exploration, alternative recovery | closest local test of support versus replacement |

## 5. Do not include

- outcome insensitivity as proof that real choice is absent;
- a claim that the study establishes a human OCD mechanism;
- a unitary habit-strength scalar that recombines the source's dissociation;
- detailed pathway timing or effect-size claims before full-method close-read.
