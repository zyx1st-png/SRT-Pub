## Suggested compressed paragraph

Propofol loss of responsiveness should not be modeled as a simple disappearance of cortical traveling waves. Abstract-level human microelectrode evidence suggests that propofol can increase propagation speed while shifting wave direction, spectral structure, firing activity, and spike-wave relationships. In SRT terms, this refines `D_align` and `C_wave`: ignition can fail because the routing field is retuned away from task-relevant `L0 -> L1` anchoring, not only because total power or coherence falls.
