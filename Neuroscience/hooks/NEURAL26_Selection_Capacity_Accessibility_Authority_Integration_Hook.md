# NEURAL26 Integration Hook — Dynamical capacity, accessibility, authority, expression, write-back

## Trigger

Reopen when any of the following workstreams becomes active:

1. `Neuroscience/SRT_Neural_Mechanisms_CompactCore.md` is revised around representation / competition / network-state architecture;
2. `Neuroscience/SRT_Neuro_Predictions_Table.md` adds anesthesia, perturbation, candidate-accessibility, or strategy-control tests;
3. `Core/SRT_Core_24_Discriminating_Predictions.md` develops a neural version of local stability vs global openness / hysteresis;
4. `SRT_NEURAL25_EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL_v0_1.md` is extended to manipulate global network regime separately from history depth;
5. consciousness work asks whether EEG complexity or connectivity can stand in for consciousness or choice capacity.

## Minimal surviving claim

> Neural selection involves at least two separable scales: a system-level dynamical regime that permits differentiated candidates to form and interact, and candidate-specific mechanisms that determine which accessible candidate obtains effective control.

## Required stack

```text
Representation
!=
Dynamical Capacity
!=
Candidate Accessibility
!=
Control Authority
!=
Expression
!=
Historical Write-back
```

The learning-process sequence from NEURAL25 remains valid and is not replaced:

```text
Acquisition -> Availability -> Authority -> Expression -> Write-back
```

NEURAL26 adds an orthogonal global-state layer, not a new canonical ontology layer.

## Proxy guardrail

Do not integrate as:

```text
permutation entropy = selection capacity
gamma connectivity = consciousness
criticality = L0
PFC = selection weight
```

Entropy, connectivity, perturbational complexity, criticality-related measures, network integration, and transition repertoire remain candidate proxy families whose relation to candidate accessibility must be independently demonstrated.

## Primary future experiment

Cross global dynamical regime with candidate-specific historical authority:

```text
global capacity proxy: high vs low
x
old-strategy authority/history: high vs low
```

Measure representation, candidate accessibility, path efficacy, switching cost, and hysteresis separately.

The key differential claim is:

> matched representation need not imply matched accessibility; matched accessibility need not imply matched authority; matched current authority need not erase history-dependent future transition geometry.

## Failure trigger

Downgrade NEURAL26 if global complexity/connectivity measures do not predict candidate accessibility or transition capacity beyond generic arousal, signal power, motor performance, and drug identity, or if a simpler ordinary network-state model predicts held-out data equally well.
