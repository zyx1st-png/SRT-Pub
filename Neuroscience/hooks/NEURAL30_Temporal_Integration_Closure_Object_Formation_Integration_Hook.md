## 3. Suggested native neuroscience paragraph

> A current percept need not mirror the temporal granularity of the events that generated it. In sequential metacontrast, individual offsets retain decodable temporal identity during an extended integration period even when those offsets are not consciously accessible as separate events. The dominant EEG pattern then shifts from an earlier occipital regime to a later centro-parietal regime associated with the integrated percept and report. This supports a bounded implementation distinction between structured pre-perceptual history and the later unitary percept, while leaving the exact timing and phenomenal status of consciousness unresolved.

---

## 4. Suggested NEURAL23 crosswalk paragraph

> Brain-body timing and temporal perceptual integration should be kept as separate interfaces. NEURAL23 asks whether respiratory, cardiac, gastric or neural phase changes a candidate's momentary eligibility before anchoring. NEURAL30 asks how event-specific representations can remain structured over hundreds of milliseconds and then resolve into one integrated percept. The joint prediction is not that a body rhythm constitutes consciousness, but that endogenous phase may alter the weighting or timing of a downstream integration process. The Menétrey et al. study does not itself measure respiration or cardiac phase; this remains a P4 differential test.
