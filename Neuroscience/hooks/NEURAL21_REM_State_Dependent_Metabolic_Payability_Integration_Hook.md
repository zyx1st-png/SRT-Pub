## 3. Suggested native paragraph

> REM sleep provides a useful state-dependent test of embodied selection. In a mouse study, cortical blood volume and astrocytic pyruvate increased while neuronal ATP decreased, and the vascular transition began before overt REM. The result does not identify metabolism with Psi_f, nor does it establish what a mouse dreams. It does show that supply, substrate, immediate neuronal energy, and recovery cannot be collapsed into one variable. SRT can therefore treat neural–glial–vascular dynamics as an implementation-level support topology for selection, while retaining Psi_f as the broader payability burden and reserving dream phenomenology and L2 writeback for separate tests.
