Recommended location: near existing sections on inferred selection friction, projection rules, or experimental proxies.

### Thermodynamic Projection of Psi_f

Thermodynamic irreversibility, entropy production, broken detailed balance, and arrow-of-time asymmetry may serve as domain-specific projections of `Psi_f` when they track the burden of selecting, maintaining, or reconfiguring a manifest `L1` slice under payability constraints.

These quantities are not identical to `Psi_f`. They support an SRT reading only when they help distinguish selection friction from generic task difficulty, raw energy expenditure, prediction error, stress markers, or measurement noise.

Preferred wording: irreversibility / entropy production / broken detailed balance = thermodynamic proxy of selection friction, not literal identity with `Psi_f`.

Boundary: do not write `Psi_f = entropy production`, `Psi_f = irreversibility`, or `Psi_f = broken detailed balance`.

## 3. Insert Block for `_SRT_D_VALUE_CANONICAL.md`

Recommended location: near the anti-panpsychism clause, stake-coupling section, or value / non-substitutability bridge.

### Irreversibility Is Not Stake

Irreversibility alone does not imply subjectivity or `d-value`. A flame, storm, or chemical reaction can be irreversible without becoming conscious in the SRT sense.

Thermodynamic asymmetry becomes consciousness-relevant only when it is coupled to stake, closure, and future selection capacity:

Delta_irr AND d > 0 AND Payable(Psi_f) => selection with concern-bearing structure.

Thus, high irreversibility may indicate strong dynamics, but high `d-value` requires that some directions of those dynamics matter for the system's own future selection capacity.

## 5. Insert Block for `SRT_EXP_MEASURE_MAP.md`

Recommended location: near proxy validation, neuroscience proxies, or `Psi_f` measurement rules.

### Thermodynamic Proxy Rule

Irreversibility, entropy production, broken detailed balance, forward/reverse time-series distinguishability, and turbulence may be used as empirical proxies for selection-like dynamics only when the proxy is tied to a concrete transition, maintenance, or reconfiguration of selection structure.

A thermodynamic proxy supports an SRT interpretation only if it satisfies all three conditions:

1. Selection relevance: the signal tracks directional organization of candidate-to-actual transition, not merely task difficulty or arousal.
2. Payability relevance: the signal bears on whether the system can maintain closure and future selection capacity while paying the transition burden.
3. Stake discrimination: the signal can be separated from generic dynamics by checking whether `d-value` changes or whether consequences return to the system.

Failure condition: if thermodynamic proxies cannot outperform simpler explanations such as arousal, prediction error, motor effort, or raw task difficulty, they should remain weak bridge indicators rather than SRT evidence.

### Core 25 Review-Risk Guardrail: Irreversibility Overreach

Core 25 introduces a useful empirical bridge between SRT and thermodynamic neuroscience, but it creates a review risk if irreversibility is treated as consciousness itself. The correct claim is narrower: thermodynamic asymmetry is a measurable signature of directional selection-like dynamics. Consciousness-relevant use additionally requires `d-value`, payability, anchoring, and loop closure.

Failure condition: if irreversibility, entropy production, or broken detailed balance cannot distinguish stake-bearing selection from generic non-equilibrium dynamics, the Core 25 bridge weakens and should be treated as a neuroscience proxy rather than a core proof.
