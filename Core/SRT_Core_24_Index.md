## 5. Current Canonical Posture

Core 24 is no longer merely an isolated supplement. Its selected claims now function as a **canonical framing and bridge-hardening layer** across several files.

However, Core 24 still should not be cited as a completed theorem package.

Correct current wording:

> Core 24 has been partially integrated into canonical and canonical-adjacent files as a framing, operational, and validation layer. Its strongest thesis is that SRT's explanatory power comes from selection-first floor replacement, while its scientific credibility depends on structural, convergent, and comparative validation.

Avoid:

> Core 24 proves SRT explains everything.

Avoid:

> Core 24 proves purpose, value, morality, and frameworks are fully formalized.

Avoid:

> Core 24 places SRT beyond measurement.

## 6. Future Canonical Promotion Candidates

The following lines are candidates for future higher-level promotion if supported by additional formalization or empirical discrimination.

### Candidate 1 — Floor Replacement

> SRT's explanatory power comes not from explaining everything, but from replacing object-first ontology with a selection-first ontology in which possibilities become manifest and repeated manifestations harden into constraints.

### Candidate 2 — Dynamic Normativity

> Purpose, value, morality, and frameworks are not subjective overlays on reality; they are stable forms of selection dynamics across different layers of constraint.

### Candidate 3 — Non-Reductive Verification

> SRT does not require its core concepts to be measured by a single objective ruler. It requires structural, convergent, and comparative validation through the consequences of selection friction, non-substitutability, and hardening.

### Candidate 4 — L₂ Hardening

> L₂ hardening can be operationally recognized as reduced local selection cost, increased global constraint, and hysteresis under perturbation.
