# SRT Core 24: Thermodynamic Signatures of Selection

> **Purpose**: This file hardens a thermodynamic bridge for SRT by mapping irreversibility, entropy production, arrow-of-time asymmetry, hierarchical information flow, and turbulence onto the SRT selection loop.
>
> **Source interface**: Kringelbach, M. L., Sanz Perl, Y., & Deco, G. (2024). *The Thermodynamics of Mind*. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 28(6), 568–581. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2024.03.009
>
> **Boundary**: This file does **not** claim that thermodynamics is identical to consciousness, nor that irreversibility alone is sufficient for subjectivity. Thermodynamic irreversibility is treated as an empirical and modeling signature of selection in dynamical systems. SRT still requires `d-value`, payability, anchoring, and loop closure for consciousness-relevant claims.

---

## 0. Core Bridge Claim

The Thermodynamics of Mind framework proposes that brain hierarchy can be quantified by the asymmetry of information flow, estimated through irreversibility and the arrow of time. More hierarchical brain states show stronger non-equilibrium structure and stronger temporal asymmetry; flatter states are closer to reversible, symmetric, or weakly hierarchical organization.

SRT translation:

> **Irreversibility is not consciousness itself. It is the dynamical trace left when a system has made a selection that cannot be costlessly undone.**

In SRT terms, a selection event is not merely a state change. It is a directional compression from `L0` into an anchored `L1`, paid through `Ψ_f`, and optionally written back into `L2`.

Thus:

\[
L_0 \xrightarrow{\hat{G}_\theta,\; \Psi_f} L_1 \xrightarrow{writeback} L_2
\]

should generally leave some form of temporal asymmetry in the domain where it is implemented.

## 2. Irreversibility as a Selection Signature

A reversible process can be watched forward or backward without a reliable difference. An irreversible process has a distinguishable direction: the forward and reversed trajectories are not equivalent.

Thermodynamic production entropy can be written as:

\[
H_P = \sum_{i,j} P_{ij}\log\frac{P_{ij}}{P_{ji}}
\]

where `P_ij` is the transition probability from state `i` to state `j`.

SRT does not identify `H_P` with `Ψ_f`. Instead, it treats `H_P` as a possible empirical readout of selection asymmetry in a given domain:

\[
H_P > 0 \Rightarrow \text{time-asymmetric transition structure}
\]

SRT bridge reading:

\[
\Delta_{irr}(X) \sim \operatorname{Distinguish}\left(X_{forward}, X_{reverse}\right)
\]

where `Δ_irr` is a domain-specific irreversibility proxy.

Then, in a neural or cognitive implementation:

\[
\Delta_{irr}^{neural} \uparrow
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\text{stronger directional organization of } L_1 \text{ dynamics}
\]

But the stronger SRT condition is:

\[
\Delta_{irr}^{neural} \uparrow
\; + \;
\Psi_f \text{ payable}
\; + \;
d \text{ stake-coupled}
\Rightarrow
\text{candidate consciousness-relevant selection}
\]

## 3. Relation to `Ψ_f`

`Ψ_f` is the payability burden of selecting, maintaining, and coordinating an actualization. Thermodynamic variables such as irreversibility, entropy production, dissipation, or broken detailed balance can serve as domain-specific projections of this burden.

Correct bridge:

\[
\Delta_{irr},\; H_P,\; \text{broken detailed balance}
\in \mathcal{P}_{thermo}(\Psi_f)
\]

where `𝒫_thermo(Ψ_f)` means: thermodynamic projection or proxy of selection friction under specific modeling assumptions.

Incorrect bridge:

\[
\Psi_f \equiv H_P
\]

or:

\[
\Psi_f \equiv \Delta_{irr}
\]

if read as literal identity.

Hard reading:

> `Ψ_f` asks whether the system can pay for a selection. Thermodynamic irreversibility estimates whether a system's dynamics reveal directional, non-equilibrium organization after such selections have been made.

## 4. Relation to `d-value`

Irreversibility alone is not enough for subjectivity.

A hurricane, a flame, and a chemical reaction can all be irreversible. This does not make them conscious in the SRT sense.

SRT therefore adds the stake condition:

\[
d(x)=\left\|\frac{\partial \mathcal{U}}{\partial \mathcal{S}}\right\|
\]

where `d-value` measures how strongly selection directions are coupled to viability, closure, identity continuity, or future selection capacity.

Thermodynamic asymmetry becomes consciousness-relevant only when it is stake-coupled:

\[
\Delta_{irr} \land d>0 \land \mathrm{Payable}(\Psi_f)
\Rightarrow
\text{selection with concern-bearing structure}
\]

Boundary:

> High irreversibility may indicate strong dynamics; high `d-value` indicates that some dimensions of those dynamics matter for the system's future selection capacity.

## 5. Brain-State Translation

### 5.1 Cognitive task states

Thermodynamics of Mind reports that cognitive tasks tend to show higher irreversibility and stronger hierarchy than resting state.

SRT reading:

> During goal-directed tasks, `Ĝθ` must compress a broader candidate space into action-relevant `L1` trajectories. This increases directional organization and selection burden.

Interpretation:

\[
Task \Rightarrow \Delta_{irr}^{brain}\uparrow,\; hierarchy\uparrow,\; \Psi_f^{task}\uparrow
\]

provided the task genuinely requires maintained selection rather than automatic routine.

### 5.2 Sleep, anesthesia, and coma

Reduced consciousness states often show reduced temporal asymmetry and flatter hierarchy.

SRT reading:

> Conscious access weakens when the system cannot maintain multi-scale directional selection, global availability, and stake-coupled payability.

This supports the SRT distinction between:

- local activity;
- global availability;
- irreversible organization;
- stake-bearing selection.

### 5.3 Movie watching

The article highlights that naturalistic movie watching can produce a flatter hierarchy than both rest and task states.

SRT reading:

> A film provides an external narrative scaffold that performs part of the selection work for the viewer. Instead of internally generating and maintaining a high-friction selection path, the brain can entrain to an externally organized `L1` stream.

Public-facing compression:

> **A movie relaxes the brain not because it has no information, but because it selects the flow of information for us.**

Formal bridge:

\[
Movie \Rightarrow External\;L1\;scaffold\Rightarrow Internal\;\Psi_f\downarrow\Rightarrow hierarchy\;flattening
\]

Boundary:

> Flattening is ambiguous. It can indicate relaxation through external scaffolding, or degradation through loss of internal organization. SRT must distinguish these by checking `d-value`, payability, and future selection capacity.

### 5.4 Neuropsychiatric disorders

Thermodynamic analyses report altered irreversibility in disorders such as ADHD, bipolar disorder, and schizophrenia.

SRT reading:

> Pathology can be modeled as a failure of orchestration: either the system cannot generate sufficient directional hierarchy when needed, cannot relax hierarchy when appropriate, or cannot couple hierarchy to stake and payability in stable ways.

Candidate SRT typology:

| Pattern | Thermodynamic signature | SRT interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Under-orchestration | Low irreversibility / flat hierarchy during demand | Insufficient selection anchoring |
| Over-orchestration | Excessive rigidity / high irreversibility | Overhardened L2 constraint or high switching cost |
| Misorchestration | Regionally heterogeneous asymmetry | Local selection conflicts or unstable cross-scale routing |
| Scaffold dependency | Flattening only under external structure | External L1 stream compensates internal Ψ_f burden |

## 6. Turbulence as Multi-Scale Selection Transfer

The Thermodynamics of Mind framework also uses turbulence to explain how slow neural signals can still support fast, efficient cognition. Turbulence enables energy and information transfer across scales through cascades.

SRT translation:

> `Ĝθ` does not select on a flat plane. It operates across nested scales, where local selections, global constraints, and historical attractors recursively shape one another.

Turbulence can be treated as an implementation-level candidate for fast cross-scale selection transfer:

\[
L_0^{local/global}\xrightarrow{turbulent\;cascade} L_1^{multi-scale}
\]

or more carefully:

\[
Turbulence \in \mathcal{P}_{neuro}(cross\text{-}scale\;\hat{G}_\theta\;implementation)
\]

Boundary:

> Turbulence is a mechanism of efficient transfer, not a sufficient condition for selection ontology or consciousness.

## 7. Whole-Brain Generative Models as L1 Mechanistic Projections

Thermodynamics of Mind uses model-free irreversibility estimates and model-based whole-brain generative models to infer generative effective connectivity.

SRT reading:

| Model component | SRT role |
|---|---|
| Empirical brain time series | Observed `L1` dynamics |
| Forward/reverse distinguishability | `L1` temporal asymmetry proxy |
| Structural connectivity | Embodied constraint substrate |
| Generative effective connectivity | Mechanistic approximation of selection routing |
| Regional drivers/receivers | Candidate implementation of hierarchical selection roles |

Boundary:

> Whole-brain models estimate the mechanism of observed neural actualization. They do not by themselves define `L0`, `L2`, or subjectivity.

## 8. SRT Predictions and Experimental Interfaces

### Prediction 1: Stake-gated irreversibility

If SRT is right, two tasks with similar sensory or motor complexity should differ in consciousness-relevant thermodynamic signatures when they differ in stake.

Expected pattern:

\[
Task_A \approx Task_B \text{ in input complexity}
\]

but:

\[
d_A > d_B \Rightarrow \Delta_{irr,A}^{stake\text{-}regions} > \Delta_{irr,B}^{stake\text{-}regions}
\]

especially in networks associated with interoception, valuation, executive control, and future-oriented action.

### Prediction 2: External scaffold flattening

Externally scaffolded narrative, music, or guided attention should flatten hierarchy when it reduces internal selection burden, but not when the content raises stake or threat.

Expected contrast:

| Condition | SRT expectation |
|---|---|
| Pleasant coherent film | internal `Ψ_f` decreases; hierarchy flattens |
| Threatening personally relevant film | `d-value` rises; selected networks regain asymmetry |
| Random sensory montage | less scaffold benefit; may increase uncertainty/friction |

### Prediction 3: Treatment-specific reconfiguration

Different treatments for depression should not merely increase or decrease global irreversibility. They should produce different paths of hierarchical reconfiguration.

SRT expects:

- SSRIs may gradually reshape `L2` priors and payability thresholds.
- Psychedelics may transiently loosen overhardened `L2` attractors and reopen `L0` candidate space.
- Durable recovery should require renewed `L1 -> L2` writeback, not only acute flattening or entropy change.

### Prediction 4: Consciousness loss is not just low activity

Loss of consciousness should correlate better with loss of stake-coupled directional organization than with raw activity level alone.

SRT expects:

\[
Consciousness\;level \not\equiv Activity\;level
\]

but rather:

\[
Consciousness\;candidate \sim \Delta_{irr}\times d \times \chi_{payable}\times \chi_{global\;availability}
\]

## 9. Non-Claims

This bridge does **not** claim:

1. that thermodynamic irreversibility is consciousness;
2. that entropy production is identical to `Ψ_f`;
3. that turbulence is the Ghost Operator;
4. that flat hierarchy is always good or always bad;
5. that higher irreversibility always means higher consciousness;
6. that brain thermodynamics proves SRT's ontology;
7. that whole-brain models directly access `L0`.

Correct claim level:

> Thermodynamic asymmetry is a strong empirical bridge for measuring the directional organization of selection-like dynamics in neural systems.

## 10. Minimal Formal Closure

Define a thermodynamic selection signature:

\[
\mathcal{T}_{sel}(X)
=
\left(\Delta_{irr}(X),\;H_P(X),\;BDB(X),\;\mathcal{H}_{hier}(X),\;\mathcal{C}_{cascade}(X)\right)
\]

where:

- `Δ_irr` = forward/reverse temporal distinguishability;
- `H_P` = production entropy proxy;
- `BDB` = broken detailed balance proxy;
- `𝓗_hier` = hierarchy / asymmetry of information flow;
- `𝓒_cascade` = multi-scale cascade or turbulence proxy.

SRT consciousness-relevant selection is not `𝒯_sel` alone, but:

\[
\mathcal{SRT}_{conscious\text{-}candidate}(X)
=
\mathcal{T}_{sel}(X)
\cdot d(X)\cdot \chi_{payable}(X)\cdot \chi_{anchor}(X)
\]

This is a bridge formula, not a canonical theorem.

## 11. Short Canonical Paragraph for Reuse

> The Thermodynamics of Mind framework gives SRT an empirical bridge for measuring the directional organization of neural selection. Irreversibility, entropy production, broken detailed balance, arrow-of-time asymmetry, and turbulence are not consciousness itself; they are thermodynamic signatures that a dynamical system is organizing information flow in a non-equilibrium, hierarchical, and temporally asymmetric way. In SRT, these signatures are read as `L1`-level traces of `Ĝθ`-mediated selection from `L0`, paid through `Ψ_f`, and potentially written back into `L2`. The missing condition is `d-value`: irreversible information flow becomes consciousness-relevant only when it is coupled to stake, closure, and future selection capacity.
