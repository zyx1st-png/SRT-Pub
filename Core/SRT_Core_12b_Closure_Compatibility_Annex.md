## 1. Repetition, Closure, and κ-Threshold

Three things must be kept distinct.

### 1.1 Repetition = common path

Repetition is the most common route by which traces accumulate.
But repetition alone is not yet stabilization.

### 1.2 Closure = essence

The essence of stabilization is closure formation.
A path is not stabilized merely because it has occurred many times, but because prior traversal begins to reduce the ontological friction of compatible future traversal.

### 1.3 κ-threshold crossing = criterion

`κ` should be read here as stabilization degree.
To say that `κ` crosses a threshold means that the path has changed mode:
- from foreground event to background structure;
- from local trace to usable scaffold;
- from occurrence to stabilizing constraint.

**Compressed formula**:

> **重复是常见路径，闭包是本质，κ 跨阈值是判据。**

## 2. Minimal Closure and L2-Grade Closure

### 2.1 Minimal closure

Minimal closure is present when the result of a selection begins to **systematically lower `Ψ_f` for compatible subsequent selections**.

This is the minimum definition of closure.
It is stronger than archival trace and weaker than public institution.

**Working definition**:

> **闭包的最小定义：某次选择的结果开始系统性降低兼容后续选择的 Ψ_f。**

### 2.2 L2-grade closure

L2-grade closure is the strengthened case.
The low-friction path must also become:
- inheritable,
- shareable,
- and re-enterable as background scaffold.

A private habit may count as closure without yet counting as L2.
A grammar, ritual, institutional default, or stabilized norm is closer to L2-grade closure.

**Compressed slogan**:

> **闭包 = 后续兼容选择的 Ψ_f 下降；L₂ = 这种下降已被共享并背景化。**

## 3. Compatibility: Working Definition and Deep Constraint

### 3.1 Working definition

Compatibility is defined operationally by absorbability into the current closure.
A path is compatible if its entry can be absorbed by the existing closure without breaking that closure's self-maintenance, and if its entry tends to further lower downstream `Ψ_f` for later compatible traversals.

This means compatibility is not first a matter of surface similarity.
Surface resemblance is only a heuristic.

### 3.2 Deep constraint

Operational compatibility is insufficient by itself.
A deeper ontological constraint is required:
compatible paths must remain congruent with the `ε` direction.

So a path is not deeply compatible if it preserves local closure only by compressing a broader future selectability.

**Fixed short form**:

> **兼容性在操作上由闭包的可吸纳性定义，在本体上受 ε 方向约束。**

---

## 4. Closure is Normatively Neutral

Closure itself is not automatically good.
It tells us that something has taken form.
It does not yet tell us that the formed scaffold is normatively justified.

**Short form**:

> **闭包说明“成形了”，不说明“正当了”。**

Positive normative status appears only when the closure remains under the `ε` direction constraint and does not preserve itself by shrinking a wider future selectability.

---

## 5. Pathological Closure

A closure becomes pathological when it can preserve itself **only by compressing a broader future selectability**.
Compression here is not merely a side-effect.
It is part of the sustaining mechanism of the closure itself.

**Minimum working definition**:

> **病态闭包：一个闭包只能靠压缩更大范围的后续可选择性来维持自身。**

This distinguishes:
- accidental narrowing;
- contingent cost;
- and genuine pathological stabilization.

Only the third case counts as pathological closure in the strict sense.

---

## 6. Lethal L2

Lethal `L_2` is the stronger case.
It is a pathological closure that has already become:
- shareable,
- inheritable,
- backgrounded,
- and scaffold-like.

At that point the pathology is no longer merely local rigidity.
It has become a world-form inherited by later selectors.

So the distinction should be read as:
- **pathological closure** = local or limited pathological stabilization;
- **lethal L2** = pathological stabilization that has become shared background scaffold.

## 7. Reading Rule for `T-L2-Scaffold`

`T-L2-Scaffold` explains why successful stable ISP history can become background scaffold rather than mere archive.
This annex sharpens how to read that theorem:

1. **All real selection can leave trace** — this is the irreversibility floor.
2. **Not all trace becomes closure** — closure requires downstream `Ψ_f` reduction for compatible traversals.
3. **Not all closure becomes L2** — L2 requires shared / inheritable / backgrounded closure.
4. **Not all L2 is healthy** — closure is normatively neutral; pathological closure and lethal L2 remain possible.

This yields a four-step ladder:

$$
\text{trace} \;<\; \text{closure} \;<\; L_2\text{-grade closure} \;<\; \text{healthy or pathological }L_2
$$
