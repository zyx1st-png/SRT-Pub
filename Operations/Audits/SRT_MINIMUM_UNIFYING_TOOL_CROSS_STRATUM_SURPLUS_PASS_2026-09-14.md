# Minimum unifying tool — cross-stratum surplus pass

> **Boundary:** noncanonical machine-side audit. No canonical edit, no Bearer theorem, no social norm, no Level promotion, no scientific-distinctiveness claim, and no HOLD exit is authorized here.
>
> **Target:** determine whether the three-stratum SRT tool yields any nontrivial inference across strata, or whether it is only a disciplined juxtaposition of mature neighboring theories.

---

## 0. Executive verdict

A **real cross-stratum inference candidate** survives strongest-rival pressure, but its status is architectural rather than locally novel.

The strongest candidate is not:

```text
higher-level freedom is good;
lower-level freedom loss is bad;
more options are always healthier;
collective integration is suspicious.
```

Those would be both theoretically crude and empirically false.

The live inference is instead:

> **A gain in effective Selection / coordination at a higher scale cannot be classified as unqualified generative health solely from the higher scale when the gain is produced by reducing the future selectability of lower-scale units that already have independent continuing One/Bearer standing, especially when the burden of the higher-scale Selection is routed to those units without adequate consequence return or generator/scaffold revisability.**

Compactly:

```text
higher-scale effective selectability ↑
DOES NOT ENTAIL
cross-scale generative health ↑

unless the ontology / standing of affected lower-scale units
and the routing of consequence, burden and revisability
are typed explicitly.
```

This is a cross-stratum **non-monotonicity / accounting guard**, not yet a theorem.

Strong rivals collectively pay nearly every local ingredient:

```text
ETI / multilevel selection
-> lower-level independence loss can generate higher-level individuality;

Levin multiscale competency
-> competent parts are nested in competent wholes and higher levels reshape lower action landscapes;

Capability / adaptive-preference traditions
-> nominal options != real opportunities / capabilities;

Active Inference / niche construction
-> policy, learning and environment mutually reshape future action spaces;

externality / principal-agent / distributed-responsibility theory
-> authority, benefit, execution and burden can be non-coincident.
```

What SRT currently contributes is a **single typed dependency architecture** connecting:

```text
Selection event typing
-> One formation
-> Bearer prospective exposure
-> future selectability
-> L2 scaffold / pre-cropping
-> multi-position consequence routing
-> generator revisability.
```

That is enough to justify an **architecture-level surplus candidate**, but not enough to establish historical novelty or whole-package non-substitutability.

## 1. The hard case: higher-level gain financed by lower-level loss

The July 12 author source isolated a genuine cross-scale generation question:

```text
individual / lower-scale independent selectability ↓
-> new collective / higher-scale effective selectability ↑
```

This cannot be treated as pathology by definition.

Major evolutionary transitions in individuality are decisive negative controls:

```text
formerly independent lower-level units
-> cooperation / conflict suppression / division of labour / interdependence
-> lower-level reproductive autonomy declines
-> higher-level unit acquires heritable variation in fitness and becomes a new evolutionary individual.
```

Michod's fitness-reorganization work goes further: cooperation can impose costs on lower-level members while producing benefits / fitness at the higher level.

Therefore SRT must accept:

```text
local autonomy loss
CAN BE constitutive of genuine higher-order formation.
```

Any rule saying `lower-level selectability loss = degeneration` fails immediately.

---

## 2. Levin control: lower-level competency need not disappear

The multiscale competency architecture provides an even stronger control than classical ETI alone.

Levin and collaborators explicitly describe living systems as nested competent layers in distinct problem spaces:

```text
molecular networks
-> cells
-> tissues
-> organs
-> organisms
-> swarms,
```

with each level exhibiting problem-solving competence and higher levels deforming the action / energy landscape of lower levels.

This means SRT cannot claim novelty from:

```text
nested agents / competent units;
multiscale problem spaces;
higher-level goals constraining lower-level dynamics;
lower-level competencies composing higher-level intelligence;
macro agency without passive micro-parts.
```

The hard question must therefore be narrower:

> **when does higher-scale constraint constitute healthy integration of still-competent lower units, and when does it become burden externalization / scaffold substitution / generative lock-in?**

That question is not answered by merely detecting multiscale competency.

## 3. SRT type distinction — component loss vs Bearer loss

The current SRT architecture contains a distinction that must be used before any social or normative judgment.

### Case A — lower-level component / One without Bearer standing

A lower-level formed unit may lose local degrees of freedom through specialization or coordinated closure while contributing to a higher-order One.

The loss is not automatically pathological.

Examples may include:

```text
cell specialization;
constrained actuator roles;
subsystem division of labour;
locally reduced option spaces that support robust macro function.
```

### Case B — lower-level Bearer standing is already present

Current author reconstruction defines Bearer-relevance by:

```text
formed One / Selection-position
+
P prospective self-indexing
+
E same-One prospective exposure / non-outsourcing.
```

Thus a Bearer is not merely a component with causal sensitivity. It is a continuing formed position whose possible futures are internally differentiated by what that same One may undergo, and at least some consequence burden cannot be reassigned while preserving the same Bearer relation.

If a higher-scale scaffold reduces such a Bearer's future selectability, the event must be tracked at **both scales**.

This does not imply:

```text
lower Bearer always has moral priority;
collective claims are invalid;
constraints are oppression;
all sacrifice is pathology.
```

It implies only:

> **the lower-level consequence cannot disappear from the accounting merely because a higher-level functional gain exists.**

This is a type consequence of the architecture, not yet a moral theorem.

## 4. Cross-stratum inference candidate CS-1 — no automatic monotonicity across Bearer boundaries

Machine-only candidate:

```text
CS-1
If H is a candidate higher-scale formed selection unit
and {B_i} are lower-scale continuing Bearer-relevant units,
then an increase in H-level effective selectability / stability / coordination
cannot by itself establish system-wide generative-health improvement
when some B_i experience reduced future selectability or increased non-outsourcable exposure.
```

Why this follows from the architecture:

```text
Bearer typing says the exposure belongs to B_i's continuing relation;
Collective Selection says consequence routing can be asymmetric;
ST-A says stability != generative health;
L2 says scaffold can lower local selection cost while increasing global constraint;
therefore higher-scale success and lower-scale generative health may diverge.
```

### Strong guard

Do **not** rewrite CS-1 as:

```text
no higher-level integration may reduce lower-level options.
```

ETI refutes that simplification.

The burden is **typed divergence**, not prohibition.

## 5. Cross-stratum inference candidate CS-2 — aggregation across Bearer positions is boundary-sensitive

A second consequence follows.

Suppose:

```text
H-level gain = +10
B1 loss = -2
B2 loss = -3
```

SRT currently has no licensed universal scalar saying:

```text
+10 - 2 - 3 = +5 therefore healthy.
```

Why not:

1. `P + E` makes some burden relations non-outsourcable at the Bearer level;
2. `Collective Selection` retains distinct `d_i`, `Psi_f_i` and consequence routes rather than collapsing them into one value;
3. the epsilon / normativity red-team already found that extending concern from self to others bottoms out in a **closure-boundary** problem rather than a derived universal aggregation rule.

Therefore machine-only candidate:

```text
CS-2
Cross-Bearer generative-health aggregation is not automatically commutative or scalarizable.
Any global score requires an explicit boundary / weighting / aggregation commitment.
```

This is methodologically important.

It means:

```text
collective performance ↑
!=
proof that the collective's constituent Bearers are generatively better off;

and

individual burden ↑
!=
proof that collective formation is invalid.
```

Both must remain visible until an explicit boundary / normative rule is supplied.

## 6. Cross-stratum inference candidate CS-3 — visible option expansion can hide generative narrowing

The primitive Selection reading matters here.

Current SRT does not define Selection as picking from a ready-made menu. L2 scaffold can alter the conditions under which later candidates become manifest / reachable before explicit downstream choice.

Therefore:

```text
visible option count ↑
CAN COEXIST WITH
generative candidate-space narrowing ↓
```

Example abstract structure:

```text
platform / institution presents more downstream options;
L2 scaffold increasingly pre-crops what becomes salient / legible / reachable;
user chooses among a larger visible menu;
endogenous candidate-generation / comparison-rule revision declines.
```

This yields:

```text
menu freedom ↑
while
generative reselectability ↓.
```

Capability / adaptive-preference traditions already strongly pressure this distinction, so SRT cannot claim it as isolated novelty.

The SRT-specific architectural gain is that the same distinction is routed from:

```text
Selection != menu picking
through
L2 scaffold formation
into
formed-position generative-health diagnosis.
```

## 7. Cross-stratum inference candidate CS-4 — externalization can stabilize the higher level

Another nontrivial consequence:

```text
externalization is not necessarily destabilizing to the exporting system.
```

Indeed, externalization may **increase** higher-level stability by moving friction / burden away from the selecting structure.

Thus:

```text
higher-level stability ↑
can be causally supported by
lower-level / external Bearer burden ↑.
```

This blocks a common hidden assumption:

```text
if the system is robust, its organization must be healthy.
```

SRT already has the pieces for the contrary diagnosis:

```text
stable L2 scaffold
+
strong asymmetric M(t)
+
weak return path to decision/generator
=
possible stable externalization regime.
```

This is not a new social-science fact. Externality and exploitation theories obviously cover it.

The cross-stratum surplus candidate is the ability to state it in the same typed grammar used for One/Bearer/L2 rather than importing a separate social vocabulary only at the end.

## 9. Deletion test — remove Bearer typing

Keep:

```text

generative reselectability.
```

Delete Bearer P+E typing.

Then the tool can still say:

```text

some higher unit gains options;
some burden is externalized.
```

But it loses a principled internal distinction between:

```text
loss of degrees of freedom in a functional component
vs
loss imposed on a continuing position whose own possible future is prospectively at stake.
```

The difference would have to be re-imported ad hoc from moral personhood, organismhood, legal status, agency or domain convention.

Result:

```text
Bearer typing is load-bearing for the cross-stratum tool,
provided P+E itself survives independent future pressure.
```

This does **not** promote Bearer into the universal ontology spine.

## 12. Strongest current SRT discriminator — not a mechanism, a typed mismatch

After subtraction, the strongest discriminator is not:

```text
SRT has a unique causal mechanism nobody else has.
```

It is:

> **SRT forces a cross-scale analysis to keep distinct the scale at which Selection capacity increases, the formed positions at which non-outsourcable consequences are borne, and the L2 level at which the future candidate landscape is modified.**

This creates a specific mismatch diagnosis:

```text
selection-capacity locus
!=
benefit locus
!=
burden locus
!=
writeback locus
!=
generator-revision locus.
```

A system may be highly successful at one locus while degenerating at another.

This is the best current architecture-level surplus candidate.

## 13. Why this matters for SRT's civilizational ambition

The civilizational use of SRT should not be:

```text
maximize freedom;
maximize number of options;
maximize decentralization;
minimize institutions;
maximize individual autonomy.
```

Those prescriptions are not licensed.

The stronger and more general diagnostic is:

```text
For any emergent order:

1. identify which scale gains Selection / coordination capacity;
2. identify which existing formed positions lose independent selectability;
3. type which of those positions are merely components, Ones, or Bearer-relevant;
4. locate non-outsourcable consequence / burden incidence;
5. locate where those consequences write back;
6. test whether the affected positions can revise / exit / renegotiate the scaffold;
7. distinguish higher-order formation from scaffold substitution / proxy inversion / stable externalization.
```

This can analyze organisms, teams, institutions, AI-mediated systems and civilizations **without asserting that the same moral answer applies at every scale**.

That is a serious gain in tool discipline.
