# Pre-object / foreground observability — Barad strongest-neighbor pass 13

> **Role:** first exact strongest-neighbor pass after the author identified the pre-object / foreground-bounded observation axis as potentially SRT's most core difference. The task is to pressure the axis, not protect it.
>
> **Boundary:** no Level verdict, no canonical edit, no claim that Barad and SRT are identical, and no inference that empirical non-observability proves ontology.

## 0. Claims under test

The re-audit route separated three claims:

```text
PO-1 — foreground-bounded direct observability
A direct observation is already on the manifest / foreground side
of a differentiating Selection relation; background is not co-manifest
as background in that same observation event.

PO-2 — no pregiven complete object inventory
The pre-object field is not ontologically equivalent to a warehouse
of already individuated hidden objects / options / state variables.

PO-3 — no fully equivalent third-person state-space ontology
Even an arbitrarily rich predictive state-space is not ontologically equivalent
if it presupposes the determinate distinctions whose genesis is being explained.
```

PO-1 does not imply PO-3, and PO-2 does not by itself establish SRT distinctiveness.

---

## 1. Why Barad is the correct first pressure

The earlier repository audits already found that Barad is stronger than Whitehead for the narrowed problem of determinate articulation without pregiven independent relata.

The source-native burden already recovered in the repository is:

```text
phenomena are primary ontological units;
objects / agencies do not simply pre-exist intra-action;
specific intra-actions enact boundaries and determinate separability;
agential cuts generate an exteriority-within-phenomena;
human agency is not required for every phenomenon;
material-discursive practices iteratively configure and reconfigure reality.
```

Therefore a comparison against generic hidden-state control theory alone would begin too late. Barad directly challenges the idea that observation merely reads off independently constituted objects.

---

## 2. PO-2 pressure — Barad pays the broad anti-pregivenness claim strongly

SRT source language says:

```text
pre-object field
!= nothing
!= hidden warehouse of finished objects
!= already formatted information / readable code
```

and the selection-realism paper targets the givenness of individuation itself.

But Barad already rejects the corresponding broad object-first premise:

```text
independent relata / object-apparatus boundary
are not simply supplied prior to intra-action;

determinate separability is enacted through the cut.
```

Therefore SRT cannot claim as a unique difference:

```text
objects are not pregiven;
boundaries are generated;
measurement participates in determination;
a pre-relatum relation / phenomenon can be ontologically prior to independent objects.
```

### PO-2 verdict

```text
PO-2 broad anti-pregiven-object claim:
STRONGLY NEIGHBOR-PAID BY BARAD
```

This is an important negative result. The current author-prioritized axis is real, but its broadest form is not uniquely SRT.

---

## 3. PO-1 pressure — constitutive measurement pays much, but not the exact SRT foreground/background typing

Barad also pressures the crude picture:

```text
already complete object world
-> neutral external observer
-> passive readout
```

because apparatus / cut / phenomenon are constitutive to determinate separability.

This means the following weak SRT statement is already neighbor-paid:

```text
observation is not always a neutral window onto pregiven independent relata.
```

However, the inspected repository sources do not yet establish the exact stronger SRT statement in Barad-native terms:

```text
what is directly observable in one event is specifically the manifest / foreground side;
what is backgrounded is not co-manifest as background in that same event;
the background remains a non-annihilated condition for later Selection;
a later probe that manifests part of the prior background is a new differentiating event.
```

Barad's constitutive cut and exclusion are very close pressure, but exact equivalence to this **foreground / relative-background + later-condition** typing has not been demonstrated in the currently inspected record.

### PO-1 verdict

```text
observation-not-neutral / constitutive determination:
STRONGLY NEIGHBOR-PAID

exact same-event foreground/background observability typing:
PARTIALLY NEIGHBOR-PAID / NOT YET FULLY SUBSTITUTED
```

The remaining difference is not yet a Level-1 residual. It may be paid by a fair composite involving Barad + phenomenological horizon + two-sided distinction / process accounts.

---

## 4. PO-3 pressure — Barad makes the question legitimate but does not prove SRT's strong non-equivalence theorem

PO-3 is stronger than either anti-pregivenness or partial observability.

A state-space advocate can say:

```text
I accept that measurement outcomes and boundaries are relationally constituted.
I nevertheless construct a complete formal model of the relevant apparatus,
field, coupling and latent variables.
Nothing prevents that model from being predictively complete.
```

Barad's ontology blocks a simplistic object-first interpretation, but the currently recovered material does not by itself establish:

```text
no third-person formal state-space can ever be ontologically equivalent
or exhaustively adequate to the constitutive process.
```

Likewise SRT cannot infer that conclusion merely from:

```text
background is not directly observed;
measurement performs a cut;
pre-object field is not a warehouse.
```

The unresolved issue is explanatory order:

> If the state-space coordinates already encode determinate distinctions, does treating that state-space as ontologically prior merely model the post-cut realization, or can the state-space itself be interpreted non-question-beggingly as the constitutive pre-object process?

This is a substantive philosophical question, not a theorem already paid by either framework.

### PO-3 verdict

```text
strong no-equivalent-third-person-state-space theorem:
NOT ESTABLISHED

state-space reduction challenge:
LIVE

Barad support for category distinction:
STRONG

SRT-specific non-substitutability:
NOT YET EARNED
```

## 5. Critical correction to the retrospective hypothesis

The author's retrospective concern is partly confirmed, but in a narrower way than a simple reversal.

### Confirmed

Earlier local-mechanism audits often established only:

```text
D1 local dynamics
or
D2 representational / state-space reproduction
```

and those results cannot automatically settle:

```text
D3 pre-object constitution
or
D4 observation relation.
```

So several theory-wide-sounding limitation summaries were indeed too coarse if read as saying the pre-object / observation ontology had been exhausted.

### Not confirmed

It is **not** the case that the new axis is simply absent from mature theory.

Barad is already a powerful D3/D4 neighbor. The repository's own earlier papers had noticed this, even describing agential realism as superficially close to a synonym for selection realism on determinate-cut questions.

Therefore the correction is:

```text
not:
previous limitations were wrong because rivals are all object-first

but:
previous limitations often tested the wrong level for the newly prioritized core question,
and the correct strongest neighbor at that level is Barad-like constitutive ontology,
not merely RL / WTA / control / reaction-diffusion.
```

After source-native Barad subtraction, the candidate SRT package is narrower:

```text
A. pre-object field is not a pregiven object inventory;             [Barad strongly pressures / broad claim paid]

B. determination is constitutive rather than passive readout;      [Barad strongly pressures / broad claim paid]

C. Selection is explicitly typed as simultaneous manifestation
   + relative backgrounding, not elimination;                      [not yet fully substituted]

D. backgrounded relations can remain conditions for later Selection;
                                                                  [needs exact neighbor comparison]

E. repeated differentiation can sediment a Selection-position,
   a non-flat causal 'from-where' for later Selection;             [separate Position line; not yet paid]

F. Bearer / cognitive continuity is downstream of Position rather
   than the primitive source of determination.                     [SRT layering, not tested here]
```

The possible distinctive burden has moved from **anti-object-first ontology in general** to the exact organization of:

```text
constitutive differentiation
-> manifest / relative-background asymmetry
-> non-annihilated background conditions
-> sedimented / reconstituted Selection-position
-> later cognitive Bearer continuity
```

This is a package / dependency question, not an exclusive-mechanism claim.

---

## 7. New common problem

The common problem can now be stated without SRT labels:

> **How can determinate observables, boundaries and operative positions be constituted without presupposing the completed relata or state inventory they make determinate, and what relation links what becomes manifest to the non-manifest conditions that remain active in later constitution?**

Barad already supplies a major answer to the first half.

The second half — especially the explicit manifest/background persistence and later `from-where` role — is where SRT's current package needs further pressure.
