# Pass 20 — active selectable-space construction strongest-neighbor pressure

> **Role:** pressure the author's latest clarification that vertical structure is not passive historical sediment but the active construction / maintenance / prediction of selectable space and that this belongs to the meaning of Selection itself. This audit does not define `prediction`, does not edit canonical theory, and does not convert a bridge comparison into evidence for SRT.

## 1. Author-controlled target

Exact controlling author source:

> **「垂直结构并不是历史被动沉淀的结果，而是一种维系、预测、主动发生的可选空间的构建。也即是选择的含义。」**

Minimum author-owned correction:

```text
Selection != picking one item from a finished menu.
Selection != passive residue deposited into later history.

Selection
-> actively constructs / maintains / anticipates a selectable space
-> thereby participates in the formation of ontological vertical structure.
```

The term `预测 / prediction` remains author language. No Bayesian, control-theoretic, conscious, or representational mechanism is inferred here.

## 2. Internal owner recovery — this is partly a recovery, not a greenfield invention

An older archived Chapter-08 draft already contains an important precursor:

`01_Source_Intuition/BOOK/Archive_52Chapter/Versioned_Drafts/Part_02_选择的本性/08_选择算子_从可成为性到显现_v5n_future_affordance.md`

```text

```

That draft therefore already paid part of:

```text
Selection -> non-flat possibility / affordance structure -> future-facing effect.
```

But it framed the decisive future-facing relation primarily as:

```text
historical writeback / persistence / future terrain redrawing
```

and its optimization metadata explicitly included:

```text
affordance_not_prediction
```

Therefore the 2026-09-10 author correction should be treated as a **re-centering / refinement** of an older SRT intuition, not as a wholly new invention:

```text
Selection itself is active construction / maintenance / anticipation of selectable space;
history is downstream from that active construction, not the ontological source of verticality.
```

Do not silently preserve the old `history first` causal wording as the current owner.

## 3. Strongest neighbor A — Skilled Intentionality / field of relevant affordances

Primary comparator family:

- Bruineberg & Rietveld, 2014, *Self-organization, free energy minimization, and optimal grip on a field of affordances*, Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 8:599, DOI `10.3389/fnhum.2014.00599`.
- Rietveld, Denys & van Westen, 2018, *Ecological-Enactive Cognition as engaging with a field of relevant affordances: The Skilled Intentionality Framework*, in *The Oxford Handbook of 4E Cognition*, DOI `10.1093/oxfordhb/9780198735410.013.3`.
- van Dijk & Rietveld / related SIF sociomaterial practice line, *Foregrounding Sociomaterial Practice in Our Understanding of Affordances*, Frontiers in Psychology (2016), DOI `10.3389/fpsyg.2016.01969`.

### What SIF strongly pays

Source-native SIF is much stronger than a weak Gibsonian foil.

It explicitly gives:

```text
skilled intentionality = selective engagement with multiple affordances;
field of relevant affordances = possibilities that stand out from the actor's lived perspective;
selective openness / action readiness = position-sensitive responsiveness;
action unfolds from within the actor's lived perspective rather than only from an observer description;
actors continuously form aspects of the sociomaterial environment;
by selectively responding, participants contribute to maintaining affordances as available in the form of life.
```

Therefore these are NOT sufficient SRT residuals:

```text
first-person engagement with possibilities;
non-neutral weighting / salience among possibilities;
selective responsiveness rather than detached observation;
active maintenance of a socially available possibility landscape;
actor-environment reciprocal reshaping;
whole-level affordance landscape exceeding one individual's current field.
```

### What SIF does not obviously pay

SIF normally begins with:

```text
an ecological / sociomaterial landscape of affordances
+ abilities available in a form of life
+ a skilled / autonomous individual whose field of relevant affordances is opened by abilities and concerns.
```

Its central explanatory question is therefore largely:

```text
how an already situated skilled agent selectively engages with / helps maintain a field of affordances.
```

The current SRT target is earlier:

```text
how Selection participates in constituting a non-flat selectable space
before a finished subject / menu / affordance landscape is assumed,
and how formed positions then continue Selection from within that space.
```

Current payment judgment:

```text
post-formation first-person active selectability: STRONG PAYMENT
social maintenance of available possibilities: STRONG PAYMENT
pre-subject selectable-field constitution by Selection itself: NOT CLEARLY PAID
same Selection relation spanning pre-subject constitution and post-formation participation: NOT CLEARLY PAID
```

## 4. Strongest neighbor B — Active Inference / future-oriented policy selection

`Materials/2026/SRC_2026_06_11_Philosophy_ActiveInference_FEP_Book_MITPress.md`

### What Active Inference strongly pays

Active inference is explicitly future-directed:

```text
generative models encode expected / preferred futures;
policy selection evaluates action-conditioned futures;
expected free energy combines pragmatic and epistemic terms;
action and perception are coupled to self-maintenance / self-evidencing;
planning is treated as inference.
```

So these are not SRT-specific:

```text
future orientation;
prediction / anticipation influencing current action;
active maintenance of viable states;
selection among possible policies;
agent identity and expected futures being structurally coupled.
```

### Current boundary against Active Inference

The existing repository source card already notes that Active Inference presupposes an already formalized agent/model/interface:

```text
states / observations / policies / priors / preferred outcomes / Markov blanket
```

and therefore operates downstream of the present pre-object question.

Pass-20 refinement:

```text
Active Inference can strongly model how an already individuated agent predicts and selects among futures.
It does not thereby establish that the selectable future-space itself is ontologically constituted by Selection before that agent / model / interface is available.
```

Current payment judgment:

```text
prediction / planning / prospective action selection: STRONG PAYMENT
self-maintenance: STRONG PAYMENT
pre-object selectable-space constitution: NOT PAID BY THE FORMALISM AS SUCH
prediction without pre-given model/state/action interface: NOT PAID
```

## 8. Whole > parts after Pass 20

SIF and participatory/enactive theories already strongly pay the claim that individuals participate in maintaining a supra-individual affordance / norm / interaction field.

Therefore the whole/parts novelty must not be stated as:


This remains a candidate explanatory advantage, not a demonstrated superiority claim.
