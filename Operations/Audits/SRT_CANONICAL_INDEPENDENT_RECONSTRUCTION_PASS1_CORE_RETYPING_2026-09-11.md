## 1. Q1 — Selection versus actualisation

### 1.1 Pressure from author source

The 2026-09-05 author record explicitly requires:

```text
Selection simultaneously generates manifestation and relative suppression;
center and edge are co-generated rather than sequentially supplied.
```

That rules against two oversimplifications.

### 1.2 Rejected reading A

```text
Selection = actualisation
```

Problem:

This collapses the whole constitutive differentiation into only its manifest/foreground pole and loses the simultaneously generated backgrounding/suppression relation.

### 1.3 Rejected reading B

```text
Selection
-> later causes actualisation
```

Problem:

This creates an unnecessary temporal or causal gap, as if Selection first existed as one process and then produced a second event called actualisation. The author direction instead treats manifestation and relative suppression as internal to the same constitutive event.

### 1.4 Current preferred typing

```text
Selection
= PRIMITIVE CONSTITUTIVE DIFFERENTIATION PROCESS

actualisation / manifestation
= MANIFEST POLE / ASPECT of that process

relative backgrounding / suppression
= CO-GENERATED RELATIONAL POLE / ASPECT
```

Compact form:

```text
Selection
:= non-neutral differentiation in which
   some possibility becomes determinate/manifest
   together with the relative backgrounding of alternatives.
```

This does **not** yet decide whether every physical or empirical realization must literally instantiate lateral inhibition. The lateral-inhibition comparison remains explanatory pressure, not the primitive mechanism definition.

### 1.5 Consequence for the minimal skeleton

Replace:

```text
subjectless Selection / actualisation
-> manifestation + backgrounding
```

with:

```text
subjectless Selection
[actualisation/manifestation + relative backgrounding co-generated]
```

This removes one artificial stage from the skeleton.

**Pass-1 verdict:** `SELECTION-BROADER-THAN-ACTUALISATION / NO-SEQUENTIAL-GAP`.

## 2. Q2 — Is One a necessary ontology node?

### 2.1 Separate the function from the name

Two different questions were previously mixed:

```text
A. Does SRT need a distinct formed-continuity standing
   between isolated Selection-events and Stable ISP?

B. Must that standing be canonically named `One`?
```

Pass 1 answers them differently.

### 2.2 Why a formed-continuity function appears necessary

Without an intermediate formed-continuity standing, the theory jumps from:

```text
historical result-to-condition recursion
```

directly to:

```text
perspective-bearing Stable ISP / subject-like standing.
```

That loses the ability to describe continuing non-subject organizations whose own lineage is reconstituted over time but which have not paid perspective, stake or subject burdens.

The minimum role is therefore:

```text
localized lineage-relative continuity
of Selection-mediated historical reconstitution
```

with at least these pressures:

```text
1. localization: the recurrence can be attributed to a bounded continuation unit;
2. lineage: later reconstitution depends on that unit's own earlier realized history;
3. recurrence/re-entry: continuation is generated repeatedly rather than by one-shot resemblance;
4. non-collapse to observer naming: continuity is carried by the process organization, not merely imposed by description;
5. no subject requirement: perspective, concern, phenomenality and agency remain downstream.
```

### 2.3 What this standing is not

```text
not a static object;
not a microstate identity;
not merely any durable pattern;
not automatically a Bearer/subject/experiencer;
not automatically Stable ISP.
```

### 2.4 Current preferred typing

```text
formed continuing organization
= FORMED ORGANIZATION / STANDING

One
= current candidate name for the unity/continuation aspect
  of that formed organization.
```

The ontology **function** is provisionally retained as necessary.
The canonical **name** remains open.

### 2.5 Selection-position consequence

If this standing exists, `Selection-position` is currently better typed as:

```text
OPERATIVE ASPECT / POSITION
of a formed continuing organization at time t
```

rather than another serial ontology node.

Thus:

```text
One-function
!= Selection-position as two independent entities;

continuation aspect
vs
operative from-where aspect
```

remains the cleaner decomposition.

**Pass-1 verdict:** `FORMED-CONTINUITY-FUNCTION = NEEDED-CANDIDATE; ONE-NAME = OPEN`.

## 3. Q5 — Stable ISP versus subject-position

### 3.1 Current owner tension

Current P1-T06 defines Stable ISP through:

```text
iterative
+ perspective-bearing
+ history-bearing
+ continued-selectable
```

and says it persists as the same selection center through recurrent historical reconstitution.

Current T-IND-2 then says subject-position entry corresponds to P1-T06 while adding:

```text
self-reference closure that does not dissolve;
internalized/non-outsourcable consequence-bearing;
stabilized concern;
local directional readability.
```

The wording says "same standing", but the listed burdens are not actually coextensive.

### 3.2 Why direct MERGE/ALIAS is now too aggressive

If Stable ISP and subject-position are simply declared identical, then one of two things must happen:

```text
A. P1-T06 silently inherits concern / non-outsourcing / directional-stake burdens;

or

B. T-IND-2's stronger subject-entry conditions become redundant decorations.
```

Neither move is justified by the present theory history.

### 3.3 Current preferred nested typing

Pass 1 prefers:

```text
formed continuing organization / One-function
        ↓
Stable ISP
= recurrently reconstituted perspective/history-bearing selection center
  with continued selectability
        ↓
subject-position
= Stable-ISP-level continuity
  + non-outsourcable consequence/stake internalization
  + stabilized concern
  + directional continuity/readability
```

This keeps three distinct burdens:

```text
continuity;
perspective/history-bearing stability;
stake-bearing subject standing.
```

### 3.4 Important guard

`perspective-bearing` here does not by itself prove phenomenality or experiencer status.

Therefore:

```text
Stable ISP
!= automatically phenomenally conscious subject;

subject-position
!= automatically experiencer / self-consciousness.
```

Self-consciousness remains a further reflexive construction question.

### 3.5 Canonical pressure exposed

If this nested typing survives author review, current canonical language stating:

```text
"this is subject-position entry (ISP entry), corresponding to P1-T06"
```

will likely require later `REWRITE`, not mere clarification.

This is exactly the kind of result the canonical-independent cycle was opened to permit.

**Pass-1 verdict:** `CURRENT IDENTITY CLAIM REOPENED; NESTED-STANDING MODEL PREFERRED`.

## 4. Q8 — Is L2 overloaded?

### 4.1 Current owner bundles several roles

The current L2 owner includes at least the following layers:

```text
1. universal historical trace / hysteresis accumulation;
2. closure and writeback;
3. sedimented low-friction scaffold;
4. inheritable/shareable/backgrounded constraint;
5. multi-agent accumulation;
6. social/institutional/collective realization language;
7. convergence / hardening / path dependence.
```

The owner itself already contains a trace -> closure -> L2-grade scaffold ladder, which is evidence that these roles are not identical.

### 4.2 Why generic history should not define L2

If every real Selection leaves history, then generic trace/writeback is too thin to define a separate domain.

A theory that writes:

```text
Selection-event
-> trace
```

already has historical asymmetry without needing every trace to count as an L2 object/domain.

Thus:

```text
generic historical trace / writeback
= CONSTITUTIVE HISTORICAL RELATION
```

is better treated as cross-cutting and upstream of any L2-grade structure.

### 4.3 Stronger candidate for the core L2 role

The more distinctive role is:

```text
sedimented historical constraint/scaffold
that has become sufficiently stable, re-enterable,
inheritable and backgrounded to shape later Selection.
```

Typing:

```text
L2-core candidate
= SEDIMENTED CONSTRAINT / SCAFFOLD DOMAIN OR FIELD
```

This is stronger than mere record and weaker than any claim that L2 is inherently social, collective, healthy or globally convergent.

### 4.4 Roles that should likely be split from the L2 definition

```text
historical trace
-> upstream relation / writeback floor;

convergence / hardening
-> dynamics or degree of scaffold formation;

shared / collective L2
-> scale-relative realization of scaffold;

institution / norm / social field
-> downstream domain realization;

collective perspective / collective ISP
-> separate higher-scale standing gate.
```

### 4.5 Revised place of L2 in the architecture

L2 should not be treated simply as a later serial ontological rung after L1.

Better first-pass geometry:

```text
Selection event
[manifestation + relative backgrounding]
        |
        +-> historical trace / writeback
                 |
                 +-> repeated localized recursion
                 |      -> formed continuing organization / One-function
                 |
                 +-> repeated sedimentation/backgrounding
                        -> L2-grade scaffold / constraint field
```

Then formed organizations and L2-grade scaffolds can recursively condition later Selection.

This makes L2 a **cross-cycle sedimentation structure**, not merely a third chronological stage.

### 4.6 Collective implication

A shared or multi-agent scaffold does not by itself establish:

```text
higher-order One;
collective Stable ISP;
collective subject.
```

Those remain separate scale/standing questions.

**Pass-1 verdict:** `L2 = OVERLOADED CURRENTLY; CORE ROLE SHOULD SHIFT TOWARD SEDIMENTED SCAFFOLD/CONSTRAINT`.
