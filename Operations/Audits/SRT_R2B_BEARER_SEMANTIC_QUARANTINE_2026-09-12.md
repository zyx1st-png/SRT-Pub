## 2. F5 — what E currently pays, and what it does not

### 2.1 The apparent contradiction

The spine currently contains both:

```text
E same-One prospective exposure
= at least some potential consequences genuinely expose the same One
  and cannot be completely reassigned while claiming the same Bearer relation.
```

and:

```text
If a consequence burden can be completely reassigned to another unit while the claimed
Bearer relation is said to remain unchanged, E has not been established for the original One.
```

The second sentence is framed through a **candidate / claimed Bearer attribution**. Read incautiously, it can look like E is already an independently applicable admission criterion for arbitrary candidate Ones. It is not.

### 2.2 Three roles must be separated

For this audit only, distinguish three roles without creating three new canonical objects:

```text
(1) E as semantic relation
    = same-One prospective exposure / non-outsourcing is part of the author-accepted
      P+E reconstruction of Bearer.

(2) current E non-outsourcing test
    = a counterfactual consistency / disconfirmation test:
      if the burden can be fully reassigned while the alleged Bearer relation is declared unchanged,
      the E attribution fails for that One.

(3) independently applicable E criterion
    = a positive, domain-general or otherwise declared procedure that can establish E
      for a candidate One without presupposing the Bearer attribution it is meant to help assess.
```

Current repository status:

```text
(1) ACCEPTED at reconstruction / semantic routing strength;
(2) AVAILABLE as a coherence / exclusion test;
(3) NOT ESTABLISHED / OPEN.
```

This resolves F5 without retracting the author's B decision.

### 2.3 Why the current test is not yet a complete admission procedure

The present wording does not independently settle:

```text
which counterfactual reassignments are admissible;
what counts as preserving the relevant One across the reassignment;
at what scale the exposure must be assessed;
what positive evidence is sufficient to establish E when reassignment is impossible;
whether the same application rule transfers across biological, artificial, collective and other domains.
```

Those missing items are not defects in the semantic relation itself. They are unpaid **application / operationalization burden**.

### 2.4 R2-B disposition for F5

Recommended disposition:

```text
KEEP E as the accepted semantic component of the current P+E Bearer reconstruction;
RETYPE the present non-outsourcing sentence as an E consistency / coherence test;
OPEN independently applicable E admission / establishment criterion.
```

Do not write:

```text

```

because that would contradict the existing author adjudication.

Do not write:

```text

```

because the repository has not paid that burden.
