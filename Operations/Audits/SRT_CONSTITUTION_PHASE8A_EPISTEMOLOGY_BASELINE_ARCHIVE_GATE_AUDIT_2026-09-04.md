## 2. Strongest mature baseline

### 2.1 Philosophy of mathematical practice already rejects the static-object picture

The current Stanford Encyclopedia entry on the **Philosophy of Mathematical Practice** describes a mature family of approaches that studies mathematics as actually practiced, often through historical or contemporary case studies rather than a purely ahistorical body of truths. It explicitly includes languages, accepted statements, reasoning methods, questions, goals/values, communities and agents as parts of mathematical practice, and presents bottom-up case-study methodology as normal within the field.

Source:

- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “The Philosophy of Mathematical Practice” (first published 2026-04-07): https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mathematical-practice/

Consequence for this well:

```text
mathematics is historically evolving
mathematicians / agents participate in practice
methods and questions are part of the epistemic object
```

are **baseline-owned**, not SRT increments.

### 2.2 Historical practice already treats evidence as present traces of an unrecoverable past

Michael J. Barany's history-of-mathematical-practice account distinguishes historical **reconstruction** from **genealogy** and emphasizes that historians infer past practices from present traces under interpretive assumptions. Genealogy tracks transmission, adaptation, preservation and change rather than assuming a timeless object survives untouched.

Source:

- Michael J. Barany, “Histories of mathematical practice: reconstruction, genealogy, and the unruly pasts of ruly knowledge,” *ZDM Mathematics Education* 52 (2020): https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11858-020-01175-5

Consequence:

```text
present reconstruction != past practice itself
historical identity requires tracing preservation / change
```

is already a mature historiographic burden.

### 2.3 Proof identity is already known to have a granularity-collapse problem

Proof-theoretic work explicitly treats **identity of proofs** as nontrivial. Peter Schroeder-Heister notes that sufficiently coarse identification rules can collapse all proofs of a provable proposition into one proof, which makes proof identity trivial rather than informative.

Source:

- Peter Schroeder-Heister, “Axiomatic Thinking, Identity of Proofs and the Quest for an Intensional Proof-Theoretic Semantics” (2022): https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-77657-2_8

This does not settle identity of historical mathematical **methods**, but it means the general warning

```text
coarse equivalence can destroy meaningful identity distinctions
```

is not SRT-specific.

### 2.4 Mathematical-practice methodology already demands case discipline

Recent methodological work in philosophy of mathematical practice explicitly asks how case-based philosophical claims should be constructed and evaluated rather than treating “practice” as a free-form interpretive license.

Source:

- “Toward a Methodology for the Philosophy of Mathematical Practice,” *Philosophy of Science* 92(5), 2025, DOI 10.1017/psa.2025.10134: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/philosophy-of-science/article/toward-a-methodology-for-the-philosophy-of-mathematical-practice/79A0FCDFD4E5A9F110502A6A71CF1881

Therefore the mere existence of an explicit audit procedure is not itself a Constitution increment.

### 2.5 Cohen/forcing scholarship already studies temporality, point of view and identity inside mathematical practice

Roy Wagner's analysis of Paul Cohen follows Cohen's mathematical construction through axes of temporality, point of view and identity and interprets the relation between formal construction and mathematical intuition within Cohen's actual practice.

Source:

- Roy Wagner, “Paul Cohen’s philosophy of mathematics and its reflection in his mathematical practice,” *Synthese* 202 (2023), article 46: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-023-04273-5

Again, this does not provide the repository's exact control protocol, but it blocks any claim that introducing position, temporality or identity into the analysis of forcing is by itself novel.

### 2.6 Representation equivalence in forcing is ordinary technical knowledge

Boolean-valued models are a standard equivalent way of presenting forcing, with their own technical advantages and disadvantages. The repository's D05b already treats ramified, generic-filter and Boolean-valued presentations under a representation-invariance audit.

Background source:

- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “The Mathematics of Boolean Algebra,” §7 Boolean-valued models: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/boolalg-math/

Therefore:

```text
same method may admit materially different formal presentations
```

is a baseline technical fact, not an SRT result.

1. a characteristic generative resource or changed functional role;

It separately freezes six audit tests and two granularity guards.

This is the strongest internal subtraction pressure in the well.
