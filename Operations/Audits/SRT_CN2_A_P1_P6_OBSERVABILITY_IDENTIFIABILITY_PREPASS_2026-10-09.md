## 1. Source-native prior art and why the stronger comparison is hard

**A1. Nosofsky (1986), GCM/MDS**, [DOI 10.1037/0096-3445.115.1.39](https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-3445.115.1.39), PMID 2937873. MDS-choice model of **human item-identification confusion** related to **classification**, with task-sensitive attention. It does NOT supply P1's free similarity ratings, nor an online feedback-driven trial-by-trial learning process by itself. Cross-task representations and selective-attention adjustment are **mature**.

**A2. Kruschke (1992), ALCOVE**, [DOI 10.1037/0033-295X.99.1.22](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.99.1.22), PMID 1546117. Error-driven feature-attention and exemplar/category readout updates can predict feedback-based classification. Do not call any learning-dependent reweighting novel, nor weaken ALCOVE to fixed weights.

**A3. Love, Medin & Gureckis (2004), SUSTAIN**, [DOI 10.1037/0033-295X.111.2.309](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.111.2.309), PMID 15065912. Learned categories can recruit and revise clusters; not a fixed-latent straw rival. **Mack, Love & Preston (2016)**, [DOI 10.1073/pnas.1614048113](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1614048113), PMCID PMC5135299, fitted SUSTAIN to subject classification performance and related model representations to hippocampal fMRI similarity under two feature-relevant tasks. **fMRI RSA is not the human P1 similarity response or P2 label-free grouping**.

**A4. Ashby, Bowman & Zeithamova (2020)**, [DOI 10.3758/s13423-020-01754-3](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-020-01754-3), PMCID PMC7415669, explicitly measured human **pairwise 1–6 similarity ratings before and after learning**, feedback-based category learning in Exp. 1 (paired-associate face names in Exp. 2), then **category-label generalization to new blended faces**; postlearning similarity bias predicted generalization performance. Study reports a public OSF data/materials project, [e8htb](https://osf.io/e8htb/). This is **DIRECT P1** and **source-native category responses to novel blended-feature items**. **P6 admissibility is AUTHOR-OPEN:** the owner says an "action with no direct training history" but does **not expressly exclude a category-label response**. If the author requires a *distinct consequence-bearing affordance/action decision*, Ashby is P6-adjacent rather than that stronger response. Neither branch alone establishes a multi-probe E2 result. Ratings occurred **before** generalization testing: time order helps prospective description but measurement itself could change learning, so the pair is not automatically a passive independent two-probe readout or a predeclared E2 head-to-head model win.

**A5. Ashby & Vucovich (2016)**, [DOI 10.1037/xlm0000277](https://doi.org/10.1037/xlm0000277), PMCID PMC5097011. Human categorization compared high vs degraded **behavior–feedback contingency** while stimuli, optimal decisions and accuracy targets were matched across conditions. **Rule-based (RB) and information-integration (II)** category structures were both examined under high/low contingency, with identical stimuli and matched optimal accuracy; **most participants in both low-contingency conditions showed little or no learning**. RB can be solved using an explicit rule, whereas II depends on integration of multiple stimulus dimensions and is often modeled with feedback-dependent procedural learning. Distinguish these structures before any later consequence manipulation. **Source-native job:** feedback contingency matters to these category tasks. **Not shown:** altered consequence topology simultaneously predicts P1–P6, or SRT Gate/Bearer.

**A6. Carvalho & Goldstone (2022), SAT-M**, [DOI 10.1111/cogs.13128](https://doi.org/10.1111/cogs.13128), model code and fitting-procedure lead [OSF q782h](https://osf.io/q782h/). The **Sequential Attention Theory Model** explicitly adapts item feature encoding to **local temporal training context**. It predicts blocked-versus-interleaved category-learning results and in the paper's compared studies outperformed implementations of ALCOVE, SUSTAIN and a no-local-context ablation. **This is stronger H2/context/history rival pressure**, not proof SAT-M would predict *our unobserved P1–P6* or any consequence-revaluation task; code link is reference only, **not permission to clone or run**.

**A7. Goldstone (1994)**, [DOI 10.1037/0096-3445.123.2.178](https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-3445.123.2.178), PMID 8014612, assessed category-learning effects on **perceptual discrimination**: a P1-adjacent mechanism/behavior, **not direct P1 similarity rating**.

**A8. Roberts, Robbins & Everitt (1988), ID/ED shift**, [DOI 10.1080/14640748808402328](https://doi.org/10.1080/14640748808402328): visual discrimination learners (human and marmoset) made **fewer errors** when the previously relevant dimension remained relevant (intradimensional, ID) than when the previously irrelevant dimension became relevant (extradimensional, ED) on novel exemplars. Source checked at publisher abstract level. This is **behaviorally measured attention-set transfer**, not direct P1 subjective similarity, P2 label-free grouping or a P1–P6 shared-geometry model.

**A9. Owen et al. (1991), human ID/ED shift**, [DOI 10.1016/0028-3932(91)90063-E](https://doi.org/10.1016/0028-3932(91)90063-E), PMID 1762678: relevant-to-irrelevant dimension transfer has a mature human neuropsychology assay; not a source-native matched multi-probe geometry battery.

**A10. Gauntlett-Gilbert, Roberts & Brown (1999), learned irrelevance vs perseveration**, [DOI 10.1016/S0028-3932(98)00049-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0028-3932(98)00049-9), PMID 10340319: the study separately tested difficulty attending to a *previously irrelevant* dimension and difficulty disengaging a *previously relevant* one. **Negative caveat:** in the Parkinson's population studied, the ED deficit was **not explained by specifically enhanced learned irrelevance**. Do not infer learned irrelevance as the universal source of ID/ED differences. Sources checked at PubMed abstract level.

**Merged #1116 follow-up:** its author trace and audit request ID/ED and learned irrelevance as **mature-neighbor comparator/readout leads within existing CN-2 first family A**, NOT a new selected task, model arm, dataset, causal proof or independent ontology claim. The 09-25 Gate-geometric/10-10 Selection-generative coarse-graining genealogy remains **AUTHOR-OPEN**. The research target is public, **joint prospective formation/re-cutting of effective distinctions across probes**, not the untenable premise that all traditional methods merely flatten differences.

**Scientific consequence:** GCM/ALCOVE/SUSTAIN/SAT-M, ID/ED set shifting, learned-irrelevance/perseveration studies, and feedback-contingency studies already cover substantial portions of representation updating, category generalization, trial order and consequence-sensitive learning. A model that merely predicts new labels from ratings, changes attention after feedback or fits task-dependent neural geometry has **no justified local SRT increment**. No named model has been implemented in this project for a fair all-probe same-target comparison.

## 3. Two different-strength paper questions (not authorized experiments)

**Q-A0 — reduced observed-source calibration, NOT owner E2:**

> Can an explicitly declared P1-derived representation forecast held-out novel-blend *category responses* under fair training and response mapping, without claiming that its fit demonstrates joint multi-probe geometric organization?

Ashby 2020 provides P1 and novel-blend category-response precedents, though dataset rows have not been checked. **Regardless of how the author later classifies category actions as P6, a single-input-probe P1 -> classification test is NOT by itself a multi-probe shared-organization E2 test**. It is a source-native calibration/negative control.

**Q-A1 — conditional owner-aligned shared-geometry E2 paper question (NOT an adopted protocol):**

> Given lawful matched consequence histories, can an explicitly specified shared geometry jointly calibrated with **distinct, independently observed input probe types** (owner design example **P1 similarity + P2 label-free grouping + P4 transition expectations**) predict prespecified held-out **P3 boundary + P5 human anomaly + P6 transfer** behavior better than named, capable H1/H2 alternatives with symmetric legal information, structural-learning permissions, readouts and budgets?

**M-layer minimum safeguard proposed for the *joint* claim:** do not call a test "shared across probe types" without **at least two independently observed training probe types and a distinct held-out observed target probe**. This is a **proposed paper-interpretation floor**, **not** a new author-adjudicated universal E2 gate, nor a substitute for the owner's illustrative P1/P2/P4 -> P3/P5/P6 design; two types alone are not sufficient for an E2 PASS. Probe-readout calibration from lawful training sources must be available to *all* models and must not leak target probe responses.

**Owner adjudication needed before fixing P6:** Does selecting a category label for a genuinely novel feature combination count as the P6 required "action with no direct training history", or does P6 require a distinct, consequential affordance/action decision? ### Paper-only observation contracts to write *before* any later model choice

For **each claimed probe**, minimally define `subject_id, session, trial_time, stimulus_id, stimulus_features, relation/context_id, action/options (where applicable), learning_feedback_history, condition, probe_type, observed_response, response_encoding`. **Not a claim that these columns exist in any public dataset**.

- **Likelihood/score type (illustrative):** ordinal categorical for P1/P5 ratings; grouped partition/assignment (non-independent within set) for P2; Bernoulli/multinomial for P3; categorical next-event for P4; categorical observed action for P6. Define response calibration and missingness before calling these runnable likelihoods. A probabilistic prediction should be assessed with a proper held-out predictive score and uncertainty, not in-sample embedding fit.
- **Probe-order reactivity:** administering P1, P2 or P3 can **itself change encoding or attention**, and repeated questions can induce explicit category labels; assessment effects may contaminate target P6. Define the actual observation order and distinguish learning `history -> probe` from `probe -> later outcome`; do not assume independent simultaneous passive readouts.
- **World A/B comparability:** matched stimuli or marginal frequencies do **not** automatically match action histories, feedback informativeness, exposure to diagnostic examples, fatigue or policy acquisition. If "consequence" means a changed category correctness signal, state so; **do not infer altered action/outcome topology** from that weaker manipulation.

## 4. Strict evaluation permissions and no-leak contract

A candidate **same-target E2 predictive** future assessment must first have separately declared estimands:

| Split / claim | What must be held out | What cannot be inferred |
|---|---|---|

| **B: new stimulus/feature/relation combination** | Untouched item/combo pool set aside before model selection and training; verify same lawful access across worlds/models. | Not earned merely by probing seen stimulus IDs through new words or labels. |
| **C: forward-time learning/revaluation** | Future outcomes/labels/intervention responses after a specified cutoff; chronology preserved. | No E3 causality or E4 recursive proof just from sequential E2 prediction; later gates remain. |
| **D: new participant/session/context** | Whole participant/session/context blocks at outer test and proper inner tuning, if that generalization is claimed. | No population forecast from randomly split trials of the same participant. |
