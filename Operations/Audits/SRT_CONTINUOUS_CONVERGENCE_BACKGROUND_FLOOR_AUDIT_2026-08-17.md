# Continuous Convergence / Background Floor Audit

> **Author input being audited**:
>
> 1. SRT primarily concerns `Selection`, not a new taxonomy of `Choice`.
> 2. Selection is irreversible differentiation: some processes are suppressed and others manifested.
> 3. “Survival” in this discussion means existential persistence / a change becoming stably continuable, not biological survival.
> 4. Selection changes friction.
> 5. Convergence toward stability is continuously operating; some parts mature enough to become a background “floor” and no longer require foreground attention.
>
> **Hard boundary**: this audit adds no new P0/P1 claim, symbol, equation, layer, or `Choice` subtype.

---

## 0. Executive verdict

**Verdict: `CONTINUOUS-CONVERGENCE / BACKGROUND-MATURITY / NO-NEW-PRIMITIVE`.**

The author clarification is largely **already latent in current SRT architecture**, especially `T-L2-Scaffold`, EX-A, and the closure hardening line. The main theoretical correction is one of reading:

> **Selection does not stop when a path becomes stable. Mature selective convergence becomes background scaffold; further Selection continues on, through, and sometimes against that scaffold.**

Five conclusions follow.

### C1 — Selection should not be read as a sequence of isolated completed acts

SRT may analyze discrete selection events for formal or experimental convenience, but the larger dynamics is continuous selective differentiation and convergence. Event boundaries are coarse-grained analysis windows, not proof that Selection itself turns on and off.

### C2 — `L2` is maturity/backgrounding, not cessation

A mature path can cross from foreground event to background scaffold while remaining maintained, refreshed, causally constraining, and historically effective.

### C3 — maturity is path-/dimension-local

Different parts of one system may be simultaneously:

- newly differentiated;
- actively converging;
- minimally closed;
- strongly scaffolded;
- destabilized after prior backgrounding.

No single global “the system is now L2” switch is required.

### C4 — backgrounding means reduced foreground regulatory burden, not zero dynamics

In a cognitive domain this may appear as reduced attention demand. Across domains, the safer structural meaning is that compatible traversal no longer requires reconstruction from scratch.

### C5 — a mature floor can re-enter the foreground

When compatibility fails, maintenance conditions change, trace decays, or perturbation makes the inherited scaffold insufficient, previously background structure can again become a high-friction site of regulation, competition, revision, or replacement.

No new term is needed for this return.

## 2. A better process picture

Do not read:

```text
Selection
-> stability
-> L2
-> Selection finished
```

Prefer:

```text
continuous selective differentiation / convergence
        |
        +-- some differences remain weak / transient
        |
        +-- some acquire trace
        |
        +-- some begin lowering compatible future friction
        |      = minimal closure
        |
        +-- some become strongly re-enterable / inheritable / backgrounded
               = L2-grade scaffold

new differences continue to arise on this inherited floor
```

The floor is therefore both:

1. **product of prior Selection**, and
2. **condition of later Selection**.

This creates an explicit historical loop:

```text
Selection
-> selective persistence / closure
-> background constraint
-> changes future accessibility / friction
-> shapes later Selection
-> further persistence / revision
```

This is not circular explanation if the history index is respected: prior convergence constrains later convergence.

## 3. “Floor” is relative, not absolute

A path is a floor only relative to:

- a scale;
- a boundary;
- a perturbation range;
- a compatible task / context family;
- a time horizon.

A structure can be mature background at one scale and still require active lower-scale maintenance.

Examples:

- fluent language structure can be background for sentence production while neural control remains active;
- a motor habit can be background for ordinary walking while balance corrections continue;
- an institutional rule can be background for routine transactions while people and infrastructure continually reproduce it;
- a physical macro-pattern can remain stable while microstates change.

Therefore “floor” must not be read as a metaphysically frozen layer.

## 4. Attention is a projection of foreground demand, not a core criterion

The author phrase “does not need to be attended” is strongest when translated structurally as:

> **the mature path no longer demands foreground reconstruction / comparison / regulation under compatible conditions.**

For human cognition, attention may be a useful proxy:

```text
maturity up
-> compatible-path marginal burden down
-> less explicit monitoring / comparison required
```

But SRT must not infer:

```text
no attention
-> therefore L2
```

or

```text
attention present
-> therefore new Selection
```

Attention is domain-specific. Backgrounding is the broader structural relation.

---

## 5. Mature does not mean costless

This distinction is essential.

`T-L2-Scaffold` includes trace decay and refresh. Therefore a mature floor may have:

- low marginal cost for compatible re-traversal;
- persistent maintenance cost;
- high cost of switching away;
- high cost when the environment ceases to fit the inherited scaffold.

So:

```text
backgrounded
!=
zero Psi_f
```

and:

```text
low compatible-path friction
!=
low total system burden
```

This protects the theory from treating automaticity as ontological free energy.

## 6. Partial maturity across dimensions

The author's phrase “some parts have matured” matters more than a global maturity scalar.

At one time, a system can contain:

```text
Dimension/path A: deeply backgrounded
Dimension/path B: maintained but fragile
Dimension/path C: actively converging
Dimension/path D: newly destabilized
Dimension/path E: transient and failing to anchor
```

Current `rho(p,t)` is already path-indexed and therefore supports this picture better than a single whole-system maturity variable.

No new maturity symbol is needed before a concrete experimental requirement demands one.

This is technical debt, not a new theory decision.

## 8. How a floor returns to the foreground

A mature scaffold is background only while its compatibility conditions remain sufficiently satisfied.

A previously background path can become foreground-relevant when at least one of the following occurs:

1. **context mismatch** — the environment / task changes so the old path no longer closes cheaply;
2. **competing constraint** — another stabilized path imposes incompatible demands;
3. **trace decay / insufficient refresh** — inherited path support weakens;
4. **resource/payability shift** — maintenance that was previously affordable becomes costly;
5. **boundary or bearer change** — the structure now operates in a different effective system context;
6. **persistent error / consequence return** — the old scaffold's outputs repeatedly generate downstream friction.

Expected structural sequence:

```text
mature background scaffold
-> perturbation / mismatch
-> compatible-path advantage weakens
-> local friction / error / switching pressure rises
-> foreground regulation increases
-> path is either refreshed, revised, bypassed, or replaced
-> new convergence profile forms
```

No claim is made that every perturbation produces revision; hysteresis can preserve the old floor for a long time.

## 9. Healthy vs pathological backgrounding

The present clarification improves the existing pathology distinction.

Stability itself is not pathological.

A healthy scaffold may be strongly backgrounded **and still remain revisable when persistent mismatch makes it no longer adequate**.

A pathological closure is different: it maintains itself by compressing broader future selectability or by preventing consequence signals from reopening the relevant structure.

Thus a useful P2/P3 reading is:

```text
healthy mature floor:
backgrounded under compatibility
+ capable of re-entering revision under sustained incompatibility

pathological mature floor:
backgrounded
+ preserves itself by blocking / exporting / suppressing the incompatibility
```

This is consistent with existing generative reselectability and pathological-closure work, but is not promoted here to a P1 theorem.

## 10. Selection and `L2` are not opposing modes

The prior discussion temporarily drifted toward:

```text
Selection vs L2 execution
```

That is too binary.

Better:

```text
L2 = mature historical selection constraints
Selection = ongoing differentiation/convergence that always occurs relative to inherited constraints
```

At a declared level, some current trajectories may be almost fully scaffolded, while other dimensions remain open or destabilized.

A system can therefore be highly automatic in one dimension and strongly selective in another without contradiction.

## 12. Empirical shell — useful but not yet D2

The continuous-convergence picture suggests a clean experimental shell.

### Phase A — scaffold formation

Repeated compatible traversal should jointly produce:

- lower marginal compatible-path cost;
- higher re-entry reliability;
- stronger hysteresis / path dependence;
- lower foreground regulation demand in domains where such demand is measurable.

### Phase B — floor challenge

Introduce a perturbation that invalidates part of the inherited compatibility relation.

Measure:

- immediate cost increase;
- old-path perseveration;
- switching / repair cost;
- time to re-stabilization;
- persistence or revision of the scaffold;
- downstream reachability after revision.

### Phase C — recovery / new backgrounding

If a new pattern becomes mature:

- its compatible traversal cost should fall;
- it should become re-enterable with less foreground burden;
- hysteresis should now favor the revised path.

### Rival warning

Ordinary habit learning, reinforcement learning, adaptive control, predictive processing, and dynamical-systems models can predict much of this shell.

Therefore this is **not yet a D2 discriminator** for SRT. Its immediate value is to operationalize L2 hardening/backgrounding with cleaner theory alignment.

A later D2 test must freeze a bounded rival and identify a prospective quantity or intervention where the rival predicts differently.

It should be pursued as an extension / refinement of the existing L2 hardening work, not as a new Choice theory.

## 15. Stop rule

Stop this audit here because:

1. the author's “continuous stability convergence” intuition is already strongly supported by existing L2 machinery;
2. no new primitive or Choice term is required;
3. mature floor = backgrounded selective convergence, not inactive structure;
4. `rho(p,t)` / `rho*` already provide a better path-specific scaffold mechanism than a new maturity variable;
5. bare `kappa` cannot be reused because of canonical symbol collision;
6. attention is correctly demoted to a domain proxy for foreground regulatory burden;
7. re-foregrounding under compatibility failure is identified as the main new integration target;
8. the empirical shell is useful but does not yet reach D2 against ordinary learning/control rivals.

**Final disposition:** proceed with L2 backgrounding / floor-challenge hardening; no new `Choice` vocabulary and no new canonical symbol.
