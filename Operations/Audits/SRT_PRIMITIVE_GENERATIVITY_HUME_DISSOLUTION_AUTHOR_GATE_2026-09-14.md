# Author gate — primitive generativity and Hume-gap dissolution

> **Role:** smallest author-decision packet supported by the post-Fable pressure test. This is not an author adjudication and does not authorize canonical edits.

## 1. Gate A — primitive generativity

### A1 — joint first-principle burden

Proposed ruling:

> Primitive generativity is one theory-floor relation with two irreducible role-aspects: **actualising Selection** and **non-maximal-indifference / directional typing**. They are co-primitive burdens, not temporally ordered mechanisms and not two independently acting entities.

```text
G0 primitive generativity
= actuality-producing differentiation
+ non-maximally-indifferent generative form

O0 -/> Selection as prior cause
Selection -/> O0 as later product
```

Recommended: `A1 = ACCEPT`.

### A2 — O0 is relation-level non-equivalence

Proposed ruling:

> O0 is **relation-level non-equivalence**, not object-level preference. Primitive generativity is not invariant under complete role-flattening: actualisation / manifestation and relative backgrounding have asymmetric generative roles. This does not rank completed outcomes, assign probabilities, specify a target, or explain why one particular content rather than another actualises.

```text
O0 != prior field
O0 != branch weights
O0 != telos
O0 != chooser preference
O0 != why-A-rather-than-B theorem
```

Recommended: `A2 = ACCEPT WITH EXPLICIT LIMIT`.

### A3 — epsilon shadow line

Proposed ruling:

> Retain epsilon-shadow as an **inheritance / compatibility conjecture**: primitive non-maximal-indifference may constrain the forms by which later Selection-generated differences remain generatively effective, but it does not primitively privilege continuation outcomes.

Any later derivation must preserve:

```text
no pregiven branches;
no survivor-screening projected backward;
genuine terminal Selection remains possible;
no continuation = goodness;
no later health/value metric back-defines O0.
```

Recommended: `A3 = ACCEPT AS OPEN SHADOW LINE`.

## 2. Gate B — O1 better/worse-for

### B1 — standing first, valuation second

Proposed ruling:

> `better/worse-for` is admissible only after a consequence-bearing standing is independently established in non-evaluative terms. Current P+E Bearer architecture is the leading repository candidate for non-outsourcable same-One exposure, while positive E admission remains OPEN.

The standing gate must not use `good / healthy / deserving / valuable / correct` as admission premises.

Recommended: `B1 = ACCEPT`.

### B2 — indexed constitutive O1

Proposed ruling:

> Once standing X is independently established, `better/worse-for` is indexed to X, a declared constitutive relation/capacity R, and horizon H. A consequence is better/worse-for X with respect to R,H insofar as it enables/sustains versus impairs/removes that independently typed relation.

```text
better/worse-for(X, R, H)
```

This is not global goodness. A tumour may have something better-for itself; an oppressive institution may have internal better-for relations; a sacrifice may be worse-for one standing while chosen for another.

Therefore:

```text
continuation != goodness
O1 != O2
```

Recommended: `B2 = ACCEPT AS O1 CONSTITUTIVE ROUTE`.

### B3 — label-swap anti-smuggling test

Proposed ruling:

> An O1 claim is legitimate only if swapping `better` and `worse` while holding all independently typed standing / consequence facts fixed would contradict the direction of the constitutive relation under comparison. If the labels can be freely swapped, the normativity was imported rather than constituted.

Recommended: `B3 = ACCEPT AS PRESSURE RULE`.

## 3. Gate C — O2 responsibility and deontic ought

### C1 — split O2 internally

```text
O2-R = responsibility / answerability attribution
O2-D = action-guiding deontic ought / all-things-considered obligation
```

Recommended: `C1 = ACCEPT`.

### C2 — responsibility as constitutive of attributable control

Proposed ruling:

> Where an agency-capable position has attributable control over a consequence-generating selection relation, access to relevant reorientation, sufficient consequence return for revision, and continuity across the attribution interval, answerability for consequences is constitutive of claiming that control relation. The agent cannot retain authorship/control while assigning all consequences of that same attributed control to no answerable position.

This supports `responsible-for`, not automatically `blameworthy`, `ought to choose the opposite`, or complete moral agency.

Recommended: `C2 = ACCEPT AS STRONG O2-R CANDIDATE`.

### C3 — complete deontic closure remains open

Pressure result:

```text
O1 comparison
+ attributable agency / responsibility
-/>
a unique all-things-considered ought automatically.
```

Conflicts among standings, horizons and consequence dimensions still require an account of normative authority / claim priority. Do not hide that burden by defining continuation, reorganizability, non-outsourcing or the four criteria as mandatory by fiat.

Recommended: `C3 = ACCEPT HOLD / OPEN`.

## 4. Gate D — Hume-gap claim strength

### D1 — retain dissolution, narrow achieved strength

Proposed ruling:

> `Dissolution Rather Than Derivation` remains the research programme, but the currently achieved result is a **typed / partial dissolution architecture**, not complete deontic closure.

Safe form:

```text
Hume's logical point about deduction from purely descriptive premises is not denied.

SRT instead rejects the metaphysical assumption:
completed value-neutral facts first + value later.

O0:
determinate actuality is a cross-section of directionally typed primitive generativity.

O1:
formed consequence-bearing standing can make some evaluative-for differences constitutive rather than externally appended.

O2-R:
attributable generative control can make answerability constitutive.

O2-D:
all-things-considered action-guiding ought remains OPEN where standings / claims conflict.
```

Recommended: `D1 = ACCEPT`.
