1. independently specified;
2. prior to objectification rather than already assuming a state/object architecture;
3. able to preserve determinate manifest actuality;
4. free of a prior chooser;
5. compatible with EX-A occurrence / persistence separation;
6. free of hidden reparameterization of the same primitive role.

Three rival classes separated cleanly:

- pre-given state-space transition vocabularies are independently specified but begin after the pre-objectification target;

- concrete domain mechanisms can replace particular realizations but require enough prior structure to be downstream of the P0 question.

This is a negative audit result, not an indispensability proof.
