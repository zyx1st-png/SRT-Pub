## 0. Executive result

### 0.1 HP-B

The positive HP-B route should **remain ACTIVE as a P3 hypothesis**, not return to HOLD.

The current hook passes the most important first gate:

```text
C1-C7 can be stated structurally
without phenomenal vocabulary in the antecedent.
```

That is a real advance over a circular definition of feeling.

However, the hook currently compresses three distinct burdens into one sentence:

```text
A. bearer / ownership structure
   = whose deformation and whose continuation?

B. structural admission dynamics
   = when can the deformation no longer remain background-local
     and must become common / selection-active?

C. phenomenal bridge
   = why is that common bearer-constitutive foreground felt at all?
```

Current evidence supports A strongly enough for routing, proposes a nontrivial B through C4-C6, and **posits rather than derives C** through BP-Q1.

Therefore the strongest defensible current wording is:

> **BP-Q1 is a testable phenomenal-admission bridge hypothesis with a mechanistic account of the structural side of admission.**

It is not yet a derivation of phenomenality from structure.

### 0.2 GRG

The multiview architecture should also **remain ACTIVE as a candidate design**.

But the historical rationale in #1062 is not supported as written:

```text
GRG v0.3
!= a layer ontology
!= a method whose core error was treating L0/L1/L2 as three ontic layers.
```

v0.3 §4.3 explicitly says verticality is a research relation, not a variable ontology, and §22 already treats “L2-facing” as a scoped measurement view rather than a canonical type.

The 09-25 proposal is therefore best understood as:

> **a new explicit multiview design hypothesis that adds L0/L1/L2-facing reconstruction as an operational completion layer over the 09-23 anti-mapping / cross-objectification method.**

That can be a real rearchitecture without being narrated as correction of a nonexistent v0.3 layer ontology.

### 0.3 Highest-priority corrections before any stronger landing

```text
1. Psi_f concept identity and claim-level routing must be separated.
2. HP-B must distinguish structural-side mechanism from the phenomenal bridge posit.
3. Stable low-conflict perception remains a scope stress test for C4.

```

## 3. F3 — stable low-conflict perception

**Verdict: VALID / HIGH SCOPE PRESSURE.**

C4 currently says the relevant burden:

> cannot be fully absorbed within one local background gate without materially changing broader bearer-level continuation.

That naturally fits cases in which a deformation becomes salient because background/local regulation is no longer enough:

- pain;
- hunger;
- anxiety;
- conflict;
- surprise;
- Aha / constraint convergence.

It does not straightforwardly explain why an already-stable, low-conflict visual field — e.g. a blue wall — remains phenomenally present.

This creates a necessity problem:

```text
If C4 is necessary for all phenomenality,
stable perception needs an account of ongoing nonlocal payment / containment failure.

If stable perception can remain phenomenal without C4,
BP-Q1 is not yet a general phenomenal-admission condition.
```

### Important refinement

The problem may be partly terminological.

There are at least two different readings:

```text
payment failure
= high burden / rising friction / regulation failure

background-containment failure
= the state cannot remain causally local/backgrounded
  because it participates in a common current foreground.
```

The second is broader and could include stable perception without requiring distress or high friction.

But replacing the first with the second changes the theory burden and must not be done silently in this audit.

### Current disposition

```text
BP-Q1 general phenomenality scope = OPEN
affect / salience / conflict scope = STRONGER CURRENT FIT
stable sensory phenomenality = ACTIVE COUNTERPRESSURE
micro-friction rescue = NOT ASSUMED
```
