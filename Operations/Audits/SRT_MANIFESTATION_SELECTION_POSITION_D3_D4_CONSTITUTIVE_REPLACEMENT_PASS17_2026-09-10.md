The current Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy account of Husserl emphasizes two relevant structures:

```text
object-oriented inquiry presupposes object-manifestation and phenomenology asks how objects can appear;
```

Reference:

## 6. Neighbor D — relational state formalisms can build a recursive from-where without hidden-object identity

### 6.1 Predictive State Representations

Littman, Sutton & Singh represent dynamical-system state by multi-step action-conditional predictions of future observations. The representation is recursively updated and grounded in the action-observation stream rather than requiring a privileged hidden-state ontology.

Primary source:

- Michael L. Littman, Richard S. Sutton & Satinder Singh (2001), `Predictive Representations of State`, NeurIPS 14: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2001/hash/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Abstract.html

The paper explicitly defines discrete action and observation sets, histories/tests, sufficient predictive state and recursive updating. Therefore it is a strong relational-state opponent **and** a clear Class-M example at the observation alphabet.

### 6.2 Computational mechanics

Shalizi & Crutchfield construct causal states as equivalence classes of past histories with the same predictive relevance for futures; the resulting epsilon-machine is minimal for accurate prediction under the paper's assumptions.

Primary source:

- Cosma Rohilla Shalizi & James P. Crutchfield (2001), `Computational Mechanics: Pattern and Prediction, Structure and Simplicity`, *Journal of Statistical Physics* 104; arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9907176

The framework begins with a process over a declared symbol alphabet and partitions histories into effective / causal states according to predictive distributions.

### Replacement payment

These formalisms demonstrate:

```text
state need not mean hidden enduring object;
current operative state can be a relational compression of history;
current state can be recursively updated by new observations/actions;
what matters about a past can be defined by its consequences for possible futures.
```

Therefore another weak residual fails:

```text
Selection-position is distinctive because it is historical / relational / recursively updated rather than a hidden object-state.
```

That is not enough.

### Hard remaining boundary

Both inspected formalisms still start with a declared observational/symbolic interface:

```text
observation alphabet;
action/test alphabet where applicable;
history elements;
predictive equivalence relation over those declared observations.
```

They therefore pressure S5 strongly **after** manifestation but do not by themselves pay S0–S3/D3-D4 genesis of the determinate observational interface.

Disposition:

```text
relational / history-defined from-where: PAID
recursive state update: PAID
hidden-object-state critique: DEFEATED
manifest-interface genesis: NOT PAID BY THESE FORMALISMS ALONE
```
