## 2. Why the split is required

Current Ledger asks one field to do two jobs:

~~~text
where does the directional burden come from?
and
who / what anticipates or models it?
~~~

These are not the same question.

Example:

~~~text
road-capacity feedback
may have:
S-TECH source structure
+
OBS-MODEL appraisal

without:
C1 model-mediated anticipation inside the transport system.
~~~

Likewise:

~~~text
RNN preregistered prediction
= OBS-MODEL

does not establish:
B-STRUCTURAL
or
C1-MODEL.
~~~

Therefore source/provenance and locus/mode must be independent axes.

## 3. E-MODEL verdict

Historical E-MODEL conflates at least three things:

1. analyst prediction;
2. institutional / engineering model used for appraisal;
3. a formed locus using a model to organize present action.

Only #3 can pay C1.

#1 is OBS-MODEL.

#2 is:
- OBS-MODEL if merely external appraisal;
- C1-MODEL only if an admitted formed locus actually uses that model in present organization.

Verdict:

~~~text
E-MODEL AS EXPECTATION PROVENANCE = RETIRE
~~~

Replacement:

~~~text
C1-MODEL
or
OBS-MODEL
~~~

with source/provenance recorded separately.

## 4. E-MIXED verdict

Current rows already work better when they state explicit layers:

~~~text
S-MAT + S-LEGAL
S-TECH + S-ORG
S-LEGAL + S-ORG
~~~

A generic E-MIXED tag adds no independent explanatory burden.

It can hide unresolved decomposition.

Verdict:

~~~text
E-MIXED = RETIRE AS LIVE TAG
~~~

Historical occurrence may remain in provenance history only.

Use explicit multi-layer source lists.


~~~text

<relation / declared objective / formed locus>

<grain>

<what capacity / relation is strengthened or weakened>
~~~

Without these fields:

~~~text

~~~

## 9. Stability / legitimacy guard

A stable institution or domination structure may have:

~~~text
strong B
relative to its own reproduction.
~~~

This establishes only:

~~~text
relation-reproductive directionality.
~~~

It does not establish:

~~~text
participant-level G-strength
justice
legitimacy
moral priority.
~~~

PH / position indexing is therefore not optional for social / civilizational uses where multiple positions are materially affected.
