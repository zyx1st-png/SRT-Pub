# Audit — new objectified-trace method versus stopped GRG cross-objectification route

## 0. Purpose

Prevent the 2026-09-28 reconstruction direction from being misrepresented as wholly new or used to rescue the stopped GRG fusion-case programme.

## 1. What the 2026-09-23 GRG method already owned

The prior architecture already required:

~~~text
source-native recovery;
objectification declaration;
legitimate re-objectification;
domain-native generative reconstruction;
missing-relation search;
late grammar extraction;
prospective held-out testing;
source absorption / no-gain;
failure preservation.
~~~

It also explicitly rejected:

~~~text
domain object -> GRG object mapping
~~~

as the research engine.

Therefore the following are **not new** in 2026-09-28:

- objectification is revisable;
- mature domain objects may hide or aggregate generative relations;
- alternative cuts should be inspected;
- reconstruction should be source-native first;
- held-out testing is required before stronger credit;
- mature source ownership must be absorbed.

## 2. Stop-loss that remains binding

The fusion-case plan §8.1 requires contraction after repeated adequate no-gain cases and forbids opening a third domain merely to rescue a favored field or GRG term.

The later engagement decomposition result also found:

~~~text
SOURCE-REAL
SOURCE-OWNED
GRG EXTRA PAYOFF = NONE
~~~

across the preregistered decomposition family, with no third rescue domain opened.

Therefore:

~~~text
2026-09-28 method
-/-> reset prior stop-loss;
-/-> new cross-domain grammar acquisition credit;
-/-> permission to repeat the same decomposition search on new domains.
~~~

## 3. Candidate delta in the new route

The new proposal becomes non-duplicate only if it tests a narrower burden:

\[
O_{i,t}=C_{i,t}(G_t)+\epsilon_{i,t}
\]

and treats **both** \(G_t\) and \(C_{i,t}\) as reconstruction targets where warranted.

The candidate delta is:

~~~text
old emphasis:
  reconstruct relation under declared / alternative objectifications
  and ask what survives;

new candidate emphasis:
  distinguish process change from cut change,
  jointly reconstruct process + objectification dynamics,
  then predict a held-out cut or the formation/change of the cut itself.
~~~

This delta remains a proposal until tested.

## 4. Nontriviality ladder

To avoid relabeling old GRG work:

~~~text
same-cut held-out prediction                 = insufficient delta;
known alternative-cut survival              = largely old-route owned;
held-out-cut prediction                      = candidate stronger delta;
prospective cut-formation / cut-change test = strongest current candidate delta.
~~~

Even the strongest level does not by itself establish primitive Selection or SRT metaphysical distinctiveness.

## 5. Diffusion-specific boundary

The 2026-09-28 author correction narrows diffusion to the sedimentation side.

Diffusion-like models are candidates for:

~~~text
formed actuality
-> writeback / retention
-> later-effective transition deformation.
~~~

They are not default models for:

~~~text
genesis of the state / candidate / comparison space itself.
~~~

This is a substantive methodological distinction from merely using a powerful generative model to fit all stages.

## 6. Required failure exit

The new route must return:

~~~text
ABSORBED / NO-GAIN
~~~

if a mature source-native model already:

- jointly represents process and measurement/cut dynamics;
- survives held-out-cut transfer;
- predicts cut change at equal or lower burden;
- or renders the proposed distinction unnecessary.

No SRT-specific credit may be claimed from successful source reconstruction alone.
