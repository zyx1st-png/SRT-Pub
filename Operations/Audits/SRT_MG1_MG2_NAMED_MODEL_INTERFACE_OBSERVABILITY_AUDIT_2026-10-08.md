## 3. Candidate card contract (paper-only checklist)

A *future named implementation*, **including the shared-geometry arm**, needs these fields:

| Field | Declaration needed prior to any model contest |
|---|---|

**R0 stop:** no *same explanatory target* and two sufficiently specified models = no empirically meaningful model win/loss. A good classification table is not a benchmark.

## 4. MG-2 ACCESS / CONTROL / OWNERSHIP: separate empirical and theoretical targets

| Type | Candidate neutral measurement / contrast (NOT frozen) | Forbidden inference |
|---|---|---|
| **A — ACCESS** | target/report availability, recognition, flexible reporting and corresponding access correlates, with report confounds | reported/ignited content does not by itself establish control-allocation, objective action efficacy or phenomenal/ontic admission |
| **C — CONTROL** | subsequent target-conditioned policy and choice differences, control expenditure/effort, maintained task-set, held-out response/transition prediction, experimentally justified change in allocation | action success does not by itself establish conscious access, H3-only mechanisms or a subject's stake |
| **O1 — *feeling of agency*** | subjective initiation/causation experience, prospective versus outcome-related cue contrasts | self-report is not ground truth for the action's causal controller |
| **O2 — *judgment/attribution of agency*** | self/other attribution, deliberate responsibility judgments, separately from O1 | judgment is not identical to feeling of agency or bodily sense of ownership |
| **O3 — *SRT Bearer–Concern consequence ownership*** | **NO DIRECT EMPIRICAL OBSERVABLE ADMITTED**. Interest, concern, history and self-description might be candidate downstream correlates only | O1, O2, control efficacy, bodily ownership, GNW ignition or L2 writeback cannot establish SRT Bearer/One/Selection |

**Critical name collision:** source literature's feeling/judgment of *agency* and feeling/judgment of *body ownership* differ (Synofzik et al. 2008). MG-2's OWNER question is stronger and theory-owned: continuing Bearer/Concern/consequence organization. **Do not treat body ownership, attribution of an action to self, and SRT Bearer as equal variables.** This audit's O1/O2/O3 labels are temporary observation bookkeeping, not new SRT terms or an author ontology adjudication.

**Dissociation stress tests (examples, not results):**
- A+ / C−: a consciously accessible target with insufficient maintenance/allocation, or a reportable distractor. ACCESS != effective CONTROL.
- A− / C+: automatic skilled task-set/action control without reflective report. CONTROL != conscious access.
- C+ / O1− or O2−: effective policy control need not yield strong agency feeling/judgment.
- O1+ / weak C: experienced agency can arise without independently established downstream control mechanism.
- A+ / C+ / O1+ / O2+: **still no O3**, no primitive Selection and no independent H3 mechanism.

Dissociations need their own measurement validity and causal-identification assumptions before stronger evidence is asserted. GNW/Cogitate findings constrain sweeping access claims; they neither establish all GNW predictions nor disprove every GNW variant.

## 5. Same-target mapping into E2, and no cross-level shortcut

The inherited E2 candidate uses P1/P2/P4 for learning and asks for held-out P3/P5/P6 predictions. The exact sensory task and actual pair of implementations remain OPEN.

| Scoped job | Plausible comparator *family* | Why this is NOT a finished test |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-probe relational predictive geometry | TEM adapted to matched items/relations vs named shared geometry and a capable H2 learner | source-native TEM does not already supply P3/P5 outputs or an exact E2 interface; H2 can itself use shared embeddings |
| Target becoming a control-effective bias | PBWM adapted to task-state updating vs specific hierarchical active-inference/control implementation | CONTROL/MG-2 problem unless an exact common P1–P6 conditional prediction is defined; EVC and RITL not standalone executable arms |
| Available content but no effective control | GNW access theory and independently measured report contrasted against specified policy/control states | ACCESS and CONTROL belong to different explanatory jobs; GNW is not an automatic model of all choices |
| Subjective agency versus objective control | Synofzik/Haggard source constraints and controlled self-attribution observations | O1/O2 measures do not identify O3 and must not be smuggled into E2 E3 E4 as separate evidence without an independent gate |
| Future effect of feedback | explicit H2 structural updating vs shared-geometry implementation only after models implemented | **E4** needs forward chronological cutoff and a later revaluation. It is not an E2 prediction score |

E2 success requires predeclared **held-out predictive improvement relative to named capable H1/H2 implementation(s)** with lawful symmetric inputs and resource budgets. Compression is supplementary **only at comparable predictive adequacy**. E3 causal intervention leverage is **not** an E2 alternative; E4 future recutting remains its own stage. W1–W4 is a **conjunctive strong whole-field burden**. H3 is effective geometric description and may be an H2 readout; observationally equivalent models cannot earn independent H3 ontology by naming.
