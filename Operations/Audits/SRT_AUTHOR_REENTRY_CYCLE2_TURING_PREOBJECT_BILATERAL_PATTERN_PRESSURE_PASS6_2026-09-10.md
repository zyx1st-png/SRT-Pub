## 0. Why reaction–diffusion is a fairer pressure than another learning model

A cognitive or reinforcement-learning rival can always be rejected as beginning too late because it usually assumes:

```text
learner
sensor / observation interface
task / objective
representational substrate
```

A classical self-organizing reaction–diffusion field does not have this problem.

Its source-native starting point can be:

```text
nearly homogeneous field
+ local fluctuations
+ fixed reaction / diffusion law
```

with no completed organizer / pattern / object position supplied in advance.

Landge et al. (2020) explicitly emphasize that reaction–diffusion systems can generate self-organized patterns **in the absence of initial asymmetries**, thereby generating de novo positional information, and can regenerate after perturbation.

Reference:

- Landge, A. N., Jordan, B. M., Diego, X. & Müller, P. (2020). *Pattern formation mechanisms of self-organizing reaction-diffusion systems*. Developmental Biology 460(1):2–11. DOI `10.1016/j.ydbio.2019.10.031`.

Meinhardt (2012) gives the stronger classical activator–inhibitor formulation:

```text
local self-enhancement
+
long-range / lateral inhibition
-> symmetry breaking
-> local high-activation region / organizer
-> spatial polarity / positional differentiation
```

Reference:

- Meinhardt, H. (2012). *Turing's theory of morphogenesis of 1952 and the subsequent discovery of the crucial role of local self-enhancement and long-range inhibition*. Interface Focus 2(4):407–416. DOI `10.1098/rsfs.2011.0097`.

These sources are used here only as strongest-neighbor pressure. They are not a Pipeline-1 claim that biological morphogenesis is universally Turing-like.

## 1. Source-native activator–inhibitor structure

The classical two-component architecture can be stated without SRT translation:

```text
activator X
-> promotes its own local production
-> promotes production of inhibitor Y

inhibitor Y
-> spreads more broadly / rapidly
-> suppresses activator production around the emerging peak

small fluctuation
-> locally amplified activation
-> coupled surrounding inhibition
-> stable spatial peak / stripe / polarity
```

The important point for the present audit is causal coupling:

```text
local enhancement
and
relative surrounding suppression
```

are not two unrelated observations.

The activator–inhibitor loop generates them together.

In the classical model, removing the inhibitory relation destroys the mechanism that limits / spaces activator maxima; the bilateral activation–inhibition relation is load-bearing for that particular pattern-forming architecture.
