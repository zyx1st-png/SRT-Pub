## 1. Is `everything is Selection` empty?

It would be empty if no downstream distinction could fail independently.

Current architecture avoids this because:

```text
Selection event can occur without durable history;
history can occur without One formation;
One can form without Stable-ISP standing;
Stable ISP can exist without Bearer;
Bearer does not automatically establish Agency;
relation-transforming Selection does not automatically establish legitimacy.
```

Therefore the downstream concepts retain counterfactual and classificatory content.

Required permanent rule:

```text
Selection-totality is ontology routing, never a sufficient proof premise.
```

## 2. Does monism imply pan-agency or pan-consciousness?

No, if `Selection` remains depsychologized.

Primitive Selection is subjectless actualising differentiation / generativity. Attention, intention, prospective self-exposure, agency, subjecthood and phenomenality are separately gated Selection organizations.

Therefore:

```text
all reality as Selection
-/> all reality as agent;
-/> all reality as Bearer;
-/> all reality as subject;
-/> all reality as conscious.
```

Reference Dynamics must be repaired because its current `Fundamental Attention` wording is the main canonical backdoor for this collapse.

## 3. Does Selection-totality make ought primitive?

No, if the accepted O0/O1/O2 split is retained.

Correct route:

```text
primitive Selection
= no moral content;

formed position / relation
= indexed generative comparison becomes possible;

relation-transforming Selection
= constitutive relation constraints become possible;

stronger agency
= answerability becomes possible;

```

Selection is the source-condition of normativity because Selection can reorganize the conditions of Selection, not because occurrence is already good.

No.

Previous:


```text

not two temporally ordered or ontically separate mechanisms.
```

This is a unifying reinterpretation, not a reversal.
