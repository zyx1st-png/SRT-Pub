# GRG G0-G11 retyping — internal red-team Pass 1

## 0. Scope

This pass attacks the new typed architecture before it replaces the old discovery scaffold.

## 1. Risk: G0-G2 are moved upstream too aggressively

If G0-G2 are removed from the relation layer entirely, GRG may lose the ability to represent domain-specific openness, exclusion, support and background structure.

Resolution:

~~~text
upstream foundational role
!= forbidden domain realization
~~~

C0/C1 should not be ordinary relation families, but domain records may instantiate typed openness / exclusion / support mechanisms where source-native evidence warrants them.

Verdict: RETAIN RETYPING + preserve realization channel.

## 2. Risk: R-HIST becomes too broad

Combining G3 and G4 could collapse retained efficacy, causal re-entry and recurrent reconstitution.

Resolution:

R-HIST must keep at least two burdens:

~~~text
R-HIST-a = retained efficacy / later conditioning
R-HIST-b = recurrent reconstitution / recurring conditioning role
~~~

and should preserve R1 imprint / scaffold / causal-reentry distinctions.

Verdict: RETAIN FAMILY, REQUIRE SUBTYPES.

## 3. Risk: R-LOC smuggles One back into every domain

Even after generalization, local generative locus could become a disguised SRT One.

Resolution:

R-LOC must be domain-neutral. A source-native individuated unit, attractor, institution or organism does not become SRT One by GRG mapping alone.

Verdict: RETAIN + HARD SRT-SPECIALIZATION GUARD.

## 4. Risk: R-POS is not independent

Operative positionality may be fully downstream of locus formation in many systems.

Current evidence does not justify calling R-POS primitive.

Resolution:

~~~text
R-POS = CORE CANDIDATE / DEPENDENCY OPEN
~~~

Do not yet decide whether R-POS is primitive, derivative or cross-cutting.

## 5. Risk: R-RECON becomes generic feedback

Any dynamical system changes later conditions in some weak sense.

Resolution:

R-RECON admission must identify a typed conditioning field / relation structure and show that the current process changes later accessibility / transition / viability / differentiation conditions through that field.

Ordinary next-state dependence is insufficient.

Verdict: RETAIN AS CORE CANDIDATE WITH STRONG ADMISSION.

## 6. Risk: G7 split loses consequence exposure

Moving G7a into R-HIST can erase the stronger question of consequences returning to the same continuing locus.

Resolution:

Keep a typed split:

~~~text
CONSEQ-H = generic consequence -> later conditioning
CONSEQ-P = position / locus-indexed consequence return
CONSEQ-B = same-One prospective exposure, Bearer-facing where paid
~~~

Only the first is automatically part of R-HIST.

Verdict: SPLIT MORE FINELY; do not collapse exposure into history.

## 7. Risk: expectation layer is detached from relation content

If E-B is merely a separate ledger, GRG could become a descriptive grammar plus arbitrary prediction layer.

Resolution:

Every mature relation candidate should specify what expectation follows from the relation's own typed dependencies.

~~~text
relation -> expectation
must be derivationally traceable
~~~

E-B is downstream in architecture but mandatory as a maturity burden for strong grammar claims.

Verdict: RETAIN SPLIT + REQUIRE TRACEABILITY.

## 8. Risk: horizontal / vertical classification becomes subjective

Because classification is question-relative, one could relabel a failed horizontal model as vertical.

Resolution:

Every retyping must declare:

- research question;
- stabilized objectification;
- what is being treated as given;
- what formation / reconstruction is reopened;
- what evidence would distinguish the vertical claim from a richer horizontal model.

Verdict: ADD V/H DECLARATION TO FUTURE RELATION SCHEMA.

## 9. Risk: composition families are just graph words

C-MULTI and C-XSC could become empty labels.

Resolution:

Composition records must specify the component relation families being composed and what new dependency burden appears at the composite level.

Verdict: RETAIN ONLY AS TYPED COMPOSITION OPERATORS.

## 10. Risk: L-REFLEX mixes world relation and research governance

Science and institutions are world processes, while GRG revision is programme governance.

Resolution:

Separate:

~~~text
L-REFLEX-W = reflexive learning as a world/civilizational phenomenon
L-REFLEX-M = GRG methodology for revising its own grammar
~~~

The programme may compare them, but should not identify them.

Verdict: SPLIT META / WORLD REALIZATION.

## 11. Risk: formation cannot stay open forever

If formation is only a cross-cutting field, GRG may lack a way to ask how organization first becomes stable enough to matter.

Resolution:

Keep R-FORM as an explicit OPEN candidate rather than removing it.

Required discriminator for author adjudication:

> Can every useful formation question be represented as the formation field of another relation, or are there recurring formation dependencies that deserve their own family?

Verdict: OPEN IS CORRECT FOR NOW.

## 12. Risk: grammar becomes only taxonomy

A typed set of relation families is not yet grammar.

Resolution:

GRG v0.x must eventually add:

~~~text
composition syntax
compatibility constraints
dependency ordering
transformation / retyping operations
expectation derivation
revision semantics
~~~

before claiming a mature grammar.

Verdict: CURRENT WORK = ARCHITECTURE OF GRAMMAR, NOT COMPLETED GRAMMAR.

## 13. Required refinements

RT-G1: R-HIST must preserve a/b subtype separation.

RT-G2: R-POS status becomes CORE CANDIDATE / DEPENDENCY OPEN.

RT-G3: consequence return splits into generic, position-indexed and Bearer-facing forms.

RT-G4: E-B remains downstream but is a required maturity burden for strong relation claims.

RT-G5: future relation schema needs explicit horizontal / vertical declaration.

RT-G6: composition families require named component relations + new burden.

RT-G7: L-REFLEX splits world realization from GRG methodological self-revision.

## 14. Verdict

~~~text
G0-G11 retyping direction = RETAIN
typed architecture = RETAIN + REFINE
G7 bundle retirement = RETAIN
R-HIST merge = RETAIN WITH SUBTYPES
R-POS primitive status = OPEN
R-FORM = OPEN
grammar completion = NOT CLAIMED
canonical consequence = NONE
~~~
