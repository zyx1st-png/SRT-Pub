# Selection / Articulation — Initial External Novelty Audit (2026-08-28)

> **Boundary:** initial external architecture audit, not exhaustive literature review. It is sufficient to block several unsafe novelty claims, but it does not establish that the remaining SRT package is unique. No canonical promotion follows from this file.

## 0. Executive result

The following claims are **not safely novel** and must not be presented as SRT discoveries:

```text
many become one
individuals are generated rather than primitive
subject/object or relata need not pre-exist the differentiating relation/process
boundaries can be enacted / generated rather than simply found
actuality can be treated as a becoming / decision from potentiality
history can sediment into habits / regularities / laws
process can precede enduring thinghood
```

The strongest prior-art pressures found in this pass are:

1. **Whitehead** — concrescence, `the many become one`, actuality as `decision amid potentiality`, actual occasions becoming data for future occasions, process as constitution of actual entities.
2. **Barad** — relata do not pre-exist relations; object / measuring agency and boundaries emerge through intra-action / agential cut; no inevitable human or prior individual agent is required.
3. **Simondon** — pre-individual potential precedes the individual; the individual is the result / phase of individuation rather than its starting point; individuation concerns the genesis of relations and units.
4. **Peirce** — natural regularities and laws as evolved habits; matter associated with highly congealed habit; possibility / actuality / habit form a deep evolutionary metaphysical grammar.
5. **Process philosophy generally** — static individuals need not be primitive; enduring selves and things can be recurrently constituted processes.

The remaining SRT novelty candidate is therefore **architectural rather than slogan-level**.

Current residual package:

```text
A. primitive actualisation is selection-first but does not require a prior chooser;
B. primitive actualisation does not itself imply persistence, objecthood, bearerhood, subjecthood, or phenomenality;
C. historical efficacy is downstream of occurrence and can sediment into later-selection constraints;
D. object individuation and bearer individuation are explicitly non-equivalent;
E. bearer admission requires consequence-return / history-writeback / non-outsourcing pressures beyond re-identifiable objecthood;
F. selector / subject position can be historically constituted without being primitive;
G. experiencer / phenomenality remains an additional open burden rather than being projected into all primitive actualisation;
H. the whole dependency structure is intended to support downstream empirical discrimination without treating P3/P4 success as proof of P0.
```

This package is **potentially distinctive**, but not yet established novel.

---

## 1. Whitehead — strongest pressure on `many -> one` and actualisation

### Prior art

Whitehead's process metaphysics treats actual entities / actual occasions as processes of concrescence. The canonical compression is:

```text
the many become one, and are increased by one
```

His mature metaphysics also states, in effect:

```text
actuality = decision amid potentiality
```

and treats completed actual occasions as causally available data for later occasions.

This is dangerously close to any SRT claim phrased as:

```text
potentiality
-> choice / decision
-> actual unity
-> future conditioning
```

### Unsafe SRT claims blocked

Do not claim:

```text
SRT uniquely discovers that many become one
SRT uniquely discovers that actuality is a selection from potentiality
SRT uniquely discovers that completed becoming conditions future becoming
```

### Residual SRT distinction candidate

Whitehead makes experience / feeling fundamental to actual occasions and develops a panexperientialist ontology. SRT currently refuses the inference:

```text
primitive Selection
-> bearer
-> experiencer
```

and explicitly holds:

```text
Object !-> Bearer !-> Selector !-> Subject !-> Experiencer
```

Therefore one possible SRT-specific difference is:

> **actualisation is primitive without phenomenalizing the primitive actualiser.**

This difference is substantive only if SRT can preserve the actualisation grammar without covertly importing a Whitehead-like subjective aim, feeling, or proto-experience.

---

## 2. Barad — strongest pressure on determinate articulation / generated boundary

### Prior art

Agential realism rejects pre-existing relata as the basis of ordinary interaction. Intra-action is used precisely to express that:

```text
relata / objects / agencies do not pre-exist the relation
```

Agential cuts enact determinate separability and generate locally meaningful boundaries / distinctions. Secondary literature also emphasizes that `agential` does not require a pre-existing human agent.

This directly overlaps the articulation proposal:

```text
one / many / boundary / relation
are not necessarily primitive
```

### Unsafe SRT claims blocked

Do not claim:

```text
SRT uniquely discovers that boundaries are generated
SRT uniquely discovers that relata need not precede relations
SRT uniquely discovers that determinate entities can arise without a prior human chooser
```

### Residual SRT distinction candidate

The potential difference is not `cut versus selection` as vocabulary. It would have to be a dependency-level difference, such as:

```text
primitive actualisation
!= persistence
!= historical closure
!= bearer
!= experiencer
```

plus SRT's explicit irreversible-history and non-jump architecture.

This difference remains to be demonstrated rather than asserted.

---

## 3. Simondon — strongest pressure on pre-individual -> individual

### Prior art

Simondon explicitly treats the individual as downstream of individuation and develops the `pre-individual` as a potentialized condition for future individuations. The theory is designed to avoid treating the already-finished individual as the explanatory starting point.

### Unsafe SRT claims blocked

Do not claim:

```text
SRT uniquely places individuation before the individual
SRT uniquely starts from a pre-individual field of potentiality
SRT uniquely treats subject / object as generated differentiations
```

### Residual SRT distinction candidate

The candidate difference is that SRT gives the upstream transition a specific Selection-first role and then separates:

```text
occurrence
persistence
object re-identifiability
bearer consequence closure
selector / subject position
phenomenal experiencer
```

The novelty burden is therefore on the **typed dependency ladder**, not on pre-individuality itself.

---

## 4. Peirce — strongest pressure on habit / matter / law hardening

### Prior art

Peirce treats nature as evolving habits rather than a static inventory governed by timeless deterministic law. Natural laws themselves can be read as highly entrenched habits, and matter is associated with extremely congealed habit.

This directly overlaps any SRT narrative:

```text
repeated history
-> habit
-> hardened physical regularity
```

### Unsafe SRT claims blocked

Do not claim:

```text
SRT uniquely interprets matter as habit
SRT uniquely interprets law as historical hardening
SRT uniquely claims that nature's regularities emerge through accumulated history
```

### Residual SRT distinction candidate

The possible SRT increment is the attempt to derive habit as a downstream effect of Selection actualisation plus effective trace, while preserving:

```text
occurrence without persistence is possible
```

and using bearer-specific consequence ownership as a further gate rather than treating all hardened habit as subject-like.

Again, this is a package distinction, not a slogan-level novelty claim.

---

## 5. Process philosophy generally — pressure on `thing = recurrent process`

Modern process philosophy already rejects fully determinate static individuals as the only proper ontological starting point and allows persons / selves / objects to be dynamically and recursively constituted.

Therefore statements such as:

```text
object is process
self is ongoing integration
individual is continuously constituted
whole and components mutually constitute one another
```

are background convergence, not SRT novelty.

## 7. The most important conceptual correction

The workline should stop asking:

```text
Is “many become one” the deep new definition of Selection?
```

Whitehead makes that route too occupied, and it is ontologically too narrow anyway.

The stronger question is:

> **What does SRT preserve or discriminate once one subtracts Whiteheadian concrescence, Baradian agential cut, Simondonian individuation, Peircean habit, and generic process ontology?**

Current answer:

```text
not the existence of process
not the generation of unity
not the generation of boundaries
not pre-individual potentiality
not history becoming habit

but possibly:
selection-first primitive actualisation
+ strict occurrence / persistence separation
+ non-phenomenal primitive floor
+ typed object / bearer / selector / subject / experiencer non-jump
+ bearer-specific consequence-return and non-outsourcing
+ historical constitution of selection-position
+ explicit downstream empirical-discrimination discipline
```

This is the package to pressure-test next.

## 8. Implication for the consciousness Perspective

The manuscript should **not** foreground:

> `Many become one` as the SRT revolution.

That invites an immediate Whitehead objection.

It should also avoid presenting:

> `boundaries are enacted rather than pre-given`

as uniquely SRT; that invites Barad / Simondon objections.

A safer and potentially stronger Collection-facing claim is:

> **Consciousness science often moves from an already individuated neural system directly to phenomenal explanation. A Selection-first architecture separates at least three burdens that process / individuation traditions have often treated differently: the formation of a re-identifiable object/system, the formation of a continuing consequence-bearing selection-position, and the still-open admission of phenomenal presence.**

This framing acknowledges the process / individuation lineage while locating SRT's candidate contribution in the non-jump architecture and its neural consequences.

## 9. Next external audit

Priority should now move away from general process ontology toward the exact residual package:

1. **Enactivism / autonomy / organizational closure** — does it already provide consequence-bearing bearerhood rather than mere organismic identity?
2. **Damasio / interoceptive self models** — do they already implement `from here` as organism-indexed consequence integration?
3. **Unity of consciousness** — is object / subject / phenomenal unity already arranged as a generative non-jump architecture?
4. **Personal identity / fission / branching** — what prior work already distinguishes information inheritance from historical ownership?
5. **Neural models of consciousness** — which theories explicitly state or silently presuppose the bearer unit whose consciousness they model?

Only after these five checks should the Collection-facing novelty claim be frozen.
