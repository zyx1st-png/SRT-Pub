# Cycle-2 strengthened vertical claim — independent `S / P` criteria prepass

> **Role:** answer the first ordered question left by the Bowler/Todd realisation-readiness audit: can the persisting Selection-position `P` and the claimed sedimented foreground/background structure `S` be specified without defining each other?
>
> **Status:** machine-side audit construction only. The labels and criteria below are **C / NOT AUTHOR-ADJUDICATED**, non-canonical, and may be discarded. They do not rewrite the 2026-09-07 author claim.
>
> **Main result in advance:** a non-circular separation is possible only if `S` is split into **historical sedimentation provenance** and **current causal stratification**. This exposes a previously hidden genesis-versus-maintenance ambiguity in the strengthened claim.

---

## 0. Fixed source constraints

### 0.1 `P` cannot simply mean `S`

Canonical L0 defines:

```text
position
= finite conditions under which Selection occurs
```

and separately defines anchoring / stability in terms of retained traces, maintenance conditions and reusable historical structure.

Therefore there is enough canonical room to ask whether a finite, persisting causal locus exists **without first defining that locus by foreground/background stratification**.

### 0.2 `S` cannot mean fixed foreground content

The author-accepted bearer cross-read explicitly allows:

```text
foreground/background contents may change
```

while the non-flat / stratified relation is reconstituted.

Therefore an audit criterion for `S` must concern a **persistent relational asymmetry / partition role**, not persistence of one winner, one component, one representation or one ranked item.

### 0.3 Event-level two-sidedness is not enough

Canonical Selection already contains event-level manifestation / latency differentiation.

Thus:

```text
one event has foreground/background
```

cannot count as the strengthened sedimentation criterion. `S` must survive beyond the event and be detectable at the sedimentation layer.

---

## 1. Bowler forces a second split inside `S`

The readiness audit used one label `S` for the strengthened claim. Bowler makes that too coarse.

Separate:

```text
S_H* = historical sedimentation provenance
       prior Selection-event differentiations leave a persistent,
       history-conditioned asymmetric residue

S_C* = current causal stratification
       the present organisation contains a non-flat causal partition / geometry
       that differentially conditions later continuations
```

Stars mark audit constructs only.

The distinction matters because Bowler supports the architecture:

```text
history H
-> current scaffold S_C
-> future strategy F
```

while a current dynamical scaffold can be manipulated / partly imposed without recreating the original shaping history.

Therefore:

```text
S_H*
!=
S_C*
```

and a claim about the necessity of historical sedimentation cannot be tested as though it were identical to a claim about the necessity of the current scaffold.

---

## 2. Candidate independent criterion for `P*`

Define only for audit purposes:

> **`P*` = a re-identifiable finite causal locus from which later Selection-like transitions repeatedly issue.**

A candidate satisfies `P*` when all four conditions hold:

```text
P1 FINITE-CONDITION:
   the relevant transition occurs from a bounded condition bundle,
   not a God-view / unconditioned state;

P2 CAUSAL-ORIGIN:
   changing / removing that bundle changes the distribution or
   admissibility of the next transition under a locked probe family;

P3 RECURRENCE:
   after at least one intervening event / perturbation / component change,
   a causally equivalent locus can be re-identified and later transitions
   again issue from it;

P4 RE-IDENTIFICATION WITHOUT S:
   equivalence is judged by a declared transition / intervention signature
   within tolerance, not by whether foreground/background sedimentation
   is present and not by exact microstate identity.
```

This is deliberately weak. It is meant to admit mature rival systems as `P*` candidates rather than define SRT victory into the target.

It does **not** establish:

```text
Bearer
Stable ISP
subjecthood
consequence bearing
SRT ontology
```

---

## 3. Candidate independent criterion for `S_H*`

Define historical sedimentation without referring to persistence of `P*`:

> **`S_H*` = a history-conditioned asymmetric carry-over produced by prior event-level differentiation, detectable after the immediate event has ended.**

Minimum criteria:

```text
H1 HISTORY CONTRAST:
   two histories differ in prior foreground/background assignments
   or equivalent event-level differential realization;

H2 POST-EVENT PERSISTENCE:
   after the immediate event/readout is removed, the histories leave
   distinguishable retained structure or response disposition;

H3 ASYMMETRIC CONTINUATION EFFECT:
   under a locked neutral probe family, the retained difference produces
   non-uniform accessibility / influence / cost / transition effects
   across candidate continuations;

H4 CURRENT-INPUT CONTROL:
   the effect is not exhausted by a current exogenous cue that simply
   tells the system which option to favor;

H5 NO P REQUIREMENT:
   detection does not require showing that a persisting Selection-position
   exists or continues.
```

`S_H*` does not require the same concrete foreground item to remain foreground. Permutations of content are allowed if the historical process leaves a persistent non-flat relational organization.

This criterion is intentionally broader than SRT distinctiveness. Ordinary learning systems may satisfy it. Its job is only to make `P* ∧ ¬S_H*` intelligible.

## 4. Candidate independent criterion for `S_C*`

Define the present causal role independently of its historical origin:

> **`S_C*` = a currently realised non-flat causal geometry in which candidate continuations do not enter later transition on equivalent terms.**

Minimum criteria:

```text
C1 CURRENT NON-FLATNESS:
   under a locked probe family, candidate continuations show a stable
   differential in causal influence / accessibility / transition threshold;

C2 ROLE-LEVEL STRUCTURE:
   the criterion does not require a fixed component or fixed foreground item;

C3 INTERVENTION SENSITIVITY:
   a nominated manipulation can alter the differential geometry while
   holding ordinary task inputs / reward conditions as constant as feasible;

C4 PROVENANCE NEUTRALITY:
   the same current geometry counts as S_C* whether learned, transplanted,
   engineered, imposed or otherwise reached;

C5 NO P REQUIREMENT:
   identifying S_C* does not use survival / recurrence of P* as its definition.
```

This is the object relevant to Todd-style mechanism realisation. It is likely to be heavily paid by recurrent / attractor / control accounts; that is a feature of the audit, not a defect.

## 5. The strengthened claim now exposes two different necessity questions

The 2026-09-07 sentence currently runs roughly:

```text
repeated event-level stratification
-> sedimented persisting foreground/background asymmetry
-> later Selection repeatedly resumes at the same position
```

Bowler/Todd show that at least two logically different burdens are compressed here.

### V-G* — genesis / provenance necessity candidate

```text
endogenously formed P*
requires S_H*
```

Counterexample shape:

```text
P*_endo ∧ ¬S_H*
```

Question:

> Can a persisting selection-like locus form endogenously while prior choices leave no persistent continuation-specific foreground/background anisotropy?

This is primarily a **necessity / ontogenesis** question.

### V-M* — current maintenance / reconstitution necessity candidate

```text
continued / reconstituted P*
requires S_C*
```

Counterexample shape:

```text
P*_continue ∧ ¬S_C*
```

Question:

> Can later transitions repeatedly issue from the re-identified locus when the current non-flat causal stratification is selectively flattened or removed?

This is the better target for a **Todd-style mechanism intervention**.

Important:

```text
V-G* and V-M*
are machine decompositions
!= author-confirmed split
```

The point is not to rewrite the claim, but to prevent a history-provenance test from masquerading as a current-mechanism test.

## 6. A scope fracture that must be exposed before necessity testing

Literal canonical `position` is broad:

```text
any finite conditions under which Selection occurs
```

The strengthened author language, however, repeatedly speaks of:

```text
forming
stabilising
converging
sedimenting
maintaining / reconstituting
```

This leaves an unresolved quantifier / scope fork.

### Scope A — all persisting Selection-positions

If the strengthened necessity ranges over **all** persisting positions, then an externally prepared or engineered finite controller/locus is an immediate pressure case:

```text
P* exists and repeatedly issues transitions;
its present architecture was not sedimented from its own prior Selection history;
therefore ¬S_H* by provenance.
```

That is a candidate:

```text
P* ∧ ¬S_H*
```

and makes V-G* look too strong unless the external history is somehow counted as the relevant sedimentation history.

### Scope B — endogenously formed / reconstituted Selection-positions

If the intended claim is only about positions whose **own formation / reconstitution** is at issue, the external prepared-controller case is out of scope.

Then the hard counterexample must be:

```text
P*_endo ∧ ¬S_H*
```

inside a system whose locus genuinely emerges rather than being supplied by the modeller.

Current source status:

```text
selection-position rather than all stable existence: AUTHOR-FIXED
all P* vs endogenous/reconstituted P*: NOT EXPLICITLY FIXED
```

This prepass does not decide the fork.

## 7. Candidate `P* ∧ ¬S_H*` pressure architectures

These are conceptual probes, not claims that the cases actually succeed.

### X0 — externally prepared recurrent locus

```text
fixed / engineered finite decision architecture
-> repeated transitions from a stable locus
-> no own historical sedimentation required to create that architecture
```

Use:

```text
Scope-A falsification pressure
```

Weakness:

```text
may be outside the intended endogenous-formation scope
```

### X1 — scaffold transplantation / reconstruction

```text
history-shaped system A produces current scaffold S_C*;
instantiate / transplant an equivalent S_C* into fresh system B;
B supports repeated P*-like continuation without A's S_H* provenance.
```

Use:

```text
tests provenance necessity for continuation / maintenance
```

Bowler makes this family especially relevant because present dynamical structure can mediate historical efficacy and can be partly imposed.

Weakness:

```text
does not show endogenous genesis of P* without S_H*;
it mainly separates provenance from current causal organisation.
```

### X2 — symmetry-preserving endogenous locus

Target architecture:

```text
an initially symmetric / uncommitted adaptive system
endogenously converges to a persistent collective transition locus P*;
individual event foreground/background assignments occur,
but their identities do not leave a persistent differential continuation bias;
only a global / permutation-symmetric maintenance variable is retained.
```

Desired result:

```text
P*_endo
+
no persistent history-conditioned anisotropy across continuation classes
= candidate ¬S_H*
```

This is the strongest conceptual G0 route because it respects endogenous formation.

But it has a strict failure condition:

> If the allegedly symmetric global variable still creates a durable non-flat continuation partition under the locked probe family, the system has `S_H*` under the criterion and X2 fails as a counterexample.

No concrete mature implementation is nominated in this file.

## 8. Bowler/Todd consequence for the three audit objects

### `P*`

A functionally / causally re-identifiable locus can be defined without mentioning foreground/background. This pays the **logical anti-circularity** requirement at machine-audit level.

### `S_H*`

Historical asymmetric carry-over can likewise be defined without using persistence of the locus. This makes a necessity counterexample logically expressible.

### `S_C*`

Current non-flat causal geometry is independently expressible, but ordinary recurrent dynamics can readily realise it. Therefore identifying `S_C*` does not create an SRT mechanism residual.

Todd burden remains:

```text
S_C* detected
!= SRT realiser identified
```

A distinct mechanism claim would still require a state partition / causal role and held-out intervention not exhausted by the rival recurrent / transductive account.

## 10. Main theoretical gain

The strengthened claim should no longer be treated as one undifferentiated statement of "vertical structure matters".

The pressure surface is now:

```text
event-level two-sided Selection
        |
        v
S_H*  historical sedimentation provenance
        |
        v
S_C*  current causal stratification / non-flat continuation geometry
        |
        v
P*    re-identifiable finite causal locus from which later Selection issues
```

but none of the arrows is granted necessity by this file.

Bowler primarily pressures:

```text
S_H* -> S_C*
```

by showing that current causal structure can carry historical efficacy and can in part be manipulated independently of original provenance.

Todd primarily pressures:

```text
S_C* -> P*
```

by requiring a realiser and held-out causal discriminator rather than a second description of the same scaffold.

This is a cleaner architecture than asking one experiment to prove all of:

```text
history
+ sedimentation
+ verticality
+ position
+ mechanism
+ SRT distinctiveness
```

at once.
