# Consciousness × multilevel gating × action field — strongest-neighbor crosswalk

> Boundary: machine analysis after explicit author acceptance of C. This audit does not establish that consciousness only acts through gates, that neural synchrony is the field, or that Levin-style bioelectric morphogenesis is mechanistically identical to cognition.

---

## 0. Executive result

The new author direction is coherent with several mature scientific architectures, but no single mature account exactly equals the proposed synthesis.

Strongly neighbor-paid components:

~~~text
abstract high-level goal without detailed motor specification
-> hierarchical motor control

parallel action candidate formation + selection during execution
-> affordance competition

release / suppression of downstream motor programs
-> basal ganglia action-selection / disinhibition models

higher levels bias lower-level policy competition
-> hierarchical active inference

conscious content becomes broadly available to multiple processors
-> Global Neuronal Workspace / conscious access

large-scale physiological control coordinates local components toward global form
-> developmental bioelectric / morphogenetic control
~~~

The current potentially useful SRT-facing synthesis is narrower:

> **A conscious intention may be a foregrounded higher-order gate relation / constraint whose causal significance lies in reconfiguring a multilevel gating field, while lower-level systems generate the detailed action.**

This is NOT established as novel and is under heavy composite-neighbor pressure.

## 1. Hierarchical motor control pays the non-micromanagement burden

Representative source:
- Merel et al. 2019, Hierarchical motor control in mammals and machines, Nature Communications 10:5489. DOI 10.1038/s41467-019-13239-6.

Source-native pressure:
- sufficiently abstract goals or intentions can be communicated without fully specifying detailed movement;
- lower-level systems can retain partial autonomy;
- many different low-level movements can realize the same high-level goal;
- motor control often exploits synergies rather than independent muscle-by-muscle control.

Therefore the cup example does not by itself distinguish SRT.

Strongly absorbed:

~~~text
I intend to pick up the cup
without consciously computing
joint torques / muscle forces / feedback details.
~~~

Open:

> what exactly changes in the lower hierarchy when a conscious intention becomes operative?

---

## 2. Affordance competition pays simultaneous specification + selection

Representative source:
- Cisek 2007, Cortical mechanisms of action selection: the affordance competition hypothesis. Phil Trans R Soc B 362:1585–1599. DOI 10.1098/rstb.2007.2054.

Source-native pressure:
- multiple potential actions are specified in parallel;
- selection and action specification unfold simultaneously;
- sensory information continues to shape the process during overt action;
- a complete preplanned action trajectory is not required.

This is very close to the author's intuition that action emerges from a distributed field rather than from a complete conscious motor script.

But affordance competition by itself does not identify:
- conscious gate-objectification;
- Bearer-indexed consequence writeback;
- L0/L1/L2 analytic recursion;
- a general gate-geometry ontology.

---

## 3. Basal ganglia pay a concrete action-gating implementation

Representative sources:
- Grillner 2024, The Basal Ganglia Downstream Control of Action – An Evolutionarily Conserved Strategy.
- Mink / basal-ganglia action-selection tradition.

Source-native pattern:

~~~text
tonic inhibition of downstream motor centres
+ selective disinhibition of one programme
+ continued inhibition of competitors
-> released action
~~~

This is a genuine biological gate-like mechanism.

Important guard:

~~~text
basal-ganglia disinhibition
!= SRT Gate by definition
!= whole cognitive field
!= consciousness
~~~

It is one implementation family that demonstrates how high-level selection can control behavior by changing access to lower motor generators rather than specifying every motor detail.

## 4. Hierarchical active inference is a very strong composite absorber

Representative source:
- Pezzulo, Rigoli & Friston 2018, Hierarchical Active Inference: A Theory of Motivated Control.

Source-native pressure:
- goal-directed action is organized over multiple hierarchical timescales;
- higher levels contextualize and bias lower-level policy competition;
- higher levels can set preferred outcomes / trajectories that lower levels realize;
- lower sensorimotor levels remain responsible for detailed policy implementation;
- motivation and control can interact across levels.

This is extremely close to:

~~~text
high-level expectation / goal
-> changes lower-level admissibility / competition
-> low-level action generation.
~~~

Therefore the current SRT route cannot claim novelty from hierarchical top-down constraint alone.

Potential pressure point:

> SRT gate-objectification emphasizes that a normally background constraint itself may become foregrounded, phenomenally or reflectively objectified, and thereby become explicitly revisable.

Whether this is distinct from metacognitive / active-inference control remains OPEN.

## 5. Global Neuronal Workspace pressures the consciousness side

Representative source:
- Mashour et al. 2020, Conscious Processing and the Global Neuronal Workspace Hypothesis.

GNW source-native burden:
- many specialized processors operate unconsciously in parallel;
- selected information can undergo amplification / ignition;
- conscious content becomes globally available to working memory, report, decision and motor systems.

This strongly absorbs:

~~~text
conscious content
-> wider downstream causal availability.
~~~

But GNW mainly answers:

> what makes a content globally accessible?

The new author route asks a different but overlapping question:

> what if the content that becomes conscious is itself a higher-order gate / constraint, and global access permits that gate to be revised or to reconfigure other gates?

This difference is currently conceptual only.

Do not infer:

~~~text
global broadcast = gate reconfiguration
conscious access = agency
conscious content = top-down control in every case
~~~

## 6. New distinction: broadcast content vs gate-changing constraint

This is the sharpest machine-side bridge produced by the current author adjudication.

Two possibilities:

### BCAST — broadcast-only reading

~~~text
conscious intention
= information made globally available
-> existing control systems use it
~~~

### GATE — gate-reconfiguration reading

~~~text
conscious intention / objectification
= a higher-order constraint made foreground
-> changes which lower-level processes / candidates / corrections
   are admissible, amplified, suppressed or maintained
-> distributed execution changes
~~~

BCAST and GATE can coexist.

A useful future discriminator asks whether conscious access merely distributes a representation or causally changes the gating policy / geometry that determines lower-level selection.

This is not yet operationalized.

## 7. Neural synchronization is a coordination candidate, not the theory

Current neuroscience supports widespread use of temporal coordination, phase coupling and cross-frequency interactions as mechanisms associated with communication and coordination across neural populations.

Safe role:

~~~text
oscillatory coordination
= possible carrier / timing mechanism
  for cross-level gate coordination
~~~

Not licensed:

~~~text
EEG synchrony = cognitive field
EEG synchrony = consciousness
phase coupling = Selection
~~~

The key causal question is whether perturbing the coordination changes which gates can jointly constrain downstream processing.

## 8. Levin bioelectric morphogenesis is a powerful architecture analogy

Representative sources:
- Whited & Levin 2019, Bioelectrical controls of morphogenesis. DOI 10.1016/j.gde.2019.06.014.
- Levin et al. 2021, Bioelectric signaling: Reprogrammable circuits underlying embryogenesis, regeneration, and cancer. DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2021.02.034.

Source-native burden:
- cell collectives communicate through endogenous membrane-potential / ion-channel / gap-junction networks;
- large-scale bioelectric organization can influence downstream gene expression and cell behavior;
- local cells can participate in robust large-scale pattern outcomes without individually encoding the entire anatomical solution.

Structural analogy:

~~~text
large-scale physiological pattern
-> local component constraints
-> coordinated global outcome
~~~

Current cognitive candidate:

~~~text
multilevel gating configuration
-> local sensorimotor constraints
-> coordinated behavior
~~~

The analogy becomes especially interesting if both systems are understood as multi-scale control architectures rather than central command systems.

Mandatory non-identity:

~~~text
morphogenetic bioelectric state
!= EEG field
!= cognitive gate field
!= SRT L0
~~~

## 11. Strongest unresolved fork

The current author acceptance still leaves one subtle distinction unresolved.

### Reading C1 — conscious content IS the high-level gate

The foregrounded intention itself is the operative constraint.

### Reading C2 — conscious content OBJECTIFIES / EXPOSES a high-level gate

The underlying gate already constrains behavior; consciousness makes that gate available for global access, stabilization, revision or reconfiguration.

These are not identical.

C2 fits especially well with the existing 2026-09-25 sentence:

~~~text
successful gate recedes into background;
strained / consequential gate can become object;
reflective gate-objectification makes revision possible.
~~~

Machine recommendation:

> Treat C2 as the safer current architecture unless the author explicitly wants the stronger identity claim C1.

This preserves:
- unconscious gating;
- pre-reflective gating;
- conscious gate-objectification;
- reflective revision;
without making every conscious intention literally identical to a gate.
