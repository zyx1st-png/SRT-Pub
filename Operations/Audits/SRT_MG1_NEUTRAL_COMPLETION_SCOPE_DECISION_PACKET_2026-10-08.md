# MG-1 neutral-science completion scope — existing evidence, specific gaps and author decision packet

> **Verdict / scope:** This is a **M-layer machine recommendation for a future author decision**, not a new MG-1 owner completion rule, declaration of MG-1 COMPLETE, experiment/pre-registration plan or CN-3 gate change. The single CURRENT NEXT remains neutral cognition research under the 09-26 cognition programme. CN-3/toy execution stays HOLD with the inherited MG-1/MG-2, FR-ADV and separate author requirements. No canonical, HP-B, Bearer or Selection claim is reopened.

## 1. Recover the actual MG-1 owner burden before specifying a finish line

Programme §14 labels MG-1 **"multilevel gating composition / strongest-neighbor map."** It asks for comparison across eight mature mechanisms and five specific questions **before freezing a new experiment**. Its current text does **not** say all eight must be reproduced in code, must beat one another in a P1–P6 task, or that proving SRT novelty is a condition of completing this **mapping task**. CN-2 E2 and CN-3 have separate, stronger implementation, experimental and author gates.

The 09-26 author source §10 frames the broader live mechanism question as:

> How do gates distributed across levels become coordinated into one effective action / objectification field, and how can a coarse conscious intention reconfigure that field without specifying the detailed execution?

That is a **research question**, not an already-solved mechanism identity. The 09-26 MG crosswalk §11–13 specifies the controlling **C2'** interpretation: `foreground_obj` target may recruit/reweight already-formed gates; **gate objectification under consequential unresolved conflict is a different route**. It does not prove `foreground_phen`, `manifest_OF`, Bearer ownership, or unique SRT causation. An **audit completion** cannot be mistaken for having solved all those substantive questions.

"Documented" here means described in existing noncanonical crosswalk/bridge; **not experimentally confirmed by this audit**, exhaustive literature searched, or shown to reproduce the same effect on a common task.

|---|---|---|
| Hierarchical motor control | 09-26 MG crosswalk §1, Merel et al. 2019: abstract goal / low-level motor autonomy | Does not identify an extra conscious central controller; explain which high-level constraints are already known |

**Coverage assessment as of this packet:** the 8/8 owner labels have initial explicit coverage in the existing materials. **This is label coverage, not 8/8 scientific mechanism validation or completion.** Particularly underdeveloped are (i) source-native *control adjustment after conflict without conscious gate-objectification*, (ii) precision about the limits of temporal coordination as a mechanism, and (iii) a compact direct answer to each of the five MG-1 owner questions.

## 3. The strongest adjacent mechanism pressure that cannot be skipped

An MG-1 answer must not imply:

~~~text
local control repeatedly fails / conflict detected
=> a conscious Gate itself MUST become foreground_obj
=> correction only possible through this SRT route
~~~

**Source-native mature counterpressure:**

- **Botvinick et al. (2001), Conflict Monitoring and Cognitive Control** (Psychological Review 108:624–652; DOI 10.1037/0033-295X.108.3.624, PMID 11488380) reports computational modeling of conflict detection/control adjustment, including a feedback loop from conflict monitoring to cognitive control. The published modeling proposal is already a mechanism for adjustment. It does **not** by itself identify the presence/absence of phenomenality in each modeled control event; avoid claiming it experimentally proves unconsciousness.
- **Shenhav, Botvinick & Cohen (2013), The Expected Value of Control** (Neuron 79:217–240; DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2013.07.007, PMCID PMC3767969) offers a normative account of allocation of control according to estimated costs/benefits, without being a ready E2 P1–P6 prediction implementation merely by citation.
- **Cole, Laurent & Stocco (2013), Rapid Instructed Task Learning** (Cogn Affect Behav Neurosci 13:1–22; DOI 10.3758/s13415-012-0125-7, PMCID PMC3557598) documents flexible task reconfiguration from instruction: target-to-control does not require proving SRT Bearer or a special new gate objectification event.

**Concrete adjustment to MG-1 review:** identify the **same explanatory target** for (a) conflict monitoring/automatic control adjustment, (b) explicit reflective control/gate revision, and (c) GNW global access. Source-native accounts **may overlap or fully absorb** proposed machinery. The burden is to describe where the evidence stops, not to invent an obligatory residual.

## 4. Five owner questions — proposed auditable output rather than claimed answers

| Original §14 question | Bounded deliverable that could be independently checked | Existing status / reason not automatically COMPLETE |
|---|---|---|
| Q1. What explains abstract intention without detailed execution? | Name hierarchical motor, affordance and policy control mechanisms and their native successes | Substantially covered in MG crosswalk §§1–4; no unique SRT residue implied |
| Q2. What explains distributed gate coordination? | Distinguish actual coupling/control mechanisms, effective high-level model descriptions and analogy | Partially mapped; coordination source/measurement limits must be stated |
| Q3. What explains conscious access but **not** gate revision? | Separate GNW access from control-effective bias and report-confounded agency | Already strongly mapped in 09-26 bridge and #1109; no automatic ACCESS -> CONTROL identity |
| Q4. What explains gate revision without phenomenality? | Credit error-based/conflict-monitoring control adjustment, task-set changes and learning; say phenomenality is unmeasured where so | Important remaining source-native check; **do not claim a particular human control act was experimentally proven nonconscious from those articles alone** |
| Q5. What, if anything, is added by gate-objectification + recursive writeback? | Separate inherited mechanisms, alternative interpretation, falsifiable candidate consequence and **NO-GAIN/OPEN** status | Positive scientific increment still **NOT ESTABLISHED**; mapping audit may document OPEN / no-gain honestly instead of fabricating a win |

**Critical distinction:** answering the five questions at **bounded comparative-audit strength** is not proving five new mechanistic hypotheses. Whether a new empirical prediction exists belongs to a later separately authorized, same-task CN-2/E2 design and independent FR-ADV.

## 5. Two author alternatives — this audit selects NEITHER

**Option A — bounded neutral-science MG-1 source comparison completion (machine recommendation):**

MG-1 **review/audit** may be independently assessed for completion when it (1) treats all eight owner-named families at source-native strength, with analogy correctly typed; (2) answers each of Q1–Q5 with explicit evidence, translation, OPEN or no-gain disposition; (3) includes conflict-monitoring and instructed-control strongest-neighbor pressure; (4) distinguishes target objectification from reflective gate objectification and access from effective control without inferring HP-B/Bearer; (5) preserves distinct CN-2 E2 model/likelihood and CN-3 experiment gates; and (6) receives an independent content review. **Only the author could then expressly declare "MG-1 neutral review COMPLETE, bounded scope."**

This option does **not** require running eight simulations, discovering unique SRT predictions, showing full neural coupling causality, or completing E2; those may remain **genuine scientific OPEN questions**, and a bounded completion must *say* so.

**Option B — MG-1 completion also waits for specific mechanistic/empirical confrontation:**

Require at least one author-selected and independently vetted same-target computational or empirical discrimination between the proposed multilevel coordination/revision account and a strong existing control account **before MG-1 as a whole is considered complete**. This is a substantively stronger completion scope and may overlap with already separately held **CN-2 E2 / CN-3** work; it risks silently adding a novel gate if adopted without a precise author description of what is required and why.

**Machine recommendation: A** as a proportionate finish line for the pre-experiment *mapping* task. It is consistent with, but **not automatically entailed by**, the author's **separate MG-2 Option A** scope choice. Only a new author decision for MG-1 could make this controlling. The alternative is left open for author adjudication; **no owner programme or STATUS edit here**.
