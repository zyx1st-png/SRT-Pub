## 2. Rival 1 — standard reversible / unitary quantum dynamics

For a closed quantum system with unitary evolution:

```text
|Psi_1> = U |Psi_0>
```

the inverse transformation exists formally:

```text
|Psi_0> = U^dagger |Psi_1>.
```

This does not mean practical macroscopic reversal is easy. It does mean that **exact invertibility is part of the formal state dynamics** unless an additional non-unitary process is introduced.

Therefore IRR-B faces a clean structural pressure:

```text
if a globally unitary and exactly invertible process can instantiate genuine Selection,
IRR-B conflicts with that implementation;

if exact invertibility is preserved,
then the reversible portion must be classified as pre-Selection / premeasurement /
correlation formation rather than genuine Selection.
```

This is already more than a vocabulary difference because it constrains which physical processes may instantiate the P0 primitive.

## 3. Measurement reversal is not yet a refutation of IRR-B

Primary pressure sources:

- Korotkov & Jordan, “Undoing a Weak Quantum Measurement of a Solid-State Qubit,” *Physical Review Letters* 97, 166805 (2006), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevLett.97.166805`.
- Katz et al., “Reversal of the Weak Measurement of a Quantum State in a Superconducting Phase Qubit,” *Physical Review Letters* 101, 200401 (2008), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevLett.101.200401`.
- Cheong & Lee, “Balance Between Information Gain and Reversibility in Weak Measurement,” *Physical Review Letters* 109, 150402 (2012), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevLett.109.150402`.
- Schindler et al., “Undoing a Quantum Measurement,” *Physical Review Letters* 110, 070403 (2013), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.070403`.

These works establish important bounded facts:

```text
- weak measurement effects can be probabilistically undone;
- partial-collapse state changes can be conditionally reversed;
- measurement information / disturbance obeys reversibility tradeoffs;
- a projective measurement on a subsystem can be deterministically reversed
  using quantum error correction when information is redundantly encoded elsewhere.
```

They do **not** by themselves show:

```text
the complete physical state of system + ancilla + apparatus + controller +
environment has been returned to the exact pre-event state
with every causally effective residue erased.
```

Therefore local measurement undoing does not immediately falsify IRR-B.

### But the same fact creates a boundary-immunity danger

IRR-B cannot answer every subsystem reversal by expanding the boundary indefinitely after observing success. A future test must prospectively freeze the candidate complete-state boundary.

## 4. Strongest mature neighbor for intrinsic physical irreversibility — dynamical collapse

The strongest relevant scientific neighbor is not Whitehead, Barad or ordinary thermodynamics. It is the family of **objective-collapse / dynamical-reduction models** such as GRW / CSL.

Representative review:

- Bassi, Lochan, Satin, Singh & Ulbricht, “Models of wave-function collapse, underlying theories, and experimental tests,” *Reviews of Modern Physics* 85, 471 (2013), DOI `10.1103/RevModPhys.85.471`.

These models explicitly modify standard quantum dynamics with stochastic / nonlinear reduction processes. They therefore already provide a scientific architecture in which:

```text
microscopic / mesoscopic evolution
-> under specified stochastic reduction law
-> genuinely non-unitary event dynamics
-> predictions can differ from standard quantum mechanics.
```

Collapse models also expose the exact burden IRR-B currently lacks:

```text
TRIGGER:
when does the non-unitary event occur / become effective?

LAW:
what state transformation occurs?

SCALE / PARAMETERS:
how does effect strength depend on mass, size, time, particle number, etc.?

OBSERVABLE:
what interference / heating / noise / recurrence / restoration difference follows?
```

IRR-B currently supplies none of these as physical law.

Therefore it cannot claim scientific distinctiveness merely from:

```text
genuine actualisation is globally irreversible.
```

That architecture is already mature prior art in dynamical-reduction physics.

## 8. Recurrence pressure

Finite-dimensional unitary systems can exhibit quantum recurrence; special spectra can even permit exact recurrence.

This creates the same structural test:

```text
if a closed evolution returns exactly to the initial complete state,
then under IRR-B either:
- no genuine Selection occurred during the reversible cycle;
- or IRR-B is false for that implementation.
```

Observed recurrence by itself would not refute SRT unless Selection had been independently admitted during the interval.

Again the trigger problem is decisive.
