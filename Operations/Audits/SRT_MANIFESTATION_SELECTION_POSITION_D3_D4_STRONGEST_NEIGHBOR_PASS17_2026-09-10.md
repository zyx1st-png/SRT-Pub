## 3. Barad — stronger D4 payment than a generic "objects are not pregiven" summary

Source-native core used here:

- Karen Barad, *Meeting the Universe Halfway* (Duke University Press, 2007), agential realism / intra-action / agential cut.
- Publisher description: https://www.dukeupress.edu/meeting-the-universe-halfway
- Repository prior strongest-neighbor pass: `SRT_PREOBJECT_FOREGROUND_OBSERVABILITY_BARAD_STRONGEST_NEIGHBOR_PASS13_2026-09-10.md`.

Fair reconstruction:

```text
phenomena are not assembled from independently pregiven relata;
specific intra-actions enact determinate separability;
an agential cut produces determinate boundaries / properties within the phenomenon;
cutting is constitutive, not merely passive observation by a human subject.
```

### Payment

Barad strongly pays R2 and much of D4:

```text
determinate separability = achieved;
object/apparatus boundary = enacted within a phenomenon;
manifest distinction need not wait for a pre-existing conscious bearer.
```

This means SRT cannot claim unique ownership of:

```text
"determination is constitutive";
"objects do not simply pre-exist the relation";
"manifest boundaries are not passive readout".
```

### Stronger pressure on R3

Pass 15 treated Barad mainly as paying constitutive cut. Pass 17 tightens the pressure: a cut is not only a positive selection of one side; it is also an exclusion / differentiation that makes some distinctions determinate while others are not enacted in that phenomenon.

Therefore the SRT phrase:

```text
manifest foreground + relative backgrounding arise together
```

cannot count as distinct merely because it contains both a positive and a negative side. Barad already gives a serious constitutive account of inclusion/exclusion through a cut.

### Remaining gap

The current repository evidence does not yet establish that Barad alone gives the exact R4-R5 sequence:

```text
non-manifest side remains a structured, later-relevant reservoir
+
repeated cuts sediment a non-arbitrary later "from-where"
that itself constrains / generates subsequent constitutive differentiation.
```

That is a live comparative question, not a demonstrated SRT residual.

## 4. Simondon — the strongest new pressure in this pass

Primary / high-quality source anchors used here:

- Gilbert Simondon, *Individuation in Light of Notions of Form and Information*, English translation, University of Minnesota Press, 2020: https://www.upress.umn.edu/9780816680016/individuation-in-light-of-notions-of-form-and-information/
- Simondon research portal summary of preindividual being / retained preindividual reality / later individuation: https://www.simondon.fr/site/content/biography
- Repository prior Simondon passes and the A-class close-read already cited by Pass 15.

Fair reconstruction:

```text
the finished individual is not the explanatory starting point;
preindividual being is metastable and exceeds stable identity;
individuation resolves tensions / disparities through transduction;
individual and associated milieu emerge together;
individuation does not necessarily exhaust the preindividual;
retained preindividual reality can support later individuations.
```

### R1 payment: strong

Simondon strongly pays the before-individual burden without requiring a menu of finished possible individuals.

```text
preindividual != hidden set of already completed objects.
```

No SRT exclusivity remains at that slogan level.

### R2 payment: strong

Transductive individuation is genuinely genetic. It does not merely choose among already individuated alternatives. This is a direct D3 comparator for subjectless constitutive formation.

### R3 payment: stronger than Pass 15 recorded

The key pressure is not only "preindividual -> individual". In Simondon, individuation can generate the **individual and associated milieu together**. That makes the comparison structurally closer to:

```text
manifest foreground
+
relative background / milieu
co-differentiated in one formation process.
```

Therefore an SRT claim that foreground/background co-generation by itself is a unique dependency is currently too strong.

### R4 payment: strong candidate

Simondon also explicitly retains a charge of preindividual reality after individuation. This matters because it pressures the SRT idea that the backgrounded / not-currently-manifest side is not deleted and remains relevant to later formation.

A fair reading is therefore:

```text
individuation
!= complete exhaustion of the prior field;
residual preindividual potential
can participate in later individuation.
```

That is much closer to SRT R4 than a simple "metastable field" comparison.

### R5 payment: partial-to-strong candidate

Transduction is iterative: a locally achieved structure can become the condition for propagation / structuration in adjacent regions, and living individuation can remain ongoing rather than terminate in a final individual.

This directly pressures any weak SRT statement of:

```text
past formation changes conditions for later formation.
```

What is not yet demonstrated as paid is the exact SRT **Selection-position** role if that role requires more than ordinary transductive conditioning — namely, a specifically non-flat "from-where" that actively generates / re-generates the later manifestation/background relation.

### Pass-17 consequence

The Simondon comparator substantially narrows the live residual.

After fair payment, SRT cannot rely on any of the following as sufficient distinctiveness:

```text
pre-object field;
subjectless formation;
individual not pregiven;
individual + milieu co-genesis;
retained non-individuated potential;
prior formation conditioning later formation.
```

If Selection-position remains distinctive, it must be specified at a sharper relational level than those six claims.

## 6. Relational / operational formalism — replace the weak hidden-state foil

Pass 15 correctly used predictive-state representations and computational mechanics to defeat the claim that every state formalism is a warehouse of hidden physical objects.

Pass 17 strengthens the formal comparator again.

### 6.1 Predictive state representations remain relevant

Littman, Sutton & Singh, `Predictive Representations of State` (NeurIPS 2001):

https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2001/file/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Paper.pdf

A dynamical state can be represented by action-conditional predictions of future observations. Thus:

```text
state != necessarily a hidden object;
state can be relationally grounded in prediction from histories.
```

But PSR still presupposes a declared set of observations / actions / tests.

### 6.2 General probabilistic / operational theories are a stronger foil

A modern GPT review defines states operationally as equivalence classes of preparation procedures and effects as equivalence classes of experimental yes/no questions:

- Martin Plávala, `General probabilistic theories: An introduction`, *Physics Reports* 1033 (2023), DOI 10.1016/j.physrep.2023.09.001.

This matters because the formal primitives are already **relational / operational equivalence classes**, not little hidden substances.

Payment:

```text
formal state need not mean ontic object inventory;
identity can be induced by operational indistinguishability;
state/effect duality can encode relation between preparation and possible readout.
```

### 6.3 The remaining MOBJ2-B boundary survives, but only narrowly

Even these stronger operational frameworks still begin with a declared operational interface:

```text
what counts as a preparation;
what counts as an effect / question / outcome;
which transformations are admissible;
which operational equivalences define identity.
```

Therefore MOBJ2-B remains coherent:

```text
formal closure after an operational distinction scheme is fixed
!= explanation of how that distinction scheme becomes determinate at all.
```

But Pass 17 sharpens the burden:

> SRT cannot simply oppose itself to "state space". A future strongest formal rival should be allowed to make partitions, observables, operational equivalences or interfaces **endogenous / dynamically constituted** rather than primitive.

Until such a comparator is actually tested, the absolute PO-3 claim remains unavailable.
