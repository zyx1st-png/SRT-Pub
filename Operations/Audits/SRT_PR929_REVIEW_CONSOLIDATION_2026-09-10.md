## 5. JFS ladder correction

The review concern is accepted.

`JFS-0...JFS-8` is **not** a canonical generation ladder, primitive inventory, or exhaustive ontology sequence.

The numbering is only a temporary integration-navigation convenience.

The map mixes at least four edge types:

```text
CONSTITUTIVE / FORMATION DEPENDENCY
one organization is required before another can first appear.

RECURRENT FEEDBACK
formed processes recursively maintain or reconstruct one another.

ANALYTIC DECOMPOSITION
separate explanatory burdens are distinguished without asserting temporal order.

DOMAIN-CONDITIONAL REALIZATION
an edge may hold only in a specified biological / cognitive / social / artificial domain.
```

Any future diagram should type its arrows accordingly. Do not infer necessity, mutual exclusivity, completeness or universal temporal order from the JFS numbering.
