# Selection-First Derivational Spine Triage — R2-CORE (2026-08-28)

> **Scope:** read-only routing / derivability audit. No canonical definition, theorem, symbol, equation, claim level, or owner text is changed here.
>
> **Purpose:** separate two questions that the repository can otherwise blur:
>
> ```text
> is target T a consequence of the SRT core structure?
> !=
> is target T a consequence attributable to the Selection primitive role?
> ```
>
> This audit is upstream of rival subtraction. A middle-level target does not become Selection-primitive surplus merely because it is already present elsewhere in SRT.

## 0. Executive finding

The current Selection-first rebase should not assume that the main theoretical weakness is P0 wording.

A more important candidate weakness is the **derivational spine** connecting the thin primitive actualisation kernel to richer structures:

```text
P0-01 primitive actualisation
        |
        ?
        +--> historical trace / writeback
        +--> object individuation
        +--> own-history attribution / bearer admission
        +--> Stable ISP
        +--> selector / subject-position
        +--> phenomenality
```

Several downstream structures are canonical, P1, P2, or mature bridge content. But existing deletion audits already show that many of them are **not regenerated by P0-01 / AM-A alone** when target-bearing premises are suspended.

That is not an inconsistency by itself. SRT has four P0 axioms plus P1/P2 structure. It does mean that scientific or novelty claims must not charge the whole downstream architecture to the Selection primitive without an explicit derivational path.

## 1. Two attribution questions must remain separate

### Q-A — core-architecture consequence

Does the full admitted SRT core, including independently primitive or separately admitted premises, support target `T`?

Form:

```text
P0-set + admitted P1/P2 premises -> T
```

A YES can establish internal SRT architecture without showing primitive Selection fruitfulness.

### Q-B — Selection-primitive fruitfulness

Does **retaining the Selection primitive role** add a non-circular consequence relative to a target-preserving rival while shared background is held fixed?

Corrected GOV-SUB01 form:

```text
B' = shared background minus direct target-bearing premises

SRT-P + B' -> T ?
Rival  + B' -> T ?
```

where `SRT-P` is the declared primitive mode (`R` or full P0-01 package).

A target may therefore be:

```text
valid SRT middle-level architecture
+
NOT currently attributable to primitive Selection
```

without contradiction.

## 8. Important architecture consequence

If this audit finds that rich SRT middle-level structure is primarily paid for by independently admitted P0-03/P0-04 conditions, Stable-ISP criteria, or P2/P3 bridge assumptions, then the correct interpretation is not automatically that SRT fails.

It means the theory should say more precisely:

> Selection-first fixes the ontological floor and ordering. Historical asymmetry, persistence, individuation, bearer admission and subject formation require additional typed structural commitments whose derivational relations must be stated rather than credited wholesale to the primitive.

That may be a stronger architecture than pretending one primitive derives everything.

Conversely, if a non-circular derivation can eliminate one or more independent commitments, that is a genuine architecture compression result and a candidate theoretical increment.
