## 4. The remaining difference is narrower: Selection-typing

The only clearly source-native difference still visible is not the existence of a preindividual / pre-object field or its restructuring.

It is how the **individuating operation itself is typed**.

### Simondon-side grammar

```text
preindividual metastability / disparation
-> transduction / dephasing / structuring
-> dimensions + structures emerge
-> individual + milieu / new metastable organization
-> constituted structure conditions further operation
```

### SRT-side author grammar

```text
pre-object relational field
-> Selection event
   = one relational direction manifests
     while competing directions are relatively backgrounded / suppressed
   as two aspects of the same actual differentiation
-> retained field reconstitution
-> later Selection-position / One formation and later Selection
```

Thus the candidate difference is:

> SRT does not merely place individuation before the individual; it **types the primitive world-side individuation event as Selection**, with manifestation and relative backgrounding/suppression as constitutive co-generated aspects of that event.

This is a narrower claim than “SRT has a pre-object field.”

## 6. Source-neutral bilateral-selection candidate for the next pressure

To avoid defining the answer as SRT, a future audit may use the following **machine-only** candidate bundle `BA*`.

A local individuation / field-reconstitution operation provisionally pays `BA*` if:

```text
B1 LOCAL ACTUALIZATION ASYMMETRY
   one relational continuation becomes locally realized / amplified / operative;

B2 COUPLED RELATIVE BACKGROUNDING
   competing continuations do not merely fail to occur;
   their immediate accessibility / influence is relatively reduced as part of
   the same operation;

B3 COMMON CAUSAL OPERATION
   B1 and B2 are not independent downstream effects;
   the same local causal organization produces both sides;

B4 GENETIC LOAD-BEARING ROLE
   deleting / neutralizing the B1-B2 coupling while preserving generic
   transduction / state update prevents formation or reconstitution of the
   later causal locus under the declared target.
```

`BA*` is not a definition of canonical Selection.

Its purpose is to create genuine counterexample space.

If a mature Simondon-compatible or other preindividual individuation mechanism can form / reconstitute the target locus while `B4` fails, the strengthened necessity claim is undercut.

If no fair rival can do so, `BA*` may become a useful discriminator candidate, but only after author adjudication and Todd-style realization design.
