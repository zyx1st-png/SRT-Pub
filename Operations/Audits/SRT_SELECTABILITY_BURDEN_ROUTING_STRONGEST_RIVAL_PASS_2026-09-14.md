The strongest coherent single-family rival is now:

```text
Active Inference / FEP
+
niche construction / agent-environment reciprocal shaping
+

+

```

This family strongly pays:


Therefore SRT does **not** retain a safe local residual from:

```text
future selectability;
anticipation;
selection-condition modification;
learning;
agent-niche reciprocal construction;
changing the generator/model;

```

However, one coupling remains incompletely paid in this strongest coherent rival:

> **explicit incidence typing of consequence / burden onto distinct positions, coupled to the change in each position's future selectability and to whether the selection-generating conditions remain revisable.**

Compactly:

```text
who can select / authorize?
who executes?
who benefits?
who bears / pays?
whose future selectable space changes?
where does writeback land?
can that position revise the generator / scaffold that shaped the event?
```

Active Inference can model many of these facts if explicitly encoded. But they are not its constitutive organizing coordinates in the way policy, generative model, preference and expected free energy are.

Machine verdict:

```text
local mechanism novelty = NOT ESTABLISHED
position-indexed burden/selectability coupling = LIVE
architecture-level explanatory compression = STRENGTHENED
ontic non-substitutability = NOT ESTABLISHED
```

## 3. The free-energy / payability distinction

A major anti-translation guard is required here.

SRT must not win by saying:

```text
our Psi_f is real cost;
their free energy is not.
```

That would be an assertion, not an audit.

But the objects are not automatically identical either.

### Active-Inference object

At formal strength, variational / expected free energy is an information-theoretic objective / bound inside a declared generative-model formalism. It can encode preferences, uncertainty, epistemic value and future policy quality.

### SRT payability target

The thinner SRT burden being tested here is:

```text
a change may be dynamically / formally available
but still differ in whether its formation, maintenance, repair or revision burden
can be borne by the relevant formed position without collapsing its future selection capacity.
```

At multi-position scale, a second question appears:

```text
is the burden borne by the position that authorizes / benefits from the Selection,
or externalized to another position?
```

### Result

Active Inference can represent costs and preferences if they are put into the model. It does not by itself make **burden incidence across distinct consequence-bearing positions** a mandatory constitutive bookkeeping axis.

Thus:

```text
cost / objective / preference = STRONGLY NEIGHBOR-PAID
actual incidence of burden on different positions = NOT AUTOMATICALLY PAID
payability × future-selectability loss = PARTIAL
```

This is a live difference, not yet a proof of irreducible ontology.

## 4. Strong control rival B — Capability Approach

The Capability Approach strongly pays one part that Active Inference does not emphasize:

```text
resources / formal options
!=
real opportunities available to this person;

conversion factors determine whether resources can become actual capabilities.
```

This is a powerful pressure on SRT's language of:

```text
visible / reachable / payable / actually available paths.
```

So SRT cannot claim social novelty from “real option set differs from formal menu.”

But Capability Approach is normatively and socially scoped. It does not provide:

```text
pre-object actualisation ontology;
manifest / relative-background event typing;
One formation;
Selection-generated L2 ancestry;
or a general event-to-generator writeback grammar.
```

Its relevance is as a control proving that **future selectable opportunity at a position** is mature social-theory territory.

## 5. Strong control rival C — delegation / distributed agency / responsibility

Distributed-agency and responsibility-gap literatures strongly pay:

```text
authority can be delegated;
execution can be distributed;
traceability can be lost;
no single actor may coincide with all causal / normative roles;
responsibility gaps can arise from institutional distribution, not only technical opacity.
```

Therefore SRT cannot claim novelty merely from separating:

```text
chooser / authorizer / executor / responsible party.
```

What they do not normally make primary is the SRT-style downstream coupling:

```text
role distribution
-> consequence lands at position i
-> changes position i's future selection capacity / selectable space
-> feeds into later Selection and possibly generator revision.
```

Again, this may be modelled, but it is not the native common grammar of delegation theory.

## 6. Strong control rival D — niche construction and evolvability

Niche construction already supplies:

```text
organism modifies selective environment;
modified environment changes later selection pressures;
other organisms / descendants may inherit modified pressures.
```

Evolvability / developmental-bias traditions already supply:

```text
the mechanisms producing variation affect what variation becomes available for later selection;
the capacity to generate selectable variation can itself evolve.
```

Therefore SRT cannot claim novelty from:

```text
Selection changes future Selection conditions;
or
the generator of candidates can itself change.
```

What remains open is whether these literatures jointly type **burden incidence / externalization / future selectability at distinct formed positions** with the same precision SRT is trying to impose.

## 8. Frankenstein-composite guard

A strongest-rival composite is fair only when interface relations are independently licensed.

The three families above can coexist in one sociotechnical case, but the following inference is **not automatically source-native**:

```text
variational policy objective
-> actual burden incidence on person/group i
-> reduction of that position's future selectable space
-> consequence-driven revision of the candidate generator
```

Each arrow needs an additional mapping.

Therefore the rival can match the SRT tool operationally if a modeler explicitly adds these mappings, but that does not yet show that one mature rival grammar already contains them as a single dependency architecture.

This is evidence for **explanatory compression**, not proof of ontic uniqueness.

## 10. New conceptually sharp distinction — objective minimization vs burden incidence

One of the strongest outcomes of this pass is a negative distinction:

```text
an agent/system minimizing an objective
!=
identifying who actually bears the consequences of the resulting policy.
```

Likewise:

```text
system remains stable
!=
all affected positions retain or expand future selectability.
```

And:

```text
collective fit / coordination improves
!=
no subgroup's future selectability is being consumed as the hidden payment.
```

This distinction is central to the user's civilizational ambition but is not itself a new primitive ontology claim.

It gives a route from SRT ontology to social diagnosis without placing social terms inside L0.



SRT loses the ability to distinguish:

```text

from

```

using a shared structural language.

Result:

```text
T4-T6 are not decorative for the higher-level SRT programme.
```

Then the tool can diagnose incidence and lock-in but cannot distinguish:

```text

from

```

Result:

```text
T7 remains independently load-bearing for generative-health analysis.
```
