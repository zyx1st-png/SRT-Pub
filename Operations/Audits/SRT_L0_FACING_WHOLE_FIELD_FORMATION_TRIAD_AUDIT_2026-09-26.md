# L0-facing whole-field formation — metastability × Skilled Intentionality × SRT triad audit

> Role: machine research audit after explicit author acceptance of the field-formation route.
>
> Boundary: this file does not establish an SRT residual, does not harden "field" as ontology, does not make Bearer necessary for all field unity, and does not treat metastability or Skilled Intentionality as evidence for SRT. It compares how three architectures pay different parts of the same formation burden.

---

## 0. Executive result

The focused three-node comparison produces a clearer decomposition than the earlier object-first / orientation-first contrast.

### Metastable coordination dynamics

Best current burden:

> **How can many distributed components become a transiently unified functional whole without losing local autonomy or requiring a central controller?**

Primary answer:
- self-assembly;
- relative coordination;
- simultaneous integration and segregation;
- transient coupling / decoupling;
- metastable preferred relations without permanent lock-in.

### Skilled Intentionality Framework

Best current burden:

> **How can a situated organism be selectively oriented toward a whole field of relevant possibilities without explicit deliberation over a list of objects?**

Primary answer:
- organism-environment coupling;
- multiple bodily action-readiness states;
- acquired skills and developmental history;
- current concerns;
- selective openness;
- tendency toward optimal grip on the field of relevant affordances;
- metastable attunement.

### Current SRT-facing burden

The strongest current question is deeper and differently typed:

> **What makes distributed selective, historical, anticipatory and regulatory processes belong to one continuing generative whole, such that some consequences become conditions of that same whole's later reconstitution and reshape its future reachability / object-formability?**

Current SRT owners provide separate candidate roles:
- Selection / generativity: non-flat actualisation;
- retained vertical efficacy: result-to-condition recursion;
- One: lineage-relative continuing process-unity;
- Selection-position: current operative from-where;
- Bearer: One-indexed consequence writeback where independently paid;
- structural Expectation: materially operative reachability shaping;
- gate geometry: equivalence / boundary / neighborhood / transition-reachability / stability organization;
- glue / support / friction-bearing maintenance: persistence under changing local content;
- L2 writeback: recursive reshaping of later L0-facing organization.

No novelty follows.

The key gain of this audit is a **formation decomposition**.

## 1. Mature-source anchors

### 1.1 Coordination Dynamics / metastability

Core references:

- Tognoli E, Kelso JAS. 2014. The Metastable Brain. Neuron 81:35–48. DOI: 10.1016/j.neuron.2013.12.022.
- Tognoli E, Kelso JAS. 2014. Enlarging the scope: grasping brain complexity. Front Syst Neurosci 8:122. DOI: 10.3389/fnsys.2014.00122.
- Kelso JAS. 2012. Multistability and metastability: understanding dynamic coordination in the brain. Phil Trans R Soc B 367:906–918. DOI: 10.1098/rstb.2011.0351.
- recent empirical guard: the 2025 human-neuroimaging scoping review of metastability reports substantial variability in definitions / measures and limited direct behavioral application across the reviewed literature.
- conceptual guard: recent "Metastability demystified" work emphasizes that metastability is often used heuristically and requires tighter dynamical definition.

Source-native commitments relevant here:
- neural ensembles dynamically engage and disengage;
- metastability balances integration and segregation;
- local autonomy can coexist with global coordination;
- large-scale context is dynamically created through combined constraints;
- coherent global function need not require permanent synchronization or a central controller.

Important guard:

~~~text
metastable coordination
!= demonstrated universal cognitive field
!= subject / Bearer
!= semantic direction by itself
!= proof of SRT unity
~~~

### 1.2 Skilled Intentionality / field of relevant affordances

Core references:

- Bruineberg J, Rietveld E. 2014. Self-organization, free energy minimization, and optimal grip on a field of affordances. Front Hum Neurosci 8:599. DOI: 10.3389/fnhum.2014.00599.

- Bruineberg / van Dijk / Rietveld line on the field and landscape of affordances and metastable attunement.

Source-native commitments relevant here:
- skilled intentionality = selective engagement with multiple affordances simultaneously;
- relevant affordances stand out relative to a landscape;
- states of action readiness self-organize;
- these states are reciprocally coupled to the landscape of affordances;
- abilities, developmental history and concerns shape selective openness;
- the whole field is lived from a particular situated individual's perspective;
- acting also reshapes the landscape over longer timescales;
- the field / landscape relation is jointly enacted.

Important guard:

~~~text
field of relevant affordances
!= literal physical field
!= a list of represented objects
!= merely a static salience map
~~~

This is a very strong absorber of generic "whole orientation" language.

## 3. Key correction — field unity is not one thing

The phrase "one whole field" risks hiding at least three distinct burdens.

### U-a — coordination unity

Question:

> Do distributed processes mutually constrain one another strongly enough to function as a coherent ensemble?

Metastability is a strong mature account.

This does not require:
- One;
- Bearer;
- subjecthood;
- self-consequence.

### U-b — continuation unity

Question:

> Is there a relatively separable process-lineage whose later operative organization is reconstituted from prior results?

Current SRT owner:

~~~text
One
= localized, lineage-relative, processual formed unity
  whose continuation is Selection-mediated recurrent reconstitution.
~~~

This is stronger than momentary coordination.

It gives a candidate answer to:

> why are these changing local processes successive organization of the same formed whole rather than only repeated coordination events?

### U-c — consequence-indexed unity

Question:

> Do consequences become history-bearing specifically for that same continuing lineage and change how its later operative position is reconstituted?

Current SRT owner:

~~~text
Bearer
= already-admitted One
+ consequence becomes One-indexed history
+ later reconstitution of same lineage changes
+ future selectability changes.
~~~

This is stronger again.

Therefore:

~~~text
metastable coordination unity
!= One automatically

One
!= Bearer automatically

Bearer
!= consciousness automatically
~~~

### Machine candidate

For the current cognition programme, the minimum whole-field unity hypothesis should probably be tested first at the **One / reconstitution** burden, not by making Bearer definitionally necessary.

Bearer then asks a stronger question:

> when does the field become consequence-indexed to that continuing whole?

This is an M-level recommendation, not author adjudication.

## 4. Direction — from preferred dynamics to lived relevance to generative reachability

### 4.1 Metastability

Metastable dynamics can produce:
- preferred phase relations;
- transient coordination patterns;
- rapid switching;
- context-sensitive global constraint.

But metastability alone does not tell us why one future matters more to the organism.

So:

~~~text
coordination direction
!= semantic / concern-relative direction automatically.
~~~

### 4.2 Skilled Intentionality

SIF gives a strong mature answer:
- current concerns;
- skills;
- abilities;
- bodily action readiness;
- disequilibrium / tendency toward optimal grip;
- environment-relative solicitations.

This produces a whole-field directional structure without explicit deliberation.

SIF therefore strongly absorbs generic claims of:

~~~text
whole-field direction
+
pre-reflective relevance
+
organism-relative selective openness.
~~~

### 4.3 SRT-facing structural Expectation

Current SRT source says:

~~~text
Expectation is materially operative:
it gates / biases generative passage
and reshapes reachability.
~~~

The potentially useful distinction is not:

~~~text
SIF has direction;
SRT has direction.
~~~

That yields no gain.

The useful pressure question is:

> **Can direction be typed as a modification of future reachability generated from the continuing organization itself, without requiring a universal optimal state or explicit goal?**

This may align with, rather than exceed, enactive / ecological accounts.

Current disposition:

~~~text
directional field = strongly source-owned by SIF / enactive neighbors
SRT residual = OPEN
~~~

Current gate geometry:

This makes normalization potentially useful as the bridge between:

distributed dynamics

## 6. Persistence — metastable change does not by itself answer "same whole"

This is one of the clearest conceptual gaps in the focused triad.

Metastability emphasizes fluidity:
- components engage / disengage;
- global networks reassemble;
- coordination need not settle into one stable state.

That is valuable for flexibility.

But "the same cognitive whole across changing coordination patterns" requires an additional identity / continuation criterion if the question is posed explicitly.

SIF supplies:
- developmental history;
- acquired abilities;
- concerns;
- form-of-life embedding;
- organism-level continuity.

SRT supplies a typed internal route:
- One = recurrent reconstitution of a relatively separable process-lineage;
- retained efficacy = prior result materially conditions later reconstruction;
- glue = maintenance burden across changing local contents;
- Bearer = stronger One-indexed consequence writeback.

The important scientific pressure is:

> Do SRT's typed continuation relations add anything beyond ordinary organismic continuity, autonomy, habit, memory, skill and self-maintaining dynamics?

OPEN.

## 8. Strongest new insight — unity may be "common reconstitution burden", not synchrony

Working diagnostic only:

> **Local processes belong to one stronger generative field when their differences are jointly load-bearing for reconstitution of one continuing operative organization, not merely because they synchronize at the same moment.**

Why this is useful:

### It separates coordination from continuation

Two processes can synchronize transiently without becoming one continuing lineage.

### It separates continuation from Bearer

A One can continue before every perturbation becomes its own consequence-bearing history.

### It gives direction a locus

Structural Expectation can be indexed to an operative from-where instead of floating as a global predictor.

### It gives normalization a reason

Differences can be flattened or preserved relative to what matters for reconstitution of the continuing organization.

### It gives objectification a place

A local cut becomes useful when it stabilizes a load-bearing relation for the field's ongoing reconstitution / action.

But this candidate faces a severe absorption test:

~~~text
autopoiesis / autonomy
enactive sense-making
active inference
homeostatic / allostatic control
dynamical systems with self-maintaining constraints
~~~

may already provide equivalent unity / self-maintenance architecture.

Therefore:

~~~text
common reconstitution burden
= useful machine diagnostic
!= admitted SRT residual
!= new term to harden
~~~

## 11. Empirical caution and foothold

Do not treat metastability as a solved empirical mechanism for whole-field cognition.

Recent reviews report:
- inconsistent operational definitions;
- heavy reliance on resting-state neuroimaging;
- methodological confounds;
- limited direct linkage to specific mental processes in much of the literature.

Therefore the current use of metastability is primarily:
- formal / mechanistic neighbor;
- not empirical confirmation.

A first bounded empirical foothold should test a **field-formation property**, for example:

> whether perturbing one global concern / consequence context reorganizes multiple otherwise matched local relevance / grouping / transition judgments in a coordinated low-dimensional way that predicts later recutting better than independent local-weight models.

Even success would not yet establish:
- Selection;
- One;
- Bearer;
- L0 ontology.

It would only support the neutral claim that a whole-field organization is behaviorally useful.
