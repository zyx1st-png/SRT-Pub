### limited K (local reparameterization)
- **d_max divisor (Y1)**: replace κ₀ with a generic positive **unit alignment cost** `c>0`; `Ψ_f^budget/c` reproduces the two-bottleneck structure exactly. ### broad K (role-level competitor model)

1. **Preserved counterfactual**: capacity-vs-stability-budget discrimination (high-rank-but-unstable vs low-rank systems) — a general resource-constrained-capacity model reproduces Y1.

5. **Origin (Y6)**: `T-L0-Kappa0` itself calls κ₀ a *structural prerequisite* (前提), not a derivation; `L0_Ontological_Status §二 / open-questions` admits its origin is unresolved. Competitor "absorbing-state asymmetry / non-degenerate IC" equally fails to close the origin gap. → the loss is **symmetric**; κ₀ **names, does not close**, the P0-04 gap.
