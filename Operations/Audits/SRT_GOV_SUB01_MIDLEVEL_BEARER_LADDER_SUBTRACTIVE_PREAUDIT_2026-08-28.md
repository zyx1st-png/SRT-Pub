## 2. Deletion-target discipline — what is and is not being removed

This is the first gate. A subtractive audit whose deletion target is misspecified produces a worthless verdict, because it refutes a construct SRT never asserted.

### 2.1 Three illegitimate deletion targets

Do **not** delete, and do not let a rival's framing silently substitute:

```text
WRONG-1  selection = choosing among presented options
WRONG-2  selection = a subject's decision or act of will
WRONG-3  selection = preference-ranking over an existing menu
```

Canonical basis for the exclusion:

- `Core_Law/SRT_L0_Metaphysics.md` — selection is a **subjectless verb**; the selector is the fossil of selection, not its prerequisite. Deleting a "chooser" therefore deletes nothing SRT claims.
- `_SRT_AGENT_RETRIEVAL_PROFILE.md` / symbol discipline — `θ / Ĝ_θ` is not subjective will.
- `01_Source_Intuition/BOOK/Drafts_26Q/Q05_选择不是挑选.md` (`companion_exposition`, cited as exposition only, not as a definition source): 「人类熟知的挑选，站在主体、选项、偏好和可见菜单都沉积完成之后」; 「把'选择'前移，不是把人的意志偷偷塞进石头、雪花和细胞里」.

Any execution round in which the rival is allowed to defeat WRONG-1/2/3 must be recorded as **target laundering** (protocol §7.5) and discarded.

### 2.2 The legitimate deletion targets

Two levels, and they must never be conflated:

```text
P0 level (already audited, do not re-run here)
  delete: the AM-A minimal actualisation role R
          non-objectified potential difference -> determinate manifest actuality
  status: Outcome C against frozen V2-PAR

MIDDLE level (this pre-audit's object)
  delete: the three-step completeness requirement
          (exclusion + form-fixing + write-in, all three required)
  delete: same-bearer consequence return / non-outsourcing
  delete: history-writeback modulation of future selectability
  retain per protocol §8.1:
          asymmetrical constraints, reachable-set restriction,
          history dependence, cost, consequence return
```

Protocol §8.1's retain-list is deliberately generous: it hands the rival almost everything. That is the point. The middle-level question is whether anything survives *after* the rival is given history dependence and consequence return for free.

## 3. Step 1 — Target lock

Five candidate targets. Each is stated in the protocol's required form ("preserve *this* prediction / classification"), not in the forbidden form ("the theory should still work").

### T1 — Three-step completeness threshold

> **Preserve** the classification boundary that separates a determination which leaves forward-constraining trace from a physical event that does not, such that reversible fluctuation, traceless collision, and thermally erased perturbation fall on the negative side.

Basis: the three-step engine with its explicit threshold — 「三步不齐，就不是选择结构」.

### T2 — Excluded-branch continued force

> **Preserve** the prediction that post-determination residual structure differs as a function of **what was excluded**, holding the realized outcome fixed — and that the residue is not one quantity but **three separably manifesting modes**.

This is the matched-endpoint / different-excluded-set contrast. Basis: `Q06` (`companion_exposition`, cited as exposition), which specifies the residue as three distinct modes on three distinct dimensions:

```text
阴影 shadow              — evaluative-reference residue
张力 tension             — structural-force residue
后续摩擦 later friction  — turnaround-cost residue
```

Q06 additionally carries a built-in dissociation: 「非人的地形通常没有阴影，但仍然会有张力和后续摩擦」 — i.e. terrain complexity is predicted to select *which subset* of the three residues appears, rather than scaling one residue magnitude. That subset-selection claim, not the bare existence of residue, is the discriminating content, and it is what execution must lock.

`Q26 §3` names the same thing as SRT's own cosmic-level death condition: 「尤其不能解释被排开者如何以阴影、张力、后续摩擦继续受力」. T2 is therefore a target the author has already accepted as load-bearing and already accepted can kill the claim.

### T3 — Bearer-specific consequence-return classification

> **Preserve** bearer-specific consequence-return classification: which continuing unit the consequences return to, and whether that return is outsourceable.

This is one of the protocol's own worked examples of an acceptable target (§6 Step 1). Corresponds to `A3 Bearer` in the PH-IND04 ladder.

#### T3 sharpening — attribution admission, not generic history dependence

Per the #860 R2-DOWN-B refinement recorded on this PR, `history dependence / consequence return` is too coarse to lock. `Core_Law/SRT_Individuation.md` (lines 65-74) already defines the σ owner on a decomposition:

```text
theta_t = theta_t^trace + theta_t^ext

theta^trace = written by P's own prior outputs through writeback (Ax-Op-03b)
theta^ext   = written by external conditions
```

So the bearer-side lower burden is first a **domain-of-definition / attribution admission** question, not a transition question:

> Under a prospectively frozen candidate unit, grain, and reset/replacement rule, can the Markov-blanket / active-inference rival reproduce the **role distinction** between parameter changes attributable to the unit's own prior outputs and changes imposed externally? If yes, does non-outsourcing add any further independent classification or prospective prediction?

Two guards bind the later Step 3-7 execution:

**G-T3-1 — role, not notation.** A rival does not fail for lacking the symbols `theta^trace / theta^ext`. It fails only if the matched attribution role cannot be implemented **without re-importing the target relation**. Scoring a rival down for vocabulary is `R0 rhetorical redundancy` on the SRT side, not rival failure.

**G-T3-2 — no σ bootstrap.** `σ` / `T-IND-1` may *identify* the role that needs testing; they may **not** be cited as evidence that bearer independence is established. The reason is internal and checkable: `Def-IND-1` already presupposes own-output writeback attribution, and `T-IND-2` condition 2 (「承担内生化：代价不再可以被环境完全吸收」) already *is* the non-outsourcing relation under test. Citing either as support would be circular. `T-IND-1` is additionally only `P1-candidate` per that file's own claim table.

#### Allowed T3 dispositions

Execution may return, in addition to the protocol's residue labels:

```text
lower boundary = sigma domain-of-definition precondition
                 (admission, not transition)
```

This disposition records that the attribution role is a precondition for σ being well-posed at all, rather than a threshold crossing — and it is a disposition, not a residue label.

### T4 — Bearer / selector separation

> **Preserve** the classification that distinguishes (a) a system whose consequences return to the same unit but whose future option-structure is unchanged, from (b) a system whose same-bearer history modulates non-equivalent future paths.

Corresponds to `A3` vs `A4 Selector-position`. This is the separation PH-IND04 §15 names as the retained synthesis (`bearer != selector`).

### T5 — Non-derivation of phenomenal presence

> **Preserve** the *absence* of an admissible inference from `A5 Subject-position` to `A7 Experiencer`.

This is a negative/ordering consequence, in the same class as X0. It is **not** a prediction and cannot yield D2. It is locked here so that a later execution cannot quietly count it as empirical surplus, and so that a rival which *does* derive phenomenality is scored as differing, not as matching.

## 8. Risk grading — prospective Outcome-C probability

T2 is also the only target whose design maps directly onto an executable empirical contrast (matched realized endpoint, different excluded set), which is why it is the natural bridge to the separate publication workline.

Before any Step 3 round may begin, the following must be frozen and recorded prospectively:

0. for T3 specifically: the candidate unit, grain, and reset/replacement rule, frozen prospectively, with `G-T3-1` and `G-T3-2` restated in the execution record;

3. one named rival family with state variables, update class, calibration range, and revision/complexity cost;
4. the declared horizon, perturbation range, and refit budget;
5. symmetric failure conditions — what result weakens SRT, and what result weakens the rival;
6. a written commitment that rival support will not be widened after outcome inspection.

Item 5 is the one most often skipped. Without it the round is not a subtractive audit.
