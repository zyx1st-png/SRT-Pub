# Independent review — O0 primitive-debt repair plan

## Verdict

```text
THEORETICAL DIRECTION: PASS
FOUR-OWNER SCOPE: INCOMPLETE
CANONICAL LANDING: HOLD
REQUIRED SCOPE: FIVE PRIMARY SEMANTIC OWNERS + REGISTRY SYNC
```

The repair logic is sound: remove obsolete proof-load without deleting useful symbols or operational objects, preserve modal openness without reifying a possibility container, and keep O0 free of moral / continuation content.

## Major finding 1 — add `Core/SRT_Core_12a_Ontology_L0L1.md`

Current `T-L0-Kappa0` still says `κ0` is a geometric refinement of `T-Core-A1C2` and carries a primitive directional-cost role. If L0 / Core_01 are repaired while Core_12a remains unchanged, Core_12a can back-define the old primitive semantics.

Therefore the primary semantic scope must be:

```text
1 Core_Law/SRT_L0_Metaphysics.md
2 Core/SRT_Core_01_Axioms.md
3 Core/SRT_Core_12a_Ontology_L0L1.md
4 Governance/SRT_CLAIM_LADDER.md §0A
5 _SRT_T_DIR_CANONICAL.md
```

Minimum Core_12a repair requirement:

```text
κ0 may remain a stronger formal / realization candidate;
κ0 must not define or sufficiently ground G0;
κ0 cost anisotropy != primitive value / continuation preference;
κ0 necessity remains OPEN.
```

## Major finding 2 — registry surfaces must follow owner semantics

Inspect and synchronize, without granting them theory authority:

```text
_SRT_SYMBOL_TABLE.md
_SRT_CROSS_DOMAIN_MATRIX.md
CANONICAL_REGISTRY.md
split mirrors / generated context bundles
```

Registry sync is mechanical / semantic reflection, not independent theory change.

## Major finding 3 — old epsilon dependency map is partly historical

Former unconditional P1-T07 has already been withdrawn under ST-A; current Irreversibility / B13 routes are more conditional and downstream.

Therefore the first primitive repair should not reopen broad P1-T07 work merely because older dependency maps list it.

Use the old maps only to find residual definitions / registries that still carry old epsilon or kappa meaning.

## Major finding 4 — `D_ref` must remain a typed argument, not a new primitive object

The proposed T_dir repair is good if read as:

```text
T_dir(D_ref)
= readability / reorientation relative to an independently established direction reference.
```

But `D_ref` must not become a universal field, L0 primitive, or one scalar shared by all domains.

## Major finding 5 — de-containerisation must preserve modal openness

Remove the primitive warehouse / field reading, but retain:

```text
completed actuality
!= primitive generativity not exhausted by completed actuality.
```

Do not collapse SRT into actualism where openness is only observer ignorance.

## Major finding 6 — co-primitivity is not identity

Preserve the functional distinction:

```text
O0 = non-maximal-indifference / non-flat burden;
S0 = actualising differentiation burden;
G0 requires both;
neither is temporally or causally prior.
```

Do not turn O0 and S0 into synonyms.

## Major finding 7 — Gate 0 remains governance, not ontology owner

The proposed type-filter repair is correct. Gate 0 may state what kind of L0 claim is admissible, but named quantities such as `κ0` or `ε_pg` require independent owner support.

## Atomicity recommendation

If canonical repair is later authorized, prefer one bounded atomic semantic landing across the five owners, because they currently cross-support the old semantics. Registry/index refresh can accompany it mechanically.
