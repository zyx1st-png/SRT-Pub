# Pass 22 — active/passive Selection transition under strongest-neighbor pressure

## 0. Scope and authority

This is a **machine-side subtractive audit**. It does not replace the author's high-level ruling:

```text
prediction = stronger anticipatory commitment / preparation
passive Selection = a form of survival
active Selection = a form of creation and reconstruction
```

The purpose is narrower: pressure candidate transition criteria without silently converting them into author definitions.

No canonical edit, Level promotion, HOLD exit, or collective-owner rewrite is authorized here.

## 1. Starting machine candidate

The immediate candidate from Pass 21 was:

```text
PASSIVE:
Selection operates within an achieved selectable organization mainly to preserve continued existence.

ACTIVE:
Selection takes the selectable organization itself as a target of reconstruction,
changing what can count as a future path / possibility.
```

Useful shorthand:

```text
PASSIVE: preserve "can continue"
ACTIVE: rewrite "can become"
```

This is a strong operational distinction, but Pass 22 asks whether it is independently distinctive.

## 2. Pressure 1 — adaptation / learning

Ordinary adaptation already changes future response distributions rather than merely repeating one action. Learning can change policy weighting, state estimates, action readiness, strategy, and later accessibility.

Therefore the weak criterion:

```text
future behavior differs because the system changed internally
```

cannot distinguish ACTIVE Selection.

Even the stronger phrase:

```text
changes how future paths are weighted
```

is insufficient, because ordinary learning and adaptive control can pay it.

### Result

```text
AP-C1 outcome change vs internal reweighting:
TOO WEAK
```

## 3. Pressure 2 — niche construction

Niche Construction Theory explicitly gives organisms an active role in modifying selective environments and thereby modifying later selection pressures on themselves and others.

Strong source-native formulation:

```text
organism is not merely object of selection;
it can be a creator / modifier of the conditions of selection.
```

Representative sources:

- Laland, Matthews & Feldman (2016), *An introduction to niche construction theory*, Evolutionary Ecology 30:191–202, DOI 10.1007/s10682-016-9821-z.
- Laland et al. (2017), *Niche construction, sources of selection and trait coevolution*, Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B.

This strongly pays:

```text
agent changes the conditions under which future selection occurs;
future selection landscape is partly created by prior organismic activity;
other agents/species may inherit the modified selective environment.
```

### Result

```text
AP-C2 active Selection = modifies future selection conditions:
STRONGLY NEIGHBOR-PAID
```

SRT cannot claim novelty merely from "the selected also helps create the conditions of later selection".

## 4. Pressure 3 — anticipatory systems / active inference

Rosen-style anticipatory systems and Active Inference already allow current organization/action to be controlled by modeled or expected future states.

Active Inference additionally pays:

```text
policy selection over expected futures;
planning as inference;
self-maintenance / self-evidencing;
current action shaped by not-yet-realized outcomes.
```

Therefore:

```text
current organization changes because of anticipated future continuation
```

is not sufficient novelty.

### Result

```text
AP-C3 anticipatory commitment in broad future-directed sense:
STRONGLY NEIGHBOR-PRESSURED
```

Important remaining SRT guard: the author's `prediction` is not yet identified with an explicit internal generative model, Bayesian posterior, or conscious forecast.

## 5. Pressure 4 — open-ended evolution / changing phase space

This is the strongest new pressure on the ACTIVE side.

Longo, Montévil & Kauffman argue that in biological evolution the relevant phase space itself changes and cannot be prestated in advance; new phenotypes, niches, ecosystems, observables and parameters emerge, changing the space of possibilities itself.

Representative sources:

- Longo, Montévil & Kauffman (2012), *No entailing laws, but enablement in the evolution of the biosphere*, GECCO'12, DOI 10.1145/2330784.2330946.
- Kauffman & Roli (2021), *Beyond the Newtonian Paradigm: A Statistical Mechanics of Emergence*, arXiv:2106.15271.
- Kauffman (2022), *Is There a 4th Law for Non-Ergodic Systems That Do Work To Construct Their Expanding Phase Space?*, arXiv:2205.09762.

This pays a much stronger claim than ordinary learning:

```text
future possibility space is not merely reweighted;
new relevant possibilities / observables can be generated;
the phase space itself may expand or change;
organisms / Kantian wholes participate in constructing new possibilities.
```

### Result

```text
AP-C4 ACTIVE = changes / expands the future possibility space itself:
STRONGLY NEIGHBOR-PAID IN BIOLOGICAL / OPEN-ENDED-EVOLUTION FORM
```

Therefore the simple machine distinction:

```text
PASSIVE changes paths inside a space;
ACTIVE changes the space of paths.
```

is **useful but not sufficient as an SRT novelty claim**.

## 6. What the strongest neighbors still presuppose

The pressure above does not erase the current SRT structural candidate.

The strongest ACTIVE-side neighbors generally begin downstream from at least one of:

```text
already-formed organism / Kantian whole;
already-formed agent;
already-defined viability relation;
already-available affordance / niche relation;
already-declared model / state / action / policy interface.
```

The current SRT workline instead attempts the longer routing:

```text
pre-subject difference
-> subjectless Selection
-> Selection constitutes non-flat selectable space / verticality
-> Selection-position / One formation
-> post-formation position-indexed Selection
-> PASSIVE maintenance of continued existence
-> ACTIVE creation / reconstruction of selectable space
-> anticipatory commitment toward not-yet-realized continuations
-> multi-subject shared reconstruction / order.
```

The potential distinctiveness is therefore not "space change" or "anticipation" separately. It is whether **the same Selection ontology** can carry both the genesis of a selectable position and the later active reconstruction performed from that position.

## 7. Candidate transition criteria after subtraction

None of the following are author definitions. They are ranked machine probes.

### AP-T1 — viability-only vs possibility-reconstruction

```text
PASSIVE:
changes are bounded by preservation of current One / Selection-position viability.

ACTIVE:
current Selection is allowed to alter the organization of future selectability
beyond what is required to preserve the current organization.
```

Status: **LIVE BUT PRESSURED**. Biological plasticity, learning and niche construction can cross this boundary in ordinary descriptions.

### AP-T2 — selectable-space object shift

```text
PASSIVE:
selects through a currently operative space.

ACTIVE:
the organization that determines what can become selectable becomes itself
an object / locus of Selection.
```

Status: **STRONGER MACHINE CANDIDATE**, but needs a non-cognitive formulation. It must not require reflective representation of a "space" as an explicit object.

### AP-T3 — anticipatory self-commitment

```text
ACTIVE Selection does not merely predict a future;
it changes the present so that a not-yet-actual continuation becomes more realizable,
while accepting exclusion / cost / reduced availability of incompatible continuations.
```

Status: **LIVE**, but Rosen / Active Inference pressure is strong. Distinctiveness would have to come from the Selection-first ontology and the ontological role of exclusion / verticalization, not future-directedness alone.

### AP-T4 — generative novelty

```text
ACTIVE Selection makes a previously unavailable continuation selectable.
```

Status: **TOO STRONG AS UNIVERSAL CRITERION / STRONGLY PRESSURED BY OPEN-ENDED EVOLUTION**.

It may describe strong creative Selection, but if required universally it would exclude reconstruction that changes organization without generating an absolutely new possibility.

### AP-T5 — recursive level change

```text
PASSIVE Selection operates under the current vertical organization.
ACTIVE Selection recursively acts on the conditions that organize later Selection,
thereby changing the selector-position / selectable-space relation itself.
```

Status: **CURRENT STRONGEST MACHINE-SIDE TRANSITION CANDIDATE**.

Why it survives better:

1. it does not require ex nihilo novelty;
2. it includes reconstruction, not only creation;
3. it distinguishes mere parameter adaptation from a change in the organization of future Selection;
4. it naturally connects to the author's stronger anticipation: the present can be reorganized in advance of a future continuation;
5. it remains compatible with the L0 guard because `recursive` here does not require reflective consciousness.

But AP-T5 is **not author-selected**.

## 8. Passive Selection is not "passivity"

Avoid a terminology trap.

`PASSIVE Selection` in the author's sense should not be equated with:

```text
inactivity;
external determination;
absence of agency;
no prediction;
no adaptation.
```

A survival-oriented system can actively regulate, repair, predict threats, learn, and alter behavior while remaining in a predominantly PASSIVE-selection mode if those changes remain subordinated to maintaining the currently constituted viable One / position.

Thus the active/passive distinction is not an activity-energy scale.

Machine-side candidate distinction:

```text
PASSIVE = Selection whose dominant ontological burden is continued viability of the current selectable organization.
ACTIVE = Selection that recursively reconstructs the organization of future selectability itself.
```

Again: candidate, not author definition.

## 9. Multi-subject implication

This also sharpens the whole/parts line.

A collective can have:

```text
collective PASSIVE Selection:
maintain existing role / norm / coordination structures sufficiently for the order to continue.

collective ACTIVE Selection:
participants jointly alter the conditions under which future roles, norms, boundaries,
positions, and coordinated possibilities can become selectable.
```

But niche construction, institutional change, innovation theory and open-ended evolution strongly pressure this language.

The SRT-specific candidate remains the **genetic continuity**:

```text
Selection constitutes positions
-> positions Select
-> positioned Selection reconstructs the future selectable relations
   among those same / new positions.
```

## 11. Next bounded burden

Do not ask whether ACTIVE simply means "creates a new possibility"; strongest neighbors already pay that in broad form.

The next author-facing issue should be narrower:

> **主动 Selection 的最低门槛，是否在于 Selection 开始递归地作用于“决定后续什么能够被选择”的结构本身，而不只是为了维持当前 One 在这个结构中的继续存在？**

If the author accepts that direction, the next pressure should distinguish it from ordinary adaptation / learning / niche construction using a genesis-sensitive criterion, not a third-person behavioral metric.
