## 1. Core disposition

The file contains a useful downstream pathology architecture, but it currently overstates its ontological and normative authority.

The salvageable core is:

```text
formed systems may enter self-reinforcing narrowing / lock-in regimes;
such regimes can be modeled with staged dynamics;
access to discrepant signals can degrade;
external perturbations, support or structural changes may create reorientation windows;
post-lock-in reorganization may be path dependent.
```

What is not licensed is the current stronger package:

```text
primitive finite positionality -> full occlusion dynamics;
sigma_sub -> universal occlusion admission gate;
d / d_c -> universal healthy/pathological order parameter;
epsilon readability -> primitive direction access;
A/B phase -> responsibility verdict;
B phase -> universal unilateral-return impossibility;
evil -> objective canonical iff;
AI / collectives -> categorical phase classification without independent admission.
```

## 2. Authority / L0 backflow

### Finding

The owner currently says that L0 promised both inevitable finite-position occlusion and its lock-in dynamics, and treats the L1 file as the canonical fulfillment of that promise.

### Conflict

Current L0 fixes only finite positionality and explicitly separates it from formed One, Selection-position, Bearer, subject-position and consciousness. It does not establish a subject-level perceptual blind spot, active denial mechanism, d-threshold pathology or lock-in dynamics from primitive positionality alone.

D3-B1 further removes any automatic inference from occurrence non-equivalence to durable trace, permanent lock-in or absorbing dynamics.

### Required disposition

Retype:

```text
finite primitive positionality
-/> full occlusion dynamics
```

Occlusion must be a downstream formed-system / subject-pathology model whose admission conditions are separately paid.

The file may retain the weaker intuition that finite formed positions have limited access, but must not call the full A/B dynamics an L0 theorem already promised in primitive ontology.

Classification: REQUIRED CORRECTIVE.

## 3. Subject / admission gate

### Finding

The current file repeatedly uses:

```text
sigma >= sigma_sub
```

as the gate for embodied position / subject entry and therefore for eligibility for occlusion dynamics.

### Conflict

D3-A fixed `sigma_sr` as P2 model-local / replaceable and `sigma_sub` as P3/P4 threshold coordinate. Crossing it is not a universal subjecthood or ISP-entry theorem. Exact subject-position sufficiency and subject-position <-> Bearer remain OPEN.

### Required disposition

The occlusion owner must require an independently admitted formed system / position / relevant subject-like organization according to the claim being made.

`σ_sr`, `σ_sub`, Stable ISP standing or other coordinates may be used only as model-local evidence/proxies after their own admission conditions are stated.

Do not invent a new universal occlusion-admission theorem in this wave.

Classification: REQUIRED CORRECTIVE.

## 4. d / d_c / health-pathology architecture

### Finding

The owner treats `d` as a universal continuous order parameter and `d_narrow`, `d_c`, `d_max` as structurally real phase boundaries. It labels the high region healthy, the middle region active occlusion and the low region B-phase pathology.

### Conflict

Canonical d is a stake-coupled concern / irreversible-risk sensitivity summary. It is not a general health score, moral score, perspective score or subjecthood score. A lower d may be pathological in a declared model, adaptive in another, or simply outside the model's admission conditions.

The existence, ordering and dynamical role of `d_narrow` and `d_c` have not been established universally.

### Required disposition

Retype the three-regime architecture to a P2/P3 declared pathology-model candidate.

Within a declared model one may use:

```text
healthy/supportive regime
active narrowing regime
locked narrowing regime
```

only relative to an independently declared criterion such as:

```text
relation-integrity;
viability / recovery capacity;
agent goal / commitment;
consequence-return / correction capacity;
domain-specific pathology criterion.
```

`d`, `d_c` and `d_narrow` are model coordinates/proxies in this architecture, not universal health boundaries.

Classification: REQUIRED CORRECTIVE.

## 5. Direction / epsilon backflow

### Finding

The owner currently uses `epsilon` as an intrinsic direction field:

```text
healthy: epsilon direction readable;
A phase: epsilon still readable but suppressed;
B phase: epsilon reading fails;
residual traces preserve epsilon direction contact;
direct epsilon contact can trigger return;
new L2 can stabilize toward epsilon direction.
```

### Conflict

Current T_dir discipline requires an independently typed, declared direction source. `epsilon_pg`, O0, primitive Selection, B-count or bare persistence do not supply universal direction.

### Required disposition

Replace bare epsilon-direction claims with declared-direction claims where a direction source has independently been admitted.

Allowed form:

```text
a formed system may lose access to / suppress / regain
an independently declared direction or discrepancy signal.
```

Natural, artistic, contemplative or extreme experiences may remain as domain hypotheses about reorientation events, but not as direct contact with a primitive epsilon field.

Classification: BLOCKING REQUIRED CORRECTIVE.

## 6. Gap traces / body / dreams

### Finding

The file gives five residual signal classes—somatic echo, dream fragments, affect mismatch, micro-openings and other-person rupture—and treats them as structural residual channels carrying `Psi_f_actual` / epsilon-direction information.

### Conflict

These are empirical/phenomenological hypotheses, not canonical structural consequences. D3-B1 also removed the inference that hidden burden or retained trace must persist merely because an earlier event occurred.

### Required disposition

Keep the taxonomy only as P3/P4 domain/phenomenology hypotheses or examples.

Do not state that these channels must exist, that dreams bypass A/B filtering, that bodily symptoms directly carry hidden `Psi_f_actual`, or that residual signals prove ongoing epsilon contact.

Classification: REQUIRED CORRECTIVE, salvageable as domain bridge.

## 7. Intervention windows / triggers / return

### Finding

The file's windows/triggers architecture is useful, but currently universalizes several claims:

```text
window + trigger are jointly necessary for return;
B-phase unilateral return is structurally impossible;
direct epsilon contact is one trigger;
vacuum period is structurally unavoidable;
return toward epsilon is the healthy direction.
```

### Conflict

D3-B1 requires lock-in and one-way transitions to be proved within declared dynamics. T_dir blocks bare epsilon direction. No universal theorem establishes that every locked formed system requires the same two-stage return architecture or mandatory vacuum period.

### Required disposition

Retain as a P2/P3 staged recovery model candidate:

```text
window-like reduction in lock-in
+ perturbation / trigger
+ transitional reorganization
```

but mark necessity/sufficiency, time course and unilateral-return impossibility as model hypotheses requiring proof/evidence.

`vacuum period` may remain a useful transition concept, not a universal unavoidable phase.

Classification: REQUIRED CORRECTIVE.

## 8. Responsibility / A-B phase

### Finding

The file infers responsibility directly from stage:

```text
A phase -> direct responsibility because perception is intact;
B phase -> reduced or absent responsibility because perception/control degraded.
```

### Conflict

Current normativity architecture distinguishes:

```text
O2-C relation-integrity
O2-A attributable responsibility
O2-M all-things-considered morality
```

O2-A requires a stronger agency/knowledge/control/reselectability gate. A/B phase may be relevant evidence, but cannot itself assign responsibility.

### Required disposition

Retype phase-to-responsibility language as bridge input only.

Allowed:

```text
A/B-like lock-in may affect evidence about knowledge, control,
reselectability or consequence access relevant to O2-A.
```

Not allowed:

```text
phase membership alone -> responsibility verdict.
```

Classification: BLOCKING REQUIRED CORRECTIVE.

## 9. Evil as objective canonical iff

### Finding

The owner currently states an objective P2 canonical iff definition of evil using:

```text
B-phase lock
+ burden externalization
+ active propagation
```

and says all three together objectively constitute evil.

### Conflict

O2-M remains OPEN. Generative substitution/objectification is a strong research direction but not complete moral wrongness automatically. A structural pathology owner cannot close all-things-considered moral adjudication by definition.

### Required disposition

Demote `evil` to a bridge label / structural-pattern candidate, or reframe the core as a non-moral pattern such as:

```text
propagating pathological occlusion / generative externalization pattern
```

The three-factor pattern may be useful for ethics/political downstream work, but:

```text
pattern -/> complete evil iff
pattern -/> final moral verdict
```

Any moral use must route to O2-C/O2-A/O2-M owners/bridges at their actual strength.

Classification: BLOCKING REQUIRED CORRECTIVE.

## 10. AI and collective categorical claims

### Finding

The file says categorical forms such as:

```text
AI has no embodied position / epsilon readability / endogenous bearing -> AI cannot occlude;
collectives can be in B phase;
```

### Conflict

D3-A does not permit subjecthood or non-subjecthood to be inferred from sigma or model class alone. Collective standing likewise requires independent formation/admission. Current AI architecture cannot be decided categorically by this owner.

### Required disposition

Replace with domain admission rules:

```text
no system—AI, biological, collective or institutional—is assigned
an occlusion phase until the relevant formed-system / stake / access /
lineage conditions for that declared model are independently established.
```

AI output bias may be analyzed as distributional/model bias without anthropomorphizing it as occlusion. But the stronger universal claim “AI cannot occlude” is not licensed here.

Classification: REQUIRED CORRECTIVE.

Supportive/pathological classification must be relative to an independently declared criterion, not a primitive direction.
