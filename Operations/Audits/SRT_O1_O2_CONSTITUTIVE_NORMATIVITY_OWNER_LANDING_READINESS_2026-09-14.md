It does **not** mean:

```text
persistence = good;
option count = good;
health = good by definition;
subjective preference = value;
Bearer standing = required universally;
```


No single current owner legitimately defines O1a.


### Readiness

```text
owner placement: NOT YET FIXED

```

Do not put O1a inside Bearer or agency. First prepare a dedicated normativity semantic owner / interface that references One / Stable ISP / generative-reselectability owners without redefining them.

# 6. O2-C — constitutive ought / relation-integrity norm

## Central result

O2-C asks:

```text
What must a relation do / not do if it is to remain the kind of generative relation it claims to be?
```

Its current strongest core is **participant-status consistency**:

```text
if R counts Y as a load-bearing generative participant,
R cannot coherently preserve that type-description while Y's Selection is recurrently displaced
and another position becomes the effective source of Y's future relation-state.
```

The correct first response may be:

```text
repair the relation;
or
retype the relation honestly.
```

This is weaker and cleaner than a universal command to preserve every position.

## Why current owners cannot own O2-C alone

### L0 / Generative Spine

Too upstream.

O2-C already presupposes formed positions and typed relations. Putting it in L0 would reintroduce primitive morality / teleology.

### One / Stable ISP

They own formation / standing, not normative relation integrity.

### Bearer / Concern

Too strong and too late for the universal structural case. O2-C can diagnose a falsely typed participation relation before moral-stake standing is proven.

### Ethics / Agency

Too downstream if used as the foundation. Agency is required for O2-A, not for O2-C's relation-level consistency.

### Collective Selection

Too domain-specific and too strong as a universal admission requirement.

## Existing adjacent support

`Core/SRT_Core_24_Normativity_Anti_Optimization_Interface.md` already contains compatible anti-scalar / non-substitutability / answerability themes and rejects normativity as scalar optimization.

But it is:

```text
canonical: false;
bridge-hardening;
concern / d / Psi_f specific;
adjacent-theory dependent.
```

It therefore cannot simply become the owner of O2-C unchanged.

## Owner recommendation

O2-C likely requires a **dedicated normativity semantic owner / interface**, but **not a new ontological layer**.

Its scope should be:

```text
position-relative generative comparison;
cross-position relation typing;
participant-status consistency;
generative substitution / objectification;
partial-order dominance;
actualisation closure != generative closure;
O2-C / O2-A / O2-M boundary routing.
```

It must explicitly depend on current formation / standing owners rather than redefine them.

### Readiness

```text
core distinction: STRONG
anti-smuggling guards: STRONG
owner need: YES, likely dedicated semantic interface
exact canonical wording: NOT YET READY
P-level: NOT YET ASSIGNED
```
