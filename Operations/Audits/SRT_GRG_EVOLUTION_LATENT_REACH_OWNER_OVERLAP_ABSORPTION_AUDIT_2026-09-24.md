## 2. Mature source-side baseline retained

### 2.1 Rutherford & Lindquist 1998 — Hsp90 capacitance

Rutherford & Lindquist reported that impaired Hsp90 in *Drosophila* exposes background-dependent morphological variation produced, in their experiments, by multiple previously silent genetic determinants. They further reported that Hsp90 normally buffers such variation; when buffering is compromised, including under temperature stress, cryptic variants can be expressed and subsequent selection can enrich phenotypes that later persist even when Hsp90 function is restored.

Source:

- Rutherford, S.L. & Lindquist, S. (1998), “Hsp90 as a capacitor for morphological evolution”, *Nature* 396:336–342.
- DOI: 10.1038/24550

Source-side minimum for this realization:

```text
pre-existing background-dependent genetic differences
+
ordinary Hsp90-dependent buffering
+
buffering perturbation
+
phenotypic release
+
possible later selection / assimilation
```

Scope guard:

```text
Rutherford & Lindquist realization
-/>
all Hsp90-associated phenotypic release is necessarily
release of pre-existing cryptic variation.
```

### 2.2 Queitsch, Sangster & Lindquist 2002 — cross-organism realization

In *Arabidopsis*, reduced Hsp90 function likewise exposed genotype-dependent morphological variation and altered responses to environmental cues.

Source:

- Queitsch, C., Sangster, T.A. & Lindquist, S. (2002), “Hsp90 as a capacitor of phenotypic variation”, *Nature* 417:618–624.
- DOI: 10.1038/nature749

This supports Hsp90 buffering / release as a biological mechanism family rather than a one-off fly description.

### 2.3 Paaby & Rockman 2014 — mature CGV framing

The mature CGV review defines cryptic genetic variation as genetic variation with little or no ordinary phenotypic effect that can contribute heritable phenotypic variation under atypical conditions. Buffering / canalization is one route by which such variation can remain cryptic and later become exposed.

Source:

- Paaby, A.B. & Rockman, M.V. (2014), “Cryptic genetic variation: evolution's hidden substrate”, *Nature Reviews Genetics* 15:247–258.
- DOI: 10.1038/nrg3688

### 2.4 Bergman & Siegal 2003 / Masel 2005 — capacitance is already a general evolutionary problem

Bergman & Siegal explicitly generalized evolutionary capacitance beyond Hsp90. Their gene-network analysis treats buffering of genotypic variation, later release of phenotypic variation, and adaptive consequence as a general possible property of complex genetic networks rather than an Hsp90-specific curiosity.

Source:

- Bergman, A. & Siegal, M.L. (2003), “Evolutionary capacitance as a general feature of complex gene networks”, *Nature* 424:549–552.
- DOI: 10.1038/nature01765

Masel subsequently analyzed conditions under which evolutionary capacitance itself may be favored by natural selection.

Source:

- Masel, J. (2005), “Evolutionary Capacitance May Be Favored by Natural Selection”, *Genetics* 170:1359–1371.
- DOI: 10.1534/genetics.105.040493

Audit consequence:

```text
storage / buffering / release / later selection
is already a mature biological problem family;
the evolution case cannot claim that sequence itself as GRG gain.
```

### 2.5 Specchia et al. 2010 — release is not always pre-existing CGV

Specchia et al. showed that altered Hsp90 function can disrupt piRNA-mediated transposon silencing, activate transposons, and generate new variation through mutagenesis.

Source:

- Specchia, V. et al. (2010), “Hsp90 prevents phenotypic variation by suppressing the mutagenic activity of transposons”, *Nature* 463:662–665.
- DOI: 10.1038/nature08739

This matters for the audit because:

```text
phenotypic variation after buffer disruption
-/>
the relevant difference necessarily existed beforehand.
```

A realization claiming latent reconstructive reach must therefore independently establish the retained difference. “Release after perturbation” alone does not pay that burden.

### 2.6 Canalization / buffering scope guard

Canalization / developmental robustness is a mature problem family tied to genotype–phenotype mapping, buffering and evolvability.

Important guards:

```text
Hsp90
!= universal canalization mechanism;

buffering
!= only one mechanism;

release after perturbation
!= proof of pre-existing cryptic difference.
```

Therefore this audit claims mature ownership of the general buffering / perturbation / release problem family, not a universal Hsp90 mechanism.

## 4. Latent reconstructive reach — audit-local operational packaging

The following is an **M-level audit-local packaging of already present owner burdens**.

It is **not**:

- a new canonical definition;
- a new scientific gain from evolution;
- a new falsifiable burden that rescues the fusion-case line;
- a reason to open a third domain;
- a reason to create v0.4.

The inherited distinction:

```text
stored information
!= generatively live memory / latent reconstructive reach
```

already exists in the 2026-09-22 owner. The contribution of this audit is only to package the existing burden more explicitly for later testing.

```text
LR-0  latency gate
      At the declared grain / condition, the relevant difference is currently
      compressed, screened or local rather than already exerting the wider
      consequence that the claim is about.

LR-1  retained difference
      The relevant difference itself must be shown to pre-exist the claimed
      reopening episode. Do not substitute “capacity” for the difference here:
      capacity already includes conditional organization and would collapse
      later LR burdens into LR-1.

LR-2  real bridge / pathway
      A bounded causal / organizational relation must currently exist by which
      a later declared condition could reconnect the retained difference to
      world-side consequence.

LR-3  evidentially discriminable bridge
      The bridge claim must have evidence that discriminates it from mere
      descriptive possibility. Intervention-sensitive evidence is especially
      strong where available, but direct intervention is not universally
      mandatory; mechanistic evidence, natural experiments, comparative
      perturbations or convergent observational evidence may also support the
      claim when rival accounts are declared.

LR-4  conditional world-side reopening
      The claimed pathway entails that, under the declared reopening condition,
      the retained difference would regain world-side consequence / participation
      in later dynamics. Actual observed re-entry is strong realization evidence,
      but prior actualisation is not required for the capacity / pathway to be real.

LR-5  scope declaration
      Declare the relevant condition, intervention / evidence family,
      consequence burden, grain and horizon.
```

This packaging distinguishes:

```text
stored difference alone
-/>
latent reconstructive reach
```

because storage may pay LR-1 while failing LR-0 or LR-2 through LR-4.

It also distinguishes:

```text
currently active consequence
-/>
latent reach
```

because latent reach requires the LR-0 suppression / locality condition.

And Specchia et al. supplies a useful source-side negative control:

```text
phenotype appears after Hsp90 disruption
-/>
the causally relevant difference necessarily pre-existed disruption.
```

That is why LR-1 cannot be inferred from “release” language alone.
