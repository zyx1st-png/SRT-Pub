## 1. Source-fidelity split

Two source burdens must remain separate.

### A. CIV-005 beaver evidence

Current record pays:

~~~text

-> modified hydrology / storage / sediment / connectivity / habitat
-> environmental effects can persist beyond immediate construction
-> conditions for other organisms are reorganized.
~~~

It strongly pays X4a.

It does not independently claim a transgenerational / successor-cycle inheritance result.

The broader source-native theory pays:

~~~text

-> modified environmental state persists
-> descendants / later organisms encounter changed conditions
~~~

That supports ecological inheritance as a source-native family.

But:

~~~text
general ecological inheritance
!=
beaver-specific X4c evidence automatically.
~~~

The broader source cannot retroactively fill a missing beaver-specific burden.

## 4. New admission-order guard

The Role Library needs an explicit order rule.

Bad loop:

~~~text
persistent environment
-> call it SUBSTRATE
-> carrier role exists
-> therefore X4c inheritance exists.
~~~

Reject.

Correct order:

~~~text
1. identify candidate reconstructed field F;

2. declare successor cohort / cycle;

3. show enough transmission / persistence of F
   across that successor boundary;

4. show successor possibilities are materially changed by F;

5. only then award COMPONENT-ROLE-PASS
   inside an admitted X4c carrier architecture.
~~~

Before steps 2–4 are paid:

~~~text
role label status =
ROLE-CANDIDATE / PRE-ADMISSION
~~~

Such a candidate may guide evidence search.

It cannot strengthen relation maturity.

## 5. Missing evidence made explicit

The old negative statement was:

~~~text
persistence != inheritance.
~~~

Role Library + admission-order analysis sharpens this into four missing fields:

~~~text
A. declared successor boundary;

B. evidence that the beaver-modified field remains identifiable
   across that boundary;

C. evidence that the successor enters under the field
   without making the original reconstruction choice;

D. evidence that the inherited field changes successor
   accessibility / viability / transition / action possibilities.
~~~

A useful source-native near-control would need to isolate at least part of:

~~~text
inherited modified condition
vs
comparable condition where the inherited modification
is absent / lost / bypassed
~~~

while preserving the ecological mechanism as much as the domain allows.

This audit does not claim such a control is already present.
