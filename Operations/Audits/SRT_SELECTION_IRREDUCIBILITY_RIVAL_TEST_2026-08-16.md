# Selection Irreducibility Rival Test — Pass 1

> **Role**: executed non-canonical audit record under `Governance/SRT_GOV_SUB01_Subtractive_Audit_Protocol_v0_1.md`.
>
> **Scope**: test how much of the primitive role currently carried by `selection` in `P0-01 / AM-A` can be removed or reconstructed under declared refit budgets.
>
> **Hard boundary**: this record does **not** modify `P0-01`, `P0-04`, `P1-T05`, or any canonical definition. It does not prove selection fundamental, and it does not prove selection unreal.
>
> **Residue gate**: `Core/SRT_OPEN_TENSIONS.md §13` currently allows a formal GOV-SUB01 residue label for `selection` only after at least one qualifying exhibit is produced against the reduced vocabulary: differential counterfactual (`E_cf`), differential intervention (`E_int`), an executed real-choice/script divergence the reduced vocabulary cannot carry, or a demonstrable `E_phen / E_norm` loss. Pass 1 produces none of those exhibits. Therefore **formal GOV-SUB01 residue remains `UNASSIGNED`**. All `R* / N* / P` references below are analysis-level **candidate readings only**, not attached residue labels.

## 0. Executive outcome

**Pass-1 outcome: K-budget analysis executed; formal GOV-SUB01 residue remains `UNASSIGNED`.**

The three refit budgets produce different analytical pressures that must not be collapsed:

1. **`K=0`**: the current architecture literally depends on `selection`; deletion causes immediate loss in the P0 grammar and several downstream constitutive statements. This establishes implementation dependence only.
2. **limited `K`**: much of the downstream work can be reconstructed from a frozen non-selection vocabulary—historical asymmetry, reachable-set change, bearer-specific consequence return, and much of the real-choice/script distinction—but the primitive actualisation role cannot be counted as removed without either changing the target or risking hidden reparameterization.
3. **broad `K`**: substantially different process/causal models can reproduce most currently operationalized downstream targets. This demonstrates substantial representational substitutability at the level of current descriptions. It does **not** establish absence of the underlying actualisation role and does not satisfy the §13 residue gate because no external counterfactual/interventional divergence was executed.

Therefore this pass supports neither overclaim:

```text
P0 primitive status -> selection is proven ontologically irreducible     [NOT LICENSED]

broad-K reconstruction -> selection is merely a word and can be deleted [NOT LICENSED]
```

What Pass 1 *does* establish is narrower and useful:

> the vocabulary-level question is substantially saturated; many downstream roles are reconstructable, while the remaining pressure concentrates on primitive actualisation and on whether retaining `selection` yields externally judgeable differential leverage.

The next gate is **rival-model adjudication at `E_int / E_cf`**, not another vocabulary-only deletion pass.

### 1.6 Perturbation set `P`

**Pass-1 execution status: not executed as a robustness sweep.** This is a deliberate limitation and blocks any robust dispensability/indispensability residue claim.

The frozen perturbation families for a later pass are:

1. ordinary case — multiple non-equivalent continuations with persistent writeback;
2. boundary case — same endpoint, different history;
3. adversarial alias case — rival introduces a renamed determinacy / resolution primitive;
4. path-dependent case — prior writeback changes later reachable states or return cost;
5. bearer relocation case — aggregate behavior preserved while cost/repair burden moves to another bearer;
6. delayed-failure case — immediate output preserved but future reachability or recovery differs;
7. scale-change case — event-level reconstruction succeeds but cross-scale translation changes.

No claim in this record may be called **robust across contexts** until this set, or a stricter preregistered replacement, is actually run.

### 1.7 Threshold `tau`

No scalar aggregate is used. A target counts as analytically unreconstructed only when the declared distinction cannot be carried without:

1. target laundering;
2. bearer relocation;
3. hidden reparameterization; or
4. introducing a new primitive that performs the deleted selection role under another name.

Because §13 imposes an additional exhibit gate, crossing this analytical threshold is **not sufficient** to attach a formal residue label to `selection`.

| Role originally carried in SRT grammar | Rival obligation |

If the six roles are merely redistributed across renamed functions, only **label-level dispensability** has been shown.

### 4.6 `Y1 Manifest actuality` — AM-A register separated from GOV-SUB01 register

The rival can say “an ordinary transition yields a determinate event,” but two readings must be separated:

1. **weak reading**: the model begins with an already objectified state space and reports which state obtains. This may be a legitimate rival ontology, but it drops the pre-objectification burden built into `Y1`.
2. **strong reading**: the model adds a primitive transition by which non-objectified potential becomes determinate. If this is merely renamed “resolution,” “determinacy,” or an unspecified kernel, deletion fails by hidden reparameterization.

Two registers must remain separate:

- **AM-A / Claim-Ladder register**: primitive actualisation remains an author-adopted P0 primitive admission. This author decision is still in force and is **not reopened** by Pass 1.
- **GOV-SUB01 residue register**: `UNASSIGNED`. Pass 1 does not attach `P`, `R4`, `N1`, or any other formal residue label to `selection` or to the Y1 role.

This separation avoids both errors: reopening AM-A as a mere technical TODO, and treating AM-A itself as proof of cross-vocabulary irreducibility.

## 5. broad `K` — substantially different rival model

### 5.1 Rival family admitted

Broad K may use a substantially different process ontology / state-transition / structural-causal architecture, provided it:

- states its primitives explicitly;
- does not define success by reproducing SRT vocabulary;
- preserves declared targets without moving bearer or horizon when claiming target preservation;
- exposes where determinacy, writeback, and reachable-set change enter;
- does not hide the full deleted role inside an unexplained operator.

### 5.2 Result

For `Y2`, `Y3` as an operational event classifier, and `Y4`, broad rival families can in principle reproduce most currently specified behavior using ordinary causal-history machinery.

For `Y1`, broad K can instead adopt an ontology in which determinate states/events are primitive or a complete transition space is assumed. That is a legitimate theoretical replacement, but it does not preserve the exact SRT pre-objectification target. Conversely, requiring a rival to preserve the target's SRT-specific pre-objectification commitment risks making the test circular.

This is the central methodological result of Pass 1:

> a metaphysical starting point cannot be declared empirically necessary merely because a rival that rejects the starting point does not preserve the starting point's own vocabulary.

The same warning applies retrospectively to limited K: unresolvedness at `Y1` partly reflects the coupling between the locked target and the allowed vocabulary.

The deciding burden therefore moves to `Y5`: externally judgeable differences in intervention, counterfactual, prediction, or bounded explanatory constraint.

### 5.3 broad-K outcome

Analysis-level pattern:

- downstream structural roles show substantial representational / implementation substitutability;
- primitive actualisation is not adjudicated by vocabulary replacement alone;
- no cross-context indispensability or dispensability result is licensed.

**Formal GOV-SUB01 residue: `UNASSIGNED`.**
