# Bearer / “From Here” Residual Novelty Audit — 2026-08-28

> **Boundary:** focused second-pass novelty audit after the upstream Selection/articulation subtraction. This file does not establish uniqueness, does not promote any claim, and does not modify canonical consciousness or individuation owners. Its purpose is to identify which parts of the current `object -> bearer -> subject -> experiencer` research programme remain potentially distinctive after comparison with autonomy/enactivism, Damasio-style organismic subjectivity, unity-of-consciousness literature, personal-identity/fission work, and leading neural theories.

---

## 0. Executive result

The second-pass subtraction is substantial.

The following are **not safely novel**:

```text
self-individuation can generate an autonomous unity
an autonomous organism can generate its own perspective
precarious viability can ground system-relative normativity / concern
organism-environment coupling can be evaluated relative to self-maintenance
interoception can index heterogeneous contents to one organism
body-state feeling + sensory perspective can support subjectivity
object unity / subject unity / phenomenal unity are distinct notions
split-brain cases can dissociate organism identity, access unity and phenomenal unity
psychological / memory continuity is insufficient for numerical identity under branching
copying / fission creates a classic one-to-many identity problem
neural theories can address conscious-system boundaries / grains rather than simply assume them
```

The strongest originality candidate is now narrower:

> **SRT may have a distinctive architecture if it can operationally separate re-identifiable object unity, autonomous/viability unity, bearer-specific consequence closure, historically constituted selector-position, and phenomenal unity, while preserving a non-phenomenal primitive Selection floor and an explicit open bridge to phenomenality.**

The strongest candidate discriminator inside that architecture remains:

```text
same-unit consequence return
+ history writeback
+ non-outsourcing / non-substitutability
+ future-selectability change
```

but even this is **not yet established novel**. Its burden is now explicitly comparative:

> Can two systems matched on self-maintenance, autonomy, adaptivity, vulnerability, current affordances and ordinary persistence differ on consequence-closure / outsourcing architecture in a way that supports independent prediction or classification?

If not, the bearer package should be read as a useful decomposition / translation of autonomy and identity theory rather than a new scientific construct.

## 2. Autopoiesis / autonomy / enactivism — major originality pressure

### Primary anchors

- Mossio & Moreno (2010), *Organisational closure in biological organisms*, History and Philosophy of the Life Sciences 32:269–288.
- Barandiaran, Di Paolo & Rohde (2009), *Defining Agency: Individuality, Normativity, Asymmetry, and Spatio-temporality in Action*, Adaptive Behavior 17(5):367–386. DOI: 10.1177/1059712309343819.
- Di Paolo (2018), *The Enactive Conception of Life*, in *The Oxford Handbook of 4E Cognition*.
- Recent enactive literature continues to connect autopoiesis/autonomy, precarious self-individuation, perspective, sense-making and subjectivity.

### Prior-art pressure

Autonomous enactivism already claims, in various strengths, that:

```text
a system can define / produce its own individuality
organizational closure can constitute an autonomous unity
precarious self-maintenance generates intrinsic normativity
adaptivity regulates organism-environment coupling relative to viability
self-individuation establishes a perspective / reference point
what happens can matter for the continued existence of that unity
agency includes individuality + interactional asymmetry + normativity
```

Barandiaran et al. are especially strong prior art because they explicitly identify the individuality problem as the problem of justifying why a system boundary is intrinsic rather than observer-imposed, and define agency as an autonomous organization that adaptively regulates its coupling while contributing to sustaining itself.

### Unsafe SRT claims blocked

Do not claim:

```text
SRT uniquely explains how a living unity defines itself
SRT uniquely derives a perspective from self-maintaining organization
SRT uniquely discovers intrinsic normativity from vulnerability
SRT uniquely discovers that consequences matter relative to one organism's viability
SRT uniquely distinguishes agent individuality from observer convenience
```

### Residual SRT question

The possible SRT increment is not `vulnerability` or `perspective` by itself. It is:

> **Does consequence non-outsourcing identify a relation that can vary independently of organizational autonomy / viability regulation?**

Required future discrimination:

```text
match:
- autonomy
- persistence
- adaptive regulation
- vulnerability / viability sensitivity
- current affordance field

vary:
- whether cost / damage / correction closes on the same continuing unit
- whether the candidate can be reset / replaced / checkpoint-restored while cost closes elsewhere
- whether the consequence rewrites that same unit's later selectability
```

If the matched cases cannot be constructed coherently, or autonomy already fixes the consequence-closure answer, PH-IND06 novelty weakens sharply.

## 3. Damasio / interoception — major pressure on `from here`

### Primary anchors

- A. Damasio & H. Damasio (2024), *Homeostatic Feelings and the Emergence of Consciousness*, Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience 36(8):1653–1659. DOI: 10.1162/jocn_a_02119.
- Current Damasio work proposes a three-part architecture: continuous interoceptive feeling, sensory images from the organism's perspective, and their combination into subjectivity relative to image contents.

### Prior-art pressure

Damasio explicitly supplies a biological route close to:

```text
body-state continuity
-> organismic feeling
-> sensory perspective
-> content-for-this-organism
-> subjectivity
```

Recent work further describes the felt organism as the anchor of a subjective perspective and the biological origin of a self process.

Therefore `from here`, when stated merely as:

```text
ongoing bodily state provides one organism-centred perspective
```

is not a safe novelty claim.

### SRT residual distinction

SRT's sharper distinction remains:

```text
for-this-bearer relevance
!= phenomenal for-me-ness
```

and:

```text
interoceptive bearer-indexing
!= bearer identity theorem
!= cross-time subjecthood
!= phenomenality proof
```

Damasio makes feeling itself intrinsically / spontaneously conscious in the positive account. SRT currently tries to leave open:

```text
structural / indexical bearer position
-> ?
-> phenomenal presence
```

This is a real conceptual difference, but it is not automatically an advantage. SRT must show that `bearing` adds a discriminating constraint rather than simply relocating Damasio's stopping point from `feeling` to `bearing`.

## 4. Unity-of-consciousness literature — taxonomy largely pre-empted

### Prior-art pressure

The unity literature already distinguishes multiple forms, including:

```text
object / objectual unity
subject unity
access unity
representational unity
spatial unity
neurophysiological unity
phenomenal unity
synchronic vs diachronic unity
```

Bayne, Chalmers, Tye and later reviews explicitly separate subject unity from phenomenal unity, and split-brain work asks whether one organism / subject can support one, two, or partially unified conscious streams.

### Unsafe SRT claims blocked

Do not claim:

```text
SRT uniquely distinguishes object unity from subject unity
SRT uniquely distinguishes subject unity from phenomenal unity
SRT uniquely notices that split-brain cases pressure one-body = one-conscious-field assumptions
```

### Residual SRT question

The possible increment is **generative ordering and typed admission**, not taxonomy:

```text
actual occurrence
!-> persistent trace
!-> re-identifiable object
!-> bearer-indexed history
!-> selector / subject position
!-> phenomenal field
```

The novelty burden is whether this ladder yields distinctions or experimental designs not already captured by ordinary access / binding / phenomenal-unity taxonomies.

## 5. Personal identity / fission — branching problem is established prior art

### Prior-art pressure

Personal-identity literature has long established that psychological continuity faces branching / fission problems. A perfect duplicate or two equally continuous descendants cannot both be numerically identical to one predecessor under ordinary identity logic. Non-branching clauses, best-candidate views, reductionist responses and animalist alternatives are mature territory.

Therefore the intuition:

```text
memory possession != numerical identity
shared past != one continuing person after branching
```

is not novel.

### Residual SRT question

PH-IND05's potentially useful increment is operational rather than metaphysical:

> **Can bearer branching be detected through counterfactual consequence separability rather than settled by a prior metaphysics of numerical identity?**

Candidate diagnostic:

```text
B0 -> B1, B2

if consequences can close independently on B1 and B2
and their histories / future selectability can be independently rewritten,
then evidence for distinct post-branch bearers strengthens.
```

This does not solve personal identity. Its value would be as a domain-neutral discrimination tool for organisms, split systems, digital agents, institutions or distributed processes.

Failure condition:

> If the diagnostic merely redescribes causal independence / system decomposition without adding a bearer-relevant prediction, treat it as translation-only.

## 6. IIT changes the manuscript's individuation claim

### Critical correction

It is unsafe to say:

```text
neural theories of consciousness simply assume an already-individuated system
```

because IIT explicitly contains an individuation / boundary programme.

IIT 3.0/4.0 uses the exclusion principle to identify a definite conscious substrate / `complex` as a maximally irreducible set of units at a definite spatial and temporal grain. Overlapping lower-integration candidate substrates are excluded.

Thus IIT asks, in a formal way:

```text
which units?
which border?
which grain?
which irreducible whole?
```

### What remains distinct

IIT's boundary criterion is:

```text
maximal intrinsic irreducible cause-effect power
```

SRT bearer admission asks a different candidate question:

```text
where do consequences close as the history of a continuing unit,
and can the cost be outsourced while the claimed bearer is restored unchanged?
```

No equivalence is established:

```text
IIT complex
!= SRT bearer by definition
```

and neither direction is licensed.

### Strong manuscript implication

The Collection paper should therefore **not** argue that neural theories lack individuation.

A stronger and more accurate thesis is:

> **Neural and consciousness theories employ different unity / individuation relations—causal irreducibility, global access, recurrent content formation, organismic interoceptive indexing, bodily selfhood, and others. The unresolved question is whether these relations identify the same explanatory unit, and which of them licenses a bearer, a subject, or a phenomenal field.**

This is more defensible against IIT and more integrative for the Collection.

## 7. GNWT / workspace theories — access is not bearer individuation by default

GNWT characterizes conscious access through global amplification / broadcasting of selected content to distributed systems supporting memory, planning, evaluation, report and intentional action.

This gives strong accounts of:

```text
content availability
large-scale access
broadcast / ignition
flexible cognitive use
```

It does not, by those constructs alone, settle:

```text
which continuing unit owns consequences
whether the workspace boundary equals a bearer boundary
whether globally available content is phenomenally owned by one subject
```

However, workspace-based accounts of the self do exist, so the safe SRT claim is not that GNWT has `no self`. The narrower question is whether workspace-level unity and bearer consequence closure can dissociate.

Candidate future contrast:

```text
matched global availability / reportability
+ differing bearer-indexed consequence architecture
-> test ownership / persistence / affective / future-policy differences
```

This remains P4 design work, not an established result.

## 10. Strongest residual package after two subtraction rounds

The current strongest package is:

```text
1. primitive Selection / actualisation is non-phenomenal and requires no prior chooser;
2. occurrence does not imply persistence;
3. persistence / re-identifiability does not imply bearerhood;
4. autonomy / viability / perspective do not automatically settle same-unit consequence ownership;
5. bearer admission is audited through consequence return, history writeback, non-outsourcing and future-selectability effects;
6. selector / subject position can be historically constituted rather than primitive;
7. bearer / subject structure does not automatically establish phenomenality;
8. copying / branching is handled through continuation and consequence-separability rather than qualitative similarity alone;
9. neural theories are compared by which unity relation they operationalize, not by assuming they all ignore individuation;
10. scientific distinctiveness must be paid by downstream P3/P4 contrasts, not claimed from ontology alone.
```

No single item above is safely unique. The candidate contribution is the **typed dependency architecture plus discriminating programme**.

## 11. Consequence for the Neural Models of Consciousness Perspective

### Retire this framing

```text
Current neural theories begin with an already individuated system
and therefore ignore individuation.
```

IIT is a direct counterexample, and Damasio/enactivism supply additional individuation / perspective accounts.

### Prefer this framing

> **Consciousness science contains multiple, non-equivalent ways of making a neural or organismic process `one`: irreducible causal complexes, globally accessible workspaces, recurrently bound percepts, autonomous organisms, interoceptively indexed selves, and phenomenally unified fields. These are often compared as if they were alternative answers to one question. A selection-first framework instead asks which kind of unity each construction establishes, and which additional transitions are required before object unity becomes bearer unity, subject-position, or phenomenal presence.**

### Candidate central question

```text
Which unity does a neural model of consciousness explain?
```

with a stronger subtitle-level contrast:

```text
causal whole
!= access whole
!= organismic bearer
!= phenomenal field
```

```text

            v

            |
      + additional tests

 consequence-bearing continuity
```

Alongside it, map competing frameworks horizontally to the rung / relation they most directly operationalize rather than treating them as simple rivals at one level.

## 12. Next required work

The residual package is now narrow enough that further broad philosophy search has diminishing returns. The next high-value task is **prospective discriminator design**.

Priority tests:

1. **Autonomy-matched / outsourcing-different cases** — construct matched systems where self-maintenance and adaptivity are comparable but consequence closure differs.
2. **Access-matched / bearing-different cases** — match reportability/global availability while varying organism/bearer-indexed consequence coupling.
3. **IIT-complex / bearer-boundary mismatch cases** — ask whether a maximally irreducible causal complex and a consequence-return bearer can in principle select different units/grains.
4. **Branching / rollback digital systems** — compare copied, checkpoint-restored and irreversibly history-bearing agents under the PH-IND05/06 diagnostic.
5. **Clinical dissociations** — depersonalization, body-ownership disorders, anosognosia, split-brain and disorders of consciousness as candidate cases where object, access, organismic and phenomenal unity need not move together.

A workline should be abandoned as SRT-distinctive if its predictions reduce to ordinary autonomy, causal integration, global access, arousal, interoception, state persistence, or generic causal independence after matched controls.

---

## 13. Bottom line

After this pass, the defensible ambition is narrower but stronger:

> **SRT should not claim to have discovered individuation, autonomy, bodily perspective, unity, or the branching problem. Its potential contribution is a disciplined dependency architecture in which different forms of `one` are prevented from doing each other's explanatory work, and in which bearerhood is subjected to an additional consequence-ownership / non-outsourcing audit before subjectivity or phenomenality is inferred.**

The Collection paper becomes stronger if it presents this as a framework for reorganizing and testing neural models, not as a claim that consciousness science has never considered boundaries, selves, or unity.
