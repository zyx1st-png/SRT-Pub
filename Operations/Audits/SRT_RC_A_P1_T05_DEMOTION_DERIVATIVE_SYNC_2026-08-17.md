# RC-A — P1-T05 Demotion / Derivative Sync

> **Author decision (2026-08-17): A.** Former `P1-T05: Real Choice Moment` is demoted from P1 to a P2/P3 agency / subjecthood guard. SRT primarily theorizes `Selection`; no new `Choice` subtype is introduced.
>
> Governing owner: `Core/SRT_Core_21b_Constitutive_Theorems.md`.  
> Downstream landing: `03_Bridges/SRT_Agency_Automation_Guard_2026-08-17.md`.
>
> **Consolidation read rule**：RC-A 是已经完成的 author-level claim decision。下游旧文件尚未完成逐段文本同步时，旧字面引用不得继续获得被撤销的 P1 权威。文本 burn-down 的任务是**消除歧义与错误依赖**，不是重新裁决 RC-A。

## 1. Architecture after RC-A

```text
P0/P1 Selection core
-> actualisation / irreversibility / L2 constraint / minimal distinction creation
-> P1-T06 Stable ISP remains canonical

P2/P3
-> CG / SEA: bounded selection-event audit surface
-> Agency / Automation Guard: blocks overpromotion from automation to agency
-> T_dir: self-readability / reorientation proxy under its own guards
```

Blocked inference:

```text
script / habit / gradient / L2 automation
-> no Selection
```

Automation can carry mature selective convergence and coexist with ongoing selection at other scales / dimensions.

## 2. A2 / A3 supersession

A2/A3 remain historical adversarial evidence that:

- no D2 discriminator was obtained for the former real-choice / agency boundary;
- no positive non-circular `live` variable was ready for empirical execution;
- ordinary causal-history models absorb many candidate agency markers.

After RC-A, their former `S_core / P1-T05` judge is read only as a **historical agency-boundary judge**, not as authority over Selection itself.

They may not be cited to infer that scripts are selection-free or that failure to define `live` directly weakens P0-01.

## 3. `OPEN_TENSIONS` §13 / §14 read rule

### §13 Selection irreducibility

The former residue route requiring `P1-T05 real-choice vs script` divergence is retired as a primitive-Selection gate.

Selection irreducibility must be earned at Selection level, especially through prospective `E_cf` / `E_int` difference or another independently specified explanatory loss against a bounded rival.

Where §13 still literally cites `P1-T05`, that clause is superseded by RC-A.

### §14 selection-event operationalization

The former open question

```text
P1-T05 <-> CG-0..CG-4
```

is closed by claim-level separation, not by proving necessity or sufficiency.

Still open:

1. domain calibration / derivation of CG thresholds;
2. whether CG gives D2 gain over bounded ordinary causal-transition rivals.

CG pass licenses only **bounded selection-event candidate** and does not prove agency, subjecthood, consciousness or freedom.

## 4. T_dir active-use override

Legacy `_SRT_T_DIR_CANONICAL.md` phrases such as `活选择`, `真实选择`, or `L2 替代选择` predate RC-A.

They may no longer be used as a Selection ontology distinction. In post-RC-A use they are only shorthand for the narrower bearer / agency question:

> Is there a currently consequence-sensitive, direction-readable opportunity for reorientation at the relevant bearer level, or is that level pre-empted by an inherited scaffold / policy?

This creates no new formal term.

Explicitly forbidden:

```text
L2 automation
-> no Selection / no L0->L1 actualisation anywhere in the process
```

`T_dir` itself is not abolished or redefined by RC-A; only the old Choice-based explanatory jurisdiction is removed.

## 5. P1-T06 / CG status

`P1-T06 Stable ISP` remains P1. In the same pass, `live, non-equivalent candidates` is tightened to `currently effective, non-equivalent candidates`; `currently effective` is ordinary descriptive language, not a new concept.

CG / SEA remain Selection-facing P2/P3 audit apparatus. The correct-SHA control is therefore coherent:

```text
deterministic transaction + CG pass
!= stronger agency proved
```

No CG threshold is promoted.

## 6. Subtractive result

Removed from P1:

- `Real Choice Moment` as constitutive theorem;
- unexplained `live` qualifier at core level;
- anti-script exclusion as a Selection criterion;
- requirement that CG eventually reproduce former P1-T05.

Retained:

- Selection core;
- P1-T06 Stable ISP;
- CG operational surface;
- anti-script agency guard at P2/P3;
- A2/A3 negative evidence;
- open D2 burden.

No new canonical variable, equation, Choice subtype or ontology layer is added.

