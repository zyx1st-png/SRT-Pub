# RC-A — P1-T05 Demotion / Derivative Reclassification

> **Author decision (2026-08-17)**: choose **A**. Former `P1-T05: Real Choice Moment` is demoted from P1 to a P2/P3 agency / subjecthood guard. SRT primarily theorizes `Selection`; no new `Choice` terminology is introduced.
>
> **Governing owner**: `Core/SRT_Core_21b_Constitutive_Theorems.md`, former P1-T05 demotion block.
>
> **Downstream landing**: `03_Bridges/SRT_Agency_Automation_Guard_2026-08-17.md`.

---

## 0. Scope of the author decision

RC-A changes **claim level and explanatory jurisdiction**, not the existence of the anti-script observation.

Before RC-A, the repository could be read as:

```text
P1-T05 Real Choice Moment
-> anti-script exclusion
-> candidate criterion for “real” selection
```

After RC-A, the allowed reading is:

```text
Selection ontology
-> P0/P1 core

agency / subject-level strengthening
-> P2/P3 guard
-> script / habit / gradient / L2 automation are not sufficient by themselves
```

The crucial blocked inference is:

```text
scripted / habitual / gradient / L2-scaffolded
-> no Selection
```

No such inference survives RC-A.

---

## 1. Why the demotion was necessary

The author clarified three points during the 2026-08-17 discussion.

### 1.1 Selection, not Choice, is the theory's main axis

`Choice` should not proliferate into new formal subclasses such as `Live Choice`.

The temporary term was useful only because it exposed an ambiguity in former P1-T05. It is not retained as an SRT object.

### 1.2 Selective convergence continues through mature background scaffolds

Convergence toward stability is continuously operating. Some parts mature enough to become an `L2` floor / background scaffold and no longer require foreground regulation.

Therefore automation / habit / script can be the mature expression of prior selective convergence and can remain dynamically active.

### 1.3 Anti-script is an agency question

The scientifically defensible remainder is weaker:

> A scripted or automated process is not thereby shown to possess stronger agency / subject-level revision standing.

That is a P2/P3 sufficiency guard, not a P1 theorem of Selection.

---

## 2. A2 / A3 supersession rule

### 2.1 Historical value retained

A2 and A3 remain valid records of a failed attempt to obtain a prospective D2 discriminator for the former real-choice / agency boundary.

They correctly showed:

- ordinary causal-history models absorb many candidate markers;
- CG and the former P1-T05 could classify cases differently;
- no non-circular positive `live` variable was ready for empirical execution;
- no D2 prediction was achieved on the tested case set.

### 2.2 What is superseded

Any A2/A3 sentence that treats former P1-T05 as `S_core` authority over **Selection itself** is superseded by RC-A.

Fresh use must translate:

```text
former S_core / real-choice judge
```

into:

```text
historical agency-boundary judge
```

A2/A3 must no longer be cited to infer:

```text
scripted process = selection-free
```

or:

```text
failure to define “live” = failure of Selection ontology
```

### 2.3 No need to rewrite historical audit bodies

A2/A3 are evidence-bearing historical audit records. Their original execution logic is preserved for provenance.

This RC-A record is the supersession notice for all post-2026-08-17 use.

### Former dependency

§13's Selection Irreducibility residue gate included a route in which an executed case had to show former P1-T05 real-choice / script divergence that the reduced vocabulary could not carry.

### RC-A decision

That route is retired as a **Selection irreducibility gate**.

Selection irreducibility must not be discharged by proving a stronger agency distinction.

The live residue burden returns to genuinely Selection-level differences, especially:

```text
E_cf — prospective counterfactual difference
E_int — prospective intervention difference
```

or another independently specified Selection-level explanatory loss that cannot be recovered by competitor vocabulary.

`E_phen / E_norm` may still be relevant to downstream theories, but cannot by themselves establish the irreducibility of primitive Selection unless the dependency is separately argued.

### Use rule until the owner text is next compacted

Where §13 still literally cites `P1-T05`, read that clause as **superseded by RC-A**.

Do not execute a new “real choice vs script” test as the next required primitive-Selection test.

## 4. `Core/SRT_OPEN_TENSIONS.md §14` reclassification

### Former problem

§14 listed the relation:

```text
P1-T05 <-> CG-0..CG-4
```

as an unresolved necessary / sufficient / neither question.

### RC-A resolution

That mapping is no longer a core hardening requirement because the two surfaces now answer different questions.

```text
CG / SEA
-> bounded selection-event operational surface

Agency / Automation Guard
-> downstream agency / subjecthood sufficiency guard
```

Therefore the open branch:

> “Are CG gates necessary or sufficient for former P1-T05?”

is **closed by claim-level separation**, not by proving a logical relation.

### What remains open in §14

Two problems remain valid:

1. whether CG thresholds have domain-specific derivation / calibration rather than audit-default status;
2. whether CG adds D2 discriminating gain over bounded ordinary causal-transition models.

Passing all five gates continues to license only:

> **bounded selection-event candidate**.

It does not prove agency, stable ISP, subjecthood, consciousness, freedom, responsibility, or moral status.

## 6. T_dir active-usage override

`_SRT_T_DIR_CANONICAL.md` contains legacy phrases such as:

- `活选择`;
- `真实选择`;
- `L2 脚本执行，非 L0->L1 导航`;
- `L2 替代选择`.

These phrases predate RC-A and may no longer be used as a Selection ontology distinction.

### Governing replacement reading

When those phrases are encountered in active T_dir use, read them as shorthand for the narrower bearer / agency question:

> **Is there a currently consequence-sensitive, direction-readable opportunity for reorientation at the relevant bearer level, or is the relevant level fully scaffolded / pre-empted by an inherited policy?**

This is not a new formal term.

### Explicitly superseded inference

Do not read T_dir as saying:

```text
L2 automation
-> no Selection / no L0->L1 actualisation anywhere in the process
```

A mature `L2` floor can remain selection-bearing history and can coexist with lower-scale / other-dimensional selective convergence.

### T_dir variable status unchanged

RC-A does not abolish or redefine `T_dir`.

It only removes `live choice` / `real choice` language as an authority for Selection ontology.

A later owner-language cleanup may replace the legacy shorthand mechanically, but no new T_dir theorem is authorized here.

## 9. Scientific consequence

A2/A3's `NO-D2` remains scientifically relevant, but the burden is now properly localized.

### For primitive Selection

The open scientific challenge is still:

> Can SRT produce a prospective difference against a bounded ordinary rival that is not merely representational relabeling?

This belongs to Selection irreducibility / external convergence work.

### For agency

A separate challenge is:

> Can SRT develop a prospective stronger-agency discriminator beyond ordinary adaptive / meta-learning / control descriptions?

Failure on the second does not directly demote the first.

This separation prevents agency theory from carrying more ontological proof burden than it can currently support.
