# Reverse-neighbor Pass 0 corrective — burden preservation, source ownership and primitive triangulation

> **Role:** corrective overlay on the first reverse-neighbor pass. It does not erase the original R0 audit; it supersedes its stronger D2/D3 interpretations where stated here.
>
> **Canonical impact:** NONE.
>
> **Main correction:** “SRT can type / reconstruct / criticize a neighbor” is not enough. The stronger burden is whether SRT can preserve the explanatory job after removing the neighbor's thicker machinery.

## 1. Independent-review findings accepted

Accepted from the independent review of PR #1074:

1. diagnostic pressure documented in the literature is not automatically SRT diagnostic surplus;
2. the organizational-closure and ETI criticisms are already substantially owned by their criticism literature;
3. reconstruction claims require explicit assumption ledgers;
4. burden-replacement / explanatory-job preservation is mandatory;
5. neighbor-surplus must be recorded symmetrically;
6. option A + the primitive residue creates a universal pressure case: genuine reversible change;
7. future diagnostic work requires a blind-step protocol;
8. identifier collisions should be removed.

These corrections are incorporated into method v0.2.

## 2. Findings not accepted without qualification

### 2.1 Simondon: “preindividual = L2 potential landscape” is too strong

Do not harden:

~~~text
preindividual = formed L2 landscape
~~~

The safer reading is:

~~~text
Simondon metastability
= a domain-thickened account of generative conditions / individuation;
its exact Facing placement is OPEN.
~~~

The live test is whether metastability is:

- a universal primitive requirement;
- a physical / biological / psychosocial realization of PB2 localization;
- or a mixed structure carrying both primitive and formed burdens.

Likewise, do not say current SRT “borrows” Simondon's tension principle merely because there is structural overlap. Record instead:

~~~text
independent derivation of those structural expectations from the SRT primitive
= NOT ESTABLISHED;

Simondon already pays substantial source-native burden
= YES.
~~~

### 2.2 Whitehead: eternal objects are not yet established as L2→L0 back-import

Do not harden:

~~~text
eternal objects = formed determinations projected back into L0.
~~~

Safer pressure:

> Whitehead explicitly introduces pure potentials / eternal objects to help account for definiteness, possibility and actualisation. The SRT question is whether this requires a sufficiently pregiven potential structure that conflicts with SRT non-preclosure, or whether it is a different metaphysical solution that cannot be reduced to “back-import.”

### 2.3 HP-B weak-experience issue is out of scope

Do not reopen O-1.

Current primitive anti-tautology does not depend on settling whether a One carries a subjective / experience-directed form.

## 4. Assumption-ledger correction

### 4.1 Whitehead

To reconstruct a Whitehead-like actual occasion from SRT currently requires adding at least:

- antecedent-world uptake / prehension-like dependence;
- event-level experiential / feeling structure;
- subjective form / valuation;
- pure potentials / eternal objects;
- divine / primordial ordering or an alternative relevance source.

Because several are constitutive Whitehead commitments, this is **not yet a strong RN-R2 result**.

### 4.2 Simondon

To reconstruct Simondonian individuation currently requires adding:

- metastability / tension;
- domain potentials;
- transductive propagation;
- individuation as partial resolution;
- retained preindividual potential after individuation.

Again, these are substantial source-native commitments. Treat as typed embedding + partial reconstruction, not a depth result.

### 4.3 Organizational closure

Adding:

- constraints;
- mutual dependence;
- maintenance relation;
- closure criterion;
- declared unit / timescale.

This is close to importing the closure account itself. The useful SRT pressure therefore lies not in reconstruction but in whether SRT can provide an independently motivated cut criterion.

### 4.4 ETI

Adding:

- lower-level reproducers;
- heritable fitness variation;
- cooperation / conflict structure;
- higher-level heredity / selection conditions.

These are domain commitments. Again, reconstruction alone is weak evidence.

## 5. Primitive triangulation replaces immediate closure/ETI deepening

The immediate next bounded work is:

~~~text
PT-WH Whitehead
PT-SI Simondon
PT-RV reversible genuine change
~~~

### 5.1 PT-WH — determinacy/relevance without forcing recurrence into the primitive

Whitehead pressures two different jobs that must be separated.

#### WH-J1 primitive-facing

Why is an actuality determinate / relevant rather than arbitrary?

Whitehead uses:

- pure potentials / eternal objects;
- antecedent-world conditioning;
- valuation / initial aim.

SRT must ask what part of that work belongs to PB0/PB1.

#### WH-J2 downstream

Why can “the same determination” recur across events?

This is **not** automatically primitive.

Route first through:

- invariant / relation stabilization;
- recurrent objectification;
- Gate / formed equivalence;
- history / retained organization.

If SRT cannot explain recurrence downstream, that is a real deficit, but it must not be repaired by silently redefining primitive Selection.

### 5.2 PT-SI — localization as specialization

Simondon supplies a strong answer to:

~~~text
why does individuation occur here / now / in this direction?
~~~

through metastability, tension, incompatibility and transduction.

Current correction:

~~~text
this is primarily a PB2 localization candidate;
not automatically a primitive PB0/PB1 criterion.
~~~

SRT's stronger claim, if any, would be:

> Simondon-style metastability is one powerful realization of generative transformation, while primitive Selection must also remain meaningful in cases lacking that thick structure.

That claim now needs the PT-RV control.

### 5.3 PT-RV — deterministic reversible actual change

Conceptual case:

~~~text
real system;
genuine actual change;
deterministic;
time-reversible at the governing-law level;
fixed rule;
no chooser;
no probability primitive;
no value;
no novelty requirement;
no history requirement;
no rule rewrite.
~~~

Question:

> Under option A, is this still genuine Selection?

#### If YES

Then the anti-tautology burden becomes:

> What does Selection add to genuine reversible actuality beyond a re-description of change?

Candidate residue:

~~~text
actuality institutes a determinate non-role-interchangeable relation
rather than merely being labelled by an observer.
~~~

But this must be shown without smuggling temporal irreversibility, thermodynamic arrow, probability, value or retained history into the criterion.

#### If NO

Then SRT must identify the excluding criterion.

Any criterion that depends on:

- irreversibility;
- entropy increase;
- probability;
- retained consequence;
- novelty;
- value;
- agency;
- pre-given alternatives

would contradict current guards unless explicitly re-adjudicated.

Therefore PT-RV is the strongest current collapse test for option A.

## 7. Cross-tradition “back-definition” hypothesis — retain only as a hypothesis

A possible unifying error form is:

> a formed partition / organization is used to define the conditions of its own formation.

Candidate locations:

- Whitehead — pure-potential / relevance structure;
- Simondon — metastable/preindividual structure;
- organizational closure — constraint decomposition;
- ETI — higher-level fitness / individuality partition.

Current status:

~~~text
CROSS-TRADITION UNIFICATION = HYPOTHESIS ONLY.
~~~

It does **not** count as RN-F2 yet because:

1. Whitehead and Simondon source-native fit is not yet established strongly enough;
2. closure and ETI criticism literatures already own much of their local diagnosis;
3. no prospective blind-step confirmation exists.

The next new target, if this route is later continued, must use the blind-step protocol.

## 8. Neighbor-surplus results that must remain visible

Do not hide these:

- Whitehead currently has richer machinery for relevance, pure possibility and antecedent conditioning.
- Simondon currently has richer machinery for metastability, localization of individuation and retained preindividual potential.
- organizational closure literature already owns the objectivity/redescription problem.
- ETI literature already owns major parts of the level-origin problem and supplies alternative mechanisms.
- Parfit/Husserl already own the distinctions for which SRT previously risked claiming surplus.

These are not defeats; they are constraints on where SRT may legitimately claim depth.
