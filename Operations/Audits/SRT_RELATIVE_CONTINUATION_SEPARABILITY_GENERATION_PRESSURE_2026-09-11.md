# Relative continuation separability — generation pressure

## 1. Author-owned starting constraints

The current accepted direction combines earlier author rulings:

```text
historical effects can mutually support a forming center;
mutual support is coupled to relative suppression of competing directions;
center and edge are co-generated;
Selection is simultaneous manifestation + relative suppression;
post-Selection history changes the later vertical differentiation field;
One is a localized vertical process-unit sustained through lineage-relative reconstitution;
multiple centers alone are insufficient for multiple Ones;
multiple Ones require differentiable continuation lineages;
One-level continuation remains weaker than Bearer-level own-history closure.
```

The present task is to explain the final relation without adding a primitive boundary.

## 2. What must be generated

`relative continuation separability` must not mean:

```text
spatial separation;
causal isolation;
independent material substrate;
observer naming;
full autonomy;
consequence ownership;
strict numerical identity.
```

The minimum target is weaker:

> two local vertical lineages can remain strongly coupled while the conditions under which each can be reconstituted, transformed, branched or dissolved are not obligatorily identical.

## 3. Candidate mechanism — differential reconstitution dependence

Suppose the wider pre-object field contains two local vertical organizations `A_t` and `B_t`.

Repeated Selection can produce:

```text
A_t-related historical effects
-> mutually support later regeneration of A-like vertical organization
-> relatively suppress / background some incompatible A-continuations

B_t-related historical effects
-> mutually support later regeneration of B-like vertical organization
-> relatively suppress / background some incompatible B-continuations.
```

The key new relation is not local strength but **differential reconstitution dependence**:

```text
prior A-lineage structure makes a specific difference to how A_(t+1) can be regenerated;
prior B-lineage structure makes a specific difference to how B_(t+1) can be regenerated;
those two dependency patterns need not be identical or fully substitutable.
```

This can arise while the memory / carrier remains distributed in `多` / the wider field.

Therefore:

```text
differential reconstitution dependence
!= own-history attribution.
```

## 4. Why relative suppression matters

Mutual support alone could still collapse into one globally reinforced pattern.

Relative suppression supplies the differentiation pressure:

```text
support within A-compatible continuation relations
+
relative suppression of some cross-cutting / incompatible continuations
-> future A-regeneration becomes less freely substitutable by arbitrary field history;

support within B-compatible continuation relations
+
relative suppression of some incompatible continuations
-> future B-regeneration acquires a different dependence profile.
```

Thus the relevant center/edge distinction shifts from current activation to future reconstitution:

```text
CENTER_A
= relations that repeatedly remain load-bearing for A-lineage regeneration;

EDGE_A
= relations / continuations that remain possible but are relatively less able to enter A's continuing reconstitution chain.
```

The same field relation may occupy a different role relative to `B`.

## 5. Relative verticality follows

This gives a stronger reading of verticality:

```text
Vertical_A
!= global ranking;

Vertical_A
= non-flat organization of what can manifest / continue / transform
  relative to the A-lineage's current reconstitution conditions.
```

Likewise for `Vertical_B`.

Therefore multiple Ones do not require multiple disconnected worlds. They require multiple local relation-indexed vertical organizations inside one coupled field.

## 6. Counterfactual pressure test

A useful machine-side test is differential sensitivity rather than absolute independence.

For candidate lineages `A` and `B`, ask whether there can exist a perturbation / historical change `p` such that:

```text
p materially changes the reconstitution viability / transformation path of A
while B is not thereby forced to undergo the same continuation change,
```

and / or conversely for `B`.

This does not require symmetry and does not require B to remain unchanged in every respect.

It only asks whether:

```text
Continuation_A
is not definitionally or causally identical to
Continuation_B.
```

If every possible continuation / dissolution change is necessarily one global pattern-level change with no lineage-differential dependence, the multiple-One case weakens toward multiple roles / peaks of one One.

## 7. Turing / multi-peak negative control

A reaction-diffusion pattern can produce multiple peaks and even regenerate them.

But if:

```text
peak identities are exchangeable;
local peak deletion is repaired only by the global pattern rule;
no prior peak-specific history differentially constrains which later peak counts as its reconstitution;
```

then:

```text
multiple peaks: YES
multiple local centers: YES
multiple One-lineages: NOT ESTABLISHED.
```

Conversely, a richer patterned system with local history-dependent competence / coupling may begin to satisfy differential reconstitution dependence. The present audit does not exclude that possibility by definition.

## 8. No pre-given boundary required

Under this candidate, a boundary is downstream bookkeeping over differential continuation relations.

```text
repeated lineage-specific support / suppression
-> differential reconstitution dependence
-> relatively separable continuation conditions
-> lineage-relative continuation / dissolution boundary.
```

Therefore:

```text
boundary
is not required before One;

boundary-like distinction
can emerge from the history of selective reconstitution.
```

## 9. One versus Bearer guard

The candidate must preserve the Cycle-1 ruling:

```text
before Bearer:
Selection sedimentation remains in 多 / wider relational field;

Bearer transition:
historical traces sediment in 一 / become own-history of the organization
that bears consequences and reconstitutes the later position.
```

Accordingly:

```text
One-level lineage:
field-distributed history can differentially regenerate this local vertical process;

Bearer-level lineage:
consequences from this position return to / become load-bearing history of the organization
responsible for reconstituting the later position.
```

So:

```text
differential reconstitution dependence
<
return–reconstitution / own-history closure.
```

## 10. Branching, merging and nesting

The same relation naturally permits:

### Branching

```text
A_t
-> A1_(t+1)
-> A2_(t+1)
```

if one prior lineage contributes to two later, subsequently differentiable reconstitution paths.

### Merging

```text
A_t + B_t
-> C_(t+1)
```

if previously differentiable continuation paths cease to retain independent reconstitution conditions and become organized as one new continuation relation.

This does not by itself decide whether strict numerical identity survives merger.

### Nesting

```text
A-lineage
inside
AB relation-level lineage
```

can be retained if A has lineage-differential continuation conditions while also participating in a higher-order relation whose own vertical organization is reconstituted.

Therefore SRT need not assume one privileged scale of One-formation.

## 11. Compact candidate derivation

```text
Selection
= manifestation + relative suppression

repeated Selection
-> historical mutual support among some continuation relations
-> relative suppression / backgrounding of incompatible continuation relations
-> field reconstitution

repeated local reconstitution
-> differential reconstitution dependence
-> relative continuation separability
-> multiple One candidates.
```

This is a machine derivation, not a canonical theorem.

## 12. Theoretical consequence

If retained, the important transition is not:

```text
many points
-> bounded objects.
```

It is:

```text
distributed differential-history field
-> several non-equivalent reconstitution dependencies
-> several relatively separable continuation lineages
-> multiple Ones.
```

This offers a direct bridge from the author's `多` intuition to multiple One-formation without treating `多` as already-objectified alternatives.

## 13. Open burden

The deepest remaining issue is now:

> **What makes a lineage-specific reconstitution dependence strong enough to count as a continuing One rather than merely a transient local causal tendency?**

Possible future pressure dimensions include:

```text
recurrence depth;
perturbation tolerance;
continuation / dissolution counterfactuals;
degree of history-specific regeneration;
relative non-substitutability of neighboring lineages.
```

Do not convert these into a scalar threshold without author adjudication and source pressure.
