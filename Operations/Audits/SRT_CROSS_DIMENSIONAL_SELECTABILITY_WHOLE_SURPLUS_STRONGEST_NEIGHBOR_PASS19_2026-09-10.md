# Pass 19 — cross-dimensional selectability / whole-level surplus strongest-neighbor pressure

> **Role:** test the author's clarified meaning of “more stable Selection” and the proposed use of SRT vertical structure to explain whole-level surplus. This is a strongest-neighbor subtraction pass, not a novelty declaration.

## 0. Author-owned target

Author clarification fixes:

```text
more stable Selection
=
cross-time / cross-space / cross-position / cross-subject selectability.
```

Together with the prior author dependency:

```text
L0 difference
-> Selection
-> ontological vertical structure
-> later differences / subjects participate under that non-flat structure
-> selectability becomes more stable across time / space / position / subject
-> multi-subject order.
```

The author also proposes that vertical structure may provide a more adequate explanatory framework for:

```text
whole > sum of parts.
```

The audit must distinguish:

```text
(a) generic emergence / non-additivity;
(b) collective organization / interaction autonomy;
(c) constraint-based whole-part dependence;
(d) SRT's specific proposed genesis of whole-level surplus through Selection-generated verticalization of future selectability.
```

## 1. Operational pressure table

The full candidate chain is decomposed only for pressure:

| ID | Candidate burden |
|---|---|
| X1 | determinate differences / boundaries are constituted rather than merely read out |
| X2 | a constitutive event changes conditions of later possibility / selectability |
| X3 | this historically generated organization survives or is re-enterable across time and spatial locus |
| X4 | the organization is not tied to one fixed Selection-position / standpoint |
| X5 | other subjects / selective centres can enter, inherit, modify, and sustain the organized selectability |
| X6 | multi-subject relations generate a higher-order organization that changes participant futures |
| X7 | whole-level surplus is explainable as relational constraints / structured future possibilities rather than mysterious extra substance |
| X8 | X1-X7 are unified by the specific dependency `Selection -> ontological vertical structure -> cross-dimensional selectability -> stabilized multi-subject order` |

X1-X7 can be heavily neighbor-paid while X8 remains a live integration claim. A live integration claim is not automatically a Level-1 discriminator.

## 2. Barad — stronger payment than a boundary-only reading

Source-native pressure from agential realism is stronger than:

```text
agential cut -> boundary.
```

Barad's apparatus / intra-action framework supports:

```text
relata and determinate boundaries are enacted through intra-action;
material-discursive configurations are iteratively reconfigured;
space / time / matter are themselves reconfigured rather than treated as neutral containers;
iterative boundary reconfiguration changes later conditions of possibility.
```

A particularly strong formulation in the Baradian secondary literature, explicitly quoting *Meeting the Universe Halfway*, characterizes iterative reconfiguration as:

```text
changing the conditions of possibility of changing possibilities.
```

### Payment

```text
X1 constitutive difference / boundary: STRONG
X2 later possibility changed by prior constitutive configuration: STRONG
X3 time/space not neutral external containers: PARTIAL-TO-STRONG
X4 fixed observer-position not required: STRONG in anti-external-observer sense
X5 cross-subject stabilized selectability: WEAK / NOT NATIVE CORE BURDEN
X6 multi-subject order: NOT THE PRIMARY EXPLANATORY TARGET
X7 whole-level constraint surplus: PARTIAL through entangled configurations, not a dedicated whole/parts account
```

### Consequence for SRT

SRT may NOT claim novelty merely from:

```text
boundaries are generated;
prior differentiations alter later possibilities;
configuration is historically reworked;
space/time/subject-object cuts are not primitive.
```

The remaining difference, if any, must concern how those changes are organized specifically as **Selection-generated vertical selectability** and how this organization becomes re-enterable across multiple subjects into order.

## 3. Simondon — very strong payment for cross-subject structuration

A fuller Simondon comparison must include psychic-collective individuation / the transindividual, not only individual + associated milieu.

Strong source-native / close-secondary reconstruction supports:

```text
individual and collective individuation are reciprocal rather than sequentially assembled;
the transindividual operates through individuals rather than merely between finished individuals;
preindividual potentials carried by different beings enter communication / signification;
the resulting metastable field can generate new structures in both individual and collective;
collective structuration can invent a higher / broader domain of compatibility;
psychic and collective processes can operate at different magnitudes while reciprocally influencing one another;
the process remains open and metastable rather than terminating in a static whole.
```

Recent Simondon scholarship also emphasizes deposition / later reactualization of signification in technical and cultural forms and describes collective individuation as an open-ended transductive convergence.

### Payment

```text
X1 genesis before finished individual: STRONG
X2 achieved structure conditions later individuation: STRONG
X3 cross-time continuation / retained potentials: STRONG
X4 multiple scales / magnitudes and relational reconfiguration: STRONG CANDIDATE
X5 cross-subject communication of preindividual potential: STRONG
X6 individual-collective reciprocal structuration: STRONG
X7 whole not reducible to aggregation: STRONG
```

### Critical narrowing

Simondon therefore pays much more than:

```text
whole is not sum of parts.
```

He pays a genetic and cross-subject account in which collective individuation generates new norms / structures and can create broader compatibility not available to isolated individuals.

SRT may NOT use any of the following as sufficient novelty markers:

```text
transindividuality;
whole beyond aggregation;
individual / collective reciprocal formation;
new structures emerging across subjects;
historical continuity of becoming;
multi-scale individuation;
collective invention of compatibility.
```

The still-live difference is narrower:

```text
Does SRT's Selection-first dependency identify the historically inherited object
not merely as metastable potential / signification / transductive structure,
but specifically as a non-flat organization of what remains selectable,
whose stability is evaluated by re-entry across time / space / position / subject?
```

Current answer: **not yet absorbed, but not yet proven unique**.

## 4. Participatory sense-making — strong payment for higher-order interaction autonomy

Participatory sense-making provides a strong foil for the social / multi-subject half of the chain:

```text
two or more autonomous agents mutually modulate sense-making;
the interaction can acquire autonomous self-sustaining dynamics;
the relational domain can form its own temporary autonomy;
the interaction can influence, form, and transform participants;
higher-order social organization need not be a simple sum of individual behavior.
```

Later work explicitly treats the interactional domain as a higher-level autonomous organization in some cases.

### Payment

```text
X5 cross-subject participation: STRONG
X6 higher-order organization changing participant futures: STRONG
X7 whole-level non-additivity / relational autonomy: STRONG
```

### Limit relative to current SRT target

The enactive starting point normally includes already-autonomous agents / sense-makers and dynamical coupling among them. It therefore does not by itself supply the same pre-object / pre-subject dependency:

```text
L0 difference
-> bearerless Selection
-> ontological verticalization
-> later subject participation.
```

This gives SRT a possible **genetic-depth difference**, but not yet a demonstrated scientific discriminator.

## 5. Organizational closure / constraint theory — strongest whole/parts pressure

The Organizational Approach (Moreno / Mossio and related work) is a stronger comparator for “whole greater than sum” than generic emergence language.

It explains biological organization through **closure of constraints**:

```text
constraints reduce degrees of freedom of underlying processes;
constraints are mutually dependent for their production / maintenance;
constraints jointly maintain the organization that in turn maintains them;
whole-level dependence can be explained by the organized network of constraints rather than a mysterious extra causal substance;
whole-to-part influence is analyzable as hierarchically organized mutual constraint.
```

Recent organizational work states the point especially sharply:

```text
the “whole” is the relation / network of constraints;
parts depend on the whole insofar as their existence/activity depends on that organized network.
```

### Payment

```text
X2 prior organization changes later degrees of freedom: STRONG
X3 persistence across time through self-maintaining organization: STRONG in biological scope
X6 higher organization constrains component futures: STRONG
X7 whole-level surplus as relational constraint organization: VERY STRONG
```

This directly blocks a weak SRT claim:

```text
whole > parts because the whole creates constraints on parts
```

That is already strongly paid.

### Remaining possible SRT repartition

SRT's possible difference is not “constraints exist”. It is the proposed **genesis of constraints / whole-level organization**:

```text
Selection does not begin with a ready set of functional constraints or ready autonomous components;
Selection verticalizes difference;
verticalized difference becomes inherited organization of future selectability;
cross-dimensional re-entry of that selectability allows multi-subject stabilization;
whole-level constraints are therefore read downstream as stabilized products of Selection history.
```

But this only earns a structural difference if “verticalized selectability” can be specified independently of generic constraint topology / metastable compatibility.

## 7. What survives subtraction

After strongest-neighbor subtraction, the only serious candidate is the exact placement:

```text
minimum non-neutral difference
-> subjectless Selection
-> Selection itself creates ontological verticality
-> verticality is specifically an asymmetric organization of later selectability
-> the resulting selectability can be re-entered across time / space / position / subject
-> multiple selective centres stabilize that selectable organization
-> multi-subject order is the higher-level persistence of this stabilized selectability
```

This is stronger than the slogan:

```text

```

It is also stronger than:

```text

constraints emerge;
interaction becomes autonomous;
individual and collective co-develop.
```

## 9. Whole > parts — current best SRT explanatory formulation

The strongest formulation licensed by the author direction and comparative subtraction is:

> **The surplus of a whole over an independent description of its parts is the historically generated organization of future selectability produced by Selection among and through those parts. Once this organization becomes re-enterable across time, space, position, and subject, it functions as a whole-level constraint on subsequent Selection while remaining generated and maintained through ongoing Selection.**

This gives an SRT account of apparent bottom-up / top-down causation:

```text
bottom-up reading:
part-level / local Selection generates and stabilizes vertical selectability structure.

top-down reading:
the already-stabilized structure changes what later local / participant Selection can access.
```

These need not be two independent causal substances. They may be two temporal / scale-indexed readings of one historically generated Selection structure.

### Comparative caution

Organizational closure already gives a mature non-mysterious account of whole-part reciprocal constraint. SRT's potential advantage is therefore **genetic depth + ontological continuity** from pre-object difference to multi-subject order, not the bare fact of reciprocal constraint.

## 11. Decisive next conceptual burden

Do **not** broaden the neighbor list again yet.

The next question is internal and exact:

> What makes an SRT “vertical structure” specifically a structure of **selectability** rather than a generic constraint / compatibility / metastability relation?

A useful future discriminator must answer at least one of:

```text
VSEL-1 What is preserved when selectability re-enters across different subjects or positions?
VSEL-2 What changes while the Selection structure is still counted as the same stabilized selectable organization?
VSEL-3 Can two systems have identical observable constraint topology but differ in SRT vertical selectability because their Selection histories differ?
VSEL-4 Does verticality encode a directional foreground/background accessibility asymmetry that cannot be recovered from a flat relation graph alone?
VSEL-5 What separates cross-dimensional selectability from ordinary path dependence or organizational closure?
```

These are audit-side pressure questions, not author definitions.
