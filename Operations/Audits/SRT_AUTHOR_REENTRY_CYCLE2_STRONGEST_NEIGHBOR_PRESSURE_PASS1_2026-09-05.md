# Author Re-entry Cycle 2 — strongest-neighbor pressure pass 1

> **Role:** bounded pressure audit after PASS1–PASS3. It does not add an author claim, edit a canonical owner, or claim novelty. The purpose is to identify which parts of the current candidate are already paid for by mature neighboring frameworks before any Level-1/2 promotion.

## 1. Author-licensed material under pressure

Current A-material from Cycle 2 is limited to the following direction:

```text
minimum non-neutral difference structure comes first;
historical consequences matter;

mutual support among historical effects is important;
```


Do not cite the whole chain as author wording.

## 2. Neighbor 1 — neural lateral inhibition / winner-take-all

Source-native point:

- lateral inhibitory circuits can generate center-surround receptive-field organization and sharpen spatial discrimination;
- WTA networks use recurrent/feedback inhibition and nonlinear dynamics to suppress competitors while maintaining one or several winners.

Representative sources:

- Thoreson & Mangel (2012), *Lateral interactions in the outer retina*, Progress in Retinal and Eye Research, PMCID PMC3401171;
- Rutishauser et al. (2015), *Neuronal networks with NMDARs and lateral inhibition implement winner-takes-all*, Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience 9:12;
- Kaski & Kohonen (1994), *Winner-take-all networks for physiological models of competitive learning*, Neural Networks 7(6–7):973–984.

What this neighbor already pays for:

```text
center excitation / support
+
surround inhibition / competitor suppression
-> contrast enhancement / selected winner / center-surround organization
```

Pressure verdict:

```text
"mutual support + lateral suppression" by itself = NOT SRT-specific
"manifestation + suppression" by itself = NOT SRT-specific
center-surround phenomenology = NOT SRT-specific
```

Why it is not the strongest overall neighbor: these models normally prespecify neurons/units, connectivity, receptive-field geometry or candidate variables. They are strong mechanism-level neighbors but weak on the upstream individuation question.

## 3. Neighbor 2 — Turing-type symmetry breaking / pattern formation

Source-native point:

Turing's morphogenesis model starts from a homogeneous state of reacting/diffusing morphogens and shows that instability can generate spatial pattern without an external pattern designer.

Representative source:

- A. M. Turing (1952), *The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis*, Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B 237:37–72.

What this neighbor already pays for:

```text
initial near-homogeneity
+ local interaction / diffusion / instability
-> spontaneous differentiation
-> center-like / edge-like or patterned structure
```

Pressure verdict:

```text
co-generated center / edge = NOT enough for SRT specificity
symmetry breaking without a predesigned macroscopic pattern = NOT enough
```

Remaining limitation relative to the Cycle-2 question: the model still declares a reaction-diffusion system, variables/species and a domain before pattern onset. It does not by itself settle SRT's more upstream question of how a persistent selection-position becomes the unit from which later Selection continues.

## 4. Neighbor 3 — Haken synergetics / order-parameter emergence

Source-native point:

Synergetics explains how systems of many interacting parts can undergo instability and self-organize into macroscopic structures; a few emergent order parameters can then constrain/slave the remaining modes.

Representative source:

- H. Haken (1985), *Synergetics—an interdisciplinary approach to phenomena of self-organization*, Geoforum 16(2):205–211.

What this neighbor already pays for:

```text
many interacting degrees of freedom
-> instability
-> emergent order parameter / macroscopic pattern
-> top-down constraint on later component dynamics
```

Pressure verdict:

```text
"a center emerges and later organizes its parts" = NOT SRT-specific
"bottom-up emergence + later top-down constraint" = NOT SRT-specific
```

Why it is still not the strongest overall neighbor: the analyzed system and its component variables are normally specified before the order parameter emerges.

## 5. Strongest overall neighbor — Simondonian individuation / transduction

Why this is strongest:

Simondon explicitly rejects the completed individual as the explanatory starting point. Individuation proceeds from a preindividual/metastable problematic field; transduction is a process without a prior subject, propagates local structuration, produces individuals, and leaves structures that alter the conditions of later operations.

Repository close-read already records:

```text
metastable / problematic domain
+ singularity
-> local form-taking / modulation
-> transductive propagation
-> structure
-> changed conditions for further operation
```

and:

```text
Operation_(t-1)
-> OperatorStructure_t
-> Operation_t
```

External pressure sources include:

- Simondon scholarship describing individuation as process prior to the completed individual and transduction as the operation producing provisional individuals from preindividual tensions;
- Anne Sauvanargues / Elizabeth Grosz discussion of individuation as a process without a subject that generates the subject/identity/position it sets in motion;
- recent secondary treatments emphasizing preindividual metastability and the individual as result rather than cause.

This neighbor already pays for:

```text
no pre-given completed individual
process before subject
relation/tension before completed unit
operation produces structure
structure changes later operation
individual + milieu co-emerge / remain dynamically coupled
```

Therefore the following are NOT enough for Level 1:

```text
"One is not pre-given"
"unit emerges from relations"
"process precedes subject"
"operation and structure recursively condition one another"
"boundary is generated rather than merely observed"
```

## 6. Substitution test against Simondon

### Candidate wording before substitution

```text
minimum non-neutral relational difference
-> subjectless Selection
-> manifestation + relative suppression
-> irreversible historical accessibility rewrite
-> mutual support + relative inhibition
-> persistent selection-position / emerging One
-> later Selection continues from that position
```

### Strongest-neighbor substitution attempt

A generous Simondonian translation can replace much of the sequence:

```text
preindividual tension / incompatibility
-> transductive operation without prior subject
-> local differentiation / resolution
-> produced structure changes neighboring conditions
-> recursive propagation / further individuation
-> provisional individual / position + milieu
```

This absorbs a large fraction of the current candidate.

### What does NOT yet survive merely by wording

- `manifestation + suppression` can be read as ordinary selective differentiation / symmetry breaking;
- `mutual support + inhibition` can be read as generic competitive self-organization;
- `center-surround` can be read as a domain-specific mechanism;
- `One emerges` is already native to individuation theory in a broad sense.

## 7. Current narrow residual candidate

The only relation not yet safely declared absorbed is the following **candidate dependency claim**:

> A persistent `selection-position` is generated when repeated selective differentiations do not merely produce a pattern, but recursively rewrite **which later consequences/relations remain admissible to the same developing historical line**, such that future Selection continues from a position constituted by this history-dependent admissibility structure.

Minimal contrast:

```text
pattern / individual formation:
relations organize -> a structure exists

Cycle-2 residual candidate:
relations organize
-> the organization changes which later consequences can count as continuations of this same developing line
-> a selection-position is thereby constituted
-> later Selection proceeds from that constituted position
```

This is NOT yet author wording. It is the strongest remaining C-candidate after the first neighbor pass.

## 8. Why this residual is still at risk

Simondon may still absorb it through:

```text
individual-milieu coupling
continuous individuation
transductive propagation
operation-structure reciprocity
```

Autonomy / enactivism may absorb it through self-maintaining organizational closure and identity-by-constraint.

Learning / adaptive-boundary models may absorb it as state-dependent gating or changing effective state space.

Therefore no Level 1 is awarded yet.
