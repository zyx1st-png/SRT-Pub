## 3. Why the HOLD cannot be lifted yet

The reason is **not** "the ontology needs more maturity."

The named blocker is:

> **No author-owned candidate currently combines a defensible Level-1 structural repartition with a specific, credible Level-2 domain-native discriminator path that survived the strongest-neighbor and ordinary-dynamics subtraction already performed.**

Opening a third deep well now would risk recreating the earlier pattern:

```text
strong ontology wording
-> premature domain probe
-> mature baseline / generic dynamics absorb the effect
-> another translation-only result
```

The two earlier pilots make that risk concrete rather than hypothetical.

Choose one author-owned relation and show, against the named strongest mature neighbor, that replacing SRT vocabulary changes at least one domain-expressible item:

```text

```


### Trigger B — state a concrete Level-2 path before opening a well

The route must name at least one domain-native consequence such as:

```text
a case distinction that the strongest neighbor collapses but SRT separates;
a model constraint that differs under the two dependency structures;
a different evidence requirement;
a different intervention target;
a counterfactual that comes apart;
a forbidden equivalence under one architecture but not the other.
```

A new toy whose only purpose is to preserve the SRT candidate does not pay this trigger.

Once one route satisfies Trigger A + Trigger B on paper under strong-neighbor pressure, rerun the HOLD EXIT REVIEW before authorizing a deep well.
