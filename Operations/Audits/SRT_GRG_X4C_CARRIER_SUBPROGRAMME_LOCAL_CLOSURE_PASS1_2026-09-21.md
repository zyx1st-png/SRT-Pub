# X4c carrier subprogramme local-closure audit — Pass 1

## 0. Question

Has the X4c carrier subprogramme become sufficiently constrained for the current evidence base that carrier vocabulary should now be frozen and mainline attention returned to broader GRG?

~~~text

REOPEN
HOLD
~~~

No new source is added.

## 1. Closure criteria

A local CLOSE requires all of:

### C1 — relation admission is explicit

X4c must have a relation-level burden independent of carrier labels.

### C2 — carrier architecture adds exclusion / correction

The architecture must do more than rename provenance.

### C3 — reusable role vocabulary is minimal

Candidate roles must have been pruned rather than accumulated.

### C4 — persistence process is separated from component role

Temporal continuation must not be conflated with causal component function.

### C5 — negative / borderline case is productive

The framework must sharpen a non-admission, not only explain positive cases.

### C6 — role labels cannot bootstrap relation admission

A pre-admission state must exist.

### C7 — positive corpus survives the stricter guard

Existing positive cases must remain independently supported.

### C8 — no unresolved internal contradiction blocks use

OPEN items may remain only if they are explicitly non-blocking future evidence positions.

### C9 — no hidden maturity / canonical promotion

The carrier line must not smuggle M4/M5, ontology or moral claims into an analysis schema.

If any of C1–C9 fails:

~~~text
CLOSE = NO
~~~

## 13. Reopen triggers

The carrier subprogramme may reopen only if at least one concrete trigger occurs.

### R1 — semantic failure on a new source-native case

A new case cannot be typed without changing the meaning of X4c or forcing an unearned role.

### R2 — role non-identity collapse

Evidence shows two retained reusable roles are not operationally distinguishable.

### R3 — admission-order failure

A new or re-audited case shows the successor / transmission guard is insufficient or circular.

### R4 — paid-role falsification

Evidence contradicts a currently paid component role in an existing positive record.

### R5 — persistence-process contradiction

A source-native mechanism cannot be represented without collapsing persistence process back into component role.

### R6 — external calibration contradiction

A legitimate external result directly conflicts with the frozen carrier architecture.

Not sufficient to reopen:

~~~text

a new provenance medium by itself
a new metaphor
a wish to make the library more complete
a possible second AUTHORITY example
mere availability of new literature
~~~
