## 1. Closure criteria

CLOSE requires all of the following:

1. X4c admission is explicit enough to reject persistence-only false positives;
2. carrier architecture can represent single- and multi-component realizations;
3. provenance metadata are separated from causal role;
4. role vocabulary has been censused rather than accumulated;
5. persistence process is separated from component causal role;
6. at least one negative / borderline application demonstrates exclusion power;
7. positive cases survive the stricter admission-order guard;
8. remaining OPEN items are not internal contradictions;
9. no external dependency is required to interpret the current carrier corpus;
10. no maturity / ontology promotion is being smuggled through closure.

## 14. Reopen triggers

The carrier subprogramme may reopen only if at least one occurs:

### R1 — internal contradiction

An existing positive record fails the frozen admission rule under a new repository audit.

### R2 — source-native role pressure

A mature new domain requires a causal carrier role that:

- cannot be represented by current CORE / SPECIALIZED roles;
- cannot remain source-local without losing a real cross-domain distinction;
- has source-native evidence and a discriminating near-control.

### R3 — role collapse

A current reusable role is shown to be redundant with another role or unable to predict any distinct control / failure condition.

### R4 — carrier / expectation conflation

A later case shows the current architecture systematically confuses inherited carrier structure with generative-expectation provenance.

### R5 — relation revision

X4c itself is materially narrowed / split / retired by broader GRG work.

Absent one of these:

~~~text
do not reopen by case accumulation.
~~~
