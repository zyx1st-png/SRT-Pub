
### R3 — Ought / legitimacy closure

`Ought-implies-selectable`, future-selectability, correction, exit, anti-domination, non-export and related tests may remain as ethical bridge candidates. They must not become primitive ontology theorems or a complete O2-M decision procedure.

### R4 — d-value moralization

```text

high d -> moral standing / legitimacy
d expansion = moral growth
cross-subject d bandwidth = sufficient legitimacy test
```

Retain `d` only within its canonical stake-coupled concern semantics plus declared bridge / proxy use.

### R5 — Psi_f moralization / transfer identity

```text

friction export automatically = suffering export
Psi_f transfer = moral wrongness / oppression / injustice verdict
```

A bridge may study distributed burden, payability or consequence transfer, but bearer, scope, relation and normative criterion must be declared.

### R6 — suffering / evil / pathology backflow

Block:

```text

signal suppression -> hidden suffering theorem
pathology label -> moral verdict
suffering signal use -> universal normative objective
```

Use the current Suffering and Occlusion owner boundaries.

### R7 — affect / cognition / neuroscience overclaim

Affective-priority, `emotion = d-gradient signal`, `pure reason does not exist`, emotion-before-reason timing and similar claims may survive only as empirical P3/P4 bridge hypotheses with explicit domain / evidence / failure conditions. They are not L0/L1/L2 constitutive theorems.

### R8 — clinical / psychotherapy overclaim

PTSD, depression, rumination, psychoanalysis, CBT, exposure, meditation and `cure` language must remain clinical / psychological hypotheses or examples. E6 must not turn SRT bridge variables into diagnostic identities, treatment rules or universal healing mechanisms.

### R9 — selfhood / Freud / moral-psychology identities

Freud-SRT layer mapping, selfhood as L2 narrative, guilt/shame identities, superego as Psi crystallization, moral injury equations and naturalization-depth constructions may be preserved for comparative research, but not as current canonical identities.

### R10 — positive moral solver replacement

The repair itself must not replace the old solver with a new one of the form:

```text

free iff payable + reselectable + ...
responsible iff agency + ...
```

These dimensions can be used as candidate lenses. O2-M remains OPEN / HOLD.
