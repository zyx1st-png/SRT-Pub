# Author gate preparation — generative non-usurpation and constitutive ought

> **Important:** this is not author adjudication. It prepares a narrower gate after the cross-position pressure test. No canonical edit is authorized.

---

## N1 — non-usurpation is relation-relative before it is moral-universal

Proposed:

```text
ACCEPT
```

Meaning:

```text
real Selection-position
-/> universal inviolability.
```

The first non-usurpation burden applies where a relation independently counts a position as a constitutive generative participant.

Do not yet turn this into a universal equality / rights theorem.

---

## N2 — participant-status consistency is the strongest current structural core

Proposed:

```text
ACCEPT
```

A relation cannot coherently maintain both:

```text
Y is a load-bearing generative participant in R
```

and

```text
Y's Selection no longer materially enters R
because X recurrently generates Y's future relation-state in its place.
```

If the second becomes true, the relation must be retyped rather than preserving a false participation label.

This is proposed as a structural anti-objectification principle, not yet a complete moral rule.

---

## N3 — introduce O2-C as constitutive ought

Proposed:

```text
ACCEPT AS RECONSTRUCTION DIRECTION
```

Use:

```text
O2-C = constitutive ought / relation-integrity norm
```

Question:

```text
What must a relation do / not do if it is to remain the generative relation it claims to be?
```

This is distinct from:

```text
O2-A = attributable answerability under stronger agency conditions
O2-M = all-things-considered moral / deontic authority
```

Do not collapse O2-C into O2-M.

---

## N4 — `ought` first constrains generative rewriting, not option retrieval

Proposed:

```text
ACCEPT
```

SRT should not model normative correctness as selecting the one accurate item from a pregiven menu.

The first normative object is instead:

```text
how Selection rewrites the future generative relation
among already formed positions / relations.
```

Thus O2-C constrains relation-transforming Selection.

---

## N5 — scoped generative dominance can yield finite actionable preference

Proposed:

```text
ACCEPT AS LIMITED RULE
```

Where two feasible transitions accomplish the same declared task, and one is no worse for every admitted constitutive position / relation while strictly reducing unnecessary generative substitution for at least one, with no independently established higher-order loss, the less-usurping transition may be preferred.

This is a partial-order dominance rule.

It does not supply weights for real tradeoffs.

---

## N6 — delegation / formation / emergency asymmetry are not usurpation automatically

Proposed:

```text
ACCEPT
```

Asymmetry remains compatible with generativity-first normativity when the relation is correctly typed and the asymmetry itself is structurally connected to delegation, formation, protection, recovery or exceptional coordination rather than falsely represented as symmetric participation.

No single checklist is promoted here to a universal theorem.

---

## N7 — generative substitution is not identical with moral wrongness

Proposed:

```text
ACCEPT
```

`Generative substitution / objectification` is a structural diagnostic.

Do not infer automatically:

```text
generative substitution
-> complete moral wrongness.
```

Stronger moral standing still requires downstream work involving scope, Bearer / Concern, agency, whole-position and conflict conditions.

---

## N8 — preserve the stronger anti-objectification result

Proposed:

```text
ACCEPT AS STRONG RESEARCH DIRECTION
```

Anti-objectification should not be limited to cognitive representation.

A deeper SRT candidate is:

```text
a position is generatively objectified when a relation continues to count it as a participant
while its effective future source-role is recurrently displaced to another position.
```

This is a relation-level failure of participant-status consistency.

Exact thresholds and domain translations remain OPEN.

---

## N9 — Hume project status

Proposed:

```text
ACCEPT
```

Current strongest claim:

```text
partial constitutive dissolution: YES / strengthened
complete deontic closure: NO / OPEN
```

The gain is that some limited `ought` statements can be read as constitutive conditions of independently typed generative relations rather than as moral predicates externally attached to neutral fact blocks.

Do not claim that all morality has been derived from Selection.

---

## N10 — tradeoff resolution remains a distinct research line

Proposed:

```text
ACCEPT
```

When:

```text
X generative standing improves;
Y generative standing worsens;
H whole-position standing changes;
no transition dominates across the declared scope;
```

then current SRT should stop rather than invent a hidden scalar.

The remaining problem is genuine conflict adjudication among non-substitutable positions / relations / scales.

This is where stronger standing, Bearer / Concern, agency, delegation, whole-formation and domain-specific normative structure may become load-bearing.
