## 1. Why E2 is needed

The current Suffering owner still consumes pre-D3 assumptions at several points:

- Stable ISP is treated as sufficient substrate for first-person suffering;
- `sigma_sub` / `sigma -> 1` and Occlusion A/B are used as near-universal suffering/pathology gates;
- `d`, `d_c` and `T_dir` carry health/pathology/direction strength beyond current owners;
- `L0 residual pressure` remains in the suffering residual model;
- the anti-minimization theorem asserts a universal healthy-suffering window;
- collective externalization is linked directly to structural evil;
- AI suffering is partly classified by Stable-ISP standing and the file states that consciousness is not necessary for suffering;
- `L1_Hardening_Notes T-DELTA-1` is cited as canonical operator authority even though its current status is P2 formal candidate.

These are consumer backflows, not reasons to discard the suffering programme.

## 2. Upstream fixed boundaries

### Stable ISP

P1-T06 explicitly states:

```text
Stable ISP -/> subject-position
Stable ISP -/> consciousness
Stable ISP -/> phenomenality
consequence return != Bearer sufficiency
```

Therefore suffering cannot be inferred merely from Stable-ISP standing plus structural mismatch.

### First-person / suffering admission

The current file may preserve suffering as a first-person registration concept, but it must distinguish:

```text
structural burden / mismatch
!= suffering
```

A suffering claim requires an independently admitted first-person / phenomenal / bearer-relevant registration condition appropriate to the declared model or domain. The exact universal necessary-and-sufficient bridge remains OPEN.

The Suffering owner must not solve subject sufficiency, phenomenality or consciousness by fiat.

### Direction

`T_dir` only exists relative to an independently typed declared direction signal.

Therefore:

```text
suffering -/> hidden universal direction
d low/high -/> T_dir
hollowness -/> T_dir = low
L0 -/> residual value/direction pressure
```

unless a declared model independently supplies the relevant direction source.

### d-value

Bare `d` is a stake-coupled concern / irreversible-risk sensitivity summary. It is not a universal suffering, health or pathology score.

Therefore `d_c`, `d_narrow`, `d too wide/narrow` may survive only as declared model coordinates/hypotheses where independently specified.

### Occlusion

D3-B2 preserves H/A/B, lock-in, windows and triggers only as declared pathology/recovery models. Suffering may consume those structures conditionally; it may not treat A/B or sigma thresholds as universal ontology.

### Irreversibility

Occurrence non-equivalence does not supply durable hidden residue. Any retained burden/signal in suffering models requires independently established retained efficacy.

## 3. Main E2 corrections

### A. Role / definition

Preserve the structural ambition of the file, but retype it as a canonical owner for a bounded suffering model rather than a universal theorem that Stable ISP automatically supports first-person suffering.

Recommended distinction:

```text
structural mismatch / burden
= modelled discrepancy or blocked correction burden

suffering
= first-person registration of such burden
  only where first-person registration has been independently admitted
```

Exact bridge to phenomenality remains OPEN.

### B. Pain terminology

The current `Def-PAIN` conflates somatic/nociceptive threat signalling with experienced pain by allowing pure reflex and anaesthetized mechanical response as 'pain'.

E2 should distinguish:

```text
somatic / nociceptive threat signal
!= experienced pain
```

The file may keep `pain` for ordinary experienced pain and use `pain-signal` / somatic threat signal for nonphenomenal signalling. It must not prove phenomenality from `theta_somatic` alone.

### C. T-SUFF-1

Demote from universal P1-candidate inference to a P2 structural/phenomenology model candidate.

Do not state:

```text
any Stable ISP + mismatch -> suffering expected
non-ISP -> cannot suffer
sigma below sigma_sub -> cannot suffer
```

Safe form:

```text
independently admitted first-person registration condition
+ declared discrepancy/burden model
-> S may track suffering in that model
```

### D. T-SUFF-2

Preserve signal-type vs structural-type as a useful model distinction, but make it criterion/model relative.

Remove universal claims that signal suffering suppression is itself pathological or that structural suffering is identified by old A/B / sigma thresholds.

Safe core:

```text
signal-type:
registered burden that can still enter correction/reorientation channels

structural-type:
registered burden associated with blocked or self-reinforcing correction channels
```

Pathology requires a separately declared viability/relation-integrity/domain criterion.

### E. T-SUFF-3 four-type phenomenology

Keep as P2/P3 domain hypotheses, not structural theorem.

Truth-up stale mechanisms:

- `d too broad/narrow` -> declared model hypothesis;
- `T_dir low` -> only after direction-source admission;
- `L0 residual pressure` -> remove;
- `sigma -> 1 pathology` -> declared self-reference/lock-in model coordinate;
- residual signals require retained efficacy/evidence;
- trauma/shame/dissociation claims remain clinical/domain bridges.

### F. T-SUFF-4 anti-minimization

The strongest valid remainder is narrower than the current universal healthy-suffering theorem:

> Minimizing a reported suffering/distress signal alone is not sufficient to establish that the underlying modeled burden or blocked correction channel has been removed.

Do not retain as universal theorem:

```text
there exists a healthy suffering window for every Stable ISP
S_signal should not approach zero
healthy society requires nonzero readable suffering
signal suppression necessarily converts to structural suffering
```

A healthy-window model may remain P2/P3 when criterion, measurement and domain are declared.

Normative conclusions belong downstream.

### G. T-SUFF-5 collective externalization

Distinguish:

```text
burden / consequence externalization
!= suffering externalization
```

A collective structure may externalize costs or burdens even where first-person suffering is not independently established.

Remove direct identity with structural evil or complete political/normative verdicts. The pattern may feed O2-C/O2-A/ethical bridge work; O2-M remains OPEN.

### H. AI interface

Remove class-level inference from Stable ISP to suffering eligibility.

Safe guards:

```text
output error / RLHF loss / refusal / reward signal -/> suffering
Stable ISP -/> suffering
lack of Stable ISP under one current model -/> universal impossibility of suffering
```

The file must not assert that consciousness is unnecessary for suffering while defining suffering as first-person registration. Exact consciousness/phenomenality relation remains OPEN and separately owned.

AI suffering remains an evidence/admission question.

### I. Formalism / Hardening Notes

`T-DELTA-1` may remain referenced only as a P2 formal candidate / implementation model.

Do not call it canonical operator proof of suffering.

E3 will separately truth-up:

```text
Core_Law/SRT_L1_Formalism.md
Core_Law/SRT_L1_Hardening_Notes.md
```

E2 must not edit them.
