# R1 Bearer author gate — prospective self-indexing vs non-outsourcable exposure

> **Role:** prepare the first post-#942 Bearer author decision after `One / Selection-position` received a dedicated canonical formation owner. This file is audit-only and noncanonical. It does not define Bearer, `承担`, concern, subjecthood or phenomenality on the author's behalf.

---

## 1. New starting point after #942

The upstream unit is no longer the main ambiguity.

Canonical formation now supplies:

```text
One
= localized, lineage-relative, processual formed unity
  continuing through Selection-mediated recurrent reconstitution;

Selection-position_t
= the time-local operative from-where of that continuing One.
```

Therefore Bearer reconstruction must add a relation **to an already admitted One / Selection-position** rather than use Bearer to individuate the unit retroactively.

Current hard guards remain:

```text
One != Bearer;
Selection-position != Bearer;
history-bearing != Bearer;
being affected != Bearer;
feedback != Bearer;
own-history writeback != Bearer;
Bearer != subject;
Bearer != experiencer;
Bearer != 承担 by definition.
```

## 2. What the final author adjudication already pays

The accepted R1 direction gives three forward layers:

```text
A1 Selection / verticality forward structuring
-> future selectable space is actively constructed / maintained.

A2 formed-position internal anticipation
-> a formed One may endogenously prepare / differentiate possible continuations.

A3 Bearer research direction
-> future branches begin to differ in terms of what this continuing position itself
   will undergo / preserve / lose / enable.
```

A3 was explicitly accepted only as a **necessary-increment candidate**, not as a sufficient Bearer theorem.

The later supersession also fixed:

```text
retrospective history-to-reconstitution dependence
= real relation / evidence surface;
= NOT a sufficient Bearer gate.
```

So the current task is not to restore the old R gate. It is to decide what makes the prospective relation thick enough to count as Bearer.

## 3. Strongest-neighbor pressure after the supersession

### 3.1 Autonomy / adaptivity already pays self-relevant future differentiation

Barandiaran, Di Paolo & Rohde (2009), *Defining Agency: Individuality, Normativity, Asymmetry, and Spatio-temporality in Action* (Adaptive Behavior 17(5):367–386, DOI 10.1177/1059712309343819), already requires intrinsic individuality, system-generated normativity and an agent-centred spatiotemporal perspective. Their generative account explicitly treats the agent's identity as jeopardizable at the relevant level and action as regulated relative to internally generated norms.

Di Paolo (2005), *Autopoiesis, Adaptivity, Teleology, Agency* (Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences 4:429–452, DOI 10.1007/s11097-005-9002-y), makes adaptivity the capacity to regulate activity with respect to conditions of viability and uses it to ground sense-making / intrinsic normativity.

Therefore a Bearer proposal of the form:

```text
future state A is better / worse for this system than future state B
```

or even:

```text
the system prospectively regulates with respect to its own continuation
```

is not a clean residual. Mature autonomy / adaptivity theory already pays much of that burden.

### 3.2 Habit / sensorimotor autonomy absorbs history-sensitive self-maintaining continuation

Egbert & Barandiaran (2014), *Modeling habits as self-sustaining patterns of sensorimotor behavior* (Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 8:590, DOI 10.3389/fnhum.2014.00590), models habits as self-maintaining sensorimotor patterns whose repeated trajectories reshape the medium so similar trajectories become more likely later.

Barandiaran & Di Paolo (2014), *A genealogical map of the concept of habit* (Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 8:522, DOI 10.3389/fnhum.2014.00522), situates the richer organicist habit tradition as an alternative to atomistic stimulus-response accounts.

Therefore:

```text
history-sensitive self-maintenance
future-biased continuation
endogenous path reinforcement
```

also cannot by themselves define Bearer.

### 3.3 Consequence

The old alternatives fail on opposite sides:

```text
retrospective consequence return alone
= too weak / superseded;

prospective self-relevance alone
= too broad / strongly neighbor-paid.
```

The remaining internal SRT question is narrower:

> **Does Bearer require not only that future branches are differentiated relative to this One, but also that at least some of those possible consequences are prospectively attributable to this same One in a way that cannot be completely rerouted to another unit without changing the Bearer relation?**

This is an internal architecture question. No scientific-distinctiveness verdict follows from answering it.

## 4. Two typed relations for the author gate

### P — prospective self-indexing

For a formed One `O`, define the current reconstruction candidate `P`:

```text
possible future branches are not equivalent merely from an external observer view;
O's own lineage-conditioned organization differentiates them
by what O itself may undergo / preserve / lose / become able or unable to continue;
and that differentiation participates in current Selection / vertical organization.
```

Guards:

```text
P != explicit prediction model;
P != Bayesian representation;
P != consciousness;
P != mature cognition;
P != subjecthood.
```

### E — same-One prospective exposure / non-outsourcing

Define the stronger candidate `E`:

```text
for at least some branch-dependent consequence q,
q is prospectively attributable to the continuation of O itself;
if q's burden can be completely reassigned to another unit while O's candidate
Bearer relation is claimed unchanged, q is not Bearer-relevant for O;
therefore the prospective consequence relation is not merely representational
or advisory — the candidate continuation of O is genuinely exposed to it.
```

`E` is a counterfactual attribution / exposure condition, not a claim that the consequence must already have occurred.

This distinction allows:

```text
Bearer standing
!= every realized burden event;

prospective exposure
!= phenomenality;

prospective exposure
!= full agency;

prospective exposure
!= explicit equation of Bearer with canonical L0 承担.
```

The exact Bearer <-> 承担 implication remains open even if `E` is accepted.

## 5. Case pressure

### Case A — forecast-only planner

A system predicts that “its” battery / mission / score will degrade and uses that representation in planning, but replacement, reset or an external operator can fully absorb the consequence without changing the relevant continuing unit.

```text
P-like representation: possible
E: NO / weak
```

If this automatically counts as Bearer, Bearer risks collapsing into self-modelled prediction.

### Case B — passive consequence receiver

A formed One is changed by a shock and the shock leaves lasting history, but before the event its future branches are not endogenously differentiated in terms of what that One itself may undergo.

```text
retrospective change: YES
P: NO
```

The final author supersession says this must not establish Bearer by itself.

### Case C — prospectively exposed but non-agentic One

A formed One endogenously differentiates future continuations according to what will happen to its own continuation / selectability, and at least some relevant potential consequences cannot be fully moved to another unit while preserving the same exposure relation. It need not actively cause those consequences, explicitly model them, or consciously experience them.

```text
P: YES
E: YES
agency: NOT REQUIRED
subjecthood: NOT REQUIRED
phenomenality: NOT REQUIRED
```

This is the cleanest current Bearer candidate.

### Case D — full autonomous agent

A viable autonomous system may satisfy P + E and additionally actively modulate environment coupling relative to endogenous norms.

```text
Bearer candidate: possible
agency: stronger / additional relation
```

This prevents Bearer from simply becoming a synonym for mature autonomy / agency.

## 6. Author options

### A — Bearer onset = P only

```text
Bearer begins once a formed One prospectively differentiates future branches
in terms of what that One itself may undergo / preserve / lose / enable.
```

**Advantage:** maximally preserves Bearer as a thin pre-`承担` / pre-subject layer.

**Problem:** strongest-neighbor pressure is severe. It risks admitting self-modelled forecasting, viability-sensitive regulation, or other prospectively self-relevant systems even when consequences remain fully outsourceable.

Machine recommendation: **DO NOT PREFER**.

### B — Bearer onset = P + E

```text
Bearer begins when an already formed One:
1. prospectively / endogenously differentiates future branches by what that One itself may undergo;
and
2. at least some of those potential consequences are same-One exposure relations that cannot be fully reassigned while claiming the same Bearer relation.
```

Important boundary:

```text
actual consequence occurrence is not required at every moment;
Bearer is a standing organization of prospective self-exposure;
realized consequence bearing / 承担 remains a separately typed downstream or realization question;
agency, concern, subjecthood and phenomenality remain separate.
```

**Advantage:** best matches the author's phrase “第一人称的预期下的后果承担” while avoiding both retrospective-history collapse and prediction-only collapse.

**Risk:** close to autonomy / intrinsic normativity accounts; it should be adopted as SRT internal architecture, not advertised as scientific distinctiveness.

Machine recommendation: **PREFER**.

### C — keep Bearer sufficiency OPEN

Adopt only:

```text
P = necessary-increment direction;
E = live stronger candidate;
neither is yet sufficient.
```

Continue pressure before any Bearer onset criterion is accepted.

**Advantage:** maximally conservative.

**Cost:** leaves the most important post-One structural boundary unresolved even though the current author wording and case pressure now support a fairly clean two-part gate.

Machine recommendation: **DEFENSIBLE, SECOND CHOICE**.

## 7. Rejected route — actual consequence occurrence as Bearer onset

Do not prefer:

```text
Bearer exists only once q actually occurs and closes on O.
```

Reason:

```text
it makes Bearer episodic rather than a standing organization;
it loses the author's prospective / first-person emphasis;
it makes the same system alternate Bearer / non-Bearer merely because a branch has or has not yet realized;
it risks collapsing Bearer back into retrospective consequence history.
```

Potential / counterfactual exposure is therefore the cleaner object if the author selects B.
