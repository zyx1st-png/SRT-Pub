# Selection-position foreground observability — pressure pass 9

> **Role:** pressure the author's provisional leaning toward SPD-B after the author tied observability to SRT's foreground/background Selection architecture.
>
> **Boundary:** this pass does not treat the leaning as final adjudication. It specifically separates generic partial observability from a potentially stronger SRT-native observation claim.

## 0. Entry state

Exact author statement:

> **「倾向于 B，结合前对象场和 SRT 提出的可观测量是前景/背景化（选择）的前景部分」**

The existing core already supplies the relevant ontology-side grammar:

```text
Selection
= irreversible manifest-latent layering
= one possibility becomes foreground / manifest
= others recede into background / latency
= backgrounded possibilities are not annihilated and may remain conditions
  for future Selection
```

Therefore the new question is not generic sensor limitation. It is whether the SRT observation surface is structurally downstream of Selection itself.

---

## 1. First subtraction — ordinary partial observability is not enough

A generic dynamical system can already be written as:

```text
x_(t+1) = f(x_t, u_t)
y_t     = h(x_t)
```

where `y_t` does not reveal all of `x_t`.

Likewise, POMDPs, hidden-state models and ordinary control systems may have:

```text
same observed y-history
<- multiple hidden-state histories
```

Therefore the following claims are insufficient:

```text
observer sees only part of the system;
internal state is hidden;
several latent states can produce one output;
reconstruction is difficult or impossible from a declared output channel.
```

Verdict:

```text
generic partial observability: NEIGHBOR-PAID / NOT SRT-SPECIFIC
```

This preserves the Pass-8 objection to the crude SPD-B formulation.

---

## 2. Stronger candidate — observation is itself Selection-bounded

The author's new clarification points to a stronger relation.

Machine notation:

```text
F_t = pre-object relational field
S_t = Selection event / manifest-background differentiation
M_t = manifest / foreground side
L_t = relative background / latent side
Y_t = SRT-native observable surface
```

Candidate relation:

```text
S_t(F_t) -> (M_t, L_t)
Y_t = projection onto / registration of M_t
```

The crucial claim is not merely:

```text
L_t is hidden from a sensor.
```

It is:

```text
M_t is observable because it has become manifest through Selection;
L_t is background relative to that same event and is therefore not
simultaneously manifest as the same observation.
```

This yields a different pressure:

> observation is not an independent God's-eye channel placed outside the Selection event; the observable is already the foregrounded product / aspect of the event.

This is a candidate ontology/observation relation, not yet an empirical no-go theorem.

---

## 3. Same-event transformation guard

A key machine-side inference follows if the stronger reading is accepted:

```text
to make a previously backgrounded relation directly manifest
requires another foregrounding / Selection relation.
```

Therefore:

```text
observe background-as-background in the exact same event
```

is not equivalent to:

```text
perform a later probe that foregrounds evidence about the previous background.
```

A later probe may reveal traces, constraints, response propensities or reconstructed latent structure. But that later result is a new manifest event.

This gives a possible distinction between:

```text
reconstructability from later evidence
and
co-manifest availability in the original Selection event.
```

The two must not be collapsed.

---

## 4. Why this still does not prove external dynamical incompleteness

Even if the foreground-bounded relation is accepted, a sophisticated third-person theory may infer a latent model of the pre-object field from repeated observations and interventions.

Therefore the following strong theorem is still unearned:

```text
no external model can ever recover a complete predictive state description.
```

The safer distinction is:

```text
MODEL RECONSTRUCTION
an observer may infer a state / field model from many manifest observations

!=

ONTOLOGICAL CO-MANIFESTATION
the model does not turn the full pre-object field and its background relation
into the directly manifest content of the same Selection event
```

So SPD-B* should not be sold as universal unpredictability.

---

## 5. Hidden-state reduction challenge becomes sharper

The rival may now say:

```text
Fine. Let L_t be a latent variable.
I will write Z_t = (M_t, L_t),
and the complete state-space model is closed.
```

This notation alone does not settle the ontology.

Two questions must be separated:

### 5.1 Representational completeness

Can a model assign variables that encode the inferred latent/background structure?

Likely yes in many realizations.

### 5.2 Ontological equivalence

Does that encoding preserve the SRT claim that foreground/background are relational roles generated by Selection, rather than two pregiven state compartments simultaneously available before the event?

That is not guaranteed by writing a larger state vector.

The anti-reduction burden is therefore:

```text
state augmentation must reproduce not merely predictions,
but the dependency that:
foreground/background status is constituted by the Selection relation itself.
```

If a standard state-space model can reproduce that dependency without remainder, SPD-B* loses distinctiveness.

---

## 6. Strongest red-team objection — relational status may still be ordinary dynamics

Even this stronger reading remains under pressure.

Ordinary competitive dynamics can generate relative roles:

```text
winner / loser
active / suppressed
center / surround
figure-like / ground-like
```

and later observers can infer the suppressed alternatives from a complete model.

Therefore SRT cannot claim distinctiveness merely from:

```text
foreground and background are relational;
foreground is what is observed;
background affects future dynamics.
```

The remaining burden is stricter:

> Is there a principled reason why the pre-object Selection-position cannot be exhaustively represented as a third-person dynamical state while preserving the same foreground/background-generating dependency?

Current answer:

```text
NOT YET ESTABLISHED
```

---

## 7. What the author's leaning successfully improves

The author's clarification does improve the original fork in one major way.

Old crude SPD-B:

```text
Selection-position -> external observer cannot recover complete dynamics
```

was too broad and confounded SRT with generic unobservability.

New candidate SPD-B*:

```text
Selection-position / pre-object field
-> Selection generates a manifest/background relation
-> SRT-native observation registers the manifest foreground
-> the background is not co-manifest in the same event
-> later reconstruction is a new observation / Selection relation
```

This is a coherent ontology/observation thesis and is much closer to the repository's manifest-latent core.

But it still needs a final author decision and a stronger non-redundancy argument before any Level promotion.

---

## 8. Revised fork

The old `SPD-A/B/C` gate should be refined rather than answered literally.

### SPD-A

```text
third-person dynamics may be complete;
Selection-position adds only ontology-level typing / unification.
```

### SPD-B* — current author leaning

```text
SRT-native observability is foreground-bounded:
what becomes directly observable is the manifest side of Selection;
the background / pre-object relational field is not co-manifest in the same event.

This does not yet imply universal unpredictability.
The stronger claim is that observation itself is downstream of / internal to
Selection rather than a neutral view that precedes it.
```

### SPD-C

```text
foreground/background and Selection-position are fully eliminable into
ordinary augmented state-space dynamics with no lost dependency or ontology work.
```

Current machine assessment:

```text
SPD-B* is substantially stronger and more coherent than old SPD-B,
but still needs author confirmation and a direct anti-reduction test.
```

---

## 9. Next valid move

If the author confirms SPD-B*, the next pass should target the exact reduction question:

```text
Can a complete third-person latent-state / dynamical model
represent the full pre-object field and foreground/background-generating relation
without converting the SRT ontology into mere labeling?
```

The test must allow the rival its strongest form:

```text
hidden state
nonlinear dynamics
history dependence
counterfactual inference
interventions
state augmentation
```

and must not rely on practical measurement limits.

The desired outcome is not necessarily a novel mechanism. Under the author's framework-level positioning, the question is whether the SRT relation supplies a non-redundant ontological constraint / explanatory compression across domains.
