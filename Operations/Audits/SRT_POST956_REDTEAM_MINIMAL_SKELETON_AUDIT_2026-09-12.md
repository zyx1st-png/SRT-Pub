## 2. Why another checkpoint after #956

#956 made real progress but retained several architecture risks:

```text
1. visible stage-like sequencing after Selection;
2. lineage language potentially presupposing the identity it was meant to explain;
3. Bearer appearing too close to a universal next rung after One;
4. consequential / relational Many appearing too downstream;
5. Position / Concern / d being over-compressed as equivalent projections;
6. repeated use of admission + profile threatening to make every node structurally heavy.
```

Independent review sharpened these risks. The creator then explicitly required that the red-team remain minimal and rejected an early repair that introduced too many mechanisms.

Primary audit principle:

> **Solve by deletion, retyping or thinner wording before adding nodes.**

# Part I — deletion / retyping results

## 3. Oriented Openness

### #956 burden

```text
open but non-flat;
pre-object inclination / bias;
not probability / menu / teleology.
```

### Red-team pressure

Negative guards alone do not give enough positive content, but a positive definition must not presuppose enumerable alternatives.

### Result

Retain:

```text
Oriented Openness = modal / admissibility role;
Selection = eventive actualization role.
```

Do not promote to canonical yet:

```text
pre-individual asymmetry;
non-maximal invariance;
asymmetry of actualizability.
```

### Audit verdict

```text
KEEP explanatory role.
DO NOT reify as earlier substance / field / event.
DO NOT yet formalize the positive modal structure.
```

---

## 4. Selection / manifestation / backgrounding / primitive verticality

#956 same-event compression survives all current pressure.

```text
Selection = process reading;
manifestation / relative backgrounding = polar reading;
primitive verticality = structural reading.
```

### Audit verdict

```text
KEEP one primitive generative operation.
REJECT separate temporal nodes.
```

This is among the most stable parts of the post-#956 architecture.

---

## 5. Retention / Re-entry

### #956 shape

Retention / Re-entry appeared as a visible intermediate gate between Selection and One formation.

### Red-team pressure

Treating Retention, Re-entry, History and One formation as successive mechanisms risks multiplying primitives.

### Result

Use a thinner relation:

```text
prior Selection becomes historically effective
when its consequences remain conditions of later Selection.
```

Retention / Re-entry remain analytically useful, but need not be independent universal-spine nodes.

### Audit verdict

```text
DEMOTE from visible universal stage.
RETAIN as establishment language for effective Selection-history.
```

S1 / S2 remains OPEN.

---

## 6. Lineage circularity

### Problem

```text
same lineage -> recurrent reconstitution -> One
```

can presuppose continuity / identity.

### Rejected over-repair

The AI temporarily introduced:

```text
proto-lineage;
reconstitutive dependency;
internalization;
reconstitutive concentration;
closure-like boundary.
```

The author rejected this as too heavy for the skeleton.

### Current repair

Do not use lineage as the admission premise. Use the thinner formation burden:

```text
prior Selection remains effective in later Selection;
this historical conditioning becomes recurrently localized;
One forms.
```

Lineage can later name the resulting historical-generative continuity.

### Audit verdict

```text
CIRCULARITY PRESSURE = materially reduced without new ontology nodes.
EXACT ONE ADMISSION THRESHOLD = still OPEN.
```

---

## 7. One strength / profile

#956 emphasized:

```text
One admission + continuous strength/profile.
```

This remains analytically useful but is unnecessary in the minimal universal spine.

### Audit verdict

```text
KEEP admission distinction conceptually.
MOVE strength/profile dimensions out of the minimum skeleton.
```

Reason: the universal spine should explain formation first; robustness, closure, autonomy, integration depth and stability can be downstream analyses.

# Part II — boundary-case pressure on One

## 8. Stone / crystal / vortex / flame / cell

The purpose of these cases is not categorical classification but pressure on overbroad or overnarrow admission wording.

| Case | Pressure |
|---|---|
| ordinary stone | `past affects future` is too broad for One |
| growing crystal | do not exclude thin One candidates merely by object category |
| vortex / flame | external dependence does not automatically defeat One |
| cell | strong realization of recurrent historical-generative continuity without making life primitive |

### Audit result

The skeleton should remain thin enough to let these cases pressure the admission boundary later rather than hard-coding a mature autonomy / autopoiesis criterion now.

```text
formation != continuation != numerical identity.
```

Working reconstruction:

```text
formation:
localized historical-generative unity first forms.

branching / fusion may preserve continuation without unique numerical identity.
```

### Audit verdict

This is a useful pressure result but **not yet a canonical identity theorem**.

# Part V — Bearer / Concern / Agency

## 14. Bearer

Bearer survives deletion pressure because:

```text
there can be different real continuation outcomes of O
without those differences yet altering current Selection_O.
```

Thus:

```text
Bearer = YES
Concern = NO
```

remains conceptually coherent.

Working compression:

```text
Bearer = continuation-indexed consequential multiplicity.
```

### Canonical tension

Current canonical route remains:

```text
formed One / Selection-position + P + E -> Bearer.
```

Post-red-team pressure suggests P/E may later be reconstructable as establishment tests rather than visible skeleton nodes.

### Audit verdict

```text
Bearer = KEEP outside universal spine.
P/E canonical retyping = OPEN / not performed.
Bearer profile / strength = remove from minimal skeleton.
```

---

## 15. Concern

The minimum must be stronger than generic input sensitivity but thinner than a mature normative valence theory.

Current preferred minimum:

```text
Concern begins when differences among possible continuations
become effective in present Selection.
```

This blocks the trivial equation:

```text
any differential response = Concern
```

while avoiding a new primitive `good / bad` ontology.

### Audit verdict

```text
C1 differential continuation relevance = current minimum candidate.
C2 directional continuation valence = possible thickening / OPEN.
```

---

## 16. Agency

Generic relational writeback or control is too broad.

Current minimum candidate:

```text
Agency begins when Concern-conditioned Selection
counterfactually reshapes continuation-relevant relations.
```

Logical implication pressure:

```text
Agency => Concern => Bearer
```

but:

```text
Bearer !=> Concern
Concern !=> Agency
```

### Audit verdict

```text
KEEP Agency as a post-One relational gate / capacity.
DO NOT treat as universal next stage.
```

# Part IX — what improved relative to #956

## 20. Architectural gains

### 20.1 Fewer primitive-looking mechanisms

```text
one primitive generative operation = Selection
```

History, recurrence and One are organized consequences / conditions rather than additional primordial operators.

### 20.2 Universal / downstream separation

```text
universal formation ends at One;
post-One becomes relational network.
```

### 20.3 Less hidden identity presupposition

Lineage is no longer required as the premise used to establish One.

### 20.4 Many no longer Bearer-created

Relational multiplicity can be real before continuation-relevant bearing.

### 20.5 Position and d stop behaving like ontological stages

Both are retyped toward descriptors.

### 20.6 Bearer / Concern / Agency gain distinct explanatory burdens

```text
Bearer = continuation multiplicity;
Concern = those continuation differences enter Selection;
Agency = Selection writes back into continuation-relevant relations.
```

Future strongest-neighbor pressure should test whether some directional valence is indispensable.
