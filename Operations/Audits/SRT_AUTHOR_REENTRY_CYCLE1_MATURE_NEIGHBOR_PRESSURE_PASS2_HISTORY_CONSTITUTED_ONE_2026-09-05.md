# Author Re-entry Cycle 1 — mature-neighbor pressure pass 2: history-constituted One

> **Role:** second source-native pressure pass after the author explicitly selected the stronger `S2/S3 history-constituted One` reading in Pass 4. This file performs subtraction; it does not define Bearer, does not change canonical owners, and does not treat prior art as a permission gate.

---

## 0. Author statement now under pressure

Pass 4 resolved the previous fork in favor of the stronger reading:

```text
portable causal memory alone is insufficient;

history sediments in One only when prior Selection / consequence history
participates in recurrently reconstituting / maintaining the One
from which later Selection continues.
```

This licenses a formation-time question but does not make the interpretation canonical.

---

## 1. Common problem in non-SRT language

The bounded problem is:

> **How does historical causal efficacy cease to be merely distributed in a wider process field and become constitutive of one continuing unit, such that later activity proceeds from a historically reconstituted position rather than from a merely memory-equipped snapshot?**

This problem contains four burdens:

```text
P1  no finished individual may be assumed at the start;
P2  past activity may alter the conditions of later activity;
P3  a continuing unit must be reconstituted rather than merely copied / labelled;
P4  there must be a non-circular way to say when history is now attributable to that unit.
```

---

## 2. Strongest neighbor tested for the historical-constitution burden — Simondon

### Strongest neighbor tested

Gilbert Simondon, using the repository's A-class full close-read and the already-integrated PH-IND03 operator/structure patch.

### Source-native neighbor claim

The Simondon-side structure already paid in the repository is:

```text
metastable / problematic domain
+ local operation / singularity
-> local structuration
-> transductive propagation
-> new structure
-> changed conditions for further operation
```

and, more compactly:

```text
Operation_t
-> Structure_(t+1)
-> Operation_(t+1)
```

The individual is a result / phase of individuation rather than a finished substance presupposed at the beginning.

### Why strongest

This is the closest mature neighbor to the newly selected author reading because it already joins:

```text
individuation-before-individual
+
history becoming structure
+
structure conditioning later operation
```

Therefore Simondon strongly pre-empts the broad claim:

```text
SRT uniquely says that a One is historically constituted by prior operations.
```

### Specific structural item changed by the SRT candidate

After subtraction, the surviving SRT candidate is narrower:

```text
before Bearer:
historical efficacy is not yet attributed to one bearer;
it sediments in 多 / wider multiplicity

Bearer genesis:
recurrent historical efficacy becomes localized / attributable to
one emerging selection-position

then:
that history-constituted One can support stable historical Selection
```

This is not simply `operation -> structure -> operation`. It adds a proposed **change in historical attribution / localization** as part of the transition into bearerhood.

Whether that change is genuinely distinct from Simondonian transductive individuation remains open at Level 2.

## 3. Strongest neighbor tested for self-reconstitution — enactive autonomy

### Strongest neighbor tested

Enactive / autonomy tradition, especially Di Paolo's self-individuation account and Mossio–Moreno organisational closure.

### Source-native neighbor claim

The relevant enactive claim is already strong:

```text
self-production
+
self-distinction
+
material precariousness
-> open, unfinished self-individuation extended through time
```

Organisational closure adds mutually dependent constraints whose operation helps constitute the causal organization of the organism.

### Why strongest

This directly pressures any claim that SRT uniquely discovers:

```text
a unit must continually regenerate itself;
history participates in maintenance;
identity is processual rather than snapshot-based;
stable agency depends on a self-maintaining organization.
```

### Specific structural item changed by the SRT candidate

The residual candidate is not self-maintenance. It is the ordered dependency:

```text
subjectless / bearerless Selection can occur
-> its consequences may sediment without yet belonging to one bearer
-> history-localization into an emerging One helps constitute Bearer
-> stable, bearer-supported historical Selection becomes possible
```

This separates **Selection before bearer** from **stable Selection after bearer** and treats bearer formation as a historical-attribution transition rather than merely autonomous self-production.

That is a Level-1 structural repartition candidate, not a novelty verdict.

## 6. Formation-time discriminator after subtraction

Pass 4 now author-licenses the following bounded test shape.

Hold approximately matched:

```text
total historical-effect magnitude
total plasticity / memory capacity
total Selection opportunities
resource budget
initial components
environmental statistics
```

Manipulate:

```text
D — distributed-history routing:
historical effects persist but are shuffled / dispersed across 多;
no single emerging One repeatedly receives a coherent historical line.

O — One-localizing routing:
comparable historical effects repeatedly re-enter one evolving organization
and participate in reconstituting the selection-position from which
later Selection continues.
```

Candidate prediction:

```text
O > D
```

on a vector including:

```text
cross-time selection-position continuity
boundary reconstitution after perturbation
same-line repair / correction
unit-specific history-conditioned reselection
resistance to writeback shuffling
continued selection from the reconstituted position
```

### Important subtraction guard

A positive `O > D` result by itself is **not** distinctively SRT.

Ordinary recurrent learning, dynamical systems, developmental systems, autonomy models or process-individuation models may also generate the result.

To earn Level 2, the test must find a case where systems matched on the strongest neighbor variables still differ under the SRT historical-attribution partition.

## 7. What a real Level-2 residual would require

A stronger discriminator would need approximately:

```text
matched:
current autonomy / self-maintenance
current task competence
memory capacity
recurrent connectivity
current environmental affordances
current process stability

but different:
history-attribution architecture
```

where one system's historical effects repeatedly reconstitute the same emerging selection-position and the other's effects remain distributable / replaceable / shuffled without such a continuing line.

Then the SRT candidate must predict a difference not reducible to the matched neighbor variables, for example:

```text
perturbation recovery preserves unit-specific historical bias only in O;
writeback shuffling destroys formation only when historical attribution is broken;
copying state reproduces policy but not the original formation trajectory / bearer continuity;
formation of stable consequence ownership tracks attribution continuity rather than memory amount.
```

These are candidate discriminators only.
