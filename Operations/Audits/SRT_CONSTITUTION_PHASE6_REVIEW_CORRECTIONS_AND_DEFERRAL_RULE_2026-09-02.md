## 3. Evidence-deferral rule for the remainder of Phase 6

Phase 6 is a role audit, not a second Constitution-construction programme。

Therefore adopt the following execution rule for this audit round：

> **If deciding a claim requires domain-native evidence, Phase 6 records only (a) the question and (b) the evidence/declaration needed to decide it. It does not give a directional truth verdict.**

Operational form：

```text
requires domain evidence?
        |
       yes
        v
record:
- unresolved question
- current owner/status
- evidence needed
- failure/revision condition if already available

DO NOT record:
- likely true / likely false
- promote / demote because it feels philosophically cleaner
- cross-domain identity by analogy
```

This rule applies especially to：

- `d` identity: stake-coupled concern vs `||∂U/∂S||` formal realization；
- `Psi_f` identity across physics / biology / cognition / society；
- cross-scale mechanism identity vs structural compatibility；
- individuation thresholds and sigma attribution；
- collective consequence-loop measurements；
- domain-specific consciousness / subjecthood mechanisms。
