## 3. Recurrence circularity — reduced to role recurrence



```text

```

Finite positionality supplies locality to each Selection but does not require:

```text

```

Therefore a mobile / transforming One is not ruled out by the repair.

**Disposition: EXPLICIT LINEAGE CIRCULARITY REMOVED; UNIT-IDENTIFICATION REMAINS OPEN.**
