# Pass 23 — from novelty tournament to integrative whole / joint future selectability

## 0. Trigger

Author ruling:

> **「认同 继续 另外我觉得SRT 也是这种整体大于部分 不需要在竞争理论的部分里面去找新颖性。而更应该关注如何整合现有理论，产生共同重构未来可选性的能力。」**

The opening `认同` also author-selects the immediately preceding high-level transition criterion:

> **主动 Selection 的最低门槛，在于 Selection 不再只在既有垂直结构里维持当前 One，而开始作用于“决定以后什么能够被选择”的垂直结构本身。**

This audit therefore changes the **programme objective**, not the historical record.

## 1. What is retired

Retire as the primary research objective:

```text
compare SRT with theory A;
subtract everything A already says;
compare with theory B;
subtract again;
keep searching until one isolated leftover can be advertised as novelty.
```

This strategy remains useful as a guard against false claims, but it is no longer the main constructive goal.

Do not erase prior strongest-neighbor results. They remain evidence about ownership, overlap, incompatibility, and translation limits.

## 2. New constructive objective

The new target is:

```text
identify what mature theories already explain well;
preserve source-native semantics;
assign each contribution to the explanatory role where it is strongest;
connect the roles through SRT's Selection grammar;
test whether the composed framework gains a real capacity to explain / model /
eventually intervene on the joint reconstruction of future selectability.
```

Success therefore means more than broad coverage.

The candidate surplus is:

> **the coordinated capacity to reconstruct what multiple subjects / processes / scales can jointly become able to select in the future.**

## 3. SRT as a metatheoretical whole > parts candidate

The author's ruling applies the whole/parts intuition to SRT itself.

Machine-safe interpretation:

```text
SRT need not be valuable because every component concept is proprietary.
Its possible contribution can lie in how independently mature explanatory modules
are composed into a cross-level Selection architecture.
```

This is not permission for eclecticism.

A theoretical whole exceeds a pile of theories only if composition adds an explanatory or constructive capacity that the disconnected parts do not jointly provide by mere juxtaposition.

## 6. Integration grammar after the author's active/passive ruling

Current noncanonical grammar:

```text
pre-object difference / non-neutrality
-> subjectless Selection
-> non-flat selectable organization / vertical structure
-> One / Selection-position formation
-> PASSIVE Selection: preserve enough organization to continue
-> ACTIVE Selection: recursively act on the organization of later selectability
-> anticipatory commitment toward not-yet-actual continuation(s)
-> multiple positions mutually enter / alter one another's selectable spaces
-> jointly reconstructed future selectability
-> multi-subject order / possible higher-order capacities.
```

The integration task is to ask which mature theory best models each transition, and whether the transitions can be made compatible without translation fraud.

## 7. Whole-level surplus — revised criterion

Earlier passes often asked whether a neighboring theory already pays a component claim.
Pass 23 instead asks whether composition creates a new **capacity**.

Candidate criterion:

```text
PARTS:
each theory separately explains one local operation or scale.

WHOLE:
the integrated architecture explains how local constitution, survival,
anticipation, reconstruction, and multi-subject participation compose so that
future selectability itself becomes jointly reconstructible.
```

The surplus is therefore not:

```text
an extra substance;
a slogan of emergence;
a proprietary SRT word;
a leftover claim after subtraction.
```

It is a candidate **coordination / reconstruction capacity**.

## 8. Integration success tests

Use these tests in future passes.

### INT-1 — role preservation
Each imported theory must retain the strongest source-native role that made it useful.

### INT-2 — non-identity
No imported operation is simply renamed `Selection` without an explicit mapping and residual.

### INT-3 — ordering
The integration must specify what is presupposed at each step. A theory beginning with a formed agent cannot silently explain pre-subject genesis.

### INT-4 — composability
Interfaces must be able to connect without contradicting unit, scale, temporality, agency, or ontology assumptions.

### INT-5 — active/passive routing
Maintenance-oriented and reconstruction-oriented processes must not be flattened into one generic adaptation bucket.

### INT-6 — constructive surplus
The integrated framework must yield at least one cross-segment dependency, model, intervention logic, or explanatory capability unavailable from simple juxtaposition.

### INT-7 — joint future-selectability target
The strongest eventual test is whether the framework can state how multiple positions jointly change what becomes selectable later.

## 9. Failure conditions

Integration fails if:

```text
SRT merely lists neighboring theories;
all source concepts are translated into SRT vocabulary until differences disappear;
the order of explanation is circular;
formed subjects are smuggled into the pre-subject layer;
"whole > parts" is asserted without a compositional mechanism;
future selectability is only renamed adaptation / prediction / innovation;
no new cross-theory explanatory or intervention capacity appears.
```

## 10. Novelty status after the methodological shift

The author ruling does not establish that novelty is irrelevant in publication or evaluation.
It changes where novelty should be expected.

Do not prioritize:

```text
concept-level novelty by isolation.
```

Prefer testing:

```text
architectural novelty;
explanatory repartition;
cross-level compositional gain;
constructive / intervention gain;
ability to jointly reconstruct future selectability.
```

A mature neighboring theory can therefore be both:

```text
strong enough to defeat a local SRT novelty claim
AND
valuable enough to become a constitutive interface in the integrated SRT architecture.
```

These are not contradictory outcomes.
