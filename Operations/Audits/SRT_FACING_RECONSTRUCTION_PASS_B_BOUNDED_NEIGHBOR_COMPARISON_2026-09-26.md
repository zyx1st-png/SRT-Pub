# Facing reconstruction Pass B — bounded mature-neighbor comparison

> **Role:** bounded seven-family comparison required by method v0.2.
>
> **Boundary:** comparison is source-native where possible. Similarity does not prove SRT; difference does not establish novelty. No eighth neighbor family is opened.

## 0. Scope

Exactly seven families are paid:

1. Whitehead — Creativity / concrescence / transition;
2. Barad — agential cut / constitutive determination;
3. Simondon — individuation / preindividual / transduction;
4. Husserl — sedimentation / reactivation;
5. organizational-function / closure-of-constraints accounts;
6. evolutionary transitions in individuality (ETI);
7. Parfit / fission pressure on identity and continuity.

External verification was added only where repository coverage was thin (Husserl, organizational function, ETI, Parfit).

## 1. Whitehead — creative process already pays much of continuity-through-actualisation

Repository verdict already recovered:

~~~text
Creativity = ultimate metaphysical principle;
concrescence = many becoming one novel actual occasion;
completed actualities become data for later occasions;
creative advance continues through concrescence + transition.
~~~

### Facing pressure

Whitehead strongly absorbs broad claims that:

- reality is ongoing process rather than static substance;
- actuality is repeatedly generated rather than manufactured once;
- prior actuality can become data for later becoming;
- many-to-one formation and later transition can be ontologically basic.

Therefore the retired SRT noun `L0-facing continuity` gains no independent burden from saying 'generation continues while history conditions later becoming'.

### What remains distinct

Current SRT owner order still differs in one important typing:

~~~text
primitive Selection
-/-> already formed One / Bearer / subject;

whereas Whiteheadian actual occasions carry a much richer concrete occasion structure.
~~~

That is a dependency difference, not permission to claim Whitehead lacks creative continuity.

### Disposition

~~~text
continuous creative advance = SOURCE_ABSORBED by Whitehead at broad metaphysical level;
this-One recurrent reconstitution = compare, do not identify;
primitive bearerless Selection typing = SRT owner burden remains separate / OPEN comparative value.
~~~

## 2. Barad — anti-pregiven objects and constitutive cuts are strongly neighbor-paid

Repository strongest-neighbor audit already established:

~~~text
objects / agencies do not simply pre-exist intra-action;

agential cuts are constitutive rather than passive readout.
~~~

### Facing pressure

Barad strongly absorbs:

- object boundaries are not necessarily pregiven;
- observation can participate in determination;
- objectification / cut is constitutive;
- independent relata need not be ontologically primary.

This directly pressures the SRT bridge/property/object route.

### Residual discipline

Do not equate:

~~~text
Barad agential cut
= SRT Selection;
= O0;
= Gate;
= One.
~~~

The repository's narrower same-event manifestation / relative-background typing and later-condition routing remain a separate comparison burden, not a proved novelty.

### Disposition

~~~text
anti-pregivenness + constitutive objectification = SOURCE_ABSORBED strongly;
SRT Gate/bridge route must be presented as typed integration / implementation hypothesis, not unique discovery;
primitive O0 remains independently owned.
~~~

## 4. Husserl — sedimentation / reactivation strongly absorbs reconstructibility at the meaning-history level

Source-native external check:

- Husserlian sedimentation concerns prior formations becoming sedimented and shaping the living present;
- sedimented meaning can be reactivated rather than remaining merely dead deposit;
- historical reactivation can recover meaning formations not personally experienced by the present subject;
- the history of meaning is an interplay of original formations and sedimentations.

Useful external anchors:

~~~text
Saulius Geniusas, 'The Origins of Sedimentation in Husserl's Phenomenology',
European Journal of Philosophy 33 (2025), DOI 10.1111/ejop.12931.

Johan Blomberg, 'Interpreting the concept of sedimentation in Husserl's Origin of Geometry',
Public Journal of Semiotics 9 (2019), DOI 10.37693/pjos.2019.9.22182.
~~~

### Facing pressure

This is extremely close to the GRG reconstructibility intuition:

~~~text
formation
-> sedimentation / compression
-> later reactivation
-> renewed access / reinterpretation / formation.
~~~

Husserl therefore strongly absorbs:

- history can become latent rather than actively present;
- sedimentation can support efficient operation while obscuring genesis;
- later reactivation can reopen genesis / meaning;
- historical inheritance need not equal frozen repetition.

### What SRT/GRG may still add

GRG expands the burden beyond phenomenological meaning-history toward:

- intervention-sensitive cuts;
- provenance / alternative cut retention;
- multi-agent / institutional reconstruction;
- latent generative reach;
- explicit generative debt.

But these are extensions / generalizations, not grounds to claim the sedimentation-reactivation insight as new.

### Disposition

~~~text
sedimentation <-> reactivation pattern = SOURCE_ABSORBED strongly in phenomenology;
generative reconstructibility = KEEP as broader GRG research construct, not primitive ontology;
methodological reconstructibility = mature precedent exists; novelty claim NO.
~~~

## 5. Organizational function / closure of constraints — mature model for internal 'for-the-sake-of' without external designer

Source-native external + repository pressure:

Montévil & Mossio characterize biological organization as closure of mutually dependent constraints: constraints act on processes while depending on other constraints in the organization for their own maintenance.

Mossio & Bich argue that biological organization can ground intrinsic teleological / functional explanation through self-determination / self-constraint rather than an external conscious designer.

Representative anchors:

~~~text
Montévil & Mossio (2015), Biological organisation as closure of constraints,
J Theor Biol 372:179-191, DOI 10.1016/j.jtbi.2015.02.029.

Mossio & Bich (2017), What makes biological organisation teleological?,
Synthese 194:1089-1114, DOI 10.1007/s11229-014-0594-z.
~~~

### Direct pressure on the author's '为了'

This neighbor is important because it shows a mature way to retain function-like language:

~~~text
X occurs / is maintained 'for' a role
can mean:
X is constitutively embedded in a self-maintaining organization whose continued existence
depends on X's contribution,

without meaning:
an external chooser consciously designed X toward a cosmic endpoint.
~~~

This supports the author's correction that `为了` should not automatically be sanitized away.

### But no automatic import

SRT must still distinguish:

~~~text
formed organizational function
!= primitive Selection telos;

closure of constraints
!= One automatically;

self-maintenance
!= Bearer / subject / consciousness automatically.
~~~

Also, organizational-function accounts face known objectivity / redescription pressure; they are a comparator, not a universal answer.

### Disposition

~~~text
constitutive function without external designer = SOURCE_ABSORBED / strong precedent;
Position 'for maintaining non-arbitrary transformation' = may use bounded constitutive-function reading at formed level;
primitive O0/S0 teleology = NO.
~~~

## 6. Evolutionary transitions in individuality — strong source-native model of many becoming a higher-order one

External source-native check:

Major evolutionary transitions in individuality concern formerly independent replicators / organisms forming higher-level individuals.

Strong recurring burdens include:

- formation of cooperative groups;
- transformation into an integrated higher-level entity;
- division of labour;
- communication / coordination;
- mutual dependence;
- attenuation / suppression of within-group conflict.

Representative source:

~~~text
West, Fisher, Gardner & Kiers (2015), Major evolutionary transitions in individuality,
PNAS 112:10112-10119, PMCID PMC4547252.
~~~

### Facing pressure

ETI strongly absorbs broad formulations of:

~~~text
many -> one;
parts become mutually dependent;
higher-order organization becomes a new individual;
lower-level autonomy can be reduced;
coordination and conflict structure matter to higher-order unity.
~~~

### Relevance to SRT branch / merge

ETI is valuable as a **domain-specific higher-order-One comparator**:

~~~text
lower units
+ increasing interdependence / coordination
+ reduced internal conflict
+ higher-level reproduction / selection structure
-> evolutionary individuality.
~~~

But these are biological/evolutionary admission conditions, not universal metaphysical One criteria.

### Disposition

~~~text
higher-order individual formation = SOURCE_ABSORBED strongly in evolutionary domain;
SRT branch/merge cannot claim novelty from many-to-one alone;
ETI criteria may serve as domain realization / stress test for relative continuation separability;
universal One identity remains OPEN.
~~~

## 7. Parfit / fission — continuity and what-matters can diverge from numerical identity

External source-native check from the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy's personal identity discussions:

Fission cases create branching psychological continuity: one past person can stand in strong psychological continuity to two future persons, while numerical identity cannot be one-to-two.

Parfit's pressure is that what matters in survival may be psychological connection / continuity even where strict numerical identity does not hold.

### Facing pressure

This is directly useful for SRT's branch / merge problem:

~~~text
continuity relation
!= numerical identity.
~~~

A branching process can preserve strong lineage / continuity relations into more than one successor without making both successors numerically identical to the one predecessor.

Therefore SRT must not define One identity simply by:

- maximal history overlap;
- causal continuity;
- psychological continuity;
- shared pre-branch Position;
- retained similarity.

### SRT use

Parfit does not supply a universal ontology of One.

It supplies a powerful negative control:

> **if a candidate continuity criterion can branch symmetrically, it cannot by itself be a criterion of unique numerical identity.**

### Disposition

~~~text
continuity != numerical identity = SOURCE_ABSORBED / strong philosophical precedent;
branch / merge SRT criterion = remain OPEN;
One recurrent reconstitution may explain lineage / continuation without automatically solving strict identity.
~~~
