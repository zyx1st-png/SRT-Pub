# SRT -> GRG foundational bridge — internal red-team Pass 1

## 0. Scope

This pass tests the new foundational bridge before it is allowed to become a harder programme owner.

It does not adjudicate canonical ontology and does not decide that the draft minimal architecture is final.

## 1. Horizontal-research strawman risk

### Risk

The phrase "static / objective entities" can be misread as saying traditional horizontal science is itself static, naive or non-generative.

That would be false.

Modern horizontal science often studies dynamics, causal mechanisms, phase transitions, learning, evolution and generative models.

### Required guard

The distinction must be about **research floor**, not scientific sophistication:

~~~text
horizontal:
objects / variables / state spaces are sufficiently stabilized
for the target question

vertical:
the formation / stabilization / reconstruction of those objects,
boundaries or state spaces is itself part of the target question
~~~

### Verdict

~~~text
RISK IDENTIFIED
GUARD PRESENT IN DRAFT
NO DIRECTION CHANGE REQUIRED
~~~

## 2. Vertical-as-hidden-cause risk

### Risk

"Behind the object" can tempt a hidden-depth metaphysics:

~~~text
observable horizontal process
<- invisible vertical cause
~~~

which would merely add another object behind the first object.

### Required guard

Verticality must be typed as generative dependency / constitution / reconstitution depth.

It is not licensed merely by invisibility, smaller scale or explanatory preference.

Any empirical vertical claim needs a bridge showing how observations constrain the proposed generative relation.

### Verdict

~~~text
HIGH-PRIORITY GUARD
DRAFT RETAINED
~~~

## 3. Micro / macro collapse

### Risk

Cross-scale language can silently turn "vertical" into "lower scale".

This would conflict with the programme's anti-reduction direction.

### Required guard

~~~text
vertical depth != spatial / physical smallness
~~~

A higher-order organization may be vertically load-bearing if its formed organization changes later generative conditions.

### Verdict

~~~text
GUARD REQUIRED AND PRESENT
~~~

## 4. Ontology / epistemology collapse

### Risk

The author's aim to unify ontology and epistemology could be misread as idealism, constructivism or the claim that objectification creates reality.

### Required guard

Preserve:

~~~text
world-side formation
!= epistemic-public objectification
~~~

The bridge is reciprocal constraint:

~~~text
world formation
-> positioned access
-> objectification / knowledge
-> intervention / consequence
-> revised knowledge and pressure on ontology
~~~

### Verdict

~~~text
SUBSTANTIVE DISTINCTION
NO COLLAPSE AUTHORIZED
~~~

## 5. RNN over-correction risk

### Risk A

The prior programme over-read the RNN NULL as if a tested spectral / decoder section could stand for vertical organization.

### Risk B

The new correction could swing too far and make vertical structure immune to evidence:

~~~text
proxy failed
-> real vertical cause is deeper and untouched
~~~

### Required guard

Both shortcuts are forbidden.

Retain:

~~~text
tested L2-facing / objectified trace
did not pay the frozen mediation burden
~~~

Reject:

~~~text
therefore vertical efficacy is absent
~~~

But also reject:

~~~text
therefore an unspecified vertical efficacy exists anyway
~~~

A future vertical causal claim must identify a new bridge / realization / perturbation surface before receiving empirical standing.

### Verdict

~~~text
RNN INTERPRETATION = RETYPE
VERTICAL EFFICACY = OPEN, NOT RESCUED BY DEFINITION
~~~

## 6. Grammar-before-evidence risk

### Risk

Once relation families are named, the programme may search for examples until each family appears universal.

### Required guard

Relation families remain research questions / navigation families.

Admission requires:

- source-native preservation;
- negative controls;
- exclusion power;
- objectification declaration;
- revision under mismatch.

### Verdict

~~~text
G0-G11 FINALIZATION = BLOCKED
MINIMAL FAMILIES = PROVISIONAL
~~~

## 7. Trivial-generativity risk

### Risk

"Generation" can become equivalent to "anything changes".

Then GRG becomes tautological.

### Required discriminator

A vertical GRG candidate should identify at least one of:

- formation of an object / boundary / organization;
- maintenance or regeneration of a relation;
- alteration of later accessibility / constraint / selectability;
- reconstruction of a state / option / relation field;
- typed position-dependent manifestation;
- cross-scale reorganization.

Ordinary state update under a fixed objectification is not sufficient by itself.

### Verdict

~~~text
ANTI-TAUTOLOGY BURDEN REMAINS OPEN BUT TRACTABLE
~~~

## 8. Horizontal / vertical boundary may be question-relative

### Pressure

The same system can be horizontal for one question and vertical for another.

Example pattern:

~~~text
a network state may be an admitted horizontal variable
for prediction of output,

while the formation of the network organization itself
is a vertical target in another study.
~~~

Therefore the distinction cannot be assigned once-for-all to an entity.

### Revision earned

Add the principle:

~~~text
horizontal / vertical status is relation-to-research-question and objectification dependent,
not an intrinsic label attached permanently to a thing.
~~~

This is a meaningful refinement and should be carried into the bridge.

## 9. "Deeper law" overclaim risk

### Risk

"More fundamental evolutionary law" can be read as a promise that vertical research must always yield a stronger universal law.

### Required guard

The programme should seek deeper generative regularities but allow outcomes where:

- relations remain domain-limited;
- several incompatible grammars are required;
- no universal compression survives;
- vertical analysis mainly exposes hidden assumptions rather than a new law.

### Verdict

~~~text
UNIVERSALITY = ASPIRATION / OPEN
NOT A SUCCESS CONDITION BY DEFINITION
~~~

## 10. Generative prediction ambiguity

### Pressure

Horizontal research already makes generative predictions.

Therefore GRG must not claim uniqueness merely from "prediction".

The vertical increment, where present, is closer to:

~~~text
prediction / expectation about changes
in the generative conditions or reachable relation structure itself
~~~

rather than only:

~~~text
prediction of the next state inside a fixed state space.
~~~

### Verdict

~~~text
IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION
CARRY INTO BRIDGE / ARCHITECTURE
~~~

## 11. Existing M3/M4 work

### Pressure

Current M3/M4 work is methodologically useful but can bias the programme toward prospective transfer before the SRT -> GRG foundation is stable.

### Recommendation

Preserve:

- M3 semantic invariance;
- M4 prospective transfer;
- M4-E0 absorption-before-execution.

Reclassify their current role:

~~~text
calibration / later grammar validation
not foundational owner
~~~

### Verdict

~~~text
RETAIN / SUBORDINATE
~~~

## 12. Resulting required revisions

Pass 1 earns three explicit additions:

### RT-1 Question-relative verticality

~~~text
horizontal / vertical status
is indexed to the research question and objectification,
not permanently attached to an entity.
~~~

### RT-2 No hidden-vertical rescue

~~~text
failure of an objectified proxy
does not refute vertical organization,
but also does not establish an unspecified deeper vertical cause.
~~~

### RT-3 Distinguish prediction targets

~~~text
horizontal generative prediction:
future state / transformation within a stabilized objectification

vertical generative expectation:
formation / reconstruction / alteration of generative conditions,
object spaces or relation accessibility
~~~

These are programme-method refinements, not canonical ontology claims.
