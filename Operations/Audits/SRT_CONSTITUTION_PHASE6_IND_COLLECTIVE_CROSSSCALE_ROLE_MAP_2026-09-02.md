## 6. `M(t)` and Constitution `(5)`

Def-C-2 says roughly：

```text
M_ij(t)
= degree to which consequences of P_j's selection
  return to P_i's future selection capacity
```

This is useful formalization **after a collective cut is declared**。

It cannot answer by itself：

```text
Why count this consequence as landing on P_i?
Why is P_i the relevant unit?
Would the attribution survive another boundary / grain / timescale?
```

Therefore the correct dependency is：

```text
declare collective cut
-> run consequence-attribution burden
-> build/estimate M(t) under that cut
```

not：

```text
M(t) exists
-> consequence attribution solved
```

### Evidence/declaration needed

Any domain use should specify：

```text
collective membership boundary
member vs institutional unit distinction
timescale
direct vs delayed consequence channels
externalized effects
feedback / recourse channel
measurement and normalization convention
```

This is especially important when member-level and institution-level consequence attribution diverge。
