# GRG PR #1016 whole-review -> author-aligned v0.2 — Pass 1

Record how the independent review of PR #1016 and the subsequent author dialogue changed the foundational architecture.

This is not a new independent review of v0.2.

PR #1016 attempted to consolidate:

- SRT -> GRG bridge;
- horizontal / vertical research;
- G0-G11 retyping;
- relation families;
- composition;
- expectation / normativity;
- reflexive learning.

Independent review identified both governance and architecture problems.

## 3. Anti-tautology / vertical-vs-rich-horizontal gap

Review finding:

The v0.1 author gate lost the open question:

> What distinguishes a vertical relation from a sufficiently rich horizontal causal model?

Author dialogue clarified:

~~~text
verticality
= reopening formation / maintenance / dissolution / reconstruction
  of conditions treated as given by the research frame

!= extra hidden causal substance
~~~

v0.2 adds:

- explicit horizontal baseline;
- what is treated as given;
- what is reopened;
- changed generative condition;
- bridge assumption;
- near-control / perturbation;
- failure condition.

Status:

~~~text
BLOCKER ADDRESSED ARCHITECTURALLY
empirical adequacy = still OPEN
~~~

- historical correlation.

Path dependence becomes mature-neighbor pressure on exclusion power.

Status:

## 7. R-HIST / R-RECON boundary

Review finding:

R-HIST and R-RECON could overlap heavily.

They are not required to be mutually exclusive.

~~~text
R-HIST:

R-RECON:
present generation -> future generative condition
~~~

A reconstruction at t0 may become historical sedimentation at t1.

v0.2 also demotes former R-RECON-a/b from two primitive-looking subfamilies to:

~~~text
structural field reconstruction
+
realization / evidence status
~~~

~~~text
BOUNDARY REFRAMED AS DIRECTIONAL VIEWS OF CONTINUOUS PROCESS
~~~

## 10. Generative Expectation drift

Review finding:

v0.1 used expectation -> mismatch -> revision language that risked collapsing E-B back into prediction.

Author-aligned correction:

~~~text
P_G
= Generative Prediction

E_G
= structural significance of continuation / transformation
  relative to an admitted generative order
~~~

E_G outcomes may include:

- preserve;
- strengthen;
- extend;
- reorganize;
- substitute;
- weaken;
- hollow;
- destroy.

Mismatch belongs to:

- grammar typing;
- prediction;
- bridge;
- boundary;
- expectation classification;

not to the fact that a relation may actually be destroyed.

Status:

~~~text
PREDICTION / EXPECTATION SEPARATION HARDENED
~~~

~~~text

+ revisable grammar
~~~

More whole does not mean more harmonious.


- multi-locus composition;
- cross-scale re-entry.
