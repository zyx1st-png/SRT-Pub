## 3. `Core_Law/SRT_Irreversibility.md` — blocking D3-B owner

### Accepted core

The strongest currently licensed irreversibility remainder is history-relative:

```text
if a realized history reaches an independently established terminal / absorbing state,
that terminated history cannot resume selection from that state by itself.
```

This is compatible with former P1-T07's retained absorption remainder.

### Blocking overreach

The current file goes substantially beyond that strength:

1. `Def-IRR-1` states that every ISP selection space under L0 irreversibility contains an absorbing-state structure. This is not established by the current core.
2. `Def-IRR-3` states that historical consequences can never be recovered to zero, conflating occurrence non-equivalence with durable future efficacy.
3. `T-IRR-1` requires learned history to retain a non-zero trace contribution and states that trauma permanently sediments into `theta_trace`.
4. The learning-signature section requires cumulative non-zero trace, monotonically increasing `Psi_f` payment, and non-erasable downstream change as universal signatures of real learning.

These conflict with the current L0 boundary:

```text
actual occurrence
!= anchoring persistence
!= durable/localized history
!= recurrent historical efficacy.
```

A past event cannot become "never occurred", but its future causal / representational residue may decay, disperse, be overwritten, become inaccessible, or cease to be load-bearing.

### D3-B requirement

Rewrite around a three-way distinction:

```text
occurrence non-equivalence
!= retained historical efficacy
!= localized durable trace.
```

Keep terminal absorption only conditionally, when the absorbing/terminal structure is independently established. Do not derive universal permanent memory, permanent trauma trace, monotonic Psi_f accumulation, or universal absorbing-state existence from primitive irreversibility.
