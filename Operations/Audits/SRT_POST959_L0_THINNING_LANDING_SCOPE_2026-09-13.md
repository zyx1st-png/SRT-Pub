## 2. What must be thinned

### 2.1 Current three-component non-neutrality package

Current L0 wording presents:

```text
kappa_0 / intrinsic granularity;
epsilon / non-self-erasure direction;
irreversibility / historical asymmetry
```

as three explicit structural asymmetries carried by L0.

Post-#957 landing rule:

```text
minimum modal burden = open but non-flat;
exact positive inheritance from kappa_0 / epsilon / irreversibility = OPEN;
none of the three is deleted in this PR;
none is newly declared redundant or derivable;
none is required as an independently necessary member of the new minimum merely because the legacy L0 listed all three.
```

Therefore the L0 prose should distinguish:

```text
minimum modal commitment
vs
legacy/current stronger decomposition candidates.
```

### 2.2 Irreversibility overreach

Retain:

```text
genuine Selection cannot become ontically equivalent to never having occurred;
occurrence != persistence;
loss of anchoring != retroactive non-occurrence.
```

Thin / withdraw as universal L0 minimum where currently asserted without separate burden:

```text
intrinsic granularity is THE source of irreversibility;
spacetime makes irreversibility absolutely impossible in every relevant sense;
Landauer erasure is dismissed as merely an atemporal abstraction;
time is fully defined as an L2 pheromone accumulated from irreversible Selections;
primitive irreversibility automatically yields durable / recurrent historical conditioning.
```

These stronger claims may survive elsewhere under their own owners or future adjudication; they are not paid by the #957 minimum skeleton.

### 2.3 Primitive Selection overloading

Current L0 terminology must not define primitive Selection by:

```text
persistence;
anchoring;
durable history;
Concern;
承担;
subject-level decision chain;
post-One closure.
```

The L0 may retain downstream distinctions and examples, but the primitive entry must remain thinner.

### 2.4 Concern / embodiment / bearer-like language

Current L0 Concern semantics are broadly compatible with #957 when read as differential endogenous incorporation into current Selection.

Retain that core, but guard:

```text
Concern != typed Bearer;
Concern != universal stage after Bearer;
Bearer != Concern by definition;
d-value != Concern definition;
embodiment / grounding closure != primitive Selection definition;
承担 / non-outsourcing != universal subject gate inside L0.
```

Do not replace the current canonical P+E Bearer route in this PR.
