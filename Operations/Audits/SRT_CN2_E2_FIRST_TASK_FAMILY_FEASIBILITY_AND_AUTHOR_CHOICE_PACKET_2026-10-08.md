## 1. Author-owned E2 target; what cannot change to fit an attractive dataset

Current programme and 2026-10-08 author Option A specify **one bounded, model-relative predictive question**:

> Given lawful matched observations, declared structural learning permissions, a comparable data/complexity/computational budget and prespecified held-out tests, does a **named, implementable shared-geometry representation** improve **held-out predictions** of grouping, boundary, transition and related cross-probe organization against **named, capable H1/H2 implementations**?

H1 = implementation/constraint; H2 = latent/representation/model organization; H3 = effective reachability/differentiability **description or consequence**, not a third mutually exclusive model mechanism. If named capable H2 accounts for the data, allow **NO LOCAL COMPARATIVE INCREMENT**; do not infer a universal H2 family reduction or SRT ontology conclusion.

**E2 pass: prespecified held-out predictive gain, not compression alone.** At similar predictive adequacy, compression is an optional *separate* score. **E3** prospective intervention causality and **E4** genuinely future recursive reconstitution cannot substitute for E2 and remain independently gated. ## 2. Four author options (NONE selected; no machine-default experiment)

### A — consequence-driven multidimensional *concept reorganization* and cross-probe transfer

**Task:** use a fixed set of multifeature stimuli in two contexts with different **learned consequence contingencies**, rather than merely announcing a new category. After interaction, collect similarity, grouping, boundary, continuation, anomaly and transfer probes. The existing 09-26 E2–E4 candidate §2–4 proposes this kind of two-world setup, **but it is NOT a frozen runnable experimental protocol**.

**Source-native strongest-neighbor family — classic categorization / multidimensional psychological space (M1 correction):** The earlier version missed the **GCM/ALCOVE/SUSTAIN/Goldstone tradition**, directly relevant to fitting an identification/similarity representation and predicting categorization or perception. **Native probe coverage / debt (recalibrated):** The **identification / similarity–classification pairing and attention-weighted category boundaries** already have mature parametric paradigms and model-fit conventions (Nosofsky, ALCOVE, SUSTAIN). **P1-adjacent** identification/perceptual-discrimination judgments, **P2** exemplar-category membership and **P3** category-boundary behavior are therefore **plausible adaptations with existing methodological precedents**, **not six wholly novel measurement inventions**. **Task:** participants or models learn transitions over a graph/relation system, then encounter a changed context or local relation and make sequence predictions and relational-transfer choices. Only extend to perceptual grouping/similarity/morph boundaries if those judgments can be independently and sensibly defined for the same learned relations.
