# Minimum unifying tool — upstream Ground / nonclosure pressure

> **Boundary:** machine-side noncanonical audit. This file does not solve the Ground question, define `Ghost`, equate yin with L0, equate friction with `Psi_f`, add a new primitive, edit L0/Spine, assign novelty, promote a Level, or close HOLD.
>
> **Purpose:** test whether an older author-confirmed nonclosure / dynamic-balance intuition contains a real latent burden that can connect the accepted `Selection as Creator != ultimate Ground` split to the later SRT programme of future selectability, friction, reselectability and L2 scaffolding.

---

## 0. Executive verdict

A real latent architecture was thinned out of the current minimum spine, but it is **not yet established as a new primitive or as a solved Ground**.

Current canonical route remains correctly minimal:

```text
non-flat openness
-> finite-position-indexed subjectless Selection
   = actual differentiation
   = manifestation / relative backgrounding
-> regenerative vertical formation
-> One.
```

Cycle 3 explicitly left open:

```text
why primitive Selection / actualisation obtains at all.
```

The July source-intuition cards contain a candidate answer-shape that was never canonically adjudicated:

```text
non-exhausted / nonclosed generative side
<->
local convergence / manifestation

local convergence does not abolish the nonclosed side;
it localizes and redistributes the relation;
formed result and its friction/constraint conditions co-change;
therefore no current Selection-result can monopolize all later generative qualification.
```

This is stronger than mere `potential remains possible` and weaker than a self-grounding mechanism.

After strongest-neighbor subtraction:

```text
continuous becoming                    = strongly neighbor-paid;
metastability / retained potential     = strongly Simondon-paid;
virtual not exhausted by actual        = strongly Deleuze-paid;
constitutive exclusion / ongoing cut   = strongly Barad-paid;
absence / constraint efficacy          = strongly Deacon-paid;
creative advance                       = strongly Whitehead-paid;

exact SRT local novelty                = NOT ESTABLISHED.
```

But one architecture-level candidate survives:

> **Selection may be intended not merely as actualisation from openness, but as a local convergent act whose own success simultaneously reconstitutes the conditions/frictions of later Selection while remaining constitutively dependent on a non-exhausted relative-background side.**

This may connect:

```text
Creator-role
-> manifest/background verticality
-> One formation
-> future selectability
-> friction/payability
-> active reconstruction
-> L2 scaffold
-> anti-absolutization / generative reselectability.
```

The next question is whether this is a genuine common grammar or only a poetic synthesis of neighbor-paid parts.

## 4. Machine reconstruction of the latent burden

Use no new canonical term. For audit only, decompose the burden into `NC1-NC7`.

### NC1 — non-exhaustion

A determinate manifestation does not exhaust the conditions of further actualisation.

### NC2 — constitutive asymmetry

The nonmanifest / relatively backgrounded side is not merely later residue; manifestation is constituted as an asymmetry relative to what is not manifest in the same way.

### NC3 — local resolution, not global closure

Selection locally resolves / actualizes a difference, but does not thereby close the generative field as a whole.

### NC4 — reciprocal condition change

The formed manifest organization and the conditions/frictions under which later Selection occurs are co-changed by the event.

### NC5 — later generative efficacy

Where retained, this changed organization can alter future selectability / candidate generation / burden.

### NC6 — anti-absolutization

No current Selection-result, option-set, comparison standard or selection authority is thereby licensed to become the final unchangeable generator of all later Selection.

### NC7 — reconstructability

At stronger downstream levels, consequences may return so that the structure organizing later Selection can itself be revised.

The key point:

```text
NC1-NC4 are candidate upstream/formation burdens;
NC5-NC7 are downstream consequences/realizations.
```

Do not back-project NC6/NC7 into bare L0 as normative or cognitive content.

## 11. The live candidate after subtraction

The July intuition should not be revived as `Ghost ontology`.

The stronger recovery is a relation:

> **Every SRT Selection is intended to be a local actualising convergence that constitutes a manifest/relative-background asymmetry. Where that event becomes historically effective, the formed organization and the conditions of later Selection co-change. No local convergence thereby exhausts or owns all later generative qualification. At stronger levels, this non-finality becomes revisability of the generator/scaffold itself.**

This relation joins three previously separated SRT burdens:

```text
A. creative actualisation / Creator-role;
B. relative-background verticality;
C. future selectability / generative reselectability.
```

It does **not** yet answer why the first Selection occurs.

That is crucial:

```text
nonclosure explains why a formed result need not be final
!=
nonclosure proves why any actualisation occurs at all.
```

So U-GROUND remains OPEN.

## 12. Why this matters for the minimum unifying tool

Machine-side candidate architecture:

### Thin ontology spine


### Cross-cutting unifying tool

Ask, at the appropriate layer:

```text
G — GENERATION
What makes a difference actual / operative, and what remains relatively backgrounded?

N — NON-FINALITY / NONCLOSURE
What does the current formation fail to exhaust, and how can that remaining relation still matter?

S — SELECTABILITY
How does retained formation reshape what can become selectable later?

I — INCIDENCE
Which position receives benefit, loss, burden, consequence and writeback?

P — PAYABILITY
Can the relevant burden be borne without destroying that position's future selection capacity?

R — RECONSTRUCTION
Can consequence return revise the conditions / standards / boundaries / candidate-generator of later Selection?

L2 — SCAFFOLD
When history becomes background infrastructure, does it amplify endogenous reselectability or substitute for it?
```

This is a **research grammar candidate**, not a new ontology ladder.

It may be closer to what SRT has actually been trying to build than an ever-thickening list of universal stages.

## 14. Stronger conceptual gain — two kinds of openness must be separated

The combined reconstruction now exposes a distinction that was previously blurred.

### Modal openness

```text
before / upstream of a determinate event in dependency order:
not all determination is already fixed;
no completed object/menu/chooser is presupposed.
```

### Generative non-finality

```text
after / through a determinate event:
the current result does not exhaust all later generative qualification;
formed structure may alter, but does not finally close, future Selection conditions.
```

These are not identical.

A theory could allow modal openness but produce irreversible final closure after one event.

A theory could also describe ongoing dynamical non-finality while presupposing already formed objects.

SRT's intended architecture appears to require both, but their exact relation remains to be author-adjudicated only if the next deletion pressure shows that the distinction is load-bearing.
