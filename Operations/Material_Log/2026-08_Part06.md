The source now has page/figure anchors in its SourceCard. SRT-side use is restricted to O-track relation placement / boundary hardening, with D-track not claimed, strict separation of signal magnitude, felt intensity, control weight, physiological burden, canonical `d`, and `Psi_f`, and no direct vagal-mediation or phenomenal-necessity claim.

**2026-08-17 Timmons–Brenner / AGING01:** the Cell Letter is retained as a bounded causal-identification pressure source, not as evidence that SRT explains aging or that the information theory of aging is false. No canonical owner was changed.
