## Current note

PH-PER01 is retained because its narrowed discriminating pressure is not merely "history affects attention" but whether history can **reparameterize the perceptual grouping / salience map** on which later priority and invitation gating operate. The material remains non-canonical and does not itself rewrite T-Cog-5, `L_2`, `d-value`, subjecthood, agency or responsibility.

PH-UNC01 is retained because the full-monograph close read yields a stable cross-author pressure stronger than the generic claim that unconscious processes exist: conscious perception, desire, belief and reasoning can depend on positive determining structures that are not themselves consciously transparent. Its distinct SRT-side delta is the embodied-belief question: repeated experience may create habit, but SRT must still ask **where consequences land and what they rewrite**. The patch therefore treats repetition as insufficient by itself for explaining bearer-specific belief weight and routes the residual into existing embodied-position / stake / writeback architecture without changing canonical `L_2`, `d`, `Psi_f`, Stable ISP, Real Choice Moment or consciousness.

NEURAL33/34 are retained because the combined package separates **realized distributed relation** from **history-conditioned relation disposition** and makes the latter experimentally risky through matched-current-state / different-history tests. The package remains non-canonical and explicitly does not equate ripple, synchrony, functional connectivity, metaplasticity or local `K^(tau)` notation with `L1`, `L2`, selection, consciousness or bearer.

NEURAL35 is retained because it adds a bounded perturbation-side discriminator to the already-existing ST-A / generative-reselectability architecture: **candidate reopening is not reselectability**. The new bridge is useful only if `Opening`, `Reanchoring` and same-bearer consequence return can be separated from standard flexibility, expectancy, arousal, salience, reward and generic learning. `under-anchoring` remains a disposable P3/P4 working label, not a canonical SRT term or clinical diagnosis.

Deleuze's *Logic of Sense* is retained as a **novelty-audited comparative pressure source**, not as a new SRT theory node. Its strongest value after deduplication is methodological: keep problem-space open against answer-menu preformation, keep irreversible occurrence distinct from later revision, and keep individuation-order-parameter claims exposed when the bearer boundary itself can move. All stronger term-to-term identifications remain prohibited.
