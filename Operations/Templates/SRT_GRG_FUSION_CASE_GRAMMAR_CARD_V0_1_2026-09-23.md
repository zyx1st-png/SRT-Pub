# GRG Fusion-Case Grammar Card v0.1 — experimental companion template

> Role: reusable noncanonical **companion overlay** for source-native GRG fusion cases.
>
> The active `Operations/Templates/SRT_GRG_GENERATIVE_TRANSFORMATION_RECORD_TEMPLATE_V0_2.md` remains the primary process-first GRG record. This Card does not replace GTS v0.2, the current GRG foundational owner, or canonical SRT.
>
> Positive GRG maturity / transfer credit continues to use the live M0–M5 framework, strongest reasonable horizontal baseline, mature-neighbor pressure when required, and the existing M4-E0 absorption-before-execution gate. Card-local labels cannot bypass those owners.
>
> Primary discipline: **source-native first, GRG second**.

## 0. Hard guards

A Card is not complete merely because every field contains text.

A valid Card may contain:

- empty fields;
- NOT ESTABLISHED;
- NOT APPLICABLE;
- mapping rejected;
- source/GRG type mismatch;
- unresolved ambiguity.

Do not force source vocabulary into GRG categories.

Do not infer:

`same word -> same role -> same mechanism -> same ontology`.

Do not use a parallel maturity progression here. Record the **Card-local mapping disposition** with the non-ordinal vocabulary in §18.1, and record live programme maturity separately with M0–M5 in §18.2.

### Selection hard guard

Source-native uses of “selection”, “choice”, “filtering”, “actualization”, “collapse”, “self-determination”, “negative selection” or similar vocabulary are not automatically SRT Selection.

SRT Selection remains a term of art. Current canonical routing (`Core_Law/SRT_Generative_Ontology_Spine.md §2`) requires two co-primitive analytic burdens:

```text
O0 — non-maximal indifference / openness
S0 — actualising Selection
O0 before S0: NO
S0 before O0: NO
```

This Card must not reduce primitive Selection to S0 alone, and must not reinterpret O0 as a prior option menu, field, container, probability distribution or outcome space.

The Selection audit below is a **Card-local compatibility / negative-control screen**, not a sufficient canonical classifier. The exact anti-tautology criterion remains OPEN.

### Selection-event audit coexistence guard

The repository already has an active bounded practice-layer Selection-event audit:

- `03_Bridges/SRT_Selection_Event_CompactCore.md`;
- `Operations/SRT_UNIFIED_SELECTION_EVENT_AUDIT_PROTOCOL_2026-08-04.md`.

This Card does **not** replace or duplicate that surface.

Division of labour:

- the Card's actuality / event-individuation / relational-non-neutrality / representation-invariance screens are **source-theory / mapping-layer membership pressures**;
- SEA / CompactCore supplies **practice-layer bounded Selection-event candidate auditing** with declared event boundary / timescale, graded negative controls and non-compensation rules;
- where a Card proceeds to a concrete event claim, its declared grain and boundary should inherit the SEA discipline against post-hoc event slicing;
- Card screening `!=` SEA pass;
- SEA pass `!=` canonical proof of primitive Selection;
- SEA consequence-bearing / historical-efficacy gates must not be imported as necessary conditions for primitive Selection occurrence, because terminal Selection remains canonically possible.

### Card authority guard

The Card is:

- a research interface;
- a compression / comparison device;
- a provenance-preserving mapping surface.

The Card is not:

- a universal ontology inventory;
- a source replacement;
- a proof of SRT;
- a proof of GRG scientific distinctiveness;
- a license to create a new GRG foundational version.

# 21. Fusion-case execution sequence

Recommended order:

`source-native reading -> source reconstruction -> Card fill -> non-identity audit -> anti-metaphor test -> generative expectation -> failure/reopening condition -> Card outcome -> cross-card reconciliation`.

Do not begin with a target GRG conclusion.

Do not force a full Card in one pass.

Stop when the source evidence no longer supports the next field.

---

# 22. Cross-card reconciliation

After at least two sufficiently developed fusion cases that have each paid the baseline block and produced a case-level closeout, compare Cards on:

- fields that repeatedly carry the same generative role;
- fields that are repeatedly empty;
- false equivalences exposed by different sources;
- fields that are source-specific rather than GRG-general;
- newly required splits;
- opportunities to remove redundant fields;
- whether a transferable generative expectation survives without ad hoc retuning.

Cross-card reconciliation is allowed to:

- revise the Card;
- demote a GRG term;
- split a GRG term;
- reject a mapping;
- preserve domain-specific vocabulary instead of forcing unification.

Case count alone is not evidence of universality.
