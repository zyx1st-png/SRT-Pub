## 0. Executive Summary

This audit reviews `Neuroscience/SRT_Neuro_09_Integ_Eq.md` §1-§4 as a pre-extraction boundary pass for future PR-D Batch 1.

Overall finding: §1-§4 are suitable for extraction in principle, but not as a blind full move. The safest future plan is to keep a short owner-file summary and move the detailed comparison material to an Annex after human review of §2.

Recommendation: **C. First human-adjudicate the absorption table, then proceed to PR-D Batch 1 with owner summary retention.**
