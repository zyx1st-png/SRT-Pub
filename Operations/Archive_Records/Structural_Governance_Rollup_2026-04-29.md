## 5. Governance pattern now established

Future domain hardening should follow this sequence:

```text
pre-audit
  -> exact inventory
  -> frontmatter / claim-status normalization
  -> read-only adjudication
  -> small copy-to-annex extraction, if safe
  -> closure report
```

Do not start with extraction.

Do not move formal material until a section-level adjudication identifies:

1. exact source section;
2. destination file;
3. owner material that must remain;
4. formulas that must not move;
5. guardrails required in the destination;
6. claim-status after movement.
