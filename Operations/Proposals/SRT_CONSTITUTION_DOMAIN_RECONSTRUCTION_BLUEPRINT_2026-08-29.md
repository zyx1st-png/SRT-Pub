# SRT Constitution + Domain Reconstruction Blueprint

> **Author-level programme decision, non-canonical.** This file governs the current reconstruction programme and repository routing. It does not itself redefine any frozen canonical term or retrospectively change already-published/submitted manuscripts.

## 0. Executive identity decision

SRT will no longer organize its highest layer as a tower that must internally derive the world from formal axioms.

The new working identity is:

```text
SRT Constitution
= a bearer-involved perspective framework
  for changing what is treated as given,
  how a One is cut,
  how history/position/bearing enter the picture,
  and what questions become visible.

Domain research
= the place where objects are operationalized,
  formalized, modeled, measured, compared and tested.
```

The Constitution is not an axiom system. It does not prove. It provides a structured viewpoint and a set of executable perspective operations.

The domains do not merely “apply” a finished metaphysics. Each domain starts from its own traditionally given objects and asks what changes when those givens are reopened under the SRT perspective. Any proof, mathematical model, empirical test, D2/D3 discriminator or rival comparison is paid at the domain level.

The unification claim, if it eventually becomes warranted, is therefore downstream:

> multiple domains independently use the same perspective grammar and produce recurring structural gains; unification is observed after domain work, not commanded by the Constitution in advance.

---

## 1. Why Constitution does not formalize

The reason is internal to the SRT perspective, not only methodological caution.

Formalization is itself a powerful objectification practice. To become cross-reader, cross-lab, cross-time and cross-context transportable, a formalism typically declares or stabilizes:

```text
unit
boundary
grain
state space
variables
equivalence classes
normalization rules
admissible transformations
measurement conventions
```

It thereby performs categorization, coarse-graining, normalization and equivalence-making. This is not a defect; it is a central achievement of public science.

But the Constitution is trying to inspect an earlier and different problem:

> What is lost or transformed when a bearer-involved, history-bearing, position-indexed event is turned into a public object that can be carried independently of the bearer who produced or bears it?

Therefore:

```text
formalism may model bearer-related variables
!=
formalism thereby occupies the bearer position
```

and:

```text
bearer-as-position
!=
bearer-as-modeled-variable
```

A formal theory can describe positions from a common external representation without thereby occupying those positions.

### 1.1 Not anti-mathematics

The programme does **not** infer that mathematics cannot express contextual, indexed or observer-relative relations. Domain mathematics may explicitly carry indices, gauges, histories, contexts or relational structure.

The guard is narrower:

> Do not treat a de-indexed or under-declared formal projection as the constitutional ground of the bearer relation it already presupposes.

Hence the Constitution keeps objectification at the minimum needed for author/reader orientation; domains are free to formalize once their objectification contract is declared.

### 1.2 “脱产” as an internal diagnostic

“脱产” is retained as an author-side diagnostic term for knowledge that has been detached from the concrete production/bearing/consequence position so that it can circulate publicly. It is not a condemnation of science and is not yet an English canonical term.

## 2. Bearer participation, not merely bearer description

SRT is not satisfied by adding an `observer`, `agent i` or `theta` variable to a third-person model.

For relevant phenomena it asks whether reality formation itself includes:

```text
who/what participates
who/what bears the history
where consequences return
which continuation is changed
which future possibilities are opened/closed from here
```

Potential bearers include an individual subject, a reader/researcher engaged in the perspective operation, an organism/process, or a social/collective unit when the domain independently justifies such a unit. This does not automatically grant subjecthood, phenomenality or epistemic privilege to every unit.

The methodological ambition is participatory only where the phenomenon may itself be bearer-constituted.

---

## 3. Two claims that must not be conflated

### A. Ontological situatedness

Some reality relations may be constitutively position-/bearer-indexed. Current source language such as `still this`, `from here`, and `for me` marks different unity burdens rather than one pre-given subject.

### B. Participatory methodology

Inquiry is itself performed by situated bearers, and in some domains the research method must preserve rather than delete the potentially constitutive participation relation.

These claims are logically distinct. The programme does not pretend A automatically proves B.

### Preservation bridge

The methodological bridge is:

> If a class of phenomena is being investigated precisely for potentially constitutive bearer-indexed relations, then a method that removes those relations before testing their role may already have changed the target phenomenon.

This licenses a participatory channel where needed; it does not imply that every science must become first-person or participatory.

---

## 4. Reflexivity: the Constitution has no God-view exemption

The Constitution itself is written from a position. It therefore cannot claim authority by pretending to stand outside all positions.

Its proposed validity mode is:

```text
author-positioned proposal
-> reader can re-enact the perspective operation from another position
-> recurring structures/problems may become visible
-> domain methods test whether the re-framing produces real explanatory/research gain
```

The relevant standard is **cross-position re-enactability + productive adequacy**, not position-free revelation.

Boundary:

```text
position-indexed access
!= general epistemic privilege
!= infallibility
```

A bearer may possess information inaccessible to another position without thereby owning the final causal or theoretical explanation.

---

## 5. Constitution as perspective interface

A substantive constitutional item should be executable, not merely contemplative.

Preferred schema:

```text
Perspective
From where are we invited to look?

Default suspended
What is temporarily no longer treated as already given?

Reader-entry operation
What can a reader actually do to re-open the phenomenon?

What becomes visible
What distinction, dependency or new question appears?

Boundary
What does this perspective not claim?
```

This is the main defence against Constitution becoming unlimited philosophical prose.

A constitutional statement that only demands assent but offers no perspective operation should be treated as suspect until the author explains its role.

## 6. Domain research template

Each domain starts with the domain’s own best practices and its traditionally given One.

```text
0. WHO IS IN THE PHENOMENON?
Who participates, bears, produces data, pays consequences or changes the future?

1. GIVEN ONE
What unit/object/system/subject does the field already treat as given?

2. OBJECTIFICATION / DETACHMENT
Which boundary, grain, equivalence, normalization, position and history choices made that public object possible?

3. SRT RE-ENTRY
What changes when bearer participation, historical attribution and position are restored as possible constitutive variables?

4. REORGANIZATION
Which old problems become one problem, move level, dissolve, or require a new distinction?

5. NEW PROBLEMS
What was invisible because the field’s standard objectification made it unavailable as a question?

6. DOMAIN TEST
Can the field’s own evidence, experiments, models and mathematics distinguish the new framing?

7. DOMAIN INCREMENT GATE
Does the field’s strongest existing framework already deliver the same gain? If yes, SRT does not claim domain increment here.
```

Subtraction/novelty audit therefore moves to its proper location: the gate on **domain increment claims**, not a permission gate for constitutional thought.

## 7. Bearer–Objectification Declaration (domain candidate)

Before a domain formalization is treated as carrying an SRT-sensitive claim, declare at least:

```text
1. Given One / unit
2. Boundary
3. Grain / timescale
4. Equivalence rule: what counts as the same?
5. Normalization / standardization
6. History loss: what history is ignored?
7. Position loss: what position differences are ignored?
8. Bearer substitution: what is assumed invariant if the bearer changes?
9. Consequence attribution: where do effects count as landing?
10. Participation: who produces, interprets, bears or changes the phenomenon?
```

This declaration is a domain-method candidate, not a constitutional axiom and not yet a universal reporting standard. It should first be used in a real deep-well domain before any general standardization claim.

## 10. Sigma as a worked warning: premature de-indexing

The 2026-08-29 sigma probes are a repository-local worked example of a narrower failure mode:

- representation change can alter bare sigma while preserving operator behaviour;
- trace attribution is not uniquely closed across owners;
- provenance magnitude and causal control can dissociate sharply.

Do **not** overread this as “mathematics cannot represent bearer indexicality.”

The lesson is:

> a bearer-indexed, boundary-sensitive, history-attributed relation cannot safely be compressed into a supposedly natural position-independent scalar before its attribution, representation and objectification contract is declared.

This is a retrospective diagnostic, not constitutional proof.

## 11. Neighbor Map: positioning without permission gating

After an author thesis has converged, a bounded Neighbor Map asks:

```text
Who has already asked a nearby question?
What did they clarify?
Where did their project fail or stop?
What vocabulary will a competent reader already bring?
What does SRT need to say so it is understood rather than mistaken for a relabeling?
```

Likely neighborhoods include process philosophy, phenomenology/lifeworld critique, situated knowledge, view-from-nowhere debates, second-order cybernetics, personal knowledge, reflexive sociology, enactivism/neurophenomenology, individuation and relational ontology.

Neighbor work uses `resonance / contrast / pressure / translation`, not `external theory proves SRT`.

Novelty is only asserted when the programme actually needs a novelty claim.

## 12. Identity freeze

For one complete cycle of:

```text
Constitution v1 author convergence
+
internal/neighbor pressure review
+
one domain deep well
```

SRT's programme identity is frozen as `Constitution + Domain Research`.

Do not initiate a fourth identity redesign merely because a newer formulation sounds elegant.

Reopen only if one of the following occurs:

1. Constitution develops a non-local internal contradiction that cannot be repaired at thesis level;
2. one complete deep well, followed by a second bounded attempt if needed, shows no meaningful explanatory/question-generating/domain-testing gain;
3. the identity repeatedly conflicts with indispensable existing research practice rather than merely requiring reclassification;
4. the author explicitly reopens the identity after reviewing evidence from the cycle.

## 14. Success and failure

Constitution is not validated by internal derivation.

Its success is progressively supported if it repeatedly:

- exposes hidden givens;
- produces distinctions that reduce explanatory conflation;
- generates researchable questions that standard objectification missed;
- helps domains organize evidence more coherently;
- survives reflexive and neighbor pressure;
- produces domain-level results that can be independently tested.

Its failure mode is practical and philosophical: if repeated deep-well use adds only vocabulary, cannot generate executable perspective shifts, or provides no domain-side gain beyond mature existing frameworks, the perspective is unproductive in that scope and must be revised or abandoned there.
