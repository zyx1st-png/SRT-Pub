# SRT -> GRG Foundational Bridge v0.1

> Role: noncanonical draft bridge from the reconstructed SRT ontology direction to the GRG research programme.
>
> This file does not redefine canonical Selection, L0/L1/L2, One, Bearer or normativity. It states the minimum research bridge that should be pressured before GRG relation libraries, M-level transfer work or agentization are allowed to define the programme by accumulation.

## 0. Root question

The immediate question is:

> Why should the reconstructed SRT direction lead to a Generative Relational Grammar programme at all?

The bridge must be strong enough that GRG is not merely:

- a new label for cross-disciplinary analogy;
- a collection of recurring words;
- a knowledge graph of object-level facts;
- a transfer benchmark detached from SRT;
- a post-hoc unification surface.

The bridge is successful only if GRG can be shown to arise naturally from SRT's generative inversion while remaining revisable by source-native science and experience.

## 1. Starting inversion from SRT

The current programme reading begins from:

~~~text
object + fixed law -> explain change
~~~

as a powerful but downstream research format.

SRT asks for the more upstream generative account:

~~~text
non-flat generative differentiation
-> formation of determinate relations / boundaries / objects / positions
-> stabilized effective regularities
-> later consequence / history / reconstruction
~~~

The first bridge principle is therefore:

~~~text
generation is not inferred only after objects are given;
the formation of objects and effective relations is itself a research target.
~~~

This is the methodological opening through which GRG becomes possible.

## 2. Horizontal research

Horizontal research begins from a stabilized objectification.

Typical first-order givens may include:

- objects;
- variables;
- agents;
- particles;
- organisms;
- institutions;
- categories;
- state spaces;
- boundaries;
- scales;
- model classes.

It asks how those admitted units transform, interact and produce later states.

Canonical form:

~~~text
given objectification O*
+ state / relation at t
-> lawful transformation
-> state / relation at t+1
~~~

Horizontal research can be:

- causal;
- dynamic;
- generative;
- interventionist;
- mathematical;
- probabilistic;
- highly predictive.

Its defining feature is not weakness or stasis.

Its defining feature is that the relevant object space is sufficiently stabilized to function as the research floor.

Therefore:

~~~text
horizontal generativity
= generative prediction within an admitted objectification.
~~~

## 3. Vertical research

Vertical research shifts the primary target from transformations among already admitted objects to the generative organization that makes those objects, boundaries, scales and effective state spaces possible.

It asks:

~~~text
how is a determinate objectification generated?

what makes one difference operatively non-equivalent to another?

how is a boundary formed and maintained?

how does a formed organization become a condition on later generation?

how can later generation reconstruct the field in which future objects,
relations and options become available?
~~~

Vertical research therefore treats generativity itself as the main research object.

Working form:

~~~text
generative conditions / relations
-> formation / stabilization
-> objectifiable organization
-> later reconstruction of generative conditions
~~~

Vertical research seeks recurring generative dependencies across multiple objectifications rather than only laws inside one object space.

## 4. Vertical != deeper object layer

A central guard:

~~~text
vertical != microphysical
vertical != hidden variable
vertical != unobserved cause
vertical != more fundamental merely because smaller
~~~

Vertical depth is generative dependency depth.

A macro-organization may have vertical relevance when its formation and maintenance become constitutive conditions on later processes.

A micro-variable can remain horizontally treated when it is already admitted as a stable unit in a fixed model.

The vertical / horizontal distinction is therefore orthogonal to ordinary micro / macro scale.

## 5. The vertical-horizontal loop

The two research modes are continuous.

Working loop:

~~~text
V0: generative differentiation / organization
-> object / boundary / scale formation
-> H: effective horizontal laws and transformations
-> intervention / consequence / history
-> reconstruction of generative conditions
-> V1: revised generative organization
~~~

Compactly:

~~~text
V -> H -> V'
~~~

The point is not to replace horizontal science.

The point is to understand how:

1. horizontal objects become possible;
2. horizontal laws remain effective;
3. horizontal consequences accumulate;
4. those consequences can alter the conditions under which later objectification and generation occur.

## 6. From Selection to relationality

The programme bridge begins with Selection as non-flat generative differentiation.

Selection is not treated as an isolated object-event.

Actual differentiation carries relational structure because what becomes manifest / effective is inseparable from relative backgrounding, support, exclusion, accessibility and non-equivalence.

Therefore:

~~~text
Selection
-> relational differentiation
~~~

This does not mean every logical relation is a GRG relation.

The relation must have generative content.

## 7. From relationality to formation

Repeated or recursively organized differentiation may form relatively stabilized organization.

Working bridge:

~~~text
relational differentiation
-> formation
-> maintained / recurrent organization
~~~

Formation does not imply:

- a permanent substance;
- a subject;
- consciousness;
- a universal One theorem.

The minimum point is that the result of generation can itself become a structured condition rather than disappear as an isolated occurrence.

## 8. From formation to re-entry / reconstruction

The key vertical burden is not mere historical trace.

It is:

~~~text
formed organization
-> participates in later generation
~~~

or, more strongly:

~~~text
formed organization
-> changes later accessibility / constraint / differentiation /
   stabilization / transition conditions
~~~

This is the research bridge behind recurrence, re-entry and reconstruction.

However:

~~~text
measurable trace
!= formed organization automatically

formed organization
!= causal re-entry automatically

causal re-entry
!= universal vertical theorem automatically
~~~

Each step requires its own bridge and evidence where empirical claims are made.

## 9. From generative relations to grammar

One generative relation does not make a grammar.

A GRG candidate requires some combination of:

- recurrence;
- composability;
- typed dependency direction;
- constraints;
- boundary conditions;
- transformability;
- cross-scale compatibility;
- cross-object / cross-domain recurrence;
- failure conditions;
- capacity for revision.

The programme therefore moves from:

~~~text
generative relation
~~~

to:

~~~text
family of constrained generative relations
~~~

and only then toward:

~~~text
candidate generative relational grammar.
~~~

Grammar here does not mean a single universal mechanism.

It means a constrained organization of relations that can recur across different realizations while preserving source-native mechanism differences.

## 10. Domain science interface

The three layers must remain distinct:

~~~text
SRT ontology
!= GRG relation / grammar
!= domain-native mechanism
~~~

### SRT ontology

Provides the generative research orientation and current canonical constraints.

### GRG

Extracts and tests recurring generative dependency organizations.

### Domain science

Owns the concrete mechanisms, variables, measurements, evidence and local laws.

Therefore the preferred flow is:

~~~text
domain-native mechanism / result
-> objectification declaration
-> candidate generative burden
-> cross-domain comparison
-> GRG revision
~~~

not:

~~~text
SRT term
-> search world for matching examples.
~~~

## 11. Objectification as bridge, not error

Objectification is not treated as a mistake.

It is a necessary stabilization by which finite positions build shareable research objects, variables and laws.

The vertical question is:

> What does the objectification preserve, what does it suppress, and what generative organization makes it adequate for the current task?

This gives a two-way interface:

~~~text
world-side formation
-> objectification
-> horizontal knowledge

horizontal evidence / anomaly / intervention
-> pressure on objectification
-> revised vertical account
~~~

This interface is one route by which ontology and epistemology become mutually constraining without becoming identical.

## 12. RNN calibration under the bridge

The RNN decoder / spectral work is retained as a calibration of objectification and measurement.

The tested features are treated as L2-facing / objectified cross-sections of a realized system.

The bounded result remains:

~~~text
selected decoder / spectral trace
did not pay the frozen history-specific causal-mediation burden.
~~~

The bridge explicitly rejects:

~~~text
tested trace NULL
-> vertical generative organization has no causal efficacy.
~~~

Instead the result strengthens:

~~~text
trace / proxy / section
!= generative organization
unless an independent bridge is paid.
~~~

This correction does not reopen the stopped RNN spectral experiment family by parameter tuning.

It changes the theoretical interpretation of what that family could and could not test.

## 13. From grammar to generative expectation

If a generative relation is more than retrospective description, it constrains what counts as relation-consistent continuation or transformation.

Therefore:

~~~text
generative relation
-> structural generative expectation
~~~

This expectation is not necessarily:

- a statistical forecast;
- an explicit internal model;
- a conscious prediction.

It is a typed directional burden carried by the relation.

Where formed positions can embody or model it:

~~~text
B structural expectation
-> C0 embodied / enacted anticipation
-> C1 model-mediated anticipation
~~~

as already adjudicated.

## 14. From expectation to normativity

The current programme direction remains:

~~~text
typed generative relation
-> generative expectation
-> actual trajectory
-> indexed comparison
-> generative strengthening / weakening / substitution / hollowing / destruction
~~~

Normative indexing must declare:

- position;
- scale;
- horizon;
- relation;
- affected bearers where established;
- externalized consequences.

No direct inference is licensed from:

~~~text
stable -> good
persistent -> good
more options -> good
more order -> good
expected -> morally right
~~~

The foundational vertical question is stronger:

> How does present generation change the field of later possible generation?

That question is upstream of many later normative problems.

## 15. From GRG to reflexive inquiry

Science, philosophy, institutions and AI-assisted inquiry are themselves formed practices inside reality.

Therefore GRG must eventually include a reflexive learning loop:

~~~text
finite positioned inquiry
-> objectification
-> horizontal result
-> cross-domain comparison
-> generative relation candidate
-> expectation / intervention
-> consequence / anomaly
-> grammar revision
~~~

This is the route from a static GRG catalogue to a grammar-learning architecture.

## 16. Foundational research questions

Before further transfer escalation, the bridge leaves the following questions open:

1. What is the minimum generative relation that is stronger than trivial change?
2. When does formation become maintained organization?
3. What exact burden permits an organization to count as vertically re-entering later generation?
4. How should vertical efficacy be represented without turning it into a hidden object?
5. Which relation families are primitive research burdens and which are composites?
6. How should objectification mediate vertical-to-horizontal translation?
7. What form of generative expectation follows from a relation without importing teleology?
8. When does grammar become more than a list of relations?
9. Which existing G0-G11 burdens survive this bridge?
10. How can ontology and epistemology constrain one another without collapse?

## 19. Failure conditions for this bridge

The bridge should be revised if:

- vertical research cannot be distinguished from generic causality;
- every object formation can be described as a GRG relation without exclusion;
- horizontal science is caricatured as static or non-generative;
- verticality silently becomes microphysical reduction;
- ontology and epistemology are collapsed;
- GRG relations merely rename source-native mechanisms;
- the bridge requires a hidden vertical substance;
- empirical failure can always be escaped by invoking an inaccessible deeper level;
- no cross-object generative regularity can be stated without semantic drift.
