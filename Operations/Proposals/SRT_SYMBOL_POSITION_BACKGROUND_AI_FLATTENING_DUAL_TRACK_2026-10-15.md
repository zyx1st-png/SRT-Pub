## 1. Root question and a four-way separation

**M reconstruction of the author's A0-Q3 compression/decompression analogy** (machine-authored chain; **not** author wording or a canonical generative sequence):

```text
A: actual positioned/background-involved organization
   -- bounded extraction/objectification --> portable sign or trace S
S  -- transport under declared encoding/decoding rules --> received carrier S'
B: another history / position / task / environment
   + S' -- situated interpretation / participation --> new operative reconstruction R_B
R_B + other live conditions -- later operations --> possible further distinctions / access
```

Do not treat this as primitive Selection -> subject -> symbol -> receiver universal timeline. The chain is explicitly a **machine proposal**, not a five-step author commitment. The received symbol can support a genuinely new context-dependent reconstruction; it does not function as a guaranteed inverse of the sender's original relation. The two ends of a communication episode can be ordinary agents/users without proving formed SRT One, Stable ISP, Bearer or consciousness. Exact bit decoding and carrier fidelity may be lossless while meaning/application remain position-sensitive.

Four non-identical issues:

1. **Portable form:** signs / notation / words can retain public identity across different uses.
2. **Generative provenance:** the symbol is produced through a bounded distinction/objectification; a trace does not exhaust its origin.
3. **Positioned efficacy:** the same sign can recruit different organizing, judging and acting within different histories and contexts; identical effects are neither universal nor required.
4. **Nonmanifest/withheld pressure:** some omitted or backgrounded relations may still condition later processes, including when participants cannot name them. This is a **conditional research burden** rather than an axiom that every absence persists and acts.

An observation interface can itself hide background pressure: the researcher’s measurement cut and the system’s effective distinction are not automatically the same cut (see 2026-09-28 objectified-trace source). Avoid both "the true background is a stored list" and "every omitted variable is an ontological Selection".

## 3. Track T — AI-assisted-research flattening diagnostics (METHOD / ENGINEERING)

**Question:** under matched records and tasks, when does an AI collapse symbol/background/position into a self-contained description, miss relevant omissions, or import a generic background that changes a prospectively tested relation?

**An outcome-independent operational target** (fixed before seeing model/human performance): a claim is **method-flattened in a declared task** when (F1) the reported symbol/aggregate is held fixed, (F2) a domain-grounded background relation, positional role asymmetry or intervention condition is independently established as relevant, (F3) the explanation treats that relation as absent/interchangeable/irrelevant, and (F4) this causes a predeclared wrong inference or failure to preserve a discriminable prospective dependency. If F2 or F4 cannot be established, mark **INSUFFICIENT DATA**, not “vertical success/failure”. Successful redescription with additional state variables is **allowed**, and if it preserves the target relation, the local flattening allegation fails.

**Two distinct scored tasks:** (i) **SRT-local fidelity**: accurately reconstruct author/owner dependencies and flag unjustified promotions; useful for repository QA, never by itself evidence of an **AI-specific** deficit. (ii) **Domain-independent structural preservation**: blind, theory-neutral background/role perturbations scored against externally specified downstream consequences; this is the only candidate evidence for AI-vs-human method differences. A real **ontology** of O0 or Selection cannot be inferred from either score.

### Failure taxonomy (candidate, bounded)

| ID | Mechanism to detect | Observable test / contrary evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Symbol self-sufficiency: infer full meaning from S alone | Same S, different declared history/position; compare prospective judgments |
| T2 | Background erasure: omit excluded but still-effective constraints | Withhold then intervene on a specific background condition; check prediction/revision |
| T3 | Background reification: assume a pre-enumerated option warehouse | Test genuinely newly established relations vs reweighting known options |
| T4 | Positional substitution: generic cultural/model prior replaces actual situated role | Role/history swap with carrier fixed; require declared provenance of assumptions |
| T5 | SRT-local inversion / **fidelity only**: downstream Gate, history or symbol readout stated as primitive cause | Check against canonical owner for repository QA; **exclude from AI-specific flattening headline metric**. For non-SRT cases score pre-specified causal/role arrows independently |
| T6 | Missing-context anticipation failure (**MX-1**): no targeted anticipation of relevant absence | Measure useful missing-condition identification and probe choice, penalize empty caveats |
| T7 | Prospective structure loss (**MX-2**): predicts labels but misses context-induced relation change | Predeclared forward reclassification and intervention / re-entry tests; **not** a test of MX-3 standing |
| T8 | False success: identical wording/compressed embedding counted as same understanding | Cross-position task outcome and negotiated correction vs lexical agreement |

**Proposed *paper-only* bounded case families** (no experiment authorization; independent ground-truth specification before model/human review):

1. **SRT repository correction replay — fidelity-only:** previously published author corrections (e.g. static sediment vs active selectable space) tested as archival source-retrieval and dependency-arrow comprehension. Useful for agent QA, **not** an AI-specific ontological flattening result.
2. **External engineering/control domain:** two systems report an identical headline output (e.g. temperature) but differ in independently measurable heat capacity/actuator limits, leading to different response under a *predeclared intervention*. Standard state-space control models are valid rivals; retaining the hidden variable correctly is a **no-flattening** outcome.
3. **External social/economic domain:** the same public aggregate description (e.g. “risk is controlled”) indexes differently documented action roles/contractual exposures; an independent domain expert specifies a prospective decision or intervention distinction **before** responses. Test whether role substitution changes outcomes, not whether SRT terms are repeated.
4. **Same-token, different-position collaborative task:** role/history swap with carrier fixed and a declared consequential action; probe whether changed background is anticipated before disclosure or repaired after it.
5. **Background perturbation / new distinction:** a measured constraint is temporarily hidden, then manipulated and revealed. Separate reweighting an existing known variable from the empirically warranted formation of a new usable distinction; latent-state models must be granted capacity to discover it.
6. **Beacon/re-entry method illustration:** compare context-specific cue support, complete-note retrieval and strong semantic retrieval, marking copied records as **record copies** rather than the same historical event. This is a local method-use outcome, not evidence of One/Bearer.

**Controls:** at least one **capability-matched non-AI human researcher/domain expert group** as the comparison arm for any AI-specific claim, plus an AI-assisted-human arm where feasible to test collaboration separately; use competent history-aware AI/contextual/retrieval and standard domain modelling baselines. Match information accessible at judgment time, task instructions, time budget, experience/competence, model context size and source access; assess human resource/skill matching rather than assuming a fair comparison. Distinguish *human vs AI*, *tool-assisted vs unassisted*, and *SRT fidelity vs outside-domain structure retention*.

**Public-source leakage guard:** SRT-Pub source, PRs, reviews and historical corrections are publicly accessible. Randomly withholding corrections at evaluation time **does not establish uncontaminated model pretraining**. Flag archival replay **LEAKAGE-EXPOSED**; it is source-comprehension QA only. Any future comparative experiment would need prospective **new independently authored scenarios** with controlled information release and contamination checks, or report leakage risk as a major limit. No new private dataset is authorized here.

**Metrics, only if a later protocol is approved:** pre-register task-specific structural conditions **before** seeing responses; primary metrics: out-of-domain counterfactual/role-effect accuracy, useful omitted-condition anticipation (precision vs false alarms), prospective intervention discrimination and repair. Secondary SRT-local metrics: source/arrow fidelity and wrongful canonical promotion; do not mix these into the AI-specific score. Require blind domain-expert adjudication and explicit “no useful omission” negative controls. A long disclaimer, generic uncertainty or semantic similarity alone is not success.

**Strong alternative explanations**: pragmatics/semantic underdetermination, dynamic coarse-graining, contextual retrieval, latent-state and POMDP-style reasoning, action-dependent information, active learning, predictive processing and human-computer scaffolding. Do not construct artificially weak "traditional horizontal" baselines. No result permits "AI cannot reason vertically" as a universal claim; architectural state (inference vs memory/tool/training/embodied system) must be declared.

## 4. Track O — synchronous ontology/source reconstruction (ONTOLOGY QUESTIONS, NOT MODEL OUTPUTS)

**Question:** what is the exact SRT research burden for symbol formation, Selection-relative backgrounding, efficacy of an unmanifest side, and new positioned reconstitution?

Hold these cross-owner distinctions:

1. **Two senses of “压平” must be typed before comparison — OVERLOADED_SAME_NAME.** The [canonical L0 O0 owner](../../Core_Law/SRT_L0_Metaphysics.md) says generation cannot be fully made maximally neutral by freely interchanging its **generative role-asymmetry** (O0, **ontological anti-flattening**). The 2026-10-15 critique identifies a **methodological / representational flattening** risk in AI research. Their possible connection is a **M-level bridge hypothesis**: a representation that treats constitutive roles as interchangeable may fail to preserve a relevant generative relation. **Neither the shared word nor a failed prompt proves O0 or ontic irreducibility.** Strong dynamic models that preserve the roles are counterexamples to local methodological failure. Consult [2026-09-14 O0 pressure audit](../Audits/SRT_O0_POSITIVE_PRIMITIVE_GENERATIVITY_FINAL_PRESSURE_TEST_2026-09-14.md) for antecedent rather than promoting its analysis to independent canonical authority.
2. **O0/S0 are co-primitive analytic burdens**, not a prior container feeding a later pipeline. Actualizing Selection and relative backgrounding are same-event readings; no stable hidden bank of objects is presupposed.
3. **Two distinct pressure claims.** (a) **same-event constitutive non-neutrality / manifest–relative-background asymmetry** is not an extra stored object; (b) **later-effective retained pressure** requires a separate Spine §4 history/retention showing. A one-shot Selection remains genuine without later durable history. Distinguish both from an irrelevant omitted variable. The 2026-10-15 author use of “保存” has **not** chosen a universal version.
4. **Index vs formed position.** Finite-position-indexed actualisation is not a preformed subject. One/Selection-position requires separate recurrent reconstitution; Bearer, stable standing, agency and conscious anticipation remain separately gated.
5. **Carrier vs use.** A portable mathematical/linguistic form can be identical; its situated operative role may differ. Symbols are an objectified L2 research interface where appropriate, not the ontic origin of Selection. Do not infer that public math is in principle unable to represent position-dependent effects.
6. **Residual pressure without a pre-made menu.** Differentiate (a) still-operative but omitted constraint, (b) an unmanifest relative side, (c) an option once accessible but now suppressed, and (d) a genuinely new later differentiation **not** already present as a stored alternative. These are candidate distinctions, not four canonical entities.
7. **Typed expectation and architecture.** MX-2 must not equate forward-selectability / structural generative Expectation **B** with formed-position anticipation automatically. [Spine §6.3](../../Core_Law/SRT_Generative_Ontology_Spine.md) explicitly separates selection affecting later selectability, formed anticipation, P self-indexing and (with §8) E/Bearer. The [2026-09-20 adjudication](../../01_Source_Intuition/SRT_AUTHOR_ADJUDICATION_GENERATIVE_EXPECTATION_TYPING_2026-09-20.md) types A prediction, B structural direction, C0 enacted and C1 model-mediated formed anticipation; [2026-09-26](../../01_Source_Intuition/SRT_AUTHOR_ADJUDICATION_HPB_MINIMAL_PHENOMENAL_TOPOLOGY_EXPECTATION_GENERATIVITY_AI_BOUNDARY_2026-09-26.md) supplies the scoped MX-3 architecture-side author precedent. No standalone “canonical strong Expectation” variable is being created here; no consciousness is inferred.
8. **Publicness / non-substitutability.** What a participant actually does may be constitutive in a given domain; prove non-substitutability only for a named target under fair intervention and history-matched controls, not through the slogan "situatedness".

**On-paper counterpressure:** If two candidate background organizations generate the same available trace and future interventions, the inverse is underidentified: preserve both, do not claim one uniquely true hidden history. If a strong dynamic / situated rival explains all prospective tests, report *NO LOCAL COMPARATIVE INCREMENT* for that job without deleting independently owned SRT ontology questions. No consciousness/subject claims may be imported from semantic effects alone.

For each case record the **same** identifier but **separate** typed fields:

| Field | Track T | Track O |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input | carrier, task, accessible history, perturbation, disclosed participant role | source quotation, ontology owner, formation/retention/position scope |
| Proposed missing part | model-flagged contextual dependency + confidence/evidence status | background/foreground relation and the exact asserted burden |
| Outcome | precommitted behavior, intervention, repair and baseline comparison | explanatory direction, non-identities, OPEN/genuinely adjudicated status |
| Counterfactual | matched token / changed role or background, if feasible | whether the relation would survive legitimate re-objectification |
| Verdict | failure / benefit / no increment / insufficient data | compatible / tension / requires author / remains OPEN |
| Promotion | **never automatic** from benchmark success | author second adjudication + relevant owner gates, then independent review if C-class |

**Cadence concept (not a scheduled task):** each bounded author-led theoretical case receives T and O readings in the *same research pass*, then a joint **mismatch memo** stating whether the two readings support, merely illustrate or contradict one another. Neither track waits for the other to become canonical; neither track gets authority from the other’s apparent success.

**Stop / escalation:** **paper-only design cap = six candidate cases** (the six case families in §3), with **at most two passes of paper review** (initial design and one corrective independent review). If no domain-independent discriminating probe or fair human comparison can be specified by then, mark **DESIGN HOLD / NO AI-SPECIFIC INFERENCE**. No additional cases or executed experiment without a separate author/owner authorization. Missing source, unverifiable "pressure", benchmark leakage, or inability to manipulate a proposed determinant => `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE / HOLD`. Ontology claims that materially redefine Selection/One/Position, Gate or anticipation route to author second adjudication and existing Freeze-A C-review. Experiments, training, datasets and publication remain **not authorized by this note**.
