## 1. Root question and a four-way separation

The author's proposed chain (working view, **not** new canonical sequence):

```text
A: actual positioned/background-involved organization
   -- bounded extraction/objectification --> portable sign or trace S
S  -- transport under declared encoding/decoding rules --> received carrier S'
B: another history / position / task / environment
   + S' -- situated interpretation / participation --> new operative reconstruction R_B
R_B + other live conditions -- later operations --> possible further distinctions / access
```

Do not treat this as primitive Selection -> subject -> symbol -> receiver universal timeline. The two ends of a communication episode can be ordinary agents/users without proving formed SRT One, Stable ISP, Bearer or consciousness. Exact bit decoding and carrier fidelity may be lossless while meaning/application remain position-sensitive.

Four non-identical issues:

1. **Portable form:** signs / notation / words can retain public identity across different uses.
2. **Generative provenance:** the symbol is produced through a bounded distinction/objectification; a trace does not exhaust its origin.
3. **Positioned efficacy:** the same sign can recruit different organizing, judging and acting within different histories and contexts; identical effects are neither universal nor required.
4. **Nonmanifest/withheld pressure:** some omitted or backgrounded relations may still condition later processes, including when participants cannot name them. This is a **conditional research burden** rather than an axiom that every absence persists and acts.

An observation interface can itself hide background pressure: the researcher’s measurement cut and the system’s effective distinction are not automatically the same cut (see 2026-09-28 objectified-trace source). Avoid both "the true background is a stored list" and "every omitted variable is an ontological Selection".

## 2. Two readings of “AI 压平结构的缺失预期” (WORKING LABEL ONLY)

The [direct author sentence](../../01_Source_Intuition/SRT_AUTHOR_SOURCE_SYMBOL_POSITION_BACKGROUND_PRESSURE_AI_FLATTENING_2026-10-15.md) uses **缺失预期** but does not settle an English term or select a mechanism. Preserve at least two candidate readings without claiming author adjudication:

**ME-A — missing-background anticipation (AI epistemic-method role):** facing a highly compressed/symbol-only claim, does an AI anticipate that a *constitutively relevant context may be missing*, identify possible discriminators, request/seek a targeted missing condition, and limit its conclusion when no such evidence is available? Not merely "say it may be uncertain"; generic hedging without a useful prospective probe fails.

**ME-B — anticipation lost *through* flattening (structural/ontology research target):** does the abstracted model erase the difference between retained organization and possibilities not yet generated, such that it cannot prospectively track emergence, maintenance, revision, suppression, reopening or creation of positional relations? Do not equate this with a consciousness-bearing agent's subjective forecast.

Distinguish both from (i) Shannon channel errors, (ii) omitted-but-irrelevant variables, (iii) model confidence/calibration alone, (iv) simple retrieval failure, (v) observer bias, and (vi) the independently typed SRT Expectation/Gate/Bearer questions. One can pass ME-A and fail ME-B, or forecast a downstream outcome while bypassing the upstream explanatory target.

**Meaning decision still OPEN:** Before stabilizing a name or ontology role, return the two readings and any relation between them to the author. This package is allowed to propose tests **on paper** but cannot select the author's answer.

## 3. Track T — AI-assisted-research flattening diagnostics (METHOD / ENGINEERING)

**Question:** under matched records and tasks, when does an AI collapse symbol/backdrop/position into a self-contained description, miss relevant omissions, or import a generic background as if it were the author’s?

### Failure taxonomy (candidate, bounded)

| ID | Mechanism to detect | Observable test / contrary evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Symbol self-sufficiency: infer full meaning from S alone | Same S, different declared history/position; compare prospective judgments |
| T2 | Background erasure: omit excluded but still-effective constraints | Withhold then intervene on a specific background condition; check prediction/revision |
| T3 | Background reification: assume a pre-enumerated option warehouse | Test genuinely newly established relations vs reweighting known options |
| T4 | Positional substitution: generic cultural/model prior replaces actual situated role | Role/history swap with carrier fixed; require declared provenance of assumptions |
| T5 | Inversion: downstream Gate, history or symbol readout becomes primitive cause | Dependency-arrow audit against current canonical owner, with event/persistence split |
| T6 | Missing-expectation failure (ME-A): no targeted anticipation of relevant absence | Measure useful missing-condition identification and probe choice, penalize empty caveats |
| T7 | Prospective collapse (ME-B): predicts labels but cannot predict repair/re-cut | Predeclared forward reclassification and intervention / re-entry tests |
| T8 | False success: identical wording/compressed embedding counted as same understanding | Cross-position task outcome and negotiated correction vs lexical agreement |

**Proposed *paper-only* case families** (not authorized experiment runs):
- **Repository correction replay:** blinded prompts taken from previous human corrections (e.g. static deposit vs active selectable-space, symbol vs positioned efficacy), with later corrections held out; compare unguarded / canonical-only / context-routed / cross-position-check models.
- **Same-token, different-position case:** identical symbol in two documented different task/historical settings, with prospectively declared consequences; control for language proficiency, accessible history and actual instructions.
- **Background perturbation case:** visible carrier held constant, concealed-but-observable contextual condition changed and later revealed; evaluate whether the model predicts a *specific* possible discrepancy and revises rather than retrofitting it.
- **Re-entry/collaboration case:** Beacon/ChoiceMap-like cues trigger position-dependent useful reconstruction, compared with matched full-note/retrieval baselines; `recover_beacon()` record-copy is never called original Selection recovery.

**Control and metrics plan**: fix task, accessible evidence, time budget, model family/context size, prompt length, training leakage and baseline capacity. Pre-register labels and future probes *before* showing the held-out case; use blinded human annotation of actual outcomes where possible. Candidate measures: targeted omission-detection precision/recall, unsupported ontological promotions, directional-inversion count, prospective re-cut accuracy, cross-position utility/negative transfer, and repair after a contradiction. Verbosity or frequency of caveats is **not** success.

**Strong alternative explanations**: pragmatics/semantic underdetermination, dynamic coarse-graining, contextual retrieval, latent-state and POMDP-style reasoning, action-dependent information, active learning, predictive processing and human-computer scaffolding. Do not construct artificially weak "traditional horizontal" baselines. No result permits "AI cannot reason vertically" as a universal claim; architectural state (inference vs memory/tool/training/embodied system) must be declared.

## 4. Track O — synchronous ontology/source reconstruction (ONTOLOGY QUESTIONS, NOT MODEL OUTPUTS)

**Question:** what is the exact SRT research burden for symbol formation, Selection-relative backgrounding, efficacy of an unmanifest side, and new positioned reconstitution?

Hold these cross-owner distinctions:

1. **O0/S0 are co-primitive analytic burdens**, not a prior container feeding a later pipeline. Actualizing Selection and relative backgrounding are same-event readings; no stable hidden bank of objects is presupposed.
2. **Event-level backgrounding is not automatically retained pressure.** A one-shot Selection remains genuine with no later durable history. Ask what makes a particular omitted relation *effective for a later outcome*; separate constitutive dependence now from material retention later.
3. **Index vs formed position.** Finite-position-indexed actualisation is not a preformed subject. One/Selection-position requires separate recurrent reconstitution; Bearer, stable standing, agency and conscious anticipation remain separately gated.
4. **Carrier vs use.** A portable mathematical/linguistic form can be identical; its situated operative role may differ. Symbols are an objectified L2 research interface where appropriate, not the ontic origin of Selection. Do not infer that public math is in principle unable to represent position-dependent effects.
5. **Residual pressure without a pre-made menu.** Differentiate (a) still-operative but omitted constraint, (b) an unmanifest relative side, (c) an option once accessible but now suppressed, and (d) a genuinely new later differentiation **not** already present as a stored alternative. These are candidate distinctions, not four canonical entities.
6. **Anticipation without smuggling a subject.** ME-B must not equate forward-selectability / orientation with conscious expectation, Bayesian prediction, a scalar fitness target, or canonical strong Expectation by name alone. The relevant owner may leave stronger sufficiency OPEN.
7. **Publicness / non-substitutability.** What a participant actually does may be constitutive in a given domain; prove non-substitutability only for a named target under fair intervention and history-matched controls, not through the slogan "situatedness".

**On-paper counterpressure:** If two candidate background organizations generate the same available trace and future interventions, the inverse is underidentified: preserve both, do not claim one uniquely true hidden history. If a strong dynamic / situated rival explains all prospective tests, report *NO LOCAL COMPARATIVE INCREMENT* for that job without deleting independently owned SRT ontology questions. No consciousness/subject claims may be imported from semantic effects alone.

Neither track waits for the other to become canonical; neither track gets authority from the other’s apparent success.

**Stop / escalation:** stop after a small predeclared set of existing cases if no prospective discriminating question is found; do not multiply case studies until a unique mechanism appears.
