# Revised Selection-position observability author gate — foreground-bounded SPD-B*

> **Purpose:** refine the previous SPD gate after the author said they lean toward B specifically because the SRT observable is the foreground side of foreground/background Selection.
>
> **Important:** `倾向于 B` is recorded as a provisional leaning, not final adjudication.

## 1. What is no longer the live B

Do not ask the author to endorse this crude statement:

```text
A genuine Selection-position means no external observer can ever reconstruct
complete dynamics.
```

That confounds SRT with ordinary partial observability and is too strong.

## 2. Revised SPD-B* — foreground-bounded ontological observability

```text
A Selection event is a foreground/background differentiation
within a pre-object relational field.

The directly observable SRT surface is the foreground / manifest side.
The relative background and full pre-object field are not co-manifest
as the same observation.

To make a previously backgrounded relation manifest requires a further
Selection / foregrounding event; this may provide evidence about the prior
background but is not a neutral same-event revelation of it.
```

This permits:

```text
later reconstruction;
latent-state modeling;
interventional inference;
predictive success;
```

without equating those with direct co-manifest access to the original pre-object field.

## 3. Stronger optional clause that remains open

A still stronger claim would be:

```text
No complete third-person state-space model can be ontologically equivalent
to the pre-object field + Selection relation.
```

This has **not** been earned.

If the author wants SPD-B*, the safe current commitment should stop before this theorem unless explicitly intended.

## 4. Remaining reduction challenge

A strongest rival may write:

```text
Z_t = (M_t, L_t, H_t, ...)
Z_(t+1) = F(Z_t, U_t)
```

and claim that manifest/background relations are fully represented inside an enlarged dynamical state.

The future burden is therefore not practical observability but:

```text
Does the enlarged state model preserve or eliminate the fact that
foreground/background status is constituted by Selection itself?
```

If it preserves all relevant dependency and explanatory work, SRT Position may be framework-redundant at this level.

If not, the missing structure must be stated source-neutrally.

## 5. Revised options

### SPD-A

```text
Third-person dynamics may be complete;
Selection-position adds an ontology-level typing / unification only.
```

### SPD-B* — current provisional author leaning

```text
Observation is Selection-bounded:
what becomes observable is the foreground / manifest side;
the background / pre-object field is not co-manifest in the same event.

This is an ontological observation constraint,
not merely a practical sensor limitation.
```

### SPD-C

```text
Selection-position and foreground/background structure are fully eliminable
into ordinary augmented state-space dynamics with no explanatory loss.
```

## 6. Author-facing short form

> 你现在倾向的其实不是旧版 B——“外部永远测不全”。更接近的是 **SPD-B***：Selection 本身就是前景/背景化，因此一次 Selection 真正显现给观察的只是前景侧；背景和前对象场不是同一事件里同时显现的对象。以后可以通过新的观察、干预、建模去反推它们，但这些已经是新的 Selection / 新的前景化，不等于站在 Selection 之外把原来的前对象场一次性完整摊开。
>
> 这比“hidden state 看不到”强，但还没有自动证明“任何第三人称动力学模型都不可能完整”。后一个命题仍需单独支付。
