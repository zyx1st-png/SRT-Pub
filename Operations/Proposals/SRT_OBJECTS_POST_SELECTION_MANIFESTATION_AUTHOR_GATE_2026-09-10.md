# Author gate — how strong is the post-Selection manifestation thesis?

The author proposed:

> **「所有对象都是选择后显现的部分，SRT 想把这种显现不当成默认，获得显现之前和 L0 和选择作为显现的要素的视野」**

Pass 14 found the proposition coherent and potentially core after guarding against epistemic triviality, idealism, hidden-substrate regress and one-mechanism universalism.

The unresolved author question is **scope**, not whether ordinary stable objects are real.

---

## MOBJ-A — level-typed universal objecthood thesis **[machine recommendation]**

```text
Anything that counts as a determinate object at a given explanatory level
is already a post-Selection manifest / foreground achievement at that level.

SRT therefore does not take manifestation / objecthood as the default floor.
It keeps explicit:

pre-object condition (L0)
-> Selection / constitutive differentiation
-> manifest foreground / objectifiable side
-> later anchoring / persistence where applicable.
```

Guards:

```text
not one identical local mechanism across all domains;
not human-observer creation;
not denial of object reality;
not claim that L0 or Selection are hidden objects;
not yet PO-3 / no-state-space-equivalence theorem.
```

Why recommended:

- preserves the author's “all objects” intuition;
- aligns with canonical `any determinate manifest actuality is produced by Selection` without silently universalizing beyond objecthood;
- keeps the strongest theoretical gain: manifestation becomes explanandum rather than default;
- remains compatible with domain pluralism and existing negative mechanism audits.

---

## MOBJ-B — universal determinacy thesis

```text
Every determinate state, event, relation, property, boundary or object whatsoever
is post-Selection manifestation;
there is no determinate entity-level content prior to Selection.
```

This is stronger than the author's literal object formulation and stronger than needed for the current retrospective.

Potential gain:

```text
maximally general Selection-first ontology.
```

Risk:

```text
scope explosion;
self-reference pressure;
possible conflict over what minimal determinate structure L0 itself is allowed to have;
forces a much larger canonical/metaphysical audit.
```

Machine recommendation: **do not adopt yet** unless the author intends to reopen that stronger universal quantification explicitly.

---

## MOBJ-C — epistemic manifestation thesis only

```text
Objects may be ontologically complete before Selection;
SRT only says observers encounter them through a selective foreground interface.
```

This is much weaker and loses the current constitutive direction.

It would largely collapse toward:

```text
perspectival access / phenomenological appearance / measurement interface.
```

Machine recommendation: **not preferred** under current author direction.

---

## Separate issue deliberately not bundled into this gate

Whichever option is chosen, the following remains separately open:

```text
PO-3:
Can an arbitrarily rich third-person state-space be ontologically equivalent
to the pre-object + Selection architecture?
```

Do not infer:

```text
MOBJ-A -> PO-3 proved
or
MOBJ-B -> empirical unpredictability.
```

The observability / state-space line requires its own fuller strongest-neighbor pass.
