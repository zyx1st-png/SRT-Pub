# Bounded method note — position-involved stability and reusable models (2026-10-10)

> **Status:** M-level analytical synthesis of [author source trace](../../01_Source_Intuition/SRT_AUTHOR_TRACE_VERTICAL_SELECTION_PARTICIPATION_UNIFICATION_2026-10-10.md). Not a new CURRENT NEXT, not an experiment authorization, not a foundational GRG version, not a universal equation, and not proof of an SRT-only empirical signature.
>
> **Role:** retain the dialogue's practical implications *without reducing its goal to formalization*. The goal is cross-context / cross-position **stable usable organization**; the author's later explicit ruling is **“不是反对数学化，而是数学化只是L2层面的脚手架，不是本体”**. Mathematics remains available as an L2 scaffold, not an ontic ground.

## 1. Main contrast — unification of what?

Do NOT silently replace the author's A0-Q7 with “all positions should share a formalization.” Three distinct targets:

| Target | What must be same? | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Universal formula | One variable vocabulary, equation, or scalar across all positions | NOT REQUIRED by this author direction; existence not prohibited |
| Identical content | Same Beacon keywords, identical private meanings, or literal duplication of previous actual Selection | NOT REQUIRED and may distort the phenomenon |
| Cross-position effective organization | Re-enterable, revisable, jointly usable relations that keep prospective force as participants and scenes change | **AUTHOR-SELECTED RESEARCH AIM**; mechanism and evidence OPEN |

“Uniformity” here must not mean all participants become one bearer or one formed One. Functional collaboration, stable coupling, process-unity, and stronger ontological standing need separate admission checks.

## 2. Two explanatory regimes, without a caricature of mature science

**Horizontal/objectified regime:** work with already stated units, variables, histories, observations, mappings or equivalences; normalize or coarse-grain for prediction, compression, intervention. This can involve fully dynamic and history-sensitive systems.

**Vertical question of SRT:** how does Selection actualize a coupled manifest / relative-background organization? Under which conditions do earlier vertical differentiations constrain later ones; when does new Selection institute relations not already constituted in the previous formed organization; what makes boundaries, objects and re-entry effective?

The former is not false or inherently static. The latter is not licensed to assert that a sufficiently rich formal domain model cannot represent relevant dynamics. The point is the *explanatory burden*, not a claim of mathematical inexpressibility.

### Three analytic roles (M-level), not three interchangeable entities

1. Primitive actualized Selection and event-level foreground/background verticality. No prior pre-formed chooser, global menu, objective fitness or formed Gate required.
2. **Coarse-graining in the author's selected vertical sense:** the later explicit A0-S9 chooses **coarse-graining as a generative manifestation of Selection** in the posed comparison, rather than treating it *only* as an already-formed organization bearing effects of Selection. Thus the research must examine how the foreground/background differentiating process operates, not merely track an already formed Gate or equivalence class. This is a selected conceptual direction, not an established domain mechanism or the global equality “all coarse-graining = all primitive Selection”.
3. **Formed organizations where separately evidenced:** retained Gate (distinct from primitive Selection), history-sensitive differential reach, anticipation and domain-specific coarse-graining *implementations* may subsequently influence Selection. Their persistence is not required for a one-shot Selection. These later roles do not exhaust or redefine the author-selected generative sense of “粗粒化”.
4. **L2 mathematical/representational scaffolds** (e.g. local landscape, similarity function, fitness, category, control model): fallible, revisable models of a declared scope, permitted and potentially useful, but **not ontology** (explicit A0-Q9). They can be used in position-involved intervention and may change later conditions. Their representational powers and losses require task-specific demonstrations.

Use arrows of explanatory dependency, not a fabricated O0 timeline or a universal dynamical equation.

## 3. “Background to foreground” and newly generated differences

Do not model a background as a static complementary set of unchosen objects. A previous background side is Selection-relative, not a global repository.

Differentiate:

| Case | What changes? | What can be tested? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reattention / local reweighting | A previously usable distinction becomes salient within a largely retained organization | Same learned dimension set, changed salience or attention |
| Formed-gate revision | Existing historical organization / reachable relations change | Persistent shift of conditional action, expectation, relation-specific perturbation |
| New relational differentiation | New Selection institutes foreground/background relation not already constituted by the former formed organization | New jointly operative relational organization and its downstream consequence, not merely a post-hoc label |

These are *M analytic cases*. None establishes ontological novelty from behavioral novelty alone; latent-structure learners and adaptive dynamics can implement the same observable changes.

## 4. Non-public participation and bounded public evidence

The author raises a real epistemic tension: a public symbol for position does not equal the position's actual participation.

Do not infer that a situated episode is empirically unstudied, private-conscious, or immunized against testing. Preserve a distinction between:

- **Occurrence:** each actual selection encounter is position-involved and cannot be numerically replayed as the *same* original event merely by copying a record.
- **Trace / consequence:** observable actions, reports where available, history, interventions, re-entry and further conditions can be shared and checked.
- **Re-enactability:** a different participant follows declared conditions and can produce an appropriately comparable organization without being forced to have the original experience or vocabulary.

This proposes a methodological bridge, not a theorem that all SRT selections are fundamentally third-person unreconstructible. The stronger SPD-B* observability line remains undecided.

## 5. Beacon as an example of situated re-entry, NOT identity-preserving memory recovery

Author source: Beacon is highly personal and scene-dependent (A0-Q7). The user's earlier personal account of keyword/tag cues was given in prior conversation, but is **not established here as an independently inspectable `main`-branch Beacon source**. Those details below are M-level reconstruction of the user's reported technique, not source-verified details from a separate repository document.

Possible operational readings:

- A Beacon is a re-entry cue/scaffold in a historically participating user, not a self-sufficient compressed archive from which the original occurrence can be perfectly reconstructed.
- Re-entry in a later scene is a **new event**. Useful reconstruction may vary with current history and context; exact lexical identity or exact original-thought reproduction is not the target.
- A cue can fail; other people's identical cues may have different effects; successful re-entry does not independently prove primitive Selection or vertical metaphysics.
- Cross-user coherence can emerge from interaction, negotiated use and consequential coordination **without** equating private semantics or claiming a new collective One.
- A proposed “semantic energy map” is a *possible interface/metaphor*, not an admitted primitive scalar, universal manifold or target of obligatory convergence.

## 6. A useful local model: non-universal contract

Any formula/model used in this research should declare at least:

1. **Position and role:** who participates, how and from what bounded historical interaction. Distinguish mere field location/agent index from independently established formed Selection-position.
2. **Purpose and perspective:** what is to be predicted, supported, corrected or regenerated.
3. **Objectification contract:** unit, boundary, grain, normalization, equivalence, lost/retained history, position loss, substitutions and consequences.
4. **Participation contract:** whether changing who acts, intervenes, responds or bears consequences would alter the phenomenon; what substitutes are legitimate.
5. **Success and failure:** predeclared future probes, controls, rivals, limits, and conditions under which it must be revised or retired.
6. **Re-entry and transfer:** what survives across context/position change, what must be renegotiated, and what genuinely failed to transfer.

This is a local **L2 scaffold-use contract**, not an L0 law, not a proposed GRG global schema, and not an instruction to privilege formalization over the author's cross-position stability objective.

## 7. PARKED downstream thought probes — NOT the existing CN-2 task family A

The following are possible **future, separate** method design ideas; they do **not** belong to CN-2 E2 task family A and are not currently an authorized study, paper task selection, research lane, charter, or preregistration. CN-2 remains the sole existing CURRENT NEXT, with its paper-only scope unchanged. No experiments may start from this section. For thought/design only:

- **Within-position re-entry:** compare Beacon cue to full-note retrieval and semantic retrieval. Pre-register useful reconstruction/decision quality and scope, not verbatim memory recovery.
- **Position-substitution test:** compare active participant history vs matched sensory/recorded traces. If effects differ, test whether action-contingent information, hidden state and motivation explain them.
- **Cross-scene transfer:** test if scaffold preserves ability to form *new* useful relations despite changed lexical contents/categories; quantify both benefit and negative transfer.
- **Cross-position collaboration:** test whether an explicit coordination protocol improves prospective downstream coordination without forcing identical conceptual labels or semantic vector spaces.
- **Prospective common burden:** predeclare what one claimed vertical-organization mechanism predicts jointly across classification, expectation, action and later recutting, not separately re-fit each outcome.

Baseline rivals should include history-aware active learning, latent-state/POMDP and representation learning, plus strong retrieval and memory-scaffold systems. A “position variable” alone is neither proof nor disproof of constitutive participation. Fair match data, model capacity, interactions, training budgets, held-out probes and explanatory targets. Every result can be local no-gain.

## 8. Failure rights and no new CURRENT NEXT

- If ordinary retrieval, retrieval augmented generation, human-computer interaction or latent learning gives equal prospective utility, mark **NO LOCAL COMPARATIVE INCREMENT** for that tested function; do not claim SRT-specific support.
- If there is no reliable position/One evidence, report field-indexed dynamics without upgrading to formed Selection-position or bearer.
- If cross-position convergence requires forced semantic homogenization, the author's proposed unity criterion fails for that implementation; do not redefine every discrepancy as success.
- If the system only collects many different examples but no reusable future constraints, it fails the intended *stability* objective, regardless of model elegance.
- If prospective transfer does not generalize beyond the training prompts/contexts, the “cross-position/cross-scene” aim remains unearned.
- Keep GRG case2 stop-loss, broad synthesis/deep-well HOLD, CN-2 E2 paper-only first task family, E3/E4 held, and independent author-reentry/FR-ADV gates exactly as owned in merged STATUS.md.
