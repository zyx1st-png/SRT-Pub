# Post-Rebase Research Topic Discovery Protocol — 2026-08-28

> **Boundary:** non-canonical research-program governance. This protocol selects neither a paper nor a theory result. It exists to prevent venue-first novelty construction after the rebase.

---

## 0. Activation and manuscript carve-out

Use this protocol for **new research-topic selection**.

Do not use it to reopen or reframe manuscripts already in editorial handling unless the journal requests a revision.

Current carve-outs include:

- Frontiers 1837760: already published;
- *Costly Selective Closure* / *Adaptive Behavior*: already submitted; no opportunistic experiment/framing expansion during editorial handling.

---

## 1. Required sequence

A new paper candidate must move through this order:

```text
landed / owner-routed SRT claim
-> owner subtraction
-> existing comparison-owner subtraction
-> external role-matched subtraction
-> derivability test
-> scientific D2 gate OR bounded explanatory/formal-surplus gate
-> manuscript contribution freeze
-> venue fit
```

Venue fit is last.

A candidate that fails an earlier hard gate cannot be rescued by strength on later dimensions.

---

## 2. Gate 0 — owner subtraction

For each candidate claim `C` answer:

```text
Who already owns C inside SRT?
What part is already canonical / P1 / P2 / bridge / P4 / P5?
What exact residual remains after subtraction?
```

Reject as a new topic if the proposed contribution is only:

- a rename of an existing owner;
- a bundle of already-owned claims without a new dependency or test;
- a bridge file promoted into definition authority;
- a source-intuition sentence restated as theorem.

Open PRs do not count as landed owner changes.

---

## 3. Gate 1 — repository comparison-owner subtraction

Before external search, route through the existing comparison family.

Primary index:

```text
Bridge/SRT_Adjacent_Theory_Interface_Index.md
```

Then use the relevant dedicated comparison files and bounded retrieval protocol.

Do not construct a second independent comparative matrix when an existing owner can receive the needed dependency-graph axis.

A candidate fails this gate if the supposed novelty is already explicitly recorded in SRT as neighboring-theory convergence / translation / pressure.

---

## 4. Gate 2 — external novelty subtraction

Novelty is evaluated at the **dependency / role structure** level, not by vocabulary.

Examples of unsafe novelty claims already blocked in the current workline:

```text
many become one
individuals are generated rather than primitive
relations / boundaries can be generated
matter / law as habit
self-maintaining autonomy creates perspective
interoception indexes content to an organism
object / subject / phenomenal unity are different notions
memory continuity fails to settle identity under fission
```

For each candidate record:

```text
strongest neighboring theory
same role?
same dependency order?
same explanatory burden?
same empirical consequence?
```

Terminal novelty statuses:

```text
N0 role-isomorphic / established prior art
N1 framing or decomposition increment
N2 dependency-architecture residual
N3 strong candidate requiring deeper audit
```

`N0` is a hard stop for originality claims. It may still support a review/synthesis article if contribution framing is explicitly non-original.

---

## 5. Gate 3 — background fairness / rival freeze

Before a discrimination claim, freeze a strong role-matched rival.

The rival must receive any background resource that ordinary theory is reasonably entitled to use, including where relevant:

```text
persistent latent state
history dependence
consequence return
adaptive updating
viability / preferred-state regulation
ordinary hysteresis
current-state expansion
flexible latent-history representation
```

Do not score a premise as SRT surplus merely because it was withheld from the rival.

This inherits the background-fairness discipline established in prior GOV-SUB01 / X1-style audits.

---

## 6. Gate 4 — derivability / theory ownership

A proposed paper contribution must be traceable to **landed SRT**, not only to a new paper-specific construct.

Classify:

### DERV-0 — not derived

The paper adds a new premise that carries the result. Do not market the result as an SRT prediction/consequence.

### DERV-1 — weak interpretation

The result is compatible with SRT but multiple architectures obtain it. Suitable only for synthesis / perspective if useful.

### DERV-2 — bounded SRT consequence

The residual follows from a declared SRT owner package under explicit bridge assumptions.

### DERV-3 — strong internal consequence

The result follows from a hardened owner dependency with little paper-specific surplus.

`DERV-0` is a hard stop for an “SRT predicts/explains uniquely” paper.

---

## 7. Gate 5A — scientific discriminator route

Do **not** restate the D2 checklist here. `Core/SRT_Core_24_Discriminating_Predictions.md §0` is controlling and must be read directly at execution time.

In addition, this protocol adds one strong-rival grant rule:

> If the target concerns history, memory, consequence return, autonomy, learning or persistent identity, a flexible latent-history / recurrent-state rival must be included unless a written reason shows it is role-mismatched.

A residual history coefficient is not automatically SRT-specific.

### Progressive / degenerative rule

Also inherit Core 24's progressive-vs-degenerative discipline directly. A post-result repair that only makes the claim vaguer or harder to refute is not progress.

### Specification-stage NO-GO

A workline can and should terminate before data collection if no prospectively different outcome can be frozen under fair rival grants.

Terminal scientific statuses:

```text
D2-ready candidate
D2-negative / rival-equivalent
spec-stage NO-GO
calibration-only
```

---

## 8. Gate 5B — non-D2 theoretical / formal route

Not every publishable paper needs to be an empirical D2 paper.

A philosophy, theoretical-biology, metatheory, formal or perspective candidate can survive without D2 only if it supplies a bounded contribution such as:

- a non-trivial dependency theorem or impossibility result;
- a formal distinction that changes model construction or admissible inference;
- a new comparative architecture that resolves a live literature mismatch;
- a well-scoped explanatory decomposition that cannot be reduced to a literature taxonomy;
- a negative result / no-go theorem with clear consequences;
- a methodological audit that changes how a field tests a construct.

It must still pass novelty and derivability gates.

Terminal non-D2 statuses:

```text
THEORY-A: strong bounded theoretical surplus
THEORY-B: useful synthesis / perspective contribution
THEORY-C: translation / exposition only
NO-GO
```

---

## 9. No additive topic score

The previous 21-point additive score is withdrawn.

Reason:

```text
Novelty = 0
```

must not be arithmetically rescued by high tractability, venue fit or importance.

Use **lexicographic gates**, not an additive score.

Minimum order:

```text
novelty
> derivability
> fairness / discrimination or theoretical surplus
> tractability
> importance
> venue fit
```

A hard failure upstream dominates all downstream strengths.

---

## 10. Candidate record template

Every new topic card should contain:

```text
Candidate ID / title

A. SRT owner
B. claim level / maturity
C. residual after internal owner subtraction
D. existing SRT comparison owner
E. strongest external neighbors
F. novelty status N0-N3
G. derivability status DERV-0..3
H. rival family and free grants, if scientific
I. O_SRT / O_R or theoretical surplus statement
J. symmetric weakening / NO-GO condition
K. progressive-repair boundary
L. tractability / data / formal feasibility
M. best-fit manuscript type
N. venue only after A-M
```

---

## 11. Special rule: history / bearer candidates

The current bearer workline is the calibration case for this protocol.

Do not use:

```text
matched current observable state
+ different history
-> different future
```

as sufficient novelty.

A fair rival may encode history in latent state.

For bearer/non-outsourcing, grant the rival:

```text
persistent state
latent history
consequence-sensitive learning
viability regulation
history-dependent policy
ordinary self-maintenance
```

Then ask only what `non-outsourcing / consequence ownership` adds beyond those grants.

If nothing prospective or classificatorily non-redundant remains:

```text
Outcome C / translation
```

is the correct result.

---

## 12. Special rule: consciousness / neural-model candidates

Do not claim neural theories simply presuppose individuation.

IIT explicitly addresses complex/border/grain selection; GNWT, bodily-self, Damasio and enactivist approaches operationalize other forms of unity.

A safe research question is:

> When a theory identifies a whole, what relation makes that whole one, and is that relation interchangeable with the unity targeted by another theory?

This is a **candidate framing**, not yet an originality verdict.

Any `bearer whole` originality claim remains contingent on the bearer subtraction gate.

---

## 13. Topic dispositions

After all hard gates, use one of these dispositions.

### PAPER-A

Proceed to manuscript planning because novelty + derivability + either D2 readiness or strong theoretical surplus survive.

### PAPER-B

Useful bounded synthesis / perspective; contribution is mainly organization, comparison or method rather than a new SRT mechanism.

### PAPER-C

Keep as internal bridge / exposition / book material; publication value weak or redundant.

### NO-GO

Do not spend a manuscript workline on it.

No disposition can override a hard-gate failure.

---

## 14. Venue gate

Only after contribution disposition:

- consciousness / neuroscience collection;
- theoretical biology / autonomy;
- philosophy of science / metaphysics;
- complex systems;
- AI / machine consciousness;
- methodology / model comparison;
- other venue as appropriate.

A call deadline is a scheduling fact, not evidence of novelty.

---

## 15. Programme success criterion

This protocol succeeds even if the best result is:

```text
no paper yet
```

provided it prevents a role-isomorphic or unfairly constructed claim from being submitted as novelty.

The intended output of the rebase is a **ranked set of genuinely residual research questions**, not a quota of manuscripts.
