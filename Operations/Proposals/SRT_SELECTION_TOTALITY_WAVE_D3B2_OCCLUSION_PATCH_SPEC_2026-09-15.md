# Part D — staged lock-in and recovery

## 9. A/B dynamics

Retain active-vs-locked narrowing as model architecture, but remove universal one-way claims.

Allowed:

```text
repeated suppression/writeback may, in a declared model,
increase future access cost or reduce correction capacity;
this can create hysteresis or lock-in.
```

Not allowed universally:

```text
each act of not-looking necessarily erodes seeing capacity;
A -> B is structurally one-way/easy;
B cannot internally return;
B is absorbing merely because d < d_c.
```

Any hysteresis, irreversible degradation or unilateral-return impossibility requires declared dynamics and evidence under D3-B1.

---

## 10. Intervention windows

Retain the taxonomy as a P2/P3 recovery-model candidate.

Possible window types may include:

```text
cost/payability change;
relational perturbation/support;
embodied/physiological change;
collective/narrative environment change.
```

Do not state these are exhaustive or universally necessary.

A window means model-relative reduction in lock-in / increased access to correction/reorientation channels, not automatic contact with a primitive direction.

---

## 11. Triggers

Retain triggers as P2/P3 model hypotheses, but retype:


Natural, artistic, contemplative or extreme experiences may remain as domain examples of perturbation/reorientation, not metaphysical evidence of epsilon-field access.
