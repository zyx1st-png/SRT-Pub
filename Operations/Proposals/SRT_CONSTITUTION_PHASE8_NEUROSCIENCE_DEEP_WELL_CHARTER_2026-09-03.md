# Phase 8 Neuroscience deep-well charter

## Matched present neural state / different relational history

> **Authorization**: the author selected Neuroscience as the first deep well on 2026-09-03。
>
> **Current state**: the study charter is active；the protocol is not locked，preregistered or executed。Evidence access and the strongest-baseline review remain open gates。
>
> **Single-well rule**: this charter opens one neuroscience question only。It does not open Consciousness，Epistemology，Religion & Spirituality or a general neuroscience-rewrite programme。

---

## 0. Exact study question

Can two within-subject neural relation candidates be closely matched on currently observed component state，current input，pre-existing relation strength and major common-driver variables，yet differ in a later relation-specific coordinated response because they underwent different prior joint，consequence-bearing histories？

Compact test-local form：

```text
same declared current-state class
+ same probe / cue class
+ matched PRE relation and component exposure
+ different relation-specific history H
-> different future relation identity / probability
```

Primary statistical comparison：

```text
Base model:
R_future ~ X + E + C + G + PRE_relation + anatomy + tau

History model:
R_future ~ X + E + C + G + PRE_relation + anatomy + tau + H
```

The target is an out-of-sample，relation-identity increment from `H`。The claim is not “history matters”，which is already ordinary neuroscience。

---

## 1. Domain-template pass

### 1.0 Who is in the phenomenon？

- a human participant undergoing clinically indicated intracranial monitoring is the preferred study system；
- the participant learns，retrieves，acts and bears the behavioral consequences of the task；
- recorded cells，ensembles and regional relations are measurement objects，not automatically subjects or bearers；
- investigators choose the task，cuts，matching rules and models；
- clinical teams determine electrode placement independently of the research question。

No consciousness，phenomenality，agency，responsibility or suffering attribution is required for the neural result。

### 1.1 Given One

The study-local Given One is：

> a present neural description—current component activity，excitability proxies，phase，context and fixed anatomy—is treated as the sufficient public object for predicting the next coordinated relation。

This is a deliberately bounded state-sufficiency projection，not the claim that neuroscience as a whole ignores history。Engram，plasticity，metaplasticity，replay，latent-state and systems-consolidation research already makes history central。

### 1.2 Objectification / detachment

The public neural object is produced by：

```text
electrode-coverage boundary
cell / ensemble / region choice
event-window choice
spike / LFP feature extraction
pair / graph construction
within-session normalization
trial equivalence and matching
current-state covariate selection
```

These operations can delete which relation-specific history produced an apparently similar current state。

### 1.3 SRT re-entry

Reintroduce as test variables：

- which components co-participated；
- whether that participation was coupled to successful retrieval / consequence；
- which organism-level continuation was altered；
- whether the same current measurement class hides different future relation dispositions。

The re-entry does not assume an unmeasured SRT substance。`H` and every outcome remain neuroscience-local variables。

### 1.4 Reorganization

The study separates：

```text
component availability
!= pre-existing pair compatibility
!= current coordination opportunity
!= history-conditioned relation disposition
!= realized future coordination
```

It also separates relation identity from generic “more synchrony”。

### 1.5 New problem

The bounded new question is：

> When current nodes look equivalent under a declared measurement grain，what additional history must be retained to predict **which relation** becomes realizable，not merely whether the network is generally ready？

### 1.6 Domain test

Use a within-subject associative-memory design with repeated learning，PRE baseline，POST/rest and retrieval periods。The preferred comparison uses the same anchor item `A`：

```text
H+ pair: A-B repeatedly participates in successful joint learning / retrieval
H- pair: A-C receives exposure- and component-activity-matched presentation
         without the same joint consequence-bearing history
```

At a common cue or neutral probe，compare the future A-B and A-C relation after matching the declared present-state variables。

### 1.7 Domain increment gate

No “SRT adds X” claim exists at charter stage。The strongest ordinary accounts must first be tested for whether they already supply the full distinction。Possible dispositions remain：

```text
Case A: existing relational-memory / plasticity models already provide the same gain
        -> stop SRT increment claim

Case B: Bearer–Objectification Declaration improves routing or reporting only
        -> translation / method contribution

Case C: consequence-indexed relation history yields a new bounded discriminator
        beyond strong state，engram and relational baselines
        -> retain as a testable domain increment

unresolved -> question generation only
```

## 2. Mandatory Bearer–Objectification Declaration

| Field | Declaration for this well |
|---|---|
| Given One / unit | within-subject relation candidate `A-B` or `A-C` across learning，rest and retrieval；the participant/organism is the task-level consequence bearer |
| Boundary | clinically recorded neural components and declared task epochs；unrecorded brain，body and environment remain outside the measurement object，not outside causal reality |
| Grain / timescale | primary regime is intermediate learning-to-rest/retrieval，minutes to hours；fast intertrial and slow days/weeks results may not be pooled as the same mechanism |
| Equivalence | “same current state” means preregistered tolerance matching on `X/E/C/G/PRE_relation/anatomy`，not microphysical identity |
| Normalization | subject/session-specific feature construction and scaling must be frozen using calibration/training data only；no outcome-informed rematching |
| History loss | a present-state-only projection discards which pair co-participated，whether the relation carried successful consequence and how often it was re-entered |
| Position loss | third-person neural records omit first-person access；this study makes no phenomenal claim，so that omission is declared rather than silently repaired |
| Bearer substitution | components，participants and sessions are not assumed interchangeable；cross-subject generalization requires hierarchical replication，not identity substitution |
| Consequence attribution | neural outcomes land at pair/network measurements；behavioral success，delay or error lands at the participant/task trajectory；neither entails moral or phenomenal consequence |
| Participation | participant learns and acts；experimenter constructs cuts and models；clinical team constrains coverage；hardware records；analyst interprets under the declared model family |

The declaration is diagnostic。It does not prove that the selected unit is the final ontological unit。

## 3. Preferred study system and access gate

### Primary system

Human associative-memory learning under clinically indicated intracranial monitoring，with：

```text
single-unit and LFP data
PRE baseline relation estimates
repeated item-pair or sequence learning
POST/rest and retrieval epochs
item identity
behavioral accuracy / RT / confidence where available
```

### Evidence-access state

The repository contains primary-literature evidence packets and draft NEURAL33/34 designs，but no NEURAL34-specific raw-data access record or collaborator authorization has yet been identified。

Therefore：

```text
DATA-ACCESS-0 = OPEN
protocol freeze = prohibited
confirmatory execution = prohibited
```

Phase 8A must identify one of：

1. a public dataset satisfying the relation-history and PRE/current-state requirements；or
2. a documented collaborator / clinical data route with governance and ethics constraints。

Published figures or summary statistics can support the baseline review but cannot be presented as a new matched-state execution。If no viable access path exists，the well must stop or be re-chartered before substituting an animal，EEG/fMRI or synthetic system。

## 4. Strongest-baseline audit required before lock

The audit must use primary domain sources and include at least：

- synaptic / engram and cell-intrinsic memory accounts；
- systems consolidation，replay and reinstatement；
- metaplasticity and hidden-state explanations；
- latent-state / population-dynamical models；
- common-driver，oscillatory-window and fixed-anatomy accounts；
- relational-memory / graph or representational-geometry models；
- ordinary predictive models using rich present and PRE history covariates。

The audit question is not whether these traditions use SRT vocabulary。It is whether they already predict relation identity from history under a matched current-state design and provide the same experimental distinction。

No novelty or superiority language is allowed before this audit returns a bounded Case A/B/C result。

## 5. Measurement and model contract

### Primary outcome

One preregistered relation-identity measure，selected before confirmatory outcomes，such as：

```text
future pair / ensemble co-firing probability
encoding-to-retrieval relation similarity
POST ripple-window relation recruitment
relation-specific evoked cross-region response
```

Generic power，event rate or global synchrony cannot be the primary result。

### Required controls

At minimum：

```text
current and recent firing / component activity
PRE pair relation
component exposure count
cue and item identity
phase / ripple power / duration
regional event rate
arousal and trial position where available
fixed-anatomy / electrode-coverage proxies
recording quality
common-driver proxies
```

### Statistical gate

Compare Base and History models with subject/session-aware held-out evaluation。A usable result requires：

- non-trivial out-of-sample gain；
- stable effect direction across subjects/sessions；
- effect-size and uncertainty reporting；
- no dependence on massive pair-count pseudo-replication；
- survival under at least one rich node-state and one common-driver specification；
- frozen matching and exclusion rules。

Exact metrics，tolerances and thresholds are not selected in this charter。They must be fixed after feasibility and before confirmatory outcomes。

---

## 6. Falsifiers and withdrawal conditions

Downgrade or withdraw the history-conditioned relational increment if high-quality analysis shows any of the following：

1. `H` adds no held-out prediction after PRE relation and rich present node state；
2. apparent pair effects vanish under common-driver or recording-drift controls；
3. one component's excitability，synaptic state or exposure history fully explains the effect；
4. the probe predicts generic readiness but not relation identity；
5. effects depend on post-outcome matching，threshold movement or subject pooling；
6. the result does not replicate across sessions or subjects；
7. the strongest ordinary relational-memory / metaplasticity model already supplies the entire gain，triggering Case A；
8. no dataset or ethical access path can test the declared question without changing the object。

A negative result remains a valid Phase-8 result。It must not be repaired by changing timescale，system，outcome or history definition after inspection。

## 7. Claim ladder and prohibited exports

At maximum，a positive result may support the domain-local statement：

> Within the declared human neural system and intermediate timescale，relation-specific history adds reproducible prediction of which coordinated relation is later realized after the preregistered present-state and PRE-relation controls。

It would not establish：

```text
relations are ontologically independent of components
the matched observations are identical microstates
K^(tau) is a canonical SRT variable
neural coordination is selection，understanding or consciousness
the participant's phenomenology has been explained
the Constitution is proven
the result transfers to AI，society，religion or spiritual practice
```

### Phase 8C — preregistration / protocol lock

Create an immutable protocol and manifest before confirmatory inspection。
