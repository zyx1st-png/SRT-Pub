# Constructive Commitments Charter — reclassified red-team pointer

C-ONE / C-RET / C-OBJ / C-BEAR / C-PHEN were useful because they exposed where the earlier axiom/derivation architecture was silently carrying extra philosophical burdens.

They are **not** five new Constitution commitments and are not active programme authority.

Current use:

```text
C-ONE
-> pressure question: does determinacy already give contrastive differentiation, and what extra work is needed for an ontic One?

C-RET
-> pressure question: how does occurrence become historically effective without making all occurrence durable?

C-OBJ
-> pressure question: what makes still-this more than similarity or observer convention?

C-BEAR
-> pressure question: what makes history/consequence belong to this continuation from here?

C-PHEN
-> pressure question: how do givenness, qualitative differentiation, unity and for-me relate without defining experience into structure?
```

These prompts are used during Constitution internal red-team and later domain analysis. They do not need to be paid as axioms.
