# Bearer return–reconstitution `R` — author gate

> **Purpose:** present the narrow author decision exposed by the source-native strongest-neighbor pass. No option in this file is an author decision until explicitly selected or reformulated by the author.
>
> **Programme routing:** this autonomy-matched / non-outsourcing line is retained as a **secondary / deprioritized route relative to IRR-B**. Preparing this gate does not restore it to first programme priority.

---

## 2. What the strongest-neighbor pass removed

The following reading is too weak:

```text
prior consequences affect later organization / later selection.
```

Reason:

```text
Barandiaran / Di Paolo / Rohde:
autonomous organization + adaptive modulation + self-maintenance feedback

Montévil / Mossio:
mutually dependent constraints + self-maintaining closure

Gross / Blasius:
state/topology coevolution + history-shaped self-organization
```

already absorb ordinary consequence-to-future-organization feedback.

Therefore the author gate is not:

> “Does history affect later Selection?”

That question is already too generic.

### 2.1 Label guard — `R-A` is not `R-0`

The audit and this author gate use two different labels for two different logical roles:

```text
R-A = positive weak-coupling claim
      q_t -> O_t -> P_(t+1)
      as an SRT increment: DROP / ABSORBED

R-0 = null additional-requirement option
      V and C may jointly suffice without requiring q_t
      to help reconstitute P_(t+1)
      as an author-meaning option: STILL OPEN

R-A != R-0
```

Absorption of `R-A` therefore does **not** remove `R-0` from the author decision surface and does not force the author to choose `R-B` or `R-C`.

## 3. The actual author question

The live question is:

> **Must the consequences borne by a candidate bearer be specifically and non-outsourcably involved in reconstituting the later Selection-position from which that candidate continues to select?**

Three readings remain available.

---

## 4. Option R-0 — no additional coupling requirement

```text
Bearer continuity requires the already accepted combination:

- a continuing higher-order consequence-bearing organization;
- continued reconstitution of a stratified Selection-position;

but there is no further requirement that the particular consequences borne at t
must causally / structurally participate in reconstituting P_(t+1).
```

Implication:

```text
V1 C1 R0 can still qualify as a bearer candidate.
```

Advantages:

```text
minimal extension beyond the author's accepted reading;
avoids turning every bearer into a learner;
avoids adding a new hidden dependency.
```

Cost:

```text
from-where and to-where remain only co-located burdens;
the deeper anti-circular genesis proposal weakens;
why they belong to the same continuing bearer remains partly open.
```

---

## 5. Option R-B — non-outsourcable return-to-reconstitution dependence

```text
A consequence q_t counts as part of the candidate's own bearer-history only if:

1. q_t closes on / is borne by O_t;
2. q_t is non-outsourcably load-bearing in how O_t reconstitutes P_(t+1);
3. later Selection proceeds from that reconstituted P_(t+1).
```

Counterfactual pressure:

```text
if apparent P can be restored / reloaded unchanged
while q_t's cost / repair / history closes elsewhere,
then functional continuity can be preserved
without preserving the same bearer-continuation relation through that route.
```

This is the **minimal machine recommendation** after strongest-neighbor pressure.

Why it is stronger than generic feedback:

```text
it ties future reconstitution to consequence ownership / unit attribution,
not merely to the fact that some prior signal changed some future state.
```

Open risks:

```text
PH-IND06 may already pay most of the non-outsourcing content;
autonomy theory may absorb the distinction by selecting a broader true unit;
R-B may still collapse into learning / plasticity if operationalized carelessly.
```

---

## 6. Option R-C — reciprocal attribution / reconstitution closure

```text
P_t is not only generated by O_t.
P_t also participates in determining which action/consequence trajectories
count as consequences of this continuing organization.

Those consequences alter O_t.
Altered O_t reconstitutes P_(t+1).

Thus:
P_t
-> own-consequence attribution
-> consequence-bearing O_t
-> reconstitution of P_(t+1)
```

This makes the deeper claim:

> **Bearer formation is the progressive reciprocal closure of `from-where` and `to-where`, rather than the later addition of history to an already individuated unit.**

Potential gain:

```text
stronger anti-circular formation story;
explains why position and consequence-bearing belong to one continuing relation;
fits the intuition that bearer identity is not fixed content or fixed component membership.
```

Open risks:

```text
may simply relabel organisational closure;
may smuggle "own consequence" in before bearerhood is earned;
can become circular if P identifies O while O identifies P;
stronger than anything yet explicitly author-adjudicated;
requires a dedicated circularity / hidden-premise red-team before Level assignment.
```

---

## 7. Machine comparison

```text
R-0:
safer / weaker / leaves from-where-to-where unity partly unexplained

R-B:
minimal nontrivial coupling;
best current balance of author continuity and strongest-neighbor resistance;
MACHINE RECOMMENDATION

R-C:
most faithful to a fully genetic bearer story;
conceptually richer but presently has the highest circularity / relabeling risk
```

Machine recommendation is **R-B**, with R-C retained as the next strengthening only if the author means that `from-where` and `to-where` mutually constitute bearer identity rather than merely form a one-way dependence.

---

## 8. What author adjudication would and would not do

Selecting one option would authorize only its **meaning** for the next reconstruction pass.

It would not automatically establish:

```text
canonical bearer law;
strict numerical identity;
scientific novelty;
Level 1;
Level 2;
a model / toy / experiment;
HOLD exit.
```

After author selection, governance requires:

```text
selected meaning
-> circularity / hidden-premise red-team
-> if R-B or R-C: habit / sensorimotor-autonomy strongest-neighbor pass 2
-> final strongest-neighbor non-substitutability test
-> only then Level 0/1/2 assignment
```

The mandatory pass-2 family is Barandiaran & Di Paolo (2014) on habit genealogy and Egbert & Barandiaran (2014) on habits as self-sustaining sensorimotor patterns. Their relevance is not pre-judged as absorption; they must be tested because they target the historically sedimented action-organization burden more directly than the 2009 minimal-agency paper.

## 9. Author-facing short form

> **你更接近哪一种？**
>
> **R-0：** Bearer 只要求“立体 Selection-position 持续重构”与“历史后果由同一高阶组织承担”同时成立，但不要求这批后果参与下一次位置重构。
>
> **R-B：** 更进一步——属于这个 Bearer 的后果，必须不可外包地参与下一次 Selection-position 的重构；如果位置可以原样恢复而代价/历史由别处承担，就不算同一 bearer-continuation。
>
> **R-C：** 更强——不仅后果改变下一次位置，Selection-position 本身也参与决定哪些后果属于“这个组织”；`from-where` 与 `to-where` 彼此生成并闭合，Bearer 就是在这种互相规定中形成。
