# SRT Selection-First Architecture Rebase — Master Plan (2026-08-28)

> **Boundary:** planning-only, non-canonical programme record. It does not modify P0/P1/P2, register `articulation` as a canonical term, redefine `L0/L1/L2`, `G_hat_theta`, Stable ISP, bearer, subjecthood, consciousness, `d`, `Psi_f` or `T_dir`, or authorize novelty claims.
>
> **Default posture after review:** this is more likely a **dependency-propagation and subtraction programme** than a new-P0 programme. The theoretical yield, if any, is expected mainly at individuation / bearer structure and source-intuition recovery, not from manufacturing a new primitive amendment.

---

## 0. Executive decision and carve-outs

Pause this workflow **for new topic construction only**:

```text
new venue / call appears
-> search for an SRT novelty angle
-> patch theory to fit
```

Replace it with:

```text
read-only architecture/subtraction pass
-> only-if-needed owner edits
-> bounded propagation
-> research-question discovery
-> paper / venue selection
```

### Existing-manuscript carve-out

This pause does **not** apply to already-submitted manuscripts or journal-requested revisions.

- Frontiers 1837760 is already published; APC / billing is administrative and is outside this rebase.
- *Costly Selective Closure* / *Adaptive Behavior* is already submitted and not yet externally reviewed. Unless the journal explicitly requests it, the rebase must not expand its framing, add experiments, or silently rewrite manuscript dependencies while it is under editorial handling.

The current consciousness / neural-models route remains a **candidate downstream venue path**, not the organizing target.

After Track A + at most **four owner-edit PRs**, stop and review programme yield.


## 13. Completion definition

The programme is complete enough for topic discovery when:

- primitive / persistence / L2 / object / bearer / subject / experiencer dependencies are readable without contradiction;
- frozen canonical anchors were either left unchanged under early-exit or edited through explicit C-class authorization;
- no parallel comparison owner was created;
