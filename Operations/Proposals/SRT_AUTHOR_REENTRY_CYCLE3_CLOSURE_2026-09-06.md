## 1. Cycle-3 problem family

Cycle 3 moved upstream of One/Bearer formation and asked:

> How can determinate actuality obtain from constrained possibility without presupposing an already completed chooser / bearer / subject, and without confusing the creative actualisation role with an ultimate self-grounding explanation?

This problem family is distinct from the Cycle-1/Cycle-2 emphasis on history localization, One/Bearer genesis and selection-position formation.

### K/A/B/C/D at closure

```text
K — current canonical constraints
Selection precedes / produces determinate manifest actuality.
Selection does not require a pre-existing selector / bearer.
Primitive actualisation is the current P0 floor.
Minimum non-neutrality / gradient / friction / payability constrain admissibility but do not currently derive first actualisation.
Persistence / One / Bearer / subject / experiencer remain downstream burdens.

A — current author adjudication
Selection is intended to carry the Creator / creative-actualisation role.
Selection-as-Creator does not automatically solve ultimate Ground.
Why primitive Selection itself obtains remains open.
Every genuinely actualising Selection is, in the strict actuality-producing sense, a local / continuous creation.

B — recovered historical SRT
June-2026 source material mapped God toward the ungrounded floor exposed by self-grounding failure and mapped Creation toward epsilon_pg + first exclusion / determination / inscription.
This historical mapping is continuous with but not identical to current author re-entry.

C — AI synthesis / organization
"Selection is the creative primitive of manifest actuality" is the safest theory-facing compression.
Creator and Ground should remain separate burdens.
Continuous creation is not one cosmic subject continuously choosing; it is repeated local actualisation.

D — unresolved pressure
Why Selection / primitive actualisation obtains at all.
Whether a deeper non-Selection derivation can be given without merely renaming Selection.
Whether any Cycle-3 formulation yields a new Level-1 structural repartition beyond already-existing SRT actualisation architecture.
What domain-native Level-2 discriminator, if any, follows.
```

## 3. Strongest-neighbor pressure

### 3.1 Whitehead — named strongest neighbor

Whitehead is the strongest mature neighbor for the Cycle-3 creative-actualisation problem because his process metaphysics already provides, in source-native terms:

```text
Creativity as an ultimate metaphysical principle;
actual occasions / actual entities as concrete actualities;
concrescence as many becoming one;
actuality as decision amid potentiality;
completed occasions becoming data for later occasions;
continuous creative advance.
```

Therefore Whitehead strongly absorbs:

```text
ongoing / continuous creation;
potentiality -> actuality;
creation without a classical external craftsman;
many -> one novelty;
past actuality conditioning later becoming.
```

Cycle 3 must not claim those as SRT discoveries.

### 3.2 Deleuze — strongest B-type derivation pressure

Deleuzian differential virtual-to-actual individuation exerts the strongest pressure on the A′ decision to keep primitive Selection as the actualisation floor rather than derive one determinate event from minimum non-neutrality alone.

It preserves the open possibility that a deeper differential genetic relation could someday replace the primitive boundary, but current SRT has not paid the non-circular realization bridge required for that move.
