## 6. Minimal causal model and toy experiment

Cycle 1 introduced a test-local 2 x 2 history-location model:

```text
H_M = causally effective history carried in 多 / surrounding field
H_1 = causally effective history carried in a declared candidate One

00  reset H_M; reset H_1
10  preserve H_M; reset H_1
01  reset H_M; preserve H_1
11  preserve H_M; preserve H_1
```

The toy adaptive-agent implementation showed a clean temporal crossing between M-side-only and One-side-only history:

```text
initial:
P(A)_10 - P(A)_01 = +0.407

late:
P(A)_10 - P(A)_01 = -0.686
```

Reference means:

| condition | initial P(A) | early P(A) | late P(A) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `00` | 0.500 | 0.599 | 0.775 |
| `10` M-side only | 0.500 | 0.242 | 0.046 |
| `01` One-side only | 0.093 | 0.146 | 0.732 |
| `11` both | 0.093 | 0.088 | 0.071 |

This proves only toy causal separability / coherence of history-location channels. It is not empirical support for SRT ontology, not a numerical-identity result, and not Level 2 by itself.
