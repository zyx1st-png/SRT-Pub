## 11. Empathy, projection and revisability

Projection:

```text
model(other) := my position
```

Stronger empathy candidate:

```text
model(other)
:= revisable estimate of
other history + stakes + reachable space + event-to-writeback transformation
```

Thus deep understanding is less:

```text
"if I were there"
```

and more:

```text
"if I approximated the position from which this event becomes yours"
```

A key quality criterion is:

```text
predictive adequacy + revision capacity
```

not subjective confidence.
