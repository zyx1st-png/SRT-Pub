# Bounded research route — unfinished direction, unified coarse-graining, sacredness and spirituality

> **Purpose:** turn the 2026-10-04 author adjudication into a neutral, falsifiable research route without changing canonical owners or the active cognition CURRENT NEXT.
>
> **Boundary:** this route is noncanonical, does not reopen HP-B, does not authorize a new consciousness theorem, and does not treat spiritual phenomenology as evidence for primitive Selection.

## 0. One bounded question

The route asks:

> **Can an explicit object be unavailable while a bearer-relative directional organization remains structured enough to constrain later objectification; and can the same abstract architecture scale from prospective-memory unfinishedness to sacred / spiritual phenomenology without collapsing those domains into one mechanism?**

This is a same-target comparison problem, not a novelty declaration.

## 1. Neutral core decomposition

Use a neutral sequence before SRT interpretation:

~~~text
N0 unresolved / incomplete state
N1 retained non-neutral directional structure
N2 directional coherence across probes
N3 selective unified coarse-graining
N4 reportable foreground / objectification
N5 local resolution / discharge
N6 writeback into later organization
~~~

Do not assume that every domain instantiates all stages in the same implementation.

## 2. Prospective-memory assay

Target state:

~~~text
participant reports:
“there is an important pending intention”
but cannot yet report the target content
~~~

Probe before successful recall:

- importance / urgency;
- temporal horizon;
- social versus nonsocial;
- action versus information;
- contact versus non-contact;
- work / family / health / finance or other preregistered broad classes;
- confidence in each directional judgment.

Primary neutral question:

> Is there above-chance, internally coherent structure before explicit target recovery?

Strongest version:

> Does cross-probe coherence predict which target will later be recovered, above generic familiarity / confidence / arousal baselines?

## 3. Sudden report versus continuous preparation

Collect time-resolved measures around recall.

Competing interpretations:

~~~text
R1 abrupt retrieval threshold:
latent target representation crosses accessibility threshold

R2 progressive constraint convergence:
multiple partial constraints become increasingly compatible
before a reportable cut stabilizes

R3 hybrid:
latent representation and constraint geometry co-evolve
~~~

SRT gains nothing if a fair R1/R3 model absorbs the held-out pattern.

## 4. False-candidate test

Critical discriminator candidate:

~~~text
true target
vs
high-confidence false target
~~~

Measure:

- subjective “this is it”;
- reduction in search pressure;
- persistence of residual unfinishedness;
- later correction;
- downstream action selection.

Working prediction candidate:

> Confidence alone need not equal closure. A false candidate may be strongly activated yet fail to produce the same local discharge or stable writeback.

This remains neutral until tested against metacognitive and memory-strength models.

## 5. Selective unity, not simple integration

The author requires broad exclusion / differentiation, not deletion.

Operationalize unified coarse-graining through:

- which distinctions remain behaviorally consequential;
- which distinctions become equivalent;
- which dimensions are backgrounded;
- which candidate transitions become easier / harder;
- whether multiple probes can be compressed into one stable actionable interpretation.

Do not substitute:

~~~text
connectivity
broadcast
global access
compression ratio
synchrony
classification accuracy
~~~

for unified selective coarse-graining without an explicit same-target argument.

## 7. Reflection assay

Test:

~~~text

vs

~~~

Compare:

Do not treat introspective confidence as transparent access.

## 8. Sacred / awe decomposition

Do not begin from a “sacred object” ontology.

Neutral dimensions:

- perceived vastness / scale expansion;
- need for accommodation / recutting;
- self-object salience reduction;
- connectedness / unity;
- importance / meaning intensity;
- ineffability / objectification insufficiency;
- action / value consequence after the state;
- persistence and writeback.

Candidate structure:

~~~text
current local cuts insufficient
+ large-scale non-neutral relation
+ self-object relatively backgrounded
+ cross-scale selective unification
-> sacred / awe phenomenology candidate
~~~

This must be compared against established awe, self-transcendent experience, mystical-experience and predictive / appraisal accounts.

## 9. Ego dissolution versus Bearer continuity

Do not identify:

~~~text
narrative-self weakening
body-boundary change
self-location change
agency change
ownership change
self-reflection reduction
Bearer dissolution
~~~

Candidate assay asks whether a state can show strong ego/self-model attenuation while preserving:

- coherent consequence-bearing;
- temporal continuity;
- structured direction;
- later writeback;
- stable differentiation of “what changes for this continuing locus.”

If yes, ego dissolution and Bearer dissolution should remain distinct.

## 10. Spirituality as longitudinal reopening capacity

Do not operationalize spirituality by peak-state intensity alone.

Candidate longitudinal dimensions:

- revisability of sedimented interpretations;
- ability to reopen a hardened local cut;
- ability to tolerate underdetermination without premature closure;
- broader consequence-bearing after recutting;
- preservation of ordinary-life responsibility;
- reduced identity capture by the practice itself;
- integration / re-entry after unusual states;
- increased ability to distinguish direction from ready-made answer.

Potential failure pattern:

~~~text
special-state intensity rises
but
care narrows
revisability falls
identity capture rises
ordinary consequence-bearing weakens
~~~

This counts against the spirituality-as-reopening hypothesis.
