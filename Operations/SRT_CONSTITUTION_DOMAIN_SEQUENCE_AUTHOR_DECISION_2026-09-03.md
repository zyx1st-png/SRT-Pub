# Constitution domain programme — author sequence decision

> **Author instruction，2026-09-03**: “依次推进，可以加入宗教和灵修部分。”
>
> **Decision effect**: the first Phase-8 deep well is Neuroscience。The remaining domains are recorded as an ordered programme intention，not as parallel authorization。

## 1. Ordered programme intention

The operative sequence is：

```text
1. Neuroscience
   -> Phase 8 deep well
   -> Phase 9 productive-adequacy review

2. Epistemology
   -> may open only after the preceding Phase 9 review

3. Consciousness
   -> may open only after the preceding Phase 9 review

4. Religion & Spirituality
   -> author-requested added candidate
   -> may open only after the preceding Phase 9 review
   -> requires a Phase-7-equivalent score and scope lock before its own Phase 8
```

The ordering records authorial direction after the Phase-7 comparison。It does not guarantee that every later well will open unchanged。A Phase-9 result may narrow，reorder，defer or stop the queue。

## 2. Current authorization state

| Domain | State | What is authorized now |
|---|---|---|
| Neuroscience | **closed at Phase 8A / Phase 9 complete** | exact access reopening only；no protocol lock or execution |

No Physics，Biology，AI or Social deep well is opened by this decision。

## 3. One-well interpretation

“Proceed in sequence” is not read as permission to run four programmes concurrently。The governing dependency remains：

```text
one Phase-8 study
-> one Phase-9 productive-adequacy review
-> only then decide whether the next queued domain opens
```

Research notes may preserve future questions，but they must not create protocols，domain claims or theory rewrites for a queued well before its gate。

## 4. Religion & Spirituality candidate boundary

The repository already has a governed domain owner in `Spirituality/`。The future candidate therefore means **Religion & Spirituality as one candidate domain with two possible internal tracks**：

1. comparative religion / sacred-object formation / tradition and institution；
2. contemplative praxis / meditation / prayer / community / ordinary-life re-entry。

Its future deep well must choose one bounded object。It may not silently combine comparative theology，mystical phenomenology，meditation neuroscience，ethics，clinical benefit and praxis into one study。

Before that domain can open，a Phase-7-equivalent addendum must score：

```text
repository depth
clarity of the traditionally Given One
bearer / position pressure
domain-native evidence and methods
visible reorganization potential
strong rival / baseline availability
manageable scope
```

The addendum must read `Spirituality/SRT_Spirituality_Claim_Status.md` first and distinguish first-person report，historical/textual evidence，anthropology/sociology，practice outcomes and neuroscience evidence。

## 5. Non-transfer guardrails for the added domain

The following are not licensed by adding Religion & Spirituality to the queue：

```text
God / Divine / Tao / Brahman = L0 or a canonical SRT operator
mystical report = metaphysical verification
meditation effect = SRT validation
neural correlate = truth of a spiritual doctrine
practice intensity = high d
community membership or teacher authority = epistemic / moral privilege
```

Religion and spiritual practice may supply domain objects，participant positions，historical traditions，rituals，reports，institutions and intervention families。Their evidence burdens remain domain-specific and cannot be paid by the future Consciousness or Neuroscience wells。
