## 1. Ordered programme intention

The operative main sequence is：

```text
1. Neuroscience
   -> first Phase-8 pass complete at 8A
   -> Phase-9 review complete
   -> later conditional reopening permitted

2. Epistemology
   -> selected second main well
   -> current work continues under its own branch / gates until landed

3. Consciousness
   -> may open as the next main sequential well only after Epistemology Phase-9 review

4. Religion & Spirituality
   -> author-requested added candidate
   -> may open only after the preceding Phase-9 review
   -> requires a Phase-7-equivalent score and scope lock before its own Phase 8
```

The queue is a **main workline sequence**，not a ban on ever revisiting an earlier well。Earlier-well reopening and opening a new queued well are different operations。

The ordering records authorial direction after the Phase-7 comparison。It does not guarantee that every later well will open unchanged。A Phase-9 result may narrow，reorder，defer or stop the queue。

## 3. Neuroscience reopening rule

The first Neuroscience result remains fixed as a historical/evidence record：

```text
strongest-baseline verdict = Case B
exact-charter DATA-ACCESS-0 = NO-GO
confirmatory execution = not started
Phase-9 disposition = translation-only + scope-limited
```

“Not permanently frozen” means that future work may reopen Neuroscience in one of three ways。

### N-R1 — exact-charter access reopening

A qualifying public dataset or documented collaborator / clinical route supplies the original PRE-relation，relation-history，common-probe and rich present-state requirements。

Then the existing charter may continue into its next gated phase without rewriting the original Phase-8A verdict。

### N-R2 — stronger differential reopening

New domain evidence，a materially stronger rival comparison，or a newly identified measurable contrast creates a sharper Case-C candidate **for the same underlying relational-history problem**。

This requires a visible addendum / versioned successor stating：

```text
what changed since the first audit
why the old Case-B verdict remains historically correct
what new discriminator now exists
what new falsifier / withdrawal condition applies
```

### N-R3 — successor-question reopening

A different but genuinely stronger neuroscience question may later be opened if it cannot be answered by silently weakening the old question to fit convenient data。

It requires a new author-visible charter and may not inherit a positive result，novelty credit or evidence standing from the first pass。

### Reopening prohibitions

```text
deleting or rewriting the first adverse result
calling a weaker dataset an execution of the old exact charter
changing outcome / timescale / system after seeing convenient data while preserving the old question label
using Epistemology or Consciousness results as neuroscience evidence
running a large parallel neuroscience programme merely because reopening is allowed
```

The default remains sequential focus on the selected Epistemology workline。Reopening is conditional and bounded，not permanent parallel authorization。

## 4. One-well interpretation after the clarification

“Proceed in sequence” means one **active main deep-well programme**，not one domain that can never be revisited。

The governing main dependency remains：

```text
active Phase-8 study
-> Phase-9 productive-adequacy review
-> only then open the next queued main well
```

An earlier well may be reopened under its explicit reopen rules，but reopening must remain bounded enough that it does not silently create two full parallel programmes。

If a Neuroscience reopening becomes large enough to compete with the active main well for programme authority，the author must explicitly reprioritize the sequence rather than letting concurrency happen by drift。

## 5. Religion & Spirituality candidate boundary

The repository already has a governed domain owner in `Spirituality/`。The future candidate therefore means **Religion & Spirituality as one candidate domain with two possible internal tracks**：

1. comparative religion / sacred-object formation / tradition and institution；
2. contemplative praxis / meditation / prayer / community / ordinary-life re-entry。

Its future deep well must choose one bounded object。It may not silently combine comparative theology，mystical phenomenology，meditation neuroscience，ethics，clinical benefit and praxis into one study。

Before that domain can open，a Phase-7-equivalent addendum must score：

```text
repository depth
clarity of the traditionally Given One
bearer / position pressure
domain-native evidence and methods
visible reorganization potential
strong rival / baseline availability
manageable scope
```

The addendum must read `Spirituality/SRT_Spirituality_Claim_Status.md` first and distinguish first-person report，historical/textual evidence，anthropology/sociology，practice outcomes and neuroscience evidence。

## 6. Non-transfer guardrails for the added domain

The following are not licensed by adding Religion & Spirituality to the queue：

```text
God / Divine / Tao / Brahman = L0 or a canonical SRT operator
mystical report = metaphysical verification
meditation effect = SRT validation
neural correlate = truth of a spiritual doctrine
practice intensity = high d
community membership or teacher authority = epistemic / moral privilege
```

Religion and spiritual practice may supply domain objects，participant positions，historical traditions，rituals，reports，institutions and intervention families。Their evidence burdens remain domain-specific and cannot be paid by the future Consciousness or Neuroscience wells。
