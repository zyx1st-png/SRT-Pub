## 5. Admission discipline

Every new library relation must state:

1. what is weaker than it;
2. what does not entail it;
3. positive admission evidence;
4. at least one plausible negative control;
5. source-native owner;
6. failure condition;
7. M-status;
8. what would force narrowing / split / retirement.

A relation with only positive examples remains under-red-teamed.

## 6. Cross-domain rule

The library does not reward the number of domains mapped.

It rewards:

~~~text
semantic invariance
+ source-native preservation
+ exclusion power
+ pre-result transfer
+ revision under evidence
~~~

A relation that maps everywhere but rejects nothing should be retired or weakened to a heuristic.

## 7. Normativity boundary

Relations may support typed generative expectation.

They do not automatically establish moral legitimacy.

~~~text
operative != legitimate
persistent != good
collectively stable != just
larger-scale success != participant-level good
~~~
