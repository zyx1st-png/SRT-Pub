# NEG-X3B-01 — formal structure without operative coupling

## Admission target

~~~text
X3b operative expectation regime
~~~

## Source-native negative pattern

Meyer & Rowan provide a mature organizational pattern in which formal structures can be decoupled from ongoing activities.

Therefore this is not sufficient:

~~~text
formal rule / formal structure exists
-> X3b
~~~

## Expected classification

~~~text
X3a articulated expectation = YES / possible

X3b through the claimed work-activity channel
= FAIL unless operational coupling is separately shown
~~~

## Why this is a real negative control

The rule / structure is present.

What fails is the stronger burden:

~~~text
represented expectation
-> operative change in participant action possibilities
~~~

## Non-identity

A decoupled formal structure may still change legitimacy, external evaluation, resource access or organizational survival.

If those are the claimed downstream channels, they require a separately typed relation record.

## Programme value

NEG-X3B-01 proves the library can reject a tempting mapping rather than merely collect positive analogies.
