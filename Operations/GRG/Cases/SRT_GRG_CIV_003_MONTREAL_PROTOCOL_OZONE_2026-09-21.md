## B. Source-native account

The Montreal Protocol controls ozone-depleting substances through international commitments and phase-out / phase-down schedules.

The regime also uses recurring scientific and technology/economic assessments.

The 2022 Scientific Assessment reports that actions under the Protocol continue to contribute to ozone recovery.

UNEP reports that nearly 99% of banned ozone-depleting substances have been phased out.

Current assessment projections place return of total column ozone to 1980 values at approximately:

~~~text
2040 near-global average
2045 Arctic
2066 Antarctic
~~~

assuming continued policy implementation / compliance and stated model assumptions.

## E. Initial relation

Industrial use of ozone-depleting substances altered atmospheric chemical conditions in ways that depleted stratospheric ozone.

Scientific assessment externalized a causal account and future-risk expectation.

## F. Intervention / institution

~~~text
institution = international treaty regime
externalized expectation = controlled-substance schedules / compliance commitments
maintenance = national implementation + reporting + scientific assessment
              + technology/economic assessment + institutional revision
technology = substitution and process change
~~~

## G. Consequences

Documented source-level trajectory:

~~~text
controls on ozone-depleting substances
-> large reduction in production / consumption
-> declining ozone-depleting substance burden
-> observed / projected ozone recovery
~~~

This is a coupled scientific, technological and institutional process.

The institutional schedules encode expectations about future production/consumption behavior required to reduce ozone-depleting emissions.

Scientific assessments update whether the resulting environmental trajectory remains consistent with recovery.
