## B. Source-native account

Combined sewer systems historically integrated storm drainage and sanitary wastewater in one network.

Many were built before modern wastewater treatment became standard.

As treatment requirements changed, new systems increasingly used separate sanitary and storm sewers.

Nevertheless many older cities still operate inherited combined networks.

In New York City, roughly 60% of the present sewer system remains combined.
