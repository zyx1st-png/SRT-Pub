## B. Source-native account

Beavers modify riverine ecosystems by building dams, excavating canals and altering vegetation.

Review literature documents substantial effects on:

- ecosystem structure and geomorphology;
- hydrology and water resources;
- water quality;
- freshwater ecology;
- human land / water use.

These effects vary across landscape and biome context.

## E. Initial relation

## F. Intervention / activity

~~~text

~~~

## G. Consequences

The environmental modification can persist beyond the immediate construction act and reorganize conditions for other organisms.

But the ecological direction and magnitude are context dependent.
