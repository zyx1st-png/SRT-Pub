# CIV-005 — beaver ecosystem engineering

## A. Case identity

~~~text
domain family = D-LIFE
period = ecological / evolutionary timescales
setting = beaver-modified riverine / riparian ecosystems
record status = active ecological source-pressure case
primary review = Brazier et al. 2021
DOI = 10.1002/wat2.1494
~~~

## B. Source-native account

Beavers modify riverine ecosystems by building dams, excavating canals and altering vegetation.

Review literature documents substantial effects on:

- ecosystem structure and geomorphology;
- hydrology and water resources;
- water quality;
- freshwater ecology;
- human land / water use.

These effects vary across landscape and biome context.

~~~

aggregation risk = effects vary across water-quality and biotic outcomes
~~~

## E. Initial relation

Beaver behavior changes physical environmental conditions that later organisms encounter.

## F. Intervention / activity

~~~text
dam building / canal excavation / vegetation modification
-> changed water flow, storage, sediment, connectivity and habitat
~~~

This is organism-driven environmental modification, not policy intervention.

## G. Consequences

The environmental modification can persist beyond the immediate construction act and reorganize conditions for other organisms.

But the ecological direction and magnitude are context dependent.

## I. Generative expectation

No human normative expectation is required.

At the descriptive level, persistent beaver engineering changes the environmental conditions available to later processes.

## J. Normativity

~~~text
normativity status = NOT OPENED
~~~

Ecosystem engineering is not inherently good or bad.

## K. Rival reading

Ecosystem engineering and niche-construction theory already provide the mature source-native explanations.

GRG adds no mechanism.

Its only possible value here is to preserve the distinction:

~~~text

from
selection feedback
from
inherited reconstructed condition
~~~

across domains.
