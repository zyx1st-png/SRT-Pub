# Framework Q Research Framework v0.1

## 0. Role

This file is the programme-level execution architecture for FRAMEWORK-Q.

It is not:

- a completed ontology;
- a universal equation;
- a vocabulary replacement for mature disciplines;
- a claim that all domains instantiate one mechanism;
- a new canonical owner.

Its job is to make the author's FRAMEWORK-Q direction operational:

> preserve source-native knowledge, extract candidate generative relations, compare them across domains without erasing mechanism, use evidence and failure to revise the grammar, and extend the same architecture to social and civilizational learning.

## 1. Framework identity

Working definition:

~~~text
FRAMEWORK-Q
= a self-revising research architecture
  for learning recurring generative dependency patterns
  across heterogeneous domains, positions, scales and histories.
~~~

The unification target is not:

~~~text
one substance
one equation
one mechanism
one vocabulary
~~~

The target is a constrained grammar of relations such as:

~~~text
formation
retention
maintenance
re-entry
position
consequence
reconstruction
cross-scale recursion
anticipation
normative comparison
~~~

A relation belongs in the grammar only if it carries admission conditions, non-identities and failure conditions.

## 2. Two-level programme architecture

### Level A — mainline framework construction

The mainline builds:

1. the relation / burden library;
2. domain and civilizational mappings;
3. evidence typing;
4. objectification declarations;
5. generative-expectation routing;
6. cross-domain translation rules;
7. revision and retirement rules.

### Level B — calibration workstreams

Calibration includes:

- experiments;
- simulations when appropriate;
- archival / historical tests;
- source-fidelity close reads;
- causal perturbation;
- transfer pilots;
- adverse-result preservation.

Calibration asks whether the framework is too permissive or wrong.

It does not block unrelated framework work.

~~~text
calibration constrains mainline
but
calibration queue != mainline work queue
~~~

## 3. Reuse of the Domain Reconstruction Layer

The existing Domain Reconstruction Framework remains the preferred entry surface for one mature discipline.

Therefore the active stack becomes:

~~~text
canonical / author constraints
        ↓
FRAMEWORK-Q Research Framework
        ↓
Domain Reconstruction Framework
        ↓
domain-native theory / mechanism / historical account
        ↓
evidence / calibration / intervention
        ↓
writeback to domain + FRAMEWORK-Q relation library
~~~

FRAMEWORK-Q does not replace domain frameworks.

It compares what their independently preserved relations pay.

## 5. Source-native layer

Every FRAMEWORK-Q mapping begins with a source-native account.

The source-native layer owns:

- field terminology;
- mechanisms;
- measurements;
- empirical regularities;
- formal models;
- historical particulars;
- local causal claims.

Mandatory guard:

~~~text
domain mechanism != FRAMEWORK-Q relation
~~~

A relation may abstract dependency structure.

It may not appropriate the mechanism as THEORY-Q ontology.

## 6. Objectification layer

Every mapping should state which public objects are being constructed and stabilized.

Relevant operations include:

- boundary;
- grain;
- timescale;
- equivalence;
- normalization;
- aggregation;
- proxy;
- canonical representation;
- archive inclusion;
- participant pooling;
- periodization.

Question:

> What does this objectification preserve, and what generative relation could disappear because of it?

HKB normalization work is a model example of why this declaration is mandatory.

~~~text
measured object
!= maintained organization automatically
~~~

## 7. Evidence architecture

FRAMEWORK-Q must accept heterogeneous evidence without pretending all evidence is equivalent.

### 7.1 Evidence families

At minimum distinguish:

~~~text
E1 controlled intervention / experiment
E2 natural or quasi-experimental causal evidence
E3 longitudinal / observational empirical evidence
E4 formal / mathematical constraint
E5 mechanistic model with independent validation
E6 historical-comparative / institutional evidence
E7 engineering / operational performance evidence
E8 structured lived / practice experience
E9 philosophical / conceptual convergence
E10 narrative / metaphor / heuristic
~~~

These codes are research-routing labels, not a universal epistemic ranking.

### 7.2 Evidence dimensions

Each record should assess separately:

- source reliability;
- causal leverage;
- replication / robustness;
- scale match;
- temporal depth;
- position diversity;
- measurement dependence;
- transportability;
- confounding;
- revision pressure.

A strong philosophical convergence may nominate a relation.

It does not substitute for a causal experiment where the claim itself is causal.

A randomized experiment may strongly establish one local mechanism.

It does not by itself establish a civilization-scale relation.

## 9. Cross-domain translation

Cross-domain translation asks:

> Do two source-native systems pay a comparable generative burden?

It does not ask:

> Are they secretly the same thing?

Required comparison fields:

- relation topology;
- dependency direction;
- maintenance provenance;
- component / whole relation;
- scale;
- intervention availability;
- consequence type;
- time structure;
- evidence family;
- failure mode.

### Mapping status

Use:

~~~text
K0 verbal resemblance only
K1 structural analogy
K2 burden-aligned candidate
K3 independently evidenced relation match
K4 transfer-generating match
K5 repeatedly cross-domain constrained grammar candidate
~~~

These are programme routing labels, not ontology Levels.

No relation progresses merely because several domains use similar words.

### 9.1 First civilizational pressure-test revision

The first three case records (Internet standards, antimicrobial resistance, Montreal Protocol) produced a productive false-convergence result:

~~~text
one-relation compression = FAIL
~~~

Two methodological fields are now required in civilizational / cross-domain comparison.

#### Expectation embodiment / enforcement provenance

When an expectation is externalized, state how it becomes causally operative.

Working routing labels:

~~~text
E-TECH  technical interface / compatibility
E-LEGAL legal / treaty obligation
E-MKT   market / price / contract incentive
E-ORG   organizational procedure / hierarchy
E-NORM  social norm / reputational enforcement
E-INFRA material / infrastructural constraint
E-MIXED mixed provenance
~~~

These are not equivalent mechanisms.

#### Position / horizon consequence split

When effects cross positions or timescales, state separately:

- immediate beneficiary;
- delayed affected position;
- local versus distributed consequence;
- time horizon;
- externalized cost;
- reversibility of future option loss.

Owner:
`Operations/Audits/THEORY-Q_FRAMEWORK-Q_CIVILIZATIONAL_FALSE_CONVERGENCE_PASS1_2026-09-21.md`.

### 9.2 Mature-source pressure revision — QX3 / QX4 split

Ostrom-style institutional analysis and niche-construction / ecological-inheritance work force two further decompositions.

#### QX3 — expectation articulation versus operative regime

~~~text
Q3a articulated / encoded expectation
= a future relation is represented in a rule, protocol, norm,
  schedule, model, plan or shared representation.

Q3b operative expectation regime
= the expectation becomes materially / institutionally operative
  through interfaces, incentives, monitoring, sanctions,
  organizational process, social norm, infrastructure or mixed provenance,
  such that participant action possibilities actually change.
~~~

Guards:

~~~text
Q3a != Q3b
written rule != operative institution
Q3b need not always require a formal written Q3a
~~~

#### QX4 — reconstruction versus recursive feedback versus inherited condition

~~~text
Q4a reconstructed environmental / option condition
= current activity materially changes later environmental,
  institutional or action conditions.

Q4b recursive selective / viability feedback
= the reconstructed condition changes specified selection,
  viability, accessibility or transition pressures on later positions.

Q4c inherited reconstructed condition
= the reconstructed condition persists, is maintained or reproduced
  across a later cycle / generation such that later participants
  inherit a changed generative field.
~~~

Guards:

~~~text
Q4a != Q4b
Q4b != Q4c
long-term effect != recursive feedback
persistence != inheritance automatically
ecosystem engineering != evolution by niche construction automatically
~~~

These are candidate programme burdens only.

Mature institutional and evolutionary theories retain mechanism ownership.

Owner:
`Operations/Audits/THEORY-Q_FRAMEWORK-Q_QX3_QX4_SOURCE_PRESSURE_PASS1_2026-09-21.md`.

## 10. Generative expectation layer

A FRAMEWORK-Q relation may carry a structural expectation about its continuation or transformation.

Existing author adjudication remains:

~~~text
B = structural generative expectation
C = formed-locus anticipatory realization
C0 = embodied / enacted
C1 = model-mediated
~~~

Framework question:

~~~text
given relation R at declared position / scale,
what continuation, maintenance, transformation or failure
would count as relation-consistent?
~~~

This expectation must be typed.

Do not infer:

~~~text
expectation = prediction
expectation = goodness
expectation = moral legitimacy
~~~

## 11. Normativity interface

FRAMEWORK-Q supplies a possible source line for indexed normativity:

~~~text
formed relation
-> generative expectation
-> actual trajectory
-> indexed comparison
-> G-stronger / G-weaker / substitution / hollowing / destruction
~~~

The index must name:

- position;
- relation;
- scale;
- time horizon;
- affected bearers where established.

Social and institutional analysis must additionally ask:

- who receives benefits;
- who carries consequences;
- whose agency is reduced;
- which costs are externalized;
- which future options are closed;
- whether persistence is maintained by domination;
- whether the larger organization erases participant generativity.

Therefore:

~~~text
stable != legitimate
persistent != good
larger whole != morally prior
more options != always better
~~~

## 12. Civilizational learning architecture

Civilization is treated as a distributed historical grammar-learning process.

### 12.1 Input families

Civilizational experience includes:

- scientific results;
- technical artifacts;
- failed technologies;
- public health;
- markets;
- firms;
- states;
- law;
- institutions;
- wars and peace processes;
- ecological interventions;
- education;
- communities;
- family / interpersonal practice;
- moral and religious traditions;
- art and cultural memory.

These are not one homogeneous dataset.

### 12.2 Learning loop

~~~text
finite positions
-> experience
-> externalization / record
-> cross-position comparison
-> regularity / theory / institution
-> intervention
-> consequence
-> anomaly / conflict / failure
-> revision
-> improved or rejected grammar candidate
~~~

Institutions themselves can be understood as partially externalized generative expectations.

They stabilize some relations and suppress others.

This is a research hypothesis family, not a blanket normative endorsement of institutions.

## 13. Civilizational experience record

For social / historical use, record:

## 16. AI role

AI can help:

- retrieve sources;
- normalize terminology;
- generate candidate relation records;
- find cross-domain structural similarities;
- generate counterexamples;
- audit provenance;
- identify contradictions;
- update maps;
- propose tests.

AI cannot by model agreement alone:

- establish author intent;
- promote a relation to canonical ontology;
- settle contested empirical claims;
- assign moral legitimacy;
- turn analogy into evidence.

The framework should preserve machine-generated hypotheses as visible machine synthesis until supported.

## 17. Anti-collapse guards

### 17.1 Anti-tautology

If every event instantiates the relation, the relation is too weak.

Require exclusion / failure cases.

### 17.2 Anti-metaphor

Shared language is not shared structure.

### 17.3 Anti-domain-erasure

Do not flatten field mechanisms into THEORY-Q labels.

### 17.4 Anti-evidence-flattening

Historical experience, RCTs, formal proof and philosophical intuition have different inferential roles.

### 17.5 Anti-God-view

Every grammar learner is positioned and finite.

### 17.6 Anti-progress myth

Historical persistence is not evidence of moral superiority.

### 17.7 Anti-scale escape

A failed mapping cannot always be rescued by moving to a different scale.

## 18. Framework scorecard

Judge the framework itself on:

### Compression

Does it connect independently grounded relations without erasing mechanism?

### Constraint

Does it reject tempting mappings?

### Transfer

Does it generate a useful question / control / distinction before target outcome inspection?

### Revision

Does evidence actually change the grammar?

### Coverage

Can it represent natural, biological, cognitive, social and civilizational cases without reducing them to one evidence type?

### Position sensitivity

Can it represent differential consequences across positions?

### Normative discipline

Can it keep generative expectation distinct from moral legitimacy?

# Author programme convergence — FRAMEWORK-Q, civilizational learning, and generative normativity

> Role: author-owned source-intuition / programme-position record after the post-#976 dialogue.
>
> This file does not edit canonical ontology, establish scientific distinctiveness, prove a universal grammar, or close moral normativity. Its job is to preserve the current root intuition so later sessions do not drift back into novelty-first strongest-neighbor competition.

## 1. Founding intuition / telos

The author reports a long-standing intuition, present since childhood: by continuously collecting experience from social life, together with regularities distilled from experience in science, and organizing them through a sufficiently good architecture, humanity may move closer to what the author calls “神性” and gain a better basis for creating a better world.

In the present reconstruction, “神性” is used as a founding / book-level expression for a larger generative relational whole or generative relational grammar that no single finite position, discipline, theory, or historical slice exhausts.

This is an asymptotic research horizon, not a new ontic entity, deity theorem, final formula, or privileged God-view. The programme does not seek a position outside reality; it seeks progressively better participation in and understanding of the generative relations of which inquiry itself is a part.

## 2. What “大数据” means here

The author's “大数据” does not mean ordinary data-science scale, sample count, database size, or sensor volume.

It means the accumulated cross-position, cross-domain and cross-historical body of lived experience, social experience, scientific laws and empirical regularities, theories and paradigms, failures and anomalies, historical and institutional experience, and other stable patterns extracted from reality.

These are different-resolution experience slices / compressions of a wider generative reality.

The objective is not indiscriminate aggregation. It is to compare slices, retain source-native differences, identify recurring relational structures, expose failed objectifications, and let disagreement or anomaly revise the current grammar.

## 3. Why THEORY-Q uses Selection

Current author convergence:

Selection is not conscious choosing, not retrieval from a completed option menu, and not a hidden operator-object behind reality.

Selection is the process-language for non-flat generative change / differentiation: change in which relations become non-equivalent in operative efficacy, and in which resulting organization can alter later conditions of generation.

This is why THEORY-Q studies Selection / change / non-flat generation rather than taking already objectified regularities as the final research floor.

The intended inversion is:

objects + fixed laws → explain change

is replaced as the ultimate floor by:

generative relational change → formation of objects / boundaries / scales / positions → relatively stable effective regularities → later reconstruction of those conditions.

“Selection” is therefore intended as a process grammar for how determinate, non-equivalent reality forms and re-forms.

## 4. Objects, symbols, language, and laws

The author accepts the current programme reading that A, B, variables, symbols, categories, and ordinary language are finite coarse-grained / normalized / categorized interfaces, not self-grounding final ontology.

This does not make objects or laws unreal. Stable objects and laws can have real scale-local efficacy while remaining finite compressions of larger generative relations.

Accordingly, A → B should not automatically be read as discovery of a position-free, final, immutable object-law. It is a stable effective capture of generative organization under declared or backgrounded unit, boundary, scale, representation, and historical conditions.

## 5. Horizontal regularity and generative causal continuity

The current direction is stronger than a simple “horizontal causality versus vertical causality” dualism.

Horizontal / object-level regularity concerns relations among already stabilized units, variables and state spaces.

Generative continuity asks how those units, boundaries, causal capacities, scales and later regularities are formed, retained, recursively re-entered and reconstructed.

The long-term target is therefore a continuous account across differentiation, vertical / non-flat organization, retained efficacy, recurrent organization, One / position formation, scale-local effective causation, consequence / history / writeback, reconstruction of later selectability, and possible higher-order formation.

This is a programme architecture, not a completed universal causal theorem.

## 6. Unification target: generative relational grammar

The author does not primarily seek one equation or one objectified law that replaces domain science.

The unification target is a more general generative relational grammar (FRAMEWORK-Q) that can preserve recurring dependency structures across domains while leaving domain mechanisms genuinely different.

Current working burdens include: how difference becomes operatively non-equivalent; how manifestation and relative backgrounding are coupled; how prior differentiation remains effective; how recurrent organization forms a relatively separable One / position; how formed positions alter later selectability; how multiple positions jointly reconstruct relation-level conditions; how higher-order structures re-enter lower-level future generation; and how inquiry itself learns and revises these relations.

This list is provisional, not exhaustive.

## 7. Mature neighbors as contributors and reciprocal constraints

The programme no longer treats whole-THEORY-Q comparison with a single strongest neighbor as its central identity.

Whitehead, Simondon, Barad, enactivist / participatory traditions, synergetics, and other mature theories may be treated as historically distinct high-quality compressions of different parts of generative reality.

Preferred workflow:

source-native theory / domain result → extract the relational burden it actually pays → preserve domain-specific mechanism → compare across positions / domains → identify convergence, mismatch and missing distinctions → revise candidate FRAMEWORK-Q → transfer a bounded relation into a new domain → let the new domain confirm, narrow, split or defeat the mapping.

Neighbor overlap is neither THEORY-Q defeat nor automatic THEORY-Q confirmation. A neighbor genuinely strengthens the programme only if it is allowed to constrain or revise THEORY-Q.

Whole-package novelty, superiority and non-substitutability remain NOT ESTABLISHED and are not the current root question.

## 8. THEORY-Q as grammar-learning architecture

Current author convergence supports:

THEORY-Q is not a final completed FRAMEWORK-Q handed down in advance.

THEORY-Q is a self-revising architecture for learning, comparing, testing and improving candidate generative relational grammar.

Programme success therefore requires more than retrospective relabeling. At minimum it should achieve explanatory compression, mapping constraints, cross-domain transfer that changes a question / control / distinction before the target result is known, and self-revision when domain evidence or mature theory pressures the grammar.

If THEORY-Q can only rename known results after the fact, programme payoff is not established.

## 9. Generative expectation and normativity

The author adds the explicit clarification:

> “generative relational grammar提供的生成的预期便是normativity。”

This is recorded as a substantive continuation of the 2026-09-14 generativity-first normativity adjudication.

Minimum author-directed reading:

FRAMEWORK-Q is not only a retrospective description of how generation happened. Once a formed position / relation can model, inherit or participate in a generative grammar, the grammar provides expectations about how generation can continue, what relations can remain generative, and how present Selection changes later generative conditions.

That generative expectation is the source line from which normative direction emerges.

This strengthens, rather than replaces, the current canonical routing that Selection is a source-condition of normativity because formed and relational Selection can alter the conditions under which later Selection proceeds.

The new emphasis is:

generative relation → expectation of generative continuation / transformation → indexed comparison of actual versus generatively implied / supportable continuation → normative direction becomes possible.

### Anti-collapse guard

This direction does not by itself establish that whatever occurs is good, whatever persists ought to persist, maximum order is good, maximum continuation is good, maximum option count is good, primitive O0 already contains morality, one global future is objectively best, statistical prediction is automatically normative, or every formed expectation is morally legitimate.

The 2026-09-14 distinction remains active:

primitive directionality != formed-position-relative good / bad != full moral ought.

Mature normative claims still need typed position / relation / scale, consequences and cost return, non-substitution / non-outsourcing where relevant, revision / reselection, independently established higher-order wholes where invoked, and explicit handling of genuine cross-position moral conflict.

Generative expectation is therefore the proposed source of normative direction, not a shortcut around O1 / O2-C / O2-A / O2-M gates.

## 10. Knowledge, civilization, and practical horizon

The founding telos can now be rendered as:

finite positions → lived and scientific experience → externalized memory / laws / theories / institutions → cross-position comparison → better candidate FRAMEWORK-Q → better anticipation of generative consequences → better capacity to reconstruct future selectable conditions.

Civilization is relevant as a distributed grammar-learning process.

The practical aspiration is not maximum order. A safer current reading is to use deeper generative understanding to build orders that can remain generative: coordinate real differences, keep consequence channels open, avoid hidden cost export, and retain capacity for reselection / reconstruction under sustained mismatch.

The existing practical guard family remains useful: 可延续 / 可协调 / 不外包 / 可再选择. These are not a complete morality and do not authorize political or social prescriptions without separate normative argument.

# Author second adjudication — typing generative expectation

> Role: second author-adjudication record for the post-#976 FRAMEWORK-Q programme.
>
> This record consolidates the already author-owned 2026-09-14 generativity-first normativity direction and the 2026-09-20 FRAMEWORK-Q founding clarification. It does not edit canonical normativity owners, close full moral ought, or establish a universal theorem.

## 1. Question returned by red-team Pass 1

The red-team separated four possible meanings of “生成的预期”:

A. statistical / predictive expectation;

B. the immanent directional structure of generation itself;

C. an anticipatory model held by a formed position;

D. B + C.

The central problem is to preserve the author's claim that FRAMEWORK-Q is a source line of normativity without collapsing prediction into ought, persistence into good, or explicit cognition into the primitive ontology.

## 2. Second adjudication

~~~
VERDICT = B + C, ASYMMETRIC / LAYERED
~~~

B is the basal programme-level meaning.

C is retained as a downstream formed-position realization, but it is broadened beyond explicit predictive modelling.

The dependency is:

~~~
B — structural generative expectation
= an actually formed generative relation has an immanent directional burden:
  some continuations / transformations preserve, realize, weaken, substitute,
  hollow out or destroy the typed relation differently.

then, where a formed locus exists:

C — anticipatory realization
= a formed position / relation can inherit, embody, enact, regulate by,
  or explicitly model that generative direction in organizing later generation.
~~~

Therefore:

~~~
B does not require C in order to exist as structural direction.

C presupposes enough formed organization to make anticipation operative from somewhere.

indexed normative comparison requires a typed evaluative locus / relation;
it is not primitive undifferentiated good/bad.

full moral legitimacy remains separately open.
~~~

The arrows here are dependency / reconstruction arrows, not a claim of pregiven temporal sequence.

## 3. C must not be reduced to explicit representation

The phrase “anticipatory model held by a formed position” is too narrow if it implies that expectation becomes real only when an agent explicitly represents a future state.

Retain two realizations:

~~~
C0 — embodied / enacted anticipation:
present organization is differentially regulated by conditions of its possible continuation
without requiring an explicit symbolic predictive model.

C1 — model-mediated anticipation:
a formed position explicitly or functionally models possible later states,
policies, consequences or continuations and uses that model in present organization.
~~~

C1 is a stronger downstream realization, not the definition of generative expectation.

Neither C0 nor C1 implies consciousness or phenomenality.

## 4. Why B+C fits the prior author adjudication

The 2026-09-14 adjudication already fixed:

~~~
primitive directionality
!= position-relative better / worse

formed-position-relative generativity preference
= legitimate reconstruction direction

consequence-bearing / non-outsourcing
!= primitive normative ground
~~~

The 2026-09-20 author source then added that a formed position / relation may model, inherit or participate in FRAMEWORK-Q and thereby gain expectations about continuation and transformation.

B+C preserves both decisions:

- B carries the more basic generative direction without requiring a prior subject;
- C explains how that direction becomes operative anticipation from a formed locus;
- indexed G-stronger / G-weaker comparison belongs after a position / relation / scale has been typed;
- stronger constitutive, responsibility and moral claims still pay their own gates.

B = complete morality
C = consciousness

whole-relative value already established

does the trajectory strengthen, preserve, weaken, substitute, hollow out or destroy

G7d still does not establish full moral legitimacy.

domain mechanism != FRAMEWORK-Q relation != THEORY-Q canonical definition.

## 2. Provisional grammar burdens

The following burdens are working slots, not ontology modules and not an exhaustive ladder.

### G0 — non-exhaustion / open remainder

Question: does current determinate organization exhaust the conditions relevant to further generation?

Purpose: prevent a completed object inventory from being silently presupposed.

Guard: open remainder does not imply randomness, a hidden possibility warehouse, or a fixed future menu.

### G1 — differential actualisation

Question: how does a difference become operatively non-equivalent rather than remain flat change?

Target: distinguish genuine efficacy difference from trivial A / not-A logical contrast.

Guard: “something happened” is insufficient.

### G2 — coupled foreground / background organization

Question: when something becomes operative, what happens to competing, supporting, excluded or background relations in the same generative process?

Target: background must have relational / generative content, not merely logical non-actuality.

### G3 — retained efficacy

Question: when does prior differentiation materially alter later generative conditions?

Target: distinguish one-shot occurrence from history.

Guard: irreversibility alone != durable history.

### G4 — recurrent organization

Question: when does retained organization re-enter later generation in a role that can itself recur?

Target: operation → organization → later operation.

Guard: ordinary path dependence or repeated correlation is insufficient.

### G5 — One / local generative locus formation

Question: when does recurrent organization become relatively separable enough to count as a formed local from-where?

Target: explain formation of units instead of presupposing units.

Guard: transient pattern, attractor or analyst grouping != One automatically.

### G6 — positioned generation

Question: when does a formed locality materially condition what becomes manifest, backgrounded, reachable or selectable from there?

Target: position as operative generative relation, not coordinate label.

Guard: index variable != formed position.

### G7 — consequence exposure, generative expectation and anticipatory realization

Second author adjudication: B+C, asymmetric / layered.

Working split:

- G7a — consequence exposure / return: what generated consequences re-enter the same formed relation / position?
- G7b — structural generative expectation (B): what continuation / transformation is immanently implied by the typed generative relation?
- G7c — formed-locus anticipatory realization (C): how is that direction embodied, enacted, inherited or modelled from a formed locus?
  - C0 = embodied / enacted anticipation;
  - C1 = model-mediated anticipation.
- G7d — indexed normative comparison: relative to an admitted position / relation / scale, does the trajectory strengthen, preserve, weaken, substitute, hollow out or destroy the relevant generative relation / capacity?

Target: connect generativity, anticipation and normativity without collapsing structural direction into prediction.

Guards:

- B does not require an explicit predictive model.
- C is downstream of enough formed organization to make anticipation operative from somewhere.
- C0/C1 != consciousness or phenomenality.
- prediction != normativity automatically.
- exposure != full moral standing.
- G7d != full moral legitimacy.

### G8 — reconstructive Selection

Question: can a formed position alter the organization that governs later differentiation / selectability itself?

Target: distinguish change under fixed rules from change of generative conditions.

Guard: ordinary adaptation or state update is insufficient unless the target organization is specified.

### G9 — multi-position / relation-level generation

Question: can multiple formed positions jointly constitute a relation-level organization that changes later selectability for the participants?

Target: relation-level grammar without prematurely positing a collective subject.

Guard: coordination != higher-order One / Bearer / subject automatically.

### G10 — cross-scale recursion

Question: when does a newly formed higher-order organization become a real later condition on lower-level generation?

Target: replace magic “emergence” with a continuous generative bridge.

Guard: neither micro-reduction nor mystical downward causation.

### G11 — reflexive grammar learning

Question: when can formed positions collectively model, test and revise the generative relations of which they are participants?

Target: place science, philosophy, civilization and AI-assisted inquiry inside the same generative picture.

Guard: no God-view exemption; grammar learner remains finite and revisable.

## 3. Horizontal floor / generative continuity interface

FRAMEWORK-Q does not reject object-level science.

Object-level laws and causal models are treated as effective, scale-bound stabilizations over already objectified units, variables, boundaries and state spaces.

The FRAMEWORK-Q question is upstream and recursive:

what generative organization makes these units and effective relations stable enough to support the horizontal model, and what happens when that organization changes?

Working cycle:

generation → formation → effective object-level relation → consequence → retained difference → reconstruction → renewed generation.

The programme must avoid saying that every conditional probability or correlation is “horizontal causality”. Modern causal inference retains its own intervention, counterfactual and mechanism standards.

## 4. Objectification interface

Every domain mapping should declare when relevant:

- what is treated as a unit;
- what boundary is fixed;
- what grain / scale is selected;
- what differences are normalized;
- what categories are imposed;
- what history is compressed away;
- what position / interface is removed;
- what residual mismatch remains;
- what evidence would force the objectification to reopen.

Objects are not declared unreal. The question is whether the current objectification is sufficient for the research target.

## 6. FRAMEWORK-Q extraction rule

A candidate FRAMEWORK-Q relation must not be created after seeing one matching example and then declared universal.

Before cross-domain transfer, freeze the candidate relation in neutral language.

Then test it in at least one new domain without changing its core meaning to fit the result.

A successful transfer must preserve:

- dependency direction / organization;
- admission conditions;
- non-identities;
- withdrawal conditions.

If these drift substantially, record semantic failure rather than “flexible unification”.

## 7. Anti-tautology red-team

Primary threat:

Selection / FRAMEWORK-Q becomes so broad that every change is redescribed as Selection.

Required tests:

- distinguish real relational efficacy from trivial logical contrast;
- require explicit changed accessibility / support / inhibition / constraint / continuation conditions;
- require a counterfactual or perturbation where removing the claimed coupling changes formation or later generative conditions;
- reject mappings where no domain-native consequence changes;
- reject “same grammar” claims that survive only by changing definitions case by case.

Key red-team question:

what observation would show that this event is ordinary state change but not the claimed FRAMEWORK-Q relation?

## 8. Anti-reduction red-team

FRAMEWORK-Q depth is generative dependency depth, not “smaller scale is more real”.

A higher-order structure can become a genuine later Selection condition after formation.

Therefore:

lower-scale realization does not eliminate higher-order generative efficacy.

Higher-order efficacy does not license a mysterious extra force.

Required cross-scale explanation:

distributed lower-level relations → formation mechanism → retained higher-order organization → demonstrable change in later lower-level / participant conditions.

## 9. Anti-relativism / anti-anti-realism red-team

Objects and laws being objectified slices does not imply arbitrary construction.

Objectivity remains a cross-position / cross-interface / cross-perturbation achievement.

FRAMEWORK-Q must permit:

- robust structures that survive changes of representation;
- domain-local laws with strong invariance;
- constraints not produced by the observer;
- failure of an objectification due to world resistance.

Working distinction:

objectification is finite and revisable;
reality is not therefore infinitely malleable.

## 10. Anti-teleology / anti-order-absolutism red-team

FRAMEWORK-Q must not smuggle a predetermined endpoint into generation.

Forbidden shortcuts:

- what persists is good;
- what is more ordered is better;
- more options is always better;
- larger whole has priority;
- maximum generativity is automatically moral good.

Generative expectation provides a source line for normative direction only after indexed positions / relations and additional normative burdens are specified.

## 11. Normativity interface

Current author direction:

generative relational grammar provides expectations of generation, and these generative expectations are the source line of normativity.

Programme decomposition:

FRAMEWORK-Q relation → expected generative continuation / transformation → comparison of actual trajectory with the relation's generative burden → indexed G-stronger / G-weaker relation → downstream constitutive / responsibility / moral routing where additional conditions are paid.

This remains compatible with the existing O1 / O2-C / O2-A / O2-M separation.

Research questions now include:

- what is the minimum form of generative expectation?
- must expectation be explicitly represented, or can it be structurally embodied?
- when does mismatch become normative rather than merely predictive?
- how are conflicting expectations across positions typed?
- how can a higher-order whole acquire evaluative standing without erasing participants?
- what distinguishes a generatively stable domination structure from a legitimate relation?

Do not answer these by definition.

## 12. Civilizational FRAMEWORK-Q learning

The programme treats social life, science, philosophy, technology, institutional history and mature theories as differently weighted records of generative experience.

They do not have equal evidential standing.

Empirical and formal evidence constrains mechanisms more strongly than metaphor.

Repeated cross-cultural or philosophical convergence can still function as candidate grammar signal, not proof.

Civilizational learning loop:

finite positions → experience → externalization → cross-position comparison → theory / law / paradigm → intervention → consequence → anomaly / revision → improved FRAMEWORK-Q candidate.

AI may assist retrieval, comparison, abstraction and transfer, but it must not become a God-view oracle or substitute model agreement for author / evidence convergence.

## 14. Programme scorecard

A FRAMEWORK-Q candidate is retained only if it scores positively on all four programme functions:

Compression — does it connect previously separated results without erasing mechanism?

Constraint — can it reject some tempting mappings?

Transfer — does it change an unseen domain question / design / expectation?

Revision — can external evidence modify the grammar itself?

Optional fifth criterion:

Generative continuity — does it clarify how one effective layer forms and becomes a condition for another, rather than inserting “emergence” as an unexplained arrow?

## 15. Failure conditions

The FRAMEWORK-Q programme should be narrowed or abandoned if repeated work shows that:

- candidate relations are only renamed domain mechanisms;
- the grammar cannot specify failure conditions;
- every mapping requires semantic drift;
- cross-domain transfer adds no new question / control / inference;
- domain mechanisms cannot be preserved without collapsing into one vocabulary;
- normativity claims require independent premises unrelated to the proposed generative expectation;
- the framework becomes immune to counterexample by moving every mismatch into “another scale” or “another position”;
- THEORY-Q cannot be revised by the very evidence it claims to integrate.

# THEORY-Q Constitution + Domain Architecture v2

## Add the Domain Reconstruction Layer between Constitution and deep wells

> **Author decision, 2026-09-04.** Constitution v1 is complete, but the programme should not jump directly from constitutional reader-operations to traditional domain theories, datasets and experiments. Each selected domain first requires a domain-level reconstruction surface derived from the Constitution, the domain's own starting objects, the repository's inherited domain assets, and the full relevant material record.

This is an architecture correction, not a new constitutional layer and not a return to domain axiom towers.

---

## 0. The repaired architecture

The active programme architecture is now:

```text
LAYER 1 — THEORY-Q Constitution
cross-domain reader operations / explanatory burdens
no domain mechanism or empirical result asserted here

        ↓

LAYER 2 — Domain Reconstruction Framework
Constitution × domain starting picture × inherited THEORY-Q assets × materials
reconstructs the domain's problem-space, objectification map,
bearer/position architecture and research families

        ↓

LAYER 3 — Domain Theory / Hypothesis / Model
specific mechanisms, formalizations, proxies and candidate explanations

        ↓

LAYER 4 — Deep Wells / Evidence
bounded questions, strongest-baseline comparison,
experiments / data / archives / historical tests / Case A-B-C gates

        ↓

LAYER 5 — Productive writeback and eventual cross-domain synthesis
framework writeback occurs after each completed or legitimately blocked deep well;
cross-domain synthesis does not begin until at least two deep wells have independently produced usable results
```

The key correction is the insertion of **Layer 2**.

Old shortcut:

```text
Constitution
-> deep-well charter
-> data / literature / experiment
```

New route:

```text
Constitution
-> Domain Reconstruction Framework
-> specific domain hypothesis / deep-well selection
-> evidence
-> framework writeback
```

## 1. What a Domain Reconstruction Framework is

A Domain Reconstruction Framework is a **domain-level THEORY-Q problem map**.

It answers:

> If Constitution v1 is brought into this domain before any one experiment, dataset or favorite theory is selected, how does the domain's problem-space get reorganized?

It is not:

- a second Constitution;
- a domain axiom system;
- a claim that Constitution deductively derives the domain;
- a literature review;
- a deep-well protocol;
- a substitute for the field's strongest existing theories;
- a place to promote old THEORY-Q formalisms by default.

The preferred role is:

```text
Constitution supplies executable perspective operations
+
domain supplies its actual objects, methods and constraints
+
repository/material history supplies inherited evidence, hypotheses and failures

->

Domain Reconstruction Framework organizes the resulting question-space
```

## 8. Cross-domain synthesis boundary

Domain Reconstruction Frameworks make later synthesis more disciplined because they provide an intermediate comparison unit.

**Threshold:** framework writeback is allowed after each deep well, but cross-domain synthesis remains blocked until at least **two** deep wells have independently produced usable results, preserving the existing Execution Plan Phase-10 gate.

Future synthesis should compare, for example:

```text
repeated problem families
repeated objectification losses
repeated bearer / history separations
repeated consequence-attribution failures
repeated translation-only outcomes
repeated domain-specific divergences
```

But cross-domain recurrence is still an empirical/programmatic result, not something inserted into every framework in advance.

---

## 9. Immediate migration order

The recommended migration is:

```text
1. land this architecture correction
2. build Neuroscience Reconstruction Framework v0.1
   using Constitution + inherited neuroscience assets + first-well result + relevant materials
3. build Epistemology Reconstruction Framework v0.1
   using Constitution + philosophy/Forcing-CH assets + relevant materials
4. locate the current Epistemology method-identity well inside its parent problem family
5. resume evidence work only after that parent structure is clear
```

No existing deep-well adverse result is erased by this migration.

## 10. Success condition for the new layer

The Domain Reconstruction Layer is doing real work only if it can answer all of the following before a new deep well begins:

```text
What does this field treat as given?
How do the six Constitution operations translate here?
What objectification choices matter?
Who/what occupies which bearer / measurement / consequence roles?
What stable problem families result?
Where do inherited THEORY-Q files belong now?
Where do high-value SourceCards / patches / hooks belong now?
What mature field traditions already own nearby distinctions?
Which one bounded deep well is worth paying for next?
```

If a framework cannot answer these without immediately turning into a literature dump or a domain axiom system, it has failed its role and should be simplified.

# FRAMEWORK-Q Civilizational Learning Architecture v0.1

## 0. Question

How can accumulated social and civilizational experience become a disciplined input to FRAMEWORK-Q without turning history into anecdote or morality into persistence?

## 1. Civilization as distributed learning

Working programme hypothesis:

~~~text
civilization
= many finite positions
  externalizing experience,
  preserving partial regularities,
  creating institutions,
  intervening on future conditions,
  receiving consequences,
  and revising inherited expectations.
~~~

This is a research perspective, not a claim that civilization is one subject or one Bearer.

## 2. Civilizational memory surfaces

Relevant memory is distributed across:

- scientific literature;
- law;
- administrative records;
- markets and prices;
- firms and organizations;
- infrastructure;
- technologies;
- education;
- customs;
- moral traditions;
- religion;
- philosophy;
- art;
- oral and written history;
- family / community practice;
- ecological landscapes;
- failures and disasters.

These surfaces have different biases and survival processes.

What survives in an archive is not automatically what mattered most.

## 3. Civilizational Experience Record

Each case should record:

~~~text
case =
period =
geography / institutional setting =
positions / participants =
initial generative relation =
rule / institution / technology =
intervention / shock =
expected continuation =
actual short-term consequence =
actual long-term consequence =
distribution of consequence =
externalized cost =
adaptation / resistance =
relation strengthened =
relation weakened =
relation substituted / hollowed =
evidence sources =
counterevidence =
alternative explanation =
~~~

## 4. Evidence discipline

Civilizational evidence should separate:

~~~text
event existence
causal attribution
mechanism
distributional consequence
long-run consequence
normative interpretation
~~~

A case may have strong evidence for the event and weak evidence for causality.

Historical recurrence may nominate a FRAMEWORK-Q candidate.

It does not prove universal law.

## 5. Institutional reading

An institution may be analyzed as:

~~~text
externalized generative expectation
+
enforcement / incentive structure
+
memory / classification system
+
allocation of consequence
~~~

Questions:

- what behavior / relation does it make easier?
- what does it make costly or inaccessible?
- what history does it preserve?
- whose position is encoded?
- who receives consequences?
- what costs are exported outside its accounting boundary?
- can participants revise it?
- does it preserve its own operation by destroying participant generativity?

Institutional stability is therefore not a sufficient positive normative result.

## 6. Social relation analysis

For social systems, FRAMEWORK-Q should distinguish:

~~~text
coordination
cooperation
dependency
domination
extraction
reciprocity
care
competition
collective action
institutional maintenance
~~~

These cannot be collapsed into generic "relation".

For each, the causal and normative burdens differ.

## 7. Generative health without moral shortcut

Candidate descriptive questions:

- does the relation reproduce its enabling conditions?
- does it require hidden depletion?
- does it export irreversible cost?
- does it maintain participant agency?
- can it adapt without destroying its participants?
- does it monopolize future selectability?
- does it produce fragile dependence?
- does it increase or reduce correction capacity?

These questions can support indexed G-stronger / G-weaker analysis.

They do not by themselves settle justice, rights or moral legitimacy.

## 8. Multi-position normativity

Civilizational analysis must permit:

~~~text
relation beneficial from position A
and harmful from position B
~~~

Therefore every normative record should state:

- whose expectation;
- whose continuation;
- whose cost;
- which scale;
- which horizon.

A whole-level gain cannot silently cancel participant-level destruction.

Participant interest cannot automatically veto every higher-order coordination structure.

Both require explicit burdens.

## 9. Civilization-level conflicts

Important research families include:

### C1 — local success / global depletion

A relation works locally by exporting costs to ecological, temporal or social background.

### C2 — short-term efficiency / long-term option loss

Optimization narrows future selectability.

### C3 — institutional persistence / participant generativity

An institution survives by reducing participants' capacity to revise it.

### C4 — coordination / domination ambiguity

Stable coordination may encode asymmetrical consequence-bearing.

### C5 — innovation / continuity tension

Novelty increases option space while destroying valuable accumulated organization.

### C6 — knowledge / power coupling

Objectification and classification improve coordination but also control what becomes visible or governable.

### C7 — technological mediation

Technology changes not only choices but the field of future choices.

These are programme problem families, not established universal laws.

## 10. Comparative civilizational learning

Do not ask only:

~~~text
Which society / institution won?
~~~

Ask:

~~~text
What relation was instantiated?
Under what constraints?
What consequences appeared at which horizons?
What hidden support was required?
What revision mechanisms existed?
What failed when conditions changed?
~~~

Comparisons should preserve:

- path dependence;
- geography;
- technology;
- power;
- ecology;
- demography;
- measurement differences.

## 11. Scientific knowledge inside civilization

Science itself is part of the civilizational learning loop.

It provides unusually strong procedures for:

- explicit objectification;
- replication;
- causal testing;
- formal constraint;
- public error correction.

But scientific objectification remains finite.

FRAMEWORK-Q should neither demote science to "one narrative" nor turn current scientific objects into the final ontology floor.

## 12. AI and civilizational memory

AI can make a new scale of cross-record comparison possible.

Potential role:

~~~text
retrieve
-> normalize
-> link
-> compare
-> generate candidate relation
-> find counterexample
-> update atlas
~~~

Risks:

- archive bias amplification;
- false equivalence;
- dominant-language bias;
- evidence flattening;
- hallucinated convergence;
- hidden normative assumptions;
- over-centralized epistemic authority.

AI must remain auditable and corrigible.

## 12.1 First source-pressure writeback

The first civilizational cases and mature-source pressure require two explicit burden decompositions.

### Expectation regime

~~~text
Q3a articulated / encoded expectation
!=
Q3b operative expectation regime
~~~

An institution, protocol or norm is not causally paid merely because it is represented.

Record how it becomes operative through typed embodiment / enforcement provenance.

### Reconstructed future conditions

~~~text
Q4a reconstructed condition
!=
Q4b recursive selective / viability feedback
!=
Q4c inherited reconstructed condition
~~~

Long-run consequence alone is insufficient for the stronger recursive or inheritance burdens.

These are programme candidates, not new canonical ontology.

## 13. Outputs

Initial civilizational programme outputs should be:

1. a case-record schema;
2. a domain/civilization atlas;
3. a relation library;
4. a cross-case comparison matrix;
5. a generative-expectation ledger;
6. a conflict / multi-position ledger;
7. calibration cases where the framework is likely to fail.

## 14. First implementation rule

Do not begin by trying to encode all of world history.

Seed the architecture with a small diverse set:

- one scientific/technical case;
- one biological/ecological case;
- one market/institution case;
- one governance/law case;
- one community/social case;
- one long-duration civilizational case.

Choose cases with reasonably strong source access and explicit failure pressure.

The purpose of the first set is to test the schema, not to prove a theory of civilization.

## 15. Canonical and political guard

This architecture is a research programme.

It is not:

- a political ideology;
- an endorsement of a party / regime;
- a policy-ranking engine;
- a moral scoring system;
- a claim of civilizational superiority.

Policy or institutional recommendations require separate evidence and explicit normative premises.
