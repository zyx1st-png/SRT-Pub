## B. Research question / objectification

Research question:

> Does a training-history-sensitive recurrent spectral organization merely record prior history, or does the selected organization materially mediate later learning?

Stabilized objectification:

~~~text

boundary = trained recurrent system
grain = whole recurrent operator

later process = subsequent learning trajectory
~~~

~~~text

a special vertical causal variable.
~~~

What is reopened:

~~~text

enter later learning as a specific causal condition?
~~~
