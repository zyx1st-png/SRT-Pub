## Domain A — Active inference

Source: `Materials/2026/SRC_2026_06_11_Philosophy_ActiveInference_FEP_Book_MITPress.md`.

### A1 — Generative model in operation

Target question:

~~~text
An active-inference agent has a declared generative model with hidden states,
observations, policies and preferred outcomes.
What explanatory burden is carried by inference and policy selection inside that model?
~~~

Source-native facts:
- perception/action are treated as inference under a generative model;
- preferred conditions are encoded as priors;
- the process theory operates over declared model variables and conditional relations.

### A2 — Learned prior carried forward

Target question:

~~~text
If earlier learning changes priors that later alter inference or policy choice,
what explanatory burden is carried by that later-effective learned prior?
~~~

Source-native fact:
- priors are part of the agent model and can organize later inference/action.
