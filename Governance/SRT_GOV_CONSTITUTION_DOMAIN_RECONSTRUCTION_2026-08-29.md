# GOV-CONST01 — Constitution + Domain Reconstruction Governance

## 0. Scope

This protocol governs the current identity-level reconstruction of SRT.

It does **not** itself change canonical definitions. Existing freeze/edit rules remain in force. Where this protocol and an older active execution plan conflict on current programme direction, the 2026-08-29 Constitution + Domain blueprint controls programme sequencing; canonical authority remains unchanged until separately edited.

---

## 1. Identity freeze rule

Current programme identity:

```text
SRT Constitution = bearer-involved perspective framework
Domain research = formalization / evidence / testing layer
```

Freeze this identity through one Constitution-v1 + one-domain-deep-well cycle.

Do not reopen because:

- a newer phrase sounds cleaner;
- an AI proposes another architecture;
- a neighboring theory creates novelty anxiety;
- a formal model looks temporarily promising.

Reopen only under the explicit conditions in the blueprint.

This rule directly addresses latest-idea drift and AI over-accommodation.

---

## 2. AI role separation

To prevent document inflation and model agreement masquerading as author judgment:

### Divergence role

AI may:

- generate alternatives;
- expose assumptions;
- propose counterexamples;
- compare orderings;
- identify likely neighbors;
- summarize existing repo material.

AI must not:

- fill in the author’s final choice;
- infer that the latest proposal is superior merely because it is latest;
- create a repository artifact for every branch of exploration;
- convert agreement between two models into proof.

### Convergence role

Only explicit author selection/rejection/hybridization closes a constitutional question.

### Red-team role

A model acting as red-team should actively search for:

- internal contradiction;
- hidden premise;
- circularity;
- reflexive self-exemption;
- prior mature solution;
- overclaim;
- domain mismatch.

A red-team output is not a new author direction by itself.

---

## 3. No-write-before-convergence rule

During Constitution dialogue:

```text
AI divergence
-> author discussion
-> author convergence event
-> only then repository write
```

Do not create new Constitution drafts while the author is still choosing between live alternatives.

Exception: bounded source-recovery notes may be written if they contain no new author position and have a clear stop condition. This exception is additionally constrained by the Phase-1 artifact budget in §14.

---

## 4. Constitution non-formalization guard

Constitution-level authority must not depend on:

- equations;
- scalar thresholds;
- natural-coordinate claims;
- fully specified state spaces;
- formal proof chains.

Reason: these are forms of objectification and public normalization whose assumptions are part of what the Constitution seeks to reopen.

This does not prohibit mathematical commentary or domain formalization. It prohibits using a formalization as the constitutional floor before the relevant unit/boundary/grain/bearer relation has been authorially clarified.

---

## 5. Reflexivity gate

Every substantive Constitution thesis must survive:

1. **Position disclosure** — from what perspective is the claim being proposed?
2. **No God-view exemption** — does the claim secretly assert position-free authority over all positions?
3. **Re-enactability** — can a reader from another position perform the proposed perspective operation?
4. **Domain consequence** — is there a plausible way the perspective could matter outside the author’s private description?

A thesis that can only be accepted by granting the author privileged access fails this gate.

---

## 6. Ontology/methodology separation gate

Always distinguish:

```text
A. bearer-/position-indexed relations may be ontologically constitutive
B. inquiry itself should retain participatory/bearer information
```

A does not automatically imply B.

If B is claimed, state the preservation argument:

> the method should not eliminate a possibly constitutive relation before testing whether it matters.

Do not generalize this into “all science must be participatory.”

---

## 7. Reader-entry operation gate

A substantive Constitution item should specify an executable perspective operation.

Minimum:

```text
Default suspended
Reader action
What becomes visible
Boundary
```

If an item only asserts a worldview sentence and no reader can do anything with it, mark it `proposition_only` and keep it out of Constitution v1 until the author decides whether it belongs in commentary instead.

---

## 8. Two-pass neighbor discipline

Neighbor work has **two different purposes** and therefore two different passes.

### Pass A — Light Neighbor Awareness, before author convergence

Run after repository source recovery / AI divergence but before the author freezes wording.

Purpose:

- prevent accidental rediscovery of a mature formulation in avoidable ignorance;
- improve terminology and problem precision;
- warn the author about an obvious inherited distinction or failure before wording hardens.

Rules:

- read-only;
- bounded to the few most obvious mature neighbors;
- no novelty score;
- no permission gate;
- no `resonance/contrast/pressure/translation` adjudication yet;
- no repository artifact by default;
- report source-native claims in their own vocabulary rather than translating them into SRT.

A Light Neighbor pass may say “Husserl/Nagel/Haraway already make a nearby move in this respect”; it may not say “therefore SRT must drop/keep this claim.”

### Pass B — Full Neighbor Map, after author convergence and internal red-team

Purpose:

- prevent rediscovering known failures;
- supply competent-reader coordinates;
- distinguish resonance from real difference;
- inherit mature vocabulary and objections;
- prepare external intelligibility after the author knows what they actually mean.

Required labels:

```text
resonance
contrast
pressure
translation
```

Not purpose:

- grant permission to think;
- score novelty as a constitutional admission gate;
- make external authority prove Constitution.

If a novelty claim is later needed, run a separate novelty/increment audit.

The two passes must not collapse into one: Pass A serves **expression precision / blind-spot reduction**; Pass B serves **comparative positioning / external legibility**.

---

## 9. Domain increment gate

A domain may freely use SRT as a re-framing tool, but it may claim “SRT adds X” only after the field’s strongest baseline is checked.

Reuse existing bounded-probe logic:

- Case A: baseline already provides the gain -> STOP SRT increment claim;
- Case B: SRT improves routing/reframing but not content -> translation/process contribution only;
- Case C: SRT yields a new bounded distinction/question with domain consequences -> proceed to test;
- unresolved -> retain as open question, no superiority language.

This is the primary future role of subtraction/novelty machinery.

---

## 10. Bearer–Objectification Declaration gate

Any domain model used to support an SRT-sensitive claim should declare, at minimum:

```text
unit
boundary
grain
equivalence
normalization
history loss
position loss
bearer substitution assumption
consequence attribution
participation
```

The declaration is diagnostic, not proof and not yet a universal publication standard.

A model can remain useful even if it intentionally discards bearer-specific information; the requirement is to say what was discarded rather than treat the projection as ontologically neutral.

---

## 11. Sigma worked-example guard

The sigma probes may be used as an example of **premature de-indexing / under-declared objectification**.

Allowed:

- coordinate sensitivity;
- attribution ambiguity;
- provenance/control dissociation;
- boundary-relative semantics.

Forbidden inference:

```text
sigma probe failure
-> mathematics/formalism cannot express indexicality
```

That conclusion is stronger than the evidence.

## 13. Manuscript carve-out

Never use this reconstruction to silently change:

- already-published Frontiers claims;
- the submitted Adaptive Behavior manuscript;
- historical manuscript-local meanings of equations/variables.

Prospective repository interpretation can change without pretending the old paper said something it did not say.

## 14. Document-load control and Phase-1 artifact budget

Anti-inflation governance is only real if the next phase produces materially fewer artifacts than the blueprint round.

Persistent limits:

- one active identity blueprint;
- one active execution plan;
- one active governance protocol;
- source/decision traces only when a real author convergence occurs;
- no per-thesis programme plan;
- no new router/index unless another is retired or repurposed.

### Phase-1 quantitative budget

For **Phase 1 source recovery** specifically:

```text
new programme / governance / map / router / index files = 0
new standalone human-authored recovery working files = target 0, hard cap 1 total
per-intuition files = 0
Light Neighbor Awareness files = 0 by default
```

Use existing files first:

- `SRT_CONSTITUTION_SOURCE_RECOVERY_MAP_2026-08-29.md` for routing;
- existing ChoiceTrace / author-trace surfaces for real decisions;
- conversation output for temporary recovery tables and Light Neighbor notes.

A single rolling recovery packet is allowed only if conversation-only handling becomes genuinely unwieldy; it must replace scattered notes rather than accompany them.

Generated context-bundle refreshes do not count as new conceptual artifacts, but they also do not justify creating new human-authored documents.

### Failure condition

If Phase 1 exceeds the hard cap, stop retrieval and consolidate before doing more work. Do not solve the breach by creating another anti-inflation document.

The operational success criterion is therefore observable:

> Phase 1 must end with **zero new programme-level documents and no more than one net-new human-authored working artifact** before the first substantive Constitution convergence write.

Superseded intermediate proposal files should be converted to short redirect/history pointers or archived; they should not remain competing active authorities.

## 15. Domain breadth stop rule

Before the first deep well is reviewed, do not launch parallel full programmes in Physics, Biology, AI, Social, Consciousness, Neuroscience and Epistemology.

A second domain opens only after Phase 9 productive-adequacy review.

## 16. Epistemic-privilege boundary

SRT may recognize asymmetric access from different positions.

It does not infer:

```text
special access
-> global interpretive authority
-> moral authority
-> infallibility
```

Any domain that needs standpoint-specific evidential weighting must justify it in that domain.
