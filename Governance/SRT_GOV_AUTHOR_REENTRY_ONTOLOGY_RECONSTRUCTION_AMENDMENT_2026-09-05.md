# GOV amendment — author re-entry and ontology reconstruction correction

## 0. Why this amendment exists

The author has made two explicit programme-level corrections on 2026-09-05:

1. the reconstruction moved too quickly from Constitution / Domain Framework construction into deep-well increment testing before enough author intuition, machine red-team refinement, mature-neighbor adaptation and common-problem extraction had occurred;
2. bearer is one important SRT ontological problem/structure, not the whole SRT ontology.

The source record is `01_Source_Intuition/SRT_AUTHOR_REENTRY_CORRECTION_2026-09-05.md`.

This amendment changes **programme sequencing and interpretation**, not canonical ontology definitions.

### 0.1 Reopen authority

This amendment is an authorized identity reopen under
`Operations/Proposals/SRT_CONSTITUTION_DOMAIN_RECONSTRUCTION_BLUEPRINT_2026-08-29.md §12`,
condition **#4**:

> the author explicitly reopens the identity after reviewing evidence from the cycle.

The 2026-09-05 author correction is that explicit reopen after review of the first
Neuroscience pilot and the ongoing Epistemology pilot. Condition #4 is sufficient for
this governance action. This amendment does **not** claim that §12 condition #2 has
independently completed, because the second well remains an open bounded pilot rather
than a completed second deep-well verdict.

---

## 1. Previous identity freeze is no longer an exhaustive identity statement

The earlier programme shorthand:

```text
SRT Constitution = bearer-involved perspective framework
Domain research = formalization / evidence / testing layer
```

was useful as a corrective against formal-axiom overreach and premature bearer deletion, but it is now too narrow if read as an exhaustive SRT identity.

Current governance reading:

```text
SRT reconstruction
= author-guided reconstruction of an open ontological problem field

Constitution v1
= current active reader-interface / perspective-operation prototype
  that suspends explanatory exemptions and opens questions
  without claiming to exhaust SRT ontology

bearer / position / participation
= one important ontological and methodological problem family
  among several; not the total ontology

Domain Reconstruction Framework
= provisional domain problem-reconstruction surface
  that must be fed by author intuition, mature-neighbor adaptation,
  common-problem extraction, inherited SRT assets and domain constraints

Deep Well / Evidence
= later bounded discrimination layer;
  not the engine that is allowed to manufacture the ontology it is testing
```

No new final ontology label is authorized by this amendment.

## 2. Constitution v1 scope correction

`Core_Law/SRT_Constitution_V1.md` remains active and canonical in its current role. Its six reader operations remain valid current Constitution content.

Governance must now state explicitly:

```text
six reader operations
!= six exhaustive ontological modules
!= complete SRT ontology
!= proof that all future SRT content is bearer/objectification content
```

The Constitution can remain frozen as an interface while the ontology that may eventually be exposed, reorganized or pressured through repeated use remains under author-led reconstruction.

A future change to the Constitution text itself still requires the existing canonical/edit rules and author adjudication.

## 3. Open ontology problem-field guard

For current reconstruction work, AI and domain frameworks may recover and pressure an **open problem field** containing questions around, for example:

```text
multiplicity / pre-One conditions
selection / determination
manifestation / One-formation
persistence / stabilization
write-in / history / sedimentation
relation / constraint
bearer / position / own-history / consequence
future selectability / later processability
order / convergence / higher-order stabilization
objectification / public representation
```

These are prompts / problem axes only.

Hard guard:

- do not turn this list into a new ladder, primitive inventory or canonical sequence;
- do not infer that every item is distinct, necessary or exhaustive;
- do not infer `One-formation = Selection`, `Selection = Order`, `stabilization -> bearer`, or `history = own-history`;
- do not require every domain to instantiate every axis;
- do not privilege bearer merely because the recent reconstruction produced unusually detailed bearer audits.

The author, not repository pattern-matching, decides whether any deeper relation among these axes becomes SRT ontology.

---

## 4. Author-reentry gate before further ontology closure

When a task could change SRT's ontological identity or promote an old relation into the reconstructed ontology, use:

```text
current author question / intuition
-> relevant source recovery
-> AI divergence
-> bounded mature-neighbor awareness
-> author provisional convergence
-> internal contradiction / circularity / hidden-premise red-team
-> fuller neighbor adaptation and pressure
-> common-problem extraction across the relevant traditions / domains
-> author second adjudication
-> only then repository ontology/interface write or domain discrimination
```

This does not require the author to invent in isolation. It requires AI and sources to **pressure and inform** author judgment rather than substitute for it.

### 4.1 AI role refinement

For identity-level reconstruction, machine review should be separated into distinct roles where useful:

```text
source-recovery auditor
internal-consistency red-team
strong-neighbor advocate
counterexample generator
conceptual-substitution / relabeling detector
hidden-ontology-commitment extractor
semantic-invariance checker across domains
author-intent drift checker
increment / novelty auditor — only when separately authorized
```

Agreement among roles/models is not author convergence.

---

## 5. Mature-neighbor adaptation now precedes increment pressure

The existing U-mode / N-mode distinction remains valid, but sequencing is tightened.

Before an SRT domain or ontological claim is pushed into novelty/subtraction, first ask:

```text
What does each strongest neighboring tradition actually claim in its own language?
Which SRT question maps onto it, and which does not?
Where is the relation a realization, translation, resonance, contrast or pressure?
What domain-specific mechanism must remain domain-specific?
What does the neighbor force SRT to narrow, split, abandon or reformulate?
What recurring difficulty appears across multiple neighboring approaches?
```

The goal is not to accumulate supportive quotations. Neighbor work must be allowed to **change SRT**.

### 5.1 Common-problem extraction gate

Before claiming a new SRT solution/increment, identify the traditional problem that remains after fair neighbor adaptation.

A useful common problem should be expressible without SRT vocabulary first.

For example, a candidate should survive questions like:

```text
Do several mature traditions independently encounter this difficulty?
Are they actually facing the same structural problem or only sharing words?
What has each tradition already solved?
What remains unresolved because of different starting cuts / ontologies / methods?
```

Only after this step may SRT propose that one of its reconstructed relations reorganizes or resolves the shared problem.

---

## 6. N-mode / Case A-B-C throttle

Case A/B/C remains a legitimate gate on a concrete claim that **SRT adds X**.

It is not the default engine for forming SRT ontology.

Do not force a Case A/B/C verdict unless all of the following are true:

```text
[ ] a bounded SRT-side solution/distinction actually exists
[ ] that solution/distinction is author-owned or explicitly author-adjudicated
[ ] strongest relevant neighbors have been mapped fairly
[ ] the common residual problem has been stated without circularly assuming SRT
[ ] a domain-native discriminator or evidential consequence can in principle distinguish the claim
```

If these are not paid, the correct state is **theory formation / mapping still open**, not an automatic Case B.

A failure to obtain immediate Case C from an early interface probe must not be generalized into the identity claim `SRT is only methodology`.

---

## 7. Domain Reconstruction Framework correction

Future and revised Domain Reconstruction Frameworks must no longer be organized primarily as:

```text
six Constitution operations
-> objectification map
-> bearer architecture
-> problem families
-> deep-well queue
```

That route remains a useful **interface-analysis slice**, but not the whole domain reconstruction.

A framework should instead distinguish at least:

```text
A. author / ontology status
   what is explicit author intuition, recovered historical SRT,
   AI synthesis, and unresolved pressure?

B. domain starting picture
   what does the field actually treat as given?

C. relevant open SRT ontology questions
   which ontology axes are genuinely relevant here, without forcing all of them?

D. mature-theory adaptation
   what do strongest neighboring traditions already explain and where do they pressure SRT?

E. common-problem extraction
   what difficulty remains across the compared perspectives?

F. Constitution/interface operations
   what does v1 help the reader reopen here?

G. objectification and bearer/position diagnostics
   use where relevant; neither is the total domain ontology

H. inherited assets / materials / domain mechanisms

I. candidate SRT response
   only if author-adjudicated; otherwise leave open

J. deep-well queue
   only after the above work is mature enough to justify discrimination
```

The active template should be updated accordingly.

---

## 8. Bearer–Objectification Declaration scope correction

The Bearer–Objectification Declaration remains a useful diagnostic for some SRT-sensitive models.

It is not:

```text
a complete ontology checklist
a universal science requirement
a proof that bearer is the fundamental SRT primitive
a substitute for world-side formation / Selection / persistence / relation / order questions
```

Use it when bearer, position, historical attribution, consequence landing or public-object construction are load-bearing in the selected problem.

A domain framework may legitimately focus elsewhere when those issues are not central.

Archive work may continue under bounded stop conditions, but completing the Case A/B/C sequence is no longer the main clock for ontology reconstruction progress.

## 11. What counts as progress now

Near-term theory progress should be measured primarily by:

```text
quality of recovered author questions
clarity of unresolved ontology relations
strength and fairness of neighbor adaptation
quality of machine red-team / counterexamples
identification of recurring cross-theory problems
author adjudication after pressure
ability to state an SRT response without merely relabeling a neighbor
```

Deep-well count, archive row count and number of Case-C candidates are secondary during this correction phase.

---

## 12. No retroactive erasure

This amendment does not declare the 2026-08-29 to 2026-09-04 work mistaken or useless.

It preserves:

- Constitution v1 reader operations;
- the Domain Reconstruction Layer as a useful intermediate surface;
- U-mode / N-mode separation;
- material re-entry;
- objectification and bearer diagnostics;
- strong-rival and stopping discipline;
- adverse / blocked pilot results.

The correction is about **scope, pace and sequencing**: interfaces and pilots must stop pretending to have already completed the ontology they are supposed to help reconstruct.
