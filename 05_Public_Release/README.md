# 05 Public Release / 公众释放

This layer prepares SRT for public readers, talks, essays, short explanations, videos, newsletters, outreach emails, and visual maps.

Public release is not a weaker version of SRT. It is the controlled release of SRT's force into public language.

## Role

`05_Public_Release` should answer:

- How can SRT be explained in one sentence, one page, ten minutes, or one essay?
- How can SRT be introduced without forcing readers into governance, equations, or internal claim-mode details first?
- Which public claims are safe, sharp, and not overextended?
- How can SRT speak to AI, consciousness, modern life, subjecthood, social order, spirituality, and philosophy without becoming vague mysticism?

## Public stance

Safe public summary:

> SRT is a selection-first theory of reality. It says that reality is not a flat inventory of objects already waiting for us, nor a fantasy created by the mind. Reality becomes determinate when possibilities are selected under constraint, anchored into experience or action, and stabilized into durable structures.

Even shorter:

> Reality is selected under constraint, stabilized through history, and tested by resistance.

## Public guardrails

Avoid saying:

- SRT explains everything.
- SRT proves all other theories wrong.
- SRT says mind creates reality.
- SRT says truth is relative.
- SRT says everything is conscious.
- SRT is beyond testing.

Prefer saying:

- SRT asks how possibilities become realities.
- SRT studies how repeated realities harden into the world we inherit.
- SRT is selection-first, not mind-first.
- Selection is broad; consciousness is thresholded.
- Stability is not the same as legitimacy.
- What becomes real must pay cost, survive resistance, and leave consequences.

## Boundary

Public release must stay connected to formal anchors. If a public sentence becomes more dramatic than the underlying claim level allows, downgrade it or link it back to the relevant anchor, bridge, or evidence card.
