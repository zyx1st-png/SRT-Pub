# Public Guardrails

Public release should be sharp, readable, and disciplined. It is not a lower-quality SRT and not a canonical source.

## Avoid Saying

- SRT explains everything.
- SRT proves all other theories wrong.
- Mind creates reality.
- Truth is relative.
- Everything is conscious.
- SRT is beyond testing.

## Prefer Saying

- SRT asks how possibilities become realities.
- Reality becomes determinate through constrained Selection; durable reality is stabilized through retained history and tested by resistance.
- Selection is broad; consciousness is thresholded.
- Stability is not legitimacy.
- Determinate occurrence does not by itself imply persistence; durable formations require independently established maintenance, resistance, and retained consequences.

## Public Style

- Lead with the founding reversal, not repository governance.
- Keep symbols minimal on the first pass.
- Distinguish selection from conscious choice.
- Distinguish stability from legitimacy.
- Distinguish bridge from evidence.
- Distinguish public summary from formal anchor.

## Downgrade Rule

If a sentence sounds like SRT settles every domain at once, downgrade it. Good public writing should preserve SRT's force without turning force into overclaiming.
