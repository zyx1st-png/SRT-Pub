# Author adjudication — OPEN register ownership

## 1. Author decision

The pre-audit `Operations/Audits/SRT_OPEN_REGISTER_RECONCILIATION_PREAUDIT_2026-09-13.md §5`
put five ownership questions to the author. An external review (GPT) proposed
answers to all five; those proposals were relayed to the author in session,
together with a verification of which of them were even implementable against the
repository as it stands.

The author's response, verbatim:

```text
我认同gpt的
```

That is the whole of the author's statement. It is recorded here without
expansion: the author adopted the relayed proposals as a set. The wording of the
five items below is the review's; the **decision to adopt them is the author's**,
and this file is that decision's provenance.

Per `AGENTS.md`, AI-generated alternatives are not author decisions and two
models agreeing is not author convergence. The review's recommendation alone
would not have authorized anything; this adoption is what does.

## 2. The five adopted answers

**A1 — no global OPEN owner.** Spine §9 keeps cross-owner ontology OPEN gates;
each local owner keeps its own structural OPEN; `Core/SRT_OPEN_TENSIONS.md` is a
cross-owner hardening ledger / companion and does not replace any local owner.

**A2 — S10 / S13 / S18 do not land together.** S10 (unique Bearer admission
threshold) and S13 (Bearer ↔ position stability) are Bearer hardening; while the
Bearer final semantic owner is unsettled — that is S22, still open — they are
routed to Spine §9 as **provisional routing**, which is not Spine acquiring final
Bearer definition authority. S18 (scale attribution under tightly coupled nested
Ones) splits: local formation / unit to One Formation, higher-order realization
to Collective Selection, cross-layer non-identity gate only to Spine.

**A3 — W1 / W2 leave STATUS without promotion.** They go to an explicit
noncanonical machine / audit taxonomy owner as retrospective writeback-depth /
audit labels; neither retained by STATUS nor raised into Spine canonical.

**A4 — S04 / S12 merge; S14 / S15 stay separate.** Concern and 关切 carry no
author-intended terminological difference, so `Concern ↔ typed Bearer` and
`Bearer ↔ 关切` are one bidirectional question. `Bearer ↔ cognition` and
`Bearer ↔ subject-position` stay two: subject-position and cognition are
different downstream burdens under the current architecture, and merging them
would re-flatten that layering.

**A5 — STATUS keeps programme-level verdicts plus pointers.** Level 2 = HOLD,
new Level, scientific distinctiveness, whole-architecture non-substitutability,
and navigation to Spine / local OPEN / hardening ledger. No duplicated ontology
OPEN register on the dashboard.
