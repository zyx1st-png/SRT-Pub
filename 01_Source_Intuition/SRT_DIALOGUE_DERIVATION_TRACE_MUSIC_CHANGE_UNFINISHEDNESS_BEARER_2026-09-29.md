# Dialogue derivation trace — music, change, unfinishedness, Bearer and vertical generation

> Role: preserve the analytical path that produced the 2026-09-29 author adjudication.
>
> Authority: machine analysis accepted directionally through repeated author “认同，继续”; use the author adjudication for controlling source meaning. This trace is not definition authority.

## 1. Entry intuition

The dialogue began from:

~~~text
music perception
is not exhausted by isolated notes;
what matters may be the change between / through notes;
this may connect to background -> foreground;
feeling and consciousness may be needed to study vertical generation.
~~~

The first correction was to avoid the literal overclaim that listeners do not perceive notes.

A safer research statement is:

> Musical identity and experience often depend critically on relational change and on how a current event changes a background organization already in force.

## 2. Change had to be relativized to a generative background

A bare physical difference cannot capture the same note / interval functioning differently under different tonal, rhythmic, grouping or learned histories.

Local working form only:

~~~text
D_t = change of current event relative to current formed background B_t
~~~

B_t is not a new canonical variable.

The important move is:

~~~text
physical delta
-> contextual / generative deformation.
~~~

## 3. Music supplied an unconscious negative control

MMN research shows that auditory regularities and violations can be registered without focal attention, including abstract inter-sound relations and musical regularities.

Therefore:

~~~text
regularity detection
!= feeling;
detected change
!= phenomenal foreground;
prediction-error-like signal
!= consciousness.
~~~

Verified neighbor coordinates:

- Garrido MI et al. 2009. The mismatch negativity: A review of underlying mechanisms. PMCID PMC2671031.
- Yu X et al. 2015. The Mismatch Negativity: An Indicator of Perception of Regularities in Music. PMCID PMC4609411.
- 2024 exploratory MMN study of physical vs abstract auditory regularities. PMCID PMC11253046.

## 4. Three-stage dissociation

The dialogue converged on:

~~~text
structural registration
-> ? phenomenal foregrounding
-> ? explicit objectification.
~~~

Examples:

Structural registration:
- automatic regularity detection;
- mismatch / grouping update;
- implicit segmentation.

Phenomenal foregrounding:
- tension;
- suspension;
- release;
- surprise;
- continuity;
- interruption;
- completion;
- “something changed”.

Explicit objectification:
- “this is the dominant”;
- “the key changed”;
- “the phrase ended”;
- “that note was wrong”.

This aligns with the repository distinction:

~~~text
foreground_phen
!= foreground_obj.
~~~

The control-line foreground_obj in the current cognition programme remains unchanged.

## 5. Same object / different background and different object / same relation

Two complementary manipulations emerged.

### 5.1 Same local target, different background

Hold the local target note / chord fixed while changing preceding context.

~~~text
same local object
+ different generative background
-> different tension / stability / completion / continuation profile?
~~~

### 5.2 Different absolute notes, preserved relational structure

Transpose a melody or preserve contour / interval relations while changing absolute notes.

~~~text
different local acoustic objects
+ preserved relation
-> preserved melodic identity / trajectory?
~~~

Relative-pitch / contour work is a mature neighbor and remains source-owned.

Verified example:
- McDermott JH et al. 2010. Is Relative Pitch Specific to Pitch? PMCID PMC2841133.

## 6. Prediction error, surprisal and uncertainty were not enough

The dialogue separated:

~~~text
prediction error
= current event violates an expectation;

surprisal
= how improbable the observed event was;

entropy / uncertainty
= how dispersed the predicted future distribution was.
~~~

But felt musical tension can be strong when the next continuation is highly constrained and relatively predictable.

Thus:

~~~text
felt tension
!= uncertainty by definition;
felt tension
!= surprisal by definition;
felt tension
!= prediction error by definition.
~~~

This motivated a broader candidate: current continuation / reachability geometry, used only as working explanatory language.

A recent broad music-perception review describes tonal perception as contextualizing pitch relative to a tone hierarchy and meter as contextualizing timing relative to hierarchical rhythmic grouping:
- Mehr SA. 2025. Core systems of music perception. Trends Cogn Sci 29(8):763–777. DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2025.05.013; PMCID PMC12328076.

## 7. Two different deformation families

### 7.1 Within-geometry directional deformation

The operative grouping / cut remains usable.

~~~text
stable
-> tension
-> suspension
-> approach
-> release.
~~~

The salient change may be in how non-flat available continuation becomes rather than in whether the organization itself changes.

### 7.2 Geometry failure / recutting

The old organization becomes inadequate.

~~~text
surprise
-> break
-> “what happened?”
-> reinterpretation
-> Aha / new whole.
~~~

This is closer to:

~~~text
K_t
-> instability
-> recutting
-> K_(t+1).
~~~

Therefore:

~~~text
phenomenal foreground
-/-> recutting universally.
~~~

## 8. Musical boundaries linked the route to event segmentation

Music phrase-boundary work supplies a strong mature neighbor.

Verified examples:

- Knösche TR et al. 2005. Perception of phrase structure in music. PMID 15678484.
- Silva S et al. 2014. Musical phrase boundaries, wrap-up and the closure positive shift. PMID 25139422.
- Segmenting and Predicting Musical Phrase Structure Exploits Neural Gain Modulation and Phase Precession. 2024. PMCID PMC11270514.
- Burunat I, Levitin DJ, Toiviainen P. 2024. Breaking (musical) boundaries by investigating brain dynamics of event segmentation during real-life music-listening. PNAS. DOI 10.1073/pnas.2319459121; PMCID PMC11388323.
- Neural and Behavioral Tracking of Musical Phrases Occurs Without Temporal Regularity. 2026. PMCID PMC13071237.

These results are not SRT evidence by themselves.

They establish that:
- musical streams are segmented online;
- phrase structure can be tracked neurally;
- boundary anticipation / detection can be dissociated from trivial temporal regularity;
- expertise changes implementation details.

## 9. Actual != complete

The strongest conceptual turn was:

~~~text
already actual
!= already complete.
~~~

At each instant the physical input has fully occurred, yet the listener can experience:

~~~text
still going
not finished
cannot stop here
finally resolved.
~~~

The dialogue used the temporary phrase prospective dependence for:

> a current formed organization whose local resolution / stability still depends on some not-yet-actualized continuation relation.

This is local working vocabulary only.

## 10. Predictability and unfinishedness should be orthogonalized

Candidate design:

~~~text
A high predictability + high completion
B high predictability + high unfinishedness
C low predictability + high completion
D low predictability + high unfinishedness
~~~

Research burden:

~~~text
predictability
!= continuation dependence / unfinishedness.
~~~

If the distinction cannot be operationalized after strong statistical controls, this branch should be narrowed.

## 11. Resolution, interruption and recutting

Resolution:

~~~text
open continuation dependence
-> discharged compatibly
-> formed whole preserved / stabilized
-> local completion.
~~~

Interruption:

~~~text
continuation opportunity stops
while the prior organization remains retrospectively unresolved.
~~~

Recutting / reinterpretation:

~~~text
later information changes the grouping / cut
under which the prior sequence is organized.
~~~

Therefore:

~~~text
silence / termination
!= completion.
~~~

## 12. Husserl is a required no-appropriation neighbor

Husserl's temporal-consciousness work already gives a powerful account of melody through retention, primal impression and protention in the living present.

Verified overview:
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Phenomenological Approaches to Self-Consciousness.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Edmund Husserl.
- Stanford Encyclopedia archive, Temporal Consciousness.

The SRT-side question is not “does the present have temporal thickness?” as a novelty claim.

The possible farther research question is:

> Can some future-facing structures described phenomenologically be operationally decomposed into intervention-sensitive continuation / reachability / consequence-return / Position-reconstitution relations?

Under the 2026-09-29 governance correction:

~~~text
no local phenomenological increment
!= subtract the SRT relation;
-> retain Husserl as a source-owned convergence coordinate.
~~~
