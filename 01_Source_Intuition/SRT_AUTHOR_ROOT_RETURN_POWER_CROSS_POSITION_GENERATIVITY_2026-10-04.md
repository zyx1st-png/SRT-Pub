## 3. Root-return

After two substitution / narrowing passes, the dialogue returned to the root question rather than opening a third residual-hunt round.

The machine-proposed root question accepted at direction level is:

> How can an already formed existence / position enter the generative conditions of a later or other existence / position, and under what relations does that conditioning support, preserve, reorganize, or displace the other's own generative source-role?

Working shorthand:

~~~text
Selection_A
-> formed A / retained efficacy
-> Conditions(B)
-> later Selection_B
~~~

The central problem is not therefore “what is power?” but the more general relation:

~~~text
A -> Conditions(B)
~~~

Power is retained as one downstream organization of this cross-position relation.

This root question is not itself presented as a novel research frontier. Mature neighbors already strongly cover parts of the general burden: Whitehead on prior actuality conditioning later becoming, and niche-construction / active-selection work on organisms modifying later selection conditions. Its role here is to restore the SRT-led problem hierarchy after subtraction, not to claim an unowned explanatory target.

## 4. Power is downstream, not the ontology root

The current preferred routing is:

~~~text
Selection
-> actuality / formed result
-> retained efficacy where established
-> cross-position conditioning where established
-> downstream relation families:
   support / coordination / influence / power / coercion / delegation / institution
~~~

This routing deliberately avoids:

~~~text
Power = existence;
Power = retained efficacy;
Power = all causation;
Power = primitive Selection;
Power = moral legitimacy.
~~~

A useful but non-definitional machine formulation is:

> power may be studied as one asymmetrically organized form of cross-position generative conditioning.

This wording is subordinate to existing source-native political / social theory and to GRG B10. It is not a canonical definition.

## 5. Conditioning vs substitution

The root-return exposes a deeper distinction than open vs closed power.

Strong influence does not automatically equal closure.

A relation may satisfy:

~~~text
cross-position efficacy(A -> B) = strong
while
B's own generative source-role remains load-bearing or even strengthens.
~~~

This is the support-side possibility.

Conversely, the structurally stronger failure candidate is:

~~~text
A's efficacy increasingly generates B's future relation-state
while B continues to be nominally counted as a participant
but B's own Selection no longer materially enters the relation.
~~~

This is already strongly connected to the accepted non-usurpation / generative-objectification owner:

> a formed position is generatively objectified when a relation continues to count it as a participant while its effective future source-role is recurrently displaced to another position.

Therefore:

~~~text
conditioning != substitution;
asymmetry != usurpation automatically;
strong support != replacement automatically.
~~~

## 6. Consequence return as a diagnostic window on source-role

The main owner reorganization preserved by this dialogue is an expansion and sharpening of an already-present connection among:

~~~text
cross-position conditioning / power
<-> consequence-return architecture
<-> participant generative source-role
~~~

This is not claimed as a new mechanism.

It is a cross-owner interpretive connection.

If consequences borne by position B can return into the relation and materially alter gate decisions, stabilization parameters, relation grammar, or—at stronger normative levels—legitimacy review, then B retains at least one route by which its differences can remain generatively load-bearing.

If consequence return is recurrently cut at:

~~~text
information
-> counting
-> attribution
~~~

then B may remain inside the relation while becoming progressively less able to contribute to the generation of that relation's future state.

This gives a bounded diagnostic reading of pseudo-openness:

~~~text
expression without structural uptake
!= preserved generative participation.
~~~

## 7. Reframing the original “stupidity inheritance” intuition

The original intuition is retained as a motivating case, not as theory terminology.

A low-quality cut can become historically effective across positions when it is repeatedly maintained, encoded, enforced, backgrounded or otherwise carried into later Selection conditions.

The key failure is not simply that later people “believe the same wrong answer.”

A stronger structural reading is:

~~~text
local cut
-> cross-position retained efficacy
-> consequence-return / reopening path weakens
-> cut becomes backgrounded / inherited
-> later positions receive it as part of their generative conditions.
~~~

The author-intuitive phrase “让本来不应该留存的愚蠢被继承下来” should therefore be kept as provenance for the motivating case, while analytical work routes through existing notions such as retained efficacy, pathological closure, consequence-return integrity, reconstructibility, reselection and generative objectification.

No canonical category of “stupidity” is created.

## 8. Open power reinterpreted

The author explicitly rejected a one-sided equation of power with closure.

The root-return permits a stronger non-binary interpretation:

~~~text
open / enabling relation
!= low influence;
closed / substitutive relation
!= high influence automatically.
~~~

A high-impact relation may be generatively enabling if its effect helps another position preserve or strengthen the capacity to form, test, reopen and reorganize its own Selection conditions.

Conversely, a superficially permissive relation may be structurally closed if the affected position's differences cannot alter the future relation.

Machine-side compression:

> openness is not merely permission to speak or the presence of options; a stronger generative test is whether the affected position can still become a real source of the relation's future differentiation.

This sentence is M-level synthesis and must not be treated as a canonical definition of openness, freedom or legitimacy.

## 9. Objectification consequence

The dialogue produced one especially useful cross-owner synthesis:

> A deep form of generative objectification is not merely describing another as an object; it is a relation in which that position's differences progressively cease to function as sources of the relation's future generation while the position is still treated as a participant.

This is a reorganization of the existing generative-objectification / participant-status-consistency line, not a new objectification primitive.

It should be pressure-tested against the existing owner before any stronger reuse.

## 10. Ontology / relation / power / normativity separation

~~~

Hard guards:

~~~text

~~~
