# Author adjudication — PR #1069 Facing-method corrective

> **Boundary:** this record does not edit Freeze-A canonical owners, does not settle branch / merge identity, does not define a new primitive, and does not reopen the frozen HP-B package.

## 0. Trigger

After reviewing #1069 and the follow-up analysis, the author responded:

> **「认同，继续」**

This acceptance applies to the corrective direction below, not to every sentence of the independent review verbatim.

## 1. D-1 — L0-facing continuity becomes an H0 reduction test

Author decision:

~~~text
DO NOT presuppose "L0-facing continuity" as a new independent ontology noun.

First test:

L0-facing continuity ?
= O0 non-preclosure
+ Selection-mediated vertical reconstitution / One-side retained efficacy
+ formed Selection-position / operative locality

If fully absorbed:
retire the independent working noun.

If not fully absorbed:
state the residual explicitly before retaining any distinct term.
~~~

Clarification accepted by the author:

~~~text
primitive Selection
= source of the generativity renewed through the process;

history / One / recurrent reconstitution
= what can make that renewed generativity belong to this formed continuing process.
~~~

Therefore the earlier shorthand 'primitive Selection = ontological source of L0-facing continuity' is too compressed. The safer wording is:

> **primitive Selection is the ontological source of the generativity renewed in the candidate L0-facing continuity; recurrent history / One supplies the formed localization and continuation burden.**

Terminal Selection remains genuine.

## 2. D-2 — Facing is an alias / execution discipline, not a new ontology term

Author decision:

~~~text
L0-facing / L1-facing / L2-facing
= aliases / operational readings of the already-canonical
  L0 / L1 / L2 analytic-aspect rule.

They are NOT new ontic layers and should not be hardened as a separate primitive taxonomy.
~~~

The new contribution of the present workline is methodological: earlier research often failed to **apply** the already-existing aspect rule consistently, allowing L2 structures to be promoted into L0 explanations.

'bare L0' is not an admissible label for O0 + S0. Use:

~~~text
primitive Selection (O0 + S0)
~~~

when the whole co-primitive primitive-Selection burden is intended.

Where the analysis concerns the open / non-preclosed side of an already formed Position, use a qualified working phrase such as:

~~~text
Position-relative L0-facing
~~~

and keep it explicitly noncanonical unless later simplified away.

## 3. D-3 — forward test uses structural constraints first; content prediction is downstream

The author does not adopt the review's strongest wording that only second-order prediction is ever allowed.

Accepted correction:

~~~text
L0-facing / primitive ontology claim
may directly support only structural / second-order constraints such as:
- where recutting should become likely;
- when existing cuts should destabilize;
- perturbation / load / friction dependence;
- frequency or regime of structure revision;
- what kinds of sedimentation changes should follow.

Specific first-order content predictions
may be made by an explicit downstream domain model,
but then the prediction belongs to that L1/L2 model realization,
not directly to primitive L0.
~~~

Thus:

~~~text
L0 structural constraint
+ declared downstream model
-> possible concrete content prediction

but

concrete content prediction
-/-> primitive L0 directly predicted that object.
~~~

Strong baselines for any later empirical test must include structure-learning families capable of creating new latent causes / categories / states, including nonparametric or equivalent expandable-hypothesis-space models.

## 5. One / Position admission circularity correction

The author accepts the review's circularity warning.

Do not define One formation by saying:

~~~text
history forms a later Selection-position / from-where
~~~

because Selection-position is canonically the operative aspect of an already formed One.

Use the existing non-circular formation route:

~~~text
Selection-generated organization_t
-> materially conditions later Selection_(t+1)
-> later Selection generates renewed organization_(t+1)
-> renewed organization can again carry the relevant conditioning role
-> recurrent non-equivalent reconstitution may support relative continuation separability
-> One candidate
~~~

After One is admitted, its current operative aspect may be read as Selection-position / from-where.

The phrase 'history forms the next from-where' may remain as an explanatory gloss only after this ordering is explicit.

## 6. Expectation correction

Do not collapse basal structural generative expectation into Position / Orientation.

Retain the 2026-09-20 asymmetry:

~~~text
B — structural generative expectation
= relation-level immanent directional burden;
  does not require a formed locus.

C — anticipatory realization
= where a formed locus exists, that direction is embodied / enacted /
  regulated / modeled from somewhere.
~~~

The reconstruction pass may test:

> whether **formed Orientation** absorbs part of C-level anticipatory realization.

It must not assume that it absorbs B.

Also preserve Spine §6.3:

~~~text
Selection affecting later selectability
!= formed-position anticipation automatically.
~~~

## 7. Ontological Friction correction

Do not redefine Ontological Friction as mismatch-only strain.

Canonical Psi_f remains the constitutive payability / impedance burden of forming and maintaining determinate reality under the declared model.

The recent phrase:

~~~text

~~~

may be retained only as:

~~~text
one contextual diagnostic / high-pressure projection / readout
of canonical Ontological Friction,
not a second friction concept and not the full definition.
~~~

HP-B remains frozen. This pass is read-only with respect to its kernel conditions.
