## 2. Minimum author-owned correction

The author rejects treating `consequence` as part of the ontological core of primitive Selection.

```text

!= consequence as such
```


## 4. Legitimate downstream use of consequence language

`Consequence` may still be useful as observer-facing scientific or audit language, for example:

```text
observed trace;
cost;
state change;
irreversibility signature;
changed transition probability;
history-sensitive behavior;
repair burden;
externalized or internalized effect.
```

These are possible readouts of what follows from a Selection-related process. Their observability does not make them primitive Selection ontology.

A safe directional shorthand is therefore:

```text
Selection-related process
-> possible observed consequence / trace / cost / repair signal
```

This is an external readout relation, not a definition of Selection.
