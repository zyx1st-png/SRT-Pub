# Selective Reality Theory (SRT): A Minimal Note on Actualization

*Yuxin Zhang — September 2026*

SRT begins from a deliberately narrow ontological commitment: **Selection is genuine actualised non-neutral differentiation.** “Selection” is a term of art here. It does not mean a subject choosing among a pre-given menu of alternatives, and it does not presuppose a prior chooser. Nor, however, does every descriptive or modelled state change automatically count as Selection.

SRT currently treats Selection as primitive. That is a starting commitment of the framework, not a proof that Selection is irreducible to every possible alternative vocabulary. One important question therefore remains open: what exactly distinguishes genuine actualised Selection from a merely descriptive change of state without defining the difference by circularly saying that “a Selection occurred”?

## 1. Occurrence is not the same as historical retention

The current SRT architecture sharply separates the **occurrence** of a Selection event from what may happen afterward.

A Selection can be genuine even if there is no later process in which its consequences persist. A terminal Selection therefore remains a Selection. Later sediment, memory, inheritance, or durable historical efficacy is **not** part of the occurrence criterion.

When a prior Selection does remain materially effective in the conditions of later Selection, SRT calls that a stronger relation: **retained historical efficacy** or sedimentation. In compact form:

```text
Selection occurrence
!= {retained historical efficacy / sedimentation / generative inheritance}.
```

This distinction is important to SRT because “something happened” and “what happened continues to shape what can happen next” are different ontological burdens.

## 2. L0 / L1 / L2 are aspects, not three substances

SRT uses three pieces of shorthand:

- **L0-side**: the open or non-preclosed generative aspect of Selection relative to determinate actuality;
- **L1-side**: Selection under determinate manifestation or actuality;
- **L2-side**: prior Selection remaining effective as retained constraint, sediment, or history on later Selection.

These are not separate worlds or substances. They are ways of distinguishing openness, actual occurrence, and historically retained efficacy without collapsing them into one state.

## 3. Subjects, valuation, and agency are not presupposed at the base

Primitive Selection is **subjectless and finite-position-indexed** in SRT. Actual differentiation is therefore never a view from nowhere: it is indexed to a finite local relation or standpoint-condition. But finite positionality does not yet imply a formed One, Selection-position, subject, observer, valuation, or reflective agency.

Stronger organizations may arise only when Selection becomes recurrently self-conditioning. SRT uses **One** for a relatively separable recurrent Selection-organization and **Selection-position** for its operative local “from-where.” Still stronger notions—stable standing, bearerhood, valuation, concern, and agency—carry additional conditions and are not inferred merely from the fact that Selection occurred.

This is one reason I no longer want to identify Whiteheadian “subjective aim” too quickly with SRT’s later stake-bearing valuation. It may instead concern a more basic constitutive feature of an actual occasion.

## 4. Quantum measurement is a bridge, not a new collapse dynamics

SRT does not presently propose an additional mechanical force that drives wave-function collapse, and this note does not select a single quantum interpretation. The quantum-measurement use of SRT is a bridge-level interpretation: depending on the interpretation, the relevant realized fact may be read as exclusive outcome anchoring, branch-relative fact formation, or operational registration and conditioning.



Operational evidence for a physical realisation is normally downstream: a record acquires intervention-relevant path efficacy, future reachability changes, or return costs become different. SRT uses such downstream effects to identify and test physical realisation candidates; it does **not** make later retention constitutive of primitive Selection occurrence.

Our earlier exchange led me to the useful compression:

```text
global constraint
-> local contextualization
-> actualization
-> global augmentation.
```

After beginning *Foundations of Relational Realism*, I now see that relational realism already carries a strong version of the last step. An actualized occasion becomes a datum for later occasions; the global is not a finished totality but is formed through successive local actualizations and their compatible relations.

The comparison I now find most important is therefore narrower than my original question:

> **Does the self-determination of an actual occasion—its locally contextualized concrescence and integration into the global totality—already exhaust what SRT calls primitive Selection occurrence? Or does “actualization” still name an event-level burden that is not exhausted by Boolean contextualization, valuation, and local-to-global gluing?**

I do not assume the answer favors SRT. If relational realism already reconstructs the relevant work, that would be an important result for clarifying SRT rather than a problem to be defended away.

## 5. Current boundary

The strongest claim of this note is therefore not that SRT has found a missing physical mechanism. It has not. The present claim is structural:

```text
actual occurrence
!= {historical retention / recurrent formation / valuation / agency}.
```

Relational realism already distinguishes an occasion’s immediate becoming from its objective / superjective role as a datum for logically subsequent occasions. The open comparative question is narrower: whether its self-determined local contextualization, concrescence, and actualization already reconstruct the event-level burden SRT names **primitive Selection occurrence**.
