# Selection-Reality Theory: Minimal Setup

*A one-page technical note — Yuxin Zhang, July 2026*

**On the annotations.** SRT grades its own claims by hardness, and I have kept the grades visible rather than presenting everything in one voice. `[P0]` primitive axiom — assumed, not derived. `[P1]` constitutive theorem — follows once the P0 layer is granted. `[P2]` canonical interpretation. `[P3]` bridge mapping to another theory or domain — depends on an interface. `[P4]` empirical hypothesis. Where something is a candidate not yet adopted, it says so.

## 1. Three domains

`L0` latent possibility — what could become determinate before any path has been selected into manifestation. Not emptiness: possibility prior to stable selection.

`L1` manifest reality — the selected slice, actual for a given position, situation or process.

`L2` stabilized convergence — what hardens as selections repeat, overlap and become durable.

The `L1`/`L2` distinction does real work: manifestation and historical consolidation are different states, not two descriptions of one. `[P2]`

## 2. Four primitives

**Primacy of selection.** Existence is an image of selection: $\exists x \iff x \in \mathrm{Range}(\hat{G})$. Selection here is not a subject choosing from a finished shelf; it is the constrained determinization by which anything becomes determinate at all. `[P0]`

**Existence as anchoring.** $E = 1 - H(L_1)/H(L_0)$. Reality is the degree to which a selected slice has been stably anchored out of open possibility. `[P0]`

**Irreversible trace.** Once anchored into `L1` and inscribed in `L2`, a selection cannot be treated as never having occurred; any reversal is itself a new selection event carrying its own trace. `[P0]`

**Operator well-formedness.** $\hat{G}_\theta : S \to S$ must be well formed over an admissible state space for SRT's claims to have an object at all. `[P0]`

`θ` names the conditions of selection — embodiment where relevant, but also history, coupling, environment, instrumental arrangement, and position.

## 3. Cost

`Ψ_f` — *ontological friction*: the impedance that must be borne when $\hat{G}_\theta$ compresses open possibility into a maintainable, actionable, coordinable `L1` slice. It is not subjective effort, not identical with energy expenditure, and not identical with suffering; those are readings of it. `[P2, canonical anchor]`

Payability: $\mathrm{Payable}(X,\Delta t) \iff \alpha P_{sel} \ge \beta \Psi_f + \gamma S_{noise}$. `[P2]`

Zero friction is not the ideal. Nothing becomes durable for free, and too little resistance is as pathological as too much.

## 4. Downward constraint

`L2` is not an inert record. Sedimented structure re-enters as a real constraint term on subsequent selection:

$$\frac{d\sigma}{dt} = \hat{G}_\theta[\sigma] - \nabla F[\sigma] - \lambda \cdot \nabla C_{L_2}[\sigma]$$

`[P1]`

This is where SRT's historical asymmetry lives: settled structure conditions what is subsequently selectable, and revising it is not symmetric with having selected otherwise in the first place.

## 5. The quantum reading — bridge layer, not core

Everything in this section is `[P3]`: a mapping that depends on an interface. It does not define collapse, `Ψ_f`, or physical law.

**Measurement as selection.** $\text{collapse} = \hat{G}_\theta[\lvert\Psi\rangle_{L_0} \to \lvert\pi_k\rangle_{L_1}]$, with $\theta = \{\theta_{basis}, \theta_{cut}, \theta_{H_{int}}\}$ — the measurement basis, the placement of the cut, and the interaction Hamiltonian through which an outcome is registered.

**Contextuality as ontology.** Outcomes depend on the full `θ` configuration rather than on pre-existing properties of the target system. Two arrangements that are statistically indistinguishable but differ in `θ` are treated as ontologically distinct actualizations rather than as two descriptions of one.

The claim is not that some new dynamical mechanism drives collapse. It is that actualization is retained as a primitive transition rather than derived from unitary evolution alone.

## 6. Where valuation enters — candidate, not adopted

SRT places valuation downstream of basal actualization, in two stages. The generative chain below is registered internally as a bridge candidate originating in the book manuscript, **not yet adopted as canonical**: `[P3, candidate]`

$$\text{selective contextualization} \to \textbf{micro-valence} \to \text{affordance} \to \text{lack} \to \text{need} \to \text{anchoring} \to \text{value depth}$$

*Micro-valence* is the minimal approach/avoid tilt that an already-manifest interface carries for a concrete embodied position — the pre-signal of value, not value itself. It is explicitly distinguished from the pre-objectal minimal non-neutrality that precedes objectification and belongs to no position.

Downstream of that chain sits a **separate stake gate**: a direction enters stake-coupled concern only if it carries genuine irreversible risk, the position's utility gradient aligns with it, and consequences actually return to that position's closure. Having micro-valence is therefore not yet having stake — the chain brings a direction to the gate; the gate decides whether it counts. `[P2 for the gate; P3-candidate for the chain]`

The chain carries an explicit failure condition: if micro-valence bears no weight between minimal non-neutrality and value depth, the section is to be withdrawn.

## 7. What SRT does not claim

- **Selection is not proven irreducible.** Its `P0` status records that SRT *treats* selection as primitive. A subtractive test — whether asymmetric constraint, reachable-set change, irreversible writeback, payability and bearer-specific consequence return can reconstruct its role with no lost difference — is defined internally but **has not been run**. Until it is, selection must not be presented as a proven-irreducible ontological ultimate.
- **The origin of selectability is open.** The well-formedness axiom supplies an admissible operator; it does not derive the first possibility of selecting from a prior non-selective ground.
- **The cut/concern coupling is a high-commitment conjecture.** The proposal that the placement of the Heisenberg cut is parameterized by the observer's concern bandwidth together with free-energy minimization is marked internally as high metaphysical commitment with no direct experimental test path. `[P4, untested]`
- **Consciousness is thresholded, not distributed.** Broad selection does not entail experience; SRT is selection-first, not mind-first.
