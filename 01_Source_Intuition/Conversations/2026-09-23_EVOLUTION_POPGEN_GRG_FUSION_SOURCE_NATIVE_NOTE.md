## 3. Source-native unit architecture

The source does not offer one privileged “evolutionary event”.

Load-bearing units include:

~~~text
molecular mutation event;

generation-to-generation allele-frequency transition;
multi-generation frequency trajectory;
fixation / loss at a declared locus/population boundary;
environmental modification;
ecological inheritance;
condition-dependent phenotypic expression.
~~~

Source locators:

- mutation / drift / fixation / allele-frequency process: S1;
- selection efficacy / reproductive contribution: S2;
- genotype-fitness representation and epistasis: S3–S4;
- standing variation: S5–S6;
- condition-dependent expression / CGV: S7;
- environmental modification / ecological inheritance: S8–S10.

Analyst synthesis:

> these units should not be collapsed into one Card “event” merely for cross-domain convenience.

## 4. Source-native process distinctions

### 4.1 Difference generation

S1 separates mutation occurrence from subsequent polymorphism, loss or fixation.

~~~text
mutation occurrence
!= natural selection
!= propagation
!= fixation.
~~~

Source-owned distinction.

### 4.2 Existing variation before a new adaptive episode

S5–S6 support the distinction between de novo mutation and adaptation from standing variation.

~~~text
difference can pre-exist
before
a later selective episode.
~~~

Source-owned distinction.

### 4.3 Expression is not identical to existence

S7 defines CGV around genetic variation with little/no phenotypic effect in ordinary conditions but heritable phenotypic effects under atypical conditions.

Source-native chain:

~~~text
genetic difference present
-> low / absent ordinary phenotypic expression
-> changed environmental / genetic condition
-> heritable phenotypic variation becomes expressed.
~~~

The step from “difference exists” to “difference is expressed” is therefore source-supported.

### 4.4 Fitness consequence is not identical to expression

S2 treats selection efficacy through relations between variant fitness and population fate, while dominance, linkage, demography and other factors modify efficacy.

S3–S4 show that genotype-fitness relations depend on background / epistasis.

Therefore:

~~~text
phenotypic / genetic difference
!= fixed fitness consequence across all backgrounds.
~~~

This is source-grounded at the level of background-dependent effect; any stronger general “consequence accessibility” grammar is analyst synthesis.

### 4.5 Population propagation is not identical to fitness bias

S1 and S2 jointly require finite-population stochasticity / demography alongside fitness-associated effects.

Source-native warning:

~~~text
fitness-associated expectation
!= realized allele-frequency transition by itself.
~~~

Near |N_e s|≈1, source models explicitly treat selection and drift jointly (S1).

### 4.6 Outcome is not mechanism

Fixation / loss is an endpoint relative to a declared locus/population boundary.

~~~text
fixation
!= proof of adaptive natural selection;

loss
!= proof of negative natural selection.
~~~

Source-owned distinction from S1.

### 4.7 Later conditions can be changed by organism/environment interaction

S8–S10 support organismal environmental modification and ecological inheritance, while disputing its theoretical status.

Source-supported minimum:

~~~text
organismal activity
-> environmental modification
-> changed selective environment
-> possible changed later evolutionary dynamics.
~~~

Do not infer from this alone that niche construction is a GRG reconstruction operator.

## 5. Philosophy-of-biology constraints

The selection/drift relation cannot be used as a cheap SRT classifier.

N1–N5 preserve live disputes over:

- fitness as propensity;
- chance / drift;
- process vs statistical interpretation;
- genic vs genotypic / level descriptions;
- causal vs statistical decomposition.

Therefore:

~~~text
natural selection
-/>
SRT Selection identity;

drift
-/>
automatic SRT non-Selection control;

selection coefficient
-/>
settled ontological cause;

one statistical decomposition
-/>
unique causal decomposition.
~~~

### Fixation / loss

Does not identify:
