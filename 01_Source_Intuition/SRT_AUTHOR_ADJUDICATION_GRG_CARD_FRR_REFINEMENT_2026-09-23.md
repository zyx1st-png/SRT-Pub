## 2. Accepted Card-development direction

The author accepts the following methodological direction:

1. GRG should not begin a fusion case by replacing source-native vocabulary with GRG vocabulary.
2. A source-native reconstruction should come first.
3. GRG mapping should be explicit, typed and reversible.
4. A source term named “selection”, “actualization”, “context”, “germ”, “coarse-graining”, “recoherence”, “valuation” or similar must not be silently identified with SRT/GRG terms.
5. The Card should preserve failed mappings, empty fields, non-identity guards and reopening conditions as research results.
6. A successful Card must do more than redescribe a source in GRG language; it should change at least one prediction, diagnostic boundary, intervention sensitivity, exclusion condition, or transferable generative expectation.
7. The Card itself is a theory-facing higher-order proxy and must preserve reconstructibility back to source-native mechanism.

## 3. FRR-derived schema pressures accepted for the Card

The FRR fusion case exposed several concrete schema requirements.

### 3.1 Source-native “selection” must be split before SRT Selection mapping

FRR uses source-native operations involving context-setting, local Boolean framing, compatibility and negative selection. Those source-native operations cannot be promoted automatically to SRT Selection.

The Card must therefore record separately:

- context-setting / localization;
- filtering / compatibility;
- source-native selection language;
- SRT Selection eligibility;
- the evidence that licenses or blocks the mapping.

### 3.2 Actuality criterion must be separate from occurrence account

The Card must distinguish:

- `actuality_criterion`: what counts as an actual event/fact/process in the source domain;
- `occurrence_account_status`: whether the source explains, constrains, probabilizes, presupposes, or leaves open why that actuality occurs.

This protects the programme from turning a source's candidate-space organization into an invented GRG occurrence mechanism.

### 3.3 Selection must appear late in the Card

The Card should permit rich pre-Selection structure:

`localization -> proxy/cut -> distinction/equivalence -> reach/compatibility -> Selection-burden landscape`

without declaring SRT Selection until source-native actuality and the non-neutrality burden are separately paid.

### 3.4 Structural reopening must be distinguished from normative reconstructibility

A source may contain an internal structural route from a coarse/local description to a richer/global organization.

That is not automatically the same as the GRG requirement that a higher-order proxy preserve enough provenance and alternative cuts to permit future re-Selection.

The Card must therefore split:

- `structural_reopening_path`;
- `generative_reconstructibility`.

### 3.5 Non-identity guard is mandatory

The FRR case showed the need for an explicit block stating what must not be identified.

Examples from this fusion case include:

- Boolean frame != GRG proxy automatically;
- negative selection != SRT Selection automatically;
- Boolean germ != GRG objectification automatically;
- recoherence != GRG reconstructibility automatically;
- global-in-formation != SRT L2 automatically.

These can be structural neighbors or candidate role mappings without ontological identity.

## 4. Selection anti-tautology direction accepted, but not canonically closed

The dialogue narrowed the current OPEN problem.

SRT need not explain occurrence by adding a new physical collapse force merely to avoid tautology.

The narrower burden is to provide non-circular individuation / membership conditions for when a source-native actuality qualifies as SRT Selection rather than mere descriptive or modelled change.

The accepted research direction includes four provisional audit burdens:

- source-native occurrence / actuality;
- source-defensible event individuation;
- relational non-neutrality;
- representation invariance.

These are Card-level research criteria, not new canonical SRT definitions.

The canonical OPEN remains OPEN until separately hardened under the repository's semantic-edit rules.

## 6. Forward method

The next cases are not to be framed as adversarial or “enemy” tests.

They are **fusion cases** analogous to FRR:

`source-native reconstruction -> Card mapping -> fusion -> failure/empty-field detection -> Card revision -> cross-card reconciliation`.

The purpose is not to force a source into GRG and not to defeat the source.

The purpose is to let heterogeneous source structures modify the Card and, where warranted, modify GRG research grammar itself.
