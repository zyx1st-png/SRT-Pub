# Author adjudication — genuine reversible actuality remains Selection

## 1. Immediate question

The author was asked:

> 「一个真实发生、完全确定性、甚至动力学可逆的变化，只要它确实是 reality 中的 actuality，而不是分析者画出来的 state-transition，在你的最初直觉里，它是不是仍然属于 Selection？」

Author reply:

> 「是」

## 2. Bounded adjudication

Accepted:

~~~text
genuine actuality
+ deterministic dynamics
+ reversible / invertible governing law
-> may still be primitive Selection.

reversibility
!= primitive Selection exclusion criterion.

primitive Selection
!= a special subclass of real changes selected by irreversibility,
   novelty, retained history, probability, value or agency.
~~~

This confirms the author's long-standing option-A intuition for the reversible-actuality pressure case.

## 3. Consequence for CURRENT NEXT

The anti-tautology task should no longer primarily search for a new criterion that partitions genuine real changes into:

~~~text
Selection
vs
non-Selection real change.
~~~

Instead, after actuality itself is independently established, the central question becomes:

> **What non-redundant ontological / explanatory work is done by reading genuine determinate actuality as Selection under O0/S0, rather than as bare actuality / process occurrence?**

This is a research-route adjudication, not yet a canonical wording change.

## 4. What is not adjudicated by the author's “是”

The reply does **not** by itself settle:

- the exact necessary/sufficient conditions for actuality admission in every scientific domain;
- whether O0 and S0 are mutually irreducible;
- whether ontic determinism is true;
- whether all domain laws are reversible;
- a physical interpretation of quantum mechanics;
- why a specific outcome occurs here/now/this way;
- cross-event recurrence / universals / invariance;
- HP-B phenomenality;
- branch / merge identity.

## 5. Authorized next step

Proceed noncanonically to:

~~~text
O0/S0 non-redundancy
+
bare-actualism collapse test.
~~~

Required guard:

> If no non-circular explanatory, structural or prospective consequence survives, record the primitive Selection package as a metaphysical stance / compression choice with incremental explanatory indispensability NOT ESTABLISHED, rather than inventing an ad hoc event-membership criterion.
