# Author adjudication — L0 B+ minimal generative grammar

## Author decision

The author selected option **B**, with an explicit strengthening:

> “B，但是要加上L0 差异、垂直结构、主动性等。”

This decision is interpreted conservatively as **B+**.

## B+ intended L0 shape

L0 should remain thin, but it must not become a static or merely negative substrate. It should retain the **minimum generative grammar** required by the current canonical spine:

```text
L0 difference / minimum non-neutrality
-> subjectless Selection
-> manifestation + relative backgrounding
-> active verticalization / non-flat differentiation of selectable space
+ minimum finite positionality
+ minimum historical asymmetry / non-erasure of occurred differentiation
```

## Typing of “active”

The author’s “主动性” is not read as downstream `agency`, subject initiative, volition, cognition, or consciousness.

At L0 it means:

```text
Selection is generative rather than merely observational or passive;
it actively differentiates manifestation/backgrounding;
and thereby generates / regenerates a non-flat vertical relation for later selectability.
```

Preferred terms for L0 are therefore:

- `active Selection`
- `active verticalization`
- `generative activity`
- `active constitution`

Avoid using `agency` as the L0 label.

## Verticality boundary

The author’s request to retain vertical structure in L0 is typed as follows:

```text
L0 owns active verticalization / the generative non-flat relation.

L0 does NOT automatically own:
- recurrent formed vertical organization;
- One;
- Selection-position as a formed continuing One;
- Stable ISP;
- Bearer;
- subject-position.
```

Those require downstream formation / recurrence / standing burdens.

## Historical asymmetry boundary

B+ retains only a thin historical asymmetry:

```text
once a selective differentiation has occurred,
its occurrence cannot simply be treated as equivalent to never having occurred;
later conditions may be changed by retained difference / trace.
```

This does **not** automatically re-ratify stronger legacy claims such as:

- absolute physical-time irreversibility;
- granular sediment metaphysics;
- Landauer-based justification;
- universal thermodynamic irreversibility;
- any specific mathematical trace law.

## Items not automatically retained as independent L0 primitives

The author decision does not re-authorize these historical items as necessary independent L0 primitives:

```text
kappa_0 as an independently necessary primitive;
epsilon as an independently necessary primitive;
cost as an independent primitive rather than a downstream realization burden;
operator well-formedness as an ontological primitive;
legacy embodiment / concern / 承担 / subject-selection content.
```

They remain subject to reverse audit, retyping, demotion, simplification, or OPEN status.

## Current concise B+ formulation

```text
L0 is the minimum active generative grammar of selective differentiation:
there is pre-object difference / non-neutrality;
Selection is subjectless and generative;
Selection co-generates manifestation and relative backgrounding;
this activity creates a non-flat / vertical relation for later selectability;
every Selection is finitely positioned;
and occurred differentiation leaves at least a non-equivalence to “never occurred”.
```

Everything stronger must pay a separate downstream burden.
