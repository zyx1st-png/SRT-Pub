# Author adjudication — L0 B+ minimal generative grammar

## Author decision

The author selected option **B**, with an explicit strengthening:

> “B，但是要加上L0 差异、垂直结构、主动性等。”

This decision is interpreted conservatively as **B+**.

The author then added a process constraint before any final skeleton is canonically written:

> “认同节奏，需要加入创造者与Ai对齐的环节最终确定骨架”

This second decision changes the status of B+ from a directly writable skeleton to an **author-selected reconstruction direction that still requires creator–AI final-skeleton alignment**.

## B+ intended L0 shape

L0 should remain thin, but it must not become a static or merely negative substrate. It should retain the **minimum generative grammar** required by the current canonical spine:

```text
L0 difference / minimum non-neutrality
⊣ subjectless primitive Selection
Selection ⇒ manifestation + relative backgrounding
Selection ⇒ active verticalization / non-flat differentiation of selectable space
+ minimum finite positionality
+ minimum historical asymmetry / non-equivalence to never-occurrence
```

Relation typing:

```text
⊣ = prerequisite / admission relation, not a causal derivation of Selection;
⇒ = constitutive consequence, not necessarily a separate temporal event;
+ = additional constraint / burden, not an automatic next rung.
```

Therefore minimum non-neutrality does **not** by itself explain why actualisation occurs. Primitive Selection remains a separately admitted burden unless the author later changes that architecture.

## Typing of “active”

The author’s “主动性” is not read as downstream `agency`, subject initiative, volition, cognition, or consciousness.

At L0 it means:

```text
Selection is generative rather than merely observational or passive;
it actively differentiates manifestation/backgrounding;
and thereby generates / regenerates a non-flat vertical relation for later selectability.
```

Preferred terms for L0 are therefore:

- `active Selection`
- `active verticalization`
- `generative activity`
- `active constitution`

Avoid using `agency` as the L0 label.

B+ retains only a thin historical asymmetry:

```text

its occurrence cannot simply be treated as equivalent to never having occurred;
later conditions may be changed by retained difference / trace.
```

This does **not** automatically re-ratify stronger legacy claims such as:

- incompletely erasable effective difference in every declared complete relevant causal state;
- absolute physical-time irreversibility;
- granular sediment metaphysics;
- Landauer-based justification;
- universal thermodynamic irreversibility;
- any specific mathematical trace law.

The stronger IRR-B result therefore remains a separately scoped claim and must not be silently identified with the L0 minimum.

## Creator–AI final-skeleton alignment requirement

No AI-generated or AI-compressed B+ wording counts as the final L0 skeleton merely because it is internally coherent, passes review, or matches prior author remarks.

Before any C-risk rewrite of `Core_Law/SRT_L0_Metaphysics.md`, the creator and AI must complete an explicit alignment cycle:

```text
1. AI proposes the minimum typed skeleton and names every hidden commitment.
2. Creator explains each node and relation in the creator's own language.
3. AI restates that meaning and identifies any mismatch, ambiguity, redundancy, or silent inference.
4. Each node is pressure-tested by deletion, counterexample, and strongest-neighbor / mature-theory pressure where relevant.
5. Creator may KEEP, MERGE, MOVE DOWNSTREAM, OPEN, or RETIRE each node.
6. AI produces a revised whole skeleton rather than patching isolated sentences.
7. Creator explicitly adjudicates the revised whole skeleton.
8. Only that final creator-adjudicated skeleton can authorize canonical landing.
```

Required alignment questions for every proposed L0 node:

```text
Why must this be L0 rather than downstream?
What is lost if it is removed?
Is it truly distinct from adjacent nodes?
Does it add a new primitive burden or only rename another relation?
What does it forbid us from claiming?
What remains OPEN after keeping it?
```

Failure exit:

```text

return to the creator-description step.
Do not resolve disagreement by repository interpolation or majority model agreement.
```
