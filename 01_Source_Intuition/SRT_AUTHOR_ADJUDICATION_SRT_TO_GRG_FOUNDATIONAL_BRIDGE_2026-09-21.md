# Author adjudication — SRT to GRG foundational bridge and vertical / horizontal research

> Role: author-owned correction and programme re-prioritization after the first GRG framework / transfer cycle.
>
> This record does not edit canonical ontology, redefine canonical L0/L1/L2, establish a universal GRG, or reopen a broad canonical landing. It records the author’s current intended bridge from the reconstructed SRT direction to GRG and corrects an over-reading of the RNN calibration.

## 1. Current author correction

The present priority is not to accelerate GRG toward an MVP, an agent, AGI, or another sequence of transfer demonstrations.

The immediate task is to construct the basic bridge:

~~~text
reconstructed SRT
-> generative research orientation
-> vertical / horizontal interface
-> generative relation
-> candidate relational grammar
-> GRG research architecture
~~~

This bridge is especially important because the SRT reconstruction has only recently changed the programme’s starting picture. GRG must grow from that reconstructed direction rather than inherit older objectified assumptions under new terminology.

## 2. Horizontal research — author meaning

The author proposes the following programme-level reading.

Traditional horizontal research typically begins after an objectification has already stabilized relatively static / objective entities, variables, units, boundaries, state spaces or categories as the first-order research givens.

It then studies transformations, interactions and causal / statistical relations among those objectified entities in order to obtain regularities, laws and generative predictions within that stabilized object space.

Therefore:

~~~text
horizontal research
!= static research
!= non-causal research
!= non-predictive research
~~~

Horizontal research may be highly dynamic, causal, mathematical and predictively powerful.

Its characteristic starting commitment is instead:

~~~text
the relevant objects / units / variables / state space
are already sufficiently stabilized to serve as the first research floor
for the question being asked.
~~~

Its generative prediction is therefore normally generation / transformation within an admitted objectification.

## 3. Vertical research — author meaning

Vertical research takes generativity itself as the primary research object.

It asks what generative organization lies behind the formation, stabilization, transformation and re-formation of the objects, boundaries, scales, state spaces and positions that horizontal research can then treat as given.

The vertical question is therefore not merely:

~~~text
given A and B, what transformation maps A to B?
~~~

but also:

~~~text
how did A / B / their boundary / their state space / their effective relation
become available as stable generative objects in the first place?

what generative relations recur across many such objects?

how do formed organizations alter the conditions of later formation?
~~~

The vertical research aspiration is to study generative regularities across multiple objectifications and domains, and thereby reach evolution / transformation relations deeper than the laws available only inside any one fixed object space.

## 4. Vertical depth is not micro-scale depth

The author’s vertical / horizontal distinction is not:

~~~text
micro = vertical
macro = horizontal
~~~

and not:

~~~text
hidden physical cause = vertical
observable effect = horizontal
~~~

Verticality concerns generative dependency / constitution / reconstitution depth, not smaller spatial scale.

A higher-order formed organization may be vertically relevant if it becomes a real condition on later generation.

A lower-level variable may remain merely horizontal if it is already treated as a fixed object inside a predeclared model.

## 5. Horizontal and vertical are continuous, not rival sciences

The author does not reject horizontal science.

A more accurate relationship is:

~~~text
vertical generation
-> formation / stabilization of objects, boundaries, scales and capacities
-> horizontal effective relations and laws
-> intervention / consequence / history
-> reconstruction or pressure on later generative conditions
-> renewed vertical inquiry
~~~

Thus:

~~~text
V -> H -> V'
~~~

is a better programme image than a simple vertical-versus-horizontal dualism.

Horizontal research can deliver extremely strong local laws and predictions.

Vertical research asks under what generative organization those laws and objects hold, how that organization changes, and which deeper relations recur across otherwise different object domains.

## 6. Ontology / epistemology interface

The author’s vertical programme is intended to reconnect ontology and epistemology more deeply than a classical object-first picture permits.

This does not mean:

~~~text
world-side formation = public objectification
~~~

or:

~~~text
ontology = epistemology
~~~

The distinction remains necessary.

The intended bridge is:

~~~text
world-side generative formation
-> positioned access / interaction
-> objectification / representation / stabilization
-> horizontal inquiry
-> intervention / consequence
-> revised objectification and revised generative understanding
~~~

Inquiry itself occurs from formed positions inside reality. Therefore the process by which stable research objects are produced, transported and revised becomes part of the same broader generative story without reducing reality to cognition or language.

## 7. RNN calibration correction

The RNN spectral / decoder work must not be read as showing that vertical structure cannot have causal efficacy.

The author’s current correction is:

~~~text
tested decoder / spectral trace
= an L2-facing / objectified cross-section of a realized system

tested trace
!= vertical generative organization itself
~~~

“L2-facing” here is an author programme interpretation of the tested observable as a convergence-history / stable-constraint-facing slice. It does not redefine canonical L2.

The NULL results therefore support the bounded statement:

~~~text
this selected L2 / objectified trace
did not earn the tested history-specific causal-mediation burden
under the frozen interventions.
~~~

They do not establish:

~~~text
vertical generative organization has no causal efficacy
vertical organization cannot re-enter later Selection
the relevant vertical organization has been directly isolated and falsified
~~~

The stronger lesson is an objectification warning:

~~~text
a measurable trace of a generative history
must not be identified with the vertical generative organization
without an independent bridge from trace -> organization -> later generation.
~~~

## 8. SRT -> GRG bridge

The author accepts the following programme route as the current starting scaffold:

~~~text
1. Selection
   non-flat generative differentiation

2. relationality
   actual differentiation is not an isolated object event;
   manifestation / backgrounding / support / exclusion / accessibility
   are relationally structured

3. formation
   generative differentiation can form relatively stable organization

4. recurrence / re-entry / reconstruction
   formed organization can participate in and alter later generative conditions

5. cross-object / cross-domain recurrence
   some generative dependency organizations may recur across different
   objectifications without implying identical mechanisms

6. grammar
   recurring, composable, constrainable and revisable generative relations
   become candidates for a generative relational grammar

7. reflexive learning
   inquiry itself compares, tests and revises that grammar
~~~

This is a programme bridge, not a completed derivation theorem.

## 9. Consequence for GRG priority

GRG should not yet be treated primarily as:

- a transfer benchmark;
- an M-level ladder;
- a relation-example collection;
- a minimum viable agent;
- an AGI architecture.

Those may become later consequences.

The current primary problem is:

> What minimal generative architecture follows from reconstructed SRT before domain examples and transfer metrics are allowed to define GRG by accumulation?

Existing relation records, X3/X4, M3/M4 work, RNN/HKB calibration and civilizational cases remain valuable evidence / pressure assets.

They no longer determine the foundational sequence.

## 10. G0-G11 status under this correction

The provisional G0-G11 burdens remain useful discovery scaffolds.

They are not yet accepted as the final GRG skeleton or as a linear ontological ladder.

The foundational bridge should first determine the minimal relation families required by SRT’s reconstructed generative direction.

Only then should G0-G11 be retained, merged, split, retyped or retired.

## 11. Generative expectation / normativity

The previous author direction remains active:

~~~text
generative relation
-> structural generative expectation
-> formed-position anticipatory realization where applicable
-> indexed comparison of actual trajectory
-> normative direction
~~~

The new vertical / horizontal clarification strengthens the question:

> Does GRG describe only transformations inside an already objectified option space, or can it also describe how the field of future objects / relations / options is itself generated and reconstructed?

The latter is the distinctive vertical burden now requiring foundational clarification.

Full moral ought remains OPEN.
