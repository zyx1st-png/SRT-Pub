## 2. Accepted A — structural objectivity precedes normative standing

The author accepts:

```text
structural evaluability
!= stake-bearing standing
```

A trajectory can be objectively compared for properties such as:

```text
continuation conditions;
coordination across declared positions;
consequence externalization / return topology;
reorganizability / generative reselectability;
```

without first proving Bearer, Concern, subjecthood, consciousness or agency.

But stronger grammar such as:

```text
“this is better / worse for this continuing entity”
```

requires a genuine exposure / stake-standing relation and cannot be obtained from causal influence alone.

## 4. Accepted C — Concern / d is stake organization, not the admission floor

The author accepts the separation:

```text
Bearer-like exposure asks:
who / what is genuinely exposed to the consequence?

Concern / d asks:
which consequence dimensions enter this formed system's own
stake / utility / risk organization, and how strongly?
```

Therefore:

```text
no Concern proven
-/>
no possible normative exposure
```

and:

```text
strong Concern
-/>
objective goodness of what is cared about.
```

`d` remains useful for endogenous stake weighting / stake coupling. It does not by itself grant moral superiority, moral status or objective validity.

Current guards remain:

```text
Bearer -> Concern : OPEN / NOT ESTABLISHED
Concern -> Bearer : OPEN / NOT ESTABLISHED
```

## 5. Accepted D — patient-side standing and responsibility-side agency are separate

The author accepts:

```text
capacity to be normatively affected
!=
capacity to bear responsibility / obligation.
```

Hence:

```text
no agency
-/>
no normative standing
```

and:

```text
Bearer
-/>
responsibility-bearing agency.
```

Agency becomes relevant when the question concerns such things as:

```text
capacity to revise action in light of returned consequences;
responsibility attribution;
obligation-bearing;
answerability for delegated or controlled selection.
```

It is not required merely for being affected.

## 6. Accepted E — scope-indexed objective comparison and generative objectivity

The author rejects objective value as:

```text
one universe-global scalar;
one complete ranking over all possible worlds;
one God-view value vector;
one terminal optimum already encoded at L0.
```

The accepted reconstruction direction is:

```text
objective comparative support
= robustness of a relational ordering
  under defensible expansion of the comparison scope.
```

A comparison scope may include declared:

```text
positions / candidate stake-bearers;
scale;
time horizon;
interfaces;
consequence channels;
perturbation range;
```

If an apparent advantage reverses as materially affected positions, delayed consequences, hidden interfaces or severed return channels are restored, the original ordering is scope-fragile.

If an ordering survives justified scope expansion and remains structurally accountable under the declared comparator, it receives stronger objective support.

This does not establish a total order. Partial orders, incomparability, unresolved tradeoffs and scope dependence remain admissible.

### Generative objectivity

The author also accepts the reconstruction direction that objectivity is stronger when the comparison procedure can revise its own:

```text
relevance boundary;
weighting;
comparison rules;
candidate-generation conditions;
```

in response to consequences from positions or interfaces previously omitted.

Thus:

```text
static comprehensiveness
!= generative objectivity.
```

A system that claims to contain all values but cannot reopen its own comparison boundary under returned consequences may be less objectively adequate than a bounded but revisable comparison process.

## 7. Current inclusion-boundary problem remains OPEN

The author has not yet closed the exact rule for:

```text
which affected position / process / time horizon / enabling condition
must enter the comparison scope.
```

The strongest current machine candidate is consequence-based relevance:

```text
inclusion relevance is triggered when the compared trajectory
produces a material consequence for a position / process,
or materially depends on that position / process as an enabling condition.
```

But this is not yet author-adjudicated as a necessary-and-sufficient rule.

Retain the guard:

```text
absence of feedback
!= irrelevance.
```

A consequence that lands where no effective feedback channel exists is a prime candidate externalization / omission case, not a reason for exclusion.
