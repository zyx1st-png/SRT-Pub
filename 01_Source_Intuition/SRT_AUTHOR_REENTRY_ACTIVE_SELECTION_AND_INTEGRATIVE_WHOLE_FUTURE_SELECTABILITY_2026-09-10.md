# Author adjudication — active Selection threshold and SRT as an integrative whole

## 1. Exact author wording

> **「认同 继续 另外我觉得SRT 也是这种整体大于部分 不需要在竞争理论的部分里面去找新颖性。而更应该关注如何整合现有理论，产生共同重构未来可选性的能力。」**

## 2. Author acceptance of the active/passive transition candidate

The opening **“认同”** accepts the immediately preceding machine candidate at the high-level intended meaning:

> **主动 Selection 的最低门槛，在于 Selection 不再只在既有垂直结构里维持当前 One，而开始作用于“决定以后什么能够被选择”的垂直结构本身。**

Minimum licensed distinction:

```text
PASSIVE Selection:
primarily sustains the continued existence / viability of the current One
within an achieved selectable organization.

ACTIVE Selection:
acts on / reconstructs the organization that determines later selectability itself.
```

This author acceptance does not yet select a unique formal metric or implementation mechanism.

## 3. SRT itself as a whole-greater-than-parts integration

The author additionally shifts the role of adjacent / competing theories.

Do not organize the programme mainly as:

```text
neighbor theory A pays claim X;
neighbor theory B pays claim Y;
find a leftover claim Z and call that SRT novelty.
```

The intended direction is instead:

```text
preserve what mature neighboring theories explain well;
assign their strongest contributions to appropriate explanatory roles;
integrate them without identity collapse;
use SRT's Selection organization to connect those roles;
seek an emergent capacity to jointly reconstruct future selectability.
```

Thus SRT is itself proposed as a kind of **whole greater than the sum of theoretical parts**: its value need not depend on every component concept being unique in isolation.

## 4. Integration target

The author names the constructive target as:

> **“产生共同重构未来可选性的能力”**

Minimum safe interpretation:

```text
integration success is not merely conceptual coverage;
it should enable a coordinated account of how multiple processes / subjects / scales
can together alter what can become selectable in the future.
```

This can include theoretical explanation, model composition, and eventually intervention design, but no specific scientific implementation is authorized here.

## 5. Boundary against novelty hunting

This ruling does **not** mean:

```text
comparative pressure is abandoned;
all neighboring theories are automatically compatible;
contradictions should be hidden by translation;
SRT superiority is established by broad synthesis;
any eclectic collection counts as integration.
```

Strongest-neighbor work remains useful to:

```text
prevent false novelty claims;
identify which theory genuinely owns which explanatory burden;
find incompatibilities and non-composable assumptions;
keep source-native semantics intact.
```

But its primary role is now **diagnostic and routing**, not a tournament whose goal is to leave SRT with an isolated proprietary residual.

## 6. Current integrative orientation

A provisional, noncanonical integration grammar is:

```text
pre-object difference / constitution
-> Selection-generated vertical / selectable organization
-> One / Selection-position formation
-> passive survival / maintenance
-> active reconstruction of later selectability
-> anticipatory commitment toward not-yet-actual futures
-> multi-position / multi-subject mutual Selection
-> jointly reconstructed future selectable space
-> multi-subject order and whole-level capacities.
```

Adjacent theories may contribute strong local accounts at different segments of this grammar. SRT's current task is to test whether these contributions can be composed into a coherent cross-level framework without flattening their differences.

## 7. Open burden

The next machine task is not “find what competitors lack.” It is:

> **Build and pressure an integration architecture that shows what each mature theory contributes, where assumptions conflict, what translation is legitimate, and whether their composition under SRT generates a real additional capacity for jointly reconstructing future selectability rather than a mere list of analogies.**
