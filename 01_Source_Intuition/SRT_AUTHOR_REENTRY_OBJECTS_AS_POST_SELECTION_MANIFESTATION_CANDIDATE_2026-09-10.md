# Author candidate — all objects as post-Selection manifestation

## 0. Exact author statement

> **「继续，有一个强命题可以考虑一下：所有对象都是选择后显现的部分，SRT 想把这种显现不当成默认，获得显现之前和 L0 和选择作为显现的要素的视野」**

This record preserves the statement as an **author-proposed strong candidate for consideration**. It is not yet a final adjudication, canonical theorem, Level verdict, empirical claim, or permission to rewrite `Core_Law/SRT_L0_Metaphysics.md`.

---

## 1. Minimum faithful reading

The narrowest machine reading that preserves the author's direction is:

```text
for anything functioning as a determinate object,
its objecthood is not the explanatory starting floor;

objecthood is encountered only after a differentiating Selection
has produced a manifest / foreground side;

SRT therefore refuses to treat manifestation as default givenness
and keeps in view two earlier ontological roles:

(1) L0 / pre-object condition;
(2) Selection as the constitutive differentiation by which
    something becomes manifest / foregrounded.
```

This reading is stronger than merely saying:

```text
observers only know objects through appearances.
```

The intended pressure is ontological / constitutive:

```text
objecthood itself is downstream of Selection,
not merely our knowledge of an independently completed object.
```

---

## 2. Immediate alignment with current canonical L0

Current canonical `Core_Law/SRT_L0_Metaphysics.md` already states:

```text
any determinate manifest actuality is produced by Selection;
actual occurrence != persistent anchoring != Stable ISP;
Selection does not require a prior selector / bearer.
```

Therefore the author candidate is **directionally continuous** with current L0.

However, the phrase:

```text
所有对象都是选择后显现的部分
```

adds two burdens that should not be silently read into the canonical owner without audit:

```text
A. universal quantification over objecthood;
B. the explicit foreground / partial-manifestation interpretation of objecthood.
```

Both remain under review here.

---

## 3. Important non-idealism guard

The candidate does **not** mean:

```text
objects are created by human observation;
objects exist only in minds;
anything can be made manifest arbitrarily;
L0 is an unknowable mental projection;
Selection requires consciousness.
```

Current SRT source material instead requires:

```text
pre-object conditions are world-side / constraint-bearing;
Selection may be subjectless;
manifestation is constrained and non-arbitrary;
object stability may later become strongly anchored and cross-operator.
```

---

## 4. What “获得……视野” can safely mean at this stage

Do not read the author's phrase as claiming direct empirical sight of L0.

A safer current distinction is:

```text
DIRECT OBSERVATION
-> already returns a manifest / foreground result.

ONTOLOGICAL / EXPLANATORY VISIBILITY
-> can still keep explicit the roles that a post-manifest object description omits:
   pre-object condition + Selection relation + manifestation result.
```

Thus SRT may seek a **view of the generative order** without claiming a God's-eye direct measurement of the pre-object field.
