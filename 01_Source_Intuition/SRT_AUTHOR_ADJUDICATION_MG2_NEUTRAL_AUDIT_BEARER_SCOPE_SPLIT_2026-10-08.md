## 2. Bounded interpretation of option A (editorial implementation)

The existing programme's three **distinct questions** remain visible and unchanged:

~~~text
ACCESS — conscious/global availability of a target
CONTROL — ability of the target to bias/reconfigure later control/gating
OWNERSHIP — stronger question about continuing Bearer/Concern/consequence integration
~~~

For **neutral-science MG-2 work**, a bounded audit may be assessed on:

1. fidelity to source-native mature explanations and their actual evidence;
2. distinct access/target maintenance/control allocation/structuring-bias roles, with dissociations and negative cases;
3. properly separated empirical **agency feeling** and **agency judgment/attribution** (and body ownership where relevant), including measurement limitations;
4. explicit comparison limits, unresolved questions, no inflated novelty, and independent review where required.

These are **review dimensions, not a newly imposed checklist of experiment thresholds**. They do not declare current MG-2 scientific work complete by themselves; the audit must actually satisfy its scope and evidential burden before any completion claim.

The SRT **Bearer / Concern / returning consequences as one's own** question is retained as a **separate OPEN theoretical interpretation/reconstruction problem**. Its direct empirical readout **has not been admitted**. An illustrative older bridge §14 table that assigns low/high/high-ish "ownership" to case examples is not a validated O3 measure; O1/O2 empirical agency data are not O3 by identity.

~~~text

Bearer / Concern / ontic OWNERSHIP: OPEN
neutral-audit completion != Bearer solution
~~~

Do **not** silently delete the OWNERSHIP question from the programme or declare that Bearer is irrelevant to the overarching SRT inquiry; keep it routeable in the distinct OPEN layer.
