## 1. Authorized Core Reconstruction Modules (`Q01, Q02, Q08–Q15`)

### 1.1 `Q01` — Noumenal Existence (`物自体`), Physical Appearance, and Psychological Value-Supervision (Dual Parallel Extraction)
- **Historical grounding (`H`)**: `[[物理]]属性是[[存在，具现，背景，指向]]的[[表象]]，[[心理]]属性是对表象选择的[[价值判断]]，[[物自体]]是存在本身，心理监督物理` (`F_4804`); `经验和生理之间不需要也不应该假设桥梁，有的只有经验与[[物自体]]、生理与物自体之间的桥梁，以避免我们建立[[主从]]的关系联系` (`F_4749`); `物理过程本不是一切认知的基础和[[核心]]，它只是我们构建的某一种认知事物（超出物理定义）的[[观察方法]]` (`F_5318`).
- **Authorized contemporary formulation (`N`)**:
  1. Primitive existence / Selection reality (`L0` / `存在，具现，背景，指向`) occupies the structural position of the **noumenal ground (`物自体`)** prior to the physical/mental split.
  2. **Physical descriptions** (state/attribute cuts) and **psychological experiences** (felt directional/evaluative readings) are **parallel selective extractions** from the same primitive reality rather than a master-slave reduction (`生理 -/> 经验` as unilateral epiphenomenon).
  3. Within formed `Bearer / Agency` systems, psychological value-judgment exercises downward supervisory reallocation over physical energy and attention budgets (`心理监督物理`), without implying substance dualism or cosmic idealism.

### 1.2 `Q02` — De-anthropomorphized Four-Regime Selection Lens (`物择 / 生物择 / 人择 / 神择`, `R3` Candidate Calibration)
- **Historical grounding (`H`)**: `所有[[属性]]和[[存在，具现，背景，指向]]都是[[物择]]、[[人择]]和[[神择]]与[[连接]]共同作用下的[[产物]]` (`F_4817`); `人择 生命择 物择` (`F_2675`).

    1. **`物择` (Physical Selection)**: Subjectless, non-teleological symmetry breaking, phase transitions, and dissipative stabilization (`S0 -> One`);

### 1.3 `Q08` — The Dual-Model Three-Conflict Matrix of Sensation, Perception, and Consciousness (Heuristic Loci)
- **Historical grounding (`H`)**: `[[预测模型]]是关于[[事物]][[是]]什么的[[模型]]，是[[属性]]的集合，[[意愿模型]]是关于事物[[应该]]是什么的模型，是[[变化]]的集合` (`F_4066`); `[[感觉]]是[[意愿模型]]和[[经验]]的[[冲突]]（比如情绪和逃跑反应）。[[知觉]]是[[预测模型]]和经验的冲突（即认知和记忆）。[[意识]]是意愿模型和预测模型的冲突（比如内省、价值判断）` (`F_5087`); `[[现象意识]]是[[意愿]]模型和[[经验]]之间的[[冲突]]，[[访问意识]]是[[预测]]模型和意愿模型之间的冲突` (`F_5205`); `自我反思和[[内省]]的行为即[[意识]]是去[[同步]]化主动[[预测]]的世界和[[被动]]获取信息的世界` (`F_5599`).
- **Authorized contemporary formulation (`N`, calibrated as `P2/P3` heuristic classification; refined by `2026-11-22 X1`)**:
  - Separate internal generative modeling into **`预测模型` (Predictive/Descriptive Model: what is / attribute-set)** and **`意愿模型` (Volitional/Normative trajectories: what ought to change / directional transition-set — later clarified in `2026-11-22 X1` as concurrent Selection-differentiated processes without a single global `M_will` controller)**.
  - Treat the three conflicts as **salient functional conflict loci** rather than necessary-and-sufficient definitions (coherent low-conflict perception and conscious experience also persist):
    1. **Sensation / Phenomenal Consciousness locus (`感觉 / P-意识`)** ← conflict between **`意愿模型` trajectories and current `经验`** (affective urgency, valence, visceral alarm);
    2. **Perception updating locus (`知觉`)** ← conflict between **`预测模型` and current `经验`** (epistemic updating, discrepancy calibration);
    3. **Access Consciousness / Introspection locus (`访问意识 / A-意识 / 内省`)** ← conflict between **`意愿模型` trajectories and `预测模型`** (deliberative arbitration and re-synchronization of active directional expectation with descriptive world-constraints).
