# Author adjudication — Bearer prospective self-indexing + same-One exposure

> **Role:** record the author's explicit decision at the post-#942 Bearer gate. This source fixes the current reconstruction direction only. It does not edit canonical owners, assign a Level, establish scientific distinctiveness, equate Bearer with `承担`, or settle subjecthood / phenomenality.

## 1. Immediate author decision

The author was presented with three options:

```text
A — Bearer onset = P only
B — Bearer onset = P + E
C — keep sufficiency OPEN; P necessary-increment + E live candidate only
```

where:

```text
P = prospective self-indexing:
    future branches are endogenously differentiated by what this One itself
    may undergo / preserve / lose / enable.

E = same-One prospective exposure:
    at least some potential consequences are attributable to this One's
    continuation in a way that cannot be completely reassigned while claiming
    the same Bearer relation.
```

The author replied:

> **“B”**

```text
B / P + E: AUTHOR-ACCEPTED
```

## 2. Current Bearer reconstruction criterion

For the current R1 reconstruction, Bearer onset requires the conjunction:

```text
already admitted One / Selection-position
+
P — prospective self-indexing
+
E — same-One prospective exposure / non-outsourcing
-> Bearer standing candidate accepted at the reconstruction level.
```

Expanded reading:

> A formed One becomes Bearer-relevant when possible future branches are differentiated from within that One's own lineage-conditioned organization by what that continuing One itself may undergo, preserve, lose or become able/unable to continue, **and** at least some such branch-dependent consequences genuinely expose that same One such that the consequence burden cannot be completely reassigned to another unit while leaving the claimed Bearer relation unchanged.

This is a **prospective standing relation**, not a requirement that a harmful or costly consequence has already occurred.

---

## 3. Why both P and E are required

### P without E is insufficient

A system may predict or regulate future states in self-referential language while the actual burden remains fully externalizable to another unit, operator, replaceable service or broader coupled system.

Therefore:

```text
self-modelled prediction != Bearer;
prospective self-relevance alone != Bearer;
viability-sensitive regulation alone != Bearer.
```

### E without P is insufficient

A formed One may be causally exposed to shocks, costs or historical changes without prospectively differentiating future branches in terms of what the continuing position itself may undergo.

Therefore:

```text
being exposed != Bearer;
being changed != Bearer;
retrospective consequence return != Bearer;
history-to-reconstitution dependence != Bearer by itself.
```

The accepted increment is the conjunction:

```text
prospective self-indexing
+
same-One non-outsourcable exposure.
```
