# Author Re-entry Cycle 2 — pre-object-field reconstitution ruling

> **Role:** record the author's clarification after Pass 2 compressed the remaining pressure into a choice between parameter learning inside a fixed candidate/comparison structure and deeper reconstitution of the conditions from which later Selection can arise.
>
> **Boundary:** this is an author-source meaning ruling only. It does not assign Level 1 or Level 2, does not canonically introduce a new primitive or symbol, and does not establish that any current empirical implementation uniquely realizes the clarified ontology.

---

## 1. Author clarification

> **selection 之后不只是显性的权重改变，更多的是前对象场（垂直结构）的改变，显性权重只是后果而不是本体。**

Status:

```text
AUTHOR-CONFIRMED MEANING CLARIFICATION
```

---

## 2. Minimum licensed distinction

The author rejects the interpretation that SED-B is exhausted by an implementation-level parameter update such as:

```text
weight change
threshold change
attractor-depth change
choice-probability change
```

These may be downstream traces, carriers or consequences of Selection history in a particular realization, but they do not by themselves state the ontological content of the sedimentation claim.

The stronger author meaning is:

```text
Selection_t
-> changes the pre-object field / vertical relational structure
   from which later manifestation/background differentiation can be generated
-> implementation-level weights / thresholds / probabilities may change as a consequence
   of that deeper reconstitution
```

Safe asymmetry:

```text
explicit parameter change
may realize / register / follow
pre-object-field reconstitution

but

explicit parameter change
!= the ontology of the reconstitution
```

---

## 3. Meaning of “pre-object field” in this ruling

`前对象场` is retained here as the author's source-language phrase.

It must **not** yet be promoted into a new canonical object, layer, scalar or physical field.

The minimum licensed role is:

```text
a relational field prior to completed object / One stabilization,
within which vertical manifestation/background differentiation is generated
and from which a Selection-position can be formed or reconstituted.
```

The present ruling does not decide:

- whether this field is continuous or discrete;
- whether it is neural, physical, informational or cross-domain;
- whether it has a single formal state-space representation;
- whether “candidate set” is an adequate ontology-level description;
- whether every implementation must expose explicit weights.

Accordingly, machine-side phrases such as `candidate space`, `comparison ontology` or `admissibility set` remain audit translations, not author definitions.

---

## 4. Relation to VG and SED-B

The author clarification sharpens the process/result architecture already fixed by VG and SED-B.

Current minimum reading:

```text
VG_t
= active vertical differentiation / generation in the pre-object field

Selection_t
= actual differentiation in which one relational direction manifests
  while alternatives are relatively backgrounded / suppressed

SED-B_t
= retained post-Selection change in the pre-object field / vertical structure
  that conditions later formation / reconstitution

VG_(t+1)
= later vertical generation occurs from a field already altered by prior Selection
```

Thus the live recursive structure is not merely:

```text
choice
-> parameter update
-> changed future choice probability
```

but, at the author-meaning level:

```text
vertical generation in pre-object field
-> Selection
-> retained reconstitution of that field
-> changed conditions of later vertical generation
```

---

## 5. Ontology / realization separation

This ruling explicitly separates:

```text
ontological role
from
implementation-level realization / readout
```

Possible implementation-level changes may include:

```text
synaptic weight
threshold
recurrent gain
coupling strength
boundary condition
attractor geometry
feature salience
transition probability
```

But none is licensed as an identity with the author-owned ontological change.

Therefore:

```text
adaptive WTA changes weights
```

is insufficient by itself to show that the strongest-neighbor family has absorbed the clarified SRT meaning.

A fair rival must show that its own source-native mechanism changes something structurally equivalent to the later **field of differentiation** itself, rather than merely tuning strengths among already fixed alternatives.

---

## 6. Strongest-neighbor consequence

Pass 2 remains valid in two respects:

```text
fixed-law WTA can pay much of machine-side VG*
VG alone is not a safe Level-1 residual
```

But its second pressure must now be narrowed.

The following is no longer sufficient for absorption:

```text
competitive outcome
-> retained parameter change
-> changed future competition
```

because the author has clarified that explicit parameter change is a consequence / realization-side manifestation, not the ontology being asserted.

The stronger comparison becomes:

> Does the mature adaptive rival merely retune an already constituted competitive field, or does its own source-native mechanism reconstitute the relational field from which later alternatives, comparisons and manifestation/background structure themselves become available?

This question remains open.

---

## 7. Anti-protection guard

The new ruling must not be used to protect SRT by definition.

Do **not** reason:

```text
neighbor changes weights
-> therefore it cannot change the pre-object field
```

A mature learning / developmental / self-organizing rival may alter representational geometry, effective variables, category boundaries, latent manifolds, topology, coupling structure or the effective space of future differentiation.

If such a rival pays the source-neutral causal role without using SRT vocabulary, the absorption pressure remains real.

Therefore the next audit must ask for a source-neutral discriminator for **field reconstitution**, not merely rename the desired residual `pre-object field`.
