## A1 — accepted direction: change relative to background, not bare delta

Accepted direction:

~~~text
physical change alone
!= the main target;

current formed background / expectation
x current sound actuality
-> deformation of the currently available continuation structure.
~~~

A simple `note_t -> note_(t+1)` difference is insufficient because the same local event may carry different musical significance under different prior tonal, rhythmic, grouping or learned histories.

Working statement:

> The relevant change is a change **relative to a currently active generative background**, not merely an acoustic delta.

## A2 — accepted direction: detection, feeling and explicit objectification must be separated

The dialogue converged on a three-way distinction:

~~~text
1. structural registration
   = a regularity / violation / transition is processed;

2. phenomenal foregrounding
   = a change is lived as tension, release, suspension, surprise,
     continuity, interruption, completion or another qualitative form;

3. explicit objectification
   = the listener can identify / report an object such as note, chord,
     key change, phrase boundary, rhythm or error.
~~~

Therefore:

~~~text
detected change
!= phenomenal foreground;

phenomenal foreground
!= explicit objectification.
~~~

Unconscious / preattentive auditory regularity detection is a required negative control.

## A4 — accepted direction: within-geometry deformation and geometry recutting are different

The dialogue distinguished at least two candidate classes.

### A4.1 Directional deformation within a still-stable organization

Example phenomenology:

~~~text

~~~

The current cut / grouping may remain usable while future continuation becomes strongly non-flat.

Candidate interpretation:

~~~text

~~~

### A4.2 Failure / revision of the current organization

Example phenomenology:

~~~text

~~~

Here the burden is stronger:

~~~text

~~~

Therefore:

~~~text

does not universally imply recutting.
~~~

## A5 — accepted direction: actual != complete

A central accepted distinction is:

~~~text
already actual
!= already complete.
~~~

A musical event, sentence fragment, action phase or unresolved thought may be fully actual **now** while still carrying an unresolved relation to possible continuation.

Working phrase used in the dialogue:

~~~text
prospective dependence
~~~

This is a temporary explanatory label, not a new SRT term-of-art.

Its intended burden is:

> Current formed organization may remain dependent on some **not-yet-arrived / not-yet-fulfilled continuation relation** for local resolution, stability or completion.
>
> Type guard: organizational incompletion does **not** mean that the current SRT actualisation / Selection occurrence is itself incomplete. A terminal Selection can remain genuine even when the formed musical organization is experienced as unresolved.

This is stronger than:

~~~text
"the system predicts a particular next object."
~~~

A system may be uncertain about the next event while still strongly registering that the present cannot yet close.

## A6 — accepted direction: completion, interruption and recutting must be separated

Working decomposition:

~~~text
resolution
= an open continuation dependence is discharged
  while the formed whole is preserved / stabilized;

interruption
= continuation opportunity terminates
  while the prior organization remains retrospectively unresolved;

recutting / reinterpretation
= later information changes the cut / grouping / relation
  under which the earlier sequence is understood.
~~~

These can produce different phenomenology even when the external sequence simply stops.

Therefore:

~~~text
physical cessation
!= generative completion.
~~~
