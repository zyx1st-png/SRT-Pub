## 1. Direct author statements

### A0-Q1 — the flattening concern

> 「我觉得AI辅助研究有一个最大的问题，就是容易把理论压平。也就是说，AI只适合研究于水平结构，而无法对垂直结构的内容进行研究。所以在数学这种水平赛道上，AI发挥很好，但是碰到经济学、物理学等，就会显得无法有突破性的进展。」

**Scope guard:** the awkward wording 「只适合研究于水平结构」 is verbatim **[sic]**, not a transcription error to silently fix. This is the author's working critique, **not** a verified impossibility claim across mathematics, physics, economics or future AI. A narrower **M** candidate: present systems may perform better when a problem's objectification and success criterion are already supplied than when they must prospectively reconstruct what distinctions and background conditions matter. “Horizontal/vertical” must be scored independently of who succeeds, or the hypothesis becomes unfalsifiable.

### A0-Q2 — non-manifest background pressure

> 「这种垂直关系相比普通的水平关系不光是生成性的。更重要的是保存了被潜藏部分的压力。也就是说，任何符号都在本质上是对背景的提取和抽取。但往往之后，符号会获得全部关注，对于背景提取和抽取会被忽略掉。」

**Scope guard:** “压力” is direct author language. Do not silently identify it with physical energy, canonical `Ψ_f`, a quantitative uncertainty score, all unexpressed information, or a fixed store of latent objects. Keep **two possible scopes apart**: (a) **event-level constitutive non-neutrality / foreground–relative-background asymmetry**, which does not require a stored trace; (b) **retained later-effective constraint**, which needs the Spine §4 history/retention burden. Whether the author means one, both in a relation or something stronger is **OPEN**.

### A0-Q3 — the portable symbol and positioned activation

> 「符号是与位置无关的。但符号往往在位置（主体）中才发挥作用。所以符号就像信号在发送端和接收端需要压缩和解压缩一样」

**Scope guard:** “位置（主体）” is author language in a communication analogy; canonical **finite-position-indexed Selection ≠ formed One / Selection-position ≠ Bearer ≠ conscious subject** remains. “符号与位置无关” may describe transportable form, **not** a verdict that its situated interpretation and effects are position-free. The more useful **M** analogy is: a received sign enters a different history/position and may help **generate a new operative relation**, not reconstruct the numerically same original episode. The earlier 2026-10-10 Q0/Q6 portable-form-versus-position-use wording tension was **OPEN**; Q3 offers fresh author evidence relevant to it but does **not** by itself close it.

## 3. Machine-side analytic distinctions to preserve (NOT author decisions)

1. **Transportable sign vs situated efficacy.** One string can remain identical across sites while the interpretations, constraints, responses and future access differ. It does not follow that every site creates a formed SRT Selection-position.
2. **Extraction vs background pressure.** A publicly usable sign records a bounded distinction; whether excluded/unexpressed relations remain operative requires a declared scene and evidence. Relative background is not assumed to be an enumerated counterfactual choice set.
3. **Decoding vs reconstruction.** Decoding carrier bits can be exact; the situated re-enactment of a generative episode need not be its numerical replay. What transfers should be tested for function, not asserted to be an identity of private content.
4. **Three different missing-expectation questions.** A model may fail to flag task-relevant missing background (MX-1); a symbolic model can miss prospective organization while still predicting labels (MX-2); a concrete AI architecture may or may not exhibit an independently established expectation-shaped position-reconstitution (MX-3). None defines 09-20 Expectation B, C0 or C1, and none by itself proves a machine impossibility. Review the specific typed owners.
5. **No automatic critique victory.** Strong dynamic coarse-graining, pragmatics, latent-state models and participatory accounts already model changing backgrounds. A matched rival must be assessed at the *same explanatory target*, without generalizing from a local failure to all AI or science.
