# Author clarification — vertical structure is active selectable-space construction, not passive historical sediment

## 1. Exact author wording

> **「垂直结构并不是历史被动沉淀的结果，而是一种维系、预测、主动发生的可选空间的构建。也即是选择的含义。」**

## 2. Minimum author-owned correction

The author rejects the machine-side reading in which ontological vertical structure is primarily:

```text
past Selection
-> passive sediment / historical residue
-> later constraint.
```

The intended direction is instead:

```text
Selection
-> actively constructs a selectable space
-> maintains a structured field of what can continue / re-enter / be anticipated
-> makes future Selection possible from a non-flat position.
```

Thus vertical structure is not merely the trace left by Selection. It is part of what Selection **does**.

## 3. Current minimum meaning of Selection

At the present author-controlled level, Selection carries at least the following ontological burden:

```text
Selection does not merely pick one item from a finished menu.
Selection actively constructs / maintains / anticipates a structured space of further selectability.
```

The author's words explicitly include:

```text
维系 / maintenance
预测 / anticipation or prediction
主动发生 / active occurrence
可选空间的构建 / construction of a selectable space
```

These should not be reduced to passive history or third-person state description.

## 4. Relation to prior author clarifications

This clarification tightens the current chain:

```text
L0 difference / minimum non-neutrality
-> subjectless Selection
-> Selection actively verticalizes and structures selectability
-> Selection-position / One can emerge
-> later position-indexed active Selection maintains / predicts / reconstructs selectable space
-> multiple centres recursively alter one another's selectability
-> cross-time / space / position / subject stability
-> multi-subject order
-> third-person observable stability as downstream readout.
```

Important correction:

```text
vertical structure != passive historical sediment
history != ontological source of verticality
```

History may still matter because earlier Selection changes later selectable space, but that is a consequence of active Selection-generated organization, not a definition of vertical structure as accumulated residue.

## 5. Prediction guard

`预测 / prediction` here is author language for the forward-oriented role of Selection in maintaining and constructing a future selectable space.

It does **not** yet authorize any specific identification with:

```text
Bayesian prediction;
predictive processing;
active inference;
control-theoretic forecasting;
conscious expectation.
```

Those are possible comparator / formalization questions only.

## 6. First-person / subjectless guard

This clarification does not introduce a pre-existing subject at L0.

```text
pre-subject layer:
Selection can be subjectless while still actively producing non-flat selectable structure.

post-formation layer:
formed Selection-positions / subjects can actively maintain, anticipate, and rebuild selectable space from within their positions.
```

So `active` does not mean `performed by an already-complete cognitive agent` at every level.

## 7. Whole / order implication

The multi-subject whole should therefore not be explained primarily as historical sediment or an externally stable constraint network.

The stronger SRT direction is:

> A multi-subject order persists insofar as participating Selection-positions actively continue to construct, maintain, anticipate, and mutually reshape the shared selectable space from within their positions.

The third-person stable order is downstream evidence of that ongoing active construction.

## 8. Current open burden

The next comparative pressure should ask what SRT means by `prediction / anticipation` and `maintenance` at the ontological Selection level, and whether mature neighboring theories can substitute for the full active selectable-space construction role without presupposing the selectable field or the formed subject.

No formal mechanism is selected here.
