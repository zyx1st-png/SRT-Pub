# Part I — strongest-neighbor closeout

## 1. One — author chooses thin regenerative recurrence

The strongest-neighbor pressure on process continuity / organizational closure produced two possible routes.

```text
A-route:
retain only the thinnest regenerative recurrence relation;
keep exact individuation / boundary OPEN;
treat closure / autonomy / autopoiesis as stronger realizations or neighboring theories.

B-route:
make mutual maintenance / closure / endogenous organization
part of universal One admission.
```

**A — author decision**: choose the A-route.

Universal One formation therefore should not require by definition:

```text
full organizational closure;
autopoiesis;
life;
self-maintenance as a primitive;
a mature boundary;
a pre-given same-lineage token;
explicit autonomy.
```

The minimum recurrence burden is:

```text
Selection-generated organization
-> conditions later Selection
-> later Selection regenerates / re-establishes that organization
-> the regenerated organization can condition Selection again.
```

Compact working line:

> **A One begins to form when Selection-generated organization is regenerated through later Selection so that the conditioning relation itself can recur.**

Chinese working line:

> **当由 Selection 形成并进入后续条件的组织，又通过后续 Selection 被重新形成，从而能够继续条件化更后的 Selection 时，One 开始形成。**

**A/D boundary**:

```text
regenerative recurrence = retained;
exact unit-identification = OPEN;
exact boundary criterion = OPEN;
formal necessary-and-sufficient One classifier = OPEN.
```

Ordinary path dependence is therefore insufficient, but the skeleton does not respond by importing a full closure theory.

# Part IV — final interpretation

## 17. What the strongest-neighbor sequence changed

The sequence did not establish a set of proprietary mechanisms.

It produced a thinner interpretation:

```text
Selection:
neighbor theories strongly pay primitive actualisation / process-before-object burdens;
SRT retains Selection-first organization and event polarity.

One:
neighbor theories strongly pay process continuity / closure burdens;
SRT retains a thin regenerative Selection-history recurrence.

Concern:
neighbor theories strongly pay viability / adaptivity / regulation burdens;
SRT demotes Concern to a relational predicate.
```

Therefore the current whole-skeleton interpretation is:

> **SRT is increasingly best read as a minimal Selection-first generative grammar rather than as a collection of proprietary mechanisms.**

Working compression:

> **Non-flat openness admits subjectless selective actualisation; repeated Selection can recursively regenerate the organization through which earlier Selection continues to matter, and a formed generative unity can thereby arise. Beyond that One, SRT opens a relational research architecture rather than a universal hierarchy of higher beings.**

Chinese compression:

> **现实开放但并非平坦；无主语的 Selection 使确定差异获得显现实在性；当 Selection 形成的组织通过后续 Selection 被递归地重新形成，使既往选择持续作为后续选择的条件时，One 形成。One 之后不再预设统一的存在升级阶梯，而进入开放的关系性研究架构。**
