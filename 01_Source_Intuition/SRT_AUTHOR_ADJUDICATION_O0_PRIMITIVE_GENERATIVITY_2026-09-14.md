# Author adjudication — O0 primitive generativity

The author accepts O0-1 through O0-10 at the exact strengths prepared in `SRT_O0_POSITIVE_PRIMITIVE_GENERATIVITY_AUTHOR_GATE_2026-09-14.md`.

This record is **noncanonical**. It fixes the research direction and future repair constraints. It does not authorize immediate canonical edits.

## A. Primitive reality is generativity, not a stock of possibilities

Accepted:

```text
primitive reality
!= a container / warehouse / field of already formed possible objects
from which a later mechanism selects one item.
```

`Oriented Openness` is to be read as the modal character of primitive generativity, not as a second entity or mechanism alongside Selection.

## B. O0 and S0 are co-primitive burdens

Accepted:

```text
G0 primitive generativity
=
co-primitive {
  O0 non-maximal indifference,
  S0 actualising Selection
}
```

with hard guards:

```text
O0 before S0: NO
S0 before O0: NO
O0 causes S0: NO
S0 produces O0: NO
```

Time is not pregiven at this level. Any use of `before`, `after`, `first`, or causal sequencing here must be explicitly marked as explanatory shorthand rather than primitive chronology.

## C. Positive meaning of primitive orientation

Accepted:

```text
orientation
= irreducible non-flat role-structure in primitive generativity.
```

The generative relation through which determinate actuality is instituted cannot be completely flattened into a role-interchangeable occurrence relation without erasing the very differentiation by which it is generative.

Do not read this orientation as:

```text
goal;
semantic order;
moral value;
probability gradient;
preferred endpoint;
continuation preference;
non-self-erasure preference.
```

## D. Positive meaning of openness

Accepted:

```text
openness
= primitive generativity is not exhausted by completed determinate actuality
  and is not fully preclosed by an already-given outcome structure.
```

This does not require an absolute possibility field, latent object inventory, completed branch tree, or probability space.

## E. Finite positionality remains structural

Primitive Selection may remain finite-position-indexed.

But:

```text
finite event indexing
!= formed evaluative position;
!= subject;
!= Bearer;
!= good-for standpoint.
```

Therefore O0 does not contain position-relative value merely because Selection is finite-position-indexed.

## F. Hard boundary: O0 does not explain why A rather than B manifests

Accepted as a hard boundary:

```text
O0 fixes the type of primitive generativity;
O0 does not fix the specific content of every Selection.
```

Trying to answer `why A rather than B` at O0 risks restoring pregiven alternatives, weights, probabilities, a contentful law, a goal, or a chooser.

Specific determination remains local to actual Selection relations.

## G. Old strong epsilon theorem no longer defines primitive directionality

Accepted:

```text
B>=2 is primitively preferred over B<=1: REJECTED AS O0 DEFINITION;
primitive non-self-erasure / continuation preference: REJECTED;
terminal Selection as ontologically defective: REJECTED.
```

Retain only:

```text
epsilon-shadow inheritance question: OPEN.
```

The open research problem is whether primitive relation-level non-flatness can later inherit into position-indexed continuation / re-entry / reselectability asymmetries once formed recurrent positions exist.

## H. Primitive-fact honesty

Accepted:

SRT does not claim to eliminate every primitive fact.

Its first-principle move is:

```text
reject a specific contentful outcome as primitive;
reject bare maximally-indifferent occurrence as the complete primitive account;
retain minimal structured generativity as primitive.
```

The primitive burden is therefore moved from a brute content outcome to the type of generativity itself. This first principle is not explained by an earlier mechanism within the present architecture.

## I. `potential field / intrinsic granularity` language is canonical repair debt

Accepted as a future repair requirement:

Current stronger language in `Core_Law/SRT_L0_Metaphysics.md` describing an absolute / indivisible possibility field, intrinsic granularity, and stronger `kappa0` realization must not back-define minimum O0.

Future repair must preserve the distinction:

```text
stronger formal / realization model
!= primitive possibility container.
```

No canonical deletion, demotion, or replacement is authorized by this record alone. Exact retyping requires bounded owner-level review.

## J. Normativity remains downstream

Accepted:


O0 contains no primitive morality.

## K. Author-level positive wording

Use the following as the current author-approved noncanonical first-principle wording:

> **SRT treats primitive reality as generativity rather than as a stock of already formed possibilities. Primitive generativity has two co-primitive burdens: it is not maximally indifferent, and it actualises through subjectless Selection. “Not maximally indifferent” means that generativity is intrinsically non-flat in its role-structure; the asymmetry through which determination becomes actual cannot be completely erased while leaving the same generative relation. “Selection” means that a finite-position-indexed determinate difference is actually instituted together with Selection-relative backgrounding. These are not temporally ordered stages and neither causes the other. This first principle contains no chooser, prior option menu, probability ranking, goal, value, continuation preference or preferred endpoint. It fixes the type of primitive generativity, not the content of what any particular Selection must actualise.**

Human compression:

> **现实最底层不是“先有一堆可能，再挑一个出来”，也不是“什么都完全一样，然后莫名其妙冒出一个结果”。SRT 把最底层直接理解为一种会实际产生差异、而且这种生成本身并非完全平坦无差别的关系。这个“不平坦”和“实际发生选择”是同一原初生成的两个面，不存在时间上的谁先谁后。**
