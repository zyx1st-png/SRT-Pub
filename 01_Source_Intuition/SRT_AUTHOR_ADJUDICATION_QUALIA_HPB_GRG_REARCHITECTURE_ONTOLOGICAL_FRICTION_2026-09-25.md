# A-6 — qualia route: reopen HP-B as a mechanistic explanation candidate

## A-6.1 Author decision

The D1-D5 recommendation to keep the gate-becomes-object route primarily inside HP-C is too conservative.

The author judges that the current materials are sufficient to attempt an explanation of why feeling arises, using the conjunction of:

- an independently formed / admitted Bearer;
- coarse-graining / gate geometry;
- generative maintenance;
- ontological friction;
- failure of fully background handling;
- foregrounding / objectification;
- common bearer-relative reconfiguration.

Therefore:

~~~text
A-6 verdict:

HP-C characterization = RETAIN
HP-B mechanism candidate = REOPEN / ACTIVE
HP-B theorem / proof = NOT CLAIMED
~~~

The relevant target is no longer merely:

> given phenomenality, why does it have this structure?

It is also:

> why would a formed Bearer need a felt / first-person mode at all when its background generative organization can no longer absorb consequential ontological friction locally?

---

## A-6.2 Non-circular structural antecedent

Do not use "phenomenal objectification" in the antecedent.

Use a fully structural chain:

~~~text
1. an independently admitted Bearer exists;

2. the Bearer maintains generative continuity through formed gate geometry:
   stable coarse-graining, equivalence, boundary, neighborhood,
   transition / reachability organization;

3. actualisation creates ontological friction (Psi_f)
   relative to that formed organization;

4. local / background gates can no longer absorb or pay the relevant burden
   without materially changing bearer-level continuation;

5. the deformation is promoted from background organization
   into a shared, selection-active foreground object / compressed state;

6. multiple otherwise partly separable gates must condition on this same
   foregrounded deformation because their resolution spaces have become coupled;

7. the foregrounded state directly reconfigures the current Selection-position
   and later selectability of the same Bearer.
~~~

All seven can be stated without phenomenal vocabulary.

---

## A-6.3 Candidate bridge principle

Working machine formulation accepted for testing:

> **When bearer-relevant ontological friction can no longer be paid entirely within background gate geometry, and the unresolved deformation must be coarse-grained into a common selection-active foreground object that directly reconfigures the same Bearer's current Selection-position, feeling is the first-person mode of that bearer-constitutive objectification.**

Compact:

~~~text
background gate geometry
+ bearer-relevant Psi_f
+ failed local payment
+ common coarse-grained foreground objectification
+ same-bearer current-position reconfiguration
-> phenomenal presence candidate
~~~

The intended explanatory move is not:

~~~text
representation
-> inner observer
-> feeling
~~~

but:

~~~text
the carrier of the consequence
=
the locus whose current operative position is being reconfigured.
~~~

There is no second receiver to whom the object must be shown.

The for-me character is therefore proposed to arise because the deformation is not merely information about the Bearer; it becomes constitutive of the current from-where through which that same Bearer must continue Selection.

---

## A-6.4 Why this is more than ordinary unconscious control

Background regulation can remain nonphenomenal when:

~~~text
burden remains local;
the local gate can pay / absorb it;
cross-gate resolution spaces need not be jointly reorganized;
the Bearer's current operative position need not be commonly recut.
~~~

Feeling becomes a candidate when:

~~~text
the deformation can no longer remain merely a hidden cause of later behavior;
it must become a common object of current bearer-level selection
because multiple generative continuations now depend on the same compressed difference.
~~~

This supplies a principled difference between:

~~~text
unconscious gating
and
felt gate deformation
~~~

without defining feeling as mere access or report.

---

## A-6.5 Qualitative character, intensity and valence

Current decomposition:

~~~text
qualitative character
<- geometry of the distinctions / neighborhoods / reachable transitions
   altered by the foregrounded objectification;

intensity / urgency
<- how much registered ontological friction must enter common foreground control,
   NOT necessarily total Psi_f_actual monotonically;

valence / directional feel
<- direction of the deformation relative to concern,
   expectation and viable continuation;

for-me-ness
<- identity / non-outsourcing relation between the affected bearer
   and the current Selection-position being reconfigured.
~~~

Guard:

~~~text
Psi_f_actual may diverge from Psi_f_felt.
~~~

Therefore intensity is not defined as raw Psi_f magnitude.

---

## A-6.6 Epistemic strength

This is now permitted as:

~~~text
HP-B MECHANISTIC EXPLANATION CANDIDATE
P3/P4 bridge strength
~~~

It is not yet:

~~~text
logical necessity theorem;
identity theorem;
canonical consciousness definition;
proof that every qualifying architecture is phenomenal.
~~~

The stronger burden is to show that removing feeling while preserving the full bearer-level objectification architecture either:

1. changes the meaning of one of the structural relations; or
2. loses a discriminating prediction that the phenomenal route supplies.

The zombie pressure remains active, but it no longer blocks constructing the positive mechanism.

current author correction:
L0 / L1 / L2 are analytic views.
~~~

Therefore a method designed to operationalize SRT ontology must be reconsidered at the root.

~~~text
A-8 verdict:

GRG current name = OPEN FOR REVIEW
GRG operational architecture = REOPEN
original programme telos = RETAIN
GRG v0.3 = historical / current foundation owner until replacement is authorized
immediate v0.4 = NO
~~~

---

## A-8.2 Original telos retained

The author does not abandon the original GRG purpose.

Current paraphrase:

> The operational programme exists to make SRT ontology usable as a research method for discovering and integrating the unified dynamics that lie before, beneath and continuously through objectification.

The target is not a universal list of grammar tokens.

The target is:

~~~text
the continuous generative dynamics through which:
- a field of possible differentiation is structured;
- objects / distinctions become foregrounded;
- stabilized regularities / habits / models persist;
- those retained structures re-enter later pre-object formation.
~~~

Because L0/L1/L2 are now views rather than spaces, operationalization should recover multiple views of one generative process, not map a phenomenon into three ontic compartments.

---

## A-8.3 Cross-domain diagnosis

Current author direction:

Other fields may already possess:

- strong L2-like views:
  - stable laws;
  - horizontal regularities;
  - mature object-level relations;
  - statistical / causal structures;
- partial L1-like views:
  - object formation;
  - boundary changes;
  - state transitions;
  - emergence / individuation;
- partial L0-like views:
  - pre-object organization;
  - affordance / possibility structure;
  - latent constraint;
  - formation conditions.

But these are often:

~~~text
partial;
locally separated;
owned by different subtheories;
not reconstructed as views of one continuous generative dynamics.
~~~

The operational programme's target is therefore completeness / integration across views, not merely lexical novelty.

Hard guard:

> Completeness is a research burden, not a presumption that every source theory is deficient.

A mature source can already carry all needed views.

---

## A-8.4 Candidate new operational engine

A possible replacement / evolution of the current grammar-first engine is:

~~~text
1. source-native reconstruction;

2. identify the current objectification / working cut;

3. recover L2 view:
   what retained organization / regularity / history makes this objectification stable?

4. recover L1 view:
   what determinate difference / boundary / object is actually being formed or maintained?

5. recover L0-facing view:
   what pre-object gate geometry / generative conditions make those determinate forms
   accessible, near, distant, equivalent, excluded or reconstructible?

6. trace recurrence:
   how does L2 retention re-enter later L0-facing gate geometry?

7. identify ontological friction:
   where does the current geometry become costly / strained / unreconstructible?

8. recut where warranted;

9. only then abstract any cross-case recurrent dynamics / grammar.
~~~

This is not yet a mandatory protocol.

It is a rearchitecture candidate.

## A-8.5 Gate geometry as a promising construction language

The author judges gate geometry to be potentially ideal for this operational reconstruction.

Why:

~~~text
coarse-graining
-> tells us which differences are treated as equivalent;

stability
-> tells us which equivalences survive perturbation;

boundary / neighborhood
-> tells us which distinctions become objects / localities;

reachability / transitions
-> tells us what can become next;

ontological friction
-> tells us where the current geometry resists maintenance / change;

re-objectification

~~~

This can potentially connect L0/L1/L2 views without reifying them as layers.

However:

~~~text
gate geometry as privileged operational language = CANDIDATE
gate geometry as mandatory universal ontology = NOT AUTHORIZED
~~~

The method must retain the right for mature source formalisms to replace, refine or defeat this language.

---

## A-9.2 Contextual emphasis

Different contexts may emphasize different aspects of the same ontological friction:

~~~text
formation / actualisation:
payability / impedance of forming a maintainable reality slice;

maintenance:
burden of keeping an achieved equivalence / cut stable;

reconstruction:
burden of revising a strained gate geometry;

consciousness / qualia:
registered / foregrounded ontological friction relative to Bearer,
Concern and Expectation;

agency:
payability of recutting / rewriting conditions of later Selection.
~~~

These are readings / projections, not separate ontologies.
