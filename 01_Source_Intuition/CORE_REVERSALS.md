# Core Reversals

These reversals state SRT's founding force in compact form. They are source-intuition pointers, not replacements for formal anchors.

## 1. Existence Is Not Background

**One-sentence version**

Existence is not a pre-given background; it is the stabilized trace of selection under constraint, cost, concern, and history.

**Easy misreading**

"SRT says nothing exists until a mind thinks about it."

**Boundary**

SRT is not mind-first idealism. Selection is broader than mental choice, and stable reality is tested by resistance, cost, and convergence.

## 2. The Subject Is Not The Premise Of Selection

**One-sentence version**

The subject is not the original chooser behind selection; it is a stabilized product of selection processes that have become self-referential enough to hold a position.

**Easy misreading**

"SRT denies subjects, agency, or first-person reality."

**Boundary**

SRT does not erase subjecthood. It relocates subjecthood from primitive premise to structured emergence.

## 3. Attention Is Not Selection Itself

**One-sentence version**

Attention is not selection itself; it is selection becoming visible at the cognitive layer.

**Easy misreading**

"SRT reduces reality to attention."

**Boundary**

Attention is a local cognitive manifestation. SRT's selection structure also includes embodiment, anchoring, cost, history, social convergence, and physical constraint.

## 4. Value Is Not Preference

**One-sentence version**

Value is not mere preference; it is consequence return where what happens cannot be fully substituted without loss.

**Easy misreading**

"SRT says value is just whatever a system wants."

**Boundary**

Preference can be cheap, reversible, or simulated. SRT value requires stake, consequence return, and non-substitutability.

## 5. Consciousness Is Not Everywhere

**One-sentence version**

Consciousness is not a property of everything; it is a threshold window where selection enters concern, burden, consequence return, and self-modulation.

**Easy misreading**

"SRT is panpsychism."

**Boundary**

SRT treats selection as broad and consciousness as thresholded. A system can process, optimize, report, or imitate without crossing the relevant threshold.
