## A. Accepted G1–G4

The author accepts all four architecture gates exposed by the four-scale machine audit.

```text
primitive Selection
```

### G2 — cross-domain unity is typed dependency continuity, not literal mechanism identity

Accepted.

SRT's unification target is not that physics, life, cognition and society instantiate one numerically identical local mechanism.

The target is that a disciplined typed dependency architecture can remain continuous across scales while domain mechanisms differ.

```text
language / institution / habit / comparable social-cultural structures
= L2-layer scaffold / formed background families
```

### G4 — architecture-level distinctiveness is an allowed primary target

Accepted.

SRT does not require every local role of Selection to be historically unprecedented.

The author allows the main distinctiveness target to reside at architecture level if SRT can eventually establish:

```text
one continuous generative dependency architecture
+ disciplined cross-domain transfer
+ exclusion / failure conditions for bad mappings
+ novel cross-domain problem generation or model constraints
+ consequences lost when SRT's architecture is deleted.
```

Local neighbor payment remains evidence against local novelty claims; it does not by itself cancel architecture-level unification value.

## B. Interpretation of Barad + Simondon pressure

The author does not treat the Barad / Simondon pressure as a reason to retreat from the Selection-first programme.

Current author interpretation:

> The strongest-neighbor pressure is evidence that the intended minimal unifying tool intersects real, mature ontological work already present in Barad and Simondon. That overlap is expected and informative. The research task is now to recover whether SRT's own earlier intuitions and downstream architecture already contain additional necessary burdens that were not made explicit in the newly thinned minimal spine, and whether any such burden is not paid by Barad, Simondon, or the strongest fair composite of other mature neighbors.

Therefore the next research question is not:

```text
"How can SRT protect Selection from neighbors?"
```

It is:

```text
"What is the thinnest adequate unifying tool SRT has actually been trying to build,
including latent burdens already used elsewhere in SRT but not yet stated in the minimum spine;
and which of those burdens remain unpaid by the strongest fair neighboring theories?"
```
