## 2. Core accepted synthesis

The author accepts preserving the following generative research arc as a candidate downstream stack:

`Selection / Actualization`

`-> retained efficacy / higher-order formation`

`-> operational One / generative re-entry`

`-> endogenous Selection position / perspective candidate`

`-> epistemic proxy / reconstruction loop`

`-> bearer / temporal lineage / self candidate`

`-> anticipation / stake / valuation / attention`

`-> intervention / agency-like organization`

`-> multi-agent power / shared proxy / institution`

`-> civilizational Selection architecture`.

This is a **dependency / thickening research graph**, not a mandatory chronological ladder.

Different mechanisms may instantiate some modules without instantiating later ones.

## 3. Accepted perspective / endogeneity direction

The dialogue proposed that perspective should not begin as a consciousness term.

A minimal research direction is:

> perspective as a locus at which distinctions are organized as neutral / non-neutral.

An endogenous perspective candidate arises when that distinction organization is maintained by the formed system's own relational organization rather than solely by an external analyst.

Accepted guard:

`position alone != perspective`

`endogenous perspective != bearer automatically`

`bearer != subject automatically`

`subject-like organization != phenomenal consciousness automatically`.

The current canonical / STATUS routing for perspective and Bearer remains authoritative where it already speaks. This record does not override it.

## 4. Ontology / epistemology continuity direction

The author explicitly endorsed the dialogue's suggestion that perspective / endogeneity may provide a route for unifying ontology and epistemology.

The accepted research direction is:

- ontology does not wait for an external knower;
- a perspective can itself be an internally generated ontological relation;
- epistemic organization begins when such a position forms revisable proxies of absent, hidden, past or possible relations;
- those proxies can guide Selection and be corrected by later actuality.

Working synthesis:

> epistemology is not outside ontology; it is a reflexive development within ontology when an endogenous position constructs and revises relation-proxies that participate in later Selection.

This remains a research hypothesis, not a closed philosophical theorem.

## 5. Accepted temporal thickening direction

The dialogue preserved a distinction among:

- persistence;
- Bearer continuity;
- self continuity.

A self candidate was developed as a temporally extended equivalence / lineage relation in which past, present and anticipated future Selection positions are treated as belonging to one operative generative lineage and that relation itself changes current Selection.

Accepted guard:

`self != self-model`.

A self-model is a possible proxy about the already formed temporal lineage.

## 6. Accepted anticipation direction

Anticipation was separated into at least:

- structural / thin anticipation;
- proxy-mediated anticipation.

The key guard is:

`future itself does not retro-cause the present`.

Rather:

`presently instantiated proxy of possible future consequence -> current Selection burden`.

This permits future possibilities to acquire current reach without invoking retrocausality.

## 7. Accepted stake / valuation / attention direction

The dialogue proposed that a mature endogenous position must move beyond binary neutrality.

Candidate roles:

- stake: how an actual or anticipated difference bears on the future integrity / generative capacity of the formed position;
- valuation: directional organization of anticipated consequences relative to that position;
- attention: allocation of limited Selection capacity among multiple currently non-neutral differences;
- salience: tendency of a difference to gain current reach / priority;
- affect-like organization: distributed compression of stakes / valuations into broader modulation of the Selection landscape.

These are candidate generative roles.

They do not establish phenomenal feeling.

## 8. Consciousness boundary accepted as OPEN

The author accepts preserving the hard stop:

A generative account may explain:

- endogenous perspective;
- memory;
- anticipation;
- self-lineage;
- stake;
- valuation;
- attention;
- affect-like global modulation;
- agency-like intervention;

without thereby establishing phenomenal consciousness.

Working OPEN boundary:

> GRG may provide a candidate grammar for endogenous generative perspective and subject-level organization; a sufficient condition for phenomenal consciousness is not established.

## 9. Accepted intervention direction

Intervention was distinguished from generic causation.

A candidate intervention structure is:

`epistemic proxy -> anticipated consequences -> valuation/stake -> intervention Selection -> modified generative conditions -> later actuality -> revision`.

Intervention changes conditions under which later actuality becomes reachable / likely / admissible; it does not directly guarantee an outcome.

This produces a broader loop:

`ontology -> epistemology -> intervention/action -> new ontology`.

## 10. Accepted multi-agent / power direction

Power was proposed as a relation in which one Selection position can materially alter another position's:

- reach;
- neutrality map;
- support conditions;
- available re-Selection pathways.

Candidate distinctions include:

- influence;
- coordination;
- coercion;
- consent;
- autonomy.

Consent was treated not as a mere yes-token but as a stronger candidate condition in which an externally proposed intervention is endogenously incorporated into the receiving position's own Selection process while consequential re-Selection remains sufficiently open.

These are research candidates, not legal definitions.

## 11. Accepted institution / money / civilization direction

The dialogue preserved several downstream GRG interpretations:

- money as a transferable multi-agent settlement proxy, not value itself;
- institutions as retained multi-agent intervention / coordination grammar;
- law as a meta-intervention grammar regulating who may change whose Selection conditions and how disputes reopen;
- rights as candidate anti-closure scaffolds that preserve specified re-Selection capacities;
- civilization as large-scale sedimentation of shared proxies that reduce repeated coordination burden.

These are candidate structural mappings.

They must later be pressure-tested against source-native economics, law, political theory, history and institutional science before stronger standing.

## 12. Scaffold / cage continuity

Across the downstream exploration, the same research distinction recurred:

- scaffold-like formation reduces repetitive Selection burden while preserving consequential reopening / reconstruction;
- cage-like formation maintains obsolete or externally imposed neutrality relations while blocking relevant differences from regaining reach.

This is a research architecture, not a moral classification shortcut.

No system, institution, identity or technology should be labeled “cage” without source-specific evidence for the relevant closure mechanism.

## 13. Why this material is parked outside the Card core

The immediate Card programme needs a minimal cross-domain research interface.

The downstream analysis is deliberately parked because prematurely inserting all these notions into the Card would:

- overfit the template to the current philosophical dialogue;
- bias later fusion cases;
- make empty fields look like source deficiencies;
- risk treating perspective / self / power as universal primitives.

Therefore this package is a hypothesis reservoir and drill-down map.

The fusion-case Card remains the active next task.
