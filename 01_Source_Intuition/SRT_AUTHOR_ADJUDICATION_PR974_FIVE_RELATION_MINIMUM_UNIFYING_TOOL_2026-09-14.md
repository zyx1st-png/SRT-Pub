# Author adjudication — five-relation minimum unifying tool

> **Boundary:** author-source record only. This file does not create a new ontology ladder, edit canonical owners, establish novelty, assign a claim level, or close Level 2 HOLD.

## 1. Decision

The author accepts the following noncanonical research statement of SRT's minimum unifying tool:

```text
Generation
Standing
Shaping
Bearing
Revision
```

These are **typed relations / research questions**, not five universal ontological stages.

They may recur and couple across scales. A valid SRT mapping need not instantiate every relation at every layer.

`Ground` remains a distinct upstream OPEN question:

```text
why actualising Selection obtains at all
!=
what the five-relation tool tracks once generation / formation / history exist.
```

## 2. Accepted relation meanings

### Generation

What becomes actual / manifest and what becomes Selection-relative background?

This relation is anchored to primitive Selection and must not be reduced to option-picking among pre-given objects.

### Standing

What has actually formed as a continuing One or stronger downstream standing?

Standing must remain separated from mere causal influence, persistence, control, subjecthood and moral status.

### Shaping

What retained history / L2 scaffold / formed condition changes later candidate generation, reachability, friction, default path or comparison context?

Strong shaping does not by itself establish standing.

### Bearing

Where do non-outsourcable exposure and consequences land?

Bearing is not identical to control, authorship, benefit, execution, subjecthood or moral priority.

### Revision

Which formed position can use consequence return to revise the shaping conditions themselves — comparison rules, boundaries, candidate-generation conditions or L2 scaffold?

Revision is stronger than action within a fixed candidate space.

## 3. Accepted type-separation guards

The author accepts the research need to preserve at least these non-identities:

```text
causal influence != standing
standing != bearing
bearing != control
control != revision
higher-level standing != automatic erasure of lower-level standing
shaping != subjecthood
stability != generative health
visible option count != endogenous future selectability
```

## 4. Recursive architecture

The five relations are not a one-way developmental ladder.

A minimal recursive reading is:

```text
Generation
   -> may produce Standing
Standing / retained history
   -> can produce Shaping
Shaping
   -> conditions later Generation
Bearing
   -> locates where consequences are actually inherited
Revision
   -> can modify Shaping
```

The exact graph, formalization and admissible domain mappings remain open research tasks.

## 5. Relation to the accepted layered architecture

The earlier three-stratum decision remains in force:

```text
Stratum I: thin ontology
Stratum II: formed-process axes
Stratum III: L2 / multi-position axes
```

The five-relation tool is the **cross-stratum research grammar** used to type relations inside and between those strata.

It does not add new universal rungs to Stratum I.

## 6. Strong guards

This adjudication does not establish:

```text
five relations are historically unique to SRT;
all five apply to every physical event;
Bearer = person / moral subject / consciousness;
Bearing creates an automatic normative priority;
Revision is required for primitive Selection;
L2 scaffold is intrinsically good or bad;
Ground is solved;
whole-package non-substitutability;
scientific distinctiveness;
Level 2 exit.
```
