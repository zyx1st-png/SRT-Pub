# Author adjudication — post-#976 O0 standing, determinism scope, reduction posture and strongest-rival gate

This record captures the author's adjudication after the broader independent review of PR #976.

It is **noncanonical**. It authorizes bounded synchronization against already-settled architecture and fixes four author-level gates. It does not itself rewrite canonical owners.

## A. O0 / S0 standing

Accepted:

```text
O0 and S0
= co-primitive analytic burdens / aspects
  of primitive Selection reality.
```

Here `co-primitive` is a **metaphysical analytic standing**. It does not require O0 and S0 to be separately numbered P0 axioms.

Therefore:

```text
metaphysical co-primitivity
!= requirement for one independent numbered P0 axiom per burden.
```

`Core/SRT_Core_21_Minimal_Axioms.md` is a formal-admission surface. It does not exhaust every analytic burden owned by the L0 metaphysical owner.

No automatic promotion of O0 to a new P0 item is authorized by this adjudication.

## B. Minimum meaning of non-preclosed

Accepted narrow reading:

```text
non-preclosed
= reality is not fully given before Selection
  as a completed inventory / finished possibility menu / already-set outcome catalogue.
```

This minimum commitment does **not** by itself entail:

```text
indeterminism;
quantum randomness;
the impossibility of law + initial-state determinism;
the impossibility of a block-universe model.
```

The relation between SRT's primitive Selection architecture and ontic determinism remains:

```text
OPEN.
```

Domain work must not silently derive a strong anti-determinist physics thesis from `non-preclosed`.

## C. O0 / S0 reduction standing

Accepted current posture:

```text
O0 / S0 co-primitive
= current architectural decomposition.

O0 -> S0 reduction = NOT ESTABLISHED.
S0 -> O0 reduction = NOT ESTABLISHED.

O0 irreducible to S0 = NOT PROVEN.
S0 irreducible to O0 = NOT PROVEN.
```

Therefore `co-primitive` currently records the architecture's analytic decomposition and burden-separation discipline; it is not itself a proof of mutual irreducibility.

If later strongest-rival or formal work shows one burden can be derived from the other without loss, that result must be separately adjudicated rather than blocked by terminology.
