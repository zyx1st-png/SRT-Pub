# Author adjudication — generativity, position and normativity

This record supersedes any machine reading of the current gate that treats consequence-bearing standing, non-outsourcing, continuation, or health as the primitive ground of normativity.

It does **not** authorize canonical edits.

## 1. Primitive generativity is not temporally sequenced

Minimum non-neutrality and actualising Selection remain co-primitive burdens of primitive generativity.

They are not to be represented as:

```text
non-neutrality -> later Selection
```

or as any other temporal causal sequence.

Time is not to be assumed as a pregiven container for the primitive relation. `Primitive`, `pre-object`, and related ordering language are ontological / dependency language unless a temporal relation has been independently established.

The author therefore keeps open the stronger possibility that temporal ordering itself is downstream of Selection-generated differentiation.

## 2. Directionality is not identical with position-relative good/bad

Primitive directionality is not restricted to one fixed Selection-position.

`better / worse` is position-relative: the evaluative direction must be typed relative to some position, relation, or scale for which the difference is genuinely consequential.

Primitive O0 directionality is therefore more basic than any one fixed-position evaluation and must not be reduced to `good-for X`.

At the same time, SRT retains as a research direction the possibility that a sufficiently justified whole can itself be treated as a position or scale of evaluation. If that construction succeeds, local and whole-relative better/worse need not coincide.

This whole-position programme is a target direction, not yet a completed canonical result.

## 3. Continuation preference is position-relative, not primitive epsilon restoration

For an already formed / fixed position, structures that preserve or enlarge that position's ability to remain in generative relations may be treated as structurally preferred relative to that position.

This does **not** restore the old primitive strong-epsilon theorem and does not establish that primitive non-neutrality itself already ranks completed continuation outcomes.

Keep distinct:

```text
formed-position-relative continuation / generativity preference
!=
primitive universal continuation preference
```

The relation between primitive non-neutrality and later continuation direction remains a reconstruction problem.

## 4. The deepest ground is Selection as ontic generation

Consequence-bearing standing and non-outsourcing are not the primitive basis of better/worse or responsibility.

The more basic question is how a relation changes **Selection as ontic generation**.

Selection is not retrieval of a pregiven correct item from an option menu. Selection generates determinate asymmetry, reorganises what can subsequently become manifest or selectable, and therefore participates in producing the later generative situation.

Accordingly, later evaluative and normative reconstruction should ask whether a relation:

- sustains or enlarges genuine Selection-capacity;
- weakens, freezes, hollows out, or externally captures Selection-capacity;
- allows Selection-produced difference to re-enter subsequent generative organisation;
- or breaks that generative relation so that Selection becomes increasingly nominal, outsourced, or non-generative.

## 5. Consequence return and outsourcing are derivative diagnostics

Consequence return matters because Selection that changes reality while remaining systematically insulated from the changed generative situation can weaken the generative closure of that position.

Outsourcing matters because a position that persistently transfers away its own Selection-capacity may remain existent while becoming thinner as a generative position.

Therefore:

```text
consequence-bearing != primitive normative ground
non-outsourcing != primitive normative ground
```

Rather, both are possible diagnostics or downstream conditions of whether a Selection-position remains genuinely generative.

This also blocks:

```text
continuation = goodness
survival = generative health
bearing harm = sufficient ground of value
```

## 6. O1 should be rebuilt around position-relative generativity

The author accepts the direction that `better / worse` should be reconstructed relative to a position, but rejects grounding that relation merely in who bears the consequence.

The stronger target is:

> relative to position X, does a change strengthen, preserve, weaken, close, outsource, or hollow out X's capacity to participate in further genuine Selection and generation?

This must still be specified without circularly defining `generativity` by `good`, `health`, or the desired evaluative conclusion.

## 7. Responsibility and ought must be rebuilt from the same generative basis

Responsibility should not be grounded merely in consequence-bearing.

Its deeper candidate ground is the relation between a Selection-position and the reality its Selection helps generate: a position cannot be fully characterised as the source of a generative intervention while the generated relation is treated as wholly external to the position's subsequent generative organisation.

This is a reconstruction direction, not yet a complete theory of blame or deontic duty.

Likewise, `ought` must not be reconstructed as discovering the one accurate pregiven option. Selection changes the option-space / generative situation itself.

Any mature SRT account of ought must therefore first explain normative constraints on **how generative Selection is maintained, transformed, coordinated, or destroyed**, before claiming that a determinate action-object is uniquely correct.

## 8. Hume-gap reconstruction is reopened on a generativity-first basis

The is/ought project remains `Dissolution Rather Than Derivation`, but the previous consequence-bearing-first route is not controlling.

The reconstruction must now begin from:

```text
primitive non-maximal indifference + actualising Selection
    -> formed generative positions and relations
    -> position-relative strengthening / weakening of generative capacity
    -> stronger responsibility / normative relations where additional conditions are paid
```

The arrows here are dependency / reconstruction arrows, not assumed temporal arrows.

The target claim is not that a neutral completed `is` logically entails a completed morality. The target is that a determinate `is` is already a partial cross-section of a generative relation whose directionality, positional effects, and later normative dimensions may be lost when reality is typed only as completed neutral facts.

