# Elevating *从存在到秩序* (From Existence to Order): A Research-Based Roadmap for SRT

## TL;DR
- **SRT's three biggest opportunities are (1) to claim the diachronic, value-generating "selection-history" territory that adjacent frameworks—Barad's agential realism, Friston's active inference, ontic structural realism—approach but never fully occupy; (2) to convert its is-ought claim from assertion into a rigorously argued "constitutivist" position; and (3) to ground its abstract chain (consequence return, pre-trimming, capability atrophy) in the substantial 2023–2026 empirical literature that already documents these mechanisms.**
- **The most urgent weaknesses to repair are the over-breadth of "selection" (it risks explaining everything and therefore nothing), the unargued status of the ε_pg axiom, and the AI chapter's exposure to obsolescence—2024–2026 agentic-AI developments must be addressed or the book will date quickly.**
- **To become "world-changing" like Rawls or Kuhn, the book needs a single portable conceptual device (the analog of the "veil of ignorance" or "paradigm shift") plus demonstrated traction on a live civilizational crisis; SRT's "scaffold vs. cage" and "hidden debt" concepts are the strongest candidates, and AI-driven capability atrophy is the strongest application.**

## Key Findings

1. **SRT is closest to Karen Barad's agential realism—and must distinguish itself sharply.** Barad's "intra-action" already holds that "distinct agencies do not precede, but rather emerge through, their intra-action" and that the "agential cut" determines "what matters and what gets excluded from mattering." This is strikingly close to SRT's exclusion→formation→inscription. SRT's defensible distinctions: a *diachronic, accumulating* selection history (Barad's cut is synchronic and apparatus-relative), a *privileged embodied position* (Barad deliberately decenters the human), and a *specific feedback mechanism* of value (consequence return) versus Barad's diffuse "entanglement ethics."

2. **The is-ought handling is the book's single greatest philosophical risk.** The "constitutive not normative" move is promising but underdefended. The contemporary literature (Pigden, Dodd & Stern-Gillet) carefully separates three distinct claims—the is-ought gap, the fact-value distinction, and the naturalistic fallacy—that are routinely conflated. SRT must show *which* it claims to escape and how. The strongest available defense is metaethical constitutivism (deriving norms from the constitutive features of agency/action), which SRT's "direction criteria as constitutive of continuation" resembles but never names.

3. **"Consequence return" has robust empirical corroboration the book is not yet citing.** Active inference (Friston) formalizes how systems minimize "surprise" by feeding consequences back into a generative model; predictive-processing accounts of trauma now reframe it as "a rigid pattern of threat prediction within the brain" rather than literal bodily storage; narrative-identity research shows self-construction through sedimented, consequence-laden autobiographical narratives. These provide SRT's most direct scientific anchors.

4. **Capability atrophy through AI dependence is now empirically documented and is SRT's best applied case.** The first real-world clinical evidence of AI "deskilling" arrived in 2025: Budzyń et al. (*Lancet Gastroenterology & Hepatology* 2025;10(10):896–903) found that the adenoma detection rate in standard non-AI colonoscopy fell from 28.4% (226/795) before AI introduction to 22.4% (145/648) after—a 20% relative and 6% absolute reduction. In education, Bastani et al. ("Generative AI Without Guardrails Can Harm Learning," *PNAS* 2025, 10.1073/pnas.2422633122) ran a Turkish high-school RCT in which unrestricted "GPT Base" students scored 48% higher on practice problems but 17% worse on the unassisted exam than controls, while a guided "GPT Tutor" condition was statistically indistinguishable from control. Survey work (PLS-SEM) further shows AI "cognitive dependence" increases "cognitive inertia" and reduces innovation capability. This is direct evidence for "pre-trimming," "hidden debt," and "scaffold vs. cage."

5. **The AI chapter is exposed to rapid obsolescence and must be updated to the 2024–2026 agentic turn.** The field has shifted from chatbots to autonomous "agents" that "perceive, decide, and act," with documented governance gaps. The EU AI Act (Article 51(2)) now presumes a general-purpose model poses "systemic risk" when cumulative training compute exceeds 10^25 FLOP, with Article 55 obligations applied from 2 August 2025 and full Commission enforcement (fines up to €15M or 3% of turnover) from 2 August 2026; McKinsey reports that 80% of organizations have already encountered risky behavior from AI agents. SRT's "AI as terrain participant" framing is well-positioned but must engage this concrete material.

6. **The consciousness chapter can be strengthened by the 2025 IIT-vs-GWT adversarial collaboration results.** The Cogitate Consortium's results (*Nature* 2025;642(8066):133–142, published 5 June 2025; 256 subjects across 12 labs; corresponding author Lucia Melloni, NYU Grossman) challenged *both* leading theories: "For IIT, a lack of sustained synchronization within the posterior cortex contradicts the claim that network connectivity specifies consciousness. GNWT is challenged by the general lack of ignition at stimulus offset and limited representation of certain conscious dimensions in the prefrontal cortex." SRT's "consciousness as late condensation of selection history" is a process/temporal view that should explicitly position itself relative to these empirical findings and to recurrent-processing/higher-order theories.

7. **Process philosophy (Whitehead) is the nearest historical predecessor and the distinction must be airtight.** Whitehead's "actual occasions" undergo "concrescence"—they "prehend" past data, achieve "satisfaction," and perish as data for successors. This is *extremely* close to SRT's selection→inscription→sedimentation. SRT's distinction must rest on: selection as *exclusion-driven* (not Whitehead's positive prehension), the *embodied position* anchor, and the *value-return* mechanism.

## Details

### A. Topics that could be discussed but haven't been

**Active inference / the free energy principle (Friston).** This is the most important missing interlocutor. Friston's framework—that organisms persist by minimizing variational free energy, "the difference between their model of the world and their sensory inputs," via a Markov blanket separating internal and external states—is a mathematically explicit theory of how a bounded system feeds consequences back into itself to maintain order against entropy. SRT's "consequence return → order" chain is a philosophical generalization of exactly this. Engaging it gives SRT (a) empirical credibility, (b) a formal apparatus (information geometry, natural-gradient descent on free energy), and (c) a clear contrast: Friston's systems minimize surprise, but as Godfrey-Smith (2024) objects, "there's more to life than that"—SRT's value/caring dimension can be positioned as what active inference lacks.

**Ontic structural realism (OSR) and grounding theory.** OSR (Ladyman & Ross, French, Esfeld) holds that "all that exists at the fundamental level are structures"—networks of relations without intrinsically identical relata. SRT is a *selection*-structural realism and should locate itself on the OSR map, including engaging the recent grounding-theory debate (the "Fundamentality Thesis" and "Priority Thesis," and the 2025 critique that grounding may be "either otiose to the scientific discourse... or detrimental"). This positions SRT within live analytic metaphysics rather than appearing self-contained.

**Post-phenomenology (Ihde, Verbeek).** Verbeek's "technological mediation" theory—that "technologies as mediators of human-world relations influence the appearing of both the world and the human beings in it," with Ihde's four relations (embodiment, hermeneutic, alterity, background)—is the natural bridge between SRT's ontology and its AI ethics. Verbeek's "background relations" map directly onto SRT's "order backgrounding." His claim that "designers materialise morality" supports SRT's structural-evil and hidden-debt concepts.

**Epistemic injustice (Fricker) and standpoint theory.** Fricker's testimonial and hermeneutical injustice—and the later distinction between "distributive" and "discriminatory" epistemic injustice—give SRT a vocabulary for how selection structures *occlude* certain positions' inscription into shared meaning. SRT's "occlusion" concept is essentially a structural-ontological generalization of hermeneutical injustice ("the lack of circulation or adoption of new terms coined at the margins"). This is a major untapped connection to social epistemology.

**Doughnut economics and post-growth.** Raworth's framework and the 2025 renewed Doughnut (Fanning & Raworth, *Nature* 2025;646(8083):47–56, 1 Oct 2025; 35 indicators) provide SRT with a concrete instance of "order backgrounding" gone wrong—GDP-growth lock-in as a "cage." That study found "the richest 20% of nations, with 15% of the global population, contribute more than 40% of annual ecological overshoot, whereas the poorest 40% of countries, with 42% of the global population, experience more than 60% of the social shortfall." SRT's "three direction criteria" (continuability/coordinability/re-selectability) could be presented as a philosophical foundation for the "safe and just space."

**New materialism (Barad), Levinas, Merleau-Ponty, Simondon.** See Key Finding 1 and the positioning table below. Simondon's "individuation" from a "pre-individual" "metastable" field (the crystal/supersaturated-solution paradigm; key terms *préindividuel*, *disparation*, *transduction*) is the closest precedent to SRT's L₀→individuation story and *must* be cited. Simondon's methodological inversion—we must "know the individual through individuation, rather than knowing individuation through the individual"—is almost a slogan for SRT.

**Other gaps:** democratic-backsliding data (V-Dem Democracy Report 2025: the share of the world's population living in electoral/liberal democracies fell from 51% in 2004 to 28% in 2024—about 2.3 billion people—with 72% now living in autocracies, and for the first time in over two decades autocracies (91) outnumbering democracies (88)); platform/recommender governance under the EU DSA/AI Act; and intergenerational-justice/Anthropocene framing.

### B. Topics discussed poorly or with problems

**The over-breadth of "selection."** If every exclusion-formation-inscription event counts as selection—from particle decoherence to human choice to institutional lock-in—the concept risks unfalsifiable universality. The fix is a *typology* of selection with differentiated mechanisms (cf. complexity science's careful distinction between "order" and "organization," and between weak/strong emergence via constraint on state transitions). SRT should specify what does *not* count as selection.

**The ε_pg (minimal non-neutrality) axiom.** As a primitive axiom this invites the same objection leveled at grounding primitives and at IIT's "axioms": that it is stipulated rather than argued. SRT should either (a) argue ε_pg abductively (as the best explanation of order's existence, paralleling Friston's "things that don't dissipate must have boundaries"), or (b) present it explicitly as a Whiteheadian-style speculative postulate and own that status.

**Hard problem of consciousness.** SRT's "late condensation" view is a process account but does not yet say how it differs from (i) Whitehead's panexperientialism, (ii) higher-order theories, or (iii) Friston/predictive-processing deflationary accounts. The 2025 adversarial-collaboration results give SRT an opening to be a "structural/temporal" theory but it must make a divergent, ideally testable, prediction to avoid the charge that it merely redescribes.

**Falsifiability conditions.** The book claims five. Genuine testability requires the form the Cogitate consortium used: "pre-registered... pass/fail criteria... expected outcomes and their interpretation ex-ante." SRT's conditions should be reformulated in that idiom or reclassified honestly as "research heuristics" rather than falsification tests.

**Process-philosophy distinction.** As above—currently the highest risk of "you've reinvented Whitehead." The distinction must be stated early and defended.

### C. Supplementary evidence and corroboration (use these to strengthen claims)

- **Consequence return / predictive feedback:** active inference (Parr, Pezzulo & Friston 2022); the social-neuroscience "predictive processing" model of self; the 2025 predictive-processing reframing of trauma.
- **Algorithmic pre-trimming:** recommender-system "filter bubble" research showing reinforcement via "data dependence + commercial logic"; the EU-law analysis of "proxies and choice architectures"; the 2025 "Bubble Escape Potential" metric.
- **Capability atrophy:** Budzyń et al. (*Lancet Gastro & Hepatology* 2025) on AI colonoscopy deskilling; Bastani et al. (*PNAS* 2025) on GPT exam effects; the "enrichment paradox" arXiv synthesis (London-taxi hippocampus studies; GPS-related spatial-memory decline); the China PLS-SEM study on AI dependence → cognitive inertia.
- **Path dependency / lock-in:** Arthur's "increasing returns," Pierson's "increasing returns, path dependence, and the study of politics," Mahoney's historical-sociology mechanisms; Thelen's caution against over-determinism—directly supporting "anchoring → irreversibility → thickness."
- **Subject as sedimentation:** narrative-identity longitudinal research (Adler et al.; the dual-cycle model); the "self-pattern" / dynamical-organization account of self.
- **Complexity / emergence:** the 2024–2025 npj Complexity and constraint-based emergence literature distinguishing order from organization; "enslavement"/synergetics (Haken).
- **Information ecosystems:** filter-bubble and "epistemic bubble" research above.

### D. Areas for deeper argumentation

1. **Ontology→ethics bridge.** Name and defend a constitutivist strategy; distinguish the three is/ought sub-claims; show how "consequence return → caring → value" either bridges or dissolves the gap, and confront the Levinasian objection that this *reduces* ethics to ontology.
2. **Consciousness.** Position against the 2025 adversarial-collaboration data and offer one divergent prediction.
3. **AI chapter.** Rewrite around the agentic turn (autonomous agents as "actors," not tools; governance/accountability diffusion; EU AI Act compute thresholds; capability-atrophy evidence). Engage named systems and the empirical 2025–2026 governance literature.
4. **Political philosophy.** Connect "pre-trimming" and "structural evil" to surveillance-capitalism scholarship (Zuboff's "epistemic coup"/"remote behavioral actuation") and to democratic-backsliding data; offer SRT's direction criteria as a justice test alongside Rawls.
5. **Phenomenology.** Explicitly engage Merleau-Ponty's "the body is our anchorage in the world" and the "flesh," and Levinas's "ethics as first philosophy" ("the first word of the face is the 'Thou shalt not kill'"), drawing the distinctions in the table below.
6. **Formal foundations.** Sketch the information-geometry connection (free energy as the objective; Fisher-information length) and consider category-theoretic structuralism (the proposal that "category theory allows us to elucidate structural interconnections between the laws of cognition and the laws of reality").

### E. Content that could elevate the book's impact

**What made the canonical books influential.** Rawls offered a *portable device* (original position / veil of ignorance) that anyone could apply, plus a clear adversary (utilitarianism). Kuhn gave a *portable concept* (paradigm shift). SRT needs one crystallized, exportable device. The strongest candidates already in the framework: **"scaffold vs. cage"** (when does an enabling structure become a trap?) and **"hidden debt"** (the deferred cost of order). These are intuitive, visual, and applicable across AI, institutions, ecology, and psychology.

**Positioning against current movements.** SRT can define itself against longtermism/EA: where longtermism (per Crary, Torres/Gebru's "TESCREAL" critique) "radically diminishes the value of the past and of humans alive today," SRT's "consequence return" and "embodied position" re-anchor value in concrete, sedimented presents. This is a sharp, marketable contrast.

**Live crises SRT can speak to:** AI capability atrophy and agentic governance; democratic backsliding via algorithmic pre-trimming; planetary-boundary overshoot as civilizational "hidden debt"; the mental-health/trauma discourse (SRT's structural-misalignment account of suffering offers an alternative to "the body keeps the score," which has itself been criticized for "poor scholarly standards").

| Tradition | Overlap (reinvention risk) | SRT's sharpest distinction |
|---|---|---|
| **Barad (agential realism)** | Subject/object emerge from a "cut"; exclusion theme; fused onto-epistemology-ethics | Diachronic selection-*history* + inscription; privileged embodied position; value as specific feedback |
| **Levinas** | Value as primary, non-derived | SRT's value is endogenous/functional (consequence-return)—the very reduction Levinas forbids; SRT *explains* rather than asserts |
| **Merleau-Ponty** | Body as "anchorage in the world"; flesh as pre-subject/object medium | SRT's position is *produced* by selection history and operational, not a pre-given elemental ontology |

| **OSR** | Relations/structures fundamental | Structures are *selected* and historically thick, not timeless |
| **Active inference** | Consequence feedback maintains order against entropy | Adds caring/value; "more to life than minimizing surprise" |

## Recommendations

**Stage 1 — Repair the foundations (before anything else).**
- Add a dedicated section naming and defending the **constitutivist** is-ought strategy and distinguishing the three is/ought sub-claims. *Benchmark to change course:* if reviewers still read the direction criteria as smuggled normativity, fall back to presenting them explicitly as pragmatic/heuristic.
- Add a **typology of selection** that states what does not count, defusing the over-breadth objection.
- State the **ε_pg axiom's epistemic status** explicitly (abductive vs. postulated).

**Stage 2 — Insert the missing interlocutors.**
- Write engagement sections for **active inference, Barad, Simondon, Whitehead, OSR, post-phenomenology, and epistemic injustice**, using the positioning table. *Threshold:* every chapter that makes an ontological or value claim should cite at least one of these and state the SRT difference.

**Stage 3 — Empirically anchor the abstract chain.**
- Attach the corroborating studies in Section C to the specific concepts (consequence return, pre-trimming, capability atrophy, sedimentation, lock-in). *Threshold:* no core mechanism should be asserted without at least one empirical or formal anchor or an explicit flag that it is speculative.

**Stage 4 — Future-proof and sharpen for impact.**
- Rewrite the AI chapter around the **2024–2026 agentic turn** and governance literature; date-stamp it.
- Foreground **one portable device** ("scaffold vs. cage" or "hidden debt") in the introduction and return to it in every applied chapter.
- Add a crisp **contrast with longtermism/EA** and an application to **one live crisis** worked end-to-end.

## Caveats
- Several supporting sources are preprints (arXiv) or secondary explainers rather than peer-reviewed primary literature; the broader capability-atrophy synthesis in particular comes from a single preprint aggregating other studies and should be verified against the underlying papers, though the two headline figures (Budzyń et al. in *Lancet Gastro & Hepatology* and Bastani et al. in *PNAS*) are peer-reviewed.
- The Simondon Finding 9 lineage rests partly on Deleuze's canonical paraphrase rather than Simondon's verbatim words; use a primary-text quotation if precision is required.
- The "body keeps the score" debate is genuinely contested; present both the embodied-trauma and the predictive-processing critiques rather than endorsing one.
- This report identifies *opportunities and risks*; it does not adjudicate whether SRT's core metaphysics is ultimately true—that judgment requires engaging the full 28-chapter text, which was not available for this research.
- Citations and source attributions here are for the author's internal use; verify all quotations against originals before publication.
