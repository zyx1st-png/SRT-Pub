# BOOK_CURRENT_STATUS Addendum: Reselectability as RC1 Revision Candidate

> This addendum registers a new book-level revision line without modifying frozen manuscript text.  
> It does not replace `BOOK_CURRENT_STATUS.md`; it should be read beside it until the main status file is safely updated.

---

## 0. Registration

**Revision line**: Objecthood as Reselectability / Reward–Reselectability Dissociation

**Status**: RC1后续修订候选，不直接动正文。

**Reason**:

The newly merged objecthood seed and the Wedge 1 toy result together clarify a major SRT advantage-line:

> SRT does not evaluate an object only by its properties, performance, utility, social value, or even ordinary robustness. It asks whether the object still retains the reselectable closure required to continue as an object.

This line should be treated as a **thematic strengthening line**, not a new structural reorganization.

---

## 1. Relation to the current book spine

Current master beam remains:

> 稳定不是起点，而是选择留下来的历史；秩序不是终点，而是后果回得来的地面。

The reselectability line is a downstream sharpening:

> 表现只是对象的输出；再选择能力才是对象性的生命线。

This should not replace the master beam. It should help the later acts clarify why order must return choice to consequence-bearing positions rather than become a high-performing cage.

---

## 2. Why this belongs in the book

The book already moves from:

1. given objects are not starting points;
2. selection leaves traces and builds terrain;
3. consequence-return produces stake, caring, value, agency, subject, and consciousness;
4. order can become scaffold or cage;
5. the final task is to return generation rather than close the theory.

The reselectability line gives this movement a sharper evaluative criterion:

> A structure has not merely succeeded when it performs well; it succeeds only when its performance does not destroy the future conditions of reselection for the relevant consequence-bearing positions.
