## 一、席位定位与背景

**评审人**：Robert Lawrence Kuhn（rlkuhn@me.com）
**席位**：R2 专业评审（R2-01）。画像匹配：意识科学全景视角（*A Landscape of Consciousness*，400+ 理论分类学）＋ 哲学/神学/神经科学交叉背景。
**信任基础**（通讯史三段）：

| 时间 | 事件 |
|---|---|
| 2021-02 | Closer To Truth 观众来信（泛序论雏形），Kuhn 回信："我们有相似的目标，我喜欢你的想法"，附赠两篇随笔，主动询问个人情况 |
| 2025-02 | AAT 反馈请求，Kuhn 回信肯定原创性（"You have an original idea"），主动提出将 AAT 列入 LoCw 候选名单，索要 250–500 词词条 |
| 2026-02 | SRT 词条提交 LoCw，Kuhn 两轮精准反馈（词条定向、bio 规范），词条与论文已收录待审（"We'll review at the next update"） |

**为什么是他**：读过并分类过的意识理论可能比世上任何人都多；其 Landscape 双论题（大问题只能在特定意识理论的光照下理解）恰是本书第三、四幕反向执行的纲领。预期他能给出的独特数据：**全景定位判断**（本书在 Landscape 分类学中坐标何处、最近邻是谁、与最近邻的真实差异）。

**派发策略：兴趣优先，分阶段释放。** 对方是日程繁重的八旬长者，首封邮件只做一件事——用一份几页的简报换取兴趣。不附问题清单，不给阅读路径，不预告靶点，不提任何时限（包括"不催您"这类话——提及时限本身就是压力）。全稿、十问、定向问题全部等他表态后再给。

**对协议的偏离登记**：
① 30 天软时限对此席位静默豁免（不在邮件中出现任何时间表述）；
② R2 行"预发问题清单：是"改为**后置**——十问移至第二阶段，首封只发简报（兴趣优先策略，作者裁决）；
③ 两阶段反馈合并为一阶段（R2 允许）；
④ 派发材料为英文全稿，靶区章节路径仅在对方主动询问"该读哪几章"时提供。

## 二、首封邮件·英文版（可直接发送）

**Subject: From SRT to a book: *From Existence to Order* — manuscript complete**

Dear Robert,

I hope this finds you well, and thank you again for taking SRT into the Landscape review queue in February.

I am writing to share one piece of news: the book-length development of SRT is complete. It is titled *From Existence to Order* — not an academic monograph but a work of foundational theory written as a sustained argument in natural language, a prologue and twenty-eight chapters, each built around a single question, the questions forming one continuous chain: from "why does the world feel like it is already there," through selection, irreversibility, and order, to value, the subject, and consciousness — and finally freedom, suffering, AI, and community. The English translation of the full manuscript is now finished.

The chain arrives at questions you have spent decades exploring through *Closer to Truth* — what consciousness is and where its boundary falls, whether free will survives in a world of structure, what makes suffering more than accumulated pain, and how AI is reshaping the ground on which all these questions stand. The book approaches them from underneath rather than head-on: it does not begin with consciousness and ask what it implies, but builds toward consciousness from a prior theory of selection, and only then turns the result onto the big questions.

I think the book may also cast the materials I sent you for the Landscape in a clearer light. The entry and the paper could only state SRT's claims in compressed form; the book is the argument behind them, built step by step from a single starting point.

The book is now at the stage where it needs eyes other than mine. The text is frozen as a review draft, and I am inviting a small number of first readers — what they see, and where they stumble, will decide the next round of revision.

I have attached a short reading brief — a few pages on the book's central argument, its architecture, and the conditions under which it would fail. It asks no more than twenty minutes of you.

If it catches your interest, I would be glad to send the full English manuscript. And any impression you might care to share — even a few lines on where this would sit in your Landscape — would mean a great deal to me.

Warm regards,

Yuxin Zhang

## 三、首封邮件·中文对照版（作者留档/核对用）

**主题：从 SRT 到一本书——《从存在到秩序》书稿完成**

Robert 您好：

见信安好。再次感谢您二月将 SRT 纳入 Landscape 的评审队列。

写这封信是想告诉您一个消息：SRT 的书籍版完成了。书名《从存在到秩序》（*From Existence to Order*）——不是学术专著，而是一部以自然语言写成的奠基理论思想书。序章加二十八章，每章围绕一个问题，二十八个问题连成一条不断裂的链：从"世界为什么感觉上已经在那里"，经过选择、不可逆与秩序，到价值、主体与意识——最后是自由、苦难、AI 与共同体。全稿的英文翻译现已完成。

这条链最终抵达的，恰恰是您通过 *Closer to Truth* 探索了数十年的那些问题——意识是什么、它的边界在哪里，自由意志在一个充满结构的世界里是否还能存活，苦难为何不只是疼痛的累加，以及 AI 正如何改写所有这些问题赖以站立的地面。这本书的路径是从下面接近它们，而不是正面迎上：它不从意识出发再追问意识意味着什么，而是从一个先行的选择理论出发搭建到意识，然后才把结果转向那些大问题。

我想这本书也能让我此前为 Landscape 提交的材料变得更清楚。词条和论文只能以压缩的形式陈述 SRT 的主张；这本书是它们背后的论证——从一个起点出发，一步一步搭起来。

这本书现在到了需要作者以外的眼睛的阶段。正文已冻结为评审稿，我正在邀请少数首批读者——他们看到什么、在哪里被绊住，将决定下一轮修订改什么。

随信附上一份简短的阅读简报——几页纸，讲这本书的中心论证、结构，以及它在什么条件下会失败。它最多只占用您二十分钟。

如果它引起了您的兴趣，我很乐意把英文全稿发给您。您愿意分享的任何印象——哪怕只是几行字，说说它会落在您的 Landscape 的什么位置——对我都意义重大。

祝好，

张昱鑫（Yuxin Zhang）

## 五、哲学评审十问·英文版（第二阶段备用，不随首封发出）

> 译自 `BOOK_RC0_REVIEW_PACKET_2026-06-12.md` §四。术语对齐：排开-定形-写入 = exclusion–shaping–inscription；承重位置 = bearing position；显影 = development（photographic sense）。

1. Chapter 4 positions its minimum non-neutrality postulate as abductive — "the cost of removing it exceeds the cost of accepting it." Does this epistemological strategy hold, in your view? Can its distinction from a brute fact be defended?

2. Chapter 5's reply to the action-theory objection is that "could have done otherwise" presupposes options already enumerated. Does this move successfully relocate the selection/mere-happening distinction above a complexity threshold, or does it merely evade the counterfactual condition?

3. Chapter 3 distinguishes the pre-object field from Aristotelian potentiality, Deleuze's virtual, and Simondon's pre-individual along three axes (less purpose, thinner structure, closer to stabilization). Is this threefold distinction sufficient to establish it as an independent concept?

4. Chapter 17 places felt experience as "what the same selection process is like when undergone from inside a bearing position," and positions this as a candidate model rather than a final solution. Compared with eliminativism, illusionism, and dual-aspect monism, wherein lies the independence of this "dissolve rather than solve" route?

5. Chapter 22 claims that "when the object of description is a living selection structure, the maintain/consume distinction is already not purely descriptive." Can this is–ought constitutivist strategy survive the open question argument?

6. Chapter 16's layered accountability (consequence return as the starting point; degree of accountability depending on sedimentation, comprehension, concern, and the re-selection window) — does it add real content beyond existing compatibilist proposals?

7. Chapter 26's five failure conditions (selection-structure threshold / consequence return / consciousness boundary / structural criteria / terrain analysis), plus its three beam-level conditions — do they constitute genuinely failable positions, or are they structurally untestable?

8. The book's stated discipline holds that cross-domain material *develops* (reveals the same structure) but does not *prove*. Is this discipline actually observed in argumentative practice? Are there passages where development in fact carries argumentative weight?

9. Chapter 25's "generative monism" (one single chain — without positing a common substrate) — does its distinction from neutral monism hold? Can the formula "the pre-object field is a premise, not a substrate" withstand sustained questioning?

10. Does chapter 27's reflexive turn (analyzing this book's own formation through exclusion–shaping–inscription) genuinely complete the reflexive test, or does it exempt itself at the final step?
