## 2. What the four answers accept (bounded M interpretation)

The immediate question context offered four specific choices. The author accepted:

1. **Contestable research assumptions:** AI can actively explore and propose an explicitly provisional challenge even to current canonical premises, without claiming that `K` has been reopened or amended. Formal canonical replacement still needs author adjudication and existing edit gates.
2. **Constructive AI participation:** AI may proactively form new ideas, concepts, explanations and cross-domain questions, accurately labeled as machine-origin candidates rather than attributing them to the author. The author retains substantive adoption, meaning and research-direction decisions. This does not independently authorize creation of new repository theory files before a scoped request or author convergence.
3. **Phase-sensitive `外部对照三项`:** The mandatory **fixed output block** is waived for open exploration/intuition dialogue. The author explicitly explained the original concern: ChatGPT sometimes failed to read concepts already present in the repository or mature adjacent theories. **AI still needs the relevant source and neighbor reading**, and should report any substantive finding actually made, briefly and with provenance, without importing neighbor vocabulary into the author's own formulation. For explicit external-comparison tasks, hardening, formal review or publication review, keep the structured three-item report. A lightweight finding-only signal is a machine-side implementation of this purpose, **not a new verbatim author instruction**.
4. **Thought-space versus formal programme:** Cross-domain ontology-to-social conjectures may be explored without treating `CURRENT NEXT` and HOLD as bans on thinking. Existing author controls, formal project/experiment authorization, promotion, scoped comparison, evidence and negative-results gates remain.

The choice numbered 3 specifically relaxes the **display requirement**, not the underlying source scrutiny, nor the author's preference to keep their originating intuitions in their own language.

## 3. What this does not authorize

- No promotion, replacement or reopening of an actual canonical `K` commitment merely because an AI proposes an alternative; preserve the distinction of `C` (AI reconstruction state) and `M` (machine provenance).
- No second `CURRENT NEXT`, new deep well, cross-domain programme, experiment, GRG fusion or HOLD release; no rewriting published/submitted manuscripts or changing published scientific claims without task-specific authorization.
- No claim that ontology-to-social connections are established entailments, empirically verified laws or normative/political legitimacy conclusions.
- No exemption from prior-art/source fidelity, valid negative verdicts, rigorous reviewer objections, independent C-class semantic review, author second adjudication or appropriate scientific evidence.
- No assertion about the frequency of defensive rewriting, the efficacy of this governance amendment or proof that existing procedures are inadequate; those require the 2026-10-15 replay/evaluation programme or other independent evidence.

The author's consent is to this **bounded collaboration and presentation correction**, not to every machine-generated explanation offered before the four-question alignment.
