# Author adjudication — generativity direction

The author fixes two points for the next reconstruction cycle.

1. Minimum non-neutrality and actualising Selection should be treated as co-primitive burdens of generativity rather than two temporally ordered mechanisms. The aim is to avoid both a maximally-indifferent brute generative floor and a prior-purpose generative model. This does not restore a pregiven option inventory or the old strong epsilon theorem.

2. The is/ought problem should continue to be approached by dissolution rather than by deriving a normative layer from a neutral completed fact layer. Working thesis: determinate `is` is a cross-section of a more primitive directional generative relation.

Keep distinct:

- O0: primitive generative directionality / non-maximal indifference;
- O1: better-or-worse-for after a genuine consequence-bearing standing exists;
- O2: responsibility only after stronger agency conditions are paid.

Do not collapse O0 into complete ethics, actual occurrence into correctness, continuation into goodness, or O1 into O2.
