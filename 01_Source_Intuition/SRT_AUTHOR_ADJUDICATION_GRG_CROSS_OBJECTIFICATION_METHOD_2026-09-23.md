## 1. Direct author anchors

### 1.1 GRG is not cross-domain word/object matching

**[A0-Q — 2026-09-23 dialogue, author correction immediately after the evolution-case plain-language review]**

> “GRg并不是去找两个不同领域的词的对应。而是用一套更底层的生成模型去让一个领域的模型更加连贯，并尝试提出这个模型缺失的一些内容。但刚才听你解释生物学这条线好像又跑成原来的样子了。”

### 1.2 GRG names and formalisms are themselves objectifications

**[A0-Q — 2026-09-23 dialogue, author clarification immediately following §1.1]**

> “grg的模型里的各名称和未来可能形式化的内容。只是一种对象化的描述。不需要在其他领域去找到相应的对象去进行对应。而是要理解各名称和形式化内容背后所想表明的深层的语法。然后将其他领域的各种概念和形式化内容去重新整理。甚至有可能GRg中的所有名称和形式化都和该领域的内容对应不上。但这并不是这种研究方法的问题。”

## 2. Faithful author-direction paraphrase

**[A0-P]**

The author's correction can be faithfully summarized as:

1. GRG terms, named constructs and future equations are revisable objectifications used to work on a deeper generative research problem; they are not a universal inventory of objects that every domain must instantiate.
2. Cross-domain work should not search for one-to-one object correspondence between domain constructs and GRG constructs.
3. The research task is to reorganize source-native concepts and formalisms so that the generative relations they express, separate, hide, combine or leave implicit can be studied more coherently.
4. A domain may contain no natural object-level counterpart for any current GRG term. That fact alone is not a failure of the method.
5. GRG should be able to reveal missing relations, new questions or stronger integration among a domain's already mature models rather than win by vocabulary substitution.

## 3. Failure-right correction

**[M — package direction accepted; no item-level A1 claim]**

Provenance:
- prompted by the independent critique that object-level mismatch cannot remove all failure conditions;
- synthesized in the assistant's subsequent analysis;
- package-level direction was accepted by §1.3, but the wording below is not separately author-adjudicated.

The author correction does **not** make GRG unfalsifiable.

The level at which failure moves is:

~~~text
object-name mismatch
!= method failure by itself

but

a declared grammar candidate can fail
and
a declared gain claim can fail.
~~~

A specific grammar candidate should be revised / withdrawn when, under its frozen scope:

- source-native reconstruction does not exhibit the claimed relation;
- the relation disappears under legitimate re-objectification / redescription;
- the proposed structure reduces to a generic triviality with no retained constraints;
- the GRG reconstruction adds no nontrivial coherence, diagnostic, predictive, intervention or failure burden beyond mature source practice;
- a prospectively claimed distinction is absorbed by the strongest mature source-native baseline.

Revision after failure is allowed, but revision is a **new candidate**. It does not retroactively turn the failed candidate into a success.

## 4. No-God-view correction

**[M — package direction accepted; no item-level A1 claim]**

Provenance:
- independent critique: a "deep grammar" cannot be treated as directly accessed outside objectification;
- assistant synthesis: reframe grammar as what is proposed to survive legitimate re-objectification;
- constrained by existing repository no-God-view / reflexivity guards.

"Deeper grammar" must not be treated as an independently accessible layer outside all objectification.

Working interpretation:

> a grammar candidate is a relation / constraint organization proposed to survive legitimate changes of objectification while retaining nontrivial admission, exclusion, consequence and failure structure.

Therefore:

~~~text
deep grammar candidate
!= final hidden object
!= God's-eye representation
!= direct access to reality without cuts
~~~

GRG itself remains one revisable cut through generative organization.

## 5. Discovery and test must be separated

**[M — package direction accepted; no item-level A1 claim]**

Provenance:
- independent critique: decomposition is lens-dependent, so post-hoc recurrence is not independent support;
- assistant synthesis: distinguish grammar-formation DISCOVERY from pre-frozen TEST;
- package-level direction was accepted by §1.3.

Two modes are required:

~~~text
DISCOVERY MODE
= source-native reconstruction
  + alternative re-objectifications
  + relation reorganization
  + missing-relation search
  + GRG self-revision allowed

TEST MODE
= a relation / burden is frozen before decisive held-out inspection
  + scope and failure conditions declared
  + legitimate re-objectifications specified
  + mature baseline / absorption gate applied
  + cross-objectification recurrence or prospective gain tested
~~~

Discovery is allowed to change the grammar.

Test mode is required before claiming the changed grammar has survived independently.
