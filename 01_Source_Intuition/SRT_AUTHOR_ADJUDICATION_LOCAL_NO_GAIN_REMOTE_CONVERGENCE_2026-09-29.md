# Author adjudication — local no-gain does not imply SRT subtraction

> **Role:** preserve the 2026-09-29 author correction to how mature-neighbor overlap is interpreted during SRT development.
>
> **Boundary:** this is a noncanonical author/governance source. It does not establish that any particular cross-domain mapping is true, does not license retrospective relabeling, and does not convert convergence into evidence for SRT identity or superiority.

## A0-Q — direct author correction

> 「有一点我想强调，srt我理解是更远的收敛，所以在路径上和其他理论对比没有增量并不应该缩减srt理论，同时应该将相近材料记录，最后才能在更远端获得增量收益，或更普适的视角」

## A1 — controlling interpretation

The author distinguishes **domain-local comparative increment** from the longer-horizon SRT objective.

A mature neighbor fully owning one local explanatory job means:

~~~text
NO LOCAL COMPARATIVE INCREMENT
for that scoped claim / explanatory job
~~~

It does **not** by itself mean:

~~~text
delete the corresponding SRT relation;
downgrade the relation merely because a neighbor already realizes it;
stop recording the neighbor;
treat overlap as a theory failure;
infer whole-SRT no-gain.
~~~

Where the SRT-side relation is independently recoverable from an earlier owner / author source, the correct U-mode action is:

~~~text
respect source ownership
+ block local novelty / superiority language
+ retain the SRT-side relation at its existing authority
+ preserve the neighbor as a mapped realization / resonance / pressure / contrast / translation coordinate
+ carry the mapping forward for later cross-domain convergence tests.
~~~

Possible later gains include, if independently earned:

- explanatory compression across domains;
- transferable distinctions;
- reciprocal constraints between otherwise separated fields;
- cross-domain prediction / intervention transfer;
- a more general generative relation or perspective that is not visible from one domain alone.

None of these gains is automatic from repeated resemblance.

## A2 — anti-universalization guard

“More distant convergence” must not become an all-absorbing theory move.

Required guards remain:

1. the proposed SRT invariant must be recoverable **before** the domain match is evaluated;
2. source-native mechanisms and explanatory ownership stay with the source;
3. verbal similarity is insufficient for realization / homology / identity;
4. failed mappings pressure the SRT mapping and, when relevant, the proposed invariant;
5. multiple local overlaps do not establish global identity, truth, superiority or primitive Selection;
6. local no-gain remains a binding restriction on the local comparative claim even when the material retains convergence value.

## A3 — terminology correction

Bare labels such as `NO-GAIN` are ambiguous because they can be misread as a whole-theory subtraction verdict.

Prefer one of:

~~~text
NO LOCAL COMPARATIVE INCREMENT
SOURCE-OWNED AT THIS EXPLANATORY JOB
LOCAL CLAIM ABSORBED / CONVERGENCE VALUE RETAINED
~~~

Then separately state the U-mode disposition:

~~~text
resonance / contrast / pressure / translation / realization / unresolved
~~~

This correction applies prospectively to governance and future audits. Existing historical `NO-GAIN` labels remain provenance unless separately corrected.
