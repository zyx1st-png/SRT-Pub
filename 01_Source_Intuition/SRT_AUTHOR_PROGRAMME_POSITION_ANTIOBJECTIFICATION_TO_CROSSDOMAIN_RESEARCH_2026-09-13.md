# Author programme position — anti-objectification as starting inversion, not terminal goal

> **Nature:** noncanonical author programme statement. It records the intended scale and direction of SRT. It does not establish scientific truth, historical priority, superiority, social legitimacy, a Level assignment, or a canonical theorem.

---

## 1. Author programme clarification

**AUTHOR-CONFIRMED direction:**

SRT did not begin from a goal of merely opposing objectification, and anti-objectification is not its terminal thesis.

The intended programme is:

```text
object-first assumptions are inverted
-> Selection / generation becomes prior
-> a continuous generative architecture is developed
-> the architecture is used as a cross-domain research grammar
-> hard problems are re-posed where object-first framing may have hidden their generative conditions
-> the framework is tested for disciplined transfer across sciences and social analysis
-> ontology-informed social / institutional guidance may be developed only with an explicit normative layer where ought-claims are made.
```

Anti-objectification is therefore the **starting methodological inversion**, not the full value proposition.

---

## 2. Relation to Simondon — overlap at the starting inversion, not identity of programme

The author recognizes a substantial but limited convergence with Simondon:

```text
completed individual / object is not the explanatory starting point;
individuation / generation precedes the finished individual;
relation and process cannot be reduced to later interactions among already-completed objects.
```

This convergence should be treated as a strong historical / philosophical neighbor, not denied for novelty protection.

At the same time, SRT's intended programme is not exhausted by the Simondonian individuation programme.

Current author positioning:

```text
Simondon:
strong preindividual / individuation programme;
strong transduction and operation-structure reciprocity;
continued individuation within that philosophical architecture.

SRT intended continuation:
anti-objectification
-> primitive subjectless Selection grammar
-> manifestation / relative-background verticality
-> Selection-mediated vertical recurrence
-> One formation
-> post-One relational architecture
-> cross-domain research grammar
-> disciplined transfer / hard-problem reconstruction
-> ontology-informed social and institutional diagnosis.
```

The comparison must not rely on a simplistic claim that Simondon has "no continuity". His transductive operation-structure continuity is real and should be preserved in strongest-neighbor comparison.

The intended SRT difference is instead the **scope, continuity, and research-programme ambition of the generative architecture**.

---

## 3. Historical-impact guard

The author has expressed the view that Simondon's programme, despite its depth, did not historically develop into a continuous cross-disciplinary research architecture and broad social / research paradigm comparable to the ambition now intended for SRT.

This is recorded as programme motivation only.

Do **not** infer:

```text
limited historical uptake -> theory false;
limited social impact -> theory structurally incomplete;
SRT broader ambition -> SRT true or superior.
```

Historical influence is not evidence for ontological validity.

The valid comparison remains architectural and programmatic.

---

## 4. Anti-objectification as a research operation

SRT's anti-objectification should become a disciplined research procedure rather than a slogan.

For any domain, the programme asks:

```text
1. What "object" is the field currently treating as already given?
2. Which parts of that object's apparent properties may actually belong to its formation conditions?
3. What Selection-like actual differentiation makes the relevant phenomenon manifest / operative?
4. What relative background structure is co-generated rather than merely omitted by the observer?
5. Which consequences of prior Selection alter later Selection conditions?
6. Is there only event-level verticality, or Selection-mediated vertical reconstitution?
7. Has a relatively separable One actually formed, or is the analyst naming a transient pattern as an entity?
8. Which downstream standing relations — perspective, Bearer, Concern, Agency, cognition, subject-position — are being silently projected backward into formation?
9. What variables, controls, scales, or interventions become visible once those conflations are removed?
```

This list is a programme-level diagnostic grammar, not a universal empirical protocol.

---

## 5. Cross-domain unification target

The author retains the 2026-09-10 theory-value position:

```text
SRT is not primarily a catalog of domain-specific novel mechanisms.
```

Its intended value is:

```text
heterogeneous domain phenomena / mechanisms
-> disciplined mapping into one more general ontological architecture
-> common distinctions and dependency order
-> cross-domain explanatory compression
-> constrained transfer of questions / controls / research expectations.
```

Accordingly, a domain theory independently possessing a local mechanism does not defeat SRT.

But SRT gains no credit merely by relabeling that mechanism.

The framework must still pay:

```text
U1 cross-domain structural invariance;
U2 explanatory compression;
U3 mapping / exclusion discipline;
U4 nontrivial transfer;
U5 anti-redundancy / removal loss.
```

---

## 6. Hard-problem reconstruction target

The stronger intended use of SRT is not simply to explain more already-framed problems.

It is to test whether some hard problems are partly generated by object-first framing itself.

Programme pattern:

```text
completed object is assumed
-> a difficult property is then asked about that object
-> the property's generative preconditions are hidden by the initial object assumption.
```

SRT therefore asks whether a problem can be productively re-posed as:

```text
pre-object / pre-unit generative conditions
-> Selection / actual differentiation
-> vertical organization
-> recurrence / formation
-> formed One
-> downstream standing or relation under separate gates.
```

Candidate domains include, without claiming current solutions:

```text
life / organism boundaries;
subject and first-person standing;
cognition and object formation;
AI agency / subject claims;
collective and institutional identity;
social organization and persistent power structures;
research-object construction across sciences.
```

No domain solution is established by this programme statement alone.

---

## 7. Cross-domain transfer, not retrospective translation

A strong SRT transfer is not:

```text
domain result already known
-> rewrite result in SRT vocabulary.
```

The intended stronger test is:

```text
a distinction / dependency is learned or clarified in domain A
-> SRT abstracts a bounded ontological relation
-> before decisive inspection in domain B, the relation generates a new question, missing control, scale distinction, failure mode, or research expectation
-> domain B then supports, weakens, or rejects that transfer.
```

This is the programme-level meaning of research-view consistency across disciplines.

---

## 8. Social and institutional guidance

The author intends SRT eventually to support a unified social / institutional diagnostic perspective.

The safe architectural relation is:

```text
ontology
-> constrains what kinds of social entities, boundaries, continuities, positions, exclusions, and histories should be treated as generated rather than pre-given
-> informs institutional diagnosis and design questions.
```

However:

```text
ontology alone
!= normative conclusion.
```

No direct inference is licensed from:

```text
what reality is
```

to:

```text
what society morally ought to do.
```

Where SRT is used for social guidance, an explicit normative / value layer must be supplied and separately defended.

---

## 9. Candidate ontology-informed social diagnostics

Without creating political doctrine, SRT may motivate questions such as:

```text
which problems are allowed to become manifest and which are structurally backgrounded?
which historical Selection consequences have become invisible conditions of later institutional Selection?
which formed positions repeatedly determine what can enter later Selection?
which boundaries are treated as natural although they are products of repeated reconstitution?
which scale of One is being privileged while nested or competing Ones are ignored?
which operators / institutions have become conditions of Selection while becoming insufficiently exposed to further Selection themselves?
```

These are diagnostic research questions, not normative answers.

---

## 10. SRT programme layers

For programme clarity, the intended architecture may be read as four nested tasks:

```text
A. Ontological inversion
   object-first -> Selection / generation first

B. Generative architecture
   Selection -> vertical relation -> vertical recurrence -> One
   -> separately gated post-One relations

C. Research paradigm
   use the architecture to reconstruct questions, variables,
   scales, controls, and transfer across disciplines

D. Social / civilizational application
   use ontology-informed diagnosis to examine institutions,
   power, identity, collective formation and long-horizon guidance,
   while keeping normative commitments explicit and separate.
```

These are programme levels, not new canonical ontology stages.

---

## 11. Anti-protection guard

This programme must not become an immunity strategy.

Do not infer:

```text
whatever a domain theory discovers = merely an implementation of SRT;
any hard problem = proof that objectification caused it;
any social conflict = Selection theory confirmed;
broader scope = deeper truth;
philosophical coherence = empirical validation.
```

The intended programme succeeds only where disciplined mapping, explanatory compression, transfer, exclusion, and problem-solving gains can be independently demonstrated.
