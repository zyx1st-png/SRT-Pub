# Author adjudication — study preformal coherence intuition as the next research object

> **Role:** records the author's decision to make the apparently preformal / intuition-like coherence capacity itself the next research object.
>
> **Boundary:** this is not a claim that the author has a unique faculty, not a self-assessment score, and not an SRT proof. The research target is explicitly universal-first and individual-difference-second.

## 0. Direct author instruction

> 「我觉得这种能力并不是我特有的。而是普遍的，只是我比较特殊一点，所以我想研究一下这一点，作为下一个Current next。」

This instruction supersedes the previous immediate CURRENT NEXT once the present routing package is merged.

---

## 1. Author correction to the prior self-assessment frame

The research question is **not**:

> Why does this author possess an unusual theoretical intuition?

The new question is:

> **Do humans generally possess a preformal capacity to detect coherence, incoherence, direction-of-fit or constraint violation before they can explicitly formalize the reason, and how do individuals differ in its sensitivity, domain breadth, calibration and ability to convert it into explicit reasoning?**

The author's own SRT / AI dialogue history may be used as one high-intensity naturalistic case.

It must not be used as sole evidence for universality or exceptional ability.

---

## 2. Neutral working construct

Preferred working description:

> **Preformal coherence sensitivity**: the ability to discriminate whether a complex representation / relation-set is globally coherent or structurally misaligned before a complete explicit justification is available.

Avoid creating a permanent acronym at this stage.

A related phenomenological description:

> **theoretical-shape intuition**: a felt sense that a proposed conceptual structure is fitting or drifting even when the exact error has not yet been articulated.

The second phrase is descriptive only.

---

## 3. Minimal signature

A candidate episode counts only if it shows at least:

~~~text
T1. early discrimination:
    coherent vs incoherent / fitting vs drifting judgment occurs before full rationale;

T2. above-chance or externally validated direction:
    the early judgment is not merely retrospective storytelling;

T3. delayed articulation:
    explicit reasons / formal corrections appear later than the initial discrimination;

T4. structural target:
    the judgment concerns relations / constraints / global organization,
    not only familiarity, preference or isolated factual recall.
~~~

Stronger signatures:

~~~text
T5. root-level correction:
    the later fix changes the organizing frame rather than a local sentence;

T6. transfer:
    similar early discrimination appears across domains or unfamiliar terminology;

T7. calibration:
    confidence tracks eventual accuracy rather than merely subjective certainty;

T8. compression:
    the person can rapidly read / reject a high-dimensional proposal
    without reconstructing every local inference explicitly.
~~~

## 4. Universal-first hypothesis family

Do not start from a special-faculty assumption.

### H0 — no distinct construct

Performance is fully explained by:
- ordinary domain knowledge;
- explicit reasoning;
- familiarity;
- verbal ability;
- working memory;
- generic confidence.

### H1 — widespread preformal coherence sensitivity

Most humans can detect some forms of coherence / incoherence before explicit solution.

Individual performance varies.

### H2 — primarily domain-specific skill

The capacity reflects learned regularities and expertise; cross-domain transfer is weak.

### H3 — partially domain-general architecture

A common human mechanism supports preformal coherence detection, but domain knowledge / history tunes the geometry on which it operates.

### H4 — higher-order integration differences

Some individuals may show unusual:
- breadth of learned relational structure;
- tolerance for unresolved explicit detail;
- sensitivity to global constraint violation;
- ability to keep multiple candidate organizations active;
- conversion from tacit mismatch to root-level recut.

H4 is a candidate individual-difference model, not an author self-description to be assumed true.

## 5. Existing scientific neighbors

The research begins from existing cognitive science rather than treating the phenomenon as unstudied.

### Semantic-coherence intuition

Bowers et al. (1990), *Cognitive Psychology*, DOI 10.1016/0010-0285(90)90004-N:
people could discriminate semantic coherence before identifying the solution, and tacit coherence could later surface as a hunch / hypothesis.

A 2024 Frontiers study continues this line by experimentally probing intuitive semantic-coherence judgments and priming effects:
Sweklej & Balas (2024), DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1406811.

### Fuzzy-Trace Theory / gist intuition

Reyna's Fuzzy-Trace Theory treats gist-based intuition as advanced meaning-based cognition rather than merely primitive impulsivity.

Useful anchors:
- Reyna (2023), "A new intuitionism: Meaning, memory, and development in Fuzzy-Trace Theory";
- Brainerd & Reyna line of work on gist / verbatim processing.

Important pressure:
expertise and development can increase reliance on gist, but gist can also generate systematic bias.

### Intuitive expertise

Kahneman & Klein (2009), DOI 10.1037/a0016755:
intuitive expertise is trustworthy only under suitable environmental regularity and sufficient learning opportunity; subjective certainty alone is not evidence of accuracy.

### Statistical / implicit learning

Statistical learning is widespread from infancy and supports extraction of environmental regularities.

Useful anchors:
- Forest et al. (2023), *Nature Reviews Psychology*, development of statistical learning;
- individual-difference work shows measurable variation but also serious psychometric / task-specificity issues.

### Neighbor families still to map

- schema abstraction;
- pattern completion / attractor dynamics;
- relational reasoning;
- metacognitive feeling-of-rightness;
- perceptual / semantic fluency;
- insight / Aha;
- naturalistic expert recognition;
- predictive / generative processing.

No one neighbor is currently identified as equivalent to the target construct.

---

## 6. Main distinction from generic intuition

The target is not "intuition" in general.

Exclude or separately type:

- affective preference;
- mere familiarity;
- fast heuristic choice;
- social intuition;
- motor expertise;
- implicit bias;
- aesthetic liking;
- random hunch;
- confidence without discrimination.

The narrow target is:

> **pre-explicit sensitivity to relational / global coherence whose later articulation can be independently checked.**

---

## 7. Why the author's recent dialogue is a useful case but not proof

The SRT / GRG / HP-B dialogue contains repeated candidate episodes of the form:

~~~text
AI produces a locally plausible package
-> author rapidly rejects / redirects it
-> reason is initially partial
-> subsequent dialogue identifies a root-level structural mismatch
-> independent review / owner crosswalk sometimes confirms the mismatch
-> theory architecture is recut.
~~~

Examples include:
- L0 / L1 / L2 from "spaces / layers" toward analytic views;
- grammar-first GRG toward reconstruction-first / grammar-late;
- qualia not reducible to friction alone;
- One / Bearer / experience proper separation;
- constitutive Ontological Friction restored after over-subtraction.

These are high-value naturalistic observations.

They are also contaminated by:
- repeated exposure;
- author ownership of the theory;
- AI adaptation to author language;
- retrospective selection of memorable successes;
- repository review procedures;
- domain-specific expertise accumulated through the project.

Therefore use them as **Phase 0/1 case material**, not population evidence.

## 8. SRT relation — allowed only as a downstream interpretation

A later SRT interpretation may ask whether preformal coherence sensitivity can be described as:

~~~text
L2 sedimented relational organization
-> L0-facing gate geometry
-> early mismatch / Ontological Friction
-> foreground hunch / "something is wrong"
-> later L1 explicit objectification / explanation
-> possible gate recut.
~~~

This is an SRT mapping candidate.

It must not be built into the neutral psychological construct definition.

The empirical programme must remain capable of concluding:

~~~text
the phenomenon is fully explained by existing cognitive science;
SRT adds no unique explanatory gain.
~~~

---

## 9. Author-specific hypothesis — deliberately weak

A bounded hypothesis for later testing:

> The author may not possess a unique faculty; instead, the same widespread coherence-detection architecture may be unusually expressed through a particular combination of long-term relational sedimentation, broad cross-domain exposure, low dependence on terminology familiarity, tolerance for incomplete explicit formalization, and repeated practice using AI as an external articulation engine.

This is a hypothesis only.

Alternative explanations must include:
- confirmation bias;
- selective recall;
- theory ownership;
- broad but shallow familiarity;
- AI accommodation;
- overfitting of explanations after the fact;
- verbal reconstruction that makes an initially vague preference look structurally deep.
