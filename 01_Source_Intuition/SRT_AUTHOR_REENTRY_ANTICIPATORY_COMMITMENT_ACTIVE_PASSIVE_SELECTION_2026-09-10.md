# Author clarification — prediction is anticipatory commitment; Selection has passive and active modes

## 1. Exact author wording

> **「预测是更强的后者。Selection暂停后，不太确定你想表达的意思。我感觉你想表达的意思，我想回答的内容是Selection有主动和被动的区别。被动的选择是一种生存，而主动的选择是一种创造和重构。」**

## 2. Prediction / anticipation ruling

The author selects the stronger of the two previously offered readings.

Not merely:

```text
current Selection organizes what remains selectable next.
```

But more strongly:

```text
current Selection includes anticipatory commitment / preparation toward
some possible future continuation(s).
```

This is an author-level directional commitment. It does not yet specify a Bayesian, cognitive, representational, control-theoretic, or teleological mechanism.

## 3. Active / passive Selection distinction

The author introduces a new distinction:

```text
passive Selection = a form of survival / continued existence / maintenance;
active Selection  = a form of creation and reconstruction.
```

At minimum, this means the previous question framed as “what happens if Selection pauses?” should not be treated as a simple on/off switch.

The more faithful current distinction is between modes / burdens of Selection.

### Passive Selection

Author wording: **“一种生存”**.

Minimum safe reading:

```text
Selection can continue by preserving / sustaining an already achieved way of existing
and its currently viable selectable space.
```

Do not silently reduce this to biological survival only. Exact cross-domain scope remains open.

### Active Selection

Author wording: **“一种创造和重构”**.

Minimum safe reading:

```text
Selection can actively create / reconstruct the selectable space itself,
including future-directed preparation / commitment that changes what later Selection can be.
```

Active Selection is therefore not merely choosing a route inside a finished option set.

## 4. Relation to the vertical-structure clarification

The previous author ruling stated:

```text
vertical structure != passive historical sediment;
Selection actively constructs / maintains / predicts selectable space.
```

The current distinction sharpens that statement:

```text
passive mode:
maintain / survive within an achieved selective organization.

active mode:
create / reconstruct selective organization and its future possibility space.
```

This does not yet establish that every instance of maintenance is passive, every instance of creation is active, or that the two modes are mutually exclusive at every scale. Those stronger formal separations remain open.

## 5. First-person / pre-subject guard

Do not infer that all Selection requires an already-formed subject.

Current layered reading remains:

```text
pre-subject:
subjectless Selection can participate in constitution of non-flat selectable space.

post-formation:
a Selection-position / One can enact Selection from within its own position.
```

The active/passive distinction is author-introduced, but its exact application to the pre-subject layer is not yet adjudicated.

## 6. Anticipatory commitment and active Selection

The current author wording supports the following minimum relation:

```text
active Selection
-> creation / reconstruction of selectable space
-> stronger anticipation than mere next-step accessibility ordering
-> preparation / commitment toward possible future continuation(s).
```

Guard:

```text
anticipatory commitment != already proved conscious intention;
anticipatory commitment != fixed purpose / final cause;
anticipatory commitment != Bayesian prior by definition.
```

## 7. Replacement of the previous “Selection pause” gate

The previous machine question asked whether selectable space remains when Selection temporarily stops.

The author does not accept that as the most useful framing.

Current replacement:

> **Distinguish survival/maintenance Selection from creative/reconstructive Selection before asking about persistence. A system may reduce or suspend active reconstruction while passive Selection continues to sustain an achieved organization.**

Whether complete cessation of all Selection implies dissolution, dormancy, or merely loss of active selectability must remain domain-scoped and is not adjudicated here.
