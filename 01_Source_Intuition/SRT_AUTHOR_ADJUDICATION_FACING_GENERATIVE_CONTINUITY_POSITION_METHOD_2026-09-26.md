## 0. Why this record exists

The author explicitly judged the last several dialogue rounds to be highly valuable and asked that both the author direction and the machine analysis path be preserved.

The author also identified a retrospective diagnosis of why earlier work repeatedly circled the same questions:

> **「之前研究之所以出现打转的情况，是没有把L0、 L1、 L2的Facing给它分出来，导致它们混在一起。」**

This record therefore treats **Facing separation** as the controlling author correction for the next reconstruction pass.

The phrase “Facing” remains an analytic-view usage, not a claim that L0 / L1 / L2 are three ontic substances or spaces.

## 1. Position is not a static state

Earlier in the same dialogue, the author clarified:

> **「一个Position的形成，就是为了让一个状态不会随意地变成另一个状态。这本身就提供了一种非平坦的指向。」**

The author then corrected the machine's concern that “为了” might imply external teleology:

> **「另外关于Position的形成，你特意反对，不要把为了变成目的论。但是我这里加为了，是因为原初选择要在这里发挥作用。是原初选择让 position持续地维持某种状态的转化。而不是一种静态的状态。准确的来说，这种转化就是生成性。」**

Author direction:

~~~text
Position
!= static state
!= coordinate in a completed state space
!= mere persistence of one state

Position formation
= a formed organization in which transformation is no longer arbitrary
  and is continuously regenerated through Selection.

The relevant continuity is therefore not state-invariance.
Transformation itself is generative.
~~~

The author's “为了” is retained as a statement about the constitutive generative role of Position, not as a prewritten goal, cosmic preference, or external final cause.

## 2. L0-, L1- and L2-facing continuity must be separated

The author explicitly stated:

> **「我觉得状态的连续性是一种L2 facing的内容。主体对象的连续性是L1 facing的内容。而L0 facing的连续性，目前来说还没有明确的定义。你可以尝试帮我推演一下。」**

Author-accepted separation:

~~~text
L2-facing continuity
= continuity of retained / historically effective state, relation, constraint or sediment.

L1-facing continuity
= continuity of determinate subject / object manifestation or identification.

L0-facing continuity
= distinct problem; must not be defined by state persistence,
  object identity, memory continuity or a completed transition geometry.
~~~

The author repeatedly accepted the machine derivation that L0-facing continuity is best investigated as continuity **through transformation**, not continuity **despite transformation**.

Current accepted direction:

> **Working candidate only:** L0-facing continuity was initially read as recurrent renewal of genuine generativity through formed Position transformation. Post-#1069 this is now an FRH-0 reduction test: first ask whether the burden is fully absorbed by O0 non-preclosure + Selection-mediated recurrent reconstitution + formed Selection-position.

It remains a candidate reconstruction, not a frozen canonical definition.

## 3. Ontological source of L0-facing continuity

The author then supplied the key source claim:

> **「关于你提出来的问题，我不先说直接的证据，但我感觉这种L0 Facing的连续性的来源就是原初选择」**

Current author direction:

~~~text
primitive Selection
= ontological source of the generativity renewed in the candidate L0-facing continuity;
  recurrent history / One supplies the formed localization / continuation burden.

But:

primitive Selection
!= universal preference for continuation
!= guarantee that every Selection forms a continuing One
!= rejection of terminal Selection
!= survival / non-self-erasure telos.
~~~

This preserves the existing canonical and author guards around terminal Selection and primitive O0 non-teleology.

The current reconstruction question is therefore:

> how does primitive Selection become localized into recurrent, historically formed generativity without being reduced to the execution of already retained L1/L2 structure?

## 5. One and Position — author-accepted simplification direction

The author accepted the proposal that One and Position may be two readings of the same formed generative organization rather than two sequential entities.

Current candidate:

~~~text
One
= diachronic / across-reconstitution reading

Position
= current operative from-where reading

Orientation
= prospective / future-facing asymmetry reading
~~~

The author also accepted the stronger reconstruction:

> **One formation may be understood as Selection-produced history becoming capable not merely of biasing later outcomes, but of participating in the formation of the later Selection's operative from-where.**

Thus:

~~~text
history affects later outcome
= L2 history dependence, not sufficient for One.

history helps form later operative from-where
= explanatory gloss only AFTER One is independently admitted by the canonical recurrent-reconstitution route;
  not an admission criterion.
~~~

This must not be hardened into the canonical One owner without a bounded pressure pass and author second adjudication.

## 6. Position does not add generativity as a second source

The author accepted the distinction:

~~~text
primitive Selection
= source of genuine generativity

Position / One
= formed localization / historical conditioning of that generativity

Position does not create a second primitive operation.
One does not manufacture Selection.
History / writeback does not become the source of primitive Selection.
~~~

The useful downstream shorthand is:

> **One formation is a move from anonymous actualisation to historically positioned generation.**

“Anonymous” here is only analytic shorthand for the absence of a formed continuing One; it does not imply a hidden subject or chooser before One formation.

## 7. Facing separation explains earlier conceptual circling

The author explicitly accepted the diagnosis that earlier research often mixed:

- a retained geometry or Gate structure;
- the current determinate object / state;
- the still-unexhausted generative side of the same process.

Typical drift pattern:

~~~text
discover a real L2 structure
-> use it to explain future direction
-> accidentally promote it into the L0 source
-> ask what generates that L2 structure
-> add a deeper objectified mechanism
-> repeat.
~~~

The author accepts the new discipline:

> **for any important concept, ask what it means under L0-facing, L1-facing and L2-facing views before promoting it to a new ontology node.**

This is intended to reduce concept proliferation and prevent formed structures from being back-read as primitive sources.

## 9. Non-objectifying research method

The author explicitly stated:

> **「我觉得这种从L2 Facing到L1 Facing，最后推出L0 Facing的方法是SRT想去做的一个事情。因为直接研究L0 Facing很难。我最初的直觉里有一块就是怎么样建立研究L0 Facing的方法。当然首先要揭露L0的存在，建立非对象化的研究方法。」**

The author strongly accepted the subsequent analysis that “non-objectifying research” cannot mean zero objectification.

Accepted methodological direction:

~~~text
research necessarily objectifies;
the error is not objectification itself;
the error is treating one objectification as final ontology
and destroying the route back to its generative conditions.
~~~

Therefore:

> **A non-objectifying SRT method should keep each cut explicit, revisable, perturbable, comparable and reconstructible.**

The method should study L0-facing indirectly through the formation, failure, recutting and sedimentation of L1/L2 structures.

## 10. Reverse reconstruction and forward test

The author accepted the methodological two-direction structure:

### Discovery direction

~~~text
L2-facing reconstruction
-> L1-facing reconstruction
-> infer the L0-facing burden
~~~

### Test direction

~~~text
candidate L0-facing account
-> predict discriminable L1 formation / recut
-> predict discriminable L2 sediment / later constraint change
-> compare with strong object-based / latent-state / dynamical baselines
~~~

This two-way discipline is important because reverse reconstruction alone can always invent a post-hoc L0 story.

## 11. Generative incompleteness as the empirical foothold

The author explicitly highlighted the machine phrase **“生成现象解释的不完备性”** as especially important.

Accepted direction:

~~~text
model failure alone
-/-> L0 established.

A useful L0-facing empirical foothold requires:
structured
+ repeatable
+ perturbation-sensitive
+ baseline-resistant
generative residuals.
~~~

Examples of the relevant burden may include:

- new distinctions forming that are not predicted by the declared object/state vocabulary;
- existing dimensions or equivalence classes being recut;
- similarity / neighborhood structure being reorganized;
- transition-space formation changing, rather than only movement inside a fixed transition-space;
- future objectification changing in a way not exhausted by existing L1/L2 model variables.

The author has not yet asserted a direct empirical proof of primitive Selection from these effects.

## 14. Branch / merge and identity — accepted new framing

The author accepted the move away from asking only:

~~~text

~~~

toward separating:

~~~text
L2-facing:
is retained historical efficacy shared / diverged / merged?

L1-facing:
is the current determinate object / subject still objectified as one or many?

L0-facing:
does genuine Selection now occur from one or multiple
relatively separable formed generative from-wheres?
~~~

A stronger One-merger candidate therefore requires more than shared memory, common constraints or one foreground object.

Candidate:

> **Post-#1069 guard:** exact higher-order One merger remains OPEN. After lower-level One candidates are independently admitted by the canonical route, the pass may test whether a new recurrent higher-order organization emerges; “a new from-where exists” must not be used as the independent admission criterion.

Nested Ones remain possible; lower-order continuities need not disappear when a higher-order Position forms.
