# Author adjudication — cross-position generativity, non-usurpation, and constitutive ought

The author accepts the previously unresolved G1-G10 and N1-N10 packages, with the exact strength limits prepared in the two author-gate files.

This record is **noncanonical**. It fixes research direction and guards only. It does not authorize immediate canonical edits.

## A. Cross-position / whole-position package

The author accepts:

```text
G1  no automatic global standpoint;
G2  no primitive position privilege from identity, scale, persistence, majority, age or power;
G3  a whole-position must be ontologically formed rather than observer-declared;
G4  whole-relative better/worse may be real but does not automatically override member-relative better/worse;
G5  relational generativity is not additive;
G6  generative substitution / objectification is a real structural failure-mode candidate;
G7  cross-position comparison begins as a partial order rather than a universal scalar;
G8  preserve-every-position morality is rejected;
G9  actualisation closure and pathological generative closure must be distinguished;
G10 generative non-usurpation is retained as a strong research direction, not yet a universal moral theorem.
```

Consequences:

- local generative strengthening does not automatically count as wider-scope good;
- larger scale does not automatically carry normative sovereignty;
- formation, transformation, merger, dissolution, irreversible commitment and terminal Selection remain possible without being classified as wrong merely because possibilities close;
- a genuine whole-relative evaluative claim requires a genuine higher-order formed relation / position, not a label such as state, society, majority, institution or ecosystem by itself.

## B. Non-usurpation / constitutive-ought package

The author accepts:

```text
N1  non-usurpation is relation-relative before any moral-universal claim;
N2  participant-status consistency is the strongest current structural core;
N3  O2-C constitutive ought / relation-integrity norm is accepted as reconstruction direction;
N4  ought first constrains generative rewriting, not retrieval of one correct pregiven option;
N5  scoped generative dominance is accepted only as a limited partial-order rule;
N6  delegation / formation / emergency asymmetry are not usurpation automatically;
N7  generative substitution is not identical with complete moral wrongness;
N8  generative objectification / anti-objectification is accepted as a strong research direction;
N9  the Hume project may claim strengthened partial constitutive dissolution, not complete deontic closure;
N10 genuine tradeoff resolution remains a separate OPEN research line.
```

## C. Participant-status consistency

Current accepted research direction:

```text
If a relation R independently counts Y as a load-bearing generative participant,
R cannot coherently preserve that participant-status description while
Y's own Selection ceases to materially enter R and another position X
recurrently generates Y's future relation-state in Y's place.
```

If the latter condition becomes true, the relation must be retyped rather than preserving a false participation label.

This is a structural anti-objectification / anti-substitution burden. It is not yet a complete moral theorem.

## D. O2 split now accepted for reconstruction

Use the following distinction going forward:

```text
O2-C = constitutive ought / relation-integrity norm
       What must a relation do or not do if it is to remain the kind of generative relation it claims to be?

O2-A = attributable answerability / responsibility
       Which formed agent-position can be held answerable for relation-transforming Selection,
       once stronger agency / knowledge / control / reselectability conditions are paid?

O2-M = all-things-considered moral / deontic adjudication
       What should finally be done when real positions, relations, scales and horizons conflict?
```

Status:

```text
O2-C: ACCEPT AS RECONSTRUCTION DIRECTION / STRONG CANDIDATE
O2-A: ACCEPT AS DIRECTION / STRONGER AGENCY GATE REQUIRED
O2-M: OPEN / HOLD
```

Do not collapse O2-C into O2-M.

## E. Limited actionable preference

The author accepts a limited partial-order rule only:

```text
Where two feasible transitions satisfy the same declared task,
and transition A is no worse than B for every admitted constitutive position / relation
within the declared scope,
and A strictly reduces unnecessary generative substitution for at least one,
and no independently established higher-order loss is introduced,
A may be preferred to B.
```

This does not provide weights where genuine tradeoffs remain.

No hidden utility function, universal scalar, option count or preservation count is authorized.

## F. Anti-objectification direction

The author accepts the stronger research direction that objectification can be generative rather than merely representational:

```text
a formed position is generatively objectified when a relation continues to count it as a participant
while its effective future source-role is recurrently displaced to another position.
```

Exact thresholds, moral standing, Bearer / Concern dependence, domain translations and empirical tests remain separately gated.

## G. Hume project status

The accepted current claim strength is:

```text
primitive O0 directional generativity: not a completed moral order;
O1 position-relative better/worse: strong constitutive candidate;
O2-C relation-constitutive ought: strong constitutive candidate;
O2-A responsibility: downstream agency-gated candidate;
O2-M complete moral/deontic closure: OPEN;
Hume dissolution: PARTIAL / CONSTITUTIVE / STRENGTHENED, NOT COMPLETE.
```

SRT therefore does not claim to derive complete morality from neutral facts. The research direction is that some evaluative and normative dimensions may become constitutive once the relevant generative relation is correctly typed, while genuine cross-position conflict still requires further work.

