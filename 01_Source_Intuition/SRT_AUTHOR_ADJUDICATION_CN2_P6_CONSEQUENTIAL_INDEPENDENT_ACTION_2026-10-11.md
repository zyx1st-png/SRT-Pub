# Author adjudication — CN-2 P6 requires independently chosen consequential action (2026-10-11)

## 1. Recoverable immediate machine question and exact author response

This is a new, **direct author scope decision** in the continuing first family A P1–P6 paper observability audit (draft PR #1112). The question was raised by Claude's review of the earlier M-level overrestriction of P6. After the machine acknowledged that the original experimental-design owner says `action with no direct training history` without explicitly excluding category-label actions, it asked:

> 对于一个从未直接训练过的新特征组合，做出类别标签判断，算不算你所说的P6“行动迁移”？
>
> 还是说，你希望P6必须涉及有实际后果的独立行动选择，而不只是分类判断？

The author's exact response:

> 必须涉及有实际后果的独立行动选择，继续

**Author-selected reading (A0-Q/A0-S anchored to the exact question):** for CN-2 first-family A **P6**, a participant must make an **independent action choice on a novel feature/relation combination without direct training of that combination**, and that action must **have genuine outcome consequences** in the task. **Mere selection of a category label or classification/generalization of a novel blend is insufficient** even where a laboratory task asks the participant to press a key or rewards correct labels. The material distinction is whether the trial creates an independently available **action-to-consequence** decision, not whether any bodily movement/button press happened.

The trailing `继续` authorizes **bounded continuation of the existing paper-only P6/E2 observability analysis and source routing**, not a frozen stimulus/action design, data access, computational reproduction, new research lane, or experimental execution. This author ruling **supersedes only** the former M-audit's P6 `AUTHOR-OPEN` *meaning question*. Other open scientific and ontological questions stay open.

## 2. Conservative paper operationalization (machine translation, not another author claim)

To test practical data suitability later, proposed **M-level necessary observation conditions** are:

1. **Novel combination/history:** the target combination of features/relations has no direct item-action outcome training history; participating action *types* may be learned earlier, but the relevant **combination-to-action consequence** cannot be memorized directly.
2. **Independent choice:** the participant selects between genuinely available action policies/options given a goal or continuing task, rather than being asked only to name the item's learned class. **A reward for pressing the correct category-label key alone is not sufficient.**
3. **Actual causal consequence under the task's operational conditions:** the chosen action changes, or is capable of changing under the defined contingency, some observable task outcome (e.g., whether a tool can open a new device, whether an agent can traverse a route, whether a material resource is preserved). **A score attached to label correctness by itself is not the action–affordance question.** Consequence significance can be task-level, not necessarily money, pain, moral stake or consciousness.
4. **Observed action and outcome:** log the decision and the contingent result (or predeclared counterfactual outcome mapping when technically required), rather than treating model reward, internal Q-values, category responses, or anticipated success as the human/agent action record.
5. **Training/test independence and fairness:** the action selection is prospectively evaluated on pre-reserved novel combinations; named capable H1/H2 competitors receive the same legal training observations, action space, outcome feedback and test context. The probe mapping and probability scoring must be calibrated without seeing the reserved target outcome.

These are **draft measurement sufficiency checks** reflecting the author's answer, not a preregistered task or universal SRT definition of agency. Even satisfying them would demonstrate **behavioral transfer within the specified task**, not automatically E2 shared geometry, E3 causal field leverage, E4 recursive whole-field formation, primitive Selection, Bearer/Concern O3, or a unique SRT mechanism.
