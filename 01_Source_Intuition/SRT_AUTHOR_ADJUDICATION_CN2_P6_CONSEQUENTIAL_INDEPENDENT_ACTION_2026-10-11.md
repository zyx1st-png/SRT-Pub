# Author adjudication — CN-2 P6 requires independently chosen consequential action (2026-10-11)

## 1. Recoverable immediate machine question and exact author response

This is a new, **direct author scope decision** in the continuing first family A P1–P6 paper observability audit (draft PR #1112), **in the ChatGPT conversation on 2026-10-11 (Asia/Shanghai, UTC+08:00)**. The immediately preceding machine question and directly following author response are preserved here from that dialogue; they were **not themselves submitted as a GitHub PR comment**. The question was raised by Claude's review of the earlier M-level overrestriction of P6. After the machine acknowledged that the original experimental-design owner says `action with no direct training history` without explicitly excluding category-label actions, it asked:

> 对于一个从未直接训练过的新特征组合，做出类别标签判断，算不算你所说的P6“行动迁移”？

> 还是说，你希望P6必须涉及有实际后果的独立行动选择，而不只是分类判断？

The author's exact response:

> 必须涉及有实际后果的独立行动选择，继续

**Author-selected meaning (A0-S, strictly the two alternatives posed):** for CN-2 family A **P6**, a mere category-label classification response **does not suffice**; the participant's **independent action choice must involve actual consequences**. The `novel feature/relation combination without direct training` requirement is **inherited from the pre-existing P6 owner §4**, not newly uttered by the author in the short answer.

**M-level interpretation, NOT an additional author quote/decision:** a correctly pressed classification button does not qualify **merely because it is motor behavior**, and a correctness-point/reward display does not automatically transform pure classification into independent consequential action. **Whether points/resource credits themselves can be genuine task consequences for an independent, goal-relevant action is NOT author-adjudicated**; such an option must be evaluated against the two conditions the author actually chose. No new prohibition on all numerical scores, virtual outcomes, or any particular kind of consequence follows.

The trailing `继续` authorizes **bounded continuation of the existing paper-only P6/E2 observability analysis and source routing**, not a frozen stimulus/action design, data access, computational reproduction, new research lane, or experimental execution. This author ruling **supersedes only** the former M-audit's P6 `AUTHOR-OPEN` *meaning question*. Other open scientific and ontological questions stay open.

## 2. Conservative paper operationalization (machine translation, not another author claim)

To test practical data suitability later, proposed **M-level necessary observation conditions** are:

1. **Novel combination/history:** the target combination of features/relations has no direct item-action outcome training history; participating action *types* may be learned earlier, but the relevant **combination-to-action consequence** cannot be memorized directly.
2. **Independent choice (M operational check):** the participant selects between genuinely available task action policies/options given a goal, rather than being asked only to name the learned class. In the **pure classification-only case**, adding a correctness score does not by itself establish a distinct independent choice. **Do not infer that all point-based consequences are excluded by the author**.
3. **Task consequence (M operational check):** the independent action must have an actual, task-relevant contingent consequence; examples of *possible* outcome measures include altered usable route/resource state **or a task-relevant score/credit balance with genuine subsequent decision consequences**. Requiring literal change to an external physical device is **NOT an author ruling**. Pure class-correctness scoring without an independent action remains outside the selected target; the exact admissibility of a genuinely contingent point/credit task is **a future M feasibility issue**, not a settled author veto. No money, pain, moral stake, consciousness, or specific external-state threshold is required by the quote.
4. **Behavioral E2 target versus consequence validation (M):** the **held-out P6 human response to predict/score is the independently chosen action** (or a separately elicited *human-reported* anticipated consequence if deliberately defined as an additional response). Record the **realized consequence only to verify** that the action had actual effects and to audit feedback/timing; **do not demand predictive gain on realized outcome, deterministic outcome maps, or exogenous environmental noise** as an extra P6 behavioral E2 response. No model reward/Q-value counts as a participant choice. An expected but never operationally instantiated consequence is insufficient validity evidence.
5. **First-encounter and feedback firewall (M):** pre-reserve novel combinations before model tuning; **score only the participant's first choice on each exact reserved combination** as unseen-combination P6. Until that action is recorded, no model or participant may have seen the **same combination's consequence feedback**. Time-stamp action, consequence, *feedback delivered*, and P3/P5 measurements; declare whether P3/P5 occur before this P6 action/feedback or afterward. If any later P3/P5 probe uses the same combination following feedback, it is **post-feedback** and cannot be scored as a naïve held-out response without a separately labeled forward-learning estimand and time split. Competitors get symmetric lawful prior history, action space, allowed context and feedback cutoffs; tuning or conditioning on the held-out combination's eventual feedback is leakage.

These are **draft measurement sufficiency checks** reflecting the author's answer, not a preregistered task or universal SRT definition of agency. Even satisfying them would demonstrate **behavioral transfer within the specified task**, not automatically E2 shared geometry, E3 causal field leverage, E4 recursive whole-field formation, primitive Selection, Bearer/Concern O3, or a unique SRT mechanism.
