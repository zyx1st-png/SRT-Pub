## 1. Vertical / horizontal research

### A0 direct author source

The author stated, in substance:

> 传统水平研究是把对象化后的静态、客观实体作为第一性存在，对相关对象之间的转化进行研究来获得规律和生成性的预测。垂直研究是把生成性作为主要研究，发现对象背后生成的规律，从而跨多对象研究生成的规律，并统一本体论和认知论，来获得超越原水平对象在对象之间的更底层的演化规律。

The author then added:

> 横向研究忽略的对象产生/生成的机制，而纵向研究把这个隐藏的房间中的大象作为连续的动力学进行研究，从而获得对象之间被忽略的东西。

Author status:

~~~text
ACCEPT_WITH_AMENDMENT
~~~

### A1 aligned form

~~~text
horizontal research
= studies transformation / law / prediction inside a sufficiently stabilized
  objectification.

vertical research
= reopens the formation, maintenance, dissolution and reconstruction
  of objects, boundaries, state spaces, loci and generative conditions
  as continuous generative dynamics.
~~~

The defining distinction is not:

~~~text
static vs dynamic
micro vs macro
observable vs hidden
weak vs strong causality
~~~

It is:

~~~text
what is provisionally treated as given,
and what generative formation is reopened as the research target.
~~~

A vertical claim must identify what objectified condition is formed, maintained or reconstructed and how this changes later generation.

Ordinary state update, feedback, history dependence or path dependence does not by itself pay the vertical burden.

## 2. The objectification warning

A core author direction is:

~~~text
object
!= first ontological unit
~~~

Objectification is useful and necessary for finite inquiry, but it cuts continuous generation into relatively stable research units.

Vertical inquiry asks what those cuts preserve, what they suppress, and which generative dynamics continue across them.

Therefore:

~~~text
object
= research-useful / world-supported stabilization where paid
!= ultimate generative primitive
~~~

## 5. Process-first core

Author status:

~~~text
AUTHOR_EXTENDED
~~~

The process-first scaffold is:

~~~text
... -> C_t -> G_t -> C_(t+1) -> G_(t+1) -> ...
~~~

where:

~~~text
C_t
= current generative conditions at declared grain

G_t
= actual generative differentiation / transformation
~~~

GRG studies how:

- prior formation remains effective in current C_t;
- current generation forms, dissolves or reorganizes local structures;
- current generation reconstructs C_(t+1);
- local generative loci and operative positions arise;
- multiple loci and scales compose;
- prediction and expectation follow from learned generative constraints.

Relations are cuts through this process.

## 8. Generative Prediction and Generative Expectation

### A0 direct author source

The author stated:

> SRT 和 GRG 应该关注是是生成产生的对现实的预测和更符合秩序的预期。

Author status:

~~~text
AUTHOR_EXTENDED
~~~

### A1 aligned dual-output architecture

GRG should mature toward two principal outputs.

~~~text
P_G — Generative Prediction
What can / is likely / is constrained to form, transform, become reachable,
lose stability, or reconstruct the object / relation / state-space field?

E_G — Generative Expectation
What does a continuation / transformation mean relative to an already formed
generative order: preserve, strengthen, extend, reorganize, substitute,
hollow or destroy?
~~~

Prediction and expectation are not identical.

~~~text
prediction
= constrains how reality may continue to generate

expectation
= constrains the structural significance of that continuation
  relative to an admitted generative order
~~~

E_G is NOT merely statistical forecast.

A real trajectory that destroys a relation does not mean E_G "predicted incorrectly".

What can fail is our relation / transformation hypothesis, boundary, bridge or grammar typing.
