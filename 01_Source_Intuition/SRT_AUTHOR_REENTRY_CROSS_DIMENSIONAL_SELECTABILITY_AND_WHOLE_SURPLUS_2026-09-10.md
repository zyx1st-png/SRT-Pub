The author now fixes the intended axis of stability as **cross-dimensional selectability**:

```text
more stable Selection
=

- time,
- space,
- position,
- subject.
```

This is stronger and more specific than any one of the earlier machine probes:


The current minimum reading is instead:

The record does **not** yet fix a metric, threshold, invariance group, topology, or formal equivalence relation for “across”.

