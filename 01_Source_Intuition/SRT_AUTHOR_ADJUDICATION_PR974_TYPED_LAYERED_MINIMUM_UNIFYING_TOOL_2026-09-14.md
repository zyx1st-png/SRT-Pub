# Author adjudication — typed layered minimum unifying tool

> **Boundary:** author-source record only. This file does not edit canonical owners, establish a new theorem, assign a claim level, establish scientific distinctiveness, or close Level 2 HOLD.

## 1. Decision

At the PR #974 architecture gate, the author accepts **Option A**.

Accepted direction:

> SRT's minimum ontology should remain thin, while the minimum unifying tool may be a **typed, layered, cross-stratum dependency architecture**. The architecture does not require every local mechanism to be historically unique and does not force L2 / social / normative concepts into primitive ontology.

Status:

```text
A — typed layered minimum-unifying-tool architecture: ACCEPT
B — synthesis/checklist only: NOT SELECTED
C — stronger independent accounting channel as present theorem/norm: NOT SELECTED AT THIS GATE
```

The author accepts the machine recommendation only at the **research-direction level**. Stronger C-like consequences remain subject to separate pressure and cannot be inferred automatically.

---

## 2. Accepted architecture shape

### Stratum I — thin universal ontology

```text
non-flat openness
-> finite-position-indexed subjectless Selection
   = actual differentiation
   = manifestation / Selection-relative background
-> regenerative vertical formation
-> One.
```

No new primitive is added here by this adjudication.

### Stratum II — formed-process axes

Only when the relevant formed continuity/history exists, SRT may ask:

```text
how retained organization changes later Selection;
future-selectability transformation;
passive maintenance vs active reconstruction;
relative continuation / One strength;
generative reselectability.
```

These are not primitive Selection admission conditions.

### Stratum III — L2 / multi-position axes

Only when history, scaffold and multiple formed positions exist, SRT may ask:

```text
what has become background scaffold;
what is pre-cropped before explicit choice;
who authorizes / executes / benefits / bears / repairs;
whose future selectability changes;
where consequence and burden return;
whether affected positions can revise the generator / scaffold.
```

Language, institution, habit and comparable social-cultural structures remain L2 scaffold realizations, not L0 contents.

---

## 3. Accepted cross-stratum research constraints

The author accepts the following as **research constraints / audit directions**, not yet canonical theorems.

### CS-1 — scale-separated generative health

```text
higher-scale effective selectability increase
!=
automatic cross-scale generative-health increase.
```

Lower-scale selectability loss can be healthy specialization or individuality transition; it is not automatically pathological.

### CS-2 — standing-sensitive incidence

Where a lower-scale unit has already acquired independently relevant formed standing, especially Bearer-relevant standing under the current P+E reconstruction, aggregation must not silently erase where non-outsourcable exposure and consequence land.

This does **not** establish rights, personhood, subjecthood, consciousness or moral priority.

### CS-3 — option-count / real-selectability separation

```text
visible option count increase
!=
endogenous generative-selectability increase.
```

L2 scaffolds may increase convenience / immediate performance while reducing endogenous candidate generation, revision capacity, exit, or consequence return.

### CS-4 — stability / externalization separation

```text
higher-level stability
!=
healthy consequence integration.
```

A stable organization may be partly subsidized by burden externalization to other formed positions. This is a structural diagnostic candidate, not a moral verdict by itself.

## 5. Interpretation of strongest-neighbor pressure

Barad, Simondon, Whitehead, Deleuze, Deacon, Active Inference, niche construction, evolutionary transitions in individuality, multiscale competency, capability theory, distributed cognition and institutional / externality theories may each pay substantial local burdens.

That is not treated as a failure of SRT.

The author-approved research target is now:

```text
Can SRT's typed dependency architecture
constrain how those local mechanisms can be connected across strata,
rule out invalid back-projections,
produce nontrivial cross-stratum classifications,
and generate research questions / model constraints
that disappear when the SRT architecture is deleted?
```

Local novelty remains a separate and currently unestablished question.
