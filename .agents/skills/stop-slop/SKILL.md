# Stop Slop

> Repository-local copy for SRT-Pub. Upstream: [hardikpandya/stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop), MIT License.

Follow the user's requested review/edit scope and existing authorization. A review reports findings without writing files. Style defaults do not override user requirements or grant publication permission.

## Editorial checks

- Remove empty openers, repeated announcements and emphasis that adds no meaning.
- Simplify formulaic reversals and dramatic fragments only when they do not carry an actual distinction or intended voice.
- Prefer concrete, supported actors and actions. Technical systems and impersonal processes can be valid subjects; do not invent a human actor.
- Preserve facts, numbers, attribution, quotes, links, code, terms, uncertainty, negation and logical scope. Keep “most,” “may” or “not” when changing them changes the claim.
- Vary rhythm where repetition distracts. Genuine three-part classifications, passive voice, questions and em dashes are allowed.
- Keep the author's voice and register. Do not force “you,” intimacy, emotional detail or a punchy ending into professional text.

Read [phrases.md](references/phrases.md) for filler, [structures.md](references/structures.md) for structural issues, and [examples.md](references/examples.md) when calibration is useful. These are contextual examples, not automatic deletion rules.

Compare the edit with the original once for fidelity. Address clear remaining issues within scope, then deliver; no numerical score is a release gate. For SRT prose, preserve claim and publication boundaries from root AGENTS.md. In the three-stage pipeline this is a final audit, not another structural rewrite.
