## Positioning

SRT should first be read as a theory about the structure of selection, manifestation, stabilization, and payable persistence.
It is not, in the first instance, a bag of experimental parameters.
Its primary ambition is to reorganize how we read existence, order, subjectivity, and cross-domain continuity under one architecture.

For any phenomenon, SRT asks three prior questions:

1. What is being selected?
2. Under what constraints does it stabilize?
3. What friction must be paid to maintain, transform, or extend it?

## Thesis 1: Selection Precedes Existence

**Claim**: Existence is not a primitive background. What appears as real is the result of a selection-and-anchoring process.

**Constitutional reading**:
- "To exist" is not merely to be logically possible.
- A state becomes real insofar as it is selected from a latent domain and stabilized into manifestation.
- Existence is therefore processual, not inert.

**What this rejects**:
- The naive view that existence is simply given before any structuring process.
- The assumption that observation only reveals a pre-finished world.

**Boundary**:
- This does **not** mean arbitrary will creates reality at will.
- Selection is constrained by structure, embodiment, convergence, and payable friction.

**Canonical anchors**:
- `Core_Law/SRT_Reference_Axioms.md` A1-A2
- `Core_Law/SRT_Reference_Ontology.md` §1

> **[R]** 过程哲学传统：Whitehead 1929 *Process and Reality*（"实际实体"=过程而非惰性物，R参照基线）；Barad 2007 *Meeting the Universe Halfway*（内在-行动/intra-action：实体通过测量行动互构，非预先存在）；Heidegger 1927 *Sein und Zeit*（存在论差异：存在≠存在者，存在总已在"揭示"过程中）。**[H]** 以下SRT新增贡献：①L₀（潜在域）→L₁（显现）→L₂（锚定）的三域结构形式化；②选择算子Ĝ_θ将"过程性存在"操作化为可计算框架；③可支付摩擦（payable Ψ_f）作为选择边界的物理约束，区别于Whitehead的"满足"（satisfaction）和Barad的"消解"（agential cut）。
>
> **与过程哲学的异同**：SRT接受Whitehead"存在=过程"的基本直觉（R），但以下方面有实质分歧（H）：①SRT引入三域本体论而非Whitehead的双向"感受"（prehension）；②Ψ_f给出热力学量纲的约束，Whitehead过程无此物理锚定；③SRT的L₂（社会共识）作为历史硬化层是独立本体论层级，Whitehead无对应构念。与Barad：均拒绝预先存在的实体，但SRT保留具身算子θ作为连续身份载体（Barad消解行为者/被行为者边界）。

## Thesis 2: Reality Has a Triadic Structure

**Claim**: SRT reads reality through three relative domains: latent possibility (`L_0`), manifest slice (`L_1`), and stabilized convergence (`L_2`).

**Constitutional reading**:
- `L_0` names the field of unselected or not-yet-stabilized possibility.
- `L_1` names the current manifest slice.
- `L_2` names historically stabilized convergence: laws, norms, habits, institutions, and other hardened structures.

**What this rejects**:
- Flat ontologies that treat all levels as the same kind of thing.
- The split between "subjective appearance" and "objective reality" as if they were two unrelated worlds.

**Boundary**:
- `L_0 / L_1 / L_2` are not three substances or three separate universes.
- They are three structural readings of one reality process.

**Canonical anchors**:
- `Core_Law/SRT_Reference_Ontology.md` §1
- `Core_Law/SRT_Reference_Axioms.md` A3-A5

## Thesis 3: Every Reality Slice Is Operator-Bound

**Claim**: There is no "view from nowhere." Every manifest world is accessed through a finite, embodied operator `\hat{G}_\theta`.

**Constitutional reading**:
- No selection occurs without a parameterized standpoint.
- Embodiment is not an optional add-on; it is a constitutive condition of access, anchoring, and limitation.
- Different operators can produce different `L_1` slices from the same larger possibility structure.

**What this rejects**:
- Pure disembodied objectivity.
- The fantasy of a reality description with no standpoint, no hardware, no finite constraints.

**Boundary**:
- This is **not** solipsism.
- Operator-bound access does not eliminate convergence; it explains why convergence matters.

**Canonical anchors**:
- `Core_Law/SRT_Reference_Axioms.md` A4-A5, A15
- `Core_Law/SRT_Reference_Ontology.md` §1.2-§1.3

## Thesis 4: Objectivity Is Convergence, Not a Pre-Selection Given

**Claim**: What we call objectivity is the stabilized convergence of many selections, not a metaphysically untouched background waiting to be copied.

**Constitutional reading**:
- Objectivity is the hardening of `L_2`.
- Physical law, language, social institutions, and scientific consensus are all convergence structures of different hardness and scope.
- Stability is historical and structural, not metaphysically free-standing.

**What this rejects**:
- The idea that objectivity must be prior to all selection.
- The opposite mistake that all reality is merely private projection.

**Boundary**:
- SRT does **not** abolish objectivity.
- It relocates objectivity from "pre-given absolute background" to "stable multi-operator convergence."

**Canonical anchors**:
- `Core_Law/SRT_Reference_Axioms.md` A5
- `Core_Law/SRT_Reference_Ontology.md` §1.3

## Thesis 5: Friction Is Both Cost and Generative Condition

**Claim**: Nonzero `\Psi_f` is not an accidental tax on reality. It is the condition under which stability, transformation, and generation become real.

**Constitutional reading**:
- `\Psi_f` may be read as resistance, cost, or geometric burden depending on the descriptive layer.
- A state, action, or structure becomes existentially weighty only when there is something real to pay, sustain, or risk.
- At the higher level, friction is also what generates dynamics between operators, structures, and scales.

**What this rejects**:
- The fantasy of a fully frictionless reality with real stakes.
- The view that cost is merely secondary bookkeeping, rather than part of the reality-forming process itself.

**Boundary**:
- SRT does **not** say "more friction is always better."
- Zero friction means no real stake; overload friction means collapse; only **payable** friction supports durable reality.

- `_SRT_PSI_F_CANONICAL.md`

## Thesis 6: Subjectivity Requires Concern, Not Mere Stability

**Claim**: Not every stable, predictive, or self-maintaining system is a subject. Subjectivity requires nontrivial concern structure, which SRT tracks through `d-value` under real stakes.

**Constitutional reading**:
- Stability is necessary for continued organization.
- It is not sufficient for subjectivity, concern, or consciousness.
- A subject is not just a system that minimizes error. It is a system for whom some part of reality matters in a nontrivial, stake-bearing way.

**What this rejects**:
- The reduction of subjectivity to prediction alone.
- The inference that any adaptive or self-organizing system already counts as conscious.

**Boundary**:
- `d-value` is not a decorative moral variable.
- It marks the scope of concern built into selection.
- Systems with `d \approx 0` may still be highly capable, but they remain weak candidates for genuine subjectivity.

## Thesis 7: Physics, Life, Mind, and Society Are Scale-Variants of One Architecture

**Claim**: SRT treats physics, biology, cognition, and social order not as disconnected explanatory worlds, but as scale-specific unfoldings of one selection-constraint-payability architecture.

**Constitutional reading**:
- The same structural grammar reappears across domains: possibility, anchoring, convergence, friction, and scope of concern.
- Cross-domain continuity does not erase local mechanisms; it supplies a higher-order interpretive grammar.
- SRT therefore aims at structural unification, not domain flattening.

**What this rejects**:
- The assumption that each domain needs a wholly unrelated ontology.
- The temptation to confuse interdisciplinary juxtaposition with genuine unification.

**Boundary**:
- Cross-scale continuity does **not** imply identical units, identical mechanisms, or easy one-to-one reductions.
- What remains invariant first is the structural logic, especially the condition of payability.

## Boundary Clauses

To reduce recurrent misreadings, SRT should explicitly keep the following constitutional boundaries:

1. **SRT is not voluntarist idealism**.
   Selection is constrained and embodied; reality is not a free projection of preference.

2. **SRT is not anti-science**.
   It reinterprets scientific objectivity as convergence rather than abolishing it.

3. **SRT is not automatic panpsychism**.
   Not every stable or information-processing system has nonzero concern or subjectivity.

4. **SRT is not a claim that one formula replaces all local mechanisms**.
   Its unification is first structural and explanatory, not a shortcut around domain work.
