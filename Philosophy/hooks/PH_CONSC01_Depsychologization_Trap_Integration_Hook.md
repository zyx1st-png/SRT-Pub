## Suggested compressed paragraph

Do not form a consciousness claim by stripping away psychological function, consequence return, access, memory, action coupling, and ethical stake until only "pure feel" remains. Once consciousness is detached from those roles, it is unusable for SRT subjecthood: it cannot be cleanly measured, cannot ground responsibility or moral status, and tends to slide toward panpsychist universalism. SRT treats first-person feel as a candidate content or field dimension only when routed through bearer-unit specification, `d`, `Psi_f` payability, boundary maintenance, L2 memory, and cross-time continuity.
