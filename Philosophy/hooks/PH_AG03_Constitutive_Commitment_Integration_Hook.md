# PH-AG03 Constitutive Commitment Integration Hook

## 1. Target: agency / subjecthood synthesis

**Target:** `Philosophy/SRT_Philosophy_Agency_Subjecthood_v0_2.md`

**Insert after:** controlled selection and PH-AG02 knowledge-biased reasoning.

**Suggested paragraph:**

A cognitive trajectory may be structured by reasons without being guaranteed by them. In some time-sensitive and intersubjectively constrained situations, action must close a practical direction while epistemic risk remains. The act can then establish a public and historical position from which the continuing bearer becomes answerable. This is a bridge from controlled selection to responsibility-bearing subject-position formation, not a claim that every decisive act creates a subject.

**Suggested ladder extension:**

```text
causal transition
-> selective transition
-> controlled selection
-> reason-constrained commitment
-> bearer-bound consequence return
-> responsibility-bearing position
-> self-readable / revisable subject position
```

**Guardrail:** A subject position may be stabilized after an act even though the acting organism or social agent existed beforehand. Do not write this as creation ex nihilo of a subject.

## 2. Target: `T_dir`

**Target:** `_SRT_T_DIR_CANONICAL.md`

**Insert near:** §3.1 readability / reorientation functional.

**Suggested note:**

Constitutive commitment and direction transparency must be separated. A person or system can enter a highly binding, publicly attributable position while remaining unable to read the direction, source or value structure of that commitment. Therefore commitment strength, confidence and post-hoc narrative coherence are not `T_dir` evidence unless the direction can be self-read and used for reorientation.

Preserve:

```text
epistemic confidence
!=
enacted commitment
!=
retroactive narrative certainty
!=
T_dir readability / reorientation
```

**Do not include:**

- anticipated certainty as a numerical `T_dir` proxy;
- confidence or decisiveness as direction transparency;
- post-hoc coherence as proof of authentic ownership;
- Lacanian subjectivation as a canonical SRT threshold.

## 3. Target: ChoiceMap

**Target:** `_SRT_DIRECTION3_CHOICEMAP_PROTOTYPE_SEED.md`

**Insert near:** S4–S5 and the convergence-return rationale.

**Suggested note:**

Not every unresolved decision is merely an information deficit. ChoiceMap should separate reducible factual uncertainty from an irreducible guarantee gap, and it should expose the cost of delay. The system may identify what can still be researched, what risk cannot be removed by further information, who becomes answerable after action, and what reselection channels remain. It must not use “certainty is impossible” to justify premature closure, and it must not use residual uncertainty to encourage endless deferral.

**Suggested questions:**

- What evidence is still obtainable at reasonable cost?
- What uncertainty would remain even after obtaining it?
- What options degrade or disappear while waiting?
- Who becomes the bearer of the resulting commitment?
- Which consequences return to that bearer?
- Which appeal, revision or reselection routes remain?

## 4. Target: irreversibility / L2 language

When using retroaction, state explicitly:

```text
retroactive meaning / responsibility indexing
!=
physical retrocausation
!=
undoing an earlier selection
```

The act can reorganize how the past is narrated and institutionally classified, but it cannot erase consequences already returned.

## 5. Future synthesis boundary

The integration must preserve:

```text
absence of final guarantee
!=
absence of reasons

binding commitment
!=
truth

subject-position formation
!=
complete subjecthood

retroactive stabilization
!=
retrocausation
```
