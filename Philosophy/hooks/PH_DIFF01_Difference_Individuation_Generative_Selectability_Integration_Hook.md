```text

```

### Suggested placement

Near the pre-objectification / selection-before-stable-existence discussion, after the boundary against a complete pre-given object space.

### Suggested native paragraph

```text
Pre-object selectability should not be pictured as a hidden inventory of completed objects awaiting display. A theory that first specifies the finished identities, options and problem boundaries and then adds an act of selection has already imported the determinacy it is meant to explain. The stronger burden is genetic: specify how structured non-neutrality and constraints can yield a determinate manifestation without presupposing the manifest form as an unrealized copy.
```

```text
virtual = L0
Deleuze proves selection-first ontology
L0 is a Deleuzian multiplicity
```
