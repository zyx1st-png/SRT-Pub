# PH-AG04 Sensorimotor Time–Agency Integration Hook

## 1. Target: agency / subjecthood synthesis

**Target:** `Philosophy/SRT_Philosophy_Agency_Subjecthood_v0_2.md`

**Insert after:** agency ladder and before the conscious-agency threshold.

**Suggested paragraph:**

Sense of agency should not be collapsed into body ownership. Online action contains at least four partially independent dimensions: objective control efficacy, authorial attribution, bodily carrier ownership, and reflective self-integration. Sensorimotor congruence can support control over a third-person avatar or external effector while bodily ownership remains weak. Conscious agency therefore requires self-related authorial integration and counterfactual control, not necessarily ownership of the bodily carrier used in a particular task.

**Suggested table columns:**

`dimension | empirical question | structural measure | subjective report | stake gate | SRT boundary`

## 3. Target: neuroscience mechanisms

**Targets:**

- `Neuroscience/SRT_Neural_Mechanisms.md`
- `Neuroscience/SRT_Consciousness_Mechanisms.md`

**Insert near:** predictive processing / sensorimotor comparison sections, not inside canonical neural axioms.

**Suggested note:**

Four preregistered VR experiments provide a bounded implementation window for controlled transition-readiness. Delay, replay kinematics, and spatial remapping perturb the relation among motor command, predicted feedback, actual feedback, and correction. These perturbations robustly influence explicit agency and body ownership, while subjective duration effects are less stable across experiments. Treat the paradigm as a sensorimotor-control bridge, not as a direct measure of `G_hat_theta`, `Psi_f`, or consciousness.

**Candidate implementation vector:**

```text
motor prediction mismatch
+ correction latency
+ residual control error
+ recalibration rate
+ aftereffect / retention
```

Only the latter cost- and history-sensitive measures are candidates for an embodied-friction bridge.

## 4. Target: subjective-time bridge

**Target:** future dedicated document; do not place in physics-time ontology by default.

**Suggested claim:**

Voluntary action exerts a compression pressure on perceived duration. Congruent live feedback may partially compensate this compression relative to replayed or delayed feedback, but duration is also shaped by expectation, attention, perceptual change, and retrospective inference. Subjective time during action is therefore a multi-process `L1` manifestation, not a direct readout of agency, selection count, or ontological friction.

**Suggested schematic:**

```text
observed subjective duration
=
action-compression pressure
+ coupling compensation
+ expectation / attention / event-change contributions
```

**Do not include:**

- sensorimotor coupling creates time;
- duration judgment validates SRT physical-time ontology;
- longer perceived time means higher agency in every condition.

## 5. Target: shared agency boundary

**Insert near:** shared agency / social coupling discussion.

**Suggested note:**

A third-person avatar driven by the participant remains compatible with an external actuator interpretation. Shared agency requires a genuine other with independent policy, reciprocal counterfactual influence, role or norm structure, and durable coordination. Similar first- and third-person coupling effects support possible predictive overlap, not shared subjecthood.

**Experimental ladder:**

1. prerecorded avatar;
2. mechanically yoked live avatar;
3. adaptive artificial agent;
4. human partner with independent goals;
5. joint task requiring communication, role repair, and mutual commitment.
