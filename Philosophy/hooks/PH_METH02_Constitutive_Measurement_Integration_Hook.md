## 2. SEA insertion: meta-audit, not a sixth gate

Suggested future paragraph:

> A selection-event audit must distinguish a change in the target system from a change in its description. Re-encoding an event, changing labels, switching an equivalent parametrization, or adopting another coordinate description can alter the representation while leaving path efficacy, consequence, and historical write-back unchanged. The audit should therefore treat representation-invariance as a meta-level negative control on the evidence for PEF, CBP, and HEF, not as an additional compensable score or a sixth gate.

Suggested methods note:

```text
redescription control
+
boundary stability
+
cross-procedure convergence
+
explicit framework-failure condition
```

Do not use this to claim that DiSalle independently derives the SEA five-gate architecture.

## 3. Physics insertion: anti-overreach guard

Suggested future guard near measurement/POVM bridge language:

> A physical principle can help constitute the empirical meaning of a quantity without thereby creating the physical event or proving an ontological identity. Accordingly, the use of measurement procedures to identify a physical quantity must not be conflated with the stronger SRT claim that measurement is itself a realized selection event. Coordinate or frame dependence must likewise be separated from invariant physical structure before any claim of path selection is made.

Do not include:

- `DiSalle -> Measurement as Selection` as an endorsement chain;
- relativity as evidence for observer-created reality;
- invariants as direct realizations of `L_2`, `d`, `Psi_f`, or `G_hat_theta`;
- a claim that general relativity confirms selection-first ontology.

## 4. Book insertion: native-prose payloads

### Q02 — objectification is constrained, not arbitrary

Suggested future native paragraph:

> 切片并不意味着任意。真正能留下来的对象边界，往往要经得住换角度、换操作、换测量办法之后的反复恢复。我们当然参与了切割，但世界也一直在回推：有些切法一换手段就散掉，有些关系却在不同操作中一再回来。对象化因此既不是把一件预先完成的东西原样搬进头脑，也不是想怎么切就怎么切；它更像是在约束里把某些差异稳定成可以反复找到的形。

### Q03 — pre-object does not mean hidden completed objects

Suggested future native paragraph:

> 如果一种理论必须先把所有对象、变量和可允许变换都完整列好，才能开始谈生成，那么它其实已经把最困难的问题悄悄做完了。前对象并不是躲在幕后的另一套成品对象，而是提醒我们：连“什么算一个东西、什么算一次测量”这样的边界，也有自己的形成史。

### Q05 — selection before picking

Suggested future native paragraph:

> 有一种更早的“选择”甚至不是从菜单里挑一项，而是参与决定什么以后才有资格成为菜单上的一项。可这仍然不等于凭空创造：能不能被稳定成一个选项，要看它是否经得住反复操作、后果和现实约束。这样的构成，比日常挑选更早，却也比任意命名更硬。

### Q26/Q27 — theory failure and imprisonment

Suggested future bridge:

> 理论真正危险的时候，不是它曾经有效，而是它忘了自己为什么有效。一个局部框架一旦把自己的适用条件抹掉，就会把“在这里成立”悄悄改写成“世界本来如此”。真正的理论进步未必把旧理论扔掉；更常见的情形，是把它重新放回一个较大的视野里，说明它在哪些条件下仍然正确，又从哪里开始把局部地形误认成了整个世界。
