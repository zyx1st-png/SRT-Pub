# SOC-COG04 Selection-History Homology Integration Hook

## 1. Core payload

Retain:

```text
similar consequence-bearing history
-> partial selection-structure homology
-> lower cross-bearer translation error
-> greater mutual intelligibility
```

with:

```text
homology != identity
understanding != direct phenomenal access
shared reality != identical experience
```

---

## 2. Social Cognition owner target

Integrate only as a synthesis layer over existing mechanisms:

```text
SOC-COG02
child-body-caregiver-environment developmental coordination

SOC-COG03
norm / script / affordance gating and reselection

SOC-COG04
how partially shared developmental / consequence histories can make
otherwise distinct bearer positions structurally translatable
```

Suggested owner paragraph:

```text
Shared coordination does not imply identical internal states. Repeated exposure to common ecological, bodily and social constraints, together with partially similar consequence-bearing histories, may stabilize homologous salience, stake and affordance relations across individuals. Such homology can support mutual prediction and translation while remaining bearer-relative and revisable.
```

Do not add a new universal social operator.

---

## 3. Empathy / projection guard

Suggested concise rule:

```text
projection:
model_j := position_i

empathic reconstruction:
model_j := revisable estimate of
(history_j, stakes_j, reachable-space_j, event-to-writeback_j)
```

Quality should track prediction and revision, not subjective certainty.

Do not infer moral authority from experiential similarity.

---

## 4. Shared reality target

Use only the bounded claim:

```text
shared reality
= historically stabilized interoperability among partially aligned objectifications
```

under common constraint / correction practices.

Do not infer:

```text
identical L1 states
collective mind
social construction without external constraint
```

---

## 5. Language / communication target

A future social-cognition synthesis may describe communication as:

```text
iterative reduction of local selection-relation mismatch
through L2 signals, examples, correction and demonstration
```

Metaphor may be introduced as a cross-domain structure-mapping device, but only at bridge level.

---

## 6. Qualia interface target

Add only a comparability guard:

```text
private phenomenality != arbitrary phenomenal structure
```

Shared embodiment / training / history may predict partial correspondence in relational qualia geometry, but:

```text
cross-person Delta-structure similarity
!= numerical quale identity
```

PH-QUAL01 remains the local owner of `RDef_B(q)` and phenomenal geometry.

---

## 7. Hard Problem target

Retain the division:

```text
PH-CONSC05:
why For-me exists / phenomenal admission

SOC-COG04:
why one bearer's For-me can be partially understandable to another bearer
```

Mutual intelligibility must not be used to prove phenomenality.

---

## 8. Empirical landing requirements

Before any stronger integration, specify at least one predeclared operational family for:

```text
history similarity
stake similarity
reachable-space similarity
translation / prediction error
```

and demonstrate incremental prediction beyond:

```text
current stimulus
simple demographic similarity
generic exposure count
ordinary semantic knowledge
```

The strongest discriminating comparison is:

```text
shared exposure
vs
shared consequence-bearing writeback
```

If the latter adds no predictive value, downgrade SOC-COG04 toward ordinary learning/social similarity.

---

## 9. Final integration guard

Do not write:

```text
similar lives = same experience
shared culture = same mind
empathy = direct access
language transfers qualia
shared history proves consciousness
selection-history homology solves the other-minds problem absolutely
```

Safe conclusion:

> structural homology can explain partial mutual intelligibility without erasing bearer difference or closing phenomenal admission.
