## Suggested compressed paragraph

Cognitive development should be read as the stabilization and reconfiguration of soft-assembled coordinative structures across child, body, caregiver, environment, and history. In SRT terms, development changes `theta` by changing the child's affordance field, co-regulation loops, motor access, rhythmic scaffolds, `L_2` traces, and variability structure together. This is a bridge claim, not a new primitive: it supports longitudinal, variability-sensitive, child-environment analysis without denying brain constraints or all representation-level descriptions.
