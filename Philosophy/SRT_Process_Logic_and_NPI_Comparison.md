# SRT Process Logic and NPI Comparison

> **Claim-status note**: This file is a comparison / bridge / orientation document. It does **not** define `L_0/L_1/L_2`, `\hat G_\theta`, `d-value`, `\Psi_f`, consciousness, subjecthood, information, or process realism for SRT. It compares SRT with Zhensong Wang's 2026 proposal for consciousness research based on Naturalistic Philosophy of Information (NPI), Logic in Reality (LIR), and Unified Information Science (UIS).
>
> **Hardness rule**: Do not cite this file as P0/P1 proof. Use it as P2/P3/P5 comparative scaffolding unless a specific section is later promoted through canonical review.

## 0. Compressed thesis

NPI moves consciousness research from **Shannon information as communication measure** toward **information-as-process**.

SRT can accept that move, but should not stop there. SRT's distinctive move is:

> **From information-as-process to selection-as-anchoring.**

In short:

```text
NPI/LIR:
energy process -> information process -> self-feedback / self-regulation -> consciousness

SRT:
L_0 latent selectability -> \hat G_\theta anchoring -> L_1 manifestation -> L_2 sedimentation -> future selection constraints
```

The shared target is the same: escape substance-first ontology and object-first consciousness theory. The divergence is that NPI places **information process** at the explanatory floor, while SRT places **selection / anchoring / occlusion / write-back / hardening** at the explanatory floor.

## 1. What the NPI / LIR article argues

The article criticizes Chalmers' double-aspect theory of information. Chalmers tries to connect physical organization and phenomenal experience through bridging principles such as structural coherence and organizational invariance. The article grants that these principles are directionally useful, but argues that they remain too weak because they only state covariance between third-person and first-person domains without giving a sufficiently deep account of why such covariance should generate subjective experience.

The article's main repair is to replace a Shannon-centered concept of information with a naturalistic and process-realist concept of information. Shannon information is treated as too narrow because it measures communication uncertainty but does not fully address information's qualitative, emergent, relational, meaningful, or experiential dimensions.

The article then introduces LIR, based on Lupasco and Brenner, as a logic for real processes rather than propositions. Its central principle is dynamic opposition: processes oscillate between actuality and potentiality, affirmation and negation, realization and counter-realization, without ever reaching absolute 0 or 100 percent. In this view, contradiction is not merely a semantic defect but a real dynamic tension within evolving processes.

The consciousness proposal can be summarized as follows:

1. Reality should be read as a **network of basic processes**, not a network of basic entities.
2. Information is a special form of natural process, grounded in energy dynamics.
3. Consciousness emerges through complex informosomes: information systems capable of self-feedback, self-regulation, and meaningful processual organization.
4. The view avoids Chalmers-style panpsychism by using **protopsychism**: proto-mental process conditions may be widespread, but full consciousness requires higher-order organization.
5. UIS is proposed as the future transdisciplinary science that can integrate physical, biological, cognitive, and first-person data.

## 3. Deep convergence

### 3.1 Anti-substance orientation

Both frameworks reject the idea that reality is best explained as a collection of already-given entities. The article frames the shift as a move from a world of basic entities to a world of basic processes. SRT's stronger formulation is selection-first: stable objects are not the starting floor but the hardened outcome of constrained selection.

### 3.2 Process before object

NPI says that information should be understood as a real natural process rather than a static signal token. SRT says that objects, meanings, selves, and institutions become stable only after repeated selections are anchored and sedimented.

The convergence is strong enough to justify describing SRT externally as a **selection-theoretic process realism**.

### 3.3 Consciousness as stabilization rather than mere signal processing

The article treats consciousness as self-feedback and self-regulation within complex information processes. SRT can translate this into its own terms: consciousness is not any information processing, but concern-weighted stable `L_1` anchoring with enough integration, memory closure, boundary maintenance, counterfactual access, and cross-time reidentification to support subject-like continuity.

### 3.4 Anti-panpsychist pressure

Both frameworks try to avoid crude panpsychism. NPI uses protopsychism. SRT should maintain an even stricter distinction:

```text
micro-selection ≠ consciousness
high salience ≠ subjecthood
local access ≠ selfhood
proto-G ≠ full \hat G_\theta subject closure
```

## 4. Essential differences

### 4.1 Explanatory floor

NPI's floor is:

```text
energy -> information -> process -> consciousness
```

SRT's floor is:

```text
selectability -> selection / anchoring -> manifestation -> sedimentation -> future constraint
```

This difference matters. If SRT adopts NPI too directly, it risks becoming another information ontology. SRT's distinct contribution is not that everything is information, but that what becomes real is what has been selectively anchored against alternative possibilities and future entropy.

### 4.2 Logic

LIR is a logic of real dynamic opposition. SRT should not simply adopt contradiction as its master term. SRT's better process vocabulary is:

```text
selection / occlusion
anchoring / drift
friction / payability
manifestation / shadowing
write-back / reopening
hardening / re-selection
```

This keeps SRT closer to choice, constraint, and generativity rather than to broad dialectical language.

### 4.3 Consciousness boundary

NPI's informosome concept is useful but too wide. If every information-bearing process is an informosome, the concept risks expanding faster than its explanatory boundary. SRT should instead keep a ladder:

```text
process-bearing system
information-bearing system
selection-bearing system
anchoring-capable system
concern-weighted anchoring system
subject-like selection system
```

Each step should require additional closure conditions.

### 4.4 Scientific ambition

NPI points toward UIS as a future science. SRT already has a claim-ladder discipline that can prevent overclaiming. This is a comparative advantage. SRT should keep P3 bridge mappings and P4 lab hypotheses clearly separated from P0/P1 commitments.

SRT should add a formal or semi-formal layer explaining why it is not a binary existence / non-existence ontology. The layer could be named:

This would give SRT its own alternative to LIR's actuality / potentiality / T-state vocabulary.

### 5.2 A four-layer information taxonomy

SRT should avoid using "information" as a single overloaded word. A useful taxonomy:

| Type | Description | SRT status |
|---|---|---|
| Shannon information | uncertainty / signal / entropy measure | bridge / technical layer |
| selection information | difference created by selection and occlusion | SRT-native bridge |
| concern information | information weighted by `d-value`, risk, body, future, self-model | consciousness / value bridge |
| sedimented information | information written into `L_2`, constraining future choices | ontology / dynamics bridge |

This helps distinguish SRT from both Shannon reductionism and overly broad information metaphysics.

For public academic positioning, SRT can be introduced as:

This formulation gives readers a known bridge while protecting SRT's unique contribution.

## 6. What SRT should not borrow directly

### 6.1 Do not make energy self-duality a SRT primitive

The article's energy self-duality thesis is interesting, but it carries a heavy physics burden. SRT can cite it as a neighboring metaphysical strategy, but should not ground its core axioms on it.

### 6.2 Do not let contradiction become the master term

LIR's dynamic opposition is useful. But SRT's stronger language is not contradiction; it is selection under friction, anchoring under entropy, and occlusion under finite bandwidth.

### 6.3 Do not adopt informosome as a core term

Informosome is too broad. It may help in comparison with NPI, but SRT should preserve sharper system types: selection-bearing system, anchoring-capable system, concern-weighted system, subject-like system.

## 7. Candidate SRT-native formal mapping

### 7.1 Actualization / potentialization mapping

Let `σ` be a candidate state in `L_0`.

```text
potentialized(σ)  := σ remains selectable but unanchored
preanchored(σ)    := ∂\hat G_\theta / ∂σ ≠ 0 but stability threshold not reached
actualized(σ)     := σ ∈ Range(\hat G_\theta) and σ stabilizes as L_1
sedimented(σ)     := σ modifies L_2 and constrains later \hat G_\theta
```

This gives SRT an internal version of the LIR-style process ladder without importing LIR as a primitive logic.

### 7.2 Dynamic opposition as selection tension

A SRT translation of LIR's dynamic opposition:

```text
For any candidate σ, selection of σ is simultaneously:
  anchoring of σ into L_1
  occlusion of competing alternatives Ω \ σ
  write-back into L_2
  reshaping of future L_0 accessibility
```

This shifts the focus from contradiction to **asymmetric consequence**.

### 7.3 T-state as SRT boundary state

SRT can use T-state only as a comparison term. Its native equivalents are:

```text
pre-anchored state
thin L_1
phase boundary at κ_c1 / κ_c2
high-Ψ_f unresolved candidate
ontological swampland boundary
```

## 10. Public-facing formulation

A compact external formulation:

> SRT can be read as a selection-theoretic process realism. It agrees with naturalistic information theories that consciousness cannot be explained by object-first ontology or by Shannon-style information alone. But SRT shifts the explanatory unit from information-as-process to selection-as-anchoring: latent possibilities become manifest through constrained selection, and repeated manifestations sediment into future constraints. Consciousness is then not mere information processing, but concern-weighted stable anchoring with enough closure, memory, boundary maintenance, and counterfactual access to support subject-like continuity.
