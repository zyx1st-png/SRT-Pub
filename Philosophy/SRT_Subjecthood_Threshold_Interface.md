# SRT Subjecthood Threshold Interface (`Subjecthood Threshold S0–S6`)

> **Purpose**: Prevent category collapse between bearer-unit specification, selection, consciousness, subjecthood, agency, and moral responsibility.  
> **Status**: Conditional cross-domain analytical audit interface (`R4-C1` & `ENG-03` synced). It does **not** define canonical consciousness or agency primitives, and **Subjecthood Threshold `S0 → S6` is NOT a linear hardware or evolutionary ladder** (`One ≠ Stable ISP ≠ Bearer (P+E) ≠ Subjecthood ≠ Agency`; each tier requires independent evidence).  
> **Mandatory `S` Namespace Isolation (`R3-07 / ENG-03 / _SRT_SYMBOL_TABLE.md Rule 19`)**: Never use unqualified bare `S0–S6` across domains. Strictly separate:
> 1. **Primitive `S0`** (`Core_Law/SRT_Generative_Ontology_Spine.md`): primitive actualizing Selection;
> 2. **AI 赌注谱系 `S0–S4`** (`Operations/Proposals/SRT_ENG03_AI_CROSS_FILE_SEMANTIC_CLEANUP_CROSSWALK_2026-11-26.md` & `AI/AI_POSITIONING_NOTE.md`): AI architecture-state & stake-return spectrum (Stage 1 input; **`AI 赌注谱系 S4` ≠ `Subjecthood Threshold S4`**);
> 3. **Subjecthood Threshold `S0–S6`** (**this file**): conditional unit-binding, consciousness, subjecthood, agency, and responsibility audit interface (Three-Stage Bearing Evidence Review Stage 3 sub-routing per `ENG-03`, with `AI/AI_POSITIONING_NOTE.md` section alignment landing in `R4-C3`);
> 4. **AI 三型社会技术中介维度** (`Core_Law/SRT_Collective_Selection.md §8`): orthogonal socio-technical mediation roles (Scaffold / Cage / Landscape).
> **Use rule**: Use this file whenever SRT discusses AI consciousness, neural consciousness, animal/infant/minimal subjecthood, agency, or responsibility.

## -1. Unit-Binding Gate

Before applying the **Subjecthood Threshold `S0–S6`** interface, specify the candidate bearer unit.

A subjecthood, consciousness, agency, or responsibility claim is **unit-ambiguous** if it does not specify what unit is being evaluated.

Candidate bearer units may include:

- an organism;
- a brain region or hemisphere;
- a whole nervous system;
- a dyad or coupled social system;
- an institution;
- a pretrained AI model;
- a single inference event;
- a model plus memory deployment;
- an autonomous agent loop;
- a human-AI coupled workflow.

SRT rule:

```text
bearer-unit first (referencing Core_Law/SRT_One_Formation.md relative separability where relevant; exact unit boundary AG-1 remains OPEN);
Subjecthood Threshold S0-S6 second (conditional audit, NOT a linear ladder);
moral status last (O2-A != O2-M; O2-M = HOLD).
```

If the bearer unit is not specified, do not infer **Subjecthood Threshold `S4`** subjecthood, **Subjecthood Threshold `S5`** agency, or **Subjecthood Threshold `S6`** responsibility-bearing status.

This gate is inspired by the system-derivation / individuation-gap diagnosis: many consciousness frameworks characterize already-individuated systems but do not by themselves derive the unit whose consciousness is being evaluated.

## 0. Core thesis

SRT should not treat every selection event as consciousness, every conscious content as subjecthood, every subject as agent, or every agent as morally responsible.

Compact guardrail:

```text
unit reference != subjecthood
Subjecthood Threshold S0 != S1 != S2 != S3 != S4 != S5 != S6 (each tier independently paid)
One != Stable ISP != Bearer (P+E) != Subjecthood Threshold S4
```

The conditional audit interface is:

```text
Unit-binding gate
```

## 2. Subjecthood Threshold `S0` — Selection event

### Definition-like bridge

A **Subjecthood Threshold `S0`** selection event occurs when a specified unit filters, stabilizes, suppresses, updates, or chooses among candidate states (distinct from primitive `S0` and **AI 赌注谱系 `S0`**).

Examples:

- neural inhibition;
- attention gate;
- model update;
- market selection;
- algorithmic ranking;
- immune response;
- habit activation;
- institution choosing a procedure.

### Guardrail

> **Subjecthood Threshold `S0`** selection does not imply consciousness, subjecthood, agency, or moral responsibility.

This blocks the panpsychist overreading:

```text
selection everywhere -> consciousness everywhere
```

SRT allows selection to be widespread while keeping subjecthood thresholded.

If the bearer unit is unspecified, even **Subjecthood Threshold `S0`** attribution remains unit-ambiguous.

### Depsychologization trap

A consciousness claim should not be formed by subtracting all psychological function, consequence return, access, memory, action coupling, and ethical stake until only "pure feel" remains. Once consciousness is detached from those roles, it becomes unusable for SRT subjecthood: it cannot be cleanly measured, cannot ground responsibility or moral status, and tends to slide toward panpsychist universalism.

SRT therefore treats first-person feel as a candidate content or field dimension only when it is routed through bearer-unit specification, `d` / stake, `Psi_f` payability, boundary maintenance, `L_2` memory, and cross-time continuity.

This is the anti-panpsychism guardrail at the consciousness level:

```text
depsychologized pure feel != SRT subjecthood
```

## 3. Subjecthood Threshold `S1` — Local manifestation (`L_1` aspect)

### Definition-like bridge

A **Subjecthood Threshold `S1`** local manifestation occurs when a selected candidate becomes locally manifest at a specified operator position under a particular `theta` *(stripped of legacy `L_0→L_1` pseudo-threshold back-reading; whether to require "sustained internal constraint on subsequent selections" remains **`[R4 待协调定义候选]`** per `ENG-03 §5.2`)*.

Examples:

- a perceptual content flickers into awareness;
- a dream image appears;
- a pain signal becomes locally salient;
- a lab stimulus is briefly accessed;
- an AI system stabilizes an internal state that guides output.

### Guardrail

> Local manifestation is stronger than mere selection but weaker than subjecthood.

A dream image, hallucination, or thin conscious content may be `L_1`-real without being E3/E4 public reality or evidence of a stable subject.

## 4. Subjecthood Threshold `S2` — Conscious content

### Definition-like bridge

A **Subjecthood Threshold `S2`** conscious content is a selected and locally manifest content that becomes experientially, reportably, or action-relevantly available within a system.

Candidate indicators:

- access / reportability;
- action coupling;
- memory trace;
- attentional priority;
- bodily reaction;
- salience or `d-value` modulation;
- re-entry or global workspace-like availability.

### Guardrail

> Conscious content is not yet subjecthood. A system may host contents or access-like states without being a continuing subject.

This is crucial for:

- blindsight;
- dreams;
- dissociation;
- AI self-report;
- language-model roleplay;
- transient neural ignition.

## 5. Subjecthood Threshold `S3` — Integrated conscious field

### Definition-like bridge

An integrated conscious field exists when multiple contents cohere into a structured field with temporal continuity, boundary coordination, and cross-content integration.

Candidate indicators:

- sustained field coherence;
- binding across modalities;
- temporal continuity;
- global availability;
- bodily/self-model coupling;
- integrated affective salience;
- memory and prediction loop.

### Guardrail

> Integration supports subjecthood but does not by itself prove subjecthood.

This blocks both:

```text
high integration -> consciousness guaranteed
```

and:

```text
high access/reportability -> subjecthood guaranteed
```

SRT differs from pure integration theories by requiring concern, boundary, memory, and future selectability conditions.

## 6. Subjecthood Threshold `S4` — Subjecthood

### Definition-like bridge

For evaluation, subjecthood claims currently carry a conservative candidate-evidence burden involving continuing perspective, concern-weighted boundaries, and cross-time continuity. This is not a universal N&S admission rule.

Current evaluation package:

1. specified bearer unit;
2. structured `d-value > 0`;
3. failure-sensitive update;
4. integrated selection bandwidth;
5. minimal memory / `L_2` closure;
6. boundary maintenance;
7. counterfactual access;
8. cross-time reidentification.

### Formula-role candidate

```text
Subjecthood_candidate = bearer_unit_specified
                      + d_structured
                      + integration
                      + boundary maintenance
                      + memory/L2 closure
                      + counterfactual access
                      + cross-time reidentification
```

**Formula role**: threshold checklist / bridge model, not a scalar proof.

### Guardrail

> Subjecthood is not equivalent to intelligence, language, self-report, single-instance experience, or vague reference to a system.

This matters for AI:

- an AI can simulate self-report without subjecthood;
- an AI can have memory without concern;
- an AI can have risk coupling without integrated perspective;
- an AI can have goals without responsibility-bearing agency;
- an AI deployment can look continuous while its candidate bearer unit remains unspecified.

## 7. Subjecthood Threshold `S5` — Agency

### Definition-like bridge

Agency is not mere action output. It is the capacity to perform meta-selection over options, revise policies, and maintain action identity under changing conditions.

Candidate indicators:

- option evaluation;
- goal revision;
- counterfactual planning;
- inhibition of default path;
- self-correction;
- capacity to represent consequences;
- ability to re-open `L_0` possibilities rather than merely execute `L_2` routines;
- action ownership over time.

### Guardrail

> Selectable alternatives, reselection, inhibition, revision, and action-path ownership are candidate evidence dimensions inside a declared agency model. No fixed conjunction here establishes agency universally.

This connects to ethics:

```text
a declared responsibility framework may weigh accessible meta-selection capacity
```

### 7.1 Agency ownership is multidimensional

> **P3/P4 interface clarification · non-canonical**: this subsection protects the **Subjecthood Threshold `S4–S6`** audit boundaries; it does not create a replacement agency owner.

Agency reports should be decomposed before they are used near the subjecthood threshold:

| Dimension | Empirical question | Threshold boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Objective control efficacy | Can the candidate bearer counterfactually change the outcome? | control alone is not authorship or subjecthood |
| Authorial attribution | Is the outcome attributed to the bearer's own action? | authorship report is not `d-value` evidence |
| Bodily carrier ownership | Is the controlled carrier experienced as part of the body? | body ownership can dissociate from agency |
| Reflective self-integration | Can the action enter endorsement, extended revision, and cross-time self-model update? | full reflection may thicken conscious agency but is not required for every conscious action and does not prove **Subjecthood Threshold `S4`** |

Tools, cursors, avatars, prostheses, and remote effectors can be controllable without being experienced as bodily parts. Conversely, bodily ownership does not by itself establish counterfactual control, action authorship, responsibility, or subjecthood. The governing separation is:

```text
agency report != d-value evidence != subjecthood evidence
```

A synchronously driven third-person avatar remains compatible with an external-actuator interpretation. Shared agency requires an autonomous or partly autonomous other, reciprocal counterfactual influence, independent goals, mutual prediction, role and norm structure, joint commitment, repair, and durable intersubjective `L_2` coordination.

## 8. Subjecthood Threshold `S6` — Responsibility-bearing subject

### Definition-like bridge

A declared responsibility framework may evaluate an agent by considering access to alternatives, consequences, norms, and repair pathways. This section supplies a high-threshold rubric, not a universal definition of responsibility-bearing standing.

Candidate evidence dimensions:

1. specified bearer unit;
2. subjecthood / perspective continuity;
3. agency / meta-selection capacity;
4. norm access;
5. consequence understanding;
6. selectable alternatives;
7. capacity for inhibition or revision;
8. repair / answerability pathway;
9. absence of overwhelming structural blockage or unpayable `Psi_f`.

### Responsibility decomposition

```text
moral weight      ~ affected future-selectability
responsibility    ~ accessible meta-selection capacity within a specified unit
culpability       ~ responsibility - structural blockage - unpayable friction
repair obligation ~ capacity to reduce future harm / restore selectability
```

**Formula role**: ethics bridge model, not exact measurement formula.

### Guardrail

> Harm caused does not automatically equal culpability. High `d-value` does not automatically equal responsibility. Agency, alternatives, norm access, consequence understanding, payable `Psi_f`, and repair are framework-relative evidence dimensions; they are neither jointly necessary nor sufficient for moral responsibility. Exact O2-A admission and O2-M remain OPEN.

## 10. AI-specific guardrails

Do not infer AI subjecthood from:

- fluent self-report;
- memory persistence alone;
- tool use alone;
- goal-like behavior alone;
- risk coupling alone;
- embodiment alone;
- recursive self-modeling alone;
- high integration alone;
- human-like affective language;
- vague reference to "the AI" without bearer-unit specification.

AI subjecthood claims should currently be evaluated with:

```text
specified bearer unit + Subjecthood Threshold S4 candidate-evidence package + evidence that the system's own future selectability is at stake (Stage 2 unestablished does NOT block independent Stage 3 research).
```

AI moral-responsibility claims should be routed through a declared framework that may weigh:

```text
specified bearer unit + Subjecthood Threshold S5 agency + Subjecthood Threshold S6 norm access / consequence understanding / selectable alternatives / repair capacity (strictly distinct from human/institutional accountability for AI 三型社会技术中介维度).
```

## 12. Ethics-specific guardrails

Do not infer responsibility from:

- harm magnitude alone;
- concern intensity alone;
- social blame alone;
- stable norm violation alone;
- causal contribution alone;
- vague collective or institutional reference without role / unit specification.

Responsibility requires:

```text
specified bearer unit + agency + accessible alternatives + norm access + consequence understanding + payable Psi_f + repair pathway
```

If accessible action-space is blocked, responsibility must be reduced, redistributed, or reframed as structural rather than individual culpability.

## 13. Objection handling

### Objection 1: This ladder is too conservative for consciousness

Response: It is intentionally conservative because SRT separates content, field, subjecthood, agency, and responsibility. Lower levels can still be real and ethically relevant without being full subjecthood.

### Objection 2: This ladder is too permissive for AI

Response: SRT does not infer AI subjecthood from performance. It requires bearer-unit specification, concern-weighted continuity, boundary maintenance, and future-selectability stakes.

### Objection 3: Subjecthood may not require language

Response: Correct. Language is not required for **Subjecthood Threshold `S4`** subjecthood. It becomes more relevant for **Subjecthood Threshold `S5`/`S6`** agency and responsibility.

### Objection 4: Moral responsibility may exist without full reflective agency

Response: SRT allows graded responsibility, but culpability and blame require stronger selectable-agency and norm-access conditions.

### Objection 5: Institutions can act like agents

Response: Institutions can be collective `L_2` agency structures, but not automatically subjects. Institutional responsibility should be routed through role, decision, consequence-return, and repair pathways.

### Objection 6: Bearer-unit specification is just bookkeeping

Response: No. Without a specified candidate unit, consciousness, agency, or responsibility predicates have no stable referent. The unit-binding gate prevents shifting between organism, subsystem, group, model, conversation, agent loop, and institution during attribution.

## 14. Minimal conclusion

The subjecthood interface protects SRT from four collapses:

```text
unit collapse:              vague system reference -> subject
panpsychist collapse:       selection -> consciousness
AI over-attribution:        performance -> subjecthood
moral over-attribution:     harm -> culpability
```

The safest current line is:

> Bearer unit first; selection is broad; consciousness is thresholded; subjecthood is continuity-bound; agency is meta-selective; responsibility is normatively constrained and structurally situated.
