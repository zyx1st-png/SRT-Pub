# SRT Philosophy Patch PH-ETH01: Moral-status attribution / recognition separation v0.1

> **Status:** bounded P3 ethics / consciousness-interface hardening.  
> **Canonical caution:** this patch does not define actual moral status, consciousness, subjecthood, `d-value`, `Psi_f`, moral legitimacy, rights or AI personhood.  
> **Source caution:** Hirschhorn, Negro & Mudrik measure **people's judgments**. Their data do not establish which entities really have moral status, and they do not establish an SRT ontology.

---

## 1. Source anchor

SourceCard:

`Materials/2026/SRC_2026_09_07_Philosophy_Hirschhorn_Consciousness_Moral_Status_Attributions.md`

Main source-native results:

```text
consciousness attribution and moral-status attribution are strongly associated
but dissociate in both directions;

valence / self-awareness / thinking and non-mental factors
also influence moral judgment;

attributor background and target-attributor relation/context
are associated with different judgment patterns.
```

The paper itself states that public attitudes do not determine normative obligation and that its results do not establish a metaethical position.

---

## 2. Why this matters for SRT

The source is valuable mainly as a **typing pressure**.

Several SRT files already separate consciousness, subjecthood, agency, responsibility and ethical caution. What is less explicit is the difference between:

```text
what a candidate entity / bearer actually is and bears

versus

what another observer / community attributes to it as moral standing

versus

what a normative or governance system decides should be protected
```

Without this separation, SRT can fail in two opposite directions:

1. use moral recognition as evidence that consciousness / subjecthood exists;
2. use an SRT subjecthood or suffering criterion as if it exhaustively settled all moral-status attribution or policy protection.

The source pressures both shortcuts.

## 5. Main SRT bridge — type the layers before linking them

Use the following as a **role separation**, not a new ontology ladder.

### Layer A — ontic / stake-bearing assessment

Question:

> What properties, organization, history, consequence-return or vulnerability does the candidate actually have?

Relevant current owners include subjecthood, suffering, `d-value`, bearer and AI-consciousness interfaces.

This layer is about the candidate, not about whether an observer likes, fears or recognizes it.

### Layer B — moral-status attribution / recognition

Question:

> What moral standing does an attributor, group or institution assign to the target?

The source supports a descriptive schematic:

```text
Attribution ≈ f(
  target properties,
  attributor background,
  target-attributor relation,
  decision context
)
```

This is **not** a source equation and must not be turned into a new SRT scalar.

### Layer C — normative legitimacy / governance protection

Question:

> What treatment, protection, rights, duties or precautionary threshold should apply?

This cannot be read directly from either Layer A or Layer B without normative argument.

Safe chain:

```text
ontic evidence
!= attribution / recognition
!= normative legitimacy
!= governance rule
```

These layers can constrain one another in practice without becoming identical.

## 7. Main negative guard

The central PH-ETH01 guard is:

```text
moral-status attribution
!= actual moral status
!= consciousness
!= subjecthood
!= suffering capacity
!= responsibility
!= governance protection threshold
```

The source itself defines survey “moral status” in terms of intrinsic interests, but the empirical object measured is still **participants' attribution of that status**.

This distinction should be explicit whenever SRT cites survey judgments.

---

## 8. Pressure on current SRT phrasing

### 8.1 Subjecthood / suffering language

A sentence such as:

```text
suffering-relevant moral status depends on stake-coupled consequence return + stable subject conditions
```

is safe only if scoped to **suffering-relevant patienthood / vulnerability assessment**.

It becomes too strong if silently generalized to:

```text
all moral standing
all moral consideration
all policy protection
```

The source does not prove the broader statement false; it shows that ordinary moral-status attribution operates with additional criteria and therefore makes the broader equivalence an unsupported compression.

### 8.2 `d-value` language

Do not respond to multi-factor judgment by putting all features into one enlarged `d` number.

Existing SRT guardrails already say:

```text
d-value intensity != moral legitimacy
```

PH-ETH01 adds:

```text
one bearer's d-value / stake profile
!= another attributor's moral-status attribution
```

### 8.3 AI governance language

Precaution under uncertainty should remain possible without treating precaution as consciousness evidence.

Likewise, a low public consciousness attribution to current AI should not be used as proof that no future AI architecture could satisfy SRT subjecthood criteria.

## 10. Possible future operational consequences

If SRT later studies moral recognition empirically, keep at least three readouts separate:

```text
A. evidence about target organization / vulnerability
B. observer attribution of consciousness / moral status
C. downstream treatment / protection / exclusion decision
```

Potential design:

```text
match target description
vary attributor background / relation / framing
measure attribution shift
then separately measure policy / treatment choice
```

A stronger causal loop test would ask whether changed recognition subsequently changes the target's effective future options or consequence exposure. That is a future bridge test, not a result of the present paper.

## 13. One-paragraph abstract

Hirschhorn, Negro and Mudrik provide preregistered survey evidence that consciousness attribution is strongly but non-equivalently related to moral-status attribution, with additional effects of valence, self-awareness, thinking, target character, relational proximity and attributor background. Most headline implications are already paid by SRT's existing consciousness / moral-status and anti-scalar guardrails. The surviving P3 increment is narrower: explicitly type **moral-status attribution / recognition** as an attributor- and relation-sensitive layer distinct from both the target's actual bearer/stake properties and from normative legitimacy or governance protection. The source supports the descriptive attribution dependence; it does not prove moral truth, SRT ontology, or the further SRT-side hypothesis that recognition causally changes a target's effective selection environment.
