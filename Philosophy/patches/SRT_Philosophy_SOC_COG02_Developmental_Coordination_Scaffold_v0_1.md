## 1. Why this matters for SRT

SRT already treats affordances as intersections of environmental structure and embodied `theta`. This chapter gives a strong developmental extension: the child is not a fixed operator applying stable parameters to an environment. The child's body, caregiver coupling, action repertoire, language field, and regulatory rhythms all change together.

The useful SRT move is therefore not "embodied cognition proves SRT." It is narrower:

```text
development changes the space of selectable affordances by changing child, environment, and their coupling at once.
```

---

## 2. Main SRT bridge claim

### Claim SOC-COG02

Cognitive development can be modeled as the stabilization and reconfiguration of soft-assembled coordinative structures across child, body, caregiver, environment, and history.

SRT compression:

```text
\Delta\theta_{child}(t)
  \sim F(
    affordance_fit(t),
    co_regulation(t),
    motor_access(t),
    rhythmic_scaffold(t),
    L2_trace(t),
    variability_structure(t)
  )
```

where the important object is not an isolated internal representation but a changing field of selectable actions, attentional anchors, language opportunities, and self-regulatory loops.

## 5. Operational consequences

This bridge suggests that developmental studies and interventions should track:

1. individual trajectories over time, not only cross-sectional age means;
2. variability structure, not only average performance;
3. motor / social / language cascades as coupled selection-field changes;
4. caregiver and environmental scaffolding as part of the system under analysis;
5. degeneracy: alternative routes to the same functional outcome;
6. rhythm and timing as possible low-cost scaffolds for language, reading, and self-regulation.

---

## 6. Boundary cautions

- Do not state that all representation talk is invalid.
- Do not reduce development to motor development.
- Do not treat developmental difficulties as deficits inside the child alone.
- Do not treat environment redesign or rhythm as a universal cure.
- Do not use a preprint review chapter as direct experimental proof.
- Do not rewrite `theta`, `d`, `Psi_f`, or `L_2` from this material.
