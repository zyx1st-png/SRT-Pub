# PH-AG03 — Constitutive Commitment and Retroactive Certainty

## 1. Source anchor

Primary source:

- Amir Sulic, *Logical Time, (Un)Certainty, and the Structure of the Subject*.
- Author version, 16 pages, accepted for *Journal of Theoretical and Philosophical Psychology*.
- DOI: `10.1037/teo0000377`.
- Full-text close-read from a user-supplied PDF.
- SourceCard: `../../Materials/2026/SRC_2026_07_16_Philosophy_Sulic_Logical_Time_Subject.md`.

Sulic gives a structural rereading of Lacan's logical time and compares it with Wittgenstein's hinge certainty. The paper argues that some acts cannot wait for epistemic certainty: a subject must assume a position before an ultimate guarantee is available, and the act retrospectively stabilizes the certainty and subject-position it seemed to presuppose.

## 2. Why this matters for SRT

SRT needs to distinguish at least four different closure processes:

```text
evidence closure
!=
action closure
!=
responsibility closure
!=
narrative stabilization
```

A decision can remain epistemically open while becoming practically binding. Conversely, a highly confident report can remain practically non-binding when no consequence returns to the reporter.

Sulic supplies a philosophy-of-action bridge for the middle transition:

```text
reasons constrain the field
+ no final guarantee is available
+ delay is consequential
-> a position is undertaken
-> the act becomes historically and publicly attributable
```

This patch does not define P0 selection and does not claim that every commitment creates a subject.

## 3. Main SRT bridge claims

### PH-AG03.1 — Epistemic non-closure can coexist with action closure

Let:

- `J_t` = available justificatory support at time `t`;
- `G_t` = availability of an ultimate guarantee that removes all relevant risk;
- `A_t` = whether a direction has been enacted into a binding action state.

The bridge permits:

\[
J_t > 0,\quad G_t = 0,\quad A_{t+1}=1
\]

That is, the agent can have reasons without having a final guarantee, and can nevertheless enter a committed state.

This does not license:

\[
J_t=0 \Rightarrow A_{t+1}\text{ is agentive}
\]

A reasonless jump may be random, coerced, pathological or externally triggered.

### PH-AG03.2 — Delay is not neutral when it changes the reachable set

A delay has action relevance when:

\[
\mathcal{R}(t+\Delta t) \neq \mathcal{R}(t)
\]

where `R(t)` is the set of practically reachable options.

The claim is not that all waiting is a choice. It is:

> When waiting predictably removes, degrades or redistributes live options, non-action becomes part of the consequence structure and must be represented in the choice model.

### PH-AG03.3 — Constitutive commitment establishes a responsibility trace

A constitutive commitment is a candidate case in which an act does more than select an external option. It establishes a publicly or historically traceable position:

```text
act a
-> identifiable bearer b
-> consequences attributed to b
-> later reasons and norms evaluate b-as-committed
```

The act need not create the external facts. It creates or stabilizes the **position from which the bearer is answerable**.

### PH-AG03.4 — Retroactive stabilization is not retrocausation

The act may reorganize the meaning of earlier hesitation, evidence and identity:

```text
pre-act history H
+ act a
-> re-indexed history H|a
```

`H|a` means the past is now narrated, classified or institutionally recorded relative to the commitment. It does not mean the physical past is altered or that consequences are reversed.

### PH-AG03.5 — Commitment strength is not direction transparency

Define:

- `C_act`: strength or irreversibility of enacted commitment;
- `R_dir`: self-readability of the direction and its source;
- `A_reorient`: ability to use that reading to revise course.

Then:

\[
C_{act}>0 \not\Rightarrow R_{dir}>0
\]

and therefore:

\[
C_{act}>0 \not\Rightarrow T_{dir}>0
\]

A coerced, panicked, institutionally locked or post-hoc rationalized action can be highly binding while directionally opaque.

## 4. Constitutive commitment gate

Before describing an act as subject-forming, ask:

| Gate | Question |
|---|---|
| live alternatives | Were multiple responses genuinely available? |
| reason constraint | Did evidence or reasons shape the field, even if they did not guarantee the result? |
| temporal pressure | Did delay alter reachability, cost or responsibility? |
| non-random commitment | Was the transition more than noise or reflex? |
| bearer continuity | Does the same continuing unit remain answerable? |
| consequence return | Do consequences return to that unit? |
| coercion check | Was the position imposed in a way that defeats ownership? |
| readability | Can the bearer read what direction was undertaken? |
| revisability | Can the position be appealed, revised or reselected? |

Only the first three and part of the fourth are directly illuminated by Sulic. The remaining gates are SRT additions.

## 5. Two failure modes

### 5.1 Premature closure

```text
ultimate guarantee is impossible
-> therefore any decisive leap is legitimate
```

This is invalid. Absence of guarantee does not erase evidence quality, proportionality, competence or duty of care.

### 5.2 Endless deferral

```text
some uncertainty remains
-> therefore no commitment is yet responsible
```

This is also invalid when waiting changes the reachable set or transfers costs to others.

A well-formed choice scaffold should identify the zone:

```text
evidence sufficient for accountable commitment
but insufficient for risk-free guarantee
```

## 6. Relationship to Wu / PH-AG02

PH-AG02 supplies a cognitive-action mechanism:

```text
many-many response field
-> structural bias
-> attended input guides response
```

PH-AG03 supplies a commitment and subject-position bridge:

```text
reasons constrain but do not guarantee
-> act closes a practical direction
-> bearer becomes retrospectively answerable
```

Combined:

```text
available continuations
-> learned / intentional bias
-> evidence-constrained trajectory
-> residual guarantee gap
-> constitutive commitment
-> consequence return and historical inscription
-> responsibility-bearing position
```

This combined sequence remains P3. It is not a derivation of SRT metaphysics.

## 7. ChoiceMap implications

ChoiceMap should distinguish:

| Uncertainty type | Tool response |
|---|---|
| reducible factual uncertainty | identify research or verification step |
| model uncertainty | compare assumptions and sensitivity |
| value conflict | expose incompatible commitments and bearer boundaries |
| irreducible guarantee gap | state that more data cannot remove all risk |
| delay cost | show which options degrade while waiting |

Add a conclusion-readiness scaffold:

1. What evidence is still realistically obtainable?
2. What uncertainty would remain even after that evidence?
3. What changes if no decision is made by a specified time?
4. Who becomes answerable once a direction is enacted?
5. What consequences return to that bearer?
6. What appeal, rollback or reselection channels remain?

The LLM may surface these conditions but must not perform the constitutive commitment for the user.

## 8. Boundary and criticism

1. **No-guarantee is not no-justification.** Sulic's prisoner already uses hesitation as evidence. SRT should translate the thesis as absence of final authorization, not absence of reasons.
2. **The act does not create external facts.** It creates a symbolic, public or responsibility position.
3. **Constitutive acts remain constrained.** They are not automatically outside all norms or reasons.
4. **Responsibility requires more than uncertainty.** Capacity, ownership, non-coercion and consequence return remain necessary.
5. **The prisoner model is special.** Mutual observation, recursive inference and time pressure may not generalize to all decisions.
6. **Alienation / separation are bridge vocabularies.** They should not become SRT variables without an independent formalization.
7. **Desire for certainty is not operationalized.** It is a philosophical explanation of recurring guarantee demand, not a measurement proxy.
8. **Post-act certainty may be rationalization.** Historical stability alone cannot certify truth, agency or high `T_dir`.

## 9. Safe surviving claim

> In some time-sensitive, intersubjectively constrained situations, reasons can narrow the field without providing an ultimate guarantee. A bearer must nevertheless undertake a direction, and the resulting commitment can establish the position from which responsibility, identity and later epistemic evaluation become applicable.
