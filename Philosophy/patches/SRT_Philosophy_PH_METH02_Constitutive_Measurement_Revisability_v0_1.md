# PH-METH02 — Constitutive Measurement, Revisability, and Redescription Guard

## 1. Source anchor

Primary source:

```text
Robert DiSalle
Understanding Space-Time: The Philosophical Development of Physics from Newton to Einstein
Cambridge University Press, 2006
```

SourceCard:

```text
Materials/2026/SRC_2026_08_09_Philosophy_DiSalle_Understanding_Space_Time.md
```

This patch extracts a philosophy-of-science / measurement-method interface. It does **not** treat DiSalle as evidence for canonical SRT ontology.

---

## 2. Why this matters for SRT

SRT repeatedly crosses a difficult boundary:

```text
formal distinction
-> empirical identification
-> cross-domain interpretation
```

The risk is to build a formal object first, then search retrospectively for a phenomenon that can be called its realization. DiSalle's reconstruction of space-time physics supplies a mature counterexample to that workflow: in Newtonian and relativistic physics, basic principles often help specify what observable motions, clocks, signals, free-fall trajectories, and deviations count as measurements of the theoretical quantities themselves.

The surviving methodological increment is therefore:

> **A mature SRT bridge must include the evidence discipline by which its target event or quantity is identified. Interpretation is not a detachable last-stage label.**

---

## 3. Main SRT bridge claim

### PH-METH02-C1 — constitutive-but-revisable interpretation

Some theoretical principles can play a constitutive role in identifying empirical quantities without becoming eternal or arbitrary:

```text
constitutive
!= ordinary empirical hypothesis
!= unrevisable a priori
!= arbitrary convention
```

For SRT, this supports a governance rule rather than a new ontology:

> Operational identification rules may be necessary for a bridge concept to have empirical content, while remaining revisable when independent evidence shows that the rules no longer produce a coherent cross-procedure structure.

### PH-METH02-C2 — interpretation belongs inside the theory-practice pair

The dangerous picture is:

```text
formal SRT object
+ later correspondence sentence
= empirical grounding
```

The safer picture is:

```text
phenomenon class
<-> identification procedure
<-> inferential role
<-> formal representation
```

No arrow in this chain establishes canonical identity by itself.

### PH-METH02-C3 — redescription is a negative control

A different coordinate system, label, perspective, encoding, or verbal decomposition may preserve all path-relevant structure.

Therefore:

```text
representation change
!= realized selection event
```

This should function as a meta-audit discipline for SEA, not as a sixth gate.

### PH-METH02-C4 — perspective enlargement as a theory-comparison standard

A stronger theory should not win merely by declaring a deeper ontology. It should explain:

1. why the previous framework worked;
2. the boundary within which it remains valid;
3. what overextension or hidden assumption failed;
4. what broader structure absorbs the old success while resolving the new tension.

For SRT, the high-value comparison question is therefore not simply `Is existing theory X wrong?` but:

> **What does X correctly capture locally, and what additional distinction becomes necessary only outside that domain?**

### PH-METH02-C5 — objectification is constrained construction

DiSalle's geometry history supports a non-arbitrary middle position:

```text
pre-given complete object
!= arbitrary conceptual invention
```

Operational standards, repeatable transformations, and invariants can participate in stabilizing physical identity, while the world constrains whether those constructions remain coherent.

SRT-safe bridge wording:

> Objectification may be treated as constraint-sensitive stabilization, not as passive copying and not as free invention.

## 5. Formal bridge: three levels of "selection" language

This is a **non-canonical working distinction** motivated by the DiSalle audit and current SEA discipline.

### R — representational selection

A choice of coordinates, labels, coding, frame, partition, or descriptive convention:

```text
D_1 -> D_2
```

with no established path-relevant difference in the target system.

Default verdict:

```text
redescription only
```

### C — constitutive selection

A rule or operational standard helps determine what will count as a variable, event, boundary, or measurement within a framework.

Default verdict:

```text
interpretive / object-forming framework operation
```

This is not yet a realized SRT selection event and does not imply a selector-subject.

### P — realized / path selection

A bounded event satisfies the relevant SEA evidence for path efficacy, consequence, and write-back at the declared boundary and compatible timescale.

Default verdict:

```text
candidate realized selection event under SEA discipline
```

Guard:

```text
R != C != P
```

The three-level distinction is not a new canonical taxonomy and must remain reversible if it fails to improve audits.

## 6. Candidate concept: constrained constitutive selection

Working label only:

```text
constrained constitutive selection
```

Minimal sense:

> A rule-governed distinction participates in determining what can count as an object, variable, option, or event for a framework, while the admissibility of that distinction remains constrained by repeatability, cross-procedure convergence, invariants, counterfactual consequences, and systematic failure.

This label is useful only if it preserves the negative boundary:

```text
constituting empirical identity
!= producing ontological existence
```

Do not promote this phrase into `Core`, `L_0`, or the canonical definition of `selection` without a separate hardening cycle.

## 7. Operational consequences for SEA

PH-METH02 proposes **no new SEA gate**.

Instead, when evaluating DMF/NER/PEF/CBP/HEF evidence, add the following meta-questions:

1. **Redescription control** — would the claimed effect survive a change of labels, coordinate representation, logging format, or equivalent model parametrization?
2. **Boundary stability** — is the claimed path change still located at the same declared system boundary after roles are separated?
3. **Cross-procedure convergence** — do independent procedures identify the same event/quantity, or is the result tied to one privileged coding?
4. **Framework failure condition** — what observation would force revision of the current identification rule rather than merely adding an ad hoc exception?
5. **Local-validity recovery** — if SEA gives a stronger distinction than an ordinary domain theory, can it explain why the domain theory remains adequate in its successful cases?

These questions harden evidence interpretation; they do not change the current five-gate protocol.

## 12. One-paragraph abstract

PH-METH02 extracts from DiSalle's history of space-time physics a bounded methodological hardening for SRT: some principles acquire empirical content by participating in the identification and measurement of their own theoretical quantities; such principles can be constitutive without being eternal or arbitrary, and can be revised when contingent physical facts break the inherited interpretation. The patch therefore adds a redescription-versus-path-change guard, treats cross-procedure convergence and invariance as anti-arbitrariness disciplines, reframes theory progress as recovery of local validity inside a broader perspective, and presses SRT to develop a theory of selection measurement rather than relying on formal objects plus post-hoc mappings. None of these claims establish a selection-first ontology, measurement-as-selection identity, or observer-created reality.
