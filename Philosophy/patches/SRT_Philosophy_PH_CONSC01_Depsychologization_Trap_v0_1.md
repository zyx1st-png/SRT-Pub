# SRT Philosophy Patch PH-CONSC01: Depsychologization Trap v0.1

> Status: philosophy-of-mind guardrail patch.
> Canonical caution: this patch does not define consciousness. It protects SRT from treating depsychologized "pure feel" as a bearer of subjecthood, agency, or moral status.

## 1. Why this matters for SRT

SRT already says selection is broader than consciousness and subjecthood. The remaining risk is subtler: even after rejecting "selection everywhere -> consciousness everywhere," SRT could still accidentally import a depsychologized conception of consciousness, where "what it feels like" is detached from access, memory, consequence return, action coupling, and ethical stake.

That remainder cannot do SRT work. It is too detached to guide measurement, too inert to ground concern, and too unbounded to distinguish subjects from arbitrary aggregates.

---

## 2. Main SRT bridge claim

### Claim PH-CONSC01

A consciousness claim becomes unusable for SRT subjecthood when it is generated by subtracting the very psychological and structural roles that make a candidate conscious state discriminable, consequential, and ethically relevant.

SRT compression:

```text
depsychologized pure feel
  - bearer-unit boundary
  - access / report / action coupling
  - memory and cross-time continuity
  - d-value / stake
  - Psi_f payability and consequence return
  -> metaphysical residue, not SRT subjecthood
```

Positive rule:

```text
first-person feel becomes SRT-usable only inside a thresholded bearer:
unit binding + L1 anchoring + d/Psi_f consequence + boundary maintenance + L2 memory
```

## 5. Operational consequences

When SRT evaluates a consciousness or subjecthood claim, ask:

1. What is the candidate bearer unit?
2. What access, report, action, or update role makes the state discriminable?
3. What `d` or stake makes the state matter to the bearer?
4. What `Psi_f` or consequence return would be paid if the state is ignored, suppressed, or altered?
5. What memory and boundary structure make the state belong to a continuing subject rather than a momentary content?
6. What ethical or responsibility conclusion is being attempted, and which S-level supports it?

If these answers are absent, the claim should stay below SRT subjecthood.

## 6. Boundary cautions

- Do not write "Frankish proves SRT."
- Do not convert this into a blanket denial of phenomenology.
- Do not use this article as empirical evidence against panpsychism.
- Do not infer that all psychological function is sufficient for S4 subjecthood.
- Do not treat reportability as the only possible access role.
- Do not collapse S2 conscious content, S3 integrated field, S4 subjecthood, S5 agency, and S6 responsibility.
