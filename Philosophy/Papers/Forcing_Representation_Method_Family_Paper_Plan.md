## 2. Research question and bounded thesis

### 2.1 Research question

> Across Cohen's ramified construction, generic-filter/name forcing, and Boolean-valued forcing, which load-bearing operation roles are preserved, which representation-specific components are replaced, and when does a change of endpoint amount to a change of operation token without splitting the higher forcing method family?

The question has three levels that must never be collapsed:

1. **operation-token identity** — whether the same historically and mathematically specified sequence of operations is performed;
2. **component-role preservation** — whether a later object performs the same load-bearing function after a typed translation;
3. **method-family continuity** — whether enough characteristic roles and relations survive to justify treating the operations as members of one bounded family.

### 2.2 Provisional thesis

The three presentations are not literally one operation token. A qualified family continuity is nevertheless defensible at a representation-neutral core:

> ordered partial information, a ground-definable truth-determining relation, an interpretation architecture for prospective extension objects, and either generic realization or an explicitly stated semantic substitute.

The qualification matters. Cohen's complete sequence and ramified terms are not recoverable canonically from arbitrary modern forcing data; their roles can be reconstructed only under presentation, countability, enumeration, and coding choices. Likewise, direct Boolean validity has a theorem-producing semantic endpoint that is weaker than formation of an ordinary two-valued generic extension. Boolean-valued forcing acquires the full extension-generating role only when the appropriate ultrafilter, homomorphism, or quotient step is included.

This thesis is case-bound. It does not propose a universal criterion for the identity of all mathematical methods.

## 3. Candidate contribution

The article has four possible contributions. All four must survive review; otherwise the paper narrows or stops.

1. **A three-level identity distinction.** It separates historical operation tokens, preserved component roles, and bounded method families instead of identifying methods by output or by notation.
2. **A componentwise reconstruction.** It maps complete sequence, ramified terms, finite conditions, forcing relation, and interpreted `N` to generic filter, names, poset, modern forcing relation, and `M[G]`, with the reverse assumptions stated.
3. **An endpoint-sensitive Boolean analysis.** It distinguishes direct Boolean validity, an ordinary two-valued quotient, and the generic extension obtained through a generic ultrafilter or equivalent bridge.
4. **A constraint on philosophy of mathematical practice.** The case shows why equal theorem output under two representations is insufficient for operation identity, while literal syntactic difference is insufficient for a family split.

The fourth contribution is the most vulnerable. It must be supported externally by work on proof/method identity and mathematical practice; the repository's internal audit distinctions are not, by themselves, a philosophical literature.

## 8. Withdrawal and narrowing conditions

The paper must narrow or stop under any of the following conditions:

1. **Historical failure:** Moore or another primary-quality history defeats the chronology or shows that the proposed component succession is misdescribed.
2. **Translation failure:** the Jech/Shoenfield bridge does not support a typed forward role map for any load-bearing Cohen component.
3. **Reverse overclaim:** the final prose implies a canonical inverse from arbitrary `G`, arbitrary names, or a bare `M[G]` to Cohen's literal sequence, syntax, clauses, or `N`.
4. **Endpoint collapse:** direct Boolean validity, arbitrary two-valued quotient, and generic extension are treated as one endpoint because they prove the same consistency result.
5. **Methodological redundancy:** the literature already provides the same three-level token/role/family analysis for this forcing case with equal or greater technical precision.
6. **Unsupported generalization:** the case-bound result is used as a general theory of mathematical method identity without comparative cases.

If (5) occurs, the output becomes a technical-historical review rather than a novelty claim. If (2) occurs, the family-continuity thesis is withdrawn rather than protected by output equivalence.
