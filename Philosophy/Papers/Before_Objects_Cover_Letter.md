# Cover Letter — *Before Objects: Selection Realism and the Constitutive Explanation of Determinacy*

To the Editors of *Philosophia*,

I am pleased to submit my manuscript, "Before Objects: Selection Realism and the Constitutive Explanation of Determinacy," for consideration as a research article.

The paper argues that *object-style determinacy*—the conjunction of boundary, identity, and practical load-bearing—should not be presupposed without explanation as the ontological floor, as object-first ontology assumes, but is better understood as the grounded result of conditions of constrained selection (cutting, anchoring, stabilization). I call the resulting position *selection realism*. The argument is deliberately staged at two strengths: a weak transcendental regress establishes only that object-first ontology owes an *explanatory debt* (it does not deductively defeat naturalness realism), and a defeasible inference to the best explanation then argues that the debt is best discharged by constrained selectability, located as the disciplined middle between Sider/Lewis-style natural joints and Hirsch/Carnap-style framework-internal convention. To forestall idealism and relativism, reality comes in graded strengths along four dimensions, with intervention-resistance read dispositionally; and the view is given empirical purchase through a worked comparison in disease classification (peptic ulcer / *H. pylori* versus the DSM-5 reclassification of Asperger's syndrome), where each strong claim is paired with an explicit withdrawal condition.

I believe the paper suits *Philosophia*'s remit in metaphysics and metaontology. It engages directly with positions the journal's readership will recognize—naturalness realism, quantifier variance and Carnapian framework-relativity, Barad's agential realism, and the natural-kinds literature—and its central contribution is a *constitutive-explanation* account that is falsifiable in the specific sense laid out in the disease case. [For *Metaphysics*: the paper's framework-level treatment of individuation and grounding, together with its open methodological stance, fits the journal's scope in foundational metaphysics.]
