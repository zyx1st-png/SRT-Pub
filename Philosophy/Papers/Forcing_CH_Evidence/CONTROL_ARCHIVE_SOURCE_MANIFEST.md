# Forcing–CH Control Archive Source Manifest

Non-canonical source register for corpus assembly under `CONTROL_ARCHIVE_ADEQUACY_CERTIFICATE_v0_1` §4.2. This manifest records sources and their evidentiary functions; it does not admit, exclude, score, rank, select, or individuate any work.

## 1. Source-role vocabulary

| Source role | Meaning in this manifest |
|---|---|
| `in-window primary publication` | Original publication inside the fixed 1938–1963 boundary. |
| `contemporaneous review` | Review or notice published within the period and close enough to record contemporary characterization. |
| `contemporaneous technical reconstruction` | Period publication that restates, reconstructs, or technically modifies an earlier construction. |
| `pre-window baseline` | Source earlier than 1938 used to establish the inherited repertoire on its own terms. |
| `later reconstruction` | Post-1963 source used for technical or historiographic clarification, never as evidence of period availability by itself. |

Only the first three roles are instantiated in this assembly. Quine, Russell, and Curry are mentioned inside the registered period record, but no separate pre-window baseline is entered here. No later reconstruction is used.
