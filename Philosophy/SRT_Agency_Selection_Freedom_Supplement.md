# SRT Agency Supplement: Freedom, Selection, and Answer Outsourcing

> **Role**: This short supplement does not replace the current `meta-selection` doctrine. It sharpens its criterion: not every self-modification counts as freedom.

---

## 1. Meta-Selection and Its Supplementary Criterion

Existing SRT agency doctrine already defines free will as a second-order capacity to modify `θ`, and locates its mechanism in event-internal re-entry and cross-event writing.

This supplement adds a missing criterion:

> **A parameter rewrite is not yet freedom merely because it is high-order.**

A rewrite counts as freedom only insofar as it helps **reopen what an existing `L_2` had compressed out of `L_0`**, rather than merely optimizing the already dominant `L_2` at a higher recursive level.

So:

- **Meta-selection** answers: *can `θ` be modified?*
- **Freedom (supplemented)** asks: *does this modification reopen reality, or only optimize closure?*

---

## 2. Selection as the Root Difference

The most fundamental distinction is not between certainty and doubt, nor between obedience and rebellion, but between:

- **selection still alive**, and
- **selection already lost**.

If selection is still alive:
- the current floor need not be believed unconditionally,
- uncanniness can remain open,
- pain need not be translated immediately into adaptation-demand.

If selection is lost:
- some floor must be believed absolutely,
- stability becomes sacralized,
- updating collapses into floor-maintenance.

---

## 3. Earliest Signal of Losing Selection

The earliest signal is not overt dogmatism.
It is the **acceleration of explanation**:

> pain, uncanniness, and hollowness are translated back into available templates too quickly.

Thus the subject no longer passes through:
- uncanniness,
- suspension,
- renewed selection,
- and rebuilt floor.

Instead, the process is short-circuited by rapid interpretive closure.

---

## 4. Minimum Sign that Selection Is Still Alive

The minimum operative sign is not full clarity or explicit access to `初心`.
It is simply:

> **the subject does not immediately return to the old template.**

This is the smallest act by which selection preserves itself.

---

## 5. AI, Search, and the Modern Crisis of Agency

A new structural danger appears when AI or search tools provide answers that are not merely useful, but **correct without the subject’s selection having participated in their becoming-floor**.

The danger is therefore deeper than misinformation.
It is:

> **the rise of correct answers that bypass agency.**

Such answers may be epistemically true, yet existentially dangerous, because they allow a subject to stand on an external floor before:
- uncanniness has unfolded,
- suspension has occurred,
- and renewed selection has rebuilt a floor from within.

---

## 6. Healthy Use Principle

An answer becomes non-dangerous not merely by being correct, but by being reinserted into the subject’s own path of:

- uncanniness,
- suspension,
- re-selection,
- and re-anchoring.

So the healthy role of AI/search is not:
- final floor,

but rather:
- material,
- mirror,
- disturbance,
- candidate scaffold.

---

## 7. Compression Thesis

This supplement can be compressed into one line:

> **Agency dies not first when the subject stops thinking, but when the subject stops selecting and begins to receive ready-made floors too quickly.**

That danger now appears not only in institutions and ideologies, but in everyday dependence on high-speed answer systems.
