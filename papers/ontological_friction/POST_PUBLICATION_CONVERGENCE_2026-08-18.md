# Post-publication convergence: executive friction, transition landscapes, and history-dependent reachability

> **Status / use rule**: This is a post-publication research and bridge note. It does **not** amend the published Frontiers article, add a new P0/P1 theorem, or establish a clinical mechanism. It records how several 2024–2026 neural-dynamics results can be jointly analyzed with the published executive-friction framework and with the repository's current RC-A / ST-A semantics.
>
> **Layer guard**: source-derived neural claims, the published executive-friction claims, and the cross-domain SRT bridge proposed here are kept separate. A neural state, a task/policy state, and an SRT candidate are not assumed to be the same state variable.

## 0. Executive verdict

The published Frontiers framework and the recent attractor / state-transition literature are compatible, but they answer different questions.

```text
neural dynamics / attractor work
-> which neural states and transition patterns are dynamically supported?

executive friction (Psi_f)
-> how costly is it to maintain or execute an already recruitable policy transition
   relative to current defaults and resources?

history-dependent reachability / SRT bridge
-> how does the same history-bearing process acquire a different future effective candidate set,
   and can mismatch later reopen or revise that set?
```

The principal post-publication refinement is therefore **not**:

```text
attractor = L2
NCT energy = Psi_f
persistent neural state = Stable ISP
rigid / automated process = no Selection
```

The useful distinction is:

```text
Structural Reachability
!= Functional Accessibility
!= Transition Friction
!= Currently Effective Candidacy
!= Generative Reselectability
```

For depression, the new neural literature does **not** overturn the published agent/policy-level claim that inaction can minimize acute executive friction. It instead blocks a stronger neural gloss that would identify that policy-level low-friction state with one static, long-dwell neural basin. The safer joint picture is a history-conditioned, imbalanced transition field in which a behaviorally persistent policy can coexist with frequent neural-state transitions and a narrowed transition repertoire.

No canonical edit is required by this note.

## 1. Source cluster and evidence roles

### 1.1 Published anchor: Zhang 2026, Frontiers in Neuroscience

**Yuxin Zhang.** *A translational cross-modal control-cost framework for executive breakdown.* *Frontiers in Neuroscience* 20 (2026), article 1837760. DOI: `10.3389/fnins.2026.1837760`.

Publication date: 2026-08-06. Article type: Hypothesis and Theory. No new empirical dataset is reported.

Load-bearing published claims relevant here:

1. `Psi_f` is operationally estimated as a cross-modal latent control/execution-cost factor.
2. Candidate mechanistic models include accumulated control effort and departure from a habitual/default policy; these are rival/related mechanisms rather than universal identities.
3. `P_sel` is the available control-budget proxy.
4. The knowing-doing gap appears when implementation cost exceeds remaining control capacity despite an intact representation of the appropriate action.
5. Slow constraints — habits, priors, hyperpriors, trait-like limits — shape future selections.
6. Slow embodiment parameters may change through learning, friction-landscape change, and homeostatic recoil.

**Boundary**: the paper does not claim that `Psi_f` is a complete theory of candidate accessibility, nor that every represented but unexecuted action is still an effective action-guiding candidate in the same operational sense.

### 1.2 Vinograd et al. 2024, Nature

**Vinograd et al.** *Causal evidence of a line attractor encoding an affective state.* *Nature* 634 (2024): 910–918. DOI: `10.1038/s41586-024-07915-x`.

Empirical role:

- provides causal perturbational evidence that an affect-related neural population can exhibit line-attractor dynamics;
- on-manifold stimulation can be integrated along the attractor;
- off-manifold perturbation relaxes back toward the attractor.

SRT / friction use:

```text
causal neural-state persistence / attraction exists
-> implementation-level support for state-landscape reasoning
!= evidence that attractor = L2 / Selection / subjecthood
```

### 1.3 Kauvar et al. 2025, Science

**Kauvar et al.** *Conserved brain-wide emergence of emotional response from sensory experience in humans and mice.* *Science* 388(6750) (2025): eadt3971. DOI: `10.1126/science.adt3971`.

Empirical role:

- emotionally salient sensory signals show a fast brain-wide broadcast followed by a slower persistent distributed pattern;
- pharmacological manipulations can attenuate the persistent component while largely preserving the fast sensory broadcast and reducing the corresponding emotional response.

Useful separation:

```text
sensory event detected / broadcast
!= persistent affective context formed
```

This is an implementation-level temporal-stage result. Persistent neural context is not by itself an SRT `L2`, Stable ISP, or consciousness criterion.

### 1.4 Kilic et al. 2026, Nature Communications

**Kilic et al.** *Spatiotemporal asymmetries on brain energy landscape uncover system entrapment related to depression severity.* *Nature Communications* 17 (2026): 5662. DOI: `10.1038/s41467-026-71961-4`.

Empirical role:

- 7T resting-state fMRI was analyzed in 38 MDD and 38 healthy-control participants;
- a DWI subset contained 26 MDD and 27 healthy-control participants;
- MDD participants showed higher entry/exit probabilities for one state and elevated fractional occupancy despite shorter dwell time;
- some transitions (including State 3 <-> State 2) were increased while others (State 4 <-> State 1) were reduced;
- the study combines empirical co-activation-pattern transitions with Network Control Theory (NCT) transition-energy estimates and reports a depression-related entrapment signature;
- importantly for the present bridge, the paper reports preferential traversal of modeled higher-control-energy trajectories despite the availability of structurally facilitated alternatives.

Source-derived implication:

```text
high transition count
!= high transition repertoire / flexibility
```

The last result already establishes a **within-neural-domain** dissociation between empirical transition preference and structural control-energy facilitation. The novel extension proposed later in this note is not that dissociation itself; it is the test of whether a preregistered mapping can relate neural structural burden to independently measured behavioral/executive friction and candidate admission.

**Nicole Rust.** *Neuroscience needs a new paradigm: The brain is not a machine.* IAI, 2025-08-12. Source URL: `https://iai.tv/articles/neuroscience-needs-a-new-paradigm-the-brain-is-not-a-machine-auid-3287` (conceptual commentary; primary empirical weight remains with the peer-reviewed studies above).

## 2. What the published Frontiers paper already provides

The Frontiers framework defines a resource-bounded mapping from a candidate policy space into an active state under slow constraints and finite embodiment parameters.

A minimal reading is:

```text
candidate policy space Pi(t)
+ slow constraints theta_t
+ finite control budget P_sel
-> selected / active implementation
```

Executive friction is the cost of maintaining or reconfiguring that active implementation relative to default drift or habitual policy.

Two mechanism families are deliberately separated.

### 2.1 Control-effort family

Conceptually:

```text
Psi_f^effort ~ accumulated control input required to resist uncontrolled drift
```

### 2.2 Default-deviation family

Conceptually:

```text
Psi_f^dev ~ accumulated divergence from a baseline / habitual policy
```

with a local Fisher approximation permitted only under declared regularity conditions.

The published knowing-doing gap can therefore be summarized as:

```text
appropriate action is represented
+
implementation / switching friction is high
+
remaining P_sel is insufficient
-> execution fails
```

The new materials do not invalidate this mechanism. They expose a neighboring construct that should not be silently folded into `Psi_f`: **whether a represented option is recruited into the current action-guiding competition before execution burden is paid.**

## 3. Post-publication construct separation

### 3.1 Structural reachability

A state or policy is structurally reachable when the declared physical / computational architecture permits a path to it.

Conceptual readout:

```text
R_struct(j | i) > 0
```

Examples include graph-theoretic reachability and NCT-based structural transition models.

Structural reachability answers:

> Is there a physically/model-supported route from state `i` to state `j` under the declared state vocabulary?

It does not answer whether the current bearer recruits that route into present action-guiding competition.

### 3.2 Functional accessibility — admission, not realized transition

To keep accessibility separable from transition friction, do **not** define it as the realized one-step transition probability. That quantity is downstream of both candidate admission and execution/payability and therefore mechanically confounds the two constructs.

Use a pre-execution admission/recruitment definition instead:

```text
A_eff(j,t)
~ P(j enters the current action-guiding candidate set
    | S_t, h_t, theta_t, physiology/context,
      before the target transition is executed)
```

Operational readouts may include:

- target option appears in an unconstrained or low-burden action set;
- target is sampled into a consideration set before execution cost is imposed;
- target reaches a preregistered decision-variable / policy-probability admission threshold under a low-burden probe;
- target can be selected when the execution burden is experimentally minimized.

A state may therefore be structurally reachable while functionally suppressed:

```text
R_struct(j | i) > 0
but
A_eff(j,t) ~ 0
```

This definition makes §8's accessibility × friction design conceptually identifiable rather than definitionally entangled.

### 3.3 Transition friction — conditional on recruitment

For the published framework, a useful post-publication notation is:

```text
Psi_f(i -> j | theta_t, h_t, j admitted)
```

meaning the effective control / execution burden of actually traversing from the current active state toward an already recruited target `j`, conditional on current embodiment and history.

This notation is a **research extension**, not a retroactive redefinition of the published estimand.

A practical experimental separation is:

```text
accessibility probe
-> does j enter operative competition under minimal execution burden?

friction probe
-> once j is admitted, how much extra control / time / physiological burden
   is required to implement i -> j?
```

### 3.4 Currently effective candidacy

A represented option should not automatically be treated as a currently effective candidate.

Bridge-level distinction:

```text
C_effective(t)
subseteq C_functionally_accessible(t)
subseteq C_structurally_reachable
```

`currently effective` is inherited as descriptive scope language from RC-A / P1-T06. It does **not** create a new `live selection` object.

An option may be:

```text
represented / verbally reportable
but
not sufficiently recruited to enter present action-guiding competition
```

This distinction provides a testable subdivision of the knowing-doing gap.

### 3.5 Generative reselectability

Generative reselectability is the stronger P2/P3 question of whether consequence-sensitive mismatch can revise the process's own comparison rules, candidate boundaries, or candidate-generation / reopening conditions.

It is **not** required for every Stable ISP and must not be promoted into the definition of Selection.

## 4. A revised five-stage joint model

The combined framework can be expressed as:

```text
1. Structural landscape
   Which transitions are physically / computationally possible in a declared state vocabulary?

2. History-conditioned accessibility
   Which of those routes are presently recruited into action-guiding competition under h_t and theta_t?

3. Executive friction
   Conditional on recruitment, what burden must be paid to maintain / switch into the target?

4. Payability and execution
   Can residual P_sel pay the selected transition burden?

5. Historical write-back / reopening
   How do consequences reshape future admission and transition burden,
   and can later mismatch revise the repertoire itself?
```

Compact form:

```text
structure
-> candidate admission / accessibility
-> friction / payability
-> execution
-> history write-back
-> changed future admission and transition burden
```

This is a bridge decomposition. No claim is made that these stages are anatomically serial modules.

## 5. Depression: retain the agent-level claim, retire only the overstrong neural gloss

The published Frontiers discussion states, at the agent/policy level, that a depressed agent may enter a stable low-energy attractor in which **inaction** minimizes acute distress / friction while longer-term burden accumulates.

That claim does **not** entail:

```text
one neural CAP state
-> long neural dwell
-> low neural transition count
```

A behaviorally persistent policy of inaction is compatible with rapid or frequent transitions among neural population states.

### 5.1 What should be retired

Retire only the **cross-level neural interpretation** that silently turns the published policy-level attractor into:

```text
one deep neural state
-> long neural dwell
-> low neural movement
```

Kilic 2026 supplies direct pressure against treating that neural gloss as the default account, because strong depression-related entrapment can coexist with shorter dwell and increased entry/exit in a particular CAP state.

### 5.2 Safer joint reading

Use:

```text
agent/policy level:
locally low acute executive friction can stabilize inaction / habitual avoidance

neural implementation level:
imbalanced transition asymmetry
+ restricted transition repertoire
+ locally recurrent state subspaces
+ state-dependent structural control burdens
```

Therefore:

```text
behavioral persistence
!= neural long dwell

transition activity
!= generative health

state lability
!= repertoire reopening
```

The anti-overpromotion guard is supplied directly by `03_Bridges/SRT_Agency_Automation_Guard_2026-08-17.md`: script / habit / gradient / `L2` automation is not sufficient for stronger agency, but it also does **not** imply a selection-free process.

## 6. NCT transition energy must not be identified with Psi_f

The Kilic paper's Network Control Theory quantity is a modeled control input required to drive a structural-network system between CAP states under the chosen linear control assumptions.

Do **not** write:

```text
E_NCT = Psi_f
```

or:

```text
NCT control energy = metabolic energy actually paid by the brain
```

Use instead:

```text
E_struct(n_i -> n_j)
= domain-specific structural control-burden estimate
  over neural CAP states n_i, n_j

Psi_f_effective(p_a -> p_b | h_t, theta_t)
= cross-modal / executive paid-burden construct or mechanism-family estimate
  over task/policy transitions p_a, p_b
```

### 6.1 Within-neural dissociation is already source-derived

Kilic already reports that observed transition preference can favor modeled higher-control-energy trajectories despite structurally facilitated alternatives. Therefore this note must not present the existence of a preference-versus-structural-energy dissociation as an SRT prediction.

### 6.2 Cross-domain comparison requires an explicit state map

A cross-domain ordinal comparison such as:

```text
E_struct(n_i -> n_C) < E_struct(n_i -> n_B)
while
Psi_f_effective(p_i -> p_C) > Psi_f_effective(p_i -> p_B)
```

is **not executable** until the study preregisters a mapping between the neural and behavioral state vocabularies.

Minimum admissible forms are:

```text
M_state: neural CAP transition -> task/policy transition label
```

or a common experimental event vocabulary in which both quantities are indexed by the same task-defined transitions.

The mapping must declare:

- which neural states correspond to which task/policy events;
- whether the mapping is one-to-one, many-to-one, probabilistic, or time-windowed;
- mapping uncertainty / held-out classification error;
- exclusion rules for ambiguous transitions;
- whether the comparison is ordinal only or claims a calibrated metric relation.

Without this shared indexing layer, `E_struct` and `Psi_f_effective` may be discussed as neighboring constructs but may **not** be rank-compared on nominally identical `(i,j)` labels.

The cross-domain hypothesis worth testing is therefore narrower:

> after a preregistered state mapping, structural facilitation and independently measured executive transition burden may dissociate in direction, and history-conditioned candidate admission may account for additional held-out variance beyond structural control burden alone.

This is P4, not an established result.

## 7. Two kinds of knowing-doing gap

### Type I — Payability failure

The target is recruited into the operative set:

```text
A in C_effective(t)
```

but:

```text
Psi_f(A | admitted) > residual P_sel
```

Predicted signature:

- target action remains rapidly recruitable when immediate execution burden is lowered;
- reducing concurrent load or noise, increasing recovery, or simplifying switching demands should restore performance comparatively quickly;
- no prolonged retraining is required merely to make the option operative again.

This is the cleanest continuation of the published executive-friction model.

### Type II — Accessibility contraction

The target remains represented:

```text
A in C_represented
```

but is weak or absent from current operative competition:

```text
A notin C_effective(t)
```

Predicted signature:

- simply increasing time or lowering immediate execution load may be insufficient to make the option enter the action set;
- contextual reframing, relearning, prolonged intervention, or history-sensitive perturbation may be required to restore admission;
- recovery should show stronger hysteresis and transfer dependence.

### Boundary

`Type II` is not an established clinical subtype. It is a P4 experimental separation to test whether executive friction and candidate accessibility can be empirically dissociated.

## 8. A minimal accessibility × friction experimental separation

The two factors must be measured / manipulated at different stages.

| | Low transition friction conditional on admission | High transition friction conditional on admission |
|---|---|---|
| **High functional accessibility / admission** | ordinary easy execution | classic knowing-doing / payability-failure candidate |
| **Low functional accessibility / admission** | suppressed candidate that becomes easy if externally cued/admitted | severe lock-in candidate |

### 8.1 Accessibility manipulation / readout

Measure target admission **before** the high-burden execution step, for example through:

- low-burden forced-choice / consideration-set probe;
- policy-probability admission threshold;
- pre-action sampling / gaze / deliberation readout if independently validated;
- explicit external cue that makes the option operative without changing the later motor/executive burden.

### 8.2 Friction manipulation / readout

Conditional on target admission, manipulate or measure:

- switching load;
- concurrent control demand;
- response conflict;
- time / effort / physiological control-cost proxies already justified by the Frontiers framework.

### 8.3 Primary readouts

1. target-admission probability;
2. switch / implementation latency **conditional on admission**;
3. recovery after acute load reduction;
4. post-probe hysteresis;
5. transfer to a novel but structurally analogous option;
6. future reachable-policy / arrival-distribution change.

A valid design must verify that the accessibility manipulation changes admission more than execution cost and that the friction manipulation changes conditional execution burden more than admission. If that orthogonality check fails, the 2 × 2 is uninterpretable rather than evidence for either construct.

Do not infer `d-value`, consciousness, agency, or moral standing from any cell of this design.

## 9. Repertoire metrics: activity is not flexibility

A simple empirical readout for transition diversity can be kept theory-neutral.

For transition probabilities `p_ij` from current state `i`:

```text
H_T(i) = - sum_j p_ij log p_ij
R_eff(i) = exp(H_T(i))
```

Use rule:

```text
R_eff = descriptive transition-repertoire proxy
!= d-value
!= Stable ISP
!= Selection count
!= generative reselectability
```

This captures a Kilic-compatible pattern:

```text
high number of transitions
+
low transition entropy
-> active but narrow loop
```

## 10. State change, landscape change, and generative revision

Therapeutic or experimental perturbations should be separated into at least three levels.

### Level A — State displacement

```text
external perturbation
-> current state changes
```

If the perturbation is removed and the system returns immediately to the old loop, this is not evidence of landscape revision.

### Level B — Landscape / repertoire change

```text
intervention
-> candidate-admission / transition probabilities change persistently
```

Previously suppressed alternatives become more accessible after the intervention.

### Level C — Generative reselectability candidate

```text
new mismatch
-> same history-bearing process can revise comparison / reopening conditions
-> adaptive alternatives become effective without merely replaying a fixed externally supplied script
```

Hard guard:

```text
state escape
!= landscape revision
!= generative reselectability
```

This is consistent with `Core/SRT_Core_21c_Bridge_Hypotheses.md P2/P3-B13` and with the NEURAL35 reopening / re-anchoring guard.

## 11. Rival-theory requirement

None of the following, by itself, establishes unique SRT explanatory value:

```text
feedback
attractors
persistent neural states
history dependence
hysteresis
restricted state repertoire
precision dominance
transition-cost asymmetry
```

These can be modeled by ordinary dynamical systems, recurrent reinforcement learning, control theory, predictive processing, and richer active-inference models.

The correct model-comparison stack is at least:

```text
M0: ordinary dynamical / transition model
M1: history-aware recurrent RL / metaplastic model
M2: richer active-inference / predictive-processing model
    (learned transitions, temporal depth, precision, priors, history)
M3: SRT bridge model adding explicit same-bearer consequence return
    and future-effective-candidate loss / reopening
```

### Negative result that SRT must accept

If `M2` predicts the relevant history, hysteresis, recovery, candidate suppression, and reopening effects under frozen complexity / refit budgets, while `M3` adds no held-out predictive or intervention gain, then record:

```text
SRT-specific bridge contribution dispensable for this target
```

Do not relabel the rival mechanism as `Psi_f`, `L2`, or reselectability after the fact.

## 12. Direct bridge to `history_dependent_reachability`

The repository already contains:

```text
papers/history_dependent_reachability/
```

whose manuscript asks whether two systems matched in their observable present can nevertheless differ in future behavioral reachability because a slow history-formed memory survives the matching boundary.

That work provides a rigorous **identification battery in designed systems**:

```text
exact present-state matching
+
master-yoked controls
+
external-action sham
+
selection-specific write-back
+
fresh-seed / frozen-threshold discipline
```

Its own scope statement is binding: the results are constructive demonstrations in designed models and establish framework feasibility, **not facts about natural systems**. The retained prediction-error NO-GO, in which the yoked arm reproduced the future effect, is especially relevant because it shows that generic history dependence does not establish selection-specific write-back.

Therefore the human MDD / neural-state literature cannot simply be "plugged into" HDR. A natural-system bridge additionally requires:

1. a preregistered neural-state ↔ task/policy-state mapping where cross-domain comparison is attempted;
2. an independently measured candidate-admission readout;
3. a perturbation or quasi-experimental design capable of distinguishing own action→consequence coupling from generic exposure history;
4. longitudinal evidence that any history-dependent remainder changes future reachability after the observable present is matched as far as feasible.

This neuroscience cluster should **feed that identification program rather than create a parallel construct family**.

Recommended translation:

```text
Frontiers Psi_f
-> transition-burden / payability axis

Kilic / Vinograd / Kauvar
-> empirical neural-dynamics existence and dissociation cases

history_dependent_reachability
-> designed-system causal-identification framework
   to be translated, not assumed, for natural systems

RC-A / ST-A / B13
-> semantic guards separating Selection, Stable ISP, agency and generative reselectability
```

## 13. Stronger matched-present / different-history test

A useful next computational experiment is to match the present while varying the consequence structure of the history.

### History A — resettable consequence

```text
failure
-> immediate loss
-> complete reset
-> no persistent loss of future sensing / action / recovery capacity
```

### History B — same-bearer persistent consequence

Match immediate reward magnitude, but let failure alter the same agent's later capacity:

```text
failure
-> sensor fidelity down
and/or action options down
and/or recovery capacity down
and/or later reachable policies down
```

At the test point, match as much current state as possible:

```text
same observable environment state
same fast values / initial policy distribution
same current reward structure
same acute sensory noise
```

Then introduce a novel mismatch and measure:

```text
candidate-admission / reopening latency
transition repertoire
post-mismatch hysteresis
future arrival distribution
transfer to novel goals
```

The purpose is not to prove SRT. It is to ask whether **same-bearer consequence history leaves a future-effective remainder after ordinary present-state variables are matched**.

## 14. Same-bearer / copy control: use as a copy-completeness audit, not a magical identity test

A naive test of:

```text
SAME:
process P continues

COPY:
all internal state is copied to fresh process P'
```

is underdetermined.

If a deterministic digital system is copied **completely** — including every state variable that affects future dynamics, the same environment coupling, and the same random-number stream — then equal future behavior is expected by construction. If the copy is incomplete, `SAME != COPY` can be explained trivially by omitted state.

Therefore a meaningful artificial-system control must predeclare a **copy boundary**.

### 14.1 Required copy inventory

Explicitly classify at least:

```text
copied:
- fast policy / value state
- slow memory / latent state
- optimizer / adaptation state if active
- replay / episodic buffer if active
- RNG state or seed policy

held fixed across both arms:
- environment state
- observation stream
- reward / task specification

intentionally not copied, if any:
- continuous environment coupling / body-specific degradation
- physically accumulated wear / resource state not represented in the copied controller
- external relational state that is constitutively part of the declared bearer model
```

### 14.2 Interpretation

If every future-relevant variable is copied and the transition law is identical, then:

```text
SAME = COPY
```

is a construction check, not evidence against bearer continuity.

If a deliberately declared non-copied coupling produces:

```text
SAME != COPY
```

the result identifies the omitted/continuous coupling as causally relevant. It still does **not** establish subjecthood or a non-copyable metaphysical residue.

Accordingly, SAME/COPY is secondary to the matched-present / different-history and yoked/sham identification battery. Use it only when the bearer model declares which continuous or relational variables are intentionally outside the copied state vector.

## 15. Treatment interpretation guard

For depression and other clinical applications, distinguish:

```text
symptom reduction
!= state displacement
!= basin / transition-landscape change
!= effective repertoire reopening
!= generative reselectability
```

A successful treatment may lower `Psi_f` for an already accessible action without reopening previously suppressed candidates.

Conversely, a perturbation may broaden accessibility while weakening stable action-guiding re-anchoring. NEURAL35 already guards:

```text
reopening != reselectability
```

Clinical claims require longitudinal evidence, nuisance controls, ethics / clinical collaboration, and the folder-level neuroscience claim-status guardrail.

## 18. Candidate next-paper framing

A possible future paper line is:

> **From Executive Friction to Effective Choice Landscapes: Distinguishing Transition Cost, Accessibility, and History-Dependent Reachability**

The academically useful contribution would not be to restate SRT ontology. It would be to separate and experimentally identify three often-confounded constructs:

```text
structural reachability
functional candidate admission / accessibility
effective transition friction / payability
```

and then ask a stronger longitudinal question:

```text
does mismatch merely change the current state,
or does it change which alternatives can become effective candidates next?
```

The paper should be allowed to fail if standard recurrent / active-inference models explain the full result under matched complexity and held-out testing.

## 19. Minimal retained conclusions

2. **Do not retroactively retire the published agent-level depression claim.** Retire only the overstrong neural reading that equates policy-level low friction with one long-dwell neural state.

4. **NCT energy is not `Psi_f`.** Structural control-energy estimates are domain-specific comparators, not cross-modal executive friction or actual metabolic payment.
5. **Accessibility is pre-execution candidate admission, not realized transition probability.** This makes it experimentally separable from transition friction conditional on admission.
6. **Represented option != effective candidate.** This creates a testable split between payability failure and accessibility contraction.
7. **High transition activity != high repertoire.** A system can change frequently while remaining trapped in a narrow subspace.
8. **State escape != landscape revision != generative reselectability.** The latter remains a stronger P2/P3 criterion.
