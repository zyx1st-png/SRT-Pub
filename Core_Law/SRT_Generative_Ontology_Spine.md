## 3. Primitive Selection — one event, multiple constitutive readings

Selection is upstream of a completed selector:

```text
Selection != a pre-existing subject choosing from a completed menu.
```

Selection is also **finite-position-indexed**. Finite positionality is a constraint on every Selection, not a separate post-One `Position` rung.

At the primitive generative level:

```text
Selection
= actual differentiation:
  a determinate actuality becomes manifest / operative
  while what is not manifest in the same way is relatively backgrounded.
```

Therefore:

```text
actual differentiation;
manifestation / relative backgrounding;
event-level / primitive verticality
```

are constitutive readings of the **same Selection event**. Do not read them as:

```text
Selection
-> manifestation
-> backgrounding
-> verticality
```

as if each arrow named a new universal operation.

### 3.1 Verticality reading

The structural reading of selective differentiation is non-flatness:

```text
what becomes manifest / operative
and what remains relatively backgrounded
are not equivalent with respect to the realized Selection event.
```

Selection may thereby alter later selectability, but this later effect must not be confused with making history or persistence part of the primitive event definition.

### 3.2 Guards

```text
manifestation != permanent existence;
backgrounding != annihilation;
Selection != conscious choice;
finite position != formed One;
finite position != perspective;
finite position != subjecthood;
verticality != passive historical sediment;
path dependence != verticality by definition;
Selection occurrence != anchoring persistence automatically.
```

Domain mechanisms may realize parts of this structure; no single domain mechanism is the universal definition.

### 3.3 Selection under formed constraints

After a One / Selection-position has formed, later Selection can be conditioned by that formed organization.

For routing purposes:

> **Selection under formed One / Selection-position constraints remains the same primitive kind of Selection; this spine introduces no second universal operation.**

This is a type-continuity rule, not a claim that every Selection already presupposes a One, and not a new formal symbol or operator.

```text
-> later Selection may therefore proceed under changed conditions.
```

```text
Selection / active differentiation
= primitive generative-operation side;

history / writeback
= retained-result / condition side when retention is established.
```

Therefore:

```text
history may condition later regenerative formation;
historical differences may become load-bearing for continuation;

but

history != source of Selection;
history != source of verticality by itself;
primitive Selection != durable history by definition;
history-bearing != perspective automatically;
history-to-reconstitution != Bearer automatically.
```

This spine does not settle the exact relation between current L0 irreversibility and P0 occurrence / anchoring-persistence distinctions. In particular, it does not infer from primitive Selection alone that every event automatically yields durable, localized or recurrently effective downstream history.

## 7. Bearer — post-One typed prospective standing, P + E retained

Bearer is **not** part of the universal formation route from non-flat openness to One.

It is a separately gated prospective standing relation for an already formed One / Selection-position.

Current canonical route remains exactly:

```text
formed One / Selection-position + P + E -> Bearer.
```

where:

```text
P prospective self-indexing
= future branches differ by what this continuing One itself may
  undergo / preserve / lose / enable / become unable to continue;

E same-One prospective exposure
= at least some such potential consequences genuinely expose the same One
  and cannot be completely reassigned while claiming the same Bearer relation.
```

### 7.1 P without E

Self-relevant prediction, regulation or viability sensitivity can occur while the actual burden remains externalizable.

```text
P alone != Bearer.
```

### 7.2 E without P

A One can be causally exposed or retrospectively altered without prospectively differentiating branches by what it itself may undergo.

```text
E alone != Bearer.
```

### 7.3 E consistency / non-outsourcing test

The current non-outsourcing counterfactual remains a **consistency / exclusion test** for an E attribution:

```text
if a consequence burden can be completely reassigned to another unit
while the claimed Bearer relation is said to remain unchanged,
E has not been established for the original One.
```

This test does **not** by itself provide a complete independently applicable positive admission procedure for E.

Therefore:

```text
E as semantic relation in the current P+E Bearer route = RETAINED;
current non-outsourcing counterfactual = consistency / exclusion test;
independently applicable positive E admission / establishment criterion = OPEN.
```

### 7.4 Concern / Agency non-nesting guard

This spine does not license either universal implication:

```text

Concern / 关切 -> Bearer.
```

Nor does it license a universal ladder:

Concern remains under its current local owner / terminology until its later owner-level reconciliation; agency remains separately gated downstream work.
