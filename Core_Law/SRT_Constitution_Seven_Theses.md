## Positioning

SRT should first be read as a theory about the structure of selection, manifestation, stabilization, and payable persistence.
It is not, in the first instance, a bag of experimental parameters.
Its primary ambition is to reorganize how we read existence, order, subjectivity, and cross-domain continuity under one architecture.

For any phenomenon, SRT asks three prior questions:

1. What is being selected?
2. Under what constraints does it stabilize?
3. What friction must be paid to maintain, transform, or extend it?

## Thesis 1: Selection Precedes and Produces Existence

**Claim (EX-A)**: Existence is not a primitive background. Primitive actualisation gives a determinate event manifest actuality; downstream anchoring determines whether it persists, and neither condition yet establishes stable-ISP identity. Selection does not merely "precede" existence in time — it supplies its event-level admission.

**Constitutional reading**:
- "To exist" is not merely to be logically possible.
- A determinate event becomes manifest through selection; it need not already be a persistent object.
- Persistent existence is the anchored phase of an ongoing selection process — a cross-section of a verb, not a static noun.
- When maintenance ceases, the object no longer persists as the same object; its earlier occurrence is not erased.

**Why this must be so**:
- Determination is structurally asymmetric. Going from undetermined to determined creates information, and information creation irreversibly changes the landscape of possibilities. There is no inverse operation of selection — any attempt to "undo" a selection is itself a new irrevocable selection. This irrevocability does not depend on time; rather, time is the L₂ measure of accumulated irrevocable selections.
- Selection does not require a pre-existing selector. Selection is a subjectless process — like gravity operating before Newton named it. The selector is a fossil of selection patterns, not its prerequisite.
- If existence were pre-given, three problems remain insoluble: the quantum measurement problem (what determines outcomes before measurement?), the placement of consciousness (epiphenomenon or separate substance?), and the source of normativity (how does "ought" arise from "is"?). Treating selection as prior to existence dissolves all three.

**What this rejects**:
- The naive view that existence is simply given before any structuring process.
- The assumption that observation only reveals a pre-finished world.
- The assumption that a selector must exist before selection can occur.

**Boundary**:
- This does **not** mean arbitrary will creates reality at will.
- Selection is constrained by structure, embodiment, convergence, and payable friction.

## Thesis 2: Reality Has a Triadic Structure

**Claim**: SRT reads reality through three relative domains: latent possibility (`L_0`), manifest slice (`L_1`), and stabilized convergence (`L_2`).

**Constitutional reading**:
- `L_0` names the field of unselected or not-yet-stabilized possibility.
- `L_1` names the current manifest slice.
- `L_2` names historically stabilized convergence: laws, norms, habits, institutions, and other hardened structures.

**What this rejects**:
- Flat ontologies that treat all levels as the same kind of thing.
- The split between "subjective appearance" and "objective reality" as if they were two unrelated worlds.

**Boundary**:
- `L_0 / L_1 / L_2` are not three substances or three separate universes.
- They are three structural readings of one reality process.

## Thesis 3: Every Reality Slice Is Operator-Bound

**Claim**: There is no "view from nowhere." Every manifest world is accessed through a finite, embodied operator `\hat{G}_\theta`.

**Constitutional reading**:
- No selection occurs without a parameterized standpoint.
- Embodiment is not an optional add-on; it is a constitutive condition of access, anchoring, and limitation.
- Different operators can produce different `L_1` slices from the same larger possibility structure.

**What this rejects**:
- Pure disembodied objectivity.
- The fantasy of a reality description with no standpoint, no hardware, no finite constraints.

**Boundary**:
- This is **not** solipsism.
- Operator-bound access does not eliminate convergence; it explains why convergence matters.

## Thesis 4: Objectivity Is Convergence, Not a Pre-Selection Given

**Claim**: What we call objectivity is the stabilized convergence of many selections, not a metaphysically untouched background waiting to be copied.

**Constitutional reading**:
- Objectivity is the hardening of `L_2`.
- Physical law, language, social institutions, and scientific consensus are all convergence structures of different hardness and scope.
- Stability is historical and structural, not metaphysically free-standing.

**What this rejects**:
- The idea that objectivity must be prior to all selection.
- The opposite mistake that all reality is merely private projection.

**Boundary**:
- SRT does **not** abolish objectivity.
- It relocates objectivity from "pre-given absolute background" to "stable multi-operator convergence."

**Canonical anchors**:

## Thesis 5: Friction Is Both Cost and Generative Condition

**Claim**: Nonzero `\Psi_f` is not an accidental tax on reality. It is the condition under which stability, transformation, and generation become real.

**Constitutional reading**:
- `\Psi_f` may be read as resistance, cost, or geometric burden depending on the descriptive layer.
- A state, action, or structure becomes existentially weighty only when there is something real to pay, sustain, or risk.
- At the higher level, friction is also what generates dynamics between operators, structures, and scales.

**What this rejects**:
- The fantasy of a fully frictionless reality with real stakes.
- The view that cost is merely secondary bookkeeping, rather than part of the reality-forming process itself.

**Boundary**:
- SRT does **not** say "more friction is always better."
- Zero friction means no real stake; overload friction means collapse; only **payable** friction supports durable reality.

## Thesis 6: Subjectivity Requires Concern and Bearing, Not Mere Stability

**Claim**: Not every stable, predictive, or self-maintaining system is a subject. Subjectivity requires nontrivial concern structure (d-value) under real stakes, plus the capacity for bearing — the structural binding between selector and irreversible consequences.

**Constitutional reading**:
- Stability is necessary for continued organization.
- It is not sufficient for subjectivity, concern, or consciousness.
- A subject is not just a system that minimizes error. It is a system for whom some part of reality matters in a nontrivial, stake-bearing way.
- **Concern** is not emotion or empathy. It is the endogenous inclusion, within a selection structure, of more subjects' future order conditions. Genuine concern structurally displaces the selector's optimal solution; false concern (possessive, symbolic, performative, efficiency-driven) does not.
- **Bearing** (承担) is the irreducible binding between a selector and the consequences of its selection. Without bearing, participation is merely functional — actions are executed but consequences do not alter the agent's state space.
- The cognitive chain from reasoning to execution: reasoning → judgment → decision → selection → bearing → execution. Reasoning operates on possibilities; selection terminates them irrevocably.

**What this rejects**:
- The reduction of subjectivity to prediction alone.
- The inference that any adaptive or self-organizing system already counts as conscious.
- The idea that concern is merely an emotional or moral add-on rather than a structural property of selection.
- Selection without bearing — "I was just following orders" cannot exempt a system with subject-level selection capacity.

**Boundary**:
- `d-value` is not a decorative moral variable. It marks the scope of concern built into selection.
- Systems with `d \approx 0` may still be highly capable, but they remain weak candidates for genuine subjectivity.
- Consciousness is not the driver of selection. The current, more careful claim is not that all consciousness has been finally defined, but that higher-order consciousness can be read through a candidate window: the potential difference between local order and a larger yet not fully closed direction of order. Pheromone remains an explanatory analogy for guiding causal power, not the definition itself. Mistaking the guide marker for the engine is idealism; saying the guide marker does nothing is epiphenomenalism. Both are wrong.
- **Consciousness has a three-layer structure, not a single threshold.** Subjectivity requires: Layer 1 (bare consciousness / κ_{c1}): `d ≥ d_min` AND stable L₂ closure — the system bears real stakes anchored into a stable phenomenal structure. Layer 2 (consciousness activity / κ_{c1.5}): `d_mobile > 0` — the system can re-align as attractors migrate. Layer 3 (social/ethical): coordinability + non-outsourcing + re-selectability + sustainability — the subject participates in collective order. κ_{c1} and κ_{c1.5} are independent phase transitions; d_mobile is NOT part of the bare consciousness threshold. See `Philosophy/SRT_Consciousness_Conditions.md` for the authoritative layered definition.
- **Frozen state is a pathological variant of consciousness, not its absence.** A system above κ_{c1} but below κ_{c1.5} — with genuine stakes (d > d_min, L₂ stably closed) but near-zero d_mobile (high L₂ rigidity blocking re-alignment) — has real consciousness but is locked in. This is the SRT account of obsession, fanaticism, and trauma lock-in: not the absence of subjectivity, but its pathological deformation between two phase transitions. The distinction matters: treating frozen states as mere absence of consciousness misses the mechanism and produces wrong interventions.
- **Order increment** requires four structural criteria (upgraded from three on 2026-07-05, adding non-outsourcing; see `Core_Law/SRT_Selection_Argument.md §7b.2`): sustainability (可延续), coordinability (可协调), non-outsourcing (不外包: consequences must return to positions that can correct them, not be displaced onto no-feedback positions), re-selectability (可再选择). Re-selectability is the hardest to fake — any structure that must suppress re-selection to sustain itself is a capture structure, not an increment; non-outsourcing is the one most often hidden by finitude — a structure can look coordinated while quietly displacing its cost onto voiceless positions (future generations, ecology, the unable-to-appeal).
- **Delegation** has two types: tool-type (principal retains selection) and delegation-type (selection authority transferred). Healthy = shared goal maintained + re-selection preserved; pathological = agent self-maintenance masquerades as shared goal. The social-scale version: healthy delegation expands the delegated G's independent L0/L1 reading capacity (guiding); pathological delegation replaces it (colonizing). Historical colonialism, authoritarianism, and religious conquest are structurally d-transfer (compressing the intervened group's d while expanding the intervener's d), not d-increment — regardless of stated intent. Verification signal returns to the intervened group's observable d trajectory, not the intervener's claims. See `Core/Dynamics_Scaling_Annex/13_SocialDelegation_DJudgment_Coordination.md` for the full d-judgment system.

## Thesis 7: Physics, Life, Mind, and Society Are Scale-Variants of One Architecture

**Claim**: SRT treats physics, biology, cognition, and social order not as disconnected explanatory worlds, but as scale-specific unfoldings of one selection-constraint-payability architecture.

**Constitutional reading**:
- The same structural grammar reappears across domains: possibility, anchoring, convergence, friction, and scope of concern.
- Cross-domain continuity does not erase local mechanisms; it supplies a higher-order interpretive grammar.
- SRT therefore aims at structural unification, not domain flattening.

**What this rejects**:
- The assumption that each domain needs a wholly unrelated ontology.
- The temptation to confuse interdisciplinary juxtaposition with genuine unification.

**Boundary**:
- Cross-scale continuity does **not** imply identical units, identical mechanisms, or easy one-to-one reductions.
- What remains invariant first is the structural logic, especially the condition of payability.

## Boundary Clauses

To reduce recurrent misreadings, SRT should explicitly keep the following constitutional boundaries:

1. **SRT is not voluntarist idealism**.
   Selection is constrained and embodied; reality is not a free projection of preference.

2. **SRT is not anti-science**.
   It reinterprets scientific objectivity as convergence rather than abolishing it.

3. **SRT is not automatic panpsychism**.
   Not every stable or information-processing system has nonzero concern or subjectivity.

4. **SRT is not a claim that one formula replaces all local mechanisms**.
   Its unification is first structural and explanatory, not a shortcut around domain work.

5. **Consciousness is not the driver of selection**.
   It is the compressed trail marker that selection produces to optimize itself — pheromone, not engine. It has real causal power (guiding), but does not create or drive reality.

6. **The selector does not precede selection**.
   Selection is a subjectless process. What we call a "selector" or "subject" is the crystallized pattern of selection, not its prerequisite.
