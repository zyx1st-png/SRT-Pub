## §0. Formation order

The canonical order is:

```text
subjectless Selection
-> event-level active verticalization
-> recurrent localized vertical organization
-> One / Selection-position
-> stronger downstream standing / bearing / subject relations where separately paid.
```

This order preserves the L0 rule that Selection does not require a pre-existing chooser. `One` is therefore not a hidden agent standing behind Selection and is not the cause required to start Selection.

---

## §1. Def-OF-1 — Active vertical organization

**Active vertical organization** is the non-flat organization generated / regenerated through Selection whereby some possibilities become manifest or operative while others are relatively backgrounded, and that differentiated organization can condition later selectability.

The minimum semantic commitments are:

```text
verticality is Selection-generated / regenerated;
verticality is not defined as passive historical sediment;
manifestation and relative backgrounding are structurally linked;
history may condition later vertical organization but is not its sole ontological source.
```

A one-shot Selection event may produce **event-level verticalization**. That alone does not establish a continuing One.

**Boundary**: neural lateral inhibition, attractor dynamics, path dependence, hysteresis, predictive processing, or other domain mechanisms may realize or approximate parts of this architecture; none is the universal definition of verticality here.

---

## §2. Def-OF-2 — One at D4a strength

`One` is a **localized, lineage-relative, processual formed unity** whose diachronic continuation is constituted through Selection-mediated recurrent reconstitution.

This definition is intentionally thinner than Stable ISP and intentionally stronger than one-shot verticalization.

A minimum One-formation sequence is:

```text
local vertical organization V_t
-> V_t materially enters later Selection conditions
-> later Selection reconstitutes a successor operative local organization V_(t+1)
-> V_(t+1) stands in a lineage relation to V_t
   as continuation / modification / branching / termination
-> a renewed operative locus can again condition later Selection.
```

Compactly:

```text
result -> later condition -> renewed operative locus.
```

`One` is therefore not a static snapshot, a hidden identity token, or merely an observer-side grouping of similar states.

### Formation boundary

```text
event-level verticalization
!= One automatically.
```

One onset requires genuine lineage-relative reconstitution into a renewed operative locus. Stability after onset may be graded; this file adopts no scalar stability threshold.

## §3. Def-OF-3 — Selection-position

For a continuing One, `Selection-position_t` is the **time-local operative from-where** through which that continuing organization participates in later Selection at time `t`.

`One` and `Selection-position` therefore name distinct ontological aspects of the same continuing formation:

```text
continuation aspect:
what is continuing?
-> One

operative aspect at t:
from where does later Selection proceed?
-> Selection-position_t.
```

These are not observer-side perspectives on an otherwise neutral object. They are relational roles within the continuing selective organization.

### Position guard

Position is necessary but not sufficient for stronger standing:

```text
Selection-position
!= perspective-bearing subject-position automatically;
Selection-position
!= Bearer automatically;
Selection-position
!= first-person bearing automatically.
```

The existence of a formed operative `from-where` does not by itself establish phenomenality, consciousness, self-model, non-outsourcable stake, or mature cognition.

## §4. History / verticality typing

History and writeback can be load-bearing for lineage continuation without becoming the ontological source of verticality.

Use the following typing:

```text
Selection
-> generates / regenerates vertical organization;

history / writeback
-> can alter later Selection conditions and reconstitution;

recurrent lineage-relative reconstitution
-> supports One continuation;

history storage location by itself
-> does not define One.
```

Historical effects may remain distributed. The relevant question is not primarily `where is the history stored?`, but which historical differences are load-bearing for reconstitution of which lineage and scale.

This section does not revive a history-localization criterion and does not define One as passive accumulation of prior traces.

Bearer remains a stronger downstream problem concerning which consequences become history of an already admitted formation and materially alter its later reconstitution / future selectability. Mere feedback, state change, or consequence return is not made sufficient here.

### Subject-position

`Core_Law/SRT_Individuation.md` may model stronger subject-position transitions. Its `σ_{sr}` family and threshold coordinates are downstream model variables and do not define active vertical formation, One, or Selection-position.

### Collective standing

Relation-level organization may be tested for One-like lineage continuation, but `Core_Law/SRT_Collective_Selection.md T-COLL-1` remains a separate gate for collective Stable-ISP standing. Higher-order One language must not bypass that gate.
