### Boundary

- Do **not** reduce reselectability to current reward, long-term reward, entropy, option count, or generic robustness.
- Do **not** identify toy `pre_div` with canonical d-value.
- Do **not** identify re-sampling capacity with canonical T_dir.
- Do **not** claim the toy wedge validates SRT ontology or morality.
- Do **not** infer equal moral status for all objects.
- Do **not** use this route to solve P0-04, closure-boundary, or externalization/X.
- Treat the toy wedge as dynamics-half evidence only: current performance and future reselectability can diverge.

### Safe answer pattern

When answering deep questions in this route, keep three layers separate:

1. **Ontology**: objecthood as maintained consequence-bearing reselectable closure.
2. **Dynamics**: reward / performance can diverge from future reselectability / robustness.
3. **Morality / governance**: moral pressure opens when one position's selection compresses another position's reselectability, but X/externalization remains under-defined.

### Short routing note

> SRT's reselectability line should be read as a meta-standard: not a claim that more options are always better, but an audit of whether a system's current success preserves or destroys the conditions under which relevant positions can continue to reselect.
