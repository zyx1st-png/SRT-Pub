# SourceCard: Levin–Wen string-net emergence and SRT objectification pressure

## 1. One-line summary

Levin and Wen provide explicit local-bosonic lattice models in which string-net-condensed phases support emergent gauge bosons and fermions, giving SRT a strong physics negative control: stable, re-identifiable object-like excitations can arise from collective organization without thereby establishing a bearer, selector, subject, or experiencer.

## 2. Primary-source boundary

The material package is anchored in three primary papers:

```text
Michael A. Levin & Xiao-Gang Wen
String-net condensation: A physical mechanism for topological phases
Physical Review B 71, 045110 (2005)
DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevB.71.045110

Michael Levin & Xiao-Gang Wen
Colloquium: Photons and electrons as emergent phenomena
Reviews of Modern Physics 77, 871 (2005)
DOI: 10.1103/RevModPhys.77.871

Michael Levin & Xiao-Gang Wen
Quantum ether: Photons and electrons from a rotor model
Physical Review B 73, 035122 (2006)
DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevB.73.035122
```

The PRB 2005 paper constructs exactly soluble string-net models and states that in three dimensions string-net condensation can give rise to emergent gauge bosons and emergent fermions. The RMP 2005 colloquium develops the broader theoretical possibility that photons, electrons and other elementary particles might be collective excitations of a deeper string-net-condensed structure. The 2006 rotor-model paper gives an explicit purely bosonic three-dimensional cubic-lattice model whose low-energy excitations behave as massless U(1) gauge bosons and massless Dirac fermions.

These are theoretical model results. They do **not** establish that the actual Standard Model vacuum is a string-net condensate, that spacetime is thereby emergent, or that SRT is physically validated.

## 3. Stable source-level results

### 3.1 Object-like excitations can be collective

The papers support the theoretical possibility that entities which appear particle-like at low energy need not correspond to elementary microscopic constituents. In the string-net picture, the relevant low-energy identities are collective excitations of an ordered/entangled phase.

Safe compression:

```text
microscopic local bosonic degrees
+ collective string-net organization
-> emergent low-energy excitation types
```

This supports a general emergence lesson but not a universal ontology.

### 3.2 Gauge-boson and fermion identity is phase-relative

The physical identity of an excitation depends on the organization of the surrounding phase and the allowed transformations/excitation sectors. The source therefore provides a clean example in which object identity cannot be read simply from a small localized constituent considered in isolation.

Safe abstract lesson:

```text
object-like identity
may depend on
local excitation + global ordered regime + allowed transformations
```

Do not translate this directly into SRT bearer language. The string-net phase is a physical realization context, not an SRT bearer by definition.

### 3.3 Emergence does not imply a unique microscopic inverse

The emergence framing explicitly shifts explanatory interest from exact microscopic details toward a general mechanism and low-energy phase structure. Multiple microscopic realizations can instantiate the same effective phase/organization class, so ordinary coarse-graining is naturally many-to-one rather than a guaranteed invertible map.

This is useful pressure against any unqualified use of:

```text
Lambda^{-1}
```

for generic cross-scale coarse-graining. It supports the current SRT governance direction in which strict conjugacy is reserved for declared invertible representation changes and ordinary coarse-graining uses approximate structural compatibility instead.

## 4. What the source does not show

Do not infer:

```text
actual electrons in our universe are proven string-net endpoints
our vacuum is proven to be a string-net condensate
string-net condensation proves emergent spacetime
collective excitation = SRT selection event
string-net phase = SRT bearer
objectification = agency
emergence = consciousness
Wen/Levin proves selection-first ontology
```

The papers establish theoretical possibility and concrete model mechanisms, not the metaphysical conclusions above.

## 5. SRT value: a negative control rather than a confirmation source

The strongest SRT use is negative-control pressure:

```text
stable objectification
can occur
without demonstrated agency, stake, consequence-bearing or phenomenality
```

Therefore SRT should not use "stable organization" or "emergent object" as sufficient evidence for a selector, subject or experiencer.

This yields the guard:

```text
objectification != agency
individuation != subjecthood
structural emergence != Real Choice Moment
```

## 6. Thin cross-domain objectification grammar

The source motivates, but does not itself prove across domains, a thin comparison grammar:

```text
differentiation
-> constraint
-> individuation
-> stabilized invariance
-> re-identifiability
```

This grammar may be used to compare particle-like excitations, biological individuals, social objects and concepts only at the level of structural roles. It does not imply shared mechanism, unit, stake, consciousness, or phenomenology.

## 7. Identity pressure

The string-net case supports replacing a naive identity picture:

```text
same object = same microscopic state
```

with a weaker domain-relative question:

```text
which transformations preserve the identity-relevant invariants of this object in this regime?
```

SRT-side hardening should therefore prefer a domain/bearer-indexed invariant family over a universal topological metric. Topological invariants are an important physical realization, not automatically the identity metric for persons, organisms, institutions or concepts.

## 8. Cross-scale pressure

The source supports the following methodological direction already present in SRT scaling governance:

```text
cross-scale comparison
should preserve declared structural roles / observables
not assume entity identity or invertibility
```

Candidate safe wording:

```text
role-preserving structural correspondence
!= universal scale isomorphism
```

No new canonical scaling theorem is created by this material card.

```text

```

The source directly supports only the bottom negative-control pressure: object-like identity can be generated without evidence for the higher roles. The transitions above are SRT-side theory work and are recorded separately in PH-IND04.

## 10. Final evidence verdict

**Assimilation value: very high.**

Not because Levin–Wen validates SRT, but because it prevents SRT from overusing "emergence" and forces a sharper question:

> If objecthood and stable identity can arise without agency, what additional structure must appear before consequences become the history of a continuing bearer, before that bearer becomes a selector-position, and before any subjectivity or phenomenality claim is licensed?
