# SourceCard: Essentia / Association as causation

## 1. One-line summary

Stephen Jarosek argues that association is an irreducible principle underlying coherence, causation, emergence, and meaning across physics, biology, mind, and culture; the essay treats association as the connective tissue by which potential becomes form and form becomes meaning.

## 2. Core claims of source

- The article proposes association as a third framing beyond upward causation from parts and downward causation from wholes.
- It claims physicalist / reductionist models cannot adequately explain how organisms, minds, and cultures persist against entropy.
- It links association to multiple domains: Peircean semiotics, von Uexküll, Rovelli relational quantum mechanics, Kochen-Specker contextuality, neural plasticity, Michael Levin / cellular intelligence, square-cube scaling, and Buddhist `śūnyatā`.
- It frames organisms as active association-builders that determine what is salient, ignorable, or actionable.
- It aligns the proposal with Bernardo Kastrup-style idealism / dual-aspect monism and describes reality as a web of meaningful relations across scales.
- It ends by calling association an irreducible axiomatic principle: not empirically falsifiable in the usual sense, but foundational to testing and interpretation.
