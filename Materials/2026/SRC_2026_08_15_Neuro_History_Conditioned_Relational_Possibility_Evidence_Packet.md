# SourceCard — History-conditioned relational possibility: primary evidence packet

## 0. Scope

This packet verifies the primary literature used by `NEURAL34` to support one bounded cross-paradigm claim:

> **Prior co-participation, statistical regularity or plastic history can change later coordination, reactivation or plastic response without requiring a continuously explicit firing-rate code.**

It does **not** assert that the seven paradigms below share one biological mechanism or one timescale. Their commonality is a functional pattern: **past history changes later transition / coordination dispositions**.

## 1. Primary sources

### A. Ji & Wilson 2007 — cross-region experience-specific replay

- Daoyun Ji & Matthew A. Wilson.
- *Coordinated memory replay in the visual cortex and hippocampus during sleep*.
- **Nature Neuroscience** 10, 100–107 (2007).
- DOI: `10.1038/nn1825`.
- Species / method: rat, simultaneous visual-cortex and hippocampal multiunit recording, `PRE sleep -> RUN -> POST sleep`.

Verified source-level pressure:

```text
RUN-high visual-cortex / hippocampal cell-pair correlations
-> high POST correlation
but not equivalent PRE correlation;
awake multicell sequences were replayed during POST sleep;
cortical and hippocampal replay events were coordinated around the same experience.
```

Boundary:

```text
experience-specific relation change
!= direct synaptic edge proved
!= unique consolidation mechanism
```

### B. Miyawaki & Mizuseki 2022 — inter-regional coactivation develops after conditioning

- Hiroyuki Miyawaki & Kenji Mizuseki.
- *De novo inter-regional coactivations of preconfigured local ensembles support memory*.
- **Nature Communications** 13, 1272 (2022).
- DOI: `10.1038/s41467-022-28929-x`.
- Species / method: rat, multi-regional electrophysiology across BLA, vCA1 and prelimbic cortex during fear conditioning and sleep.

Verified source-level pressure:

```text
some local ensembles were configured before acquisition;
inter-regional coactivation was enhanced / developed after conditioning;
post-acquisition coactivation occurred during brief fast oscillatory events;
the coordinated ensemble pattern reappeared during retrieval.
```

Boundary:

```text
local preconfiguration
+ experience-dependent cross-region coactivation
!= all relations created from zero by learning
```

### C. Abbaspoor, Aljishi & Hoffman 2026 — experience reorganizes content-specific memory traces

- Saman Abbaspoor, Ayman Aljishi & Kari L. Hoffman.
- *Experience reorganizes content-specific memory traces in macaques*.
- **Nature Neuroscience** (2026).
- Published: 29 July 2026.
- DOI: `10.1038/s41593-026-02357-2`.
- Species / method: freely moving macaques; hippocampal and associated-region ensemble activity for new, recent and old item-sequence memories and subsequent sleep.

Verified source-level pressure:

```text
memory age / experience was associated with reorganization of content-specific assembly interactions;
older / more experienced assemblies showed stronger or more stable network interaction and sleep reactivation patterns;
experience-related coupling effects were not reducible to simple assembly-membership similarity in the reported controls.
```

Boundary:

```text
cross-sectional memory-age comparison
!= longitudinal proof that the same pair acquired K from a matched pre-learning baseline
```

### D. Panichello et al. 2024 — mnemonic information in connection structure during low-rate Off states

- Matthew F. Panichello, Donatas Jonikaitis, Yu Jin Oh, Shude Zhu, Ethan B. Trepka & Tirin Moore.
- *Intermittent rate coding and cue-specific ensembles support working memory*.
- **Nature** 636, 422–429 (2024).
- DOI: `10.1038/s41586-024-08139-9`.
- Species / method: macaque, large simultaneous lateral-prefrontal neuronal populations during working memory.

Verified source-level pressure:

```text
single-trial mnemonic coding alternated between On and Off states;
during Off states firing approached spontaneous levels and cue selectivity in firing diminished;
cue-specific ensemble / functional-connection structure retained mnemonic information not captured by firing magnitude alone.
```

Boundary:

```text
matched observed firing
!= identical complete microphysical state;
functional connection estimate
!= direct synaptic connection proved
```

### E. Barbosa et al. 2020 — latent synchrony trace, reactivation and behavioral bias

- Joao Barbosa, Heike Stein, Rebecca L. Martinez, Adrià Galan-Gadea, Sihai Li, Josep Dalmau, Kirsten C. S. Adam, Josep Valls-Solé, Christos Constantinidis & Albert Compte.
- *Interplay between persistent activity and activity-silent dynamics in the prefrontal cortex underlies serial biases in working memory*.
- **Nature Neuroscience** 23, 1016–1024 (2020).
- DOI: `10.1038/s41593-020-0644-4`.
- Species / method: monkey and human electrophysiology plus human PFC TMS.

Verified source-level pressure:

```text
previous-trial stimulus information was not decodable from ordinary spiking between trials;
a latent trace was inferred from PFC spiking synchrony;
the trace reactivated before the next stimulus;
reactivation related to serial behavioral bias;
PFC TMS during the intertrial period altered subsequent bias.
```

Boundary:

```text
causal perturbation of a latent-state period
!= synchrony uniquely identified as the complete substrate
```

### F. Duncan, van Moorselaar & Theeuwes 2023 — neutral ping reveals selection-history priority

- Dock H. Duncan, Dirk van Moorselaar & Jan Theeuwes.
- *Pinging the brain to reveal the hidden attentional priority map using encephalography*.
- **Nature Communications** 14, 4749 (2023).
- DOI: `10.1038/s41467-023-40405-8`.
- Method: human EEG; spatial statistical learning / selection history plus task-irrelevant visual ping.

Verified source-level pressure:

```text
learned spatial priority was not reliably readable from ongoing intertrial EEG;
an identical task-irrelevant ping made the history-mediated priority map decodable;
reported controls addressed gaze, temporal confounds and immediate preceding-trial repetition.
```

Boundary:

```text
history-conditioned probe response
!= relation ontology
!= proof that all latent history is activity-silent
```

### G. Hamada et al. 2009 — human inter-regional metaplasticity

- Masashi Hamada, Ritsuko Hanajima, Yasuo Terao, Shingo Okabe, Setsu Nakatani-Enomoto, Tatsuya Furubayashi, Hideyuki Matsumoto, Yuichiro Shirota, Shinichi Ohminami & Yoshikazu Ugawa.
- *Primary motor cortical metaplasticity induced by priming over the supplementary motor area*.
- **The Journal of Physiology** 587, 4845–4862 (2009).
- DOI: `10.1113/jphysiol.2009.179101`.
- Method: human TMS; supplementary motor area priming followed by primary motor cortex quadripulse stimulation.

Verified source-level pressure:

```text
SMA priming altered the later M1 plastic response to QPS;
SMA priming did not itself change motor-evoked potential size;
the authors interpret the history dependence as human cortical metaplasticity / homeostatic plasticity.
```

This provides a particularly clean functional pattern:

\[
X_{observed}^{primed}\approx X_{observed}^{control}
\]

while the response to a later common induction differs.

Boundary:

```text
similar measured output
!= identical hidden node state;
metaplasticity
!= SRT L2
!= NEURAL34 K by definition
```

## 2. Cross-source time-scale stratification

The sources span different temporal and mechanistic regimes and must **not** be presented as one demonstrated mechanism:

| Regime | Representative evidence | What can be retained |
|---|---|---|
| fast / intertrial | Barbosa 2020; Panichello 2024 | latent or intermittent working-memory state can persist in interaction / connection structure beyond mean firing readout |
| learning-to-rest / consolidation | Ji & Wilson 2007; Miyawaki & Mizuseki 2022 | experience-specific coordinated relations can be enhanced or replayed after learning |
| days-to-weeks memory age | Abbaspoor et al. 2026 | experience / memory age is associated with reorganization and stabilization of higher-order assembly interactions |
| learned priority | Duncan et al. 2023 | statistical selection history can remain latent and be exposed by a neutral probe |
| metaplasticity | Hamada et al. 2009 | prior activity can alter future plastic response despite no change in the measured baseline output used in the protocol |

Therefore the evidence packet supports a **cross-scale functional analogy** only:

```text
history
-> altered later transition / coordination disposition
```

It does not establish:

```text
K_fast = K_slow;
one molecular substrate;
one oscillatory mechanism;
one universal memory mechanism.
```

## 3. What this packet supports for NEURAL34

### Supported synthesis pressure

```text
past history can alter later relation-specific response;
current mean activity need not exhaust the history-bearing state;
experience can change inter-regional / inter-assembly coordination beyond simple component availability;
neutral or common probes can reveal history-conditioned response differences;
prior activity can alter future plastic response without a persistent change in the measured baseline output.
```

### Still hypothesis-level

```text
all of these effects instantiate one K variable;
K is an ontologically independent relation;
K is stored outside component states;
ripple opens a universal gate onto K;
history is primarily stored in relational geometry.
```

## 4. Strongest safe compression

> **Across multiple paradigms and timescales, prior experience can change how a neural system later coordinates, reactivates or changes in response to a common opportunity or probe, even when the relevant history is not continuously explicit in mean firing. This supports treating history-conditioned future relation structure as a testable bridge-level property, while leaving its substrate and cross-scale unity open.**
