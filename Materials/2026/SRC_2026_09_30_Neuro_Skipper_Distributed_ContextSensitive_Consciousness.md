# SourceCard: Skipper et al. (2026), distributed / decentred / context-sensitive consciousness

## 1. One-line summary

Skipper et al. aggregate 579 consciousness-focused neuroimaging studies and report little evidence for an invariant cross-domain anatomical core; instead, corrected meta-analytic maps are broadly distributed, functional contributions vary by context, and coactivation / hypergraph analyses point to a shared network repertoire with context-dependent higher-order coalitions. For SRT, the strongest use is methodological rather than confirmatory: treat variable neural coalitions as candidate realizations of a more abstract organization, and test whether coalition geometry participates in predictive, causal and recursive recutting rather than equating any region, network or coalition with consciousness.

## 3. Source-owned claims verified from the abstract

The following are claims reported by the authors and should remain source-owned.

1. The analysis includes **579 consciousness-focused neuroimaging studies**.
2. Semantic clustering of titles and abstracts identified **seven domains**. The verified abstract / public metadata available in this pass did not expose the seven domain labels, so their names are **not transcribed or inferred here**.
3. Corrected meta-analytic maps showed **limited overlap** and no invariant cross-domain core.
4. The resulting maps occupied **18.0% of grey matter** while extending across **91.0% of brain regions**.
5. After controlling for reporting topography, distributed **cognitive**, **sensorimotor**, and **organismic-affective** maps reconstructed the residual consciousness landscape better than spatial surrogates.
6. Organismic-affective maps contributed most overall, while the relative functional contributions varied by domain.
7. Theory-associated regional loci added no additional meaningful prediction in the authors' analysis.
8. Information carried by the functional families was both **redundant** and **synergistic**.
9. Coactivation-network and hypergraph analyses revealed a **shared network repertoire** but **context-dependent higher-order coalitions**.
10. The authors interpret the package as evidence against an invariant neural core and in favor of a distributed, context-sensitive neurobiology of consciousness.

## 6. Redundancy / synergy guard

The authors report that the functional families carry both redundant and synergistic information.

Repository-safe use:

```text
synergy
= evidence that joint functional organization can contain predictive information
  not reducible to treating each family independently in the scoped analysis
```

Do not infer:

```text
statistical synergy = IIT Phi
statistical synergy = phenomenal unity
statistical synergy = Selection
statistical synergy = SRT whole-field proof
```

The source makes higher-order relational analyses empirically relevant; it does not identify the ontology of those relations.

## 9. Relation to major neighboring theories

### Global Neuronal Workspace

The source pressures a simplistic fixed anatomical workspace reading, but does not refute a functional broadcast / global-availability architecture.

Guard:

```text
distributed / context-dependent realization
!= GNW falsification
```

The SRT-side unresolved burden remains whether broad availability is sufficient to explain a difference becoming a structuring condition for later generation.

### Integrated Information Theory

The source's redundancy / synergy and higher-order organization are relevant to integration, but the reported information decomposition is not IIT's cause-effect structure and cannot be substituted for Phi.

The live distinction remains:

```text
how integrated is the current organization?
vs
why this coalition, now, and what does it change next?
```

### Active inference / predictive / latent-state models

These are strong absorbers of:
- context-dependent weighting;
- hierarchical control;
- changing state partitions;
- policy / precision changes;
- learned representational geometry.

Therefore:

```text
context-sensitive coalition formation
!= SRT-specific residual
```

Any SRT-facing increment must survive strong latent-state / generative-model baselines.

## 12. Main limits

1. This is a preprint.
2. This repository pass did not independently close-read the full methods and results.
3. Meta-analysis of consciousness-focused neuroimaging literature is not direct measurement of consciousness itself.
4. The corpus may carry task, reporting, contrast-selection, publication and domain-sampling structure.
5. Regional non-invariance does not establish causal necessity or sufficiency.
6. A context-dependent coalition can be an ordinary distributed computation.
7. Hypergraph structure does not establish phenomenality, Bearer standing, ownership or subjecthood.
8. Organismic-affective prominence does not identify a subject.
9. Theory-associated loci adding little prediction does not refute full mechanistic versions of GNW, IIT, recurrent-processing or predictive theories.
10. The source does not measure canonical SRT variables and should not be cited as direct evidence for Selection, Bearer, Psi_f, foreground_phen or Position reconstitution.
