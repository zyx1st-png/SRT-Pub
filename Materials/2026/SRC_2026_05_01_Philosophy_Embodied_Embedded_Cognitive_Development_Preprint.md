# SourceCard: Embodied and Embedded Cognitive Development

## 1. One-line summary

De Jonge-Hoekstra and Cox argue that cognitive development emerges from soft-assembled, interaction-dominant coordinative structures spanning brain, body, caregivers, and environment, and that developmental change is best studied through longitudinal dynamics, variability, cascades, and child-environment fit.

## 2. Core claims of source

The chapter presents an embodied and embedded account of cognitive development. Its strongest SRT-usable claims are:

1. cognition is not disembodied internal representation but a set of functional coordinative structures emerging in an open complex adaptive system;
2. these structures are soft-assembled, history-dependent, context-sensitive, and interaction-dominant;
3. developmental cascades link motor, language, social, and regulatory domains, so a change in one affordance field can reorganize other domains;
4. temporal variability is not mere measurement error; it can indicate flexibility, attractor strength, and impending transition;
5. co-regulation with caregivers and environmental rhythms can scaffold the emergence of self-regulation;
6. developmental difficulties should be read through child-environment fit, degeneracy, affordance availability, and rhythmic scaffolding, not as isolated internal deficits.

## 3. Evidence / method

This is a preprint chapter dated 2026-05-01. It is a synthesis / review rather than a new primary experiment. The chapter draws on coordination dynamics, ecological psychology, nonlinear time-series methods, dynamic field modeling, developmental cascade literature, language development, infant agency work, and developmental difficulties.

## 5. SRT relevance

SRT already says affordance emerges at the intersection of environment and embodied capacity. This chapter adds the developmental version:

```text
child's changing theta
  + caregiver / environment coupling
  + temporal variability
  + L2 trace from prior action
  -> developmental affordance field
  -> new coordinative structure
```

The useful SRT compression is:

```text
cognitive development = repeated reconfiguration of selectable action / attention / language fields
```

where learning is not merely internal content acquisition but the hardening of future selection rules through embodied and social loops.
