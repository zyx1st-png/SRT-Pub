# SourceCard: Carhart-Harris & Friston (2019), REBUS

## 1. One-line summary

REBUS proposes that psychedelic states relax the precision weighting of high-level priors / beliefs and thereby increase the influence of bottom-up and intrinsic information. This already pays the broad "constraint relaxation exposes normally suppressed internal material" explanatory job; SRT should inherit that mechanism family rather than relabel it as a unique Selection effect.

## 2. Core source-owned claims

1. REBUS integrates the free-energy / predictive-processing framework with the entropic-brain hypothesis.
2. The model proposes that psychedelics relax the precision of high-level priors or beliefs.
3. Reduced high-level precision is proposed to liberate bottom-up information flow, including intrinsic sources such as limbic signals.
4. The framework uses this relaxation to organize a broad range of psychedelic phenomena and therapeutic hypotheses.

### Source anchors

- Abstract — definition of REBUS and high-level prior relaxation.
- Main theoretical sections — hierarchical priors, precision weighting and intrinsic information flow.
- Therapeutic discussion — pathological overweighted priors as a proposed target of psychedelic relaxation.

## 3. Evidence / method

This is a peer-reviewed theoretical review. It synthesizes empirical psychedelic neuroscience but its central REBUS construct is a model-level interpretation, not a single direct measurement of "belief relaxation."

Appropriate use:

- mature-neighbor mechanism;
- strongest-baseline / guardrail for psychedelic-opening claims.

Inappropriate use:

- treating REBUS as experimentally proving all psychological interpretations of psychedelic experience.

## 4. Limits

The source does not imply that:

- more entropy or more candidate accessibility equals better Selection;
- psychedelic states are inherently more truthful;
- high-level prior relaxation guarantees stable reanchoring;
- altered self-model automatically means Bearer loss.

These distinctions align with, but do not prove, the repository's existing NEURAL35 guard `reopening != reselectability`.
