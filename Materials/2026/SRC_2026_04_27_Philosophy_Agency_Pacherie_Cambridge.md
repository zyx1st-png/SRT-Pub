# SourceCard: Elisabeth Pacherie, *Agency* \(Cambridge Elements in Philosophy of Mind\)

## 1. One-line summary

Pacherie's *Agency* offers a philosophy-of-mind account that moves from classical human intentional action to minimal agency, conscious agency, and shared agency, making it highly relevant for SRT's distinction between selection, action-readiness, concern, control, and subject-level agency.

## 2. Core claims of source

The publisher summary states that philosophers often use human intentional action as the paradigm case for agency, but that this makes it difficult to give positive accounts of more elementary forms of agency. The Element therefore proceeds from classical intentional agency to minimal forms of agency, then to conscious agency, and finally to joint/shared agency.

The accessible table of contents confirms four major sections:

1. Intentional Agency
2. Minimal Forms of Agency and Their Building Blocks
3. Conscious Agency
4. Shared Agency
