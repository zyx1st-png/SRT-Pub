# SourceCard: Todd — *Bayesian Descriptions are not Mechanisms*

## 1. One-line summary

Todd argues that a successful Bayesian description, algorithmic fit, or behavioural prediction does not by itself identify a mechanism. A mechanism claim requires a prospectively specified causal mapping from physical organisation to the nominated computational states and transitions, plus interventions capable of distinguishing that mapping from bounded rivals. When histories grouped into one candidate state respond differently because of timing, phase, contact or relational structure, the mapping is too coarse for the declared task and horizon.

For SRT, the central value is symmetrical pressure: a system can be described fluently in `selection`, `d`, `Psi_f` or `T_dir` language without thereby establishing that those terms identify indispensable causal organisation. Todd therefore supplies a governance hardening route, not positive evidence for SRT ontology.

## 3. Core source claims

### 3.1 Computational description, algorithm and physical implementation must be separated

Todd distinguishes three levels:

```text
computational-level Bayesian description
!= algorithmic approximate inference
!= physical implementation / mechanism
```

The same input-output behaviour may be produced by Bayesian inference, a heuristic, a control law, a recurrent network or a tuned dynamical system. Behavioural fit therefore underdetermines mechanism.

### 3.2 Realisation requires a causal state-transition mapping

Todd's realisation criterion treats an abstract computational state as an equivalence class over physical histories. A candidate physical system realises that state only when differences grouped together are irrelevant to the required abstract transition within the declared tolerance and horizon.

If histories assigned to the same candidate state differ in timing, relative phase, contact, field structure or another nominated relation and those differences change the intervention response, then:

```text
candidate state mapping
-> too coarse for the mechanism claim
```

The mapping must be enriched or abandoned for that task / scale / horizon.

### 3.3 More data on the same projection need not resolve observational equivalence

Todd explicitly argues that incompatible mechanistic partitions may fit the same behavioural or recorded-data projection. Additional fit on that same projection does not identify which causal mapping is realised when the competing mappings remain observationally equivalent there.

Mechanism discrimination therefore requires a new regime in which the nominated mappings make prospectively different predictions.

### 3.4 Interventions must discriminate mappings, not merely disrupt a common causal medium

A generic impairment after disrupting a component establishes causal relevance but not the identity of a Bayesian, coupling or other mechanism when all rivals predict impairment.

Todd's stronger requirement is a selective intervention whose predicted effect differs across mappings in sign, timing, dose-response, mediation pattern or state transition while preserving the ordinary task and declared sensorimotor channel as far as feasible.

### 3.5 Post-hoc rescue changes the hypothesis

A mapping that fails cannot be retrospectively repaired by adding variables, changing factorisation or redescribing the realiser and still count the revised account as the original prediction.

Safe reading:

```text
post-hoc richer mapping
= new hypothesis
!= rescue of the preregistered mechanism claim
```

### 3.6 Negative results are local, not family-wide

Todd repeatedly limits rejection to a declared mapping, task, scale, horizon, margin, budget and cost rule. Failure of one predictive-processing mapping does not eliminate all Bayesian models; failure of one coupling mapping does not eliminate all dynamical or relational accounts.

This localism is important for SRT governance because a failed bridge or discriminator should not be generalized into a universal ontology verdict.

### 3.7 Formal closure and representational size do not identify mechanism

Todd notes that Bayesian algorithms can be continuous, high-dimensional, nonparametric and representation-growing. Every specified model nevertheless has a reachable closure under its allowable dynamics. Formal closure or large representational capacity therefore does not by itself decide whether the mechanism is Bayesian, recurrent, coupled or hybrid.

## 4. Source-proposed empirical portfolio

Todd's preferred evidence is convergent rather than single-test:

```text
prospectively specified candidate mapping
+ independent physical / operational realiser
+ transfer
+ held-out intervention
+ residual / state-collision test
+ adaptation
+ physical realiser manipulation
-> bounded mechanism support or rejection
```

His resource-bounded coupling hypothesis adds a separate economy claim: on preregistered contact-rich tasks and budgets, future-relevant distinctions in organism-environment relations may make an intervention-predictive contribution beyond tested internal controller states. Todd states explicit conditions under which adaptive internal controllers would count against that local prediction.

This coupling hypothesis is the author's proposal. It is not SRT and is not adopted as an SRT mechanism.

## 5. Direct SRT relevance

### 5.1 Selection deletion: description is not mechanism

Current SRT subtraction already asks whether the word and primitive role of `selection` can be removed while preserving asymmetric constraints, reachability restriction, history dependence, cost and consequence return.

Todd adds a missing stronger condition:

```text
selection language fits the phenomenon
!= selection is mechanism-level indispensable
```

A future mechanism-level selection claim must therefore nominate:

```text
M_S = SRT-selection mapping
R_S = independently specified physical / operational realiser
M_K = bounded constraint / dynamics rival
I*  = held-out intervention not used to fit or choose the mappings
```

and require a prospective divergence:

```text
P(Y | do(I*), M_S) != P(Y | do(I*), M_K)
```

within declared margins.

If the rivals remain interventionally indistinguishable under the declared test, selection has not earned mechanism-level irreducibility there.

### 5.2 `d`, `Psi_f` and `T_dir`: proxy fit is not realisation

The same rule applies to bridge variables:

```text
neural / metabolic / behavioural variable correlates with an SRT quantity
!= physical realiser of that SRT quantity
```

A mechanism-level mapping requires the candidate realiser to carry the correct causal role and intervention signature. Generic correlation, predictive fit or shared impairment is insufficient.

This does not alter the canonical definitions of `d`, `Psi_f` or `T_dir`; it hardens the burden for empirical projection claims.

### 5.3 Matched-state and history-provenance design

Todd is especially useful when two histories or mechanisms are matched on current behaviour or another observable projection.

The correct next question is not whether more same-projection data improve fit. It is:

```text
which physically or relationally distinct histories were collapsed into the same candidate state?
```

and then:

```text
can a held-out intervention reveal different transitions?
```

This connects directly to the repository's matched-present-state / different-history designs while blocking a weak provenance claim. If the relevant history effect is fully mediated by present structure and the competing mapping predicts the same intervention response, provenance is not independently identified.

## 6. Relation to Bowler / NEURAL36

Bowler and Todd form a useful two-paper sequence:

```text
Bowler:
structured history can be sedimented into ordinary recurrent organisation
and can alter later strategy / generalization.

Todd:
matching outputs or fitting the same projection does not identify which
mechanism realises that organisation; discriminate mappings prospectively.
```

Together they imply:

```text
history matters
!= history provenance is irreducible

same current performance
!= same internal mechanism

same generalization readout
!= same mechanism

mechanism claim
-> requires held-out intervention where rivals diverge
```

This strengthens future healthy/lethal `L2` and Cycle-2 audits without itself creating an SRT discriminator.

## 9. Explicit non-claims

Do not write:

- Todd proves predictive processing is false;
- Bayesian models cannot be mechanisms;
- dynamical / coupling models are automatically mechanistic;
- SRT is mechanistic because it is not Bayesian;
- behavioural mismatch alone identifies a mechanism;
- any generic perturbation proves an SRT variable;
- formal closure distinguishes SRT from Bayesian or recurrent models;
- a failed mapping eliminates an entire theory family;
- Todd's resource-bounded coupling hypothesis is an SRT theorem or established result.
