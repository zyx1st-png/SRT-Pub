# SourceCard: Charles Borges, *Brain-Body-Life: Towards a panpsychist theory of embodied cognition*

## 1. One-line summary

Borges argues from anti-dualism and embodied cognition toward a process ontology in which organism and milieu co-emerge, stable individuals are temporary selective closures within ongoing relational dynamics, perspective and temporal retention arise with such organization, and attention further selects which processes become available to working memory; the paper then advances a panpsychist conclusion by treating perspective/intensity as degrees of consciousness, but it does not independently establish the transition from system-relative perspective to phenomenal experience.

## 3. Author's argumentative route

The paper's main route can be compressed as:

```text
anti-dualism
-> cognition cannot be grounded in an already-separated brain or disembodied mind
-> cognition occurs in embodied matter acting with a world
-> Damasio's homeostasis / affect line is retained but read through a post-adaptationist frame
-> Developmental Systems Theory: organism and milieu co-emerge through reciprocal constraint
-> form emerges through successive interactions and mutual selectivity
-> molar organization is a stop / selection produced by individuation
-> open systems exhibit momentary closures
-> closures establish boundary / perspective / temporal retention
-> attention selectively gates representations into working-memory availability
-> consciousness is interpreted as perspective/intensity in matter
-> panpsychist conclusion
```

The SRT-relevant parts of this route do not all have the same evidential status. The process/individuation claims are philosophical proposals; the final panpsychist transition is an additional thesis rather than a demonstrated consequence of the earlier steps.

## 4. Core source claims

### 4.1 Anti-dualist starting point

Borges rejects both a picture in which a pre-given material world is represented by a later mind and a picture in which a pre-given mind projects the real world. He argues that beginning from either already-separated pole preserves the dualist framework.

His preferred ground for cognition is not the material brain alone or the essence of a mind, but the emergence of both within embodied matter acting with an environment.

### 4.2 Damasio, homeostasis and the adaptationism pressure

Borges treats Damasio's homeostasis, emotion, feeling and somatic-marker account as an important anti-Cartesian move, but argues that it can be misread if the environment is treated as a pre-given external judge to which organisms merely adapt.

He therefore proposes reading the Damasian affect line through Developmental Systems Theory, emphasizing organism-milieu co-evolution, reciprocal affecting, open-system organization and emergence.

### 4.3 Form through successive interaction

Using Susan Oyama and DST, the paper rejects a blueprint picture in which form is imposed on passive matter. Form instead emerges through successive interactions, mutual selectivity, reactivity and constraint across multiple levels.

This is the first strong interface with SRT's anti-preobjectification orientation: the stable form is treated as an outcome of process rather than as the unquestioned starting object of explanation.

### 4.4 `molarity = stop / selection`

Using Deleuze/Guattari, Massumi and Simondon, Borges distinguishes molecular from molar modes of composition and writes that molarity is a `stop or selection`, a categorization resulting from individuation.

The important structure is:

```text
ongoing local relational dynamics
-> selective stabilization / stop
-> momentary organization
-> boundary
-> individuated whole
```

The paper does not provide a formal criterion for when such stabilization counts as a new individual.

### 4.5 Open system and momentary closure

The brain-body system is described as an open system in irreversible time in which `momentary closures` occur.

This offers useful language for a process account of boundaries: the individual is not necessarily a permanently pre-given substance; its boundary can be the historically sustained closure of a relational process.

### 4.6 Perspective and temporal retention

Borges then proposes that consciousness can be understood as a perspective of matter and that different kinds of consciousness differ through their capacity for contraction-retention of time.

Two distinct claims must be separated:

```text
organized closure -> system-relative perspective
```

and

```text
system-relative perspective -> phenomenal consciousness
```

The paper motivates the first much better than it establishes the second.

### 4.7 Attention as selective availability

Using Jesse Prinz's AIR theory, Borges treats attention as a change in processing that makes intermediate-level representations available to working memory. He uses this to distinguish a broad `blind intentionality` from a narrower phenomenal consciousness and to explain how some processes become explicit while many others remain backgrounded.

The paper's cognitive architecture can be compressed as:

```text
many ongoing perceptual / interaction processes
-> attentional selection
-> working-memory availability
-> identifiable / reflectively usable conscious content
```

This should not be read as proof that attention is necessary or sufficient for all consciousness; it records Borges's use of Prinz within his philosophical synthesis.

### 4.8 `The subject is a legion`

Borges argues that many perceptual/interaction processes operate beneath what enters working memory and describes the attentional gatekeeper as giving voice to some processes while muting others. His phrase `the subject is a legion` presents unity as an emergent result of selective integration rather than as an unquestioned primitive controller.

### 4.9 Selection of images and objectification

Through Bergson, the paper treats action, perception and conceptualization as selection of images in matter, moving from heterogeneous intensive flux toward homogeneous/discrete space in which fixed points, trajectories and objective/subjective positions can be articulated.

For SRT this is useful as a historical-philosophical analogue of the claim that objectification can be downstream of selective organization. It does not license the identity:

```text
SRT L0 = Bergsonian duration / intensive matter
```

### 4.10 Selection history reshapes the next environment

The conclusion links homeostasis to habit-memory, somatic markers to image-memory and attention to an intentional-attentional process, ending with the phrase that consciousness is its own `selected evolutionary environment`.

A safe structural reading is:

```text
past selective history
-> habit / memory / boundary / attentional bias
-> changes what differences become salient or actionable next
-> reshapes future path accessibility
```

## 5. Strongest SRT interfaces

### 5.1 Selective stabilization before stable individuality

The strongest interface is:

```text
ongoing relational process
-> selective stabilization
-> momentary closure
-> boundary
-> individuation
```

This independently converges with the current SRT individuation line that the selector/subject position need not precede selection.

SRT use should remain P3:

> Borges supplies process language for how an individuated closure may be described, not an empirical proof or canonical derivation of SRT subjecthood.

### 5.2 Closure is not yet bearer

Borges's closure concept is useful only if SRT keeps a stronger gate:

```text
momentary closure
!= bearer
```

A closure becomes a stronger bearer candidate only when SRT-side conditions such as consequence return, history-bearing writeback, boundary maintenance and change in future selectability are separately examined.

### 5.3 Perspective must be typed

The paper pressures SRT to distinguish:

```text
system-relative perspective
!= bearer-relative perspective
!= phenomenal perspective
```

A system can have inside/outside, viable/nonviable or reachable/unreachable asymmetries without this alone proving `what-it-is-like` phenomenality.

### 5.4 Temporal closure / retention

Borges/Bergson's contraction-retention language offers a useful philosophical background for bearer continuity:

```text
past consequence
-> retained in the same continuing organization
-> changes present parameters
-> changes future reachable paths
```

This suggests a P3 research question: bearer closure may be temporal as well as spatial.

### 5.5 Attention as a local realization of selective gating

Attention can be treated as one cognitive realization of selection-like gating, not as the definition of SRT selection.

Useful decomposition:

```text
bearer-relative occurrence
!= attentional access
!= working-memory availability
!= memory stabilization
!= reportable self-knowledge
```

### 5.6 Organism-environment co-constitution

The DST line is useful as a guard against modeling an organism as a finished chooser confronting a pre-given option menu. Selection processes can also alter the chooser's boundary, comparison scale and future environment.

## 6. Reverse correction / pressure on SRT

### 6.1 Do not repeat the paper's panpsychist jump

The paper's strongest unsupported transition is:

```text
selection / organization
-> perspective
-> consciousness
-> panpsychism
```

SRT should preserve:

```text
selection event
!= subjecthood
!= phenomenal consciousness
```

and, more specifically:

```text
perspective
!= experiencer

bearing candidate
!= phenomenality proof
```

### 6.2 `raw bearing -> L2 closure -> qualia` should not become a one-step mechanism claim

The paper's attention / working-memory / retention material suggests that several separable transitions may intervene between an event occurring for a bearer and its becoming identifiable/reportable conscious content.

Pressure decomposition:

```text
bearer-relative occurrence
-> possible phenomenal occurrence [open]
-> selective integration / attentional access
-> working-memory availability
-> temporal retention
-> memory anchoring / L2 stabilization
-> reportable / self-identifiable content
```

This is a pressure note, not a replacement theory and not evidence that AIR theory is universally correct.

### 6.3 Emergence must not hide the missing criterion

The paper repeatedly uses emergence, assemblage, intensity and chaos. SRT should not use these terms as substitutes for a discrimination rule. `Momentary closure` is useful only if later work asks what maintains the closure, what consequences close on it, and what history is written back.

## 7. Panpsychism quarantine

The following source claims should be recorded but not promoted:

1. consciousness is in matter `all the way down`;
2. `blind intentionality` is a broad form of consciousness;
3. perspective/intensity is sufficient to justify panpsychism;
4. deterministic chaos provides an ontological basis for consciousness;
5. Bergson's intensive matter can be identified with SRT `L0`;
6. the paper's critique of adaptationism is a sufficient characterization of contemporary evolutionary biology.

The paper itself acknowledges that panpsychist theory still requires further theoretical development and empirical evidence.

## 9. Bidirectional gain card

### New interface

- selective stabilization as a route into momentary closure;
- boundary as historically stabilized relation rather than unquestioned primitive;
- temporal retention as a possible ingredient in continuing bearer closure;
- attention as selective availability control inside a richer selection architecture;
- unity of subject as possible selective-integration result rather than primitive controller.

### Reverse correction

- perspective alone does not establish subjectivity;
- closure alone does not establish a bearer;
- bearer-relative consequence does not by itself derive phenomenality;
- attention / memory / report must not be collapsed into one consciousness variable;
- panpsychist language should remain quarantined from SRT subjecthood claims.

### SRT-specific remainder

The source leaves open the question SRT is positioned to sharpen:

> What transforms a temporary selective closure into a continuing bearer such that consequences close on the same unit, history rewrites that unit, and its future selectability is nontrivially changed?

That question belongs to P3 individuation/subjecthood hardening, not to P0/P1 canonical promotion.
