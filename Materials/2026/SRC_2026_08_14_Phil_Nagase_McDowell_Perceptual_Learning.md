# SourceCard: Nagase, *The Intimate Harmony Between the Intellect and Sensibility*

## 1. One-line summary

Nagase reconstructs McDowell's conceptualism around capacities rather than static perceptual contents: a capacity is conceptual insofar as it can be intelligently improved by exploiting systematic relations among relevant features; perceptual learning can therefore reshape grouping and salience before explicit deliberation, while language further enables patterns and inherited constraints to be de-contextualized, shared, criticized and revised within a space of reasons.

## 2. Source and bibliographic boundary

The supplied PDF identifies:

```text
Daniel Arvage Nagase
The Intimate Harmony Between the Intellect and Sensibility:
McDowell and Perceptual Learning
Principia 30(2): 279–305 (2026)
DOI 10.5007/1808-1711.2026.e103531
```

The paper is a philosophical argument about McDowell, perception, rationality, virtue and language. It uses examples from skilled action, perceptual learning, radiology, reversing goggles and bee navigation, but it does not itself report a new experiment and does not establish a low-level neural mechanism for perceptual reorganization.

## 3. Author's argumentative route

The paper's route can be compressed as:

```text
conceptualism should be analyzed through capacities rather than isolated contents
-> a capacity is systematically rational when it can be improved by exploiting interconnections among features relevant to its exercise
-> skilled action depends on sensitivity to situation-specific relevance
-> perception is organized rather than a passive aggregate of sensory inputs
-> perceptual learning can improve discrimination, grouping and salience
-> theoretical knowledge can reshape perceptual organization itself
-> virtue can be modeled as learned perception of action opportunities rather than post-perceptual computation between desire and reason
-> rationality is a matter of systematic integration among capacities and therefore comes in degrees
-> non-linguistic animals can possess local "islands" of systematic rationality
-> language de-contextualizes patterns, supports higher-order relations, transmission, joint attention and reflective revision
-> the space of reasons is an interpersonal practice of understanding, accountability and negotiating what matters
```

## 4. Core source claims

### 4.1 Capacity-centered conceptualism

Nagase shifts the conceptualism debate away from whether each perceptual content is explicitly conceptual. What matters is whether a capacity can be acquired, sustained and improved through an intelligent grasp of relations among the features involved in its exercise.

Source-level definition:

```text
systematically rational capacity
=
a capacity the subject can improve by actively exploiting interconnections among features involved in its exercise
```

The paper therefore locates much of rationality in the history of acquiring and sustaining a capacity, not in explicit rule consultation during every exercise.

### 4.2 Perception is organized, discriminative and selective

Perception is not treated as a neutral aggregate of colors, sounds or shapes. It presents a unified scene whose features are grouped, differentiated and selectively singled out for attention.

Nagase's working perceptual-capacity structure is:

```text
sensory registration
-> scene organization
-> discrimination
-> selective singling-out / salience
```

The reversing-goggles case is used to argue that preserved sensory registration can coexist with severe breakdown of usable perception when ordinary sensorimotor organization is disrupted.

### 4.3 Perceptual learning can reshape salience before explicit deliberation

Although perception itself is not an intentional action with a straightforward means-end structure, it can serve action and goals. Goals therefore help determine which perceptual features are relevant, and theoretical knowledge can be used during training to reorganize later perception.

The radiologist example is central: expert knowledge of anatomy and x-ray physics helps train grouping and attention so that some white traces become salient as diagnostic signs while irrelevant traces recede. Nagase explicitly argues that this is not merely a later interpretation of an unchanged experience; the perceptual organization itself changes.

Safe source compression:

```text
knowledge / goal
+ perceptual training
-> altered grouping and salience
-> altered discrimination capacity
```

The paper cites rapid expert lesion recognition as support against a purely slow, explicit post-perceptual inference account, but this example alone does not settle the broader empirical debate over cognitive penetration or the exact processing stage at which expertise effects arise.

### 4.4 Virtue can reshape the action-opportunity field

Nagase uses McDowell's contrast between continent and temperate persons to reject a simple dualistic model in which cognition and desire independently present considerations that are later weighed.

On the perceptual model, a virtuous person's training can change which courses of action become salient as opportunities. The difference is therefore not exhausted by:

```text
same option set + different weights
```

but can involve:

```text
different organization of what appears as a live action opportunity
```

This is especially clear in the example where a disgraceful course of action may remain physically possible while failing to register as a salient opportunity for the temperate person.

### 4.5 Systematic rationality is graded and integration-sensitive

The paper treats rationality as a matter of how extensively capacities can be integrated and used to improve one another. This permits non-linguistic animals to possess limited conceptual capacities.

Bees are the main example. Their flexible use of solar/gravitational cues outside the hive and olfactory cues inside the hive supports local "islands of rationality", but the inability to integrate these navigational systems into a single cross-context spatial capacity marks a genuine limitation.

The source therefore supports:

```text
rich local capacity
!=
full cross-capacity integration
```

It does not by itself establish that more integration always entails more agency, freedom or reselectability.

### 4.6 Language de-contextualizes and makes patterns jointly revisable

Nagase, drawing on McDowell and Clark, assigns language two important functions beyond memory storage:

1. stable labels let patterns be detached from their original context and related to other patterns, enabling higher-order systematization;
2. labels make patterns transmissible and jointly attendable, allowing later agents to refine, criticize and modify them.

Language is therefore presented as a repository of historically accumulated practical and rational tradition that is also open to reflective modification.

### 4.7 Space of reasons as interpersonal understanding

The conclusion expands Sellars's "space of reasons" beyond abstract propositional justification. Giving reasons can include trying to make another person perceive a situation differently, understand why a consideration moves someone, and enter that person's point of view.

The paper's final emphasis is interpersonal: lives affect one another, actions leave "footprints", and conversation lets agents negotiate the meaning of those footprints, calibrate reactions, assume responsibility and participate in deciding what matters.

### 5.2 Salience shaping -> live candidate-field shaping

The virtue discussion strengthens the existing SRT separation between a broad physically/socially available action field and the smaller field that becomes live or inviting for a bearer.

Local bridge:

```text

-> which action opportunities become salient
-> effective invitation field
-> later action selection
```

This is best treated as complementary to `SOC-COG03`, not as a new competing social-affordance architecture.

### 5.3 Language as constraint objectification route

The most useful SRT-side increment is not "language creates rationality". It is the route:

```text
transparent pattern / constraint
-> stable linguistic articulation
-> de-contextualization
-> joint attention
-> contestability
-> possible revision
```

This provides a plausible social-cognitive pathway by which a constraint that shapes what becomes selectable can itself become an object of inspection and revision. The pathway belongs inside the repository's existing `reselectability` family; it is not a new canonical second-order-selection gate.

### 5.4 Interpersonal footprint -> responsibility-position thickening

The source does not show that every interpersonal impact produces responsibility. It is therefore safer to treat the footprint as candidate material that can thicken a `responsibility-position trace`, subject to SRT's separate S4/S5/S6 gates.

## 6. Reverse correction / pressure on SRT

1. **Do not identify concepts with `L_2`.** Nagase's concept is a systematic grasp of interlocking features; `L_2` has a different and broader historical role in SRT.
2. **Do not identify salience or goal relevance with `d-value`.** A feature can become salient because of task, expertise or norm without independently establishing bearer-level stake.
3. **Do not identify the paper's "what matters" with canonical `d-value`.** The conclusion concerns socially and ethically structured significance in a shared form of life; SRT's stake/concern layer is more basic and cannot be derived from linguistic normativity alone.
4. **Do not infer that systematic integration is the same as reselectability.** A system may be highly integrated and internally coherent while its generating constraints remain difficult to inspect, contest or reopen.
5. **Do not infer language from subjecthood or subjecthood from language.** Non-linguistic animals can possess perceptual organization, concern and possibly agency; SRT keeps subjecthood, agency and responsibility separately gated.
6. **Do not treat rapid expert perception as a direct proof of early cognitive penetration.** The source argues for perceptual reorganization, but the exact empirical boundary between early perception, attention and very rapid post-perceptual processing remains open.
7. **Do not equate interpersonal footprint with responsibility.** Responsibility requires accessible alternatives, norm/consequence understanding, revision and repair conditions, not mere causal or social impact.
8. **Do not treat the paper as evidence for canonical `G_hat_theta`, `Psi_f`, `d-value` or `L_2` identities.** It is a philosophy/perception bridge, not a direct test of SRT primitives.
