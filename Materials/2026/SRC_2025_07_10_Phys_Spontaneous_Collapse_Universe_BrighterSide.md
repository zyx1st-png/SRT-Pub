# SourceCard: Brighter Side / JHEP spontaneous collapse cosmology

## 1. One-line summary

The Brighter Side article reports on a JHEP 2024 paper arguing that spontaneous collapse models may explain how a universe initially in a superposition of different geometries becomes effectively classical without requiring an external observer.

## 2. Core claims of source

The news article presents the idea as a cosmic-scale solution to the measurement problem: if quantum mechanics applies universally, the early universe may have been in a superposition of possible space-time geometries, but a spontaneous-collapse modification of quantum dynamics could localize it into one classical geometry.

The primary JHEP paper's abstract states that if quantum mechanics is universal, the universe may be in a quantum superposition of states corresponding to different space-time geometries. Since decoherence-based quantum-to-classical transition relies on external physical entities, ordinary decoherence cannot by itself account for emergence of classical behaviour of the universe. The paper argues that spontaneous collapse models can provide a mechanism: starting from a general superposition of different geometries, collapse dynamics leads to a single geometry. It also explores parametrized unimodular gravity, where collapse occurs on the basis of the cosmological constant, selecting a precise value.

## 3. Evidence / method

Primary technical source:

- Gaona-Reyes, J. L., Menéndez-Pidal, L., Faizal, M., & Carlesso, M. (2024). *Spontaneous collapse models lead to the emergence of classicality of the Universe*. Journal of High Energy Physics, 2024, 193. DOI: `10.1007/JHEP02(2024)193`.

Methodological elements:

1. assume universal quantum mechanics;
2. consider superpositions of different space-time geometries;
3. apply spontaneous collapse dynamics to a simple general-relativistic cosmological model with gravity and a perfect fluid;
4. show collapse toward a single geometry;
5. apply a related treatment to parametrized unimodular gravity and cosmological-constant selection.

## 4. Limits

1. The Brighter Side article is secondary science journalism.
2. The primary paper is theoretical; it does not experimentally prove objective collapse.
3. Spontaneous collapse models are one family of approaches to the measurement problem and remain debated.
4. The paper uses simplified cosmological models, not a complete quantum gravity theory.
5. SRT should not identify its Ghost Operator with physical collapse dynamics.
6. SRT should not claim that the universe literally "chose" in an intentional sense.
