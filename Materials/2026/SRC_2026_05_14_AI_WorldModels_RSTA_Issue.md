## 5. SRT relevance

This collection is a dense SRT-adjacent bundle. Its strongest candidate interfaces are:

- **AI world-model guardrail**: current AI world-model competence must not be collapsed into stake, subjecthood, or `G_hat_theta` anchoring.
- **Life–mind continuity pressure**: biological, basal, collective, and unconventional embodiments pressure the current SRT subjecthood threshold and stable-ISP criteria without closing them.
- **Collective intelligence / cognitive glue**: price systems, shared scarcity models, and human–AI social learning may help operationalize `M(t)`, shared `L_2`, and collective consequence-return distortions.
- **Self-modelling**: auxiliary self-models may act as regularizers or simplifiers, but self-modelling alone is not subjecthood.
- **Causal learning / empowerment**: empowerment and causal model construction are useful proxies for controllability and intervention space, not canonical `d` unless stake-coupled consequence return is shown.
- **Emergence hygiene**: LLM emergence claims fit the existing SRT rule that emergence must specify mechanism, order parameter/effective variable, implementation channel, and boundary of explanation.
