# SourceCard: Gilles Deleuze, *Difference and Repetition*

## 1. One-line summary

Deleuze constructs a philosophy in which difference is not a derivative relation among already-identical things and repetition is not recurrence of the Same. Identity, subject, object, problem framing and stable forms are treated as generated effects of differential relations, passive syntheses, problematic multiplicities, intensive asymmetries and individuation; the book culminates in a model where different relates to different through difference itself and where repetition is productive of difference rather than dependent on prior identity.

## 3. Introduction — repetition is not generality

The Introduction separates repetition from generality. Generality permits substitution among equivalent instances; repetition concerns singular and non-substitutable events. Deleuze's stronger claim is that true repetition is not secured by the identity of what repeats. Variations, masks and displacements are internal to repetition rather than accidental decorations added to a bare Same.

Compact source structure:

```text
generality -> exchangeability / substitutability
repetition -> singularity / non-substitutability

deep repetition
!= repeated copy of an identical term

deep repetition
includes difference, displacement and disguise
```

Learning is treated similarly. Learning is not imitation of an already-complete model but an encounter between heterogeneous signs and responses; the swimmer does not simply copy a movement but enters a relation with the wave.

## 4. Chapter I — difference before identity

Chapter I attacks representation's fourfold subordination of difference:

```text
identity in the concept
analogy in judgment
opposition in predicates
resemblance in perception
```

Deleuze's target is not ordinary empirical distinction but the philosophical assumption that difference becomes intelligible only after it has been subordinated to a prior Same.

The chapter develops a line through Duns Scotus, Spinoza and Nietzsche in which identity becomes secondary. Individuating differences and modes are not derived from already-fixed species or subjects. Deleuze repeatedly insists that individuation precedes the constituted individual, matter/form divisions, species and parts.

His criticisms of Hegel and Leibniz differ. Hegel makes contradiction the maximal form of difference, but for Deleuze contradiction is already too late because opposition presupposes a more primary differential field. Leibniz comes closer through differential relations, singularities and divergent series, but ultimately subordinates them to convergence, compossibility and sufficient reason.

The Platonic problem is reframed as a selective test among claimants. Deleuze's overturning of Platonism rejects the original-copy hierarchy and gives priority to simulacra: systems in which divergence and decentring are not measured by resemblance to a model.

## 5. Chapter II — syntheses of time and repetition

Chapter II develops three syntheses of time.

### 5.1 Habit / living present

Successive instants do not constitute a present by themselves. Passive synthesis contracts repetition into a living present, retention and expectation. Organisms are described as compositions of contractions before reflective subjecthood.

```text
repeated encounters
-> contraction
-> retention / expectation
-> living present
```

### 5.2 Memory / pure past

The second synthesis is a transcendental memory in which the past is not constituted only after a present has disappeared. Deleuze argues that the past is contemporaneous with the present that passes and coexists virtually at different levels of contraction.

### 5.3 Empty form of time / future

The third synthesis breaks the circle. Time becomes "out of joint": before, caesura/during and after are ordered by a metamorphic event. The future is associated with production of the new and with a repetition that does not return the conditions and identity of the previous agent unchanged.

The important source-level outcome for this card is not a SRT theory of time, but Deleuze's insistence that subject identity is produced through temporal synthesis rather than presupposed as a fixed bearer of synthesis.

## 6. Chapter III — the Image of Thought

Chapter III argues that philosophy commonly relies on an implicit dogmatic image:

```text
thought naturally seeks truth
+ thinker has good will
+ faculties harmonize in recognition
+ the Same object anchors their agreement
```

Recognition is not treated as the highest act of thought. Genuine thought begins when something is encountered that cannot be cheaply reabsorbed into the existing recognition scheme: "something in the world forces us to think." Each faculty is pushed to its own limit in a discordant rather than harmonious exercise.

The chapter also gives problems priority over ready-made solutions. A problem is not merely a proposition with a question mark and does not disappear when one response is produced. Deleuze treats badly posed, insignificant or imposed problems as more philosophically serious failures than ordinary error.

Compact source structure:

```text
recognition -> Same under existing categories
encounter -> breakdown of recognition -> problem -> thought

problem
!= temporary ignorance awaiting a pre-given answer
```

## 7. Chapter IV — Ideas, problems, multiplicity and the virtual

Chapter IV is the key ontological construction.

Deleuze identifies Ideas with problems and substantive multiplicities. An Idea is not a completed object. It is constituted by:

```text
differential elements
+ reciprocal differential relations
+ singular / distinctive points
```

The elements do not first possess complete sensible form or conceptual identity. Their reciprocal relations and singularities determine a problematic structure from which actual solutions can emerge.

### 7.1 Problem != incomplete solution

Problems are objective structures, not merely subjective ignorance. Solutions are cases of solution generated under a problem; solving does not abolish the problem's structure.

### 7.2 Virtual != possible

Deleuze distinguishes:

```text
possible -> realisation
virtual -> actualisation
```

The possible is commonly represented as resembling the real minus existence. The virtual is instead real without being actual. It is fully determined in its differential relations and singularities while lacking the determinations of actual qualities, parts and species.

Actualisation therefore does not reproduce a preformed copy. The actual does not resemble the virtual structure it actualises.

### 7.3 Differentiation / differenciation

The English translation preserves Deleuze's technical distinction:

```text
differentiation
= determination of differential relations and singularities in the virtual Idea

differenciation
= actualisation into qualities, extensities, species and parts
```

The object has two dissimilar halves: virtual differential determination and actual differenciation.

## 8. Chapter V — intensity and individuation

Chapter V asks what determines actualisation. Deleuze's answer is intensity.

Difference is not diversity: diversity is given, whereas difference is that by which the given is given as diverse. Phenomena correlate with inequalities of level, temperature, pressure, tension, potential and other intensive differences.

### 8.1 Signal and sign

A signal is a system constituted or bounded by at least two heterogeneous series capable of communication. The phenomenon that flashes across their communication is a sign.

```text
heterogeneous series
+ disparity / intensity
+ communication
-> phenomenon / sign
```

### 8.2 Intensity generates and is covered by extensity and quality

Intensity is implicated difference. In explicating itself it creates qualities and extensities, but the resulting extensive/qualified system tends to cancel or cover the difference that generated it.

This creates a recurrent Deleuzian pattern:

```text
generative condition
-> stable / explicit result
-> result obscures the generative condition
```

### 8.3 Intensity determines actualisation

The chapter explicitly says intensity is the determinant in actualisation and that intensity "dramatises": intensive differences are expressed in spatio-temporal dynamisms that determine virtual differential relations to be incarnated in actual qualities and extensities.

### 8.4 Individuation before differenciation

Using Gilbert Simondon, Deleuze describes individuation as arising from a metastable pre-individual field containing heterogeneous orders, potentials and singularities. Individuation is like solving a problem by establishing communication and internal resonance between disparates, but it does not suppress the problem.

```text
metastable pre-individual field
+ disparity / distributed potentials
-> coupling / internal resonance
-> individuation
-> qualities / extensities / species / parts
```

Deleuze explicitly states that individuation precedes differenciation in principle. The individual is not derived by adding particularity to an already-complete species. The individual remains attached to a pre-individual reserve of singularities.

### 8.5 Interiorisation of difference

In complex systems, constitutive differences increasingly become interiorised in centres of envelopment. Difference and repetition become less dependent on externally reproducing identical conditions and more internal to the organisation of the system.

## 9. Conclusion — complete generative architecture

The Conclusion restates the four illusions of representation and gives a compact description of simulacral systems. A system in which different relates to different through difference itself includes:

```text
1. depth / spatium of organised intensities
2. disparate series and fields of individuation
3. a dark precursor enabling communication
4. linkages, internal resonances and forced movements
5. passive selves / larval subjects and spatio-temporal dynamisms
6. qualities, extensions, species and parts as differenciated results
7. centres of envelopment preserving traces of the preceding factors
```

The problematic is described as a state or dimension of the world rather than a temporary defect in knowledge.

The Conclusion also returns to two repetitions:

```text
bare / material / horizontal repetition
vs
clothed / intensive / vertical repetition
```

Deleuze argues that the deep repetition including displacement and difference is the reason or cause of the bare repetition that appears to repeat the Same on the surface.

The eternal return is finally presented as selective: not the cyclical return of identical entities, but a repetition that affirms difference, becoming and divergence and eliminates the primacy of the Same, Similar, Analogous and Opposed.

## 10. Stable source-level claims for SRT comparison

The following are supported as Deleuze-side claims by the complete close read:

1. difference should not be derived from prior identity, opposition, resemblance or analogy;
2. identity, subject and object are products of deeper differential and temporal processes rather than mandatory starting points;
3. repetition can generate difference and stable form rather than merely reproduce an identical term;
4. problems have objective structure and are not reducible to pre-given propositions/solutions;
5. virtual multiplicities are real but non-actual structures of differential relations and singularities;
6. actualisation is not realisation of a resembling preformed possible;
7. intensity/asymmetry determines actualisation through spatio-temporal dynamisms;
8. individuation precedes qualities, parts and species in principle;
9. stable actual results can conceal their generative problem/intensive conditions;
10. simulacral systems relate different to different through difference, making identity and resemblance secondary effects.

## 11. Limits / non-claims

This source does **not** establish any of the following:

```text
Deleuze's virtual = SRT L0
Deleuze's intensity = Psi_f
Deleuze's dark precursor = G_hat_theta
Deleuze's selective test = SRT Real Choice Moment
Deleuze's eternal return = SRT ontological time
pre-individual field = SRT L0
Simondon/Deleuze individuation = consciousness
thermodynamic examples prove Deleuze's transcendental ontology
modern physics validates univocity / eternal return / simulacra
```

The book is philosophical evidence for conceptual alternatives and pressure tests, not empirical evidence for SRT primitives.

## 12. SRT relevance

### Strong structural affinities

```text
selection before completed selector
identity as downstream stabilization
pre-objectification before object identity
generated problem/option spaces rather than fixed menus
history-dependent transformation
individuation before reflective self-identity
```

### Main SRT pressure points

1. **Preformed-possibility pressure**: SRT must keep `L0` from being read as a warehouse of finished objects/options waiting to be picked. Deleuze's possible/virtual distinction sharpens this risk without defining SRT's answer.
2. **Actualisation mechanism pressure**: writing `L0 -> L1` through `G_hat_theta` is not by itself a genetic explanation of how differential conditions become determinate form.
3. **Productive-asymmetry vs cost pressure**: Deleuzian intensity is closer to productive disparity/potential difference than to canonical `Psi_f`, which is payability burden / ontological friction.
4. **Individuation-before-selector pressure**: "selection does not require a selector" still needs a process account of how a persistent selector-position becomes individuated; this does not close P0-04.
5. **Metastability pressure**: stabilization should not be silently equated with terminal equilibrium or maximum closure; living/agentive persistence may require retained differential reserve and reselectability.
6. **Problem-space occlusion pressure**: a stable solution can hide the problem-field that generated it; by SRT analogy, `L2` can constrain not only answers but what becomes visible as a problem/option.
7. **Repetition/stability pressure**: stable identity may be better modeled as recurrent regeneration through changing states than as literal microstate identity.
8. **Image-of-selection pressure**: SRT must not let "selection" become a universal relabeling of ordinary dynamics. A selection claim must specify difference/non-neutrality, path efficacy, consequence bearing and historical writeback at the appropriate claim level.
