# SourceCard: Laukkonen & Slagter — *From many to (n)one*

## 1. One-line summary

这篇综述／理论论文用 predictive processing 组织 focused attention（FA）、open monitoring（OM）与 non-dual（ND）practice，提出随练习连续谱推进，预测加工的时间／反事实深度与根深蒂固的预测习惯逐步降低；其价值是提供可检验的局部机制框架，不是建立 SRT 层级身份映射。

## 2. Source boundary

来源是 peer-reviewed review and theoretical framework，不是随机干预试验、统一元分析或对 SRT 的检验。作者把模型限定在 meditation practices and associated states，而不是不同传统的 ultimate goals；结尾要求更严格实验、neurophenomenology 与 no-report paradigms。

因此本卡区分：

```text
source-proposed predictive-processing continuum
!= established universal meditation mechanism
!= SRT ontology
```

## 3. Source-backed claims

### 3.1 Practice continuum

论文把 FA、OM 与 ND 放在一个候选连续谱上：每种练习逐步放松更根深蒂固的预测习惯，包括与 self-model 相关的高阶预测。

**Anchor:** abstract；§1 introduction；论文 Fig. 1 及其 surrounding discussion。

### 3.2 Temporal and counterfactual depth

“many-to-(n)one” 模型的中心变量是 temporal/counterfactual depth 或 abstraction 的降低。作者用它组织 episodic future thinking、decision making、self-model、time/body/space experience 等现象的理论联系。

**Anchor:** §1 definition of the model；§2 predictive processing；practice-specific sections。

### 3.3 Testable, state- and technique-specific predictions

作者提出 practice/state-specific predictions，涉及 habitual responding、learning、sense of self 与神经／现象学指标；同时承认现有证据不足以把整套框架视为已确证机制。

**Anchor:** end of §1；future-research and conclusion sections。

## 4. What the source does not establish

不得从本文推出：

```text
meditation eliminates selection
reduced counterfactual depth eliminates agency in every sense
FA, OM and ND form one universally valid developmental sequence
ND proves pure awareness as an ontological primitive
DMN is identical with a self, L2 or consciousness
predictive processing variables are identical with L0/L1/L2
```

论文中的 pure-awareness 语言属于框架解释及其现象学来源，不是 phenomenal necessity、ontological access 或 no-content consciousness theorem。

## 5. SRT mapping

### 5.1 Source-derived relation

```text
meditation practice
-> candidate change in prediction precision / abstraction / temporal depth
-> candidate change in habitual response and self-model expression
```

### 5.2 SRT-side corrective inference

按照 RC-A：

```text
selection of a practice
or suppression of one habitual policy
or reduced temporal/counterfactual depth
!= absence of Selection
```

按照层级非同一性纪律：

```text
predictive model != L2
sensory input != L0
prediction error != L1 - L2
DMN != L2
```

predictive-processing variables可以在具名 P3/P5 model 中与 `L_2` 历史约束做关系映射，但不能作定义或神经身份映射。

## 6. O-track / D-track verdict

- O-track：成立。增量是关系定位与 owner 纠偏，尤其关闭“预测深度下降 = `L_2` 停用／接触 `L_0`／无 Selection”的误读。
- D-track：不成立。论文没有把 SRT 与 predictive-processing、active-inference 或其他冥想模型放入冻结 rival 设计，也没有给出 SRT 独有结果。
