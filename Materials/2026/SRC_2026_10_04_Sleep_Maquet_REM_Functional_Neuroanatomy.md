# SourceCard: Maquet et al. (1996), REM functional neuroanatomy

## 1. Source fact

In seven participants scanned during sustained REM sleep with later dream recall, the study reported:

- increased regional cerebral blood flow in pontine tegmentum, thalamus, bilateral amygdala, anterior cingulate and right parietal operculum;
- negative correlations with REM sleep across a broad bilateral dorsolateral prefrontal region, parietal cortex, posterior cingulate and precuneus.

## 2. Boundary

This paper provides primary neuroimaging evidence for REM-associated regional activity differences, including dorsolateral prefrontal reduction.

It does **not** by itself establish:

- reduced reality checking;
- reduced reframing / reanchoring;
- dream single-mindedness as a causal consequence;
- SRT Selection or Position dynamics.

Those cognitive interpretations require separate sources such as Muzur et al. (2002).

## 3. Revival trigger

Use as a primary physiological baseline only when a dream-specific Reading-C assay needs a neural implementation comparator.
