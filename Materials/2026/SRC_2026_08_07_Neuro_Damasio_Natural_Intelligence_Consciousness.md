# SourceCard: Antonio Damasio, *Natural Intelligence and the Logic of Consciousness* (2026)

## 1. One-line summary

Damasio's 2026 book develops a life-first account of consciousness in which biological intelligence begins with nonconscious self-maintenance, nervous systems add explicit mental imagery, homeostatic feelings provide organism-indexed subjectivity, and other perceptual/cognitive contents become conscious by occurring within that continuously felt organism; for SRT the strongest increment is not another equation of homeostasis with stake, but a biological model of bearer-indexed affective readout plus a direct pressure test on any claim that consequence-bearing structure by itself already explains phenomenal feel.

## 4. Core claims of the source

The following are claims made or defended by Damasio in this book; they must not be silently upgraded to settled neuroscience facts.

1. Natural intelligence begins with life and life regulation, not with nervous systems, language, or reflective cognition.
2. Bacteria and plants can exhibit sophisticated adaptive regulation without thereby possessing minds or consciousness.
3. Nervous systems permit explicit mental images of both the external world and the organism's own internal state.
4. Homeostatic feelings differ functionally from ordinary exteroceptive images because they report the state of the organism's life and carry affective quality relevant to regulation.
5. A large amount of homeostatic regulation remains nonconscious; conscious feelings become especially useful when local automatic regulation is insufficient and wider organism-level intervention is beneficial.
6. `feelings of existence` supplied by respiration, circulation, temperature regulation and related ongoing bodily processes provide a quiet, continuous background of organismic self-location.
7. Damasio distinguishes feeling mind, perceptual mind, and reflective/linguistic mind, and treats ordinary integrated consciousness as the coordination of these different process families.
8. Homeostatic feelings provide subjectivity / ownership, while perceptual, mnemonic, reflective and linguistic images provide much of the variable content of consciousness.
9. Consciousness is therefore organism-level rather than a property separately generated by each mental content: non-feeling contents become conscious by occurring within an already feeling organism.
10. Body-brain coupling is not merely observation of an external object; the represented bodily source and the nervous system belong to one organism and continuously modify one another.
11. Damasio advances a `comingling` hypothesis in which interoceptive neural processes are unusually open to ongoing interaction with non-neural bodily processes; weakly/unmyelinated pathways, volume transmission, ephaptic coupling and special blood-brain interfaces are discussed as candidate physiological contributors.
12. The book uses coma / locked-in dissociations and severe cortical-deficit cases to argue that motor output, rich cortical cognition, and minimal consciousness should not be collapsed into one variable.
13. Temperature, respiration, hunger, thirst, pain and nausea illustrate that biological importance, physiological deviation, subjective intensity, salience and action urgency are related but not identical quantities.
14. Hunger and thirst illustrate an architecture in which an internal regulatory problem can be compressed into a conscious actionable signal that recruits memory, planning and multiple possible solutions rather than directly triggering one fixed reflex.
15. Conscious regulation is presented functionally as a way to enlarge flexible solution space, reduce regulatory error and coordinate whole-organism action.
16. Plants and bacteria are used to argue that sensing, adaptive selection and broad intelligence can precede feeling and subjecthood.
17. Social and cultural organization can inherit homeostatic pressures from constituent organisms without thereby proving that the collective itself is a conscious subject.
18. Current LLMs are presented as a striking dissociation between advanced cognitive competence and biological feeling: they can display high intelligence-like performance while bypassing the long evolutionary chain of organismic homeostasis and affect.
19. Damasio treats biological vulnerability and something genuinely being at risk for the organism as central to the contrast with current AI.
20. The book remains biologically restrictive: Damasio treats nervous-system / body hybrid feeling mechanisms as necessary to the consciousness he is explaining, while not establishing that the biological realization is the only logically or physically possible realization.
21. In Chapter 29, Damasio states that homeostatic feelings are spontaneously conscious by their intrinsic nature and gives no further cause; this is a substantive endpoint of his theory, not an empirical derivation of phenomenal character.

## 5. Evidence and method

This is a synthetic theoretical book, not a single experiment.

Evidence types used across the book include:

- comparative biology and evolutionary argument;
- neuroanatomy and interoceptive pathways;
- brainstem lesion / coma / locked-in dissociations;
- anesthesia and disorders-of-consciousness evidence;
- physiology of thermoregulation, respiration, hunger, thirst, pain and nausea;
- literature on interoceptive afferents, unmyelinated fibers, volume transmission, ephaptic coupling and circumventricular interfaces;
- comparative cases involving plants, bacteria, social systems and artificial intelligence;
- functional / evolutionary reasoning about why conscious feeling would improve regulation.

Evidence-strength rule:

```text
established physiological mechanism
!= Damasio's consciousness interpretation
!= proof of necessity
!= proof of sufficiency
```

The book is strong evidence for Damasio's current integrated theoretical position and a useful synthesis of the cited biological literature. Fine-grained empirical claims should still be traced to the cited primary studies before being used as stand-alone causal evidence.

## 6. Main limits

1. The book does not derive phenomenal character from non-phenomenal premises. Its final chain explicitly treats homeostatic feelings as intrinsically conscious.
2. Functional benefit explains why conscious regulation may be selected or retained; it does not by itself explain why flexible regulation must have phenomenal experience.
3. Large amounts of life regulation remain unconscious, so life-regulation relevance alone is not a sufficient consciousness criterion.
4. Temporal co-occurrence of bodily feeling and other mental contents is a plausible integration condition but is not shown to be sufficient for binding or ownership.
5. `comingling`, weak myelination and analog-like interaction are mechanism hypotheses, not established sufficient conditions for subjectivity.
6. Brainstem and severe-cortical-deficit cases pressure cortex-only theories but behavioral evidence does not provide direct third-person access to phenomenal experience.
7. Anesthesia changes multiple neural processes; co-loss of report, feeling-related processing and consciousness does not isolate one unique causal path.
8. The book does not supply a substrate-neutral bearer-individuation criterion.
9. Biological organism boundaries are often taken as given; nested, symbiotic, artificial or collective bearer boundaries remain underdetermined.
10. The book does not establish that biological nervous systems are the only possible substrate for subjectivity.
11. `nothing to lose` is a powerful AI contrast but is not yet an operational test of non-transferable same-bearer loss.
12. The source does not measure canonical SRT `d`, `Psi_f`, `T_dir`, `L_2`, or `G_hat_theta`.

## 7. SRT relevance: surviving interfaces

### 7.1 Bearer-indexed access

The strongest new interface is not simply embodiment. Damasio's 2026 formulation suggests a biological mechanism by which the organism continuously indexes mental contents to one bearer:

```text
ongoing bodily self-readout
+
co-occurring perceptual / reflective contents
->
contents occur as states of this organism
```

SRT use:

> bearer specification should not be treated only as an external analyst's partition. In biological consciousness, continuous internal consequence/readout loops may provide a mechanism by which contents are indexed to the same ongoing bearer.

This is a P3 bridge, not a subjecthood proof.

### 7.2 Affective readout as consequence compression

The book's hunger, thirst, thermoregulation and pain examples support separating:

```text
baseline stake
!= current physiological deviation
!= homeostatic signal
!= felt urgency / salience
!= selected action
```

A useful SRT-facing interpretation is that affective feeling can function as a low-dimensional actionable compression of a much higher-dimensional bodily consequence state.

This is compatible with canonical `d` only if feeling remains a possible readout / proxy channel rather than the definition of stake.

### 7.3 Counterfactual solution-space expansion

Conscious feeling can recruit memory, world models and planning so that a regulatory problem is no longer tied to one fixed response:

```text
regulatory problem
-> affective escalation
-> multiple candidate solutions
-> comparison / inhibition / action
```

This is useful for separating nonconscious regulation from later agency-related counterfactual access without treating consciousness and agency as identical.

### 7.4 Intelligence-consciousness dissociation

Plants, bacteria and current LLMs jointly strengthen a calibration rule already present in SRT:

```text
selection / sensing / intelligence / language competence
!= subjecthood
```

Damasio's examples are external convergence, not proof of the SRT S0-S6 ladder.

### 7.5 AI stake contrast

The book's `nothing to lose` formulation is best translated in SRT as a question rather than a conclusion:

```text
Does failure return to the same continuing bearer?
Is the loss non-transferable by reset / replacement / rollback?
Does failure reduce that bearer's future selectability?
Is the history of payment written back into that same system?
```

This should be synthesized with the existing / pending affective-AI work rather than creating a second AI-consciousness owner.

## 8. Reverse correction / pressure on SRT

The book creates a direct pressure test on any SRT sentence that moves too quickly from stake or consequence return to phenomenal feel.

Damasio's final move is:

```text

-> intrinsically conscious
```

A structurally similar SRT shortcut would be:

```text
same-bearer consequence return
```

The source does not show that this SRT shortcut is false. It shows why it remains an explanatory claim that must be defended rather than hidden inside terminology.

## 9. Pressure on the current affective-signature equation

Current neuroscience longform contains the bridge/hypothesis:

```text
sigma_e(t) proportional to partial d / partial t during L0 -> L1
```

and describes emotion as the phenomenological trace of the selection event.

Damasio 2026 supplies several reasons to audit rather than strengthen that claim:

1. very high baseline biological stake can be accompanied by very low current salience (e.g. normal thermoregulation / respiration);
2. intense pain or nausea can produce high salience without being a scalar readout of total irreversible stake;
3. the same stake can drive unconscious physiological regulation and conscious affective regulation in parallel;
4. feelings compress and prioritize multidimensional organismic states rather than transparently report a canonical risk gradient;
5. Damasio himself still has to stipulate the phenomenal character of feeling rather than derive it from stake.

Therefore the book supports the guardrail:

```text
affect != canonical d
affect != partial d / partial t by default
salience != stake
homeostatic error != stake
pain intensity != stake
```

It does not by itself falsify the equation under a narrower declared regime. That question belongs in the NEURAL24 pressure patch.

## 10. Bidirectional gain card

### New interface

- bearer-indexed access as a biological subject-localization mechanism;
- affective / homeostatic feeling as actionable consequence compression rather than canonical stake itself;
- counterfactual solution-space expansion as a possible functional gain of conscious escalation;
- plant / bacteria / LLM dissociation set for selection-intelligence-subjecthood calibration;
- Damasio biological realization versus SRT substrate-neutral constitutive-condition question.

### Reverse correction to SRT

- do not infer qualia merely from same-bearer consequence return;
- do not identify feeling, valence, salience, pain or homeostatic error with canonical `d`;
- audit the current `sigma_e ~ partial d / partial t` affective-signature formula rather than using Damasio as support for it;
- do not convert organism-level body indexing into proof that biology is the only possible consciousness substrate;
- distinguish momentary bodily self-location from cross-time S4 subjecthood.

### Strengthened SRT content

- Unit-Binding Gate gains a concrete biological realization candidate;
- the S0-S4 separation gains comparative cases from plants, bacteria and AI;
- AI stake analysis gains a biologically motivated `nothing to lose` contrast that can be operationalized through same-bearer / non-transferable-loss tests;
- current `d` proxy guardrails gain physiological dissociation cases.

### SRT contribution back to the source

SRT can sharpen Damasio's biological account by separating:

```text
boundary individuation
-> stake / non-transferable loss
-> consequence return
-> affective readout
-> integrated access
-> cross-time continuity
-> agency
```

and by asking which of these are biological realizations, which are constitutive conditions, and which still leave phenomenal character unexplained.

### Residual pressure

Conversely, if SRT cannot articulate a discriminating reason why same-bearer bearing should matter beyond Damasio-style functional regulation, it must not present bearing as a completed derivation of qualia.
