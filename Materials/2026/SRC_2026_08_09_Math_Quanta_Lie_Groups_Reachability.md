# SourceCard — Quanta: *What Are Lie Groups?*

## 1. One-line summary

Quanta's explainer is useful to SRT not because Lie groups validate a selection-first ontology, but because the Lie-group / Lie-algebra relation supplies a mature mathematical example of describing global transformation structure through local generators; when combined with standard geometric control theory, this becomes a disciplined prompt for asking whether history can alter not only path probabilities but the composition structure of future-generating transformations.

## 2. Core claims of the source

The Quanta article itself supports only the following bounded mathematical exposition:

1. A Lie group combines group structure with a smooth manifold structure.
2. Continuous symmetries such as planar rotations form Lie groups such as `SO(2)`.
3. Near the identity, the Lie group can be studied through its tangent-space / Lie-algebra structure, making linear-algebraic methods available for a nonlinear global object.
4. Lie groups and their algebras are central mathematical tools in symmetry analysis and physics.

The article does **not** discuss SRT, history-dependent reachability, selection-specific write-back, Lie-bracket controllability, or memory.

## 3. Technical verification beyond the source

Because the SRT bridge developed from this material goes beyond the Quanta explainer, its control-theory component is anchored separately.

A standard primary reference is:

- Héctor J. Sussmann and Velimir Jurdjevic (1972), **"Controllability of nonlinear systems"**, *Journal of Differential Equations* 12(1), 95-116. DOI `10.1016/0022-0396(72)90007-1`.

That work develops controllability / accessibility criteria for nonlinear systems using the geometry generated by system vector fields and their derivatives / Lie-algebraic structure. The broader Chow-Rashevskii theorem supplies the standard bracket-generating template: under appropriate smoothness and bracket-generating conditions, iterated Lie brackets can open locally reachable directions not visible from the primitive vector fields alone.

These mathematical results are **background machinery**, not evidence for SRT ontology.

## 5. Limits

The strongest risk is category inflation.

Do not infer:

- `Lie algebra = T_dir`;
- `dim Lie algebra = d-value`;
- `Lie group = L_2`;
- `symmetry breaking = selection` in general;
- `history-dependent reachability = Lie controllability` in general;
- `memory = generator algebra`;
- the current SRT tiny-MDP already demonstrates a Lie-algebra rewrite;
- noncommutativity alone establishes a real choice event, subjecthood, consciousness, stake, or agency.

Also preserve the local/global guard: the infinitesimal Lie algebra can carry major information about a connected Lie group, but local generator structure does not by itself exhaust every possible global-topological or history-bearing distinction relevant to SRT.
