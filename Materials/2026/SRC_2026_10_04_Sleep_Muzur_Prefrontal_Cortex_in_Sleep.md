# SourceCard: Muzur, Pace-Schott & Hobson (2002), prefrontal cortex in sleep

## 1. Source fact

The review argues that diminished self-awareness in dreaming can be explained by dorsolateral prefrontal cortex deactivation during REM sleep and proposes a cholinergic mechanism for that deactivation.

## 2. Boundary

This is a direct mature-neighbor mechanism for part of R5 Reading C:

    dream generation continues
    while reflective self-awareness / monitoring is reduced

But it does not establish a distinct "reselective control" faculty, nor does it prove that all reframing or reanchoring deficits reduce to DLPFC activity.

## 3. Revival trigger

If Reading C is operationalized, include ordinary executive / metacognitive / prefrontal-control explanations before any stronger SRT mapping.
