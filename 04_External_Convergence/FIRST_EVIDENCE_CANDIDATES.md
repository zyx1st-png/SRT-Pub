# First Evidence Candidates

This is not an evidence index.

This file does not claim that any external field proves SRT.

It only lists candidate directions for future evidence cards.

Each future card must be independently sourced, graded E0-E5, and paired with alternative explanations / pressure checks.

## Candidate 1: Information geometry / Fisher metric

- Possible SRT anchors: `Psi_f`, selection cost, and possibly `d-value` only where stake-coupled concern can be independently operationalized.
- Why this is promising: May provide structural language for distinguishability, curvature, update cost, and parameter-space friction.
- Required source check: Primary sources on Fisher information geometry, Cencov uniqueness, natural gradient, and information geometry.
- Alternative explanations: Generic optimization difficulty, prediction error, ordinary statistical distance.
- Pressure / downgrade condition: If Fisher proxy does not distinguish SRT constructs from generic model difficulty, downgrade to E1/E2.
- Likely starting evidence level: E1/E2 candidate until variable mapping and boundary conditions are sourced.
- Should become:
  - evidence card? Possible, if sourced and scoped.
  - bridge note? Yes, likely first route.
  - lab hypothesis? Possible only after measurable proxy conditions are specified.
  - public example? Not initially.
- Do not claim: Fisher geometry establishes `Psi_f` or `d-value` as canonical definitions, or that statistical distinguishability alone implies stake-coupled concern.

## Candidate 2: Thermodynamics / Landauer / irreversible selection cost

- Possible SRT anchors: selection cost, payability, `Psi_f`.
- Why this is promising: May support the idea that durable selection / erasure / reset / record formation is not free.
- Required source check: Landauer principle, information thermodynamics, and limits of applying thermodynamic metaphors.
- Alternative explanations: Ordinary energy cost, implementation-specific hardware cost.
- Pressure / downgrade condition: Reversible computation or purely formal selection may weaken strong claims.
- Likely starting evidence level: E1/E2 candidate; E3 only if a scoped operational proxy is defined.
- Should become:
  - evidence card? Possible, if the card separates physical cost from metaphor.
  - bridge note? Yes.
  - lab hypothesis? Possible for record formation / reset cost interfaces.
  - public example? Possible after guardrail review.
- Do not claim: Thermodynamics directly defines SRT selection cost across all domains.

## Candidate 3: Neuroscience / PCI / Lempel-Ziv / perturbational complexity

- Possible SRT anchors: consciousness-state complexity, `L1` state richness, and subjecthood threshold only as a weaker downstream candidate if stake coupling, consequence return, and self-modulation are separately shown.

- Required source check: PCI literature, Lempel-Ziv complexity under anesthesia, and disorders of consciousness.
- Alternative explanations: Generic neural complexity, arousal, task difficulty.
- Pressure / downgrade condition: Complexity without stake does not establish subjecthood.
- Likely starting evidence level: E2/E3 candidate for complexity proxies; lower for subjecthood unless stake coupling is separately shown.
- Should become:
  - evidence card? Yes, likely after source verification.
  - bridge note? Yes, especially for consciousness-mechanism translation.
  - lab hypothesis? Possible.
  - public example? Maybe, with careful anti-panpsychism guardrails.
- Do not claim: Complexity measures alone establish consciousness, subjecthood, `d-value`, or stake-coupled concern.

## Candidate 8: Complex systems / robustness-fragility tradeoff (选择空间收窄→脆弱性提升)

- Possible SRT anchors: degeneration reading of `L2` lock-in, selection-space narrowing, and a possible (unverified) proxy contribution to `\Psi_f`-adjacent maintenance-cost readings. Does not currently anchor `d-value`.
- Why this is promising: a source-intuition choice-trace (`01_Source_Intuition/SRT_FIRST_INTUITION_SELECTION_BEFORE_EXISTENCE_CHOICE_TRACE_2026-07-09.md`, P12) proposes that SRT-style degeneration ("没有选择就是没有生成；没有生成，存在退化为被动执行") is identical in structure to the robustness-fragility tradeoff already used correctly and without close-read verification claims in `01_Source_Intuition/BOOK/Drafts_26Q/Q22_方向.md`'s 章末注二 (Holling's resilience/efficiency distinction is cited there as an existing, book-provenance reference point, not independently re-verified in this entry).
- Required source check: Holling (1973) resilience/stability distinction in ecology; Carlson & Doyle's Highly Optimized Tolerance (HOT); Taleb's antifragility framing. None of these have been closed-read or DOI-verified as part of registering this candidate — this entry only names the literature family per the promotion rule below.
- Alternative explanations: generic complex-systems robustness theory (no selection-specific content required), ordinary engineering safety-margin analysis, statistical variance reduction under optimization pressure — none of these require SRT's selection-space vocabulary to explain the same empirical pattern.
- Pressure / downgrade condition: if the fragility literature's "optimized for known perturbations, fragile to novel ones" pattern can be fully captured without any reselection-capacity concept (i.e., by variance/robustness tradeoffs alone), downgrade to E1 generic-structure overlap rather than a genuine SRT-specific proxy.
- Likely starting evidence level: E1/E2 candidate; not yet source-verified.
- Should become:
  - evidence card? Possible, only after at least one primary source (Holling 1973 or Carlson & Doyle HOT) is actually read and DOI-checked, per the Promotion Rule below.
  - bridge note? Possible, paired with the theory-track "熵—随机—再同步" bridge (`Operations/_SRT_CHOICE_TRACE_WRITEBACK_PLAN_2026-07-10.md` T-B) once that bridge exists, since both concern degeneration/entropy-adjacent readings.
  - lab hypothesis? Not yet.
  - public example? Not initially.
- Do not claim: that robustness-fragility tradeoff literature proves SRT's degeneration reading, that HOT or antifragility frameworks were designed with SRT in mind, or that fragility is a validated `\Psi_f` proxy.

## Promotion Rule

A candidate may become an evidence card only if:

1. At least one reliable primary or high-quality secondary source is identified.
2. The card states what the evidence does not prove.
3. The card lists alternative explanations.
4. The card includes a pressure or downgrade condition.
5. The card receives an E0-E5 level.
6. It does not use proof / proves / verified / confirms language.

Do not add real citations unless you actually verify them.

Do not write full evidence cards in this PR.
