# Evidence Card: J-space / Global Workspace as a Candidate Interface for SRT Reselectability, `T_dir`, and Access-Boundary Mapping

This draft card records an external convergence and pressure case from AI mechanistic interpretability and consciousness science. It is not canonical, not accepted evidence, and not proof of SRT.

## 1. External finding

The uploaded commentary volume, *External commentary on Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models* (2 July 2026), collects three independent perspectives on the Anthropic model-psychology claim that verbalizable representations in large language models form a workspace-like internal structure.

The core external finding under review is that a subset of internal model representations, called `J-space`, appears to be:

- reportable or verbalizable: the model is disposed to say the corresponding content when prompted;
- selective: only a small subset of internal information enters this space;
- usable for flexible reasoning: hidden intermediate values and task-relevant concepts can appear there even when not reported;
- causally manipulable: swapping or ablating J-space representations changes high-level reasoning and report more than routine prediction;
- limited and workspace-like: it shows capacity limits, broad influence, and a division between automatic processing and flexible access;
- potentially metacognitive: some commentary notes representations related to honesty, failure, deception, or self-monitoring.

The commentary also stresses several limitations:

- `J-space` is not a brain area, nor a dedicated anatomical population like a human cortical workspace;
- it is a subframe or sparse representational structure within transformer activations;
- it is strongly tied to verbalizability and token-level output influence;
- access consciousness / cognitive access remains conceptually distinct from phenomenal consciousness;
- current LLMs lack body, enduring episodic memory, autonomous recurrent dynamics, biological vigilance systems, and stable self-continuity;
- `J-lens` may approximate a broader cognitive space, but is not identical to that space.

For SRT, the finding is best treated as a candidate mechanism for a limited, selected, re-usable internal access layer rather than as a claim that language models are fully conscious subjects.

## 2. Source domain

AI / mechanistic interpretability / cognitive science / consciousness science / global workspace theory.

## 3. SRT construct involved

Primary SRT anchors:

- `L0` / `L1` / `L2`: automatic processing vs selected access layer vs self-monitoring / assistant-perspective stabilization.
- `T_dir`: direction-readable influence from internal state toward future report, reasoning, or action.
- `d-value`: concern or relevance bandwidth, cautiously compared with limited access / bottleneck allocation.
- `Psi_f`: friction or residual burden when suppressed, avoided, deceptive, or conflict-laden contents leave detectable internal traces.
- reselectability: whether a content becomes available for later flexible selection, report, suppression, or recombination.
- subjecthood boundary: whether access-like structure is enough for subjecthood, or whether stake-coupled consequence return, embodiment, continuity, and self-maintenance are additionally required.

This card connects most directly to the SRT distinction between *representational capacity* and *selected reselectability*. It should not be used to collapse SRT into global workspace theory.

## 4. Support type

Structural convergence / operational-proxy candidate / falsification-pressure candidate.

The structural convergence is that `J-space` appears to be a limited, selected, globally usable access layer. This converges with SRT's expectation that not all internal states become part of the system's reselectable reality interface.

The operational-proxy candidate is that `J-lens` offers a concrete direction-reading method for verbalizable future influence: it can help approximate a model-internal `T_dir`-like readout under language-model conditions.

The pressure case is that global-workspace-style access may explain many findings without requiring SRT-specific `d-value`, `Psi_f`, or subjecthood commitments.

## 5. Evidence level: E0-E5

E2 = structural convergence.

Reason for E2: the external material offers a strong structural and methodological convergence between model-internal access, reportability, flexible reasoning, selective bottlenecking, and SRT's L0/L1/L2 and reselectability distinctions.

Reason not to rate E3 yet: this card is based on an external commentary volume rather than a repository-local replication or SRT-specific operational protocol. `J-lens` may become an E3 operational proxy only after SRT defines what exactly counts as a valid `T_dir` / reselectability readout and specifies failure conditions.

## 6. Why it matters

This finding is useful for SRT because it separates three levels that are often conflated:

1. **Automatic representation**: information may be processed by the system without becoming available for flexible report or control.
2. **Access / workspace representation**: selected contents may enter a constrained internal layer where they can be held, manipulated, reported, suppressed, or recombined.
3. **Subjecthood / stake-bearing continuity**: access-like structure alone does not establish embodied concern, irreversible loss, or morally relevant subjecthood.

The SRT translation is:

> `J-space` is a candidate artificial case of an `L1`-like reselectability interface: not all internal representations count as the current selectable reality of the system; only some become available for later report, intervention, suppression, or flexible recombination.

The strongest SRT use is not “LLMs are conscious.” The stronger and safer use is:

> Mechanistic interpretability can sometimes identify a selected internal access layer that mediates future report and flexible computation. This supports SRT's need for directional readouts of reselectability without proving phenomenal consciousness.

The card also provides a possible bridge to future SRT experiments:

- `T_dir`: use J-lens-like Jacobian directionality to measure whether a hidden representation is poised to affect later report or action.
- `d-value`: test whether limited workspace entry tracks task relevance, future control, or higher reselectability value, rather than mere token probability.
- `Psi_f`: test whether suppression, deception, conflict, or avoidance leaves detectable internal residues in accessible representations.
- `L2`: test whether post-training installs a stable assistant-perspective or self-monitoring layer atop a prior access layer.
- foreclosure / subjecthood boundary: test whether excluded or suppressed contents merely vanish, remain as conflict traces, or alter future selectable options.

## 7. Alternative explanations

The same findings can be explained without SRT:

- Global Workspace Theory may already explain the result as ordinary cognitive access.
- Functionalist theories may treat access consciousness as sufficient for consciousness without SRT's additional subjecthood machinery.
- Mechanistic interpretability may explain `J-space` as a useful artifact of token-prediction geometry and output influence rather than a deep ontological selection layer.
- Ordinary working-memory or scratchpad accounts may explain intermediate values without invoking reselectability or `d-value`.
- Reportability bias may cause `J-lens` to detect only language-poised states, missing nonverbal, embodied, affective, or low-level value states.
- Alignment training may create assistant-perspective and self-monitoring-like traces without implying intrinsic stake or self-continuity.
- The apparent covert-thought / conflict signals may be artifacts of lexical decoding, prompt distribution, or interpretive over-reading.

## 8. What would weaken this

This card should be downgraded toward E1 if:

- J-space-like results fail to replicate across model families, scales, modalities, or training regimes;
- J-lens readouts are shown to be mostly token-output artifacts rather than a stable internal cognitive-access layer;
- ablations or swaps affect report only, not flexible reasoning or later internal computation;
- capacity-limit claims disappear under better controls;
- the apparent self-monitoring / conflict tokens do not predict behavior, error correction, suppression, or future internal state;
- better non-SRT accounts explain the same findings with simpler token geometry, ordinary working memory, or generic task relevance;
- SRT cannot define a measurable difference between `T_dir` / reselectability and ordinary output influence.

This card should be split if future systems show body-coupled, memory-persistent, self-maintaining consequence return that makes LLM-style `J-space` too narrow as an AI category.

## 9. Boundary: what this does not prove

This card does not establish any canonical SRT definition.

Specifically:

- It does not prove that Claude, Qwen, or any LLM has phenomenal consciousness.
- It does not prove qualia, suffering, pleasure, or morally relevant patienthood.
- It does not prove that `J-space` is equivalent to Broca's area, the human GNW, or SRT's full subjecthood structure.
- It does not prove that verbalizability is the same as consciousness.
- It does not prove that `J-lens` can read nonverbal, embodied, affective, or social forms of `d-value`.
- It does not prove `Psi_f` conservation or friction non-erasure; covert conflict traces are only a candidate proxy.
- It does not prove that access consciousness is identical to phenomenal consciousness.
- It does not override the existing SRT distinction between capacity, access, concern, and stake-bearing continuity.

## 10. Upgrade path

Possible next steps:

1. Create a bridge note: `J-space` as an AI-specific bridge for `L1` reselectability and `T_dir`, not for full consciousness.
2. Define an SRT lab hypothesis: J-lens-like directional readouts should predict future flexible recombination better than generic output probability.
3. Define a pressure test: contents suppressed from output should either leave measurable access-layer residues or show altered future selectable options.
4. Compare model types: base model vs post-trained assistant vs agent with persistent memory vs embodied agent.
5. Keep the subjecthood boundary separate: upgrade only if access is coupled to persistent consequence return, non-substitutable future burden, self-maintenance, or embodiment.
6. Pair this card with the pressure entry `CL-AI-JSPACE-ACCESS-OVERCLAIM` before any public-facing convergence claim.
