# Evidence Card: Index Strength, Compression, and World Resolution

This draft card records a conceptual convergence and pressure case from Rin Kuryloski, *The Strength of the Index: Compression and the Resolution of Worlds* (July 2026). It is not canonical, not accepted evidence, and not proof of SRT.

## 1. External proposal

The manuscript distinguishes two operations that can easily be conflated:

1. **world selection**: why a system resolves this world or task frame rather than another;
2. **within-world resolution**: once a world has been selected, why it is resolved sharply, specifically, and with high internal constraint.

The paper explicitly leaves world selection open. It proposes that resolution strength is **compression depth**: a configuration with more accumulated constraint per degree of freedom leaves fewer admissible worlds available, so the selected world is resolved more strongly.

The load-bearing proposal is not merely that more constraint leaves fewer possibilities. It is a cross-timescale identity claim:

> diachronically accumulated constraint is the same stock that is synchronically expended as resolving power.

The manuscript then develops a two-pressure geometry:

- **collapse pressure** removes admissible worlds through accumulated constraint;
- **expansion pressure** regenerates admissible worlds under existing constraints;
- their opposition forms a driven steady state that holds open a self-coherence region rather than preserving one fixed state.

The paper distinguishes several failure modes:

- **corridor**: excessive collapse leaves only one admissible world; local resolutions may remain correct while alternatives die;
- **smear**: expansion outruns resolution, leaving no stable world to occupy;
- **diachronic weld failure**: each moment may resolve one world, but successive worlds fail to inherit constraint from one another, illustrated through dreaming;
- **mis-selection**: a wrong world is selected and then resolved deeply and reasoned through correctly.

## 2. Source domain

Philosophy of mind / cognitive science / AI reasoning / global workspace / predictive processing / compression-based cognition.

## 3. SRT constructs involved

Primary SRT anchors:

- `L0`: field of selectability / reachable candidate worlds;
- `G_hat_theta`: selection and manifestation interface;
- `L1`: current manifest anchoring and resolution;
- `L2`: accumulated history that both supports current resolution and constrains future alternatives;
- stable ISP: diachronic continuity plus bounded mobility and real reselectability;
- `T_dir`: readability of a change in selection direction rather than ordinary within-frame inference;
- ChoiceMap: explicit handling of alternative frames, delayed commitment, reversibility, and hidden irreversible stakes.

The paper is most useful as a decomposition pressure on SRT's current operator vocabulary. It suggests that at least five functional roles should be kept conceptually distinct even if they are not assigned separate canonical operators:

1. candidate availability;
2. world / frame selection;
3. resolution depth within the selected world;
4. within-world inference and action;
5. diachronic weld and writeback into future selectability.

## 4. Support type

Structural convergence / conceptual decomposition / pressure case / bridge-hypothesis candidate.

The structural convergence is strongest at the `L1 -> L2 -> future L1` interface:

> prior selection history can become current resolution capacity, not only future constraint.

This adds a useful support-side reading of `L2`. A hardened structure may:

- reduce local resolution cost;
- make a current world quickly intelligible;
- stabilize context, premises, and action;
- carry accumulated constraint into later selection moments.

At the same time, the corridor model converges with SRT's pathological / lethal `L2` distinction:

> a structure can improve local correctness, fluency, and stability while progressively destroying access to alternative worlds and real reselection.

The paper also supports a ChoiceMap-facing diagnosis:

> deep within-world reasoning cannot repair a prior frame-selection failure.

ChoiceMap's plausible role is therefore not merely to improve inference inside a selected frame, but to reduce the risk of committing deeply inside the wrong frame under hidden or irreversible conditions.

## 5. Evidence level: E0-E5

E2 = structural convergence and conceptual pressure.

Reason for E2:

- the manuscript provides a disciplined decomposition of selection, resolution, inference, continuity, and writeback;
- it preserves explicit boundaries around phenomenality and world selection;
- it offers coarse empirical contact through global-workspace and activation-geometry work;
- its strongest claims remain candidate identities rather than established results.

Reason not to rate E3:

- `compression depth`, admissible-world volume, index strength, self-region, and weld are not independently operationalized;
- activation compactness is a coarse and ambiguous proxy;
- the manuscript reports that a finer prediction reversed in its pilot;
- no SRT-specific differential prediction or repository-local replication has been run.

## 6. Key SRT implications

### 6.1 `L2` has a dual role

A useful bridge-level restatement is:

> `L2` is both a constraint on future selection and a resource for present resolution.

This duality must not be flattened into either "hardening is good" or "hardening is foreclosure." The relevant comparison is between:

- current resolution support;
- future reselection loss.

A healthy scaffold should increase current intelligibility without silently deleting the ability to reopen, revise, or recompose the operative world.

### 6.2 Stable ISP should preserve a region, not a point

The manuscript's strongest subjecthood-adjacent contribution is the distinction between continuity and room to move.

A bridge-level candidate criterion is:

> a stable ISP is not merely a persistent trajectory; it maintains a bounded region in which history is inherited, anomalies can enter, and real reselection remains possible.

Candidate dimensions:

1. diachronic continuity;
2. bounded internal mobility;
3. anomaly responsiveness;
4. reselection capacity;
5. writeback into later choice conditions.

This is not a canonical stable-ISP definition. It is a pressure against definitions that can be satisfied by a perfectly persistent but non-reselectable corridor.

### 6.3 Resolution strength is not selection correctness

The paper sharply separates:

- choosing the right world;
- resolving the selected world deeply;
- reasoning correctly inside it;
- maintaining continuity across time;
- preserving future alternatives.

SRT should not infer one from another. In particular:

- high `L1` stability does not establish correct selection;
- high internal coherence does not establish healthy `L2`;
- fluent reasoning does not establish a live `L0 -> L1` transition;
- continuity does not establish subjecthood if no real movement or reselection remains.

### 6.4 Selection events should be separated from coasting

The manuscript distinguishes continuous computation, intermittent index motion, and rarer world generation. This pressures SRT not to label every state update as selection.

A candidate event decomposition is:

1. coasting within an existing scaffold;
2. anomaly or fit failure;
3. reopening of candidate frames;
4. frame selection and resolution;
5. downstream persistence;
6. writeback and reachable-set change.

High surprisal alone is insufficient. A valid resolution-event proxy should also show frame reorganization and persistent downstream consequence.

## 7. Alternative explanations

The same observations can be explained without the manuscript's compression identity or without SRT:

- predictive processing can explain history-sensitive narrowing and context-dependent inference;
- global workspace theory can explain limited access and flexible reasoning;
- working-memory and attention accounts can explain privileged representations;
- ordinary learning and attractor dynamics can explain stabilization and loss of alternatives;
- activation compactness may reflect topic similarity, repetition, low content variance, lexical geometry, or mode collapse;
- frame selection may be explained through goals, attention, abduction, Bayesian model selection, or task-conditioned control;
- diachronic continuity may arise from memory and recurrent state without forming a subject-involving self-region.

## 8. Pressure on SRT

This source creates five direct pressures.

### 8.1 `G_hat_theta` role overload

If `G_hat_theta` is used indiscriminately for candidate admission, frame selection, resolution depth, inference, continuity, and writeback, SRT risks hiding several distinct explanatory gaps inside one operator symbol.

### 8.2 Selection remains unsolved

Compression depth may explain why a selected world is resolved strongly while leaving untouched why that world was selected. SRT must not use an `L2` compression bridge to claim the origin or correctness of selection has been derived.

### 8.3 Compression proxy ambiguity

Compact activation geometry is not automatically resolution strength. It may equally indicate repetition, attractor lock-in, low semantic variance, or corridor-like overcompression.

### 8.4 Occupation and subjecthood gap

A history-dependent compression does not automatically become a lived vantage, a bearer of non-transferable consequence, or a stable ISP. The move from compression to occupation requires an independently specified stake / consequence-return bridge.

### 8.5 One-world overclaim

A single dominant action frame does not imply that only one world is represented. Alternative, counterfactual, metacognitive, and suppressed frames may remain available at lower access levels.

## 9. What would weaken this card

This card should be downgraded toward E1 if:

- selection, resolution, and within-world inference cannot be operationally distinguished;
- compression measures fail to predict resolution after controlling for repetition, topic variance, confidence, and attractor strength;
- the diachronic-constraint / synchronic-resolution identity fails under direct measurement;
- a strong attention, working-memory, predictive-processing, or attractor account explains the same pattern with fewer commitments;
- self-region language cannot be separated from ordinary state-space reachability;
- corridor diagnostics fail to distinguish healthy expertise from irreversible foreclosure;
- ChoiceMap's gains disappear against strong risk-sensitive or constrained-search baselines.

## 10. Boundary: what this does not prove

This card does not establish any canonical SRT definition.

Specifically:

- it does not solve `P0-04`, the origin of selectability;
- it does not show that compression causes world selection;
- it does not show that activation compactness measures `L1`, `L2`, or `Psi_f`;
- it does not show that one world is represented at a time;
- it does not show that a functional workspace is an index, subject, or conscious field;
- it does not show that reportability equals exportability or phenomenality;
- it does not establish an irreducible phenomenal residue;
- it does not prove the ChoiceMap architecture is uniquely necessary;
- it does not promote the corridor / smear geometry into canonical SRT.

## 11. Upgrade path

Possible next steps:

1. Add a scoped bridge note separating frame selection, resolution depth, within-frame inference, diachronic weld, and writeback around `G_hat_theta`.
2. Define a corridor diagnostic using current performance, anomaly responsiveness, branch recoverability, and future reachable-set loss.
3. Test whether prior history improves current resolution after controlling for repetition and topic similarity.
4. Test whether stronger current resolution can coexist with reduced future reselection, as predicted by the corridor distinction.
5. Pair the conceptual card with ChoiceMap's hidden-irreversible experiments without treating either as proof of SRT ontology.
6. Preserve the world-selection frontier as open unless a differential mechanism outperforms goal-conditioned attention, abduction, Bayesian selection, and risk-sensitive search.
7. Pair this card with pressure entry `CL-PHIL-COMPRESSION-SELECTION-CONFLATION` before any public-facing convergence claim.
