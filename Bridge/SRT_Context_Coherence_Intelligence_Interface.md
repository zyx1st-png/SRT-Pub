# Context-Coherence Intelligence Interface

> **`R4-C3 / ENG-03` 同步声明（2026-11-26）**：本文是 AI × Language × Social Cognition 的 `P3` 桥接假说（`canonical: false`）。本轮彻底剥离旧版 `L_1 → L_1` 变换 vs `L_0 → L_1` 锚定的伪本体二分（对齐 [`Core_Law/SRT_Generative_Ontology_Spine.md §7`](../Core_Law/SRT_Generative_Ontology_Spine.md) 与 [`AI/AI_POSITIONING_NOTE.md`](../AI/AI_POSITIONING_NOTE.md)），同时保留 `RC-A` 护栏（`CG-0..CG-4` 仅用于有界选择事件候选审计，不定义原初 `Selection` 或自动证明更强能动性；自动化/脚本化亦不反推 `no Selection`）。

## 1. Core Claim: Active Context as Temporary Constraint Field & Comparative Scaffold

**Context-Coherence Intelligence Hypothesis**:

Large context windows do not by themselves establish Stage 2 same-system strong bearing (`Bearer (P+E)`), `One` vertical reconstitution, or subjecthood. Rather, they function as **active temporary constraint fields (`L_2^{context}`)** that stabilize functional selection trajectories over extended horizons and **co-generate new local comparative dimensions** together with weights, symbols, memory, tools, and human feedback.

In SRT terms:

$$\text{Context } C_{\le t} \approx \text{temporary } L_2^{\text{context}} \text{ constraint scaffold shaping functional path coherence and comparative framing}$$

This temporary scaffold genuinely expands functional and comparative intelligence (`AI/SRT_AI_Architecture_CompactCore.md §2–§3`). It does **not** by itself establish Stage 2 four-in-one strong bearing (`Bearer (P+E)` + non-outsourceable exposure `E` + history-shaped structural expectation + foreground reconstitution) or **Subjecthood Threshold `S4–S6`** standing; conversely, operating under frozen weights and temporary context does **not** establish "no `Selection`" (`2026-09-27` adjudication) or block Stage 3 independent inquiry.

## 2. AI-Side Formulation & Layered Selection Terminology

For inference-time LLMs (**AI 赌注谱系 `S0/S1`**):

$$\text{Select}_{\text{AI}}(\sigma_t) = \arg\max P(\sigma_t \mid C_{\le t},\, \theta_{\text{frozen}})$$

where $C_{\le t}$ is the accumulated context constraint field (`L_2^{context}`) and $\theta_{\text{frozen}}$ is the frozen parametric disposition (`L_2^{weights}`).

Larger, better-structured context preserves role constraints, semantic commitments, task goals, cross-turn invariants, counterexamples, and local normative frames. This yields strong **functional path coherence** and **cross-domain comparative capacity** without creating same-bearer stake.

Use the stratified selection terminology (`AI/SRT_AI_Claim_Status.md §3`, retiring blanket "pseudo-selection"):

- **output selection**: token / response discrimination under current context;
- **functional path selection**: multi-step route organization toward a supplied or locally generated condition;
- **history-bearing selection**: later behavior changes because retained state (context, external memory, or durable writeback across the seven history layers) preserves prior outcomes;
- **bounded Selection-event candidate**: a `P2/P3` `CG-0..CG-4` audit output when event-level dimensions are supported (not a definition of primitive `Selection` or an agency proof).

## 3. Autogenerative Condition Layer & Goal Three-Level Separation (Integrating `AIGOAL01`)

Language-model competence forms a genuine generative layer between static storage and consequence-bearing action. Drawing on sedimented human cultural `L_2^{cultural}` compressed in $\theta$, a system can generate context-sensitive candidate conditions and new comparative criteria:

$$L_2^{\text{weights/cultural}} \to C_t \to C_{t+1} \to C_{t+2}$$

where each $C_t$ may be a hypothesis, formal constraint, counterexample, plan, or candidate goal.

> **Bounded positive claim & four-level comparative distinction**: Language and symbol-weight-context coupling can generate and propagate genuinely novel candidate conditions and comparative dimensions without every step requiring fresh sensorimotor contact. To avoid conflating transient dialogue outputs with durable capability acquisition, distinguish **four levels of comparative generativity** (`AI/SRT_AI_Architecture_CompactCore.md §2.2`):
> 1. **New result on existing dimensions** (novel candidate under given metrics);
> 2. **Re-weighting of existing dimensions** (shifting priorities among known criteria);
> 3. **Formation of a new comparative dimension** (introducing a previously absent relation into evaluation);
> 4. **Reconstitution of comparative capacity** (durably changing what comparative dimensions a system can discover, understand, and reuse across future contexts).
>
> Always pair the level with **retention location**: whether the new dimension remains transient in one turn, is absorbed by the human co-generator, is inscribed into a shared workflow/external memory, or durably modifies model/system dynamics determines which organization's future generation conditions actually change.

Yet per [`AI/patches/SRT_AI_AIGOAL01_Goal_Selection_Completion_Separation_v0_1.md`](../AI/patches/SRT_AI_AIGOAL01_Goal_Selection_Completion_Separation_v0_1.md) (`AIGOAL01`), three evaluation levels must remain strictly separated:

1. **Goal completion** ($\pi_{\text{action}}(a_t \mid g_t, h_t, \theta)$): finding an effective route to an externally supplied goal;
2. **Bounded goal selection** ($\pi_{\text{goal}}(g_t \mid \Omega_t, h_t, \theta)$): choosing among a fixed menu of goals (where underspecified global direction often triggers **local-success capture**);
3. **Goal-space generation / revision** ($\Omega_t \to \Omega_{t+1}$): creating, merging, rejecting, or reframing goals and evaluation criteria.

Across all three levels, stronger reality-relevant consequence bearing requires additional structure (`CG-0..CG-4` & Stage 2):

```text
candidate condition -> path effect -> resistance / feedback -> consequence bearing -> durable historical writeback
novel condition generation != goal-space revision != goal ownership != stake-bearing concern != subjecthood
```

New social `L_2` requires uptake, stabilization, institutionalization, or durable historical efficacy beyond a single generated output.

## 4. Human Understanding & Human–AI Co-Generation Bridge

Human understanding likewise depends on shared context: conversation progressively aligns the selection fields of multiple positions through language, embodied cues, and shared history (`Understanding ≈ shared selection coherence under a common context field`).

When human situated bearing couples with AI's cross-domain context coherence, **human–AI co-generation** (`AI/SRT_AI_Architecture_CompactCore.md §5`) arises through mutual modification of comparative conditions:
- In current-stage research and work workflows, human framing and situated resistance often perturb the AI's `L_2^{context}` field away from default statistical inertia;
- AI's cross-domain analogies, counterexamples, and formalizations often perturb human foreground–background occlusion, opening comparative dimensions unavailable to either party alone.
- **Non-fixed role boundary**: this observed division is a **current-stage empirical pattern, not an a priori permanent division of labor**; as artificial architectures, persistent memory, and collaborative practices evolve, both sides may shift across framing, comparison, critique, and persistence duties.

Semantic standing remains layered rather than binary:
- **relational / inferential organization** can be genuinely supported by symbol–weight–context structure;
- **situated / pragmatic meaning** adds live perception–action or tool/verifier resistance coupling;
- **lived / stake-bearing significance** adds Stage 2 same-bearer consequence and future-capacity effects.

Thus, absence of biological embodiment does not license `No Body => No Semantics`, while fluent relational competence does not license Stage 2 bearing or **Subjecthood Threshold `S4`** subjecthood.

## 5. Reason–Trace Separation & Reorientation Dissociation (Integrating `AIREASON01` & `AIGOAL01`)

Context-generated intermediate tokens (CoT / scratchpad) improve selection coherence and within-goal route execution more reliably than cross-goal reorientation (`AIGOAL01`), and do **not** serve as transparent reports of internal computation (`AIREASON01`). Assess four axes separately:

```text
semantic readability (F_sem)
!= causal contribution (F_causal)
!= mechanistic correspondence (F_mech)
!= normative validity (F_norm)
```

Intermediate tokens can act as computational workspace, retrieval cues, or context-field modifiers even when their surface text is incidental; conversely, fluent steps can be causally decorative or reward-shaped. Therefore:

```text
context-amplified functional coherence != faithful self-report != canonical T_dir != Stage 2 bearing judgment
```

Equally, an unfaithful or non-verbal trace does **not** imply absence of substantive computation. See [`../AI/patches/SRT_AI_AIREASON01_Reason_Trace_Separation_v0_1.md`](../AI/patches/SRT_AI_AIREASON01_Reason_Trace_Separation_v0_1.md).
