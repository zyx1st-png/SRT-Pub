# Precision Guardrail Pass Notes

> Purpose: record the review response to the latest assessment of the prologue and chapters 1–5, especially the L0 localization risk, ε_pg / ε distinction, anchoring-to-L2 boundary, and the need to shift from defensive negation toward positive construction after chapter 5.

---

## 1. Review diagnosis accepted

The review’s overall judgment is accepted:

- Prologue works because it starts from modern cracks rather than announcing a grand theory.
- Chapter 1 can stand as the baseline opening chapter.
- Chapter 2 has strong philosophical thickness and only needs precision against localizing L0.
- Chapter 3 is conceptually strong but needs a light guardrail around ε_pg and downstream ε.
- Chapter 4 is currently the strongest chapter and should not be heavily rewritten.
- Chapter 5 successfully pushes “existence” toward “order”, but must preserve the boundary between anchoring and L2.

This pass therefore avoids broad rewriting. It adds style-level guardrails and a narrow chapter 5 content clarification.

---

## 2. Recovered into style guide

### 2.1 L0 localization guardrail

**Recovered location:** `book_writing_style_guide.md`, §9.1.

**Core motif:**

> L0 不是某个系统“拥有”的内部空间，也不是认知系统、社会制度或 AI 平台自己的局部储备库。

Preferred language:

- “与 L0 的接触窗口”;
- “可成为性接口”;
- “预裁剪进入显现之前的可成为性”.

Avoid:

- “系统拥有 L0”;
- “AI 的 L0”;
- “某个个体内部的 L0 空间”.

### 2.2 ε_pg / downstream ε guardrail

**Recovered location:** style guide §9.2.

**Core motif:**

> 严格形式层里，ε_pg 首先标记 L0 的非零不对称；它被读作“非自我抹除”的最小偏向，依赖不可逆性与接续条件的联合约束。

Function: prevents downstream ISP-layer ε or anti-closure parameters from being prematurely collapsed into ε_pg.

### 2.3 Positive construction after chapter 5

**Recovered location:** style guide §15.

**Core motif:**

> 第 6 章要把“秩序”建出来，不要只把误读挡出去。

Function: records that the first five chapters can use “not X” heavily because they are dismantling the old floor, but chapter 6 should reduce negative guardrails and build order positively.

### 2.4 Checklist updates

**Recovered location:** style guide §16.

Added checks:

- 是否把 L0 写成某个系统私有的内部空间？
- 是否把 ε_pg 与下游 ISP 层 ε 混在一起？

## 4. Deferred / future work

### 4.1 Chapter 2 precision pass

Future revision should replace localizing wording such as:

> 系统拥有或失去这种开放维度

with safer language:

> 系统保留、扩展或压缩与 L0 的接触窗口 / 可成为性接口。

This can be done in a later dedicated chapter 2 pass to avoid touching too many already-stable chapters inside the current PR.

### 4.2 Chapter 3 precision pass

Future revision should add a light note near the ε_pg definition:

> 严格形式层里，ε_pg 首先标记 L0 的非零不对称；它被读作“非自我抹除”，依赖不可逆性与接续条件的联合约束。

This should remain a short guardrail, not a new formal symbol section.

### 4.3 Chapter 6 opening strategy

Chapter 6 should not continue the defensive pattern. Recommended direction:

- open with order becoming invisible and habitable;
- build the three-step progression from trace → anchoring → L2 order;
- emphasize that order is not merely governance, but do so through positive construction rather than a long denial list;
- show how order both makes the world livable and risks locking future becoming.

## 6. Do-not-recover notes

- Do not overcorrect by rewriting chapters 1–5 into a uniform positive-building style. Their negative dismantling role is legitimate.
- Do not add a large symbol taxonomy to chapter 3 at this stage.
- Do not introduce downstream ISP-layer ε in the main chapter prose unless absolutely necessary.
- Do not turn chapter 5 into the full L2 chapter; it should remain the anchoring chapter.
- Do not make chapter 6 another “not X, not Y” chapter.
