# Dehomogenization Pass Notes

> Purpose: record the review response to the risk that chapters 3–5 are becoming structurally predictable and stylistically homogeneous.

---

## 1. Review diagnosis accepted

The review correctly identified a rising risk:

- chapters 3, 4, and 5 increasingly used similar section functions;
- “bridge shadow” sections were beginning to feel like disclaimers;
- the “honest tension + bridge shadow + next chapter” tail shape was becoming predictable;
- chapter 5 needed a fuller phenomenological entrance;
- the phrase “选择者是选择的化石” was too important to remain a brief line;
- the prologue still revealed too much of chapter 1’s conclusion.

This pass does not attempt to rewrite all chapters into a fully varied literary structure. It adds targeted corrections and, more importantly, records anti-homogenization guidance for future chapters.

---

## 2. Recovered into style guide

### 2.1 Anti-template rule

**Recovered location:** `book_writing_style_guide.md`, §2.

**Core motif:**

> 章法服务概念，不是概念服从章法。

**Function:** prevents the effective early-chapter rhythm from becoming a rigid 10-section template.

### 2.2 Bridge shadow anti-fatigue rule

**Recovered location:** style guide §6.

**Core motif:**

> 桥接影子可以有，但不要总是写成同一种格式：这是影子 → 不等于 X/Y/Z → 后续层展开。

**Function:** avoids repeated disclaimer-like bridge sections.

### 2.3 Action skeleton boundary

**Recovered location:** style guide §7.

**Core motif:**

> 每章最好有一个“动作骨架”，但骨架不等于目录。

**Function:** preserves structural clarity while allowing chapter form to vary.

### 2.4 Checklist additions

**Recovered location:** style guide §14.

**Added checks:**

- 本章章法是否与前两章过于相似？
- “桥接提示”“张力悬置”“下一章预告”是否已经变成免责声明模板？

## 3. Recovered into prologue

### 3.1 Reduced spoiler in §5

**Recovered location:** `00_序_为什么要从存在走向秩序.md`, §5.

**Removed / softened:**

- Direct statement that “存在” is a stable mark of a deeper generative process.
- Final sentence directly stating “现实不是先给定的”.

**New direction:**

> 有一块我们通常放在最底层的东西，需要被移开；移开之后，世界不再只是对象的集合，而会显露出更深的生成、承担和稳定问题。

**Function:** prologue now creates need and direction rather than giving chapter 1’s conclusion too early.

## 4. Recovered into chapter 4

### 4.1 “Selector as fossil” expansion

**Recovered location:** `04_选择不是挑选.md`, §5.

**Core motif:**

> 化石不是原始生命体原封不动地留在那里。原物早已不在，留下的是形状，是压力、沉积、替换和保存之后在另一种材料中继续可辨认的形态。

**Function:** makes “选择者是选择的化石” precise enough to support later chapters on subjecthood, freedom, collective action, and AI agency.

### 4.2 Human retrospective scene

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §5.

**Core motif:** a person looking back at a decision from ten years ago and realizing that the present evaluative frame was itself partly shaped by that earlier decision.

**Function:** gives the fossil thesis a human phenomenological entrance rather than leaving it as a slogan.

### 4.3 Bridge shadow compression

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §9.

**Change:** the earlier three-part science sweep was compressed into a shorter “bridge shadow” section.

**Function:** reduces mechanical repetition and keeps the main chapter centered on selection.

## 5. Recovered into chapter 5

### 5.1 Phenomenological anchoring entrance

**Recovered location:** `05_锚定让一留下.md`, §3.

**Core motif:**

> 那句话开始改变后来每一次靠近同一话题的坡度。下一次谈到类似问题时，它会先一步在场；沉默里有它，解释里有它，防御里有它，绕开这个话题的动作里也有它。

**Function:** gives anchoring a full phenomenological entrance instead of relying only on analytic categories.

### 5.2 Bridge shadow compression

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §5.

**Change:** the stand-alone bridge section was compressed into a short paragraph at the end of “锚定不是记住，也不是命名”.

**Function:** avoids the “bridge disclaimer” template.

### 5.3 Anti-relativism boundary for truth-thickness

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §8.

**Core motif:**

> 这不等于“被更多人相信就更真实”。相信可以帮助锚定，但相信不是锚定本身。

**Function:** prevents “真实性是锚定的厚度” from sliding into crude consensus relativism.

## 6. Deferred / future work

### 6.1 Chapter 6 should deliberately change rhythm

The next chapter should not begin simply with “上一章留下的问题”. Recommended forms:

- open with one extended scene of order becoming invisible;
- or begin with a philosophical paradox about order being both enabling and suffocating;
- or use a narrative arc of one small anchor becoming a rule, then a path, then a world.

### 6.2 “选择先于稳定存在” requires formal marking

The review correctly notes that “选择先于稳定存在” is the book’s spine sentence. It now appears naturally in chapter 4, but should be formally marked in a卷一 synthesis chapter or chapter 6.

### 6.3 Chapter 5 may still need later rhythm tuning

Chapter 5 now has a thicker phenomenological entrance, but still ends in a relatively familiar chain-and-handoff style. If chapter 6 changes rhythm enough, this is acceptable; otherwise chapter 5 may later need a more irregular ending.

## 8. Do-not-recover notes

Do not respond to the homogenization problem by forcing every chapter to be experimental.

Do not remove all structure; the book still needs a cumulative chain.

Do not turn bridge shadows into footnote-heavy scholarship in the book layer.

Do not overexpand “selector as fossil” in chapter 4 into the full subjecthood theory; that belongs later.

Do not make chapter 5 primarily a relationship/therapy chapter; the relationship scene is an entrance, not the main theory.
