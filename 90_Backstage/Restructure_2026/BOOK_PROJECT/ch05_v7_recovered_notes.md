# Chapter 5 v7 Recovery Notes

> Purpose: record the v7 optimization pass for chapter 5, especially the non-anthropocentric anchoring pass requested after v6 review.

---

## 1. Optimization direction

This pass builds on `05_锚定让一留下_v6_candidate.md` and creates `05_锚定让一留下_v7_candidate.md` without replacing earlier drafts.

Main goals:

- deepen the old-floor critique from “history automatically preserves events” to “the world is not an event archive”;
- strengthen the opponent by adding path dependence, affordance-like thinking, and institutional theory pressure;
- clarify anchoring as a criterion rather than a medium;
- add explicit non-anthropocentric anchoring;
- add anchoring carrier surfaces;
- strengthen the cost of de-anchoring;
- adjust the truth/thickness line from “真实性” to “现实性” while preserving truth and legitimacy guardrails;
- sharpen the pathology of anchoring before the handoff to L2.

## 2. Recovered into chapter 5 v7

### 2.1 Old floor upgraded: event archive ontology

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v7 §2.

**Core motif:**

> 旧图景把世界想成事件的档案。  
> SRT 把世界重新看成承重后果的地形。  
> 发生只进入档案。  
> 锚定才进入地形。

**Function:** deepens the old floor from “history automatically saves events” to an ontological critique of treating reality as an event archive.

### 2.2 Strong opponent upgraded

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v7 §3.

**Core additions:**

- path dependence;
- affordance-like thinking without naming it as doctrine;
- institutional theory;
- memory / trauma / storage.

**Core motif:**

> 锚定不是媒介名，而是判据。

**Function:** gives the chapter a stronger reason for why anchoring is not redundant with existing explanatory routes.

### 2.3 Non-anthropocentric anchoring section added

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v7 §4.

**Core motif:**

> 在 SRT 中，锚定比人类主体早。  
> 它也比意识、命名和叙事早。

**Examples added:**

- repeatedly trodden path becoming easier to follow;
- bent / cut / worn material changing later force paths;
- riverbed formation through repeated flow.

**Guardrail:**

> 这不是要把自然过程拟人化。恰恰相反，它是要避免把锚定收缩成人类心理学。

**Function:** prevents chapter 5 from collapsing into memory, trauma, or relation-only phenomenology.

### 2.4 Human phenomenological scene repositioned

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v7 §5.

The “那句话仍在房间里” scene is retained but reintroduced as “anchoring in the human world,” after the non-human-centered section.

**Function:** keeps the strong human entry but prevents it from defining the whole concept.

### 2.5 Carrier surfaces added

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v7 §7.

**Carrier surfaces:**

- body;
- relation;
- material;
- symbol;
- institution;
- computation;
- ecology.

**Core motif:**

> 记忆只是一个承载面。  
> 锚定可以穿过多个承载面，把一次显现变成后来世界的多点约束。

**Function:** gives the concept more dimensionality without turning the chapter into a technical taxonomy.

### 2.6 Strength criteria added

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v7 §9.

**Core motif:**

> 一个锚越强，通常越满足三点：越难忽略，越难撤回，越能跨情境再入场。

**Function:** converts the trace / constraint / terrain distinction into a usable criterion.

### 2.7 De-anchoring cost added

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v7 §10.

**Core motif:**

> 维护锚需要支付。  
> 拆除锚也需要支付。

**Function:** gives the chapter more bite around institutional change, trauma, truth-revelation, and the limits of mere declaration.

### 2.8 Reality thickness wording adjusted

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v7 §11.

**Changed from v6 concern:**

- v6 preserved “真实性是锚定的厚度” with guardrails.
- v7 changes the main formulation to “现实性是锚定的厚度.”

**Guardrail retained:**

> 厚度不自动等于真理。  
> 厚度也不自动等于正当。

**Function:** reduces truth-theory misreading while preserving the stronger reality-thickness claim.

### 2.9 Anchoring pathologies sharpened

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v7 §12.

**Three pathological lock forms:**

- anchors that lock the body;
- anchors that lock interpretation;
- anchors that lock the future.

**Function:** prepares the scaffolding/cage distinction without prematurely writing the L2 chapter.
