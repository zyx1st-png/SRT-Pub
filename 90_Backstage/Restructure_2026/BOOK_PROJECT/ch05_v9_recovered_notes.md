> Purpose: record the v9 optimization pass for chapter 5, especially the integration of concrete SRT theory materials requested after v8 review.

## 1. User concerns addressed

The v9 pass responds to the following issues:

1. The relationship between selection and anchoring was not clear enough.
2. “Bearing weight,” “friction,” and “payability” may not be sufficiently clear for new readers.
3. “Anchoring is a criterion” was too abstract and needed a more intuitive bridge.
4. Stake should be integrated into the chapter.
5. The chapter had not clearly related anchoring back to `L0` and `ε_pg`.
6. Non-anthropocentric anchoring needed more substantive physical / biological / social content, rather than only defensive boundary language.
7. The chapter’s necessity between chapters 4 and 6 needed to be strengthened.

## 3. Recovered into chapter 5 v9

### 3.1 New selection / anchoring relation section

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v9 §2.

**Core motif:**

> 选择是切口。锚定是切口留下的后果开始承重。

**Function:** clarifies that choice produces manifestation while anchoring determines whether the manifestation enters later selection conditions.

### 3.2 New `L0 / ε_pg / selection / anchoring` relation section

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v9 §3.

**Core motif:**

> L0 让可成为性不是虚无。  
> ε_pg 让生成不归零。  
> 选择让一出现。  
> 锚定让一留下。  
> 秩序让留下来的东西反过来组织下一次选择。

**Function:** ties chapter 5 more explicitly to chapters 2–4 and prepares chapter 6.

### 3.3 Stake section added

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v9 §7.

**Core motif:**

> 一个发生是否成为锚，要看它有没有进入 Stake 回路。

**Function:** makes the “anchoring is a criterion” idea intuitive. A trace bears weight when its consequences return to a system and reorganize future selectability, continuity, or cost.

### 3.4 Friction / payability explanation for new readers

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v9 §8.

**Core motifs:**

> 摩擦，是现实不让你免费改写它。  
> 承重，是过去留下的东西让未来必须支付摩擦。  
> 可支付性，是系统在承担这笔摩擦时，还能维持自身闭包、连续性和后续选择能力。

**Function:** translates `Ψ_f` into source-intuition prose while preserving canonical boundaries.

### 3.5 Non-anthropocentric anchoring strengthened

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v9 §9.

**Added domains:**

- physical / material: hysteresis, fatigue, cracks, wear, channelization;
- geomorphological: river channel formation;
- biological: training, injury, immune response, developmental windows, neural plasticity;
- social: standards, infrastructure, money, law, workflows, platform defaults, evaluation metrics.

**Function:** gives the non-human-centered anchoring claim substantive cross-domain pressure rather than only a defensive statement.

### 3.6 Chapter-to-chapter necessity strengthened

**Recovered locations:** chapter 5 v9 §2, §3, §18, §19.

**Core motif:**

> 没有锚定，选择只是闪现；没有秩序，锚还只是局部承重。

**Function:** explains why chapter 5 is necessary between chapter 4 and chapter 6.
