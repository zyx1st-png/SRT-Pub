# Chapter 5 v6 Recovery Notes

> Purpose: record the optimization pass applied to chapter 5 after the chapter 2–4 candidate refinement sequence, without overloading the main chapter or replacing the existing v5 draft.

---

## 1. Optimization direction

This pass keeps the existing chapter 5 architecture intact:

`selection makes one appear → anchoring makes one remain → occurrence is not enough → anchoring = trace / bearing / re-entry → not memory/naming → anchoring strength → payment → reality thickness → anchoring can lock → order handoff`.

The revision mainly applies the recent chapter-optimization direction from earlier chapters:

- strengthen old-floor pressure;
- add a strong opponent rather than only explaining the concept;
- reduce repeated explanatory phrasing;
- make the anchoring / L2 boundary clearer;
- distinguish reality thickness from truth and legitimacy;
- compress the final judgments into stronger propositions.

---

## 2. Recovered into chapter 5 v6

### 2.1 Stronger opening reversal

**Recovered location:** `05_锚定让一留下_v6_candidate.md`, §1.

**Core motif:**

> 现实不是因为出现而变厚。现实是因为出现之后被锚定，才开始变厚。

**Function:** tightens the opening around the chapter’s minimum reversal instead of only rephrasing chapter 4.

### 2.2 Strong opponent section

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v6 §3.

**Core opponent:**

> 锚定不过是因果、记忆、制度化和存储的混合说法，并没有新的哲学必要性。

**Response compressed as:**

> 锚定比因果窄，比记忆宽，比制度早，比存储深。

**Function:** gives chapter 5 the same pressure structure as recent chapter 4 candidates: the old route is not dismissed, but shown to begin too late, too broadly, or too narrowly.

### 2.3 Phenomenological entrance preserved but tightened

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v6 §4.

**Core motif:**

> 这句话不是以声音留下。它是以坡度留下。

**Function:** preserves “那句话仍在房间里” as the main phenomenological entry while making the transition to terrain/pitch sharper.

### 2.4 Anchoring action skeleton preserved

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v6 §5.

**Core motif:**

> 留痕让显现不消失。承重让痕迹不只是记录。再入场让承重继续进入未来选择。

**Function:** keeps the existing threefold action skeleton, parallel to chapter 4’s exclusion / formation / inscription.

### 2.5 Memory / naming boundary compacted

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v6 §6.

**Core motif:**

> 记忆是锚定的可能媒介。命名是锚定的可能接口。制度化是锚定的高阶凝固。

**Function:** avoids over-defensive “not X” repetition while retaining the necessary guardrail.

### 2.6 Anchoring / L2 boundary clarified

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v6 §7 and §11.

**Core motif:**

> 一个锚可以接近地形，但孤立的锚还不是 L2。

**Function:** protects chapter 5 from becoming chapter 6. A single anchor can approach terrain, but order begins only when many anchors are inherited, shared, connected, and backgrounded.

### 2.7 Reality thickness / truth / legitimacy distinction

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v6 §9.

**Core motif:**

> 现实厚度问的是：一个显现被锚定到什么程度，能否持续改变后续选择。  
> 真理问的是：这个显现能否承受材料、后果、检验、反例和多重位置的持续回应。  
> 正当性问的是：这个被锚定的东西是否应该继续被维护、继承和支付。

**Function:** responds to the risk that “truth as anchoring thickness” could be misread as social constructionism or stability-based legitimacy.

### 2.8 Final proposition group strengthened

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v6 §12.

**Core motif:**

> 锚定不是因果、记忆、命名、制度或存储的替名，而是显现从“发生过”变成后续选择条件的门槛。

**Function:** makes the final section less like a summary and more like a set of pressure-bearing propositions.
