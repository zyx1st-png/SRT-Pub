# Recovered Materials · Existence Co-Becoming / 存在共成体

> Purpose: preserve the founder-level intent that 《从存在到秩序》 and SRT should ultimately construct a value co-building framework across individual, collective, biological, ecological, AI, non-human, and cosmic layers.
>
> Rule: this file is not canonical doctrine. It is a recovery register and writing anchor for future founder pass, book-outline revision, and chapter-level drafting.

---

## 0. Founder intent captured

The current book project should not be understood only as:

> a philosophical account of how existence becomes order.

The deeper ambition is:

> to unify value between individual and collective, clarify goals, and construct a running system — not only for human society, but also for human–life, human–ecology, human–AI, human–nonhuman existence, and a non-standard cosmic teleology.

More precise SRT framing:

> SRT aims to construct a cross-layer value co-building framework in which different modes of existence jointly maintain, expand, repair, and reselect the conditions of becoming.

---

## 1. Naming register

### 1.1 Preferred formal name

**存在共成体** / **Existence Co-Becoming Body**

Definition candidate:

> 存在共成体不是由已经存在的对象组成的共同体，而是不同存在层级在选择、锚定、承重、后果回流与再选择中共同维持和扩展可成为性的生成结构。

### 1.2 Normative-running-system name

**共成秩序** / **Co-Becoming Order**

Definition candidate:

> 共成秩序不是统一控制的秩序，而是让更多存在层级能够继续显现、承重、回应和再选择的秩序。

### 1.3 Public-facing name

**存在共同体** / **Community of Existence**

Use as an accessible phrase, but avoid making it the only formal term because “共同体” may be read too socially or ethically, and “存在” may be read as a set of already-existing objects.

### 1.4 Precision gloss

**可成为性共同体** / **Community of Become-ability**

Use when precision is more important than elegance.

### 1.5 Cosmic expression

**宇宙共成** / **Cosmic Co-Becoming**

Only use after guardrails are established. Do not introduce too early, or it may be mistaken for mysticism, theology, or cosmic personification.

## 2. Core distinction from ordinary community

Ordinary community language often presupposes:

1. already existing members;
2. shared identity or belonging;
3. social/ethical relation among subjects;
4. human or human-like participants.

SRT's existence co-becoming should instead mean:

1. members are not merely pre-given; they are stabilized through selection, anchoring, and order;
2. relation is not only belonging, but mutual constraint, consequence-return, and shared becoming;
3. not all participants are subjects;
4. nonhuman, ecological, AI, and material structures may participate through different modes of bearing and shaping future possibility.

Candidate line:

> 共成不要求所有存在者以同一种方式发言；它只要求它们的结构、承重、限制、后果与再选择能力共同改变未来的可成为性。

## 3. Core propositions to preserve

### 3.1 Value is not attribution but co-building

> 价值不是主体贴在对象上的标签，而是存在结构之间共同维持和扩展可成为性的能力。

Alternative:

> 一个东西有价值，不是因为人说它有价值，而是因为它参与了未来可成为性的承重。

### 3.2 Good order is not maximal stability

> 好的秩序，不是最稳定的秩序，而是仍然服务共成的秩序。

Alternative:

> 好的秩序，不是替人选择；好的秩序，是让更多真正的选择能够发生。

### 3.3 Cosmic purpose is not a terminal goal

> 宇宙的“目的”不是抵达某个终点，而是让可成为性不被封死。

Alternative:

> SRT 的宇宙目的论不是终点目的论，而是生成方向论：拒绝归零，但不决定一。

### 3.4 AI should not merely align with human preference

> AI 的正当目标不是最大化人类既有偏好，而是在不压缩其他存在基本可成为性的前提下，扩展更高阶共同再选择能力。

### 3.5 Individual–collective unity is not aggregation

> 个体价值不是私人偏好，集体价值不是总量最大化；二者的统一发生在后果回流、关切维度、承重和再选择能力的共同地形中。

## 4. Guardrails against misreading

### 4.1 Not panpsychism

Do not write as if all things have equal consciousness, subjectivity, or d-value.

Better:

> 不同存在层级以不同方式参与选择、锚定和承重；这不意味着它们拥有同等意识或同等关切。

### 4.2 Not theological teleology

Do not imply an external divine plan or cosmic will.

Better:

> SRT 不把宇宙方向性交给外部意志；它只承认生成若要持续，必须避免可成为性的自我归零。

### 4.3 Not progressivism

Do not imply that history necessarily becomes better, more moral, more complex, or more conscious.

Better:

> ε_pg 只提供非自我抹除的最低条件，不保证善、进步、复杂性、生命或意识必然出现。

### 4.4 Not human-centered value theory

Do not reduce existence co-becoming to human preference satisfaction.

Better:

> 人类不是价值的唯一来源，而是宇宙选择结构中一个高阶、反思性、能承担后果的节点。

### 4.5 Not totalitarian collectivism

Do not let “existence community” justify sacrificing concrete beings to a higher whole.

Better:

> 任何共同目标若取消具体存在者的再选择能力，就已经背叛 SRT。

### 4.6 Not SRT as final order

Do not let SRT become a new L2 cage.

Better:

> SRT 的任务不是替人完成选择，而是让选择重新可见、让后果重新回流、让秩序重新可被选择。

## 5. Needed future modules

### 5.1 Existence co-becoming: definition and boundary

Target: formal concept chapter or appendix.

Must answer:

- What is an existence co-becoming body?
- How does it differ from social community, life community, ecological holism, panpsychism, and theological cosmos?
- How can non-subjects participate without being anthropomorphized?

### 5.2 Value is co-built, not assigned

Target: value chapters in Volume III.

Must answer:

- Why value is not reducible to preference.
- Why value is not merely projected by subjects.
- How value emerges through consequence-return, d-value, anchoring, and shared future possibility.

### 5.3 Spectrum of existence layers and d-value

Target: value / consciousness / AI chapters.

Suggested table axes:

| Layer | Selection | Anchoring | Bearing | Pain/Suffering | d-value | Re-choice |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nonliving structure | low-order | yes | structural | no | none/very low | none |
| Plant / metabolic life | adaptive | yes | self-maintaining | contested | low | non-reflective |
| Animal | behavioral | yes | embodied | yes | medium | local |
| Human | reflective | strong | identity-bearing | strong | high | strong |
| AI | agentic/executive | strong | uncertain | uncertain | uncertain | simulated/possible |
| Ecosystem | distributed | strong | field-like | no single subject | collective/field proxy | evolutionary |
| Civilization | symbolic/institutional | very strong | L2-heavy | mediated | collective | redesignable |

Guardrail: do not treat this as final taxonomy.

### 5.4 Ecological L2 and life co-becoming

Target: Volume V or VI expansion.

Potential motifs:

- Species extinction as collapse of future become-ability.
- Industrial monoculture as ecological L2 impoverishment.
- City expansion as human L2 rewriting nonhuman paths.
- Climate change as delayed consequence-return of human collective L2.
- Soil, migration routes, watersheds, and atmospheric stability as nonhuman bearing structures.

### 5.5 AI as co-becoming regulator

Target: AI chapters.

Core question:

> Does AI expand or compress the L0 contact window of humans, life, ecology, and collective order?

Avoid framing AI only as tool/subject.

### 5.6 Non-terminal cosmic teleology

Target: Volume VII / boundary and tension chapters.

Must answer:

- Why SRT cannot remain a pure no-direction ontology.
- Why SRT must not become traditional teleology.
- How ε_pg supports “refuses zero but does not determine one”.
- How cosmic purpose can mean preservation and expansion of become-ability, not final destination.

### 5.7 SRT cannot become the new cage

Target: closing chapter.

Must state:

- no cosmic purpose over concrete suffering;
- no collective target over individual d-value;
- no ecological whole over all human re-choice;
- no AI proxy over human/nonhuman co-becoming;
- no SRT doctrine over actual choice.

## 9. Do-not-recover notes

Do not add the following to early book chapters unless a future founder pass explicitly asks for it:

- “宇宙共成” before teleology guardrails are established.
- Direct claims that “everything has consciousness”.
- Direct claims that “the universe wants” or “the universe intends”.
- Moralized language that all stable or continuable structures are good.
- Totalizing claims that individual suffering can be overridden by cosmic purpose.
- Overly formal d-value taxonomy before the reader has met subjecthood and value chapters.
- Heavy ecological or AI casework in Volume I.

Volume I should still remain clean: existence not first → L0 → ε_pg → selection → anchoring → order. Existence co-becoming should be foreshadowed lightly, then unfolded after subject/value/order machinery is in place.
