# Chapter 4 v2/v3 Recovery Notes

> Purpose: record the specific material recovered or deferred during the v1 → v2 → v3 revisions of chapter 4, without overloading the main chapter or rewriting the full global recovery register.

---

## 1. Recovered into chapter 4 v2

### 1.1 Stronger philosophical opening

**Recovered location:** `01_Source_Intuition/BOOK/Part_01_从存在到成为/04_选择不是挑选.md`, §1.

**Core motif:**

> 如果没有预先存在的对象，选择就不是挑选对象。  
> 如果没有预先存在的主体，选择就不是主体动作。  
> 如果没有预先存在的菜单，选择就不是选项比较。

**Function:** shifts chapter 4 from a life-oriented explanation of choice to the harder philosophical question: how can selection occur before stable objects, subjects, and options?

### 1.2 Threefold selection structure

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §1.

**Core motif:**

> 选择至少包含三件事：排除、定形、写入。

**Function:** gives the chapter a reusable conceptual spine:

1. exclusion — some possibilities lose current access to manifestation;
2. formation — one direction gains encounterable form;
3. inscription — that form changes the later selection landscape.

### 1.3 Modern science pressure points

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §3.

**Core motif:**

> 现代科学越深入，越会在不同位置遇到相似的缝隙：状态如何从未定、分布、模型或可能性中变成某个确定结果？

**Function:** adds controlled scientific depth without turning the chapter into a bridge paper.

**Included pressure points:**

- physical measurement / manifestation is not just reading off a pre-given property;
- cognitive models can rank, estimate, and predict without yet producing real selection;
- computation / AI can update or roll back states without undoing ontological history.

**Guardrail:** chapter 4 does not use quantum mechanics, cognitive science, or computation as evidence for SRT. It only shows why the old “already-given object/state/option” floor faces pressure across domains.

### 1.4 Latent as residual tension, not ghost-object storage

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §5.

**Core motif:**

> 未被选择的可能性更像一种张力残留。

**Function:** strengthens the “pressed back into latent” section so it does not turn L0 into a warehouse of unchosen ghost objects.

### 1.5 Selector-first problem sharpened

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §6.

**Core motif:**

> 如果每一个动词都必须先有一个主语，我们就永远无法解释第一个主语如何出现。

**Function:** makes the selector-as-fossil section philosophically heavier.

### 1.6 Choice versus execution

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §7.

**Core motif:**

> 选择是路径被结构性锁定；执行是这条路径在时间中的展开。

**Function:** prevents choice from collapsing into execution, while preserving the chain from reasoning to judgment to decision to selection to bearing to execution.

### 1.7 Excluded possibilities as lost force

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §8.

**Core motif:**

> 被排除的不只是风景，也是力量。

**Function:** deepens selection cost by linking exclusion to lost support, lost capability, lost relation, and lost future.

### 1.8 Irreversibility and time sequence

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §9.

**Core motif:**

> 时间不是装选择的容器。至少在 SRT 的这个层面上，时间首先是不可撤回选择留下的序。

**Function:** connects chapter 4 to the existing SRT line that time order emerges from accumulated irreversible selection, while avoiding a full formal treatment of `t_onto`.

## 2. Recovered into chapter 4 v3

### 2.1 Stronger “one appears” opening

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §1.

**Core motif:**

> 第四章面对的不是人类心理学中的 choice。它面对的是一个更早的问题：在未定形的可成为性中，某一个形状如何获得现实重量？

**Function:** aligns chapter 4 with the upgraded prologue and chapters 1–3 by making the chapter-level problem more ontological than life-philosophical.

### 2.2 Threefold structure threaded through the chapter

**Recovered locations:** §§2, 4, 10.

**Core motif:**

> 排除让它不是所有可能性同时显现。  
> 定形让它成为可遭遇之物。  
> 写入让它改变后续选择景观。

**Function:** ensures “exclusion / formation / inscription” is not a one-off list but the spine of the chapter.

### 2.3 Science section unified around “how determinacy happens”

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §3.

**Core motif:**

> 现代科学越深入，越会在不同位置遇到同一种压力：状态空间可以被建模，概率分布可以被计算，系统更新可以被描述，但某一个结果如何成为现实地形的一部分，并不是自动完成的。

**Function:** reduces field-list feeling and makes physics/cognition/AI examples serve one question: how determinate manifestation becomes part of reality.

### 2.4 Stronger L0 warehouse guardrail in latent section

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §5.

**Core motif:**

> L0 不是储藏室。  
> 潜在也不是未发生对象的陈列柜。

**Function:** links chapter 4 back to chapter 2 and prevents “pressed back into latent” from becoming a hidden-object model.

### 2.5 Subjecthood preview guardrail

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §6.

**Core motif:**

> 这里还不是主体性的完整理论。这里只先挡住一个错误前提：不能把选择者放在所有选择之前。

**Function:** keeps “selector as fossil” in chapter 4 while preventing premature subjecthood closure.

### 2.6 Proxy/delegation callback

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §7.

**Core motif:**

> 代理系统尤其容易放大这个混淆。一个平台、模型或制度可以执行大量动作，却不等于它承担选择；也可以替人执行路径，从而让真正的选择在界面出现之前已经发生。

**Function:** ties chapter 4 back to the prologue’s “age of delegation / proxy” without turning the chapter into an AI critique.

### 2.7 Formation cost added

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §8.

**Core motif:**

> 第二种代价，是定形代价。被选出的形状不是自动站住的。它一旦进入显现，就需要边界，需要解释，需要与其他形状协调，需要在阻力中保持自己。

**Function:** makes the cost section match the threefold structure: exclusion cost, formation cost, maintenance cost, switching cost.

### 2.8 Stronger anchoring handoff

**Recovered location:** chapter 4 §10.

**Core motif:**

> 选择让一出现。  
> 锚定让一留下。  
> 秩序让留下来的东西反过来塑造下一次选择。

**Function:** gives chapter 4 a stronger final cadence and a cleaner handoff to chapter 5.
