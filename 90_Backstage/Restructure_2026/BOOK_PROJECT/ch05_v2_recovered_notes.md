# Chapter 5 v2 Recovery Notes

> Purpose: record the specific material recovered or deferred during the v1 → v2 revision of chapter 5, without overloading the main chapter or rewriting the global recovery register.

## 1. Recovered into chapter 5 v2

### 1.1 Write-in versus anchoring boundary

**Recovered location:** `01_Source_Intuition/BOOK/Part_01_从存在到成为/05_锚定让一留下.md`, §1.

**Core motif:**

> 写入是选择切入现实的那一瞬间。  
> 锚定是这个切入没有立刻消散，而是开始获得持续摩擦、继承和再入场能力。

**Function:** prevents chapter 5 from overlapping with chapter 4’s “inscription / 写入”.

### 1.2 Stronger old floor

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §2.

**Old floor:**

> 历史会自动保存发生过的东西。

**SRT reversal:**

> 历史不会自动保存发生过的东西。现实由被锚定的事件变厚。

**Function:** replaces the weaker target “发生过就算存在过” with a more precise automatic-continuation / automatic-preservation illusion.

### 1.3 Networked anchoring

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §3.

**Core motif:**

> 锚定不是把一个孤立对象锁进保险箱。它更像把一个显现缝进关系网。

**Function:** makes anchoring relational, preparing the handoff to order as a network of mutually connected anchors.

### 1.4 Bridge shadow section heading and guardrail

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §5.

**Core motif:**

> 这里仍然要守住本书的桥接方式：这些材料不是 SRT 的证明。它们只是桥接的影子，说明旧地板并非只在哲学语言里出问题。

**Function:** explicitly adds the “bridge shadow” style rule missing from v1.

### 1.5 Anchoring and friction image

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §6.

**Core motif:**

> 如果完全没有摩擦，任何痕迹都会滑走。锚定就是让历史生出倒刺。

**Function:** adds a more physical / ontological image without turning the chapter into Ψ_f formalization.

### 1.6 Bearing versus payability

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §7.

**Core motif:**

> 承重，是锚对外的作用。  
> 支付，是锚对内的条件。

**Function:** clarifies the boundary between chapter 5’s bearing structure and later Ψ_f/payability treatment.

### 1.7 Reality thickness line

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §8.

**Core motif:**

> 现实的厚度，不是出现的强度，而是锚定的深度。

**Function:** sharpens the chapter’s reality-thickness claim.

### 1.8 Falsehood can gain reality thickness

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §8.

**Core motif:**

> 虚假的东西如果被足够深地锚定，也会获得现实厚度。打破它，不能只靠揭示真相；还要支付拆除锚点的代价。

**Function:** strengthens the social/political force of the anchoring chapter while preserving the stability-versus-legitimacy boundary.

### 1.9 Stronger handoff to order

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §10.

**Core motif:**

> 秩序让许多留下来的“一”彼此牵连，形成后来的世界默认面对的结构。

**Function:** strengthens the transition from anchoring to order.

## 2. Recovered into prologue / style guide during this pass

### 2.1 Prologue tone: less manual-like, more philosophical tension

**Recovered location:** `01_Source_Intuition/BOOK/00_序_为什么要从存在走向秩序.md`, §§6–8.

**Core motif:**

> 写这本书最容易的诱惑，是把它写成一种已经完成的系统：先给定义，再排章节，再告诉读者每个概念应该放在哪里。那样会清楚，但也会过早地把 SRT 变成它自己正在警惕的东西：一个替人完成选择的地板。

**Function:** responds to the review concern that the prologue was too much like a manual / project statement and not enough like a philosophical book facing a real difficulty.

### 2.2 Prologue commitments split and strengthened

**Recovered location:** prologue §8.

**Core motif:**

> 本书首先承诺一件事：它会把世界的地板，从静态存在，移到选择生成的秩序。

**Function:** avoids stacking too many “does not” claims and turns the commitments section into a positive promise plus controlled refusals.

### 2.3 Style guide additions

**Recovered location:** `90_Backstage/Restructure_2026/BOOK_PROJECT/book_writing_style_guide.md`.

**Added rules:**

- “现象学入口要有呼吸，特别是困难概念”;
- “边界要挡具体误读，不要堆叠否定”;
- “让思考过程适度可见”.

**Function:** makes the Claude review actionable for later chapters without imposing a rigid template.
