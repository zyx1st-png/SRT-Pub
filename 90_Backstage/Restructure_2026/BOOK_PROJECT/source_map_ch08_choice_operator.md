# Source Map · Chapter 8 Choice Operator

> Purpose: keep external conceptual support for Chapter 8 in the backstage layer. The book chapter should remain SRT-native and should not become a literature review.

---

## 0. Chapter 8 SRT claim

Chapter 8 introduces the choice operator as a structure-name:

> 选择算子不是选择者，而是选择发生的形状。

It supports the transition:

```text
L2 terrain enters choice-before-choice
→ choice operator names the concrete structuring of manifestation
→ contact window / formative conditions / manifestation
→ exclusion follows as the shadow of manifestation
```

Main nochange candidates:

- 显现不是取出，而是定形。
- 选择算子不是选择者，而是选择发生的形状。
- 现实的厚度，不只来自它被看见，而来自它在选择算子中被定形、支付、承重并接受后果回流。
- 再选择不是换选项。再选择是重写选择算子的定形条件。

## 1. Embodied cognition / ecological psychology

### Why relevant

Supports the intuition that cognition and perception are not merely internal computation. Body, environment, movement, and situated interaction participate in how a world is encountered.

### Use in SRT

Do not write: SRT = embodied cognition.

Write instead:

> SRT goes one layer deeper: not only cognition, but manifestation itself depends on how contact window and formative conditions shape what can become encounterable.

### Bridge lines for future note/appendix

- Embodied cognition challenges brain-only/computational pictures of cognition.
- Ecological psychology emphasizes organism-environment coupling and perception through active movement.
- SRT can use this as a bridge, but its core claim is ontological rather than cognitive-scientific.

### Public source notes

- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Embodied Cognition”: embodied cognition emphasizes the significance of an agent's physical body and body-environment interaction for cognitive abilities.
- SEP also describes ecological psychology as rejecting the isolation of visual perception to eye/brain processes and emphasizing the whole organism moving in the environment.

## 2. Affordance / situated action

### Why relevant

Chapter 8 claims that the same world appears differently under different choice operators. This is compatible with the idea that environments present action possibilities relative to organisms, skills, and situations.

### Use in SRT

Avoid reducing SRT to affordance theory.

SRT version:

> Affordances describe action possibilities in an organism-environment field; choice operator names the deeper structuring by which pre-objective become-ability is compressed into encounterable reality.

---

## 3. Choice architecture / default effects

### Why relevant

Supports Chapter 7–8 claims that default channels and pre-arranged structures can shape what is chosen before reflective decision.

### Use in SRT

Do not over-psychologize.

SRT version:

> Defaults do not merely bias final choices; as L2 structures, they can enter the formative conditions by which possible actions become visible, low-friction, and anchorable.

### Public source notes

- Jachimowicz, Duncan, Weber & Johnson meta-analysis: when people face a pre-selected default, they are more likely to choose it; defaults are widely used in the choice architecture toolbox.
- Their meta-analysis covered 58 studies with pooled n = 73,675 and found a considerable overall influence of defaults, with variation across studies.

---

## 4. Habitus / embodied social structure

### Why relevant

Chapter 8 treats body, habit, history, and social terrain as formative conditions. Habitus helps bridge how social structures become embodied dispositions.

### Use in SRT

Do not write: SRT = Bourdieu.

SRT version:

> Habitus helps describe how social terrain becomes bodily slope. SRT asks a broader ontological question: how does such slope enter the choice operator and shape manifestation itself?

---

## 5. Niche construction

### Why relevant

SRT claims past choices can become terrain for later choices. Niche construction provides a biological/evolutionary bridge: organisms modify niches and selection pressures for themselves and others.

### Use in SRT

Do not make Chapter 8 an evolution chapter.

Use later as bridge for existence co-becoming and ecological L2.

SRT version:

> Niche construction is a biological instance of a more general SRT pattern: stabilized activity modifies the future landscape of selection.

### Public source notes

- Nicheconstruction.com overview: organisms modify the selection they experience by physically changing environments or choosing habitats.
- Recent Synthese paper notes niche construction is traditionally understood through organisms altering selection pressures, and argues organismic activities can also alter drift probabilities.
