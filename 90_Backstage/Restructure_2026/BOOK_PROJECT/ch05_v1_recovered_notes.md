## 1. Recovered into chapter 5 v1

### 1.1 Chapter-level old floor and SRT reversal

**Recovered location:** `01_Source_Intuition/BOOK/Part_01_从存在到成为/05_锚定让一留下.md`, §§1–2.

**Old floor:**

> 发生过就算存在过。

**SRT reversal:**

> 发生过不等于留下；现实由被锚定的事件变厚。

**Function:** gives chapter 5 the same old-floor → reversal structure used in the prologue and chapters 1–4.

### 1.2 Threefold anchoring structure

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §3.

**Core motif:**

> 锚定至少包含三件事：留痕、承重、再入场。

**Function:** gives chapter 5 its action skeleton, parallel to chapter 4’s exclusion / formation / inscription.

### 1.3 Anchoring is not memory or naming

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §4.

**Core motif:**

> 锚定不是“我知道它”。也不是“我给它起了名字”。锚定是：它已经在后续选择里继续发生作用。

**Function:** prevents anchoring from collapsing into subjective memory or linguistic labeling.

### 1.4 Scientific pressure points for anchoring

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §5.

**Included pressure points:**

- neuroscience / memory consolidation;
- complex systems / hysteresis / attractors / metastability;
- social science / institutions / standards / infrastructure / defaults;
- computation / logs / databases / version control / weights / configurations.

**Guardrail:** none of these prove SRT. They only show the recurrent pressure point: how occurrence becomes future-state-space alteration.

### 1.5 Anchoring strength: trace / constraint / terrain

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §6.

**Core motif:**

> 痕迹，是发生没有完全消失。约束，是痕迹开始要求后续选择回应它。地形，是约束被反复继承，变成后续选择默认面对的现实结构。

**Function:** provides an intuitive thickness scale for anchoring without overformalizing.

### 1.6 Anchoring has payability

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §7.

**Core motif:**

> 没有支付，锚会松。

**Function:** connects anchoring to later Ψ_f / maintenance cost without introducing formulas.

### 1.7 Anchoring and reality thickness

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §8.

**Core motif:**

> 真实性不是一句话的属性。真实性是锚定的厚度。

**Function:** connects anchoring to the book’s repeated theme that reality has thickness.

### 1.8 Anchoring danger: support becoming lock-in

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §9.

**Core motif:**

> 锚定的危险在于：它一开始是为了让选择留下，后来却可能让选择不能再发生。

**Function:** creates the bridge toward L2, lethal L2, and the scaffolding/cage distinction.

### 1.9 Handoff to order

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 §10.

**Core motif:**

> 选择让一出现。锚定让一留下。秩序让许多留下来的“一”彼此排列，形成后来的世界默认面对的结构。

**Function:** gives chapter 5 a clean handoff to the next chapter on order.
