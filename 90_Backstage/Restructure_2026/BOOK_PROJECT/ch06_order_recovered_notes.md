# Chapter 6 · Order as Background · Recovered Notes

> Purpose: record source motifs, guardrails, and deferred material for chapter 6 `06_秩序如何成为背景.md`.

---

## 1. Chapter task

Chapter 6 completes the first major chain of Part 1:

```text
L0 → ε_pg → selection → anchoring → order / L2
```

It must shift from mostly defensive ground-clearing to positive construction:

- define order as connected, inheritable, shareable, backgrounded anchoring;
- show order as habitable ground, not merely constraint;
- introduce L2 as second-layer stability without turning this into the full L2/lethal-L2 chapter;
- prepare the next chapter: L2 as condition for later selection.

## 2. Style constraints applied

### 2.1 Avoided mechanical “上一章留下的问题” opening

The chapter opens with a morning route scene instead of a direct analytic handoff.

The chapter does not build around long “秩序不是 X” lists. It uses positive construction:

- 脚手架;
- 牢笼;
- L2 as backgrounded second-layer stability.

### 2.3 Bridge shadow kept internal

No stand-alone “桥接提示” section. Scientific/social bridge material is implied via:

- roads / language / habits / institutions;
- technical standards;
- platform defaults;
- AI pre-pruning;
- institutions and social order.
