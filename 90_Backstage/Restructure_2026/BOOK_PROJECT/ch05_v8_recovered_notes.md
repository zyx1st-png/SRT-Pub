## 2. Recovered into chapter 5 v8

### 2.1 Stronger epigraph / founder line restored

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v8 opening quote and §12.

**Core motif restored:**

> 真实性不是一句话的属性；真实性是锚定的厚度。

**Guardrail retained:**

> 厚度不自动等于真理。  
> 厚度也不自动等于正当。

**Function:** preserves founder voice while protecting against truth-theory and legitimacy misreadings.

### 2.2 Traditional philosophy positioning added

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v8 §3.

**Traditions / routes positioned against:**

- substance / entity persistence;
- causal consequence;
- memory and collective memory;
- phenomenology / hermeneutics / sedimentation;
- process philosophy;
- social construction / institutional theory.

**Core motif:**

> 锚定卡在一个传统概念常常掠过的位置：不是“什么存在”，不是“事件有什么因果后果”，不是“主体如何记住”，不是“共同体如何承认”，而是：一个显现如何获得持续承重、跨情境再入场和改变未来地形的能力。

**Function:** gives chapter 5 stronger philosophical pressure and prevents the concept from reading as a life-experience insight.

### 2.3 Stronger opponent upgraded to “mixture of existing traditions”

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v8 §4.

**Core opponent:**

> 锚定没有新的哲学地位。你只是把实体持续性、因果后果、现象学沉积、习惯理论、路径依赖、制度化、社会记忆和技术存储混合在一起，然后给它们起了一个更有诗意的名字。

**Core response:**

> 锚定不是媒介名，也不是总括名。锚定是判据。

**Function:** raises the opponent from domain-level redundancy to conceptual redundancy, which is the more serious challenge for a third-tier philosophical chapter.

### 2.4 Life scene compressed and reframed

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v8 §8.

The “那句话仍在房间里” scene is retained but shortened and explicitly framed as:

> 人类场景只是显影剂。

**Function:** keeps the useful phenomenological entry while preventing the chapter from becoming relationship/life-philosophy prose.

### 2.5 Carrier surfaces compressed

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v8 §7.

The v7 carrier-surface list is retained but compressed into a prose paragraph, with explicit anti-taxonomy guardrail:

> 这不是要把锚定做成分类学。

**Function:** preserves dimensionality while avoiding a list-like explanatory feel.

### 2.6 Non-anthropocentric anchoring retained

**Recovered location:** chapter 5 v8 §5.

The v7 non-human examples remain:

- trodden path;
- bent / cut / worn material;
- riverbed.

**Function:** keeps the requested non-anthropocentric anchoring, but after philosophical positioning and before human phenomenology.
