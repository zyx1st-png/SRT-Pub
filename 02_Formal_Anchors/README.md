# 02 Formal Anchors / 形式锚点

This layer is the home for SRT's canonical definitions, minimal axioms, formal claims, equations, symbols, open tensions, and test-facing variables.

If `01_Source_Intuition` says why SRT begins, this layer says what SRT commits to.

## Role

`02_Formal_Anchors` should answer:

- What are the minimal commitments of SRT?
- Which claims are primitive, constitutive, bridge-level, lab-level, companion-level, or public summaries?
- What are the canonical meanings of `L0`, `L1`, `L2`, `G_hat_theta`, `d-value`, `Psi_f`, and `T_dir`?
- Which equations are canonical, which are proxies, and which are bridge hypotheses?
- What remains open, unresolved, or under pressure?

## Formal posture

This layer should preserve SRT's hardness without turning the whole repository into a governance surface.

Preferred style:

- explicit definitions;
- claim-level labels where needed;
- proxy versus canonical separation;
- clear failure conditions;
- no rhetorical overreach;
- no use of external fields as proof unless routed through `04_External_Convergence`.

## Boundary

Formal anchors do not need to carry the full public force of SRT. They serve as the load-bearing skeleton. Source intuition belongs in `01_Source_Intuition`; translation to other frameworks belongs in `03_Bridges`; public-facing versions belong in `05_Public_Release`.
